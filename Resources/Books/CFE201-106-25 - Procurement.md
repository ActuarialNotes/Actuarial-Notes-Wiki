A McKinsey article of May 2022 on how procurement leaders at industrial companies can avoid product shortages and mitigate the effects of price volatility. It says procurement departments need a comprehensive view of their supply chain vulnerabilities, organized around their companies' product lines, and describes an approach built on three potential problems: absolute-shortage risk, supplier risk and inflation risk.
