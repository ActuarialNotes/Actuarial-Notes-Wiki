An article in the Society of Actuaries' Health Section News, October 2004 (Issue No. 48), on signing the actuarial opinion on a health insurer's annual statement. It reviews the six opinions the signing actuary must offer for each item in the statement of actuarial opinion, what they mean in practice, and some case studies, focusing on weaknesses in practice.
