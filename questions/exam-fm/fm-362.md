A payment of 55,400 made today returns payments of 10,000 at the beginning of each year for ten years. These payments are deposited into an account that earns interest at an annual effective rate of 6% payable at the end of each year. The interest is immediately reinvested at an annual effective interest rate of 4%. The original payment earns an annual effective yield rate of $i$ over the ten-year period.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 9.0%
- B) 9.5%
- C) 10.0%
- D) 10.5%
- E) 11.0%

## Explanation

At the end of ten years the account holds the ten payments of 10,000 plus the interest they earned, with that interest accumulated at the 4% reinvestment rate. The payments are deposited at times 0 through 9, so the balance earning 6% during year $k$ is $10{,}000k$ and the interest paid at the end of year $k$ is $600k$, for $k = 1, 2, \ldots, 10$.

Split these interest payments into a level 600 at the end of each year, which accumulates to $600\,s_{\overline{10}|0.04}$, plus the increasing amounts $0, 600, 1200, \ldots, 5400$, which accumulate to $600\,\dfrac{s_{\overline{10}|0.04} - 10}{0.04}$. With $s_{\overline{10}|0.04} = 12.006107$, the value at time 10 is:

$$
\begin{align*}
AV &= 10{,}000(10) + \left[600\,s_{\overline{10}|0.04} + 600\,\frac{s_{\overline{10}|0.04} - 10}{0.04}\right] \\
   &= 100{,}000 + 600(12.006107) + 600\left(\frac{12.006107 - 10}{0.04}\right) \\
   &= 100{,}000 + 7203.66 + 30{,}091.61 \\
   &= 137{,}295.27
\end{align*}
$$

The yield rate $i$ makes the payment of 55,400 today accumulate to this amount over the ten years:

$$
\begin{align*}
55{,}400(1+i)^{10} &= 137{,}295.27 \\
(1+i)^{10} &= 2.478254 \\
i &= 0.095
\end{align*}
$$
