A 479-page text from the Society of Actuaries presenting financial reporting topics that are important to general insurance actuaries. It covers a review of basic accounting concepts, key topics from U.S. statutory accounting for general insurers, the measurement of the financial health of general insurers, the Statement of Actuarial Opinion and federal income taxes for general insurers.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GI-302 (SOA)|Exam GI-302]] — Outcomes 3a-c: Part 1, Insurance Accounting (sections 2.3, 2.5-2.8, 3.4, 4.4-4.6 and 4.8.3-4.9), Part 2, U.S. Statutory Accounting (chapters 5 to 9), and Chapter 15, Federal Income Taxes for General Insurers (sections 15.2-15.10, excluding the Canadian specific reference on p. 443); Outcome 3d: Part 3, General Insurance Financial Health Topics (sections 10.4.3, 10.8.3, 11.8, 11.10-11.11, 12.9, 12.10.2 and 12.11); Outcome 4c: Chapter 14, The General Insurance Actuarial Opinion (sections 14.2.2-14.4.1, 14.6 and 14.8, excluding 14.2.6.3 and all other Canadian specific references) (as the syllabus states it)
