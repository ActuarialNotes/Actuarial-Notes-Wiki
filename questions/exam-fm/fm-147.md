Determine which of the following statements regarding asset-liability management techniques is true.

- A) Redington immunization requires that the convexity of the liabilities is greater than the convexity of the assets.
- B) An advantage of the Redington immunization technique over the cash-flow matching technique is that the portfolio manager has more investment choices available.
- C) Both Redington immunization and full immunization are based on the assumption that the yield curve has higher yields for longer term investments.
- D) A fully immunized portfolio ensures that the present value of assets will exceed the present value of liabilities with non-parallel shifts in the yield curve.
- E) A cash-flow matched portfolio requires less rebalancing than a Redington immunized portfolio, but more rebalancing than a fully immunized portfolio.

## Explanation

**A. False.** Redington immunization requires the convexity of the *assets* to be greater than the convexity of the *liabilities*, not the other way round.

**B. True.** Cash-flow matching limits the portfolio manager's investment choices to a subset of those available for immunization. A cash-flow matched portfolio must produce asset inflows that meet each liability outflow exactly. Redington immunization only requires equal present values and durations for assets and liabilities, with the assets having the greater convexity, and many different portfolios meet those conditions.

**C. False.** Both techniques are developed under a flat yield curve: one interest rate $i$ discounts cash flows at every term, and a rate change moves that single rate. Neither assumes an upward-sloping curve.

**D. False.** Full immunization protects against any change in that single rate $i$, which is a parallel shift of a flat yield curve. It gives no such guarantee under non-parallel shifts.

**E. False.** In a cash-flow matched portfolio the asset inflows exactly meet the liability outflows, so interest-rate movements do not require it to be rebalanced. Redington and fully immunized portfolios both have to be rebalanced periodically to keep asset and liability durations equal. Cash-flow matching therefore needs *less* rebalancing than both, not more than full immunization.
