![[Fixed Income Securities (Veronesi - 2010) - Cover.svg]]

A textbook on the forces that affect the valuation, risk and return of fixed income securities and their derivatives, published by Wiley in January 2010. Its publisher describes it as a thorough discussion of these complex securities, the forces affecting their prices, their risks and the appropriate risk management practices, and as providing a methodology rather than a shopping list: examples and methodologies that can be applied quite universally once the basic concepts are understood.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-351 (SOA)|Exam CP-351]] — Chapter 5, Interest Rate Derivatives: Forwards and Swaps, and Chapter 6, Interest Rate Derivatives: Futures and Options; outcome 3f.
