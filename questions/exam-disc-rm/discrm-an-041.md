An insurer plans to expand into commercial auto insurance and faces four obstacles. Which is an internal constraint?

- A) A competitor has cut its commercial auto rates by 10%
- B) The state requires its rates and policy forms to be filed
- C) Rising interest rates are slowing truck sales in its region
- D) It has no underwriters experienced in commercial auto

## Explanation

Internal constraints come from the insurer itself: its capital and surplus, its people and their expertise, its systems, distribution and reputation. A lack of experienced underwriters limits the lines it can serve well. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Competition is an external constraint that limits the price the insurer can charge.
- **B** — Regulation of rates and forms is an external constraint.
- **C** — Economic conditions such as interest rates are external constraints that change demand.
