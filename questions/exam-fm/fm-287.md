A loan with an annual nominal interest rate of 9%, convertible monthly, is repaid with 60 monthly payments. The first payment is 1000 and each succeeding payment is 2% less than the previous payment.

Calculate the outstanding loan balance immediately after the 40th payment is made.

- A) 6889
- B) 7289
- C) 7344
- D) 7407
- E) 7862

## Explanation

The outstanding loan balance at any time equals the present value, at that time, of the remaining payments.

Monthly rate $j = 0.09/12 = 0.0075$. Payment $t$ is $1000(0.98)^{t-1}$, so the payments still due after the 40th are payments 41 to 60: the first of them is $1000(0.98)^{40}$, paid one month after time 40, and each later one is 2% smaller.

With $v = 1/1.0075$, these 20 payments form a geometrically decreasing annuity-immediate whose discounted terms fall by the ratio $r = 0.98/1.0075$:

$$
\begin{align*}
B_{40} &= 1000(0.98)^{40} \sum_{k=1}^{20} (0.98)^{k-1} v^{k} \\
       &= 1000(0.98)^{40} \cdot \frac{v\left(1-r^{20}\right)}{1-r} \\
       &= 1000(0.98)^{40} \cdot \frac{1-(0.98/1.0075)^{20}}{0.0075+0.02}
\end{align*}
$$

The denominator is $1.0075 - 0.98 = 0.0275$: the interest rate plus the 2% rate of decrease.

$$
\begin{align*}
(0.98)^{40} &= 0.445700 \\
(0.98/1.0075)^{20} &= 0.5749372
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
B_{40} &= 1000(0.445700) \cdot \frac{1-0.5749372}{0.0275} \\
       &= 445.700 \times 15.45683 \\
       &= 6889.11 \approx 6889
\end{align*}
$$
