A paper of the International Actuarial Association, April 2015, produced by the Joint Own Risk Solvency Assessment (ORSA) Subcommittee of its Insurance Regulation Committee and Enterprise and Financial Risk Committee. It gives the members of an insurer's Executive Board insight into the value of the ORSA process, regardless of the ORSA requirements of a given regulatory environment, and sets out the expectations a Board should have of the information senior management communicates to it.
