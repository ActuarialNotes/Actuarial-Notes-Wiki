The proportion of cotton plants on a large farm infested with boll weevils is modeled by a continuous random variable with probability density function

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases}
20x^3(1-x), & \text{for } 0 \leq x \leq 1 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the standard deviation of the proportion of cotton plants on the farm that are infested with boll weevils.

- A) $0.032$
- B) $0.178$
- C) $0.476$
- D) $0.690$
- E) $0.959$

## Explanation

Compute the first two moments directly:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X] &= \int_0^1 20x^4(1-x)\,dx = 20\left(\frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{6}\right) \\
     &= 20\left(\frac{1}{30}\right) = \frac{2}{3}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X^2] &= \int_0^1 20x^5(1-x)\,dx = 20\left(\frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{7}\right) \\
       &= 20\left(\frac{1}{42}\right) = \frac{10}{21}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X) &= \frac{10}{21} - \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 \\
              &= \frac{30 - 28}{63} = \frac{2}{63} = 0.031746
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{SD}(X) &= \sqrt{0.031746} \\
             &= \mathbf{0.178}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (A), 0.032, is the *variance* rather than the standard deviation — the square root is the last step and the easiest to skip. This is a beta density with $\alpha = 4$, $\beta = 2$; the general formulas $\mu = \alpha/(\alpha+\beta) = 2/3$ and $\sigma^2 = \alpha\beta/[(\alpha+\beta)^2(\alpha+\beta+1)] = 8/(36 \cdot 7) = 2/63$ give the same result with no integration.
