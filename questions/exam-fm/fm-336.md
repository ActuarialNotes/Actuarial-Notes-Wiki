An actuary invests 1000 at the end of each year for 30 years. The investments will earn interest at a 4% annual effective interest rate and, at the end of each year, the interest will be reinvested at a 3% annual effective interest rate.

Calculate the accumulated value of the investment at the end of the 30-year period.

- A) 51,625
- B) 53,434
- C) 55,260
- D) 58,437
- E) 58,938

## Explanation

The principal accumulates: 30 deposits of 1000, total principal $= 30{,}000$.

Each deposit earns 4% simple interest per year (reinvested at 3%). Deposit at end of year $k$ earns $1000 \times 0.04 \times (30-k)$ total interest over its lifetime, but each year's interest is reinvested at 3%.

Using the formula for accumulated value with reinvestment:
$$
\begin{align*}
AV &= 30(1000) + 40(Is)_{\overline{29}|0.03} \\
   &= 30{,}000 + 40\cdot\frac{\ddot{s}_{\overline{29}|0.03}-29}{0.03}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
30{,}000 + 40\cdot\frac{46.5754-29}{0.03} &= 30{,}000 + 40(585.85) \\
                                          &= 30{,}000 + 23{,}433.9 \\
                                          &= 53{,}433.9 \approx 53{,}434
\end{align*}
$$
