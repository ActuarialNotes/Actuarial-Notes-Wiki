The **quarterly supervisory return** — the exhibits behind the core financial statements, filed every quarter. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objectives C1–C4. The syllabus assigns **pp. 40.74, 60.25, 60.80, 70.50, 70.60 and 80.15**.

## The six assigned pages

| Page | Exhibit | Why it is assigned |
|---|---|---|
| **40.74** | **Investment return** | The investment result, separate from the insurance result — the split [[IFRS 17]] enforces between the [[Insurance Service Result\|insurance service result]] and [[Insurance Finance Income or Expenses\|insurance finance income or expenses]] |
| **60.25** | **[[Insurance Service Result\|Insurance service result]]** | [[Insurance Revenue\|Insurance revenue]] less [[Insurance Service Expenses\|insurance service expenses]] less net expenses from reinsurance held — the IFRS 17 replacement for "underwriting income" |
| **60.80** | **[[Onerous Contract\|Onerous contracts]]** | The [[Loss Component\|loss component]] recognised in the period. A rising loss component is direct evidence of inadequate pricing |
| **70.50** | Reinsurance contracts held summary — **[[Registered Reinsurance\|registered]] reinsurance** | |
| **70.60** | Reinsurance contracts held summary — **[[Unregistered Reinsurance\|unregistered]] reinsurance** | The registered/unregistered split drives capital: unregistered cessions earn [[MCT\|MCT]] credit only to the extent they are collateralised, and count toward the exposure limit in [[OSFI Concentration]] |
| **80.15** | **Commissions** | |

## The full return

**Consolidated exhibits** — general information (10.41); investments (40.08 summary; 40.72 interest revenue on financial assets not at FVTPL; **40.74 investment return**; 40.80 other loans and other invested assets; 40.84 equity-accounted investees; 40.88 summary of provisions; 40.92 provisions, accruals and other liabilities); miscellaneous assets and liabilities (50.32 intra-group transactions; 50.40 receivable from/payable to); insurance results and onerous contracts (**60.25**, **60.80**); provincial and territorial summaries of insurance revenue, insurance service expenses, net expenses from reinsurance held and insurance service result (67.40–67.70); reinsurance ceded (**70.50**, **70.60**); commissions and expenses (**80.15**, 80.25 insurance service and other operating expenses, 80.35 other income).

**Non-consolidated financial statements and exhibits** — assets and liabilities (92.10, 92.11), the four liability roll-forwards (92.12–92.18), statement of profit or loss (92.22), reserves (92.40), comprehensive income (92.42), changes in equity (92.54), insurance service result (93.14), and the provincial and territorial exhibits of premiums written, insurance revenue, insurance service expenses, net reinsurance expenses and insurance service result (93.30, 94.10–94.40).

## Why the provincial split exists

Canada regulates insurance provincially for market conduct and federally for solvency (see [[Federal-Provincial Jurisdiction]]). The provincial and territorial exhibits are how one federal return serves every provincial [[Superintendent of Insurance|Superintendent]] — and they are the source of the direct-written-premium figures [[PACICC]] uses to compute an assessment.
