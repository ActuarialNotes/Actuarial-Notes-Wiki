Chapter 10, The Taxation of Life Insurance Policies, of Canadian Insurance Taxation, fourth edition, by Jason Swales and Erdem Erinc, published by LexisNexis Canada. Its publisher describes the book as covering the tax rules that are unique to businesses in the insurance industry.
