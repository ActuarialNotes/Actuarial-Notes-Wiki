Which description fits an exploratory visualization rather than a presentation visualization?

- A) It has a title, annotations and labels so that a board can read it unaided
- B) It is designed once, for an audience without the analyst's background
- C) It is styled in the company's report template before anyone sees it
- D) It is made quickly for one of the analyst's own questions, then often discarded

## Explanation

An exploratory plot is made by the analyst for the analyst: they know which variables it will show before looking, make it for a purpose, look at it quickly and move on, and most of the many plots made in an analysis are thrown away. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — titles, annotations and labels that let a board read the plot unaided are the work done on a presentation graphic.
- **B** — designing for an audience without the analyst's background is the defining aim of a presentation graphic.
- **C** — styling to a report template is polish for an audience; an exploratory plot is looked at by the analyst and moved past.
