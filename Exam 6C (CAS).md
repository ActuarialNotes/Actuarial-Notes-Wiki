# Exam 6C

The **Canada Regulation and Financial Reporting (6C) Exam** is a 4 hour exam with mixed question types about Canadian insurance regulation, government insurance programs, financial reporting, and solvency.
## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- A. Regulation of Insurance and Canadian Insurance Law {20–25%}
> Candidates will be able to demonstrate an understanding of the role of the [[Insurance Industry|insurance business]] as a supplier of a [[Insurance Industry|vital service]]. They will be able to navigate Canadian [[Insurance Legislation|insurance legislation]], [[Insurance Regulation|regulations]], and [[Court Case|judicial decisions]] that affect [[Insurance Regulation|insurance regulation]] and [[Insurance Policy|insurance benefits]] to the extent that they interpret the law and thereby modify regulatory behavior.
> 1. Discuss the current state of [[Insurance Regulation|insurance regulation]] in Canada (e.g., [[Market Conduct Regulation|market conduct]], [[Federal-Provincial Jurisdiction|provincial vs federal jurisdiction]], [[Automobile Insurance Reform|motor vehicle injury compensation systems]], [[Rate Regulation|rate regulation]], [[Industry Self-Regulation|industry codes of conduct]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[OSFI]] · [[Superintendent of Insurance]] · [[Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario]] · [[Autorité des marchés financiers]] · [[Canadian Council of Insurance Regulators]] · [[Solvency Regulation]] · [[Risk Classification Restrictions]] · [[Territorial Rating]] · [[Unfair Discrimination]] · [[Bias in Actuarial Practice]] · [[Take-All-Comers Rule]] · [[Usage-Based Insurance]] · [[Compulsory Auto Insurance]] · [[No-Fault Insurance]] · [[Statutory Accident Benefits]] · [[Minor Injury Guideline]] · [[Catastrophic Impairment]] · [[Tort Threshold and Deductible]] · [[Direct Compensation Property Damage]] · [[Fault Determination Rules]] · [[Uninsured Automobile Coverage]] · [[Insurance Bureau of Canada]] · [[Code of Consumer Rights and Responsibilities]]
> 2. Discuss the [[Tort Litigation|litigation environment]], issues, outcomes, rationale, and implications of [[Court Case|court case]] decisions for the [[Insurance Industry|insurance industry]].
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Duty of Good Faith]] · [[Bad Faith Damages]] · [[Punitive Damages]] · [[Duty to Defend]] · [[Vicarious Liability]] · [[Limitation Period]] · [[Prejudgment Interest]] · [[Collateral Benefits]] · [[Cap on Non-Pecuniary Damages]]
> 3. Discuss [[Tort Litigation|trends]] in [[Tort Litigation|tort litigation]] (e.g., [[Tort Reform|tort reform]], [[Class Action|class action]] suits).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Structured Settlement]]
>
> **Readings:** Baer and Rendall · CFAI · CIA Bias · CIA CSOP (Ratemaking, Section 2600) · CIA Reliance · CIA Territories · Davidson · FSCO Coverages · FSCO Private Auto · FSCO Tech Notes · FSRA Risk Management · GAO · IBC Code of Conduct · KPMG PACICC · KPMG Regulatory Oversight · Landmark Legal · Marshall · McDonald · Ontario Reg. 664

> [!example]- B. Canadian Government and Industry Insurance Programs {10–15%}
> The candidates are expected to understand and apply the objectives, operations, and effectiveness of the following insurance programs: [[Agricultural Insurance]]; [[Employment Insurance|Employment insurance]]; [[Flood Insurance|Flood insurance]]; [[Guaranty Funds|Guaranty funds]] including the [[PACICC|Canadian Property and Casualty Insurance Compensation Corporation (“PACICC”)]]; [[Health Care Insurance|Health care insurance]]; [[Residual Market|Residual personal insurance markets]], e.g., auto, property; [[Workers Compensation Insurance|Workers compensation insurance]].
> 1. Describe the origin, purpose, and philosophy of specific [[Government and Industry Insurance Programs|government and insurance industry programs]] (e.g., [[Agricultural Insurance|Agricultural programs]], [[Facility Association|FARM]], [[Risk Sharing Pool|RSP]], [[Plan de Répartition des Risques|PRR]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Meredith Principles]] · [[Public Auto Insurance]] · [[Disaster Financial Assistance Arrangements]] · [[Social Insurance]]
> 2. Describe the operations, [[Risk Transfer|risk transfer process]], [[Government and Industry Insurance Programs|funding mechanisms]], and [[Guaranty Funds|sources of funding]] of [[Government and Industry Insurance Programs|government and insurance industry programs]] and their interactions with the [[Voluntary Private Insurance Market|voluntary private insurance sector]].
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Adverse Selection]] · [[Moral Hazard]]
> 3. Evaluate the effectiveness of a [[Government and Industry Insurance Programs|government and insurance industry program]] (e.g., [[Solvency|solvency]], [[Government Program Evaluation|efficiencies]], [[Government Program Evaluation|stability]], [[Government Program Evaluation|viability]] and [[Government Program Evaluation|long-term prospects]], effects of [[Government Program Evaluation|external factors]]).
>
> **Readings:** Agricultural Programs · Dutil · GOC Flood Risks · Government Insurers Study Note · Morneau Shepell · PACICC

> [!example]- C. Canadian Financial Reporting, Solvency, and Professional Responsibility {60–70%}
> Candidates will be able to demonstrate detailed knowledge of the contents, purposes, and recent changes in the [[Canadian Annual Return]], including recent [[OSFI|guidelines]] issued by the [[OSFI|Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI)]] and the [[Superintendent of Insurance|provincial regulatory authorities]]. Specifically, candidates are expected to understand and utilize the sections of the [[Canadian Annual Return]] related to [[Financial Statements|financial statements]] (such as the [[Financial Position|balance sheet]] and [[Net Income|income statement]]), [[MCT|capital statements]], insurance, and [[Reinsurance Accounting|reinsurance]].
>
> Candidates must be able to demonstrate a knowledge of [[Professional Responsibilities of the Actuary|professional responsibilities]] of the [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] related to the reporting of [[Financial Reporting|financial results]] by [[Insurer|property and casualty insurers]] in Canada. The candidate will understand and be able to apply the various [[Insurance Legislation|statutory requirements]] of the [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] under the [[Insurance Companies Act]] and the [[Insurance Legislation|Provincial Insurance Acts]] related to [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]] and general [[Corporate Governance|corporate governance]].
> 1. Describe the key elements of the [[Canadian Annual Return]] using standards that are current in accordance with [[IFRS 17]] (e.g., [[Financial Position|financial position]], [[Net Income|net income]], [[Excess (Deficiency) Ratio|excess (deficiency) ratio]], [[Statement of Changes in Equity|changes in equity]], [[Earthquake Reserves|earthquake reserves]], [[OSFI Annual Return|OSFI annual return]], [[Notes to Financial Statements|notes to financial statements]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Comprehensive Income]] · [[Quarterly Return]] · [[MSA Ratios]]
> 2. Explain and apply [[Reinsurance Accounting|reinsurance accounting]] concepts (e.g., [[Risk Transfer|risk transfer]], effect of different [[Types of Reinsurance|types of reinsurance]] on [[Financial Statements|financial statements]], [[Commutations|commutation]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Reinsurance Contracts Held]] · [[Registered Reinsurance]] · [[Unregistered Reinsurance]] · [[Finite Reinsurance]] · [[Reinsurance Contract Liabilities]]
> 3. Evaluate the [[Financial Health|financial health]] of an [[Insurer|insurance entity]] based on various [[Solvency Regulation|solvency frameworks]] (e.g., [[MCT]], [[Stress Testing]], [[Key Financial Measures|key financial measures]] used by [[Rating Agency|rating agencies]], [[Rules-Based Regulation|Rules-based]] and [[Principles-Based Regulation|principles-based]] [[Solvency Regulation|solvency regulation]] - [[ORSA]], [[FCT]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Capital Available]] · [[Capital Required]] · [[Base Solvency Buffer]] · [[Insurance Risk Margin]] · [[Market Risk Margin]] · [[Credit Risk Margin]] · [[Operational Risk Margin]] · [[Risk Margin]] · [[Diversification Credit]] · [[Earthquake Exposure Risk Margin]] · [[Probable Maximum Loss]] · [[Supervisory Target Capital Ratio]] · [[Internal Target Capital Ratio]] · [[Reverse Stress Testing]] · [[Ripple Effect]] · [[Risk Appetite]] · [[Concentration Risk]] · [[Climate Risk]] · [[Solvency II]] · [[Events Not in Data]]
> 4. Understand and apply [[Accepted Actuarial Practice|best practices]] and methodologies in the actuarial assessment of [[Insurance Contract Liabilities|insurance company liabilities]] and [[Capital Required|capital requirements]] for [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]] (e.g., [[Educational Note|CIA Educational Notes]], [[Insurance Regulation|regulatory requirements]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Liability for Remaining Coverage]] · [[Liability for Incurred Claims]] · [[Fulfilment Cash Flows]] · [[Risk Adjustment for Non-Financial Risk]] · [[IFRS 17 Discount Rates]] · [[Contractual Service Margin]] · [[General Measurement Model]] · [[Premium Allocation Approach]] · [[Contract Boundary]] · [[Level of Aggregation]] · [[Coverage Units]] · [[Onerous Contract]] · [[Loss Component]] · [[Insurance Acquisition Cash Flows]] · [[Insurance Revenue]] · [[Insurance Service Expenses]] · [[Insurance Service Result]] · [[Insurance Finance Income or Expenses]] · [[Other Comprehensive Income Option]] · [[Transition to IFRS 17]] · [[Margin for Adverse Deviations]] · [[Materiality]] · [[Subsequent Events]] · [[Model Risk]] · [[Runoff]]
> 5. Explain [[Standards of Practice|standards of practice]] and [[Insurance Legislation|legal requirements]] for an [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] and actuarial work in Canada (e.g., [[Statement of Actuarial Opinion]], Contents of [[Appointed Actuary's Report]], [[Standards of Practice]], [[Insurance Companies Act]], [[Actuary and Auditor Relationship|Actuary and Auditor relationship]]).
>    - *Key concepts:* [[Professional Responsibilities of the Actuary]] · [[Insurance Regulator]] · [[Duty to Report]] · [[Peer Review]] · [[External Auditor]]
>
> **Readings:** CAS Financial Reporting · CCIR Instructions · CIA Appointed Actuary · CIA CSOP · CIA Discount Rates · CIA Duration · CIA FCT 1 · CIA FCT 2 · CIA IFRS 1 · CIA IFRS 17 - Comparison · CIA IFRS 17 - LRC · CIA IFRS 2 · CIA Materiality · CIA Models · CIA PAA · CIA Runoff · CIA Subsequent Events · CIA Valuation · Feldblum · Freihaut and Vendetti · IAA Climate · IBC IFRS 17 - Metrics Discussion · ICA · IFOA · KPMG PACICC · MSA Legend · OSFI AA · OSFI Annual Return · OSFI Climate · OSFI Concentration · OSFI Core Return · OSFI Corporate Governance · OSFI Earthquake · OSFI MCT · OSFI Memorandum · OSFI Minimum Capital · OSFI ORSA · OSFI Quarterly Return · OSFI Reinsurance · OSFI Stress Testing · OSFI Target Capital
