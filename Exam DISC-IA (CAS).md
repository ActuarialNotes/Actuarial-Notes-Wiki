# DISC IA

**Insurance Accounting, Coverage Analysis, Insurance Law, and Insurance Regulation (DISC IA)** is one of the three *Data and Insurance Series Courses* (DISCs) the CAS requires for the [[Associate of the Casualty Actuarial Society (ACAS)|ACAS]]. The DISCs are online, self-paced courses run by The Institutes. Each ends in an exam of 75 application-based multiple-choice questions, with 100 minutes to answer them, and the course fee includes one attempt. The course itself contains the learning objectives and all the material needed to meet them; a textbook with the same content, condensed review notes, a course guide of sample questions and answers, and flashcards are sold separately and are optional. It replaced CAS Online Course 2 in 2023.

The objectives below are the course syllabus The Institutes publishes: sixteen numbered topics, each broken into sections that the syllabus letters a, b, c. It gives the topics no exam weights.
