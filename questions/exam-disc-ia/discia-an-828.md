A shopping-mall owner's contract with a cleaning company requires that (1) the cleaner's CGL respond to claims against the mall owner first, without seeking contribution from the mall owner's own liability policy, and (2) the cleaner's insurer give up any right to recover from the mall owner what it pays. The cleaner's CGL already has the mall owner as an additional insured. Which two ISO endorsements meet these requirements?

- A) CG 20 37 (completed operations additional insured) and CG 25 03 (designated construction project aggregate)
- B) CG 20 10 (ongoing operations additional insured) and CG 20 37 (completed operations additional insured)
- C) CG 25 03 (designated construction project aggregate) and CG 24 04 (waiver of transfer of rights of recovery)
- D) CG 20 01 (primary and noncontributory) and CG 24 04 (waiver of transfer of rights of recovery)

## Explanation

- **Requirement 1** is primary and noncontributory: CG 20 01 makes the policy primary to, and not seek contribution from, other insurance under which the additional insured is a named insured, when the named insured agreed to this in a written contract. Without it, the CGL's own other insurance condition could have the policies share.
- **Requirement 2** is a waiver of subrogation: CG 24 04 waives the insurer's right of recovery against the scheduled party for payments arising out of the named insured's ongoing operations or work done under a contract with it.

The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** and **B** add insureds (already done) or change aggregates; neither sets the order of payment or waives subrogation.
- **C** includes the waiver but not the primary and noncontributory endorsement; a per-project aggregate does not change which policy pays first.
