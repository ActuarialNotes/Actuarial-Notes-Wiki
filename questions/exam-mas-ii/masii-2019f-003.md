An actuary is constructing a credibility formula to apply to claim severity. To visualize the partial credibility, a plot showing credibility as a function of claim counts is created.

![Plot of credibility Z against claim counts from 0 to 1000. The curve rises steeply from the origin and flattens out, passing through the labelled points (200, 0.65) and (1000, 0.90) without ever reaching 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ActuarialNotes/Actuarial-Notes-Wiki/main/Media/Attachments/masii-2019f-q03-credibility-curve.png)

Determine which credibility method the actuary is using.

- A) Limited-fluctuation credibility with a full-credibility standard of 1300 claims.
- B) Limited-fluctuation credibility with a full-credibility standard of 800 claims.
- C) Limited-fluctuation credibility with $\lambda_F = 541$ and $C_X = 1.053$.
- D) Bühlmann credibility with EVPV = 0.266 and VHM = 0.016.
- E) Bühlmann credibility with EVPV = 0.844 and VHM = 0.008.

## Explanation

Test each candidate against the two labelled points, $Z(200) = 0.65$ and $Z(1000) = 0.90$.

**The limited-fluctuation options (A, B, C)** all use the square-root rule $Z = \sqrt{n/\lambda_F}$. Solve for the standard implied by the first point:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.65 &= \sqrt{\frac{200}{\lambda_F}} \\
\lambda_F &= \frac{200}{0.65^2} = 473
\end{align*}
$$

But a standard of 473 claims would make $Z = 1$ well before 1,000 claims, and the plotted curve is still below 1 at $n = 1000$. No square-root curve can pass through both points, which eliminates A, B and C. (Checking directly: a standard of 1,300 gives $Z(200) = 0.39$; a standard of 800 gives $Z(200) = 0.50$ and $Z(1000) = 1$; option C's severity standard $\lambda_F C_X^2 = 541(1.053^2) = 600$ gives $Z(200) = 0.58$ and $Z(1000) = 1$.)

**The Bühlmann options (D, E)** use $Z = n/(n+k)$ with $k = \text{EVPV}/\text{VHM}$ — a curve that approaches 1 asymptotically without reaching it, which matches the shape shown.

Option D: $k = 0.266/0.016 = 16.6$, so

$$
Z(200) = \frac{200}{216.6} = 0.92 \neq 0.65.
$$

Option E: $k = 0.844/0.008 = 105.5$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
Z(200) &= \frac{200}{200 + 105.5} = 0.655 \\
Z(1000) &= \frac{1000}{1000 + 105.5} = 0.905
\end{align*}
$$

Both labelled points are reproduced, so the answer is **E**.
