A McKinsey Financial Services Practice article of July 2025 arguing that, amid mounting margin pressure, asset management firms must transform technology from a cost driver into a true enabler of scalable productivity. It says the potential impact of AI, gen AI and agentic AI for an average asset manager could be equivalent to 25 to 40 percent of its cost base.
