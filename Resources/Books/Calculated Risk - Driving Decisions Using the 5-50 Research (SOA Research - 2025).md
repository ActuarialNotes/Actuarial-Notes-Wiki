A January 2025 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, sponsored by the Health Care Cost Trends Strategic Research Program Steering Committee, that introduces total risk analysis (TRA). TRA builds on existing projection techniques and risk models to help a decision-maker answer questions such as the chance of losing more than $1 million on a decision, and the report applies it to setting a health care claims budget. It also presents cost distributions consistent with the "5/50" principle, that 50% of all health care costs are associated with 5% of the population.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-101 (SOA)|Exam GH-101]] — Outcomes 3e (Excel Model: Calculated Risk); Sections 1 (background only), 2 and 3 of the report, with its companion Excel model.

## Abstract
- Total risk analysis (TRA) is designed to help the decision-maker answer key questions at the time the decision is made, and builds on existing projection techniques and risk models in a manner intended to reflect all possible scenarios.
- The paper focuses on setting a budget for claims in the upcoming year, and presents quantitative research on health care cost distributions that give the information needed to project the random variation risk.
- The 5/50 ratio holds for the United States population as a whole, but the concentration percentage, the 50%, varies by population.
