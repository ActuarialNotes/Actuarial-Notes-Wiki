A reference on budgeting edited by William R. Lalli, in its sixth edition (January 2012, 864 pages). Wiley describes it as covering budgeting for exempt organizations and nonprofits in light of the IRS's newly issued Form 990, manufacturing CFOs' budgeting needs, current technology solutions, and updated information on value-based budgets, for controllers, budget directors and CFOs, from the initial planning process through forecasting to specific industry budgets.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-311 (SOA)|Exam CP-311]] — Ch. 2 Strategic Balanced Scorecard-based Budgeting & Performance Management (2a, 2b); Ch. 29 Budgeting: Key to Corporate Performance Management, excluding pp. 667-676 (2a-2c, 2e); Ch. 30 Zero-Based Budgeting (2a-2c, 2e).
