You are given the following information about a 20-year bond with face amount 7500:

  i) The bond has an annual coupon rate of 7.4% paid semiannually.
  ii) The purchase price results in an annual nominal yield rate to the investor of 5.3% convertible semiannually.
  iii) The amount for amortization of premium in the fourth coupon payment is 28.31.

Calculate the redemption value of the bond.

- A) 7660
- B) 7733
- C) 7795
- D) 7879
- E) 7953

## Explanation

Let $C$ = redemption value. Semiannual coupon = $7500(0.037) = 277.5$. Semiannual yield = $0.0265$.

The amortization of premium at coupon $k$ is $(Fr - Cj)v^{n-k+1}$, where $j = 0.0265$ and $n = 40$.

For the 4th coupon ($k = 4$), use the difference in book values directly:

$$
\begin{align*}
BV_3 &= 277.5\, a_{\overline{37}|0.0265} + C(1.0265)^{-37} \\
&= 6493.05 + 0.379943C
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
BV_4 &= 277.5\, a_{\overline{36}|0.0265} + C(1.0265)^{-36} \\
&= 6387.61 + 0.390012C
\end{align*}
$$

The amortization of premium at coupon 4 equals $BV_3 - BV_4$:

$$
\begin{align*}
(6493.05 + 0.379943C) - (6387.61 + 0.390012C) &= 28.31 \\
105.44 - 0.010069C &= 28.31 \\
C &= (105.44 - 28.31)/0.010069 \\
&= 7{,}660
\end{align*}
$$
