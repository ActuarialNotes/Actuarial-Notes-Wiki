A CFA Institute Investment Series textbook that Wiley describes as providing the most up-to-date detail for the successful assessment of company performance and financial position regardless of country of origin. It covers all aspects from financial reporting mechanics and standards to understanding income statements and balance sheets, with guidance toward effective analysis techniques. This is the 4th edition, of 1,008 pages, published in February 2020.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CFE-201 (SOA)|Exam CFE-201]] — Ch. 6 Financial Analysis Techniques (2a); Ch. 9 Income Taxes (2c); Ch. 11 Financial Reporting Quality (2a, 2b); Ch. 15 Multinational Operations (2c).
