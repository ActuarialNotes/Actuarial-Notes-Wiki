The second edition (August 2006) of Aswath Damodaran's book on valuation, completely revised and updated. Wiley describes it as the book for CEOs and corporate strategists who need to understand the valuation process, giving an understanding of today's valuation models and the acumen needed for the most complex and subtle valuation scenarios.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-311 (SOA)|Exam CP-311]] — Ch. 13 The Value of Control (2d, 2f); Ch. 15 The Value of Synergy (2d, 2f).
