A Swiss Re publication introducing reinsurance to newcomers and to people who deal with reinsurers in their daily work. Its foreword says it describes the essence of reinsurance, its basic forms and the principal functions of a reinsurance company, draws attention to the regulatory environment in which reinsurers operate and stresses the importance of risk management, and shows the role reinsurance plays in the stability of the economy and society.
