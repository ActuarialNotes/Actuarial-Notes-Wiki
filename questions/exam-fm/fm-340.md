A 15-year bond with semiannual coupons is purchased for 2895.28. The bond is redeemable for 1000. The first coupon payment is equal to $R$ and each subsequent coupon is 1% larger than the previous coupon payment. The annual nominal yield rate on this bond is 6.2% convertible semiannually.

Calculate $R$.

- A) 110
- B) 114
- C) 122
- D) 128
- E) 132

## Explanation

The semiannual yield is $j = 6.2\%/2 = 3.1\%$, and there are 30 coupons, the $k$-th equal to $R(1.01)^{k-1}$. With $v = 1/1.031$, the price is the present value of the coupons plus the redemption value:

$$2895.28 = Rv + R(1.01)v^2 + R(1.01)^2v^3 + \dots + R(1.01)^{29}v^{30} + 1000v^{30}$$

The coupons form a geometrically increasing annuity-immediate with growth rate $g = 1\%$:

$$
\begin{align*}
2895.28 &= R\cdot\frac{1-(1.01/1.031)^{30}}{0.031-0.01} + 1000(1.031)^{-30} \\
        &= R\cdot\frac{1-0.539363}{0.021} + 400.17 \\
        &= 21.9351R + 400.17
\end{align*}
$$

So $R = (2895.28 - 400.17)/21.9351 = 2495.11/21.9351 = 113.75 \approx 114$.
