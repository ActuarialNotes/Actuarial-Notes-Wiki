A July 2009 report to the Chairman of the House Committee on Education and Labor. It asks what key risks U.S. workers face in accumulating and preserving pension benefits, what approaches other countries use to address them and with what trade-offs, and what approaches key U.S. proposals for alternative plan designs suggest and what trade-offs they entail. It draws on research, interviews, and a microsimulation of strategies to increase pension coverage.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — RET101-112-25, pp. 1-35; Outcomes 2a, 2d. The SOA course strategy guide says it discusses risks faced by participants in general, how the retirement systems of the Netherlands, Switzerland and the United Kingdom might address some of them, and how certain proposals for modifying the U.S. system might address them.

## U.S. Workers Face a Number of Risks in Accumulating and Preserving Pension Benefits
- Workers Face Risks Accumulating Pension Benefits Related to Coverage, Contributions, and Investment Returns
    - Coverage
    - Contributions

- Workers Face Risks Preserving Pension Benefits Related to Portability, Leakage, Fees, and the Drawdown of Benefits in Retirement

    - Administrative and Investment Fees
    - Drawdown of Benefits during Retirement

## Other Countries’ Experiences Offer Alternative Approaches for Addressing Risks Faced by U.S. Workers but also Involve Trade-offs
- Some Level of Mandate Is Effective in Achieving Increased Rates of Coverage or Contributions
    - Coverage
    - Contributions
- Indexation of Benefits and Guaranteeing Returns Mitigate Investment Risk for Individual Workers
- The Netherlands’, Switzerland’s, and the United Kingdom’s Pension Systems Facilitate Benefit Portability in the Workforce and Limit Preretirement Benefit Leakage
- Widespread Use of Lifetime Annuities and Limits on Lump-Sum Payments Reduce Risks in Drawing-down Benefits during Retirement

## Domestic Proposals Incorporate Different Approaches to Reduce Retirement Risks to Workers but also Pose Trade-offs
- Key Proposals in the U.S. Use Voluntary and Mandatory Approaches to Reduce a Broad Range of Risks
    - Super Simple Saving Plan
    - A New Benefit Platform for Life Security
    - Universal 401(k) Plan
    - Guaranteed Retirement Accounts Plan
    - Annuitization Proposals
- Proposals also Pose Trade-offs for Workers and Employers
    - Mandates
    - Guarantees
    - Life Cycle Funds
    - Annuities
    - Other Trade-offs
- Options to Expand Coverage Substantially Increase Projected Benefits
