![[INV101-100-25 - Chapters 3 and 4 of Alternative Investments - A Primer for Investment Professionals - Cover.svg]]

An overview of alternative investments for an institutional asset allocator or overseer of a portfolio containing both traditional and alternative assets, building on the reader's knowledge of investing in general and of traditional asset allocation in particular. It examines the four broadest and largest categories of alternative investments: hedge funds, private equity, real assets and structured products. The book emphasizes the financial economics of alternative assets in a well-diversified institutional portfolio. The SOA supplies Chapters 3 and 4 as study note INV101-100-25.
