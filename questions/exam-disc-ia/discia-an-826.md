A contractor's claims-made CGL (ISO CG 00 02) runs from January 1 to December 31, 2025, with a retroactive date of January 1, 2024, and is not renewed. No supplemental extended reporting period is bought. Which claim is covered?

- A) Bodily injury in June 2023; claim first made in March 2025
- B) Bodily injury in October 2025, never reported to the insurer; claim first made on February 10, 2026
- C) Bodily injury in November 2025, never reported to the insurer; claim first made in May 2026
- D) Bodily injury in January 2026; claim first made in February 2026

## Explanation

A claims-made CGL covers a claim first made during the policy period or an extended reporting period, for injury that occurs on or after the retroactive date and before the policy ends. When the policy is not renewed, the basic extended reporting period applies automatically: claims from occurrences reported within 60 days after the policy ends are covered if made within five years, and claims from unreported occurrences are covered if made within 60 days after the policy ends (here, by March 1, 2026). Claim B was made 41 days after the policy ended, for injury during the policy period. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — the injury occurred before the retroactive date.
- **C** — the occurrence was never reported and the claim came more than 60 days after the policy ended. Only a supplemental extended reporting period would have covered it.
- **D** — the injury occurred after the policy period; no extended reporting period covers injury after the policy ends.
