A study note, CP321-113-25, custom written by the Society of Actuaries for the CP 321 syllabus. According to the course strategy guide, it describes actuarial valuation methods, assumptions, benefit cost and the determination of financial entries for non-pension benefits, with references to the corresponding paragraphs of IAS 19, and is meant to be read before the IAS 19 standard itself.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Outcomes 6a-6g; the whole note.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> The SOA supplies this study note, and its syllabus states that study notes are not available electronically. No copy could be found online, and no source names its author or lists its sections. This page records only the citation and assignment from the syllabus and the course strategy guide.
