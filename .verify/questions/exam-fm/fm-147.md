## [F-001] Note on (C) says neither technique assumes a yield-curve shape; the syllabus development assumes a flat yield curve
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, line 44
- claim: 'C. False. Neither technique assumes any particular shape of the yield curve.'
- evidence: The Alps study note (required FM reading, §1, PDF p.3, page image) states: 'The developments in this note are also predicated on a flat yield curve, that is to say that cash flows at all future times are discounted to the present using the same interest rate.' Finan §55 (PDF p.480) lists among Redington immunization's limitations '(c) yield curve is assumed to change with the change in i' (a single rate i), and §56 (PDF p.486) defines full immunization as PVA(i+ε) > PVL(i+ε) at a single rate i. Both techniques are developed under a flat, single-rate curve shifted in parallel; (C) is false because it names an upward-sloping curve, not because no shape is assumed. SOA's solution (solutions PDF p.40) explains only (B). Key B unaffected.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: Replace the reason for (C) with one stating that Redington and full immunization are developed under a flat yield curve (one rate i, shifted in parallel), not an upward-sloping one. Needs an author.
