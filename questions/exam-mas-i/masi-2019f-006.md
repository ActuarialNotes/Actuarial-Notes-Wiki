You are given a system which consists of the following minimal cut sets:

$$
\{1\}, \{2,3\}, \{4\}, \{5,6\}
$$

The system is comprised of independent and identically distributed components, each with reliability 0.9.

Calculate the lower bound of the reliability of the system by using the first two inclusion-exclusion bounds from the method of inclusion and exclusion.

- A) Less than 0.75
- B) At least 0.75, but less than 0.77
- C) At least 0.77, but less than 0.79
- D) At least 0.79, but less than 0.81
- E) At least 0.81

## Explanation

With minimal cut sets, the system fails exactly when every component of at least one cut set fails. Let $F_i$ be the event that all components of cut set $C_i$ fail, so

$$
1 - r = \Pr\left(\bigcup_i F_i\right).
$$

The inclusion-exclusion bounds on this union alternate: the first bound, $\Pr(\bigcup F_i) \le \sum_i \Pr(F_i)$, is an upper bound on the failure probability and therefore gives the **lower** bound on reliability; the second (subtracting the pairwise terms) gives the upper bound.

Each component fails with probability $q = 0.1$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\sum_i \Pr(F_i) &= q + q^2 + q + q^2 \\
&= 0.1 + 0.01 + 0.1 + 0.01 \\
&= 0.22
\end{align*}
$$

so

$$
r \ge 1 - 0.22 = 0.78.
$$

(The second bound adds back the pairwise intersections, $0.001 + 0.01 + 0.001 + 0.001 + 0.0001 + 0.001 = 0.0141$, giving $r \le 0.7941$; the exact reliability is $0.9^2 \times 0.99^2 = 0.7939$.)

The lower bound falls in $[0.77, 0.79)$, so the answer is **C**.
