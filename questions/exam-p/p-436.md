An insurance policy has been purchased for a windmill farm. The policy will pay to compensate for the loss of revenue resulting from certain weather hazards that shut down the farm. Each such loss is exponentially distributed with standard deviation 1000.

Calculate the probability that a random loss exceeds 1500 given that it exceeds the mean.

- A) $0.22$
- B) $0.39$
- C) $0.50$
- D) $0.61$
- E) $0.78$

## Explanation

For an exponential distribution the standard deviation equals the mean, so the mean is also 1000 and the conditioning event is $X > 1000$.

The exponential distribution is **memoryless**: given that a loss has already exceeded 1000, the excess over 1000 is exponential with the same mean 1000. Exceeding 1500 then just means the excess exceeds 500:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 1500 \mid X > 1000] &= P[X > 500] \\
                          &= e^{-500/1000} \\
                          &= e^{-0.5} \\
                          &= \mathbf{0.6065}
\end{align*}
$$

Written out the long way, $P[X>1500]/P[X>1000] = e^{-1.5}/e^{-1} = e^{-0.5}$ — the same cancellation.

Choice (A), 0.22, is the unconditional $P[X > 1500] = e^{-1.5}$. For an exponential distribution the mean sits at roughly the 63rd percentile ($1 - e^{-1} = 0.632$), not the 50th, so the conditioning event $X > 1000$ has probability $e^{-1} = 0.368$, not one half.
