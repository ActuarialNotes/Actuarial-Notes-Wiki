In the context of the dynamic capital adequacy testing (DCAT) analysis, a property and casualty insurance company wants to evaluate the impact of a decline in the stock market. Based on an analysis of past common shares performance, the following distribution was established:

| Percentile | Decline of common shares portfolio |
|---|---|
| 80.0% | -10.0% |
| 90.0% | -20.0% |
| 99.0% | -40.0% |
| 99.5% | -50.0% |

The following additional information is available:

- The shock is tested at the end of 2018.
- Assume that there is no tax implication.
- Assume that the impact of the decline in common shares flows to the income statement.
- The MCT risk factor for equity risk is 30%.
- Assume that the MCT total uncapped operational risk margin is less than 30% of the sum of asset and insurance risk.
- Assume that there is no impact on investments other than common shares following the market decline.

The following information from the DCAT base scenario for 2017 and 2018 is available:

| | 2017 | 2018 |
|---|---|---|
| Common shares portfolio | 90,000 | 90,000 |
| Capital available | 100,000 | 100,000 |
| Capital required for insurance risk | 20,000 | 20,000 |
| Capital required for credit risk | 5,000 | 5,000 |
| Capital required for equity risk | 27,000 | 27,000 |
| Capital required for interest rate risk | 2,000 | 2,000 |
| Capital required for foreign exchange risk | 0 | 0 |
| Capital required for operational risk | 5,000 | 5,000 |
| Diversification credit | 6,714 | 6,714 |
| MCT ratio | 287% | 287% |

Evaluate the impact of a plausible adverse scenario on the 2018 MCT ratio.

### Explanation
DCAT tests a plausible adverse scenario at the 95th–99th percentile, so test the 40% decline in the common shares portfolio:

$$90{,}000 \times 0.4 = 36{,}000 \text{ decrease}$$

Capital available: $\text{CA} = 100{,}000 - 36{,}000 = 64{,}000$

Capital required (CR):

- Insurance risk unchanged $= 20{,}000$
- Credit risk unchanged $= 5{,}000$
- Equity risk $= 27{,}000 - 0.3 \times 36{,}000 = 16{,}200$
- Interest rate risk unchanged $= 2{,}000$
- Operational risk $= 5{,}000 - 0.085 \times (0.3 \times 36{,}000) = 4{,}082$

Asset risk $A = 5{,}000 + 16{,}200 + 2{,}000 = 23{,}200$; insurance risk $I = 20{,}000$.

$$\text{Div Cr} = 23{,}200 + 20{,}000 - [23{,}200^2 + 20{,}000^2 + 23{,}200 \times 20{,}000]^{0.5} = 5{,}754$$

$$\text{CR} = A + I + \text{op risk} - \text{Div Cr} = 41{,}528$$

$$\text{MCT} = \frac{64{,}000}{41{,}528 / 1.5} = 231\%$$

MCT impact $= 231\% - 287\% = -56\%$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to select an appropriate DCAT percentile scenario and adjust the components of the MCT accordingly.
Common errors include:

- Using an incorrect percentile scenario
- Not adjusting for operational risk
- Not adjusting for diversification credit
- Not dividing capital required by 1.5 before calculating the MCT ratio
