The General regulation under Ontario's Pension Benefits Act, R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 909, in the e-Laws consolidation from July 1, 2026 (last amendment O. Reg. 170/26). Part I covers interpretation, registration and amendments, jointly sponsored pension plans, funding of pension plans, reports, valuation, commuted value and portability of pension benefits, plan wind ups, grow-in benefits and the Pension Benefits Guarantee Fund; Part II covers pension fund requirements; its schedules set requirements for life income funds, locked-in retirement income funds and locked-in retirement accounts, and for letters of credit.

> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcomes 3a, 3b; Outcomes 4a, 4b (the syllabus codes it RET301-103-26, "R.R.O. 1990, Reg 909: General Regulations under Ontario Pension Benefits Act"; legislation in this note pertaining to JSPP & MEPP and historical funding relief is background only).
