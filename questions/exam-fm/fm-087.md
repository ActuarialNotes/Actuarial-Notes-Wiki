A company takes out a loan of 15,000,000 at an annual effective discount rate of 5.5%. You are given:

- i) The loan is to be repaid with $n$ annual payments of 1,200,000 plus a drop payment one year after the $n$th payment.
- ii) The first payment is due three years after the loan is taken out.

Calculate the amount of the drop payment.

- A) 79,100
- B) 176,000
- C) 321,300
- D) 959,500
- E) 1,180,300

## Explanation

The annual effective interest rate is:

$$
\begin{align*}
i &= (1-d)^{-1} - 1 \\
&= (1-0.055)^{-1} - 1 \\
&= 5.82\%
\end{align*}
$$

The options were built with $i$ rounded to 5.82%, so carry that rate. The balance at time 2 (one year before the first payment) is $15{,}000{,}000(1.0582)^2 = 16{,}796{,}808.60$.

Solve for $n$: $1{,}200{,}000 \cdot a_{\overline{n}|} = 16{,}796{,}808.60$ gives $n = 29.795$, so there are 29 full payments of 1,200,000 and the drop payment is at time $2 + 30 = 32$.

The drop payment $X$ satisfies:

$$
\begin{align*}
1{,}200{,}000 \cdot a_{\overline{29}|} + X(1.0582)^{-30} &= 16{,}796{,}808.60 \\
X &= (16{,}796{,}808.60 - 16{,}621{,}012.24)(5.45799) \\
&= 959{,}495
\end{align*}
$$

This is answer **D** (959,500). Carrying the unrounded $i = 0.055/0.945 = 0.0582011$ instead gives a drop payment of 960,724, and D is still by far the nearest option.
