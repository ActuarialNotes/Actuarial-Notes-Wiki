Basketball team Z has a 60% chance of winning any particular game. The team plays $n$ games this season, where $n > 1$, with the outcomes of these games being mutually independent. The probability that the team wins exactly three games this season is five times the probability that the team wins exactly two games this season.

Calculate $n$.

- A) $6$
- B) $8$
- C) $10$
- D) $12$
- E) $14$

## Explanation

The number of wins is binomial with $p = 0.6$. Write the two probabilities and set up the stated relationship:

$$
\binom{n}{3}(0.6)^3(0.4)^{n-3} = 5\binom{n}{2}(0.6)^2(0.4)^{n-2}
$$

Rather than expanding, divide one side by the other. Most factors cancel:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\binom{n}{3}}{\binom{n}{2}}\cdot\frac{0.6}{0.4} &= 5
\end{align*}
$$

The ratio of consecutive binomial coefficients is $\binom{n}{3}/\binom{n}{2} = (n-2)/3$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{n-2}{3}\left(\frac{3}{2}\right) &= 5 \\
\frac{n-2}{2} &= 5 \\
n &= \mathbf{12}
\end{align*}
$$

Cancelling before expanding is what keeps this tractable — written out, both sides carry an $(0.4)^{n}$ that would otherwise have to be tracked through. As a check, with $n = 12$ the two probabilities are 0.0125 and 0.0025, a ratio of exactly 5.
