![[OSFI Annual Return - Cover.svg]]

The uniform annual return, approved by the Canadian Council of Insurance Regulators, in which P&C insurers file their supervisory annual supplement. This is the 2023 form (PC3) for Canadian and foreign property and casualty insurers. Its table of contents groups the pages into general information; consolidated exhibits on investments, miscellaneous assets and liabilities, claims and insurance contracts, reinsurance ceded and out-of-Canada operations; non-consolidated claims exhibits; and the affidavits verifying the annual supplement return, and marks which pages a Canadian insurer files and which a foreign insurer files.

## 10.60 Summary of Selected Financial Data for five years
- Five years of consolidated data: operations (assets or assets vested in trust, liabilities, adjusted equity, premiums received for insurance contracts, [[Insurance Revenue|insurance revenue]], [[Insurance Service Expenses|insurance service expenses]]) and profitability ([[Insurance Service Result|insurance service result]], investment return, investment yield, [[Net Income|net income (loss)]], return on equity).
- The [[MCT|Minimum Capital Test]] / Branch Adequacy of Assets Test section reports the excess of capital (margin) available over capital (margin) required and the MCT/BAAT ratio.
- Canadian insurers also report dividends, share capital and contributed surplus paid in and redeemed, and other ratios: adjusted equity as a % of liabilities, and agents and brokers balances and amounts due from subsidiaries and associates, and claims development, each as a % of adjusted equity.
- Foreign insurers report their home office results in the currency of the home jurisdiction.
- Further lines ask for the average discount rate for liabilities for incurred claims, the average durations of bonds and of liabilities for remaining coverage and for incurred claims, and the number of automobiles insured at year-end.
- In the year of IFRS 17 adoption OSFI expects current and prior year comparative data, and the rest of the prior year data can be left blank.

## 60.45 Net Ultimate Claims and Adjustment Expenses - Claims Development
- A consolidated [[Development Triangle|development triangle]]: for all prior accident years and each accident year to the current one, the end-of-year [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate claims]] and adjustment expenses, net of reinsurance and undiscounted, as estimated at each year-end.
- Further columns give cumulative net claims and adjustment expenses paid, net claim liabilities and adjustment expenses, and the prior year development excess or (deficiency), column (09) less column (10).
- Below the total, the effect of discounting and the effect of the [[Risk Adjustment for Non-Financial Risk|risk adjustment for non-financial risk]] lead to net [[Liability for Incurred Claims|liabilities for incurred claims]], which are then shown excluding [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|ULAE]], "Facility Association" and "Plan", and other provisions.
