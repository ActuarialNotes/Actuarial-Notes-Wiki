A textbook for high-performing actuarial team members who are transitioning from technical roles to managerial positions. Its publisher says it aims to equip them with the skills to manage teams, projects and their professional development, and that it was produced to help readers build the skills they need to become confident and effective actuarial managers. Its author, a qualified actuary (FIAA) and credentialed coach, developed the Guardian Actuarial Leadership Program to help actuaries make that transition.
