A five-year 1000 face amount bond with 10% annual coupons is purchased at time of issue for 1216.47 based on an annual effective interest rate of 5%.

Calculate the Macaulay duration.

- A) 2.9
- B) 3.2
- C) 3.7
- D) 4.1
- E) 4.3

## Explanation

The bond pays coupons of $0.10(1000) = 100$ at the end of each of years 1 to 5 and its face amount of 1000 at time 5. The Macaulay duration at an interest rate of 5% is the present-value-weighted average of these payment times. The coupons contribute $100\,(Ia)_{\overline{5}|}$ to the numerator and the face amount contributes $5(1000)v^{5} = 5000v^{5}$; the denominator is the bond's price, 1216.47:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC} &= \frac{100\,(Ia)_{\overline{5}|} + 5000v^{5}}{100\,a_{\overline{5}|} + 1000v^{5}} \\
        &= \frac{100(12.56639) + 3917.631}{100(4.32948) + 783.526} \\
        &= \frac{1256.639 + 3917.631}{432.948 + 783.526} \\
        &= \frac{5174.270}{1216.474} \\
        &= 4.2535
\end{align*}
$$
