An article that reviews the current status of the market for catastrophic risk (CAT) bonds and other risk-linked securities. It describes these vehicles as financing mega-catastrophes and other losses by accessing capital markets directly, and reports that the CAT bond market grew steadily with record amounts of risk capital raised in 2005, 2006 and 2007. It appeared in Risk Management and Insurance Review, Vol. 11, No. 1, March 2008, pp. 23-47.
