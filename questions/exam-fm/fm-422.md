A home buyer borrows 225,000 and has two options to repay the loan, both at an annual nominal interest rate of $i$, convertible monthly:

- Option 1: Level payments of 1960 at the end of each month for 180 months.
- Option 2: Level payments of $X$ at the beginning of each month for 360 months.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 1415
- B) 1418
- C) 1422
- D) 1427
- E) 1435

## Explanation

Let $j = \dfrac{i}{12}$ be the monthly interest rate. Option 1 determines it: the loan is the present value of 180 payments of 1960 made at the end of each month.

$$225{,}000 = 1960\,a_{\overline{180}|j}$$

Solving with a calculator gives $j = 0.0054167$ (so $i = 12j = 6.5\%$). Option 2 repays the same loan with 360 payments of $X$ made at the beginning of each month:

$$
\begin{align*}
225{,}000 &= X\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{360}|0.0054167} \\
          &= 159.0671X \\
X &= 1414.50
\end{align*}
$$
