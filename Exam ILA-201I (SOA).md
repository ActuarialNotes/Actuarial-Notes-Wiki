# Exam ILA-201I
**ILA 201-I – Valuation and Advanced Product and Risk Management, International** is an exam of the Society of Actuaries' Fellowship (FSA) pathway. Its syllabus covers the Nov 2026/Mar 2027/Jul 2027 sittings.

The learning objectives below are transcribed from the Nov 2026/Mar 2027/Jul 2027 [syllabus](https://www.soa.org/globalassets/assets/files/edu/2026/ila-201i/2026-2027-ila-201i-syllabus.pdf) the SOA publishes, word for word; the SOA lists its outcomes a), b), c), numbered 1, 2, 3 here.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- International Financial Reporting Requirements {40–50%}
> The candidate will understand and apply [[Valuation Principle|valuation principles]] to [[Individual Life Insurance|individual life insurance]] and [[Annuity Product|annuity products]] issued by [[International Life Insurance Company|international life insurance companies]].
>
> 1. Describe the appropriate [[IFRS]] 17 accounting and [[Valuation Standard|valuation standards]] for [[Life Insurance|life insurance]] and [[Annuity Product|annuity products]]
> 2. Evaluate the appropriate [[IFRS]] 17 accounting and [[Valuation Standard|valuation standards]] for [[Life Insurance|life insurance]] and [[Annuity Product|annuity products]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - ILA201-600-25: International Actuarial Note 100: Application of IFRS 17 (Ch. 1, section A – Introduction to GMM only, Ch. 5, 7-9 & 16) (1a, 1b)
> - CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 Risk Adjustment for Non-Financial Risk for Life and Health Insurance Contracts, Jun 2022 (1a, 1b)
> - ILA201-601-25: The IFRS 17 Contractual Service Margin: A Life Insurance Perspective (Sections 2-4.8) (1a, 1b)
> - CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 Coverage Units for Life and Health Insurance Contracts, Dec 2022 (1a, 1b)
> - CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 Estimates of Future Cash Flows for Life and Health Insurance Contracts, Jun 2022 (1a, 1b)
> - CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 Discount Rates for Life and Health Insurance Contracts, Jun 2022 (1a, 1b)
> - IFRS 17: Reinsurance Contracts Held and Loss-Recovery Component, SOA Reinsurance News, Feb 2021 (1a, 1b)
> - IFRS 17 Spreadsheet Model (1a, 1b)
> - CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 Market Consistent Valuation of Financial Guarantees for Life and Health Insurance Contracts, Jun 2022 (1a, 1b)
> - CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 – Fair Value of Insurance Contracts, Jun 2022 (1a, 1b)
>     - Companion Excel Spreadsheet: Educational Note: IFRS 17 – Fair Value of Insurance Contracts
> - ILA201-602-25: OSFI B-3 Sound Reinsurance Practices and Procedures (1a, 1b)
> - ILA201-603-25: OSFI Guideline E15: Appointed Actuary – Legal Requirements, Qualifications and External Review, Aug 2023 (1a, 1b)

> [!example]- Capital Management {20–30%}
> The candidate will understand [[International Capital Requirement|international capital requirements]], the approaches and [[Tool|tools]] of [[Financial Capital|financial capital]] management for [[International Life Insurance Company|international life insurance companies]].
>
> 1. Explain and calculate [[Regulatory Capital|regulatory capital]] using various [[International Framework|international frameworks]]
> 2. Explain and evaluate the respective [[Perspective|perspectives]] of [[Regulator|regulators]], [[Investor|investors]], [[Policyholder|policyholders]] and [[Insurance Company|insurance company]] management regarding the role and [[Determination|determination]] of [[Capital|capital]]
> 3. Describe the purpose and application of [[Economic Capital|economic capital]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - Regulatory Capital Adequacy for Life Insurance Companies: A Comparison of Four Jurisdictions, SOA Research Institute, Jul 2023 (2a)
>     - Companion Excel Spreadsheet: Comparison of Jurisdictions Tool
> - ILA201-604-25: OSFI Guideline – Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test (LICAT), November 2024, Ch. 1-6 (excluding Sections 4.2-4.4) (2a)
> - ILA201-605-25: OSFI Guideline A-4 Internal Target Capital Ratio for Insurance Companies, Dec 2017 (2a)
> - A Multi-Stakeholder Approach to Capital Adequacy, Conning Research, Actuarial Practice Forum (2b)
> - Economic Capital for Life Insurance Companies, SOA Research Paper, Oct 2016 (only sections 2 & 6) (2c)

> [!example]- Management and Evaluation of Life Insurance Risks {10–15%}
> The candidate will understand various approaches to manage and evaluate [[Life Insurance Risk|life insurance risks]].
>
> 1. Analyze the impact of [[Risk Diversification|risk diversification]], including considerations for [[Modeling|modeling]] and [[Offset|offsets]] between [[Mortality|mortality]] and [[Longevity Risk|longevity risk]]
> 2. Understand the role and framework used by [[Regulator|regulators]] and [[Credit Rating Agency|credit rating agencies]] for evaluating [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance companies]]
> 3. Explain and understand the use and application of the [[Own Risk Solvency Assessment]] ([[ORSA]]) [[Report|report]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - ILA201-100-25: Diversification: Consideration on Modelling Aspects & Related Fungibility and Transferability, CRO Forum, Oct 2013 (pp. 1-18) (3a)
> - CIA Educational Note: Financial Condition Testing, Jan 2023 (Appendix A only) (3a, 3b)
> - Rating Agency Perspectives on Insurance Company Capital, SOA Research Institute, Aug 2023 (excluding Appendices) (3b)
> - ILA201-606-25: OSFI: Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (E-19) (3c)

> [!example]- Advanced Product Management {20–30%}
> The candidate will understand value [[Creation|creation]] and [[Inforce|inforce]] management techniques for [[Life|life]] and [[Annuity Product|annuity products]].
>
> 1. Describe and evaluate [[Fundamental Strategy|fundamental strategies]] for enhancing value and [[Profitability|profitability]] through [[Active In-Force|active in-force]] and [[Operational|operational]] management
> 2. Describe, evaluate, and apply the [[Economic Value Creation|economic value creation]] framework
> 3. Recommend and justify changes to [[Policyholder Dividend|policyholder dividends]]
> 4. Understand [[Corporate Taxation|corporate taxation]], [[Policyholder Taxation|policyholder taxation]] and calculate [[Investment Income Tax|investment income tax]]
> 5. Describe and apply the methods and [[Principle|principles]] of [[Embedded|embedded]] value for an [[Insurance Enterprise|insurance enterprise]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - ILA201-101-25: Life in-force Management: Improving Consumer Value and Long-Term Profitability (4a)
> - ILA201-102-25: Economics of Insurance: How Insurers Create Value for Shareholders, pp. 4-31 (4b)
> - Mechanics of Dividends, SOA Research Institute, Mar 2022 (4c)
> - Canadian Insurance Taxation, Swales, et. al., 4th Edition, 2015 (4d)
>     - Ch. 4: Income for Tax Purposes – General Rules
>     - Ch. 5: Investment Income
> - ILA201-607-25: IFRS 17 and Navigating Financial Condition Testing (4d)
> - ILA201-608-25: IFRS 17: Tax and the Contractual Service Margin (4d)
> - ILA201-609-25: Impact of 2017 Tax Changes on Life Insurance (4d)
> - Canadian Insurance Taxation, Swales, et. al., 4th Edition, 2015 (4d)
>     - Ch. 9: Investment Income Tax
> - Embedded Value: Practice and Theory, Actuarial Practice Forum, Mar 2009 (4e)
