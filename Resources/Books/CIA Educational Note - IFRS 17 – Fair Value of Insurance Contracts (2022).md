A CIA educational note that provides guidance on how to determine the fair value of insurance contracts within the scope of IFRS 17, with further guidance on performing a fair value measurement given in IFRS 13. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated June 30, 2022 (document 222088), it covers fair value approaches, market participants, the assumptions to consider in determining fair value, and other considerations, with numerical examples provided in an Excel spreadsheet that accompanies the note.

> - [[Exam ILA-201I (SOA)|Exam ILA-201I]] — International Financial Reporting Requirements, outcomes 1a and 1b; CIA Educational Note: IFRS 17 – Fair Value of Insurance Contracts, Jun 2022, with its companion Excel spreadsheet.
