A baseball-pitching machine is used for batting practice. The machine is out of adjustment such that every pitched baseball arrives at the batter's box between 0 and 2 feet higher than intended.

Let $X$ equal the difference, in feet, between the actual arrival height and the intended arrival height of a pitched baseball.

The density of $X$, $f(x)$, is proportional to $x$.

Calculate the 80th percentile for $X$.

- A) $0.40$
- B) $0.89$
- C) $1.26$
- D) $1.60$
- E) $1.79$

## Explanation

Write $f(x) = cx$ on $(0,2)$ and normalize:

$$
\begin{align*}
1 &= \int_0^2 cx\,dx = \frac{c(2)^2}{2} = 2c \\
c &= 0.5
\end{align*}
$$

Integrate to the 80th percentile $p$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.8 &= \int_0^p 0.5x\,dx \\
    &= 0.25p^2 \\
p^2 &= 3.2 \\
p   &= \sqrt{3.2} \\
    &= \mathbf{1.7889}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (D), 1.60, is $0.8(2)$ — the position 80% of the way along the interval, which would be the percentile only for a uniform density. Because the density increases linearly, probability piles up toward the right, so the distribution function $0.25x^2$ is *below* the uniform's $x/2$ everywhere inside the interval and every percentile sits further right than the uniform's.
