A single \$8,000,000 claim lands in a small book's experience period. What is the standard ratemaking treatment?

- A) Exclude the claim entirely and price off the remaining experience
- B) Cap it for the base analysis, then load separately for the excess layer
- C) Leave it in uncapped — it is a real loss the rates have to fund
- D) Spread it evenly across the accident years in the experience period
