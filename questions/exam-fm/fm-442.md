An asset earns an annual force of interest given by $\delta(t) = \dfrac{1}{20+t}$, where $t$ is the time in years since January 1, 2020. The equivalent annual effective rate of interest for the year 2024 is $i$.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 4.00%
- B) 4.08%
- C) 4.17%
- D) 4.35%
- E) 5.00%

## Explanation

The year 2024 runs from $t = 4$ to $t = 5$. The equivalent annual effective rate for that year comes from the accumulation factor over it:

$$
\begin{align*}
1 + i &= \exp\left(\int_{4}^{5} \frac{1}{20+t}\,dt\right) \\
      &= \exp\left[\left.\ln(20+t)\right|_{4}^{5}\right] \\
      &= \exp(\ln 25 - \ln 24) \\
      &= \frac{25}{24}
\end{align*}
$$

so

$$
\begin{align*}
i &= \frac{1}{24} \\
  &= 0.041667 \\
  &\approx 4.17\%
\end{align*}
$$
