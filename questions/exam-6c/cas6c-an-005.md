A homeowner insured by an Insurance Bureau of Canada (IBC) member company finishes a major renovation in March. Her policy renews in November. In June, a claim she makes on a different matter is denied.

Determine which of the following statements are consistent with the IBC's Code of Consumer Rights and Responsibilities.

- I. When it denies her claim, the insurer is expected to tell her why.
- II. If she is unhappy with how her complaint is handled, she may contact her provincial insurance regulator or the General Insurance OmbudService.
- III. She may wait until her November renewal to tell the insurer about the renovation.

- A) I only
- B) I and II only
- C) I and III only
- D) II and III only
- E) The answer is not given by (A), (B), (C), or (D)

## Explanation

The Code sets out consumers' rights (to be informed, to timely and transparent claims handling, to complaint resolution and to privacy) and their responsibilities (to understand their needs, provide accurate information, update that information, and report the facts).

- **I is true.** Under the *right to timely and transparent claims handling*, "if your claim is denied, you have the right to be informed why."
- **II is true.** Under the *right to complaint resolution*, the consumer can use the company's complaint resolution process and may also contact the provincial insurance regulator or the independent General Insurance OmbudService.
- **III is false.** Under the *responsibility to update your information*, the consumer must **promptly** inform the insurer, broker or agent of a change in circumstances, and the Code names renovations to the home as an example. Waiting for renewal is the misconception that information only matters when the policy is re-rated.

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — leaves out statement II. The Code points the consumer outside the insurer, to the regulator or the OmbudService.
- **C** and **D** — both include statement III, which reverses the consumer's duty to update information promptly.
- **E** — would need all three statements to be true, and III is false.
