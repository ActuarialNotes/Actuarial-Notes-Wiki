An insurance company sells flood and fire insurance. This year, the company's profit from selling flood insurance is normally distributed, and its profit from selling fire insurance is normally distributed with three times the mean and three times the standard deviation as from flood insurance.

The profits from the two types of insurance are independent. The probability that the company earns a positive profit from selling flood insurance this year is 0.67.

Calculate the probability that the insurance company earns an overall positive profit this year.

- A) $0.71$
- B) $0.73$
- C) $0.81$
- D) $0.92$
- E) $0.96$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the flood profit with mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$; then the fire profit $Y$ has mean $3\mu$ and standard deviation $3\sigma$.

The given probability fixes the ratio $\mu/\sigma$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.67 &= P[X > 0] = P\left[Z > \frac{0-\mu}{\sigma}\right] \\
\frac{0-\mu}{\sigma} &= -0.44 \\
\mu &= 0.44\sigma
\end{align*}
$$

For the total $S = X + Y$, means add and — because the two are independent — *variances* add:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[S] &= \mu + 3\mu = 4\mu \\
\text{Var}(S) &= \sigma^2 + (3\sigma)^2 = 10\sigma^2 \\
\text{SD}(S) &= \sqrt{10}\,\sigma
\end{align*}
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
P[S > 0] &= P\left[Z > \frac{-4\mu}{\sqrt{10}\,\sigma}\right] \\
         &= P\left[Z > \frac{-4(0.44)}{\sqrt{10}}\right] \\
         &= P[Z > -0.56] \\
         &= \mathbf{0.71}
\end{align*}
$$

The standard deviations do *not* add: $\sigma + 3\sigma = 4\sigma$ would make the $z$-score $-0.44$ and the answer 0.67 — no improvement at all. Adding in quadrature gives $\sqrt{10}\,\sigma \approx 3.16\sigma$, which is why combining the two lines improves the odds only modestly, from 0.67 to 0.71.
