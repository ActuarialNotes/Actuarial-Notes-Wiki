An in-depth primer on economic scenario generators (ESGs), published by the Society of Actuaries in July 2016. An ESG is a software tool that simulates future paths of economies and financial markets. The first half of the guide covers what ESGs are, how they evolved, how they meet regulatory and business needs in the insurance and pension industries, and what makes a good one, with guidance on model specification, calibration and validation; the second half gives the technical treatment of arbitrage-free modeling, risk-neutral scenarios, interest rate, corporate bond and equity index models, and international considerations.
