# RET Module 2
**RET Module 2 – Professional considerations in the retirement benefits environment** is one of the two modules of the retirement benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the module
>
> The primary audience for the FCIA modules is individuals who desire to be admitted as Fellows of the CIA.
>
> Individuals seeking continuing professional development credit can also register for the FCIA modules.
>
> Each FCIA candidate through Pathways 1 and 2 is required to complete both FCIA modules, while Pathway 3 candidates will have to complete the second module. It is anticipated that FCIA module candidates will have six months from the date of module purchase to complete each FCIA module. As a general guideline, candidates should allocate approximately 100 hours to complete all activities included in a module.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Canadian pension legislation
>
> 1. Describe federal and [[Provincial Pension Benefit Act|provincial pension benefit acts]] and regulations.
> 2. Understand the various [[Retirement Benefit Arrangement|retirement benefit arrangements]] available to a [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsor]].
> 3. Describe and apply applicable [[Agreements Respecting Multi-Jurisdictional Pension Plan|agreements respecting multi-jurisdictional pension plans]] between [[Provincial Regulator|provincial regulators]].
> 4. Recognize, define, interpret, and apply [[Minimum Standard|minimum standards]] of [[Registered Pension Plan|registered pension plans]].
> 5. Describe [[Funding|funding requirements]] under applicable [[Provincial Minimum Standard Legislation|provincial minimum standard legislation]].
> 6. Outline and understand the different [[Rate Regulation|filing requirements]] for [[Registered Pension Plan|registered pension plans]].

> [!example]- Pension plan projections
>
> 1. Understand various [[Assumption|assumptions]] needed for a forecast.
> 2. Understand the basics of [[Climate Science|climate science]] and its impact on the work of [[Actuary|actuaries]].
> 3. Understand the different approaches to [[Liability Forecasting|liability forecasting]].
> 4. Understand the different approaches to [[Asset Forecasting|asset forecasting]].
> 5. Describe the process to [[Conduct Climate|conduct climate]] scenario analysis for [[Pension Plan|pension plans]].
> 6. Design and construct [[Replication Portfolio|replication portfolios]] using available assets or products to mitigate [[Risk|risk]].
> 7. Evaluate various [[Governmental Rule|governmental rules]] and policies given a hypothetical regulatory framework.
> 8. Analyze the [[Investment Strategy|investment strategy]] of a provider of benefits, taking into account:
>    - a. any [[Asset-liability Matching|asset/liability matching]] requirements;
>    - b. the trade-off between [[Risk|risk]] and reward;
>    - c. an awareness of adding value to the shareholders of the business; and
>    - d. integration of ESG factors.
> 9. Describe how projection models may be used to develop [[Strategy|strategies]].
> 10. Evaluate risk-sharing and [[Liability Transfer Transaction|liability transfer transactions]].
> 11. Assess the components of [[Dynamic Rebalancing|dynamic rebalancing]].
> 12. Analyze a forecast study in the context of [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|actuarial standards of practice]] in Canada.

> [!example]- Pension plan de-risking
>
> 1. Understand and describe how [[Glide Path|glide paths]] are used as a de-risking tool.
> 2. Discuss how margins in assumptions can be used as a [[De-Risking|de-risking]] tool.
> 3. Discuss how change in [[Group Health Insurance|plan provisions]] can be used as a de-risking measure.
> 4. Consider, assess, and design [[Target-Benefit Pension Plan|target-benefit pension plans]] and [[Shared-Risk Pension Plan|shared-risk pension plans]].
> 5. Interpret, analyze, and apply jurisprudence and [[Legal Environment|legal environment]].
> 6. Understand the [[Process Relative|process relative]] to [[Annuity Purchase|annuity purchases]]:
>    - a. [[Annuities Buy-In|Annuities buy-ins]] versus buyouts.
>    - b. Legal and contribution requirements.
>    - c. Transfers in kind.
>    - d. [[Legal Contract Review|Legal contract reviews]].
>    - e. Coordination among [[Annuity Provider|annuity providers]].
>    - f. Conversion from [[Annuity Buy-In|annuity buy-in]] to buyout.
>    - g. Discharge of liabilities under applicable legislation.
> 7. Describe how Assuris and [[PBGF Protection|PBGF protection]] can be optimized.
> 8. Consider the impact of [[Ancillary Provision|ancillary provisions]] (such as indexation, [[Survivor Benefit|survivor benefits]], etc.).
> 9. Understand legislative changes to [[Plan Provisions Allowing Annuity Purchase|plan provisions allowing annuity purchases]].
> 10. Outline the issues arising from the discontinuance of [[Benefit Provision|benefit provision]], including:
>     - a. the rights and expectations of beneficiaries;
>     - b. the availability and selection of a method of provision of [[Discontinuance Benefit|discontinuance benefits]]; and
>     - c. the level of available assets.

> [!example]- Reporting, valuation, and management of benefit arrangements
>
> 1. Compare and contrast the objectives of [[Funding|funding]], [[Solvency|solvency]], hypothetical [[Plan Termination|wind-up]] and accounting actuarial [[Valuation|valuations]].
> 2. Describe the issues that arise from the transfer or amendment of [[Benefit Right|benefit rights]]; for example, following significant [[Corporate Activity|corporate activity]] (such as an acquisition or [[Plan Merger and Spin-Off|plan merger]]), including:
>    - a. the [[Interest|interests]] and responsibilities of the parties involved;
>    - b. the terms set out in the [[Legal Documentation|legal documentation]];
>    - c. [[Financial Aspect|financial aspects]], such as the calculation of the [[Transfer Value|transfer values]] and benefits provided; and
>    - d. related accounting implications.
> 3. Determine an appropriate method and appropriate financial and demographic assumptions for a [[Valuation|valuation]] of a [[Pension Arrangement|pension arrangement]] and [[Placing Value|placing values]] on benefits and contributions.
> 4. Describe how [[Climate-Related Risk|climate-related risks]] could impact the [[Valuation|valuation]] assumptions for liabilities and [[Normal Cost|normal cost]] of [[Pension Plan|pension plans]].

> [!example]- Communication and organizational behaviour
>
> 1. Explain differences between effective and [[Poor Communication|poor communication]] techniques and their implications.
> 2. Apply techniques to [[Structure Ideas|structure ideas]] logically.
> 3. Develop clear, [[Fact-Based Message|fact-based messages]] that can be communicated persuasively.
> 4. Explain the role of [[Cognitive Bias|cognitive biases]] on [[Making Suboptimal Individual|making suboptimal individual]] decisions.
> 5. Evaluate the role of organizational behavior on [[Organizational Decision-Making Process|organizational decision-making processes]] and efficacy.
> 6. Explain why communication is strategically important to [[Organization|organizations]].
> 7. Describe how information is communicated within [[Organization|organizations]].
> 8. Describe organizational and [[Individual Barrier|individual barriers]] to [[Effective Communication|effective communication]].
> 9. Identify the [[Risk|risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 10. Explain how to [[Overcome Communication Barrier|overcome communication barriers]] and [[Minimize Risk|minimize risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 11. Recognize the influence of culture and diversity on [[Communication|communication]].
> 12. Apply the [[CIA Standard|CIA Standards]] of Practice and Rules of [[Professional Conduct]] in [[Business Communication|business communication]].
