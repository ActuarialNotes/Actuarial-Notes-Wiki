With regard to the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' publication "Educational Note: Discounting":

## Part a (0.5 points)

Explain the difference between "present value" and "actuarial present value."

### Explanation
1. **Present value** – the sum of expected future payments after recognizing the time value of money
2. **Actuarial present value** – the sum of the present value and the provision for adverse deviations

Alternatively:

- **Present value:** the estimated cash flows of assets and liabilities discounted at the discount rate.
- **Actuarial present value:** the present value and also accounting for the provision for adverse deviations for claims development, reinsurance collectible and investment return rates.

### Examiner Report
Some candidates had trouble defining the terms in part a) which should be basic actuarially knowledge.

## Part b (1 point)

Identify four considerations in the determination of return rate on assets underlying the discounting of policy liabilities.

### Explanation
1. The method of valuing assets and reporting investment income
2. The allocation of those assets and that income among lines of business
3. The return on the assets at the balance sheet date
4. The yield on assets acquired after the balance sheet date
5. The capital gains and losses on assets sold after the balance sheet date
6. Investment expenses and losses from default (C1 risk)

Alternatively:

1. Method of valuing the assets and reporting investment income.
2. Return on assets at the balance sheet date.
3. Yield of assets after the balance sheet date.
4. Allocation of assets among lines of business.

### Examiner Report
A number of candidates were confused by part b) of the question and it was therefore, not answered well.
