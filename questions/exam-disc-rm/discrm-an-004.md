Under the CAS's enterprise risk management framework, a risk committee has built a probability distribution for each of its material risks. It now combines them in a single simulation model that reflects their correlations, so that it can express the results as an effect on the company's key performance indicators. Which step of the process is the committee carrying out?

- A) Analyze and quantify risks
- B) Assess and prioritize risks
- C) Monitor and review
- D) Integrate risks

## Explanation

The CAS framework's **integrate risks** step aggregates all the risk distributions, reflecting correlations and portfolio effects, and expresses the result as the aggregate risk profile's effect on key performance indicators. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Analyzing and quantifying builds the distribution for each risk, which the committee has already done; it does not combine them.
- **B** — Assessing and prioritizing comes after integration: it measures each risk's contribution to the aggregate profile and ranks the risks for treatment.
- **C** — Monitoring and review gauges the risk environment and how well the chosen strategies are performing once they are in place.
