The number of calls received by a certain emergency unit in a day is modeled by a Poisson distribution with a standard deviation of 2.

Calculate the probability that on a particular day the unit receives at least two calls.

- A) $0.092$
- B) $0.147$
- C) $0.238$
- D) $0.762$
- E) $0.908$

## Explanation

A Poisson distribution has its mean and variance both equal to $\lambda$. The standard deviation is given, not the mean, so square it first:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X) &= \sigma^2 = 2^2 = 4 \\
\lambda        &= 4
\end{align*}
$$

The probability mass function is therefore $p(x) = e^{-4}4^x / x!$ for $x = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$

"At least two" is easiest as the complement of the two smallest counts:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X \geq 2] &= 1 - p(0) - p(1) \\
            &= 1 - e^{-4} - 4e^{-4} \\
            &= 1 - 5e^{-4} \\
            &= 1 - 5(0.018316) \\
            &= 1 - 0.091578 \\
            &= \mathbf{0.908}
\end{align*}
$$

The whole question turns on the first line: the 2 given is a standard deviation, so $\lambda = 4$, not 2. Choice (A), $0.092$, is $5e^{-4} = P[X \leq 1]$ — the correct computation reported as its own complement.
