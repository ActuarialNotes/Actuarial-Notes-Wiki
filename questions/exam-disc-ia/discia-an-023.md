A physician's negligent treatment injures a patient in November 2025, and the patient first reports a claim to the physician's insurer in February 2026. Under SSAP No. 55, in which year does the insurer recognize the loss if the policy is written on an occurrence basis, and if it is written on a claims-made basis?

- A) Occurrence: 2025; claims-made: 2026
- B) Occurrence: 2026; claims-made: 2026
- C) Occurrence: 2025; claims-made: 2025
- D) Occurrence: 2026; claims-made: 2025

## Explanation

Losses are recognized as expenses when the covered or insured event occurs. In most cases that is the occurrence of the incident, so under an occurrence policy the loss belongs to 2025, carried as IBNR until reported. For a claims-made policy the covered event is the reporting of the incident to the insurer, which happened in 2026. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — recognizes the occurrence-basis loss only when reported; an unreported loss that has occurred is IBNR, not unrecognized.
- **C** — treats the claims-made policy like an occurrence policy.
- **D** — reverses the two triggers.
