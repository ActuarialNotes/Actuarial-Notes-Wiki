A five-page Sun Life article, marked for advisor use only, on how the Income Tax Act's exempt test for life insurance policies changes for policies issued as of January 1, 2017. The exempt test compares the savings component of the actual policy with that of a theoretical benchmark policy, the exempt test policy, to separate exempt, protection-focused policies from investment-accumulation, non-exempt ones. The article is copyright Sun Life Assurance Company of Canada, 2015.

## Grandfathering of Life Insurance Policies Issued Prior to January 1, 2017
- Loss of grandfathering occurs when a policy issued before January 1, 2017 is converted to another type of policy on or after that date, or when coverage that requires medical underwriting is added on or after January 1, 2017.
