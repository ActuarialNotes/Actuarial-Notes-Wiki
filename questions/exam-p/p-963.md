Let $X$ follow a uniform distribution on $(0, 1)$, and define $Y = -2\ln X$.

Calculate $P(Y > 3)$.

- A) $0.135$
- B) $0.223$
- C) $0.368$
- D) $0.607$
- E) $0.777$

## Explanation

Use the CDF method: write the event in terms of $X$, then evaluate with $F_X$. For $y > 0$:

$$
\begin{align*}
F_Y(y) &= P(-2\ln X \leq y) \\
       &= P\!\left(\ln X \geq -\frac{y}{2}\right) \\
       &= P\!\left(X \geq e^{-y/2}\right) \\
       &= 1 - e^{-y/2}
\end{align*}
$$

(The inequality **flips** when dividing by $-2$ — the step most often missed.) That CDF is exponential with mean 2, so:

$$
\begin{align*}
P(Y > 3) &= e^{-3/2} \\
         &= \mathbf{0.223}
\end{align*}
$$

This transformation is the inverse-CDF method in action: applying $F^{-1}$ to a uniform variable generates a sample from any target distribution, which is how simulations produce exponential waiting times.
