A student would like to estimate the upper bound of a uniform distribution, $U(0, \theta)$ using the method of maximum likelihood.

The true value of $\theta$ is 10.

$N$ is the minimum sample size required such that the absolute value of the bias of the estimator is less than 0.1.

Calculate $N$.

- A) Less than 20
- B) At least 20, but less than 40
- C) At least 40, but less than 60
- D) At least 60, but less than 80
- E) At least 80

## Explanation

For $U(0, \theta)$ the likelihood $\theta^{-n}$ is decreasing in $\theta$ but requires $\theta \ge$ every observation, so the MLE is the sample maximum $X_{(n)}$. Its distribution is $\Pr(X_{(n)} \le x) = (x/\theta)^n$, which gives

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X_{(n)}] &= \int_0^\theta x \cdot \frac{n x^{n-1}}{\theta^n}\,dx \\
&= \frac{n}{n+1}\theta
\end{align*}
$$

The estimator is biased low:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Bias} &= \frac{n}{n+1}\theta - \theta \\
&= -\frac{\theta}{n+1}
\end{align*}
$$

With $\theta = 10$ we need

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{10}{n+1} &< 0.1 \\
n + 1 &> 100 \\
n &> 99
\end{align*}
$$

At $n = 99$ the bias is exactly $0.1$, not less, so the minimum sample size is $N = 100$.

This is at least $80$, so the answer is **E**.
