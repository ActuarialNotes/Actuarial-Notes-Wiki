## [F-001] Thousands commas inside inline math render with a thin space ('12, 000')
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, line 47
- claim: '$12,000 - 10,000 = 2,000$' and '$162,000 - 110,000 = 52,000$' use bare thousands commas inside math.
- evidence: KaTeX treats ',' in math as punctuation (mpunct) and adds a thin space after it; quiz/src/lib/vaultMath.ts only moves delimiters and does not touch commas. The vault's own convention elsewhere is '{,}'. Cosmetic; no value changes.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Write thousands separators in math as '{,}'.
- applied: true
