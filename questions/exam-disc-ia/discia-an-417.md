A fire on the residence premises of an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) damages several items. Which is covered under Section I?

- A) A detached cottage on the premises that the insured rents to a student
- B) The insured's registered pickup truck, parked inside the attached garage
- C) A detached shed from which the insured runs a small engine repair business
- D) A suitcase belonging to a houseguest staying for the weekend

## Explanation

Coverage C covers personal property owned or used by an insured, and, at the named insured's request after a loss, property of a guest while it is in a residence an insured occupies. The guest's suitcase qualifies. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Coverage B excludes other structures rented or held for rental to anyone who isn't a tenant of the dwelling, unless used solely as a private garage.
- **B** — "motor vehicles" are property not covered; the truck belongs on the auto policy's physical damage coverage.
- **C** — Coverage B excludes other structures from which a business is conducted.
