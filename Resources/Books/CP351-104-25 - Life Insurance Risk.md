The first of two Milliman reports, dated November 2023, on how life insurers are responding to the fast-changing economic environment, based on conversations with life insurers in the UK, European, U.S. and Bermudian markets. Paper 1 asks whether insurers' risk inventories and taxonomies captured the risks their business was exposed to, how they identify risks, and how they have recalibrated their quantification of risk exposures, including through stress and scenario testing.
