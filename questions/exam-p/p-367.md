A study is to be conducted on health risk factors of insurance applicants. The study needs exactly 268 people with heart disease, 268 with diabetes and 268 with high cholesterol. The study also needs exactly 68 people with only heart disease, 68 with only diabetes and 68 with only high cholesterol. The study needs exactly 84 people with all three risk factors and 155 people with no risk factors.

Calculate the total number of people the study needs.

- A) $443$
- B) $462$
- C) $617$
- D) $636$
- E) $791$

## Explanation

Draw the three-circle Venn diagram. Five of the seven regions inside the circles are given: the three "only" regions are 68 each and the centre is 84. What remains are the three **exactly-two** regions. Name them by the factor each one excludes:

- $X$ = heart and diabetes only
- $Y$ = heart and cholesterol only
- $Z$ = diabetes and cholesterol only

Each factor's total of 268 is its "only" region, plus the centre, plus the two pairwise regions that touch it:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{heart:}       \quad 68 + 84 + X + Y &= 268 \\
\text{diabetes:}    \quad 68 + 84 + X + Z &= 268 \\
\text{cholesterol:} \quad 68 + 84 + Y + Z &= 268
\end{align*}
$$

Each equation gives the same pair sum, $116$. Subtracting any two shows the three unknowns are equal, so $X = Y = Z = 58$.

Now add every region of the diagram, including the 155 outside all three circles:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Total} &= \underbrace{68 + 68 + 68}_{\text{only one}} + \underbrace{58 + 58 + 58}_{\text{exactly two}} + \underbrace{84}_{\text{all three}} + \underbrace{155}_{\text{none}} \\
             &= 204 + 174 + 84 + 155 \\
             &= \mathbf{617}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (B), 462, is the count inside the circles with the 155 unaffected people left out. Choice (E) comes from a botched inclusion-exclusion: $3(268) - 2(84) = 636$ subtracts the triple overlap but never the pairwise ones, and adding 155 to that gives 791. Working region by region avoids the issue entirely, because the seven Venn regions are disjoint and can simply be added.
