After a fire, the insured and the insurer agree the loss is covered but disagree by \$90,000 on the amount. The insured demands an appraisal in writing under the BPP. Which statement about the appraisal is correct?

- A) Each party pays its own appraiser, and they share the umpire's cost equally
- B) The insurer pays all appraisal costs, because the insured demanded it in writing
- C) The appraisers' decision binds only if both appraisers and the umpire all agree
- D) Once an appraisal is demanded, the insurer gives up any right to deny the claim

## Explanation

Under the appraisal condition, each party selects a competent and impartial appraiser, the two select an umpire, and a decision agreed to by any two is binding. Each party pays its chosen appraiser and bears the other expenses of the appraisal and the umpire equally. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Costs are split as above whoever makes the demand.
- **C** — Agreement by any two of the three is binding; unanimity is not required.
- **D** — The condition expressly says the insurer retains its right to deny the claim; appraisal decides only value and amount of loss.
