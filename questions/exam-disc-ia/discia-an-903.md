A Maryland resident is injured in Maryland by a hit-and-run driver who is never identified. She has no insurance of her own that applies, and no other insurance recovery is available. Which source may pay her claim?

- A) The state's insurance guaranty association, which pays claims of uninsured drivers
- B) The Uninsured Division of the Maryland Automobile Insurance Fund
- C) The state's automobile insurance plan, which assigns her claim to an insurer in rotation
- D) No source, because a claim requires an identified at-fault driver

## Explanation

Maryland's Automobile Insurance Fund has an Uninsured Division that pays claims of Maryland residents involved in Maryland accidents with uninsured or unidentifiable motorists, or hit-and-run incidents, when no other insurance recovery is available. It is funded by uninsured motorist fines, investment income and collections, and may recover what it pays from an uninsured at-fault party. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A guaranty association pays claims against insolvent insurers, not claims against uninsured drivers.
- **C** — An automobile insurance plan places high-risk *applicants* with insurers so they can buy coverage; it doesn't pay accident victims.
- **D** — Hit-and-run and unidentified drivers are exactly the cases the fund, and uninsured motorists coverage, are designed for.
