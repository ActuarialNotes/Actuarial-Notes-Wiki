The time $X$, in years, from when a claim is reported until it is settled has distribution function

$$
F(x) = \begin{cases}
0, & x < 0 \\
\dfrac{x^2}{16}, & 0 \le x \le 4 \\
1, & x > 4
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the expected time to settlement.

- A) 1.33
- B) 2.67
- C) 2.83
- D) 3.00
- E) 4.00

## Explanation

$F$ is continuous with no jumps, so $X$ is a continuous random variable and its density is the derivative of the distribution function:

$$
\begin{align*}
f(x) &= F'(x) \\
     &= \frac{x}{8}, \qquad 0 < x < 4
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X] &= \int_0^4 x \cdot \frac{x}{8}\,dx \\
     &= \left[\frac{x^3}{24}\right]_0^4 \\
     &= \frac{64}{24} \\
     &= \mathbf{2.67}
\end{align*}
$$

The survival shortcut gives the same value without finding $f$: $E[X] = \int_0^4 [1 - F(x)]\,dx = 4 - \tfrac{64}{48} = 2.67$. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** (1.33) integrates the distribution function itself, $\int_0^4 F(x)\,dx$, instead of the survival function $S(x) = 1 - F(x)$.
- **C** (2.83) is the median, $\sqrt{8}$, where $F(x) = 0.5$, not the mean.
- **D** (3.00) treats $F$ as if it were a density: rescaling $x^2/16$ to integrate to 1 gives the density $g(x) = 3x^2/64$, whose mean is 3.
- **E** (4.00) is $\int_0^4 x\,F(x)\,dx$, again putting $F$ where $f$ belongs.
