The unallocated loss adjustment expense factors GISA applies to Ontario private passenger automobile (excluding farmers) experience in its 2025 Actual Loss Ratio Exhibit, valued as of December 31, 2025. GISA derives each as one plus the ratio of calendar-year internal loss adjustment expenses to calendar-year direct incurred claims, and notes that IFRS 17 and changes to insurers' expense allocation make the ratios before and after IFRS 17 differ. Its implied age-to-ultimate development factors are on pages 10–11 and aren't transcribed here yet.
