![[The Volatility Smile (Derman - 2016) - Cover.svg]]

A Wiley Finance book, written with contributions by David Park, that presents a unified treatment of the Black-Scholes-Merton model and the more advanced models that have replaced it, and is also a book about the principles of financial valuation and how to apply them. Its first half, Chapters 1 through 13, can serve as a standalone textbook on option valuation and the Black-Scholes-Merton model; the second half focuses on the behavior of the volatility smile.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam INV-201 (SOA)|Exam INV-201]] — Chapter 8 (The Smile: Stylized Facts and Their Interpretation) and Chapter 10 (A Survey of Smile Models) for outcome 2j; Chapter 3 (Static and Dynamic Replication) for outcomes 3c and 3e; Chapter 5 (The P&L of Hedged Option Strategies in a Black-Scholes-Merton World) for outcomes 3c and 3d; Chapter 6 (The Effect of Discrete Hedging on P&L) and Chapter 7 (The Effect of Transaction Costs on P&L) for outcomes 3c and 3e.
