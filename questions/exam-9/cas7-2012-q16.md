## Part a (1 point)

Explain what the chi ($\chi$) and lambda ($\lambda$) variables measure.

### Explanation
$$\chi_i = \frac{H_i - F_i G_i}{\sqrt{F_i(1 - F_i)\,G_i(1 - G_i)}}$$

$\chi$ measures how far the joint distribution is from the independent distribution: $F_i$ and $G_i$ are the marginal distributions and $H_i$ is the joint distribution, so the numerator is zero under independence.

$$\lambda_i = 4 \times \text{sign}\left(\tilde{F}_i \tilde{G}_i\right) \times \max\left(\tilde{F}_i^2, \tilde{G}_i^2\right), \qquad \tilde{F}_i = F_i - \tfrac{1}{2}, \quad \tilde{G}_i = G_i - \tfrac{1}{2}$$

$\lambda$ measures where the point lies in the distribution: $-1$ indicates the top left or bottom right, $1$ the top right or bottom left, and $0$ near the midpoint.

Alternatively:

$\chi$ measures the difference between the value of the empirical copula at a specific point ($H$) and the null hypothesis of independence of the two distributions ($F \cdot G$); the denominator $\sqrt{F(1-F)G(1-G)}$ is a scaling factor that keeps it between -1 and 1. $\lambda$ is the signed distance between the point and the point of the bivariate distribution with the median coordinates, where $F = G = 0.5$.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Based on the $\chi$-plot, draw a conclusion regarding the auto physical damage book of business.

### Explanation
If the bulk of the observations are near the $\chi = 0$ line, the two series are independent. Based on this chart, losses and ALAE on the auto physical damage book are independent.

Alternatively:

There appear to be more positive $\chi$ values than negative ones. If they were randomly scattered around 0 you could say that indemnity and ALAE were independent, but with more positives than negatives the empirical relationship shows a little more dependence than none. The dependence does not appear to vary along the distribution, though; it appears steady.

### Examiner Report
A few candidates defined lambda as the distance from the mean of the distribution rather than the median.  Also, some candidates failed to illustrate the signed nature of lambda.

A few candidates defined chi as a residual and while mathematically correct, this definition does not provide any insights into how chi measures dependency.

A few candidates did not adequately explain their conclusions from the graph.
