![[Fundamentals of Machine Learning for Predictive Data Analytics (Kelleher - 2020) - Cover.svg]]

An introductory textbook that, in the publisher's words, offers a detailed and focused treatment of the most important machine learning approaches used in predictive data analytics, covering both theoretical concepts and practical applications. Technical and mathematical material is augmented with worked examples, and two case studies show the application of these models in the broader business context. The second edition adds a chapter on deep learning and two chapters that go beyond predictive analytics to cover unsupervised learning and reinforcement learning.
