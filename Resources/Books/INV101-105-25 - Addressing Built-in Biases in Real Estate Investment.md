A Fidelity International study of behavioural finance and its application to real estate, written for investment professionals. It explores five of the most entrenched biases in the institutional commercial real estate market (framing, anchoring, loss aversion, home bias and herding), outlines their consequences and proposes practical workarounds to minimise their effect. Each bias is introduced with what it is, how it applies to real estate investing, and the workarounds.
