Let $X$ be a continuous random variable with density function

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{|x|}{10}, & \text{for } -2 < x < 4 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate $\text{Var}(X)$.

- A) $0.25$
- B) $2.52$
- C) $3.32$
- D) $4.13$
- E) $4.93$

## Explanation

The absolute value forces the integrals to split at $x = 0$, where $|x|$ changes form. Confirm the density is valid first:

$$
\int_{-2}^{0}\frac{-x}{10}dx + \int_0^4\frac{x}{10}dx = \frac{4}{20} + \frac{16}{20} = 1
$$

**First moment:**

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X] &= \int_{-2}^{0}\frac{-x^2}{10}dx + \int_0^4\frac{x^2}{10}dx \\
     &= -\frac{8}{30} + \frac{64}{30} \\
     &= \frac{56}{30} = \frac{28}{15}
\end{align*}
$$

**Second moment.** Here $x^2|x|$ is positive on both sides, so the pieces add rather than cancel:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X^2] &= \int_{-2}^{0}\frac{-x^3}{10}dx + \int_0^4\frac{x^3}{10}dx \\
       &= \frac{16}{40} + \frac{256}{40} \\
       &= \frac{272}{40} = \frac{34}{5}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X) &= \frac{34}{5} - \left(\frac{28}{15}\right)^2 \\
              &= 6.8 - 3.4844 \\
              &= \mathbf{3.3156}
\end{align*}
$$

The sign is what makes the first moment small: the negative branch subtracts, leaving a mean of only 1.87 despite the range reaching 4. Choice (E), 4.93, is $6.8 - 56/30 = 6.8 - 1.8667$: subtracting the mean itself instead of its square.
