A perpetuity-due with annual payments is priced at $X$ based on an annual effective interest rate of 7%. The amount of the first payment is 350. Each payment, from the second through the thirtieth, is 3% larger than the previous payment. Starting with the 31st payment, each payment is equal to the 30th payment.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 7508
- B) 7855
- C) 7925
- D) 7971
- E) 8033

## Explanation

Break the perpetuity into two parts: the first 30 increasing payments, and the level perpetuity that follows. Payment $k$ is made at time $k-1$ (a perpetuity-due).

**Part 1: payments 1–30.** A 30-payment geometrically increasing annuity-due with first payment 350, growth 3% and interest 7%:

$$
\begin{align*}
PV_1 &= 350\left[\frac{1-\left(\frac{1.03}{1.07}\right)^{30}}{0.07-0.03}\right](1.07) \\
&= 374.5 \cdot \frac{1-0.3188625}{0.04} \\
&= 374.5(17.02844) \\
&= 6377.15
\end{align*}
$$

**Part 2: payments 31 onward.** Each equals the 30th payment, $350(1.03)^{29} = 824.80$. The 31st payment is at time 30, so these form a level perpetuity-immediate valued at time 29, which is then discounted 29 years:

$$
\begin{align*}
PV_2 &= v^{29}\left(\frac{1}{0.07}\right)350(1.03)^{29} \\
&= \frac{350}{0.07}\left(\frac{1.03}{1.07}\right)^{29} \\
&= 5000(0.3312455) \\
&= 1656.23
\end{align*}
$$

**Total.**

$$X = PV_1 + PV_2 = 6377.15 + 1656.23 = 8033.38$$

So $X = 8033$.
