A building experiences a power failure. The probability density function of the length (in days) of this power failure is

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{(4-x)^3}{64}, & \text{for } 0 < x < 4 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the median length (in days) of this power failure.

- A) $0.0$
- B) $4 - \sqrt[4]{128}$
- C) $0.8$
- D) $4 - \sqrt[3]{32}$
- E) $2.0$

## Explanation

The median $m$ is the value where the cumulative probability reaches one half. Integrate the density from 0 to $m$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.5 &= \int_0^m \frac{(4-x)^3}{64}\,dx \\
    &= -\frac{(4-x)^4}{256}\Bigg|_0^m \\
    &= \frac{1}{256}\left(256 - (4-m)^4\right) \\
    &= 1 - \frac{(4-m)^4}{256}
\end{align*}
$$

Solve for $m$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{(4-m)^4}{256} &= 0.5 \\
(4-m)^4 &= 128 \\
4 - m &= \sqrt[4]{128} \\
m &= \mathbf{4 - \sqrt[4]{128}}
\end{align*}
$$

Numerically that is $4 - 3.3636 = 0.636$ days. The density is largest near $x=0$ and falls to zero at $x=4$, so the median sitting well below the midpoint of the range is exactly what the shape predicts — choice (E), the midpoint 2.0, would be the median only for a symmetric density. Choice (D) comes from integrating as though the exponent were 2 rather than 3, leaving a cube root instead of a fourth root.
