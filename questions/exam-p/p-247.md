Random variables $X$ and $Y$ have joint distribution

| | $X = 0$ | $X = 1$ | $X = 2$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $Y = 0$ | 1/15 | $a$ | 2/15 |
| $Y = 1$ | $a$ | $b$ | $a$ |
| $Y = 2$ | 2/15 | $a$ | 1/15 |

Let $a$ be the value that minimizes the variance of $X$.

Calculate the variance of $Y$.

- A) 2/5
- B) 8/15
- C) 16/25
- D) 2/3
- E) 7/10

## Explanation

From the sum constraint: $4a+b=3/5$. The marginal $P(X=k)$ gives $E[X]=1$ (constant) and $\text{Var}(X)=2a+2/5$, minimized at $a=0$.

With $a=0$: $b=3/5$. Marginal of $Y$: $P(Y=0)=P(Y=2)=1/5$, $P(Y=1)=3/5$.

$E[Y]=1$, $E[Y^2]=7/5$, $\text{Var}(Y)=7/5-1=\mathbf{2/5}$
