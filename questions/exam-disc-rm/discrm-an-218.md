A garden center's net income plus continuing operating expenses is \$260,000 a month from March through June and \$90,000 in each other month. The underwriter judges that a total loss would take at most 4 months to restore, and that extra expense would be about \$50,000. Ignoring the waiting period, what is the probable maximum loss of business income?

- A) \$636,667
- B) \$920,000
- C) \$1,040,000
- D) \$1,090,000

## Explanation

A probable maximum loss uses the worst probable timing. The worst 4-month window is March through June:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{PML} &= 4 \times 260{,}000 + 50{,}000 \\
&= 1{,}040{,}000 + 50{,}000 \\
&= 1{,}090{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Uses an average month: $\frac{4(260{,}000) + 8(90{,}000)}{12} \times 4 + 50{,}000$. Averages hide the peak of a seasonal business.
- **B** — Uses a window that misses one peak month, April through July: three peak months, one off-peak month and the extra expense.
- **C** — Leaves out the extra expense the insured would incur to keep going.
