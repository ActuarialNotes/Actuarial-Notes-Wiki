A retailer with three stores of similar value is choosing between one blanket limit for all three and a specific limit for each store, with the same total amount of insurance. Which statement about the ISO BPP is correct?

- A) A blanket limit removes the coinsurance condition for all three stores
- B) A total loss at one store can draw on more than that store's share of the limit
- C) Specific limits let a loss at one store use the unused limits of the others
- D) Under a blanket limit, coinsurance is tested store by store against each value

## Explanation

A blanket limit is a single limit over more than one location, so a large loss at one store can draw on the whole amount. When one limit applies to two or more items, the BPP's coinsurance condition applies to the total value of all the property the limit covers. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — coinsurance still applies, tested on the total value; only an option such as agreed value suspends it.
- **C** — describes the blanket limit's advantage; a specific limit cannot borrow from another location's limit.
- **D** — the BPP tests a blanket limit against the total of all the property it covers, not store by store.
