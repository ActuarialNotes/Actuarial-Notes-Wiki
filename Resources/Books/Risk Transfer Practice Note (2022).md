![[Risk Transfer Practice Note (2022) - Cover.svg]]

A November 2022 public policy practice note developed by the American Academy of Actuaries' Committee on Property and Liability Financial Reporting. Its purpose is to provide information to actuaries on current practices in which their peers are engaged related to the determination of the existence of risk transfer within insurance and reinsurance contracts. It updates the Risk Transfer Testing Practice Note released in January 2007, and is not an actuarial standard of practice or binding upon any actuary.
