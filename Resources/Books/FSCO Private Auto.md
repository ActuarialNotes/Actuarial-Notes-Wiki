![[FSCO Private Auto - Cover.svg]]

The October 2016 guidelines governing a **major rate filing** for Ontario private passenger automobile insurance — the document that says what an insurer must put in front of the regulator to change its rates or [[Risk Classification Restrictions|risk classification system]]. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objective A1. The syllabus assigns **pp. 1–5 (excluding filing format), pp. 11–21 (from Section 3), and Appendix B2**. Now administered by the [[Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario|FSRA]], FSCO's successor.

## A. General information

- The rate and risk classification legislation and regulations behind the filing requirement, and the **prior approval** process for private passenger auto in Ontario — rates cannot be used until approved.
- Processes for approval and authorization; filing requirements; **affiliated insurers** (each files, but the relationship must be disclosed); required rates and risk classification system elements; the approval process itself.

## C. Guidelines for a major filing

| Section | What it requires |
|---|---|
| 1 | Table of contents |
| 2 | Summary of information |
| 3 | **Certificates** — of the officer/designate, and **of the actuary**. The actuary certifies the rates are not excessive, inadequate or [[Unfair Discrimination\|unfairly discriminatory]] and were prepared in accordance with [[Accepted Actuarial Practice\|accepted actuarial practice]] |
| 4 | **Actuarial support** — the substance of the filing |
| 5 | Discount/surcharge changes |
| 6 | Rating rule changes |

### Section 4 — the actuarial support

The overall rate level indication and the rate differential indications, item by item:

- **4.a** Overall description of the ratemaking methodology and summary
- **4.b** [[Loss Development|Losses]] — development, [[Loss Trend|trend]], [[Large Loss|large loss]] treatment, catastrophe provision, and reform adjustment factors
- **4.c / 4.d** [[Allocated Loss Adjustment Expense|ALAE]] and [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|ULAE]]
- **4.e** [[Premium]] — [[On-Leveling|on-level]] premium, rate group drift, finance fees, tax rates
- **4.f** Other expenses
- **4.g** [[Underwriting Profit|Underwriting profit provision]]
- **4.h** [[Credibility]]
- **4.i** Other adjustments
- **4.j** Summary [[Rate Level Change|rate level indications]]
- **4.k** [[Territorial Rating|Territorial]] indications and proposed differentials
- **4.l** Implementation of rate group differentials
- **4.m** Classification, limit of liability, [[Deductible|deductible]] and other differential indications
- **4.n** Rating based on group membership
- **4.o** Usage-based insurance pricing (UBIP) programs

## Why it is on the syllabus

This is [[Rate Regulation|rate regulation]] made concrete. The exam asks what an Ontario filing must contain and what the regulator will and will not accept; the technical support behind each of these line items is set out in [[FSCO Tech Notes]], which is the companion reading.
