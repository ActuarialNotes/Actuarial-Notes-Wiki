Compared with retaining a risk, which is a disadvantage of transferring it by insurance?

- A) The firm cannot keep operating after an insured loss occurs
- B) Premiums must be paid in advance instead of staying in the business
- C) Insurers provide no loss control or claims adjusting services
- D) Premiums cannot be deducted as a business expense for taxes

## Explanation

Under retention, the money that would have paid premiums can stay invested in the business until claims are paid. Insurance requires the premium **in advance**. The answer is **B**. Other disadvantages are the cost of the premium, the time spent negotiating coverage, and the risk that an insured takes less care over loss control.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — The reverse is true: being indemnified after a loss is what lets the firm keep operating, an advantage of insurance.
- **C** — Insurers do provide services such as loss control and claims adjusting; that is an advantage.
- **D** — Premiums are deductible as a business expense, another advantage.
