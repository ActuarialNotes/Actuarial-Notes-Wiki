Chapter 5 of US GAAP for Insurers, third edition, edited by Robert G. Frasca and Mark J. Freedman and published by the Society of Actuaries in 2024. The SOA describes the book as a comprehensive analysis of US Generally Accepted Accounting Principles, whose third-edition scope expands to cover property and casualty insurance, designed to assist actuaries, accountants and other professionals who help prepare and review insurance company financial statements submitted in accordance with GAAP.
