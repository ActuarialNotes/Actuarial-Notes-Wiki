The amount of beverage filled into each bottle by a bottling machine is independently and normally distributed with mean 12.0 ounces and standard deviation $\sigma$ ounces. Approximately 20% of all 24-bottle cases contain more than 290 ounces of total beverage.

Calculate $\sigma$.

- A) $0.01$
- B) $0.10$
- C) $0.49$
- D) $2.38$
- E) $10.10$

## Explanation

A case is the sum of 24 independent normal fills, so it is normal with

$$
\begin{align*}
E[S] &= 24(12.0) = 288 \\
\text{SD}(S) &= \sqrt{24}\,\sigma
\end{align*}
$$

Means multiply by 24, but the standard deviation grows only by $\sqrt{24}$.

The 20% upper tail puts 290 at the 80th percentile, where $z_{0.80} = 0.8416$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{290 - 288}{\sqrt{24}\,\sigma} &= 0.8416 \\
\sqrt{24}\,\sigma &= \frac{2}{0.8416} = 2.3765 \\
\sigma &= \frac{2.3765}{4.899} \\
       &= \mathbf{0.485}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (D), 2.38, is $\text{SD}(S)$ — the standard deviation of the whole case rather than of a single bottle. Dividing by $\sqrt{24}$ is the last step, and skipping it is the natural error here.
