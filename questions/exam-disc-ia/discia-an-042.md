A BPP policy insures Building 1 for \$150,000 and Building 2 for \$80,000, with a \$2,500 deductible and no coinsurance penalty. One windstorm causes a \$100,000 loss to Building 1 and a \$95,000 loss to Building 2. How much of the total \$195,000 loss does the insured bear?

- A) \$2,500
- B) \$15,000
- C) \$17,500
- D) \$20,000

## Explanation

The deductible is applied only once per occurrence, and the losses are not combined. It comes off Building 1, whose loss is below its limit plus the deductible; Building 2 is paid its limit.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Pay}_1 &= 100{,}000 - 2{,}500 \\
&= 97{,}500 \\
\text{Pay}_2 &= \min(95{,}000,\ 80{,}000) \\
&= 80{,}000 \\
\text{Insured bears} &= 195{,}000 - 177{,}500 \\
&= 17{,}500
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$2,500 counts only the deductible and forgets that Building 2's loss exceeds its limit.
- **B** — \$15,000 counts only the excess over Building 2's limit.
- **D** — \$20,000 subtracts the deductible from each building, but it applies once per occurrence.
