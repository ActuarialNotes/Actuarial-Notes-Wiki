A textbook, edited by Robert Eaton and Matt Morton, on the risks of long-term care (LTC) benefits in the United States in their many forms. ACTEX Learning describes its goal as improving the understanding of LTC risks among practitioners, in the hope of fostering new generations of LTC risk management and products, and lists among its topics experience studies, regulation, public programs, in-force management, mergers and acquisitions, valuation, claims administration and fraud.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Chapter 2, History of Long-Term Care Products, and Chapter 4, Hybrid Life and Long-Term Care Products (Outcomes 1b, 1c); Chapter 9, Long-Term Care Insurance Experience Monitoring, and Chapter 10, Long-Term Care Insurance Premium Rate Increases (2b-2d); Chapter 8, Long-Term Care Insurance Valuation (3a, 3b).
