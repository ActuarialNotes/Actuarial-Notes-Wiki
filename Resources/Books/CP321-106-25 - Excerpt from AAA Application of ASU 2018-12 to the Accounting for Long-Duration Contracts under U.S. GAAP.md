## IV Claim Reserves and Long-Duration Health Insurance
- Scope
    - ASU 2018-12 applies to the sections of ASC Topic 944 that address long-duration contracts, so it affects health insurance contracts such as long-term care and individual long-term disability that are classified as long-duration, and not those classified as short-duration; Medicare Advantage contracts are generally short-duration, while Medicare Supplement contracts are often long-duration.
    - The new standards apply to all long-duration contracts, whether or not the contract had an active life reserve under GAAP before the ASU; assumptions, with the exception of discount rates and the possible exception of expenses, are no longer fixed at issue and are reviewed and updated at least annually.
- Long-tail claim liabilities
    - The discount rates used for long-tail claim liabilities and IBNR of long-duration contracts must be the same as the rates applied to the cohort in which the contract resides, and do not change when the contract moves from the active-life to the claim-payment phase; the discount rate guidance does not apply to claim liabilities arising from short-duration contracts.
    - There is no new guidance on classifying contracts as "limited-payment", so classifications developed at contract inception remain appropriate.
- Updates for actual experience and assumption changes
    - The net premium ratio is updated for "actual historical benefits" and "updated remaining expected benefits"; the guidance does not specify an order for measuring assumption changes and new claims in the roll-forward disclosure, so each company determines an appropriate ordering and applies it consistently.
