## [F-002] Thousands commas inside math render with a space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 45-55
- claim: Math spans write 7,968.89 and 19,549.25 with a bare comma.
- evidence: In KaTeX math mode ',' is punctuation and is followed by a thin space, so $7,968.89$ renders '7, 968.89'. The vault's own convention elsewhere (e.g. fm-161, fm-169) is {,}. Text-mode numbers in the stem are unaffected.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Write thousands separators inside math as {,}.
