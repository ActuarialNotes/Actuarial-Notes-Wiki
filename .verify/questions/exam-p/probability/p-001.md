- evidence: The stem fixes P(D) = 0.30, P(+|D) = 0.80 and P(+|not D) = 0.20. Independent blind recomputation gave 0.63 (C). For 0.50 to hold, P(+|not D) would have to be (0.50 - 0.24)/0.70 = 0.371. The question is unanswerable as posed and the key is wrong under the standard Bayes plus total-probability reading (the method of SOA sample Q532, Aug 2026 rev., solutions PDF pp.147-148).
