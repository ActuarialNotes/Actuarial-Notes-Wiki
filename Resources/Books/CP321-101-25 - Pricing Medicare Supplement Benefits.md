A study note, CP321-101-25, supplied by the Society of Actuaries for CP 321 under the title "Pricing Medicare Supplement Benefits, 2020". According to the course strategy guide, it describes Medicare Supplement coverage, which is specific to the United States and sold to members covered by Medicare, and its pricing; it is read with the critical illness article as one of two additional long duration products.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Outcomes 2a-2d; sections II, IV, V and the Appendix.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> The SOA supplies this study note, and its syllabus states that study notes are not available electronically. No copy could be found online, and no source names its author or lists its section titles. This page records only the citation and assignment from the syllabus and the course strategy guide.
