A December 2024 article from J.P. Morgan Asset Management by Dan Oldroyd, Portfolio Manager and Head of Target Date Strategies. It names three pivotal trends set to redefine the defined contribution plan space: a dual focus on low-cost options and active fixed income, the integration of alternative and real assets in target date funds, and the rise of hybrid default solutions that combine target date funds with guaranteed income.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — RET101-124-26; Outcomes 1a, 1c; 4l. The SOA course strategy guide says it describes some new developments related to DC plan investments.

## 1 Balancing low-cost options with active fixed income management
- Index funds have become possibly the most popular choice for their cost-effectiveness and perception of “safety”, but the fixed income landscape presents challenges that passive strategies may not fully address.

## 2 The growing role of alternative and real assets in target date funds
- Target date funds are now incorporating alternative and real assets, including private equity, real estate and commodities, to enhance returns and diversification.

## 3 The emergence of hybrid default solutions—aka TDFs with guaranteed income
- Hybrid default solutions combine target date funds with guaranteed income products, giving a transition from accumulation to decumulation.

## Conclusions: A new era for DC plans
