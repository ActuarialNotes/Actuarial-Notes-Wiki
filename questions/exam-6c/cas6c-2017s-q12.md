The following information is available for a property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2016. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| | Held-to-Maturity Bond Portfolio | Available-for-Sale Bond Portfolio | Fair Value Option Bond Portfolio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash flow from 2017 | 1,000.0 | 500.0 | 2,000.0 |
| Cash flow from 2018 | 1,000.0 | 750.0 | 1,500.0 |
| Cash flow from 2019 | 500.0 | 1,000.0 | 1,500.0 |
| Market Value | 2,291.3 | 2,020.3 | 4,561.1 |

Calendar year claims payout pattern

| 2017 | 2018 | 2019 |
|---|---|---|
| 40% | 35% | 25% |

Other information:

- Effective market yield: 5%
- Undiscounted losses and adjustment expenses: 5,000
- Assume all asset and liability cash flows are made at the end of the year.
- The market value of each bond corresponds to the present value of cash flows discounted at the effective yield.
- MfAD for claims development: 8%
- MfAD investment return rates: 0.5%
- Assume that there are no income taxes.

A change in interest rates occurs on December 31, 2016 which causes the yield to decrease from 5% to 4%.

Calculate the impact of the rate decrease on the following:

- i. Net Income
- ii. Other Comprehensive Income
- iii. Equity

### Explanation
**Claim liabilities.** Discount the undiscounted 5,000 on the 40% / 35% / 25% end-of-year payout pattern at each rate:

$$\text{PV} @ 5\% = 5{,}000 \times 40\% \times 1.05^{-1} + 5{,}000 \times 35\% \times 1.05^{-2} + 5{,}000 \times 25\% \times 1.05^{-3} = 4{,}571.86$$

$$\text{PV} @ 4.5\% = 5{,}000 \times 40\% \times 1.045^{-1} + 5{,}000 \times 35\% \times 1.045^{-2} + 5{,}000 \times 25\% \times 1.045^{-3} = 4{,}611.77$$

$$\text{PV} @ 4\% = 5{,}000 \times 40\% \times 1.04^{-1} + 5{,}000 \times 35\% \times 1.04^{-2} + 5{,}000 \times 25\% \times 1.04^{-3} = 4{,}652.30$$

$$\text{PV} @ 3.5\% = 5{,}000 \times 40\% \times 1.035^{-1} + 5{,}000 \times 35\% \times 1.035^{-2} + 5{,}000 \times 25\% \times 1.035^{-3} = 4{,}683.44 \text{ (as printed)}$$

Add the investment-return PfAD (the rate lowered by the 0.5% MfAD) and the 8% claims-development PfAD:

$$\text{PV w/PfAD} @ 5\% = 4{,}571.86 + (4{,}611.77 - 4{,}571.86) + 4{,}571.86 \times 8\% = 4{,}977.52$$

$$\text{PV w/PfAD} @ 4\% = 4{,}652.30 + (4{,}683.44 - 4{,}652.30) + 4{,}652.30 \times 8\% = 5{,}065.62$$

Change in liabilities $= 5{,}065.62 - 4{,}977.52 = 88.10$

**Assets.**

- HTM: MV @ 5% = 2,291.30; MV @ 4% = 2,291.30 — H-T-M uses book yield, not market yield, so no impact.
- AFS: MV @ 5% = 2,020.30

$$\text{MV} @ 4\% = 500 \times 1.04^{-1} + 750 \times 1.04^{-2} + 1{,}000 \times 1.04^{-3} = 2{,}063.18$$

Change $= 2{,}063.18 - 2{,}020.30 = 42.88$, which flows through OCI.

- FV option: MV @ 5% = 4,561.10

$$\text{MV} @ 4\% = 2{,}000 \times 1.04^{-1} + 1{,}500 \times 1.04^{-2} + 1{,}500 \times 1.04^{-3} = 4{,}643.41$$

Change $= 4{,}643.41 - 4{,}561.10 = 82.31$, which flows through NI.

**Impact.**

- i. NI change $= 82.31 - 88.10 = -5.79$
- ii. OCI change $= 42.88$
- iii. Equity $=$ NI change $+$ OCI change $= -5.79 + 42.88 = 37.09$

Alternatively, using duration:

$$\text{Modified Duration of Liabilities} = \frac{1 \times 5{,}000 \times 40\% \times 1.05^{-1} + 2 \times 5{,}000 \times 35\% \times 1.05^{-2} + 3 \times 5{,}000 \times 25\% \times 1.05^{-3}}{4{,}571.86} = 1.82$$

(as printed: the formula has no division by 1.05, so this and the durations below are Macaulay durations)

Change in liabilities $= -1.82 \times -1\% \times 4{,}977.52 = 90.57$

- Modified Duration of FVO = 1.87; change in FVO $= -1.87 \times -1\% \times 4{,}561.10 = 85.13$
- Modified Duration of AFS = 2.19; change in AFS $= -2.19 \times -1\% \times 2{,}020.30 = 44.28$

Then:

- Change in NI $= 85.13 - 90.57 = -5.44$
- Change in OCI $= 44.28$
- Change in Equity $= 44.28 - 5.44 = 38.84$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to determine the impact that a change in interest rates would have on both the claim liabilities and the various bond portfolio classifications (HTM, AFS, FVO) and how a change in any of these elements would impact income, other comprehensive income and equity.
Common errors include:

- Calculating an increase in net income from an increase in liabilities
- Forgetting to include changes in liabilities in the change in net income
- Assuming that payments occurred mid-year rather than end of year (question specifically stated end of year)
- Calculating a change in the value of HTM bonds and including it the change in net income, OCI or equity
- Forgetting to include claims PfAD in calculation of APV of liabilities
