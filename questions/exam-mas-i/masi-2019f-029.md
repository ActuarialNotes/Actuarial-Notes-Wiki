An actuary has a dataset with four observations and wants to use Leave-One-Out Cross Validation (LOOCV) to determine which one of two competing models fits the data better. The model preference will be based on minimizing the mean squared error.

The values of the dependent variable are:

$$
\mathbf{y} = [y_1, y_2, y_3, y_4] = [1.55, 1.55, 1.60, 1.95]
$$

Corresponding fitted values under each model and training data subset are:

| Training Obs. Used | Model 1 $\hat{y}_1$ | Model 1 $\hat{y}_2$ | Model 1 $\hat{y}_3$ | Model 1 $\hat{y}_4$ | Model 2 $\hat{y}_1$ | Model 2 $\hat{y}_2$ | Model 2 $\hat{y}_3$ | Model 2 $\hat{y}_4$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1,2,3 | 1.50 | 1.60 | 1.20 | 1.80 | 1.60 | 1.70 | 1.60 | $Z$ |
| 1,2,4 | 2.00 | 1.50 | 1.10 | 1.90 | 1.80 | 1.40 | 1.30 | 1.70 |
| 1,3,4 | 1.75 | 1.55 | 1.70 | 2.10 | 1.40 | 1.30 | 1.50 | 1.95 |
| 2,3,4 | 1.70 | 1.65 | 1.60 | 2.00 | 1.60 | 1.70 | 1.20 | 2.00 |

Calculate the maximum value of $Z$ for which the actuary will prefer Model 2.

- A) Less than 1.5
- B) At least 1.5, but less than 1.8
- C) At least 1.8, but less than 2.1
- D) At least 2.1, but less than 2.4
- E) At least 2.4

## Explanation

In LOOCV each row's model is scored only on the observation it left out. The row trained on observations 1, 2, 3 predicts $y_4$; the row trained on 1, 2, 4 predicts $y_3$; 1, 3, 4 predicts $y_2$; and 2, 3, 4 predicts $y_1$. All other fitted values in the table are training fits and play no part.

**Model 1** held-out predictions and squared errors:

$$
\begin{align*}
(1.55 - 1.70)^2 + (1.55 - 1.55)^2 + (1.60 - 1.10)^2 + (1.95 - 1.80)^2 &= 0.0225 + 0 + 0.25 + 0.0225 \\
&= 0.295
\end{align*}
$$

**Model 2**, where the held-out prediction of $y_4$ is $Z$:

$$
\begin{align*}
(1.55 - 1.60)^2 + (1.55 - 1.30)^2 + (1.60 - 1.30)^2 + (1.95 - Z)^2 &= 0.0025 + 0.0625 + 0.09 + (1.95 - Z)^2 \\
&= 0.155 + (1.95 - Z)^2
\end{align*}
$$

Both LOOCV errors divide by the same $n = 4$, so compare the sums. Model 2 is preferred when its error is smaller:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.155 + (1.95 - Z)^2 &< 0.295 \\
(1.95 - Z)^2 &< 0.14 \\
|1.95 - Z| &< 0.3742
\end{align*}
$$

The upper end of that interval is the maximum:

$$
\begin{align*}
Z &< 1.95 + 0.3742 \\
&= 2.324
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[2.1, 2.4)$, so the answer is **D**.
