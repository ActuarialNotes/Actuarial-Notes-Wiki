You are given the following six observed values of the autoregressive model of order one time series

$$y_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \text{ with } Var(\varepsilon_t) = \sigma^2.$$

| $t$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y_t$ | 31 | 35 | 37 | 41 | 45 | 51 |

The approximation to the conditional least squares method is used to estimate $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$.

Calculate the mean squared error $s^2$ that estimates $\sigma^2$.

- A) 13
- B) 21
- C) 22
- D) 26
- E) 35

## Explanation

The approximate conditional least squares estimate of $\beta_1$ is the lag-1 autocorrelation $r_1$. The mean of the six observations is $\bar{y} = 240/6 = 40$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
b_1 = r_1 &= \frac{(31-40)(35-40) + (35-40)(37-40) + (37-40)(41-40) + (41-40)(45-40) + (45-40)(51-40)}{(31-40)^2 + (35-40)^2 + (37-40)^2 + (41-40)^2 + (45-40)^2 + (51-40)^2} \\
&= \frac{45 + 15 - 3 + 5 + 55}{81 + 25 + 9 + 1 + 25 + 121} \\
&= \frac{117}{262} \\
&= 0.4466.
\end{align*}
$$

The estimator of $\beta_0$ is

$$
\begin{align*}
b_0 &= \bar{y}(1 - r_1) \\
&= 40(1 - 0.4466) \\
&= 22.136.
\end{align*}
$$

The residuals are then

$$
\begin{align*}
e_2 &= 35 - (22.136 + 0.4466 \times 31) \\
    &= -0.9806 \\
e_3 &= 37 - (22.136 + 0.4466 \times 35) \\
    &= -0.7670 \\
e_4 &= 41 - (22.136 + 0.4466 \times 37) \\
    &= 2.3398 \\
e_5 &= 45 - (22.136 + 0.4466 \times 41) \\
    &= 4.5534 \\
e_6 &= 51 - (22.136 + 0.4466 \times 45) \\
    &= 8.7670.
\end{align*}
$$

The average residual is $\bar{e} = 2.78252$, and with $T = 6$ observations the mean square error is

$$
\begin{align*}
s^2 &= \frac{(-0.9806 - 2.78252)^2 + (-0.7670 - 2.78252)^2 + (2.3398 - 2.78252)^2 + (4.5534 - 2.78252)^2 + (8.7670 - 2.78252)^2}{6 - 3} \\
&= 21.969.
\end{align*}
$$
