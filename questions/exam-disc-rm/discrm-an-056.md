Determine which of the following statements about the environment of modern risk management are true.

- I. Regulators, rating agencies and institutional investors increasingly expect senior management to take responsibility for risk across the whole enterprise.
- II. Under enterprise risk management, risk is viewed only as a threat to be minimized or avoided.
- III. An organization's total risk equals the sum of the risks of its separate units.

- A) I only
- B) I and II only
- C) I and III only
- D) II and III only

## Explanation

- **I. True:** External pressure from regulators, rating agencies, stock exchanges, institutional investors and governance bodies is one of the forces behind the move to enterprise risk management.
- **II. False:** ERM recognizes the opportunistic side of risk; an organization may keep or pursue a risk it is especially able to manage.
- **III. False:** Portfolio risk is not the simple sum of its parts. Offsetting risks reduce the total, and correlated risks can concentrate it.

The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Includes II, which describes the defensive posture ERM moved away from.
- **C** — Includes III, which ignores the interaction between risks.
- **D** — Leaves out I and includes the two false statements.
