A perpetuity-due with annual payments consists of ten level payments of $X$ followed by a series of increasing payments. Beginning with the eleventh payment, each payment is 1.5% larger than the preceding payment.

Using an annual effective interest rate of 5%, the present value of the perpetuity is 45,000.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 1,679
- B) 1,696
- C) 1,737
- D) 1,763
- E) 1,781

## Explanation

The present value of the first ten payments is $X\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|0.05} = 8.10782X$.

The present value of the geometrically increasing perpetuity starting at payment 11 is (using the first payment at time 10 of $X$, growing at 1.5% per period):

$$
\begin{align*}
&v^{10} \cdot \frac{X}{1-1.015v} \cdot (1+i) \\
&= v^{10} \cdot \frac{X \cdot 1.05}{0.05-0.015} \\
&= X(1.05)^{-10}\cdot\frac{1.05}{0.035} \\
&= 18.69366X
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
45{,}000 &= 8.10782X + 18.69366X \\
&= 26.80148X \\
X &= 1{,}679
\end{align*}
$$
