# Exam GI-101
**GI 101 – Ratemaking and Reserving** is an exam of the Society of Actuaries' Fellowship (FSA) pathway. Its syllabus covers the Nov 2026/Mar 2027/Jul 2027 sittings.

The learning objectives below are transcribed from the Nov 2026/Mar 2027/Jul 2027 [syllabus](https://www.soa.org/globalassets/assets/files/edu/2026/gi-101/2026-2027-gi-101-syllabus.pdf) the SOA publishes, word for word; the SOA lists its outcomes a), b), c), numbered 1, 2, 3 here.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Introduction and Key Considerations {0–6%}
> The candidate will understand the key considerations for and key concepts underlying general [[Insurance|insurance]] actuarial work.
>
> 1. Identify sources of [[Professionalism Requirement|professionalism requirements]] and [[Good|good]] practice
> 2. Demonstrate knowledge of [[Good|good]] practice related to actuarial work
> 3. Use the actuarial control [[Cycle|cycle]] to [[Frame|frame]] actuarial work
> 4. Understand the [[Component|components]] of [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]]
> 5. Explain how [[Estimate|estimates]] of [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]] are used
> 6. Demonstrate the importance of understanding key terminology and [[Interrelationship|interrelationships]]
> 7. Identify different types of [[Data|data]] used for actuarial work
> 8. Explain the [[Responsibility|responsibilities]] of the [[Actuary|actuary]] related to [[Data|data]]
> 9. Describe how and why [[Data|data]] are [[Segregated|segregated]] and [[Aggregated|aggregated]]
> 10. Describe [[Qualitative Information Required|qualitative information required]] for actuarial work
> 11. Understand the sources of [[Data|data]] and information for actuarial work
> 12. Understand [[Credibility|credibility]] as used for actuarial work
> 13. Explain the application of [[Trend|trend]] adjustments in actuarial work
> 14. Illustrate the types of [[Trend|trend]] that influence the [[Future Cost|future cost]] of [[Claim|claims]], [[Expenses|expenses]], and [[Insurance Premium|premiums]]
> 15. Identify the [[Actuary's Responsibility|actuary’s responsibilities]] with [[Regard|regards]] to [[Documentation|documentation]] for actuarial work
> 16. Describe the role of [[Professional Judgment|professional judgment]] in actuarial work
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (1a-p)
>     - Chapter 2
>     - Part 2: Key Concepts (excluding Chapter 10)
> - Actuarial Standards of Practice, Actuarial Standards Board of the American Academy of Actuaries, No. 25, Credibility Procedures, 2013 (1l)

> [!example]- Preparing Claims and Exposure Data for Actuarial Work {8–18%}
> The candidate will demonstrate the [[Ability|ability]] to prepare [[Claim|claims]] and [[Exposure Base|exposure data]] for general [[Insurance|insurance]] actuarial work.
>
> 1. Create [[Development Triangle|development triangles]] of [[Claim|claims]] and [[Count|counts]] from detailed [[Claim Transaction Data|claim transaction data]]
> 2. Describe the different types of [[Exposure Base|exposures]] used for conducting actuarial work
> 3. Calculate [[Written|written]], [[Earned|earned]], [[In-Force|in-force]] and [[Unearned Premium|unearned premiums]] for [[Portfolio|portfolios]] of [[Policy|policies]] with various [[Policy|policy]] terms and [[Earnings Pattern|earnings patterns]]
> 4. Adjust historical [[Earned Premium|earned premiums]] to current [[On-Leveling|rate levels]]
> 5. Understand types of changes [[Influencing Average Premium Level|influencing average premium levels]]
> 6. Describe adjustments needed to estimate [[Ultimate Premium|ultimate premiums]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (2a-f)
>     - Part 3: Preparing the Data (excluding 12.6.4 and 12.6.6)
>     - Appendices A through C and F (excluding Sections A.3.6, A.5)

> [!example]- Projecting Ultimate Claims {42–56%}
> The candidate will know how to calculate and evaluate [[Projected Ultimate Value|projected ultimate values]].
>
> 1. Identify considerations for selecting methods for estimating [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate claims]]
> 2. Identify and describe [[Good|good]] practice related to projecting [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate claims]] with regard to [[Required Data|required data]], [[Large Loss|large claims]], and comparison of current to [[Prior|prior]] actuarial work
> 3. Identify the types of [[Development Triangle|development triangles]] that can be used for [[Data Diagnostic Analysis|investigative testing]]
> 4. Analyze [[Development Triangle|development triangles]] for [[Data Diagnostic Analysis|investigative testing]]
> 5. Describe the key assumptions underlying the following [[Projection|projection]] methods: [[Development|development]] method, [[Development-Based Frequency-Severity|development-based frequency-severity]] method, expected method, [[Bornhuetter Ferguson]] method, [[Benktander]] method, [[Cape Cod]] method, and [[Generalized Cape Cod]] method
> 6. Demonstrate knowledge of [[Good|good]] practice related to projecting [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]]
> 7. Estimate [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]] using the methods cited in (e)
> 8. Describe the [[Berquist-Sherman Method|Berquist-Sherman data]] adjustments and their key assumptions
> 9. Adjust [[Data Triangle|data triangles]] for changes in the [[Adequacy|adequacy]] of [[Case Estimate|case estimates]] or changes in the [[Rate|rate]] of [[Settlement Rate|claim settlement]] using a [[Berquist-Sherman Method|Berquist-Sherman data]] adjustment
> 10. Estimate [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate claims]] using the appropriate [[Berquist-Sherman Method|Berquist-Sherman data]] adjustments
> 11. Explain the effect of [[Changing Conditions|changing conditions]] on the [[Projection|projection]] methods cited in (e)
> 12. Assess the [[Appropriateness|appropriateness]] of the [[Projection|projection]] methods cited in (e) in [[Varying Circumstance|varying circumstances]]
> 13. Evaluate and justify [[Selection|selections]] of [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]] based on the methods cited in (e)
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (3a-m)
>     - Part 4: Basic Methods for Estimating Ultimate Claims (excluding 15.8, 16.6, 16.7, 17.8, 18.8, 19.6, 20.6, 21.3.5, and 21.3.6)
>     - Appendices A through C and F (excluding A.3.6 and A.5)

> [!example]- Financial Reporting {4–12%}
> The candidate will understand [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]] of [[Unpaid Claims|claim liabilities]] with [[Respect|respect]] to [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|unpaid unallocated loss adjustment expenses]].
>
> 1. Describe the key assumptions underlying ratio and [[Count-Based|count-based]] methods for estimating [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|unpaid unallocated loss adjustment expenses]]
> 2. Estimate [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|unpaid unallocated loss adjustment expenses]] using ratio and [[Count-Based|count-based]] methods
> 3. Evaluate and justify [[Selection|selections]] of [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|unpaid unallocated loss adjustment expenses]] based on ratio and [[Count-Based|count-based]] methods
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (4a-c)
>     - Chapter 23 (excluding section 23.4)

> [!example]- Trending {6–16%}
> The candidate will understand [[Trend|trending]] procedures as applied to [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate claims]], [[Exposure Base|exposures]] and [[Insurance Premium|premiums]].
>
> 1. Identify and describe the [[Influence|influences]] of [[Portfolio|portfolio]] changes on [[Frequency|claim frequency]] and [[Severity|severity]]
> 2. Identify the time [[Period|periods]] associated with [[Trend|trending]] procedures
> 3. Analyze and evaluate [[Trend|trend]] for [[Claim|claims]] (including [[Frequency|frequency]], [[Severity|severity]], and [[Pure Premium|pure premium]]) and [[Exposure Base|exposures]] (including [[Inflation-Sensitive Exposure|inflation-sensitive exposures]] and [[Insurance Premium|premiums]])
> 4. Choose [[Trend|trend rates]] for [[Claim|claims]] ([[Frequency|frequency]], [[Severity|severity]], and [[Pure Premium|pure premium]]) and [[Exposure Base|exposures]]
> 5. Calculate [[Trend|trend]] factors for [[Claim|claims]] and [[Exposure Base|exposures]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (5a-e)
>     - Part 6: Trending Procedures
>     - Appendices A through C, and F (excluding A.3.6, A.5)

> [!example]- Ratemaking {10–22%}
> The candidate will understand how to apply the [[Fundamental Ratemaking|fundamental ratemaking]] techniques of general [[Insurance|insurance]].
>
> 1. Quantify different types of [[Expenses Required|expenses required]] for [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]] including [[Expense Trending|expense trending]] procedures
> 2. Incorporate [[Underwriting Profit|underwriting profit]] and [[Contingency Margin|contingency margins]] in [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]]
> 3. Explain the [[Requirement|requirements]] for [[Loading|loadings]] for [[Catastrophe|catastrophes]] and [[Large Loss|large claims]] in [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]]
> 4. Calculate [[Loading|loadings]] for [[Catastrophe|catastrophes]] and [[Large Loss|large claims]]
> 5. Apply [[Loading|loadings]] for [[Catastrophe|catastrophes]] and [[Large Loss|large claims]] in [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]]
> 6. Describe the [[Claim|claim]] ratio and [[Pure Premium|pure premium]] methods of [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]]
> 7. Calculate indicated [[Rate|rates]] and [[Rate|rate]] changes using the [[Claim|claim]] ratio and [[Pure Premium|pure premium]] methods
> 8. Demonstrate the use of [[Credibility|credibility]] in [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (6a-h)
>     - Part 7: Ratemaking (Chapters 30 through 32)
>     - Appendices A through C, and F (excluding A.3.6, A.5)

> [!example]- Analyze and Evaluate Real-Life Scenarios (Included in Topics 2, 3, 5 & 6) {0%}
> The candidate will be able to define an approach for actuarial analyses supporting [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]] and [[Ratemaking|ratemaking]] analyses under various [[Real-Life Scenario|real-life scenarios]]. The candidates will be able to define [[Funding Requirement|funding requirements]] for [[Self-Insurer|self-insurers]].
>
> 1. Identify [[Data|data]] and information [[Requirement|requirements]]
> 2. [[Conduct Investigative Testing|Conduct investigative testing]]
> 3. Choose [[Projection|projection]] methods to estimate [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]]
> 4. Estimate [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]]
> 5. Justify [[Selection|selections]] of [[Ultimate Loss|ultimate values]]
> 6. Develop [[Reserve|reserves]] for [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis, J. Friedland, 2nd Edition, 2022 (7a-f)
>     - Appendices A through C, and F (excluding A.3.6, A.5)
