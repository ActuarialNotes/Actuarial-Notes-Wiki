An IAA Risk Book chapter (May 2023) on reinsurance, from the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association. It outlines the key features of reinsurance arrangements, how they are priced and their potential impact on both the cedant and the reinsurer, with a focus on property and casualty reinsurance and a high-level treatment of life and health reinsurance.
