An insurer wrote a new ISO personal auto policy (no state amendatory endorsement) 100 days ago. The underwriter now wants to cancel it. Which development lets the insurer cancel?

- A) The named insured caused a minor at-fault accident in a parking lot last week
- B) The named insured received a speeding ticket for driving 20 miles over the limit
- C) Her resident son's driver's license was suspended during the policy period
- D) The named insured moved to a territory with much higher rates

## Explanation

During the first 60 days of a new policy the insurer may cancel for any reason. After that the policy may be cancelled only for nonpayment of premium, suspension or revocation of the license of a driver who lives with the named insured or customarily uses the covered auto, during the policy period, or material misrepresentation. The son's suspension qualifies. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — An accident is not a listed reason; the insurer can reflect it at renewal or decide not to renew.
- **B** — A ticket is not a suspension or revocation of a license.
- **D** — A move changes the rate, and the premium can be adjusted; it is not grounds to cancel.
