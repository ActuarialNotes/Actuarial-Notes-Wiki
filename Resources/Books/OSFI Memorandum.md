## §5 and Appendix II — the capital link

The AAR must also document the **actuarial calculations supporting the regulatory capital tests**. Appendix II lists the actuarial items feeding the [[MCT]] and the Branch Adequacy of Assets Test — the [[Insurance Risk Margin|insurance risk]] margins on the [[Liability for Incurred Claims|LIC]] and on unexpired coverage, the reinsurance credit, and the [[Earthquake Exposure Risk Margin|earthquake]] items. That is the mechanism by which one actuarial report supports both the financial statements and the capital return.

## How to read it against the CIA material

[[CIA CSOP]] §2400 says what an actuary's report must contain as a matter of professional standard; this memorandum says what **OSFI** additionally requires of a federally regulated insurer's report. Where they overlap, the memorandum is more specific; where they differ, both bind. Section 4.5 (changes influencing the valuation) and 4.9 (actual versus expected) are the two OSFI reads first, because they are where a deteriorating estimate shows up.
