A study note, CP321-108-25, supplied by the Society of Actuaries for CP 321 under the title "ALM for Life, Annuities, and Pensions". According to the course strategy guide, it was custom written for the QFI track on asset liability matching, and CP 321 uses its sections 2, 5 and 6 to provide the basics of ALM; the note refers throughout to R code, which candidates are not expected to know how to use.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Outcomes 5a, 5b; Sections 2, 5 and 6.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> The SOA supplies this study note, and its syllabus states that study notes are not available electronically. No copy could be found online, and no source names its author or lists its section titles. This page records only the citation and assignment from the syllabus and the course strategy guide.
