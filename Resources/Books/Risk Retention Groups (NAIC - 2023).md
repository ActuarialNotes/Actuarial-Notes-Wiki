## Background
- Risk retention groups are member-owned liability insurers formed under the federal Liability Risk Retention Act of 1986 and state law, and they operate in multiple states.
- They are regulated under NAIC accreditation standards and differ from traditional insurers: they report under GAAP, have limited rate and form filings, and have no access to state guaranty funds.

## Actions
- The section summarizes NAIC work on RRG regulation, including GAO studies, the application of reinsurance model law revisions to RRGs, a best practices document and a registration form.
