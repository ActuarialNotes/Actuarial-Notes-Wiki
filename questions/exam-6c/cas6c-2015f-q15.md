A company is performing a cash flow simulation for risk transfer analysis.

## Part a (0.5 points)

Briefly describe how parameter risk can be accounted for explicitly and implicitly.

### Explanation
Explicit: By including a distribution of the parameter and doing a Monte Carlo analysis/distribution around each of the parameters

Implicit: By increasing the parameter by a PfAD/higher expected loss or increase volatility of losses/ be conservative when selecting parameter

### Examiner Report
This was a very challenging question with many candidates receiving no credit. Only a few candidates received full marks and a few received partial credit. A common error was for candidates to have mixed the implicit/explicit concepts. Some candidates were confused by the concept of deterministic versus stochastic models.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Briefly describe one advantage and one disadvantage of using reinsurance pricing assumptions.

### Explanation
Advantage: More in line with reinsurer's view on the risk transfer, and incorporates market view of the risk/Provides view as how reinsurer or market see the contract

Disadvantage: Pricing assumption is more market driven and hard/soft market stage will impact pricing assumptions, but should not impact risk transfer analysis/ Risk transfer shouldn't fluctuate based on market conditions

### Examiner Report
This was a very challenging question with many candidates receiving no credit. Only a small number received full marks.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Explain why it would be reasonable to use an interest rate above risk-free rate and identify one of its flaws.

### Explanation
Reasonable: Because reinsurer's average rate of return is above the risk free rate

Flaw: Investment strategies of different reinsurers should not have impact on the risk transfer itself as it may result in different conclusions drawn for reinsurers with good or bad investment strategies./An analysis of risk transfer shouldn't take into account the reinsurer's investment strategy.

### Examiner Report
Candidates performed better on this part compared to parts a) and b). Most candidates could explain why a higher rate was reasonable but often had trouble identifying a flaw.
