Under an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11), which watercraft accident does Section II personal liability cover? None of the boats is being raced, rented to others, used for a charge or used in a business.

- A) The insured's own 90-horsepower outboard boat, owned for years and never declared or reported, hits a swimmer
- B) A 150-horsepower inboard ski boat the insured rented for the weekend hits a dock worker
- C) The insured's own 30-foot sailboat swings its boom into a guest
- D) A 200-horsepower inboard boat the insured borrowed from a friend hits another boat

## Explanation

An inboard or inboard-outdrive engine of more than 50 horsepower is covered if the boat is **not owned by or rented to** an insured. A borrowed boat is neither, so the accident is covered. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — an owned outboard over 25 horsepower acquired before the policy period is covered only if declared at inception or reported in writing within 45 days of acquisition.
- **B** — the same inboard rule excludes a boat over 50 horsepower that is rented to an insured.
- **C** — an owned sailing vessel is covered only if it is less than 26 feet long.
