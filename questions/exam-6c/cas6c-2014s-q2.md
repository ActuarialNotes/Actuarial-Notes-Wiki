Answer the following with respect to the case of *Whiten v. Pilot Insurance Co.*:

## Part a (1.5 points)

Describe the facts of the case and the Supreme Court's final decision.

### Explanation
Insured's house was destroyed in a fire. After some time, the insurer cuts off the rent without notice alleging arson ("that insured deliberately set the house on fire"). The allegation was wholly discredited at trial as no evidence of arson. Jury first awarded punitive damage of \$1M which was reduced to \$100k by the court of appeal. Supreme Court reversed the court of appeal's decision to reduce the award as though very high the award was within the rational limit as the act was highly reprehensible as an effort to force an unfair settlement.

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally were able to list the facts of the case leading to the trial. However, some candidates were unable to fully provide details of the case from the initial trial, to the appeal and finally to the Supreme Court's verdict.

## Part b (1.5 points)

Identify and briefly describe three considerations the Supreme Court used to determine the quantum of punitive damages.

### Explanation
- Degree of vulnerability of the plaintiff. Punitive damage would be higher if defendant abuse of a position of power
- Degree of harm or potential harm directed at plaintiff. The more harm is done the higher the quantum
- Other advantage wrongfully gained – so that the defendant don't see the award simply as a fee

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally were able to list the considerations but some did not earn full credit by not being able to expand on the considerations.
