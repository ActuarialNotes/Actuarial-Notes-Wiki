Rank these four buildings from **least** to **most** fire resistive under ISO's construction classes: (W) concrete block walls with a steel deck roof and noncombustible floors; (X) wood frame with stucco on the exterior; (Y) concrete walls, floors and roof rated for two hours; (Z) brick walls with wood joists and a wood roof deck.

- A) Z, X, W, Y
- B) X, W, Z, Y
- C) X, Z, W, Y
- D) Y, W, Z, X

## Explanation

- **X is frame (Class 1):** stucco on wood is a frame exterior.
- **Z is joisted masonry (Class 2):** masonry walls with a combustible floor and roof.
- **W is masonry noncombustible (Class 4):** masonry walls with a noncombustible floor and roof.
- **Y is fire resistive (Class 6):** rated for at least two hours.

From least to most fire resistive: X, Z, W, Y. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Ranks the brick building below the frame one. Masonry walls make joisted masonry more fire resistive than frame.
- **B** — Puts the noncombustible-roof building below the one with a wood roof.
- **D** — Lists the buildings from most to least fire resistive, the reverse of what was asked.
