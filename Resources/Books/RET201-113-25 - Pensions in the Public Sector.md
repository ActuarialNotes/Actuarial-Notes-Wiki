A Pension Research Council volume edited by Olivia S. Mitchell and Edwin C. Hustead. It takes stock of public pension developments in the US and Canada, highlighting the challenges these financial institutions face in coming decades, and is the first Pension Research Council study of public pensions in a quarter-century, written by a team of international actuarial, legal and economic experts. The syllabus assigns its Chapter 9.
