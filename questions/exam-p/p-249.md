Losses follow an exponential distribution with mean 1. Two independent losses are observed.

Calculate the expected value of the smaller loss.

- A) 0.25
- B) 0.50
- C) 0.75
- D) 1.00
- E) 1.50

## Explanation

Let $X_1,X_2$ be the two losses, each exponential with mean 1, i.e. rate $\lambda=1$. The smaller loss is $\min(X_1,X_2)$, and the minimum of $n$ independent exponentials with rate $\lambda$ is exponential with rate $n\lambda$: here $P(\min(X_1,X_2)>z)=e^{-z}e^{-z}=e^{-2z}$.

$$
\begin{align*}
E[\min(X_1,X_2)] &= \frac{1}{n\lambda} \\
                 &= \frac{1}{2\times1} \\
                 &= \mathbf{0.50}
\end{align*}
$$
