## Status conventions used

`verified/high` — every check against the source agreed and nothing above nit is open.
`verified/medium` — the same, with an open major or minor that does not affect the answer or a
formula; also the cap for commercial texts read only through their contents pages, and for
original questions (no official solution: verified means every formula was sourced and the
blind recomputation reproduced the key). `verified/low` — sourced, but an open major sits on a
formula or defining sentence. `disputed` — an open critical. `in_review` — substantive claims
could not be sourced.
