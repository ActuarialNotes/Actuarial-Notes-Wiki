![[The Discriminating (Pricing) Actuary (Frees and Huang - 2021) - Cover.svg]]

A paper reviewing the social and economic principles that can be used to assess the appropriateness of insurance discrimination. Because discrimination issues vary by line of business and by country and legal jurisdiction, it examines those principles from the vantage of specific lines and jurisdictions, describes the considerations for prohibitions based on a diagnosis of COVID-19, and discusses privacy and algorithmic proxies as insurers' use of Big Data grows. The copy is the draft of March 16, 2021, with its online supplement.
