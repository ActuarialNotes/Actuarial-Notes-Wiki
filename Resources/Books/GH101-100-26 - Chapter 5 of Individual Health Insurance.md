A textbook on the individual health insurance market, now in its third edition, updated with developments since the rollout of the Affordable Care Act and with updated and expanded material on other topics such as Medicaid Managed Care. ACTEX Learning describes it as covering the history of the individual marketplace, the products currently available, the role of regulation and government health policy, and all aspects of the development and management of the business.
