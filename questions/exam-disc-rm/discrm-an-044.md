In one state, an insurer writes 10% of the homeowners premium and received 45 of the 900 homeowners complaints filed with the insurance department last year. What is its complaint index, and what does it indicate?

- A) 0.05, meaning 5% of its policyholders complained
- B) 0.50, meaning its share of complaints is half its share of business
- C) 2.00, meaning its share of complaints is twice its share of business
- D) 45, meaning it drew more complaints than a typical insurer

## Explanation

A complaint index divides the insurer's share of complaints by its share of the business:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Complaint share} &= \frac{45}{900} \\
&= 5\% \\
\text{Index} &= \frac{5\%}{10\%} \\
&= \mathbf{0.50}
\end{align*}
$$

It draws half as many complaints as its share of the market would suggest, a sign it is meeting customers' needs comparatively well. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — 0.05 is its share of complaints, not the index, and it is a share of complaints filed, not of policyholders.
- **C** — 2.00 divides market share by complaint share, inverting the index.
- **D** — A raw count says nothing without the insurer's size; the index exists to adjust for it.
