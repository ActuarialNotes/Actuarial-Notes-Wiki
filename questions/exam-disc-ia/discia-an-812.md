A fitness club's CGL includes Coverage C. Which injury, all during the policy period, would Coverage C pay for?

- A) A member who sprains an ankle during a spin class
- B) A club employee who slips in the locker room and receives workers compensation benefits
- C) A beverage supplier's delivery driver who trips at the club's front door
- D) A customer injured at home when an exercise band the club sold her snaps

## Explanation

Coverage C pays medical expenses for bodily injury caused by an accident on premises the named insured owns or rents, regardless of fault. A supplier's delivery driver is a member of the public, not an insured or a person hired by the insured, so the answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — exclusion e removes injury while practicing, instructing or participating in physical exercises or games, sports or athletic contests.
- **B** — an employee is an insured (exclusion a), and workers compensation benefits are payable (exclusion d).
- **D** — injury away from the premises from a product the club sold is within the products-completed operations hazard, which exclusion f removes.
