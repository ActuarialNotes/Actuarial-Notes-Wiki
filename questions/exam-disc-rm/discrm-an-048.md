A regional insurer plans to launch a specialized liability product for large construction contractors, a segment in which it has no producers. The buyers need advice on complex coverage and are spread across several states. Which distribution approach best fits?

- A) Direct response through a website and call center, to keep costs low
- B) Independent agents and brokers who already serve large contractors
- C) An exclusive agency force recruited and trained over several years
- D) Mass merchandising through an affinity group of homeowners

## Explanation

The choice weighs the target market's needs, the insurer's existing relationships, cost and geographic reach. Large commercial buyers need a producer's advice on complex coverage, and independent agents and brokers who already serve them give the insurer reach across several states without building a sales force. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Direct response suits standardized products that customers buy on price, such as personal auto, not complex commercial coverage.
- **C** — An exclusive agency force gives control, but building and training one takes years and cost the insurer would bear before selling a policy.
- **D** — An affinity group of homeowners is the wrong market; mass merchandising suits simple personal products.
