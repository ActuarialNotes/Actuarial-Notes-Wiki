A May 2018 white paper of the Defined Contribution Institutional Investment Association (DCIIA) on the distribution options available to defined contribution plan sponsors, the "rules" that determine how participants may access their accumulated retirement savings. It highlights recent research on the impact of plan design on participant behavior with respect to distributions, and suggests that plan sponsors evaluate their plans' objectives for retired and separated participants and whether their distribution options align with them.
