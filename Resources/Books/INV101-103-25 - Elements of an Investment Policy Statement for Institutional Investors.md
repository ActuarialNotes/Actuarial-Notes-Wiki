A CFA Institute paper that suggests desirable components of an investment policy statement (IPS) for institutional investors, published in May 2010. The IPS serves as a strategic guide in the planning and implementation of an investment program, and the paper gives, for each element, an example of IPS language that is entirely fictitious. Not every element will be appropriate for every investor or every situation, and additional components reflecting unique investor circumstances may be desirable.

## Introduction

## 1 Scope and Purpose
- 1a Define the investor.
- 1b Define the structure.

## 2 Governance
- 2a Specify who is responsible for determining investment policy, executing investment policy, and monitoring the results of implementation of the policy.
- 2b Describe the process for reviewing and updating the IPS.
- 2c Describe the responsibility for engaging and discharging external advisers.
- 2d Describe the roles and responsibilities of boards and staff.
- 2e Assign responsibility for determination of asset allocation, including inputs used and criteria for development of input assumptions.
- 2f Assign responsibility for risk management, monitoring, and reporting.

## 3 Investment, Return, and Risk Objectives
- 3a Describe the overall investment objective.
- 3b State the return and risk requirements.
- 3c Define the risk tolerance of the investor.
- 3d Describe relevant constraints.
- 3e Describe other considerations relevant to investment strategy.

## 4 Risk Management
- 4a Establish performance measurement and reporting accountabilities.
- 4b Specify appropriate metrics for risk measurement and evaluation.
- 4c Define a process for rebalancing portfolios to target allocations.
