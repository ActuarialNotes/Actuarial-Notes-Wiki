Kylie bought a 7-year, 5000 par value bond with an annual coupon rate of 7.6% paid semiannually. She bought the bond with no premium or discount.

Calculate the Macaulay duration of this bond with respect to the yield rate on the bond.

- A) 5.16
- B) 5.35
- C) 5.56
- D) 5.77
- E) 5.99

## Explanation

Since the bond is bought with no premium or discount, the yield rate equals the coupon rate: $i = 0.038$ per half-year.

Semiannual coupon $= 5000(0.038) = 190$. The Macaulay duration (in half-years) can be calculated directly:

$$
\begin{align*}
d &= \frac{95(Ia)_{\overline{14}|0.038} + 7(5000)v^{14}}{190a_{\overline{14}|0.038} + 5000v^{14}} \\
  &= 5.5554 \text{ years}
\end{align*}
$$

**Shortcut:** For a par bond, the Macaulay duration equals $\ddot{a}_{\overline{n}|}$ at the coupon rate:

$$
\begin{align*}
d &= \ddot{a}_{\overline{14}|0.038} \\
  &= 11.1107 \text{ half-years}
\end{align*}
$$

Converting to years: $d = 11.1107/2 = 5.5554$ years.
