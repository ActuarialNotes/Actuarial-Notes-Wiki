A February 2019 Society of Actuaries paper, subtitled Credibility Applications for Life and Health Insurers and Pension Plans. It aims to promote more informed applications of credibility methods for life insurers and pension plans, giving an overview of the most common credibility methods (limited fluctuation and greatest accuracy) and a review of the relevant credibility literature.
