An underwriter receives a small trucking submission, orders motor vehicle records for its drivers, and later, after the policy is issued, checks that the insured installed the dash cameras that were a condition of acceptance. In which steps of the underwriting process do these two activities fall?

- A) Developing alternatives, then implementing the decision
- B) Selecting an alternative, then determining the premium
- C) Determining the premium, then implementing the decision
- D) Evaluating the submission, then monitoring the decision

## Explanation

Ordering motor vehicle records gathers information the underwriter needs to judge the submission, which is part of evaluating it. Checking after issue that a condition of acceptance was met is follow-up, which is monitoring. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Developing alternatives lists accept, accept with modifications and decline; it doesn't gather facts. Implementation is binding and issuing the policy, not checking conditions later.
- **B** — Selection and pricing come after the facts are in, and neither involves post-issue follow-up.
- **C** — Pricing doesn't gather driver records, and implementation ends with issuing and delivering the policy.
