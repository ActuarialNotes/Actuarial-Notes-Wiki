At the beginning of the year, an entrepreneur deposited money into a savings account. At the end of the year, the entrepreneur had earned a total of 400 in interest and had an account balance of 8000.

Determine which annual quantity exactly equals 5%.

- A) Nominal discount rate
- B) Discount factor
- C) Effective discount rate
- D) Effective interest rate
- E) Force of interest

## Explanation

The account balance at the beginning of the year was $8000 - 400 = 7600$, so the effective interest rate, which measures the interest against the starting balance, is $400/7600 = 5.26\%$.

The effective discount rate measures the interest for the year against the balance at the end of the year. The total interest of 400 in the 1-year period is 5% of the new balance of 8000:

$$
\begin{align*}
d &= \frac{400}{8000} \\
  &= 0.05
\end{align*}
$$

So, by definition, 5% is the effective discount rate.
