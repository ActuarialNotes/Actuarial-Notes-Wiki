You are given a vector of data, $\boldsymbol{y}$, a vector of parameters, $\boldsymbol{\theta}$, and a probability density function, $p$.

Determine which of the following expressions represents the likelihood function in a Bayesian model.

- A) $p(\boldsymbol{y} \mid \boldsymbol{\theta})$
- B) $p(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \boldsymbol{y})$
- C) $p(\boldsymbol{y}, \boldsymbol{\theta})$
- D) $p(\boldsymbol{y})$
- E) $p(\boldsymbol{\theta})$

## Explanation

Bayes' theorem names the four pieces of a Bayesian model:

$$
\underbrace{p(\boldsymbol{\theta} \mid \boldsymbol{y})}_{\text{posterior}} = \frac{\overbrace{p(\boldsymbol{y} \mid \boldsymbol{\theta})}^{\text{likelihood}} \; \overbrace{p(\boldsymbol{\theta})}^{\text{prior}}}{\underbrace{p(\boldsymbol{y})}_{\text{marginal likelihood}}}
$$

The likelihood is the probability (density) of the observed data given the parameters, $p(\boldsymbol{y} \mid \boldsymbol{\theta})$, read as a function of $\boldsymbol{\theta}$.

The other choices are the other pieces: B is the posterior, C is the joint distribution of data and parameters (likelihood times prior), D is the marginal likelihood (the evidence) and E is the prior.

The answer is **A**.
