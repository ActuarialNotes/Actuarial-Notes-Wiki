This document outlines the individual life and annuities (ILA)-track exams and modules syllabuses. The Canadian Institute of Actuaries' syllabus for one of the five education tracks to its Fellowship (FCIA) designation: the track's 3 exams — each an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses, with the weight of each topic in parentheses and the level of each objective on the revised Bloom's taxonomy — and its two online modules.

## Individual life and annuities track exams
- Exam F1ILA: Finance and Valuation
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Regulations and applicable standards (15%)
    - 3. Data (10%)
    - 4. Principles of valuation - basic topics (15%)
    - 5. Principles of valuation – advanced topics (25%)
    - 6. IFRS 17 financial reporting (25%)
    - 7. Financial management (10%)
- Exam F2ILA: Product Design and Pricing
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Data (10%)
    - 3. Drivers of product design (25%)
    - 4. Design and purpose of various product types, benefits, and features (25%)
    - 5. Relationship between the product features, risks, assumptions, and modelling (25%)
    - 6. Actuarial requirements of product implementation and monitoring (15%)
- Exam F3ILA: Risk Management
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. ERM concept, framework, and process (10%)
    - 3. Risk categories and risk identification (10%)
    - 4. Economic capital modelling and aggregation of risks (25%)
    - 5. Risk measurement and assessment (20%)
    - 6. Stress and scenario testing (15%)
    - 7. Capital management (10%)
    - 8. Asset/liability management (10%)
