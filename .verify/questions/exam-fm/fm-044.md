- evidence: SOA Q44 (questions PDF p.21, image) states the condition the answer rests on: 'The liability has the same present value and duration as the asset portfolio. Calculate the amount invested in the five-year zero-coupon bonds.' The vault drops that sentence and asks for Redington immunization instead. The convexity condition fails. Any other split breaks the PV or duration match, so no purchase of these two bonds achieves Redington immunization. The answer computed by matching PV and duration is still 208,556 (C = SOA's key). The vault's 'amount of the 5-year bond that should be purchased' can also be read as a face amount (281,738, not an option), where SOA says 'amount invested'.
