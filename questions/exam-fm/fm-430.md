A zero-coupon 1000 face amount bond sells for a price of 890 and matures in $n$ years, where $n$ is a whole number.

A second bond has the same price, same time until maturity, and same annual effective yield. It pays annual coupons at an annual rate equal to 50% of the annual effective yield rate.

Calculate the face value of the second bond.

- A) 938
- B) 942
- C) 945
- D) 948
- E) 951

## Explanation

Let $i$ be the yield rate, $n$ the term in years of the two bonds, and $F$ the face value of the second bond. Then the coupon rate for the second bond is $\dfrac{i}{2}$.

The equation of value for the first bond is

$$
\begin{align*}
890 &= 1000v^{n} \\
v^{n} &= 0.89
\end{align*}
$$

For the second bond we have

$$
\begin{align*}
890 &= \frac{i}{2}F\,a_{\overline{n}|i} + Fv^{n} \\
    &= \frac{i}{2}F\,\frac{1-v^{n}}{i} + Fv^{n} \\
    &= \frac{F}{2}\left(1 - v^{n} + 2v^{n}\right) \\
    &= \frac{F}{2}\left(1 + v^{n}\right)
\end{align*}
$$

The $i$ cancels, so

$$
\begin{align*}
F &= \frac{1780}{1 + v^{n}} \\
  &= \frac{1780}{1.89} \\
  &= 941.80
\end{align*}
$$
