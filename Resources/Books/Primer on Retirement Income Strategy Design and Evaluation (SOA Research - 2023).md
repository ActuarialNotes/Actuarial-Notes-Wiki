A January 2023 primer on strategies to generate income during retirement, from the perspectives of their design and evaluation. Design means specifying a joint investment and withdrawal strategy, and evaluation means analysing how well the potential outcomes of a strategy satisfy a retiree's objectives, as the foundation for choosing one. It centres on catering to differences between retirees, in particular their income objective, appetite for income risk and personal attributes.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — Executive Summary, Sections 1, 2, 5, 6 and Appendix A; Outcomes 2a, 2d; 3b. The SOA course strategy guide says it provides a more in-depth look at retirement income options in DC plans and how they might be evaluated against one another.
