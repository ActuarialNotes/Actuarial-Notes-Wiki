## [F-001] Explanation's algebra step double-counts the 0.01 coupon term

- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: major

- locus: Explanation, lines 58-61
- claim: Explanation sets 2r + 0.01 = (1.6 - 0.29729 - 0.08514)/8.5136 = 0.14301, then r = (0.14301 - 0.01)/2 = 0.0715.
- evidence: From 1.6 = 0.29729 + (2r+0.01)(8.5136), 2r+0.01 = (1.6-0.29729)/8.5136 = 0.15302. The two slips cancel, so the key (B, 7.15%) is right, but a reader following the lines gets 6.65%. SOA solution Q163 (solutions PDF p.43, image) writes 1.6 = 0.29729 + 17.02713r + 0.08514, r = (1.6-0.29729-0.08514)/17.02713 = 0.0715. Recomputed: r = 0.071508.
- source_rank: 1
