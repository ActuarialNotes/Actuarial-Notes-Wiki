A January 2025 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, sponsored by the Health Care Cost Trends Strategic Research Program and the Peterson Health Technology Institute, on Digital Diabetes Management Solutions, V1.1 and Virtual Musculoskeletal Solutions, V1.0. It examines, from an actuarial perspective, how well current evaluation techniques serve the needs of decision-makers such as payers, providers and consumers, and gives guidance on applying them.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-101 (SOA)|Exam GH-101]] — Outcomes 2c; the whole report.

## 1 Executive Summary
- 1.1 About this Research
    - The report evaluates two assessments by the Peterson Health Technology Institute (PHTI), prepared under the ICER-PHTI Assessment Framework for Digital Health Technologies, in a two-step process: a gap analysis, then a case study applying typical actuarial methods over one to three years.
    - For diabetes the actuarial focus was the budget impact of adopting a new technology, and the analysis introduced "Total Risk Analysis"; for musculoskeletal care it was return on investment, with a new technique, the "Value Stack".
