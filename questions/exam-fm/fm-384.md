A 15-year bond with a face amount of 5000, a redemption value of $C$, and an annual coupon rate of 7% paid quarterly is purchased at a price that yields an annual nominal rate of 5.6% convertible quarterly.

The amount for amortization of premium in the third coupon payment is 6.88.

Calculate $C$.

- A) 5150
- B) 5165
- C) 5190
- D) 5235
- E) 5275

## Explanation

Each quarterly coupon is $5000\left(\dfrac{0.07}{4}\right) = 87.5$, the quarterly yield rate is $j = \dfrac{0.056}{4} = 0.014$, and the bond has $4(15) = 60$ coupons. The amount for amortization of premium in the third coupon is the decrease in book value from just after the second coupon (58 coupons remaining) to just after the third (57 remaining):

$$
\begin{align*}
BV_{2} - BV_{3} &= 6.88 \\
\left(87.5\,a_{\overline{58}|} + Cv^{58}\right) - \left(87.5\,a_{\overline{57}|} + Cv^{57}\right) &= 6.88
\end{align*}
$$

At $j = 0.014$, $a_{\overline{58}|} - a_{\overline{57}|} = v^{58}$ and $v^{58} - v^{57} = -0.014v^{58}$, with $v^{58} = 0.446477$. So

$$
\begin{align*}
87.5v^{58} - 0.014Cv^{58} &= 6.88 \\
39.0667 - 0.0062507C &= 6.88 \\
0.0062507C &= 32.1867 \\
C &= 5149.3
\end{align*}
$$
