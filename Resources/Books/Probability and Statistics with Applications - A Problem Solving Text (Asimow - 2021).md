![[Probability and Statistics with Applications - A Problem-Solving Text - Cover.png]]

An introductory textbook designed to make probability and statistics accessible to college freshmen and sophomores concurrent with their study of calculus. It aims to combine the qualities of a standard textbook and an actuarial exam study manual, with full explanations, derivations and many problems drawn from earlier actuarial exams. The second edition covers the material for SOA Exam P in Chapters 1–8 and expands Chapters 9–12 for CAS Exam ST and the statistics portion of CAS Exam S, adding mixture distributions, non-homogeneous Poisson processes, Bayesian analysis with conjugate priors, nonparametric methods and graphical methods.

> - [[Exam P-1 (SOA)|Exam P]] — a suggested text (the syllabus requires none); Chapter 1; Chapter 2; Chapter 3 (exclude 3.4.5, 3.7); Chapter 4 (exclude 4.6.2, 4.6.3, 4.6.5); Chapter 5 (exclude 5.6); Chapter 6 (exclude 6.3.4, 6.4.2, 6.4.6, 6.7); Chapter 7 (exclude 7.5, 7.6, 7.7, 7.9, 7.10); Chapter 8: 8.1.5, 8.3, 8.4 (exclude continuous), 8.6.
