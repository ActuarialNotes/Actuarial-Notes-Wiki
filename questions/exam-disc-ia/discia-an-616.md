A windstorm destroys a dental office's \$6,000 lighted sign, which stands on a post near the street. The office's BPP (ISO CP 00 10 10 12) has a building limit of \$800,000 and the causes of loss special form. Ignoring the deductible, what is the most the BPP pays for the sign?

- A) \$1,000
- B) \$2,500
- C) \$5,000
- D) \$6,000

## Explanation

The limits of insurance clause caps loss to outdoor signs, **whether or not the sign is attached to a building**, at \$2,500 per sign in any one occurrence. The building limit's spare room does not change that. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$1,000 is the fire department service charge limit, not the outdoor sign limit; the 10 12 edition states \$2,500 per sign.
- **C** — \$5,000 is the limit of the special form's property in transit extension, not the sign limit.
- **D** — Pays the full value, ignoring the per-sign cap.
