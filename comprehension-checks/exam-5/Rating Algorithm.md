A policy carries both a flat \$50 policy fee and a 10% discount. Does it matter which is applied first?

- A) No — multiplication and addition can be taken in any order at all
- B) Only when the discount is expressed additively rather than multiplicatively
- C) No, as long as both of them fall within the same step of the sequence
- D) Yes — discounting before the fee is added gives a different premium
