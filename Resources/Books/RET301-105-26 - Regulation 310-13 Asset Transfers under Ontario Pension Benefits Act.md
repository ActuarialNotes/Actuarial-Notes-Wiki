Ontario Regulation 310/13 under the Pension Benefits Act, on asset transfers under sections 80 and 81 of the Act, in the e-Laws consolidation from January 1, 2025 (last amendment O. Reg. 391/24). It applies to every transfer of assets from an original pension plan to a successor pension plan, with general rules, and separate parts on the transfer of defined benefits, of defined contribution benefits and of target benefits, followed by schedules of application contents and standard and special notices.
