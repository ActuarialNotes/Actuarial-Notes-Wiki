A borrower takes out a loan of 4000 at an annual effective interest rate of 6%.

Starting at the end of the fifth year, the loan is repaid by annual payments, each of which equals 600 except for a final balloon payment that is less than 1000.

Calculate the final balloon payment.

- A) 616
- B) 639
- C) 642
- D) 688
- E) 696

## Explanation

First determine the number of regular payments. The payments of 600 start at time 5, so with $v = 1/1.06$:

$$4000 = 600v^4a_{\overline{n}|0.06} \qquad a_{\overline{n}|0.06} = \frac{4000}{600}(1.06)^4 = 8.4165$$

Using the calculator, $n = 12.07$. So the loan takes 12 payments, the last of them enlarged: there are 11 regular payments of 600 (at times 5 through 15), and the final balloon payment $X$ is made at time 16.

The equation of value for the balloon payment is

$$4000 = 600v^4a_{\overline{11}|0.06} + Xv^{16} = 3748.286 + 0.393646X$$

so

$$X = \frac{4000 - 3748.286}{0.393646} = 639.44$$

which is less than 1000, as required.
