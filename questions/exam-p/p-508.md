A statistician determines that the probability density function $f(x)$ of the losses from construction accidents is proportional to $\dfrac{x}{\left(1+x^2\right)^9}$, for all nonnegative $x$.

Calculate the 2nd percentile of the losses from construction accidents.

- A) $0.003$
- B) $0.020$
- C) $0.045$
- D) $0.050$
- E) $0.222$

## Explanation

Let $a$ be the 2nd percentile. Writing the density as a constant times $x(1+x^2)^{-9}$, the constant cancels in the ratio of a partial integral to the whole:

$$
0.02 = \frac{\int_0^a x(1+x^2)^{-9}dx}{\int_0^\infty x(1+x^2)^{-9}dx}
$$

The substitution $u = 1 + x^2$ makes both integrals elementary, with antiderivative $-(1+x^2)^{-8}/16$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\int_0^a x(1+x^2)^{-9}dx &= \frac{1 - (1+a^2)^{-8}}{16} \\
\int_0^\infty x(1+x^2)^{-9}dx &= \frac{1}{16}
\end{align*}
$$

so the equation reduces to

$$
0.02 = 1 - (1+a^2)^{-8}
$$

Solving:

$$
\begin{align*}
(1+a^2)^{-8} &= 0.98 \\
1 + a^2 &= 0.98^{-1/8} = 1.0025285 \\
a^2 &= 0.0025285 \\
a &= \mathbf{0.050}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (B), 0.020, is the probability 0.02 mistaken for the percentile. The final square root is what separates the answer from choice (A), 0.003, which is roughly $a^2$ — with $a$ this small, squaring changes the value by more than an order of magnitude.
