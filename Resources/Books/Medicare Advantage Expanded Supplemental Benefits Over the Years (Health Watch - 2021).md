An article by Jackie Lee in the Society of Actuaries' Health Watch, Special Edition, March 2021, on the expanded supplemental benefits offered by Medicare Advantage plans. Per the SOA's description of it, the article uses public plan benefit package (PBP) category 13 data to look at the frequency and enrollment of plans offering additional, or expanded, "other" supplemental benefits from contract year 2019 through contract year 2021.
