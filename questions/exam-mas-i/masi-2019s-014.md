You are given the following simulation process to generate random variable $X$ using the rejection method:

- $X$ has a beta distribution, $f(x)$, with parameters $a = 2, b = 10, \theta = 1$, and is concentrated on the interval (0, 1).
- The rejection method uses $g(x) = 1$, for $0 < x < 1$.
- The rejection procedure is as follows:
  - Step 1: Generate independent random numbers $Y$ and $U$, which are both uniform on (0,1).
  - Step 2: If $U \leq \dfrac{f(Y)}{c\,g(Y)}$ stop and set $X = Y$. Otherwise return to Step 1.

Calculate the minimum possible value for $c$.

- A) Less than 3.0
- B) At least 3.0, but less than 3.5
- C) At least 3.5, but less than 4.0
- D) At least 4.0, but less than 4.5
- E) At least 4.5

## Explanation

Step 2 accepts $Y$ with probability $f(Y)/[c\,g(Y)]$, which must never exceed 1, so the method needs $f(x) \leq c\,g(x)$ for every $x$ in $(0, 1)$. The smallest valid $c$ is therefore $\max_x f(x)/g(x)$, which here is just the maximum of the beta density since $g(x) = 1$.

With $a = 2$, $b = 10$ and $\theta = 1$:

$$
\begin{align*}
f(x) &= \frac{\Gamma(12)}{\Gamma(2)\,\Gamma(10)}\,x(1 - x)^9 \\
&= 110\,x(1 - x)^9
\end{align*}
$$

Setting the derivative to zero,

$$
f'(x) = 110\,(1 - x)^8\big[(1 - x) - 9x\big] = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x = 0.1
$$

so the density peaks at $x = 0.1$:

$$
\begin{align*}
c &= f(0.1) \\
&= 110(0.1)(0.9)^9 \\
&= 4.2616
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[4.0, 4.5)$, so the answer is **D**.
