A practical guide to traditional and alternative investment vehicles for professional and individual investors who want to understand the benefits and pitfalls of using these products. It presents the major principles and methods of investing and their risks and rewards, covering equities, all types of fixed income securities, investment-oriented insurance products, mutual funds, closed-end funds, investment companies, exchange-traded funds, futures, options, hedge funds, private equity and real estate.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam INV-101 (SOA)|Exam INV-101]] — Ch. 10 Structured Credit Products (outcome 1a); Ch. 18 Investing in Capital Venture Funds and Ch. 19 Investing in Leveraged Buyouts (outcome 1b)
