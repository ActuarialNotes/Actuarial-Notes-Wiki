The first volume of the 2018-2019 series Social Security Programs Throughout the World, reporting on the countries of Europe. It describes each country's social security programs from laws and regulations in force in January 2018, with comparative tables on program types, mandatory old-age income security, demographic statistics and contribution rates. The Social Security Administration released it in September 2018, researched jointly with the International Social Security Association.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — RET101-110-25; Outcomes 1b, 2b. The SOA course strategy guide says the reading is the introduction to this document about European social insurance programs, and that essentially the same introduction is used in the guides to other regions.
