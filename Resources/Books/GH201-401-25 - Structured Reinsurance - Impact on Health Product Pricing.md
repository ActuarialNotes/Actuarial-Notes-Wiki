A Guy Carpenter paper on structured reinsurance as a capital management tool for health insurers. It sets out how risk-based capital (RBC) requirements shape the capital a health product needs, how a structured reinsurance transaction releases capital, when that is capital efficient, and what it does to the profit and growth margin in pricing.
