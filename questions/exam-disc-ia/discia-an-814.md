An insurer defends its insured under a CGL (CG 00 01 04 13) and loses at trial. Which of the following is **NOT** paid as a supplementary payment?

- A) Court costs taxed against the insured in the suit
- B) The insured's actual loss of earnings, up to \$250 a day, for days it spent at trial at the insurer's request
- C) Interest on the full judgment that accrues after it is entered and before the insurer deposits its share in court
- D) The plaintiff's attorneys' fees that the court taxes against the insured

## Explanation

Item e pays all court costs taxed against the insured, but it says these do not include attorneys' fees or attorneys' expenses taxed against the insured. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — court costs taxed against the insured are item e.
- **B** — item d pays reasonable expenses the insured incurs at the insurer's request, including actual loss of earnings up to \$250 a day.
- **C** — item g pays all interest on the full amount of a judgment that accrues after entry and before the insurer pays, offers or deposits the part within its limit.
