Determine which of the following statements about the P&C quarterly returns are true.

- I. All P&C insurers complete uniform quarterly and annual returns, although a jurisdiction may apply conditions of its own.
- II. A quarterly filing deadline is the date by which the regulator must receive the filing, not the date it must be sent.
- III. The Supervisory Quarterly Return (PC2) reports the insurance service result by class of insurance, and reports registered and unregistered reinsurance held on separate pages.

- A) I only
- B) I and II only
- C) I and III only
- D) II and III only
- E) The answer is not given by (A), (B), (C), or (D)

## Explanation

- **I is true.** The introduction to the P&C return instructions states that all P&C insurers must complete uniform quarterly and annual returns, although jurisdictions may apply certain conditions to meet their own requirements.
- **II is true.** Section II (Filing Requirements) states that the deadlines in Section V are the dates by which the regulators must **receive** the quarterly and annual filings. There are penalties for late filing in every jurisdiction.
- **III is true.** PC2 contains page 60.25, *Insurance Service Result*, by class of insurance (insurance revenue less insurance service expenses less the net expense from reinsurance contracts held). It also contains two reinsurance summaries: page 70.50 for **registered** reinsurance and page 70.60 for **unregistered** reinsurance.

All three statements are true. The fixed key has no "I, II and III" option, so the answer is **E**.

**Why not the others**

- **A**, **B**, **C** and **D** each leave out at least one true statement. The common slips are treating a deadline as a mailing date (dropping II) and assuming the quarterly filing lacks the annual return's IFRS 17 and reinsurance detail (dropping III).
