Given the following information for an insurance company:

| | Market Value (\$000s) | Book Value (\$000s) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local government bonds | \$50,000 | \$55,000 | 6.8 |
| Investment grade bonds | \$190,000 | \$178,000 | 5.6 |
| Below investment grade bonds | \$30,000 | \$28,000 | 3.4 |
| Non-affiliated preferred stocks | \$3,500 | \$3,000 | 7.6 |
| Non-affiliated common stocks | \$50,000 | \$42,000 | -- |
| Cash | \$2,000 | \$2,000 | -- |
| Receivable from agents | \$1,800 | \$1,800 | -- |

| Ceded assets | Balances (\$000s) | Reserve Deficiency (\$000s) | Rating | Capital Factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reinsurance Company A | \$50,000 | \$850 | A++ | 2% |
| Reinsurance Company B | \$20,000 | \$2,000 | A | 6% |
| Reinsurance Company C | \$5,000 | \$530 | B+ | 20% |

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Capital factor for: Receivable from agents | 5% |
| Spread of risk factor | 1.35 |
| Gross PML (\$000s) | \$35,000 |
| Liquid assets (\$000s) | \$700,000 |
| Reinsurance dependence factor | 1.25 |

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Unadjusted surplus (\$000s) | \$78,000 |
| Adjusted surplus (\$000s) | \$66,000 |

| Net required capital for: | (\$000s) |
|---|---|
| Fixed income securities risk | \$4,577 |
| Equity securities risk | \$10,842 |
| Interest rate risk | \$1,839 |
| Loss & LAE reserves risk | \$41,000 |
| Net premiums written risk | \$18,000 |
| Business risk | \$35 |

Calculate the A.M. Best's Capital Adequacy Ratio (BCAR) and briefly comment on the capital strength of the company based on the calculated ratio.

### Explanation
Credit risk ($B_4$):

- Required capital for receivable from agents $= 1{,}800 \times 5\% = 90$
- Required capital for reinsurance recoverables $= (50{,}000 + 850) \times 2\% + (20{,}000 + 2{,}000) \times 6\% + (5{,}000 + 530) \times 20\% = 3{,}443$
- Indicated reinsurance dependence required capital $= 3{,}443 \times (1.25 - 1) = 861$
- Adjusted reserve amount $= (50{,}000 + 850) + (20{,}000 + 2{,}000) + (5{,}000 + 530) = 78{,}380$
- Minimum reinsurance dependence required capital $= 78{,}380 \times 1\% = 784$
- Total required capital for credit risk $= 90 + 3{,}443 + \max(861, 784) = 4{,}394$

Net required capital:

$$\text{NRC} = \sqrt{B_1^2 + B_2^2 + B_3^2 + \left(\frac{B_4}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{B_4}{2} + B_5\right)^2 + B_6^2} + B_7$$

$$= \sqrt{4{,}577^2 + 10{,}842^2 + 1{,}839^2 + \left(\frac{4{,}394}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{4{,}394}{2} + 41{,}000\right)^2 + 18{,}000^2} + 35 = 48{,}374$$

BCAR:

$$\text{BCAR} = \frac{\text{Adjusted surplus}}{\text{Net required capital}} = \frac{66{,}000}{48{,}374} = 136\%$$

Interpretation: given a BCAR score greater than 100%, the implied balance sheet strength is secure.
