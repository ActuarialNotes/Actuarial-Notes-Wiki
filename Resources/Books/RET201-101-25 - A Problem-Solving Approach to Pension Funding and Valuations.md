An introductory textbook on the mathematics of pension funding and valuation by William H. Aitken, taught through a large number of worked examples. Each unit is introduced with enough explanatory material to move quickly to problem solving, and each major section carries discussion questions and exercises with numerical answers. Chapters 2 to 4 cover the standard cost methods, Chapters 5 to 7 cover gain and loss analysis, plan changes and ancillary benefits, and options and assets, and Chapter 8 summarizes the earlier material on a conceptual level.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 1b; the SOA study note RET201-101-25 reproduces Chapter 5 of the 2nd edition, and it is background only.
