The following information is available from the December 31, 2012, Appointed Actuary's Report of an insurance company.

Incremental Paid Losses and ALAE

| Accident Year | 12 month (\$000s) | 24 month (\$000s) | 36 month (\$000s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | \$40,000 | \$20,000 | \$10,000 |
| 2011 | \$45,000 | \$15,000 | |
| 2012 | \$35,000 | | |

Discounted Outstanding Losses and ALAE

| Accident Year | 12 month (\$000s) | 24 month (\$000s) | 36 month (\$000s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | \$48,000 | \$30,000 | \$18,000 |
| 2011 | \$47,000 | \$28,000 | |
| 2012 | \$50,000 | | |

Annual Yield

| | CY 2010 | CY 2011 | CY 2012 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Yield | 2.75% | 3.0% | 2.5% |

Calculate the discounted excess or deficiency ratio for accident year 2010 as at December 31, 2012.

### Explanation
XS/deficiency ratio for AY 2010:

$$= \frac{48{,}000 - 20{,}000 - 10{,}000 - 18{,}000 + 0.03 \times \left[\frac{48{,}000 + 30{,}000}{2}\right] + 0.025 \times \left[\frac{30{,}000 + 18{,}000}{2}\right]}{48{,}000}$$

$$= \frac{1{,}770}{48{,}000} = 3.7\% \text{ of excess}$$

Alternatively:

$$\text{2011 investment income} = \frac{48{,}000 + 30{,}000}{2} \times 0.03 = 1{,}170$$

$$\text{2012 investment income} = \frac{30{,}000 + 18{,}000}{2} \times 0.025 = 600$$

$$\text{Excess amount} = 48{,}000 - (20{,}000 + 10{,}000) - 18{,}000 + (1{,}170 + 600) = 1{,}770$$

$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{1{,}770}{48{,}000} = 0.0368 = 3.69\%$$

Alternatively:

$$\text{Investment income} = \frac{48{,}000 + 30{,}000}{2} \times 0.03 + \frac{30{,}000 + 18{,}000}{2} \times 0.025 = 1{,}770$$

$$\text{Excess / deficiency} = 48{,}000 - 18{,}000 - 20{,}000 - 10{,}000 + 1{,}770 = 1{,}770$$

$$\text{Excess ratio} = \frac{1{,}770}{48{,}000} = 3.69\%$$
