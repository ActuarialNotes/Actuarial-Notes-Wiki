An International Actuarial Note, an educational document dated August 2021, written to assist actuaries in complying with IFRS 17 and ISAP 4 by offering practical examples of ways in which they might implement the ISAP and IFRS 17 in the course of their work. It is organised into five sections and 17 self-standing chapters discussing the main topics of IFRS 17, written as a series of questions and answers, and is based on the Standard issued in May 2017 and the amendments published in June 2020.
