This document outlines the finance, investments and enterprise risk management (FIE)-track exams and modules syllabuses. The Canadian Institute of Actuaries' syllabus for one of the five education tracks to its Fellowship (FCIA) designation: the track's exams (F3BNK, Banking Applications, an option in place of F3FIE) — each an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses, with the weight of each topic in parentheses and the level of each objective on the revised Bloom's taxonomy — and its two online modules.

## Finance, investments and ERM track exams with option in banking
- Exam F1FIE: Financial Products
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Mathematics, statistics and stochastic calculus (15%)
    - 3. Introduction to option pricing theory (20%)
    - 4. Introduction to credit and liquidity risk (20%)
    - 5. Derivatives and hedging (10%)
    - 6. Fixed-income securities (20%)
    - 7. Guaranteed investment products and annuities (15%)
- Exam F2FIE: Valuation and Financial Considerations
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Investment policy and governance (10%)
    - 3. Asset allocation (15%)
    - 4. Equities (20%)
    - 5. Advanced option pricing (20%)
    - 6. Alternative assets (10%)
    - 7. Machine learning, neural networks, application of blockchain on insurance (10%)
    - 8. Financial reporting (15%)
- Exam F3BNK: Banking Applications
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Operations of a banking institution (15%)
    - 3. The banking regulatory framework (20%)
    - 4. Risk management in a banking operation (25%)
    - 5. Bank capital management framework (25%)
    - 6. Banking strategies and problem solving (15%)
- Exam F3FIE: Risk Management
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. ERM concept, framework, and process (10%)
    - 3. Risk categories and risk identification (10%)
    - 4. Economic capital modelling and aggregation of risks (25%)
    - 5. Risk measurement and assessment (25%)
    - 6. Stress and scenario testing (15%)
    - 7. Economic capital and ORSA (15%)
