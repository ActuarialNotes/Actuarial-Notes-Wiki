## Gaps, by the page that should teach them

Ranked by how many questions each page's gaps cost. The page is the one named for the item's concept; "no page" means the vault has no page by that name.

### **no page for Automatic Sprinkler Systems** — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-214` (fact): Only one or two sprinkler heads are usually needed to control a fire, so head density is not the main driver of sprinkler system failure.

### **no page for Claims Handling Performance Measures** — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-239` (fact): Total paid dollars reflect the number of claims closed and their sizes, not the quality of claim handling, so it is not a valid performance measure by itself.

### **no page for Maximum Foreseeable Loss** — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-217` (definition): Maximum foreseeable loss (MFL) is the loss from a worst-case fire with no sprinkler system working and no effective fire department response, stopped only by fire walls or space separation.

### **no page for Normal Loss Expectancy** — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-217` (definition): Normal loss expectancy (NLE) is the loss expected from a fire when all fire protection is in place and operating as intended.

### **no page for Property Loss Estimates** — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-217` (fact): Loss estimates rise as less protection is assumed to work: NLE is smaller than PML, which is smaller than MFL, and MFL can be less than the maximum possible loss (total destruction) when fire walls or separation stop the fire.

### **no page for Wet Pipe and Dry Pipe Sprinkler Systems** — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-214` (fact): Wet pipe sprinkler systems operate somewhat more often than dry pipe systems, but the difference is smaller than the effect of a shut-off system.

### `Concepts/Premium Audit.md` — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-237` (fact): Florida's biennial (every other year) physical audit provision applies to per capita policies, not to ordinary payroll-rated policies.

### `Concepts/Probable Maximum Loss.md` — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-217` (definition): Probable maximum loss (PML) is the loss expected from a fire with some impairment of protection, normally one sprinkler system out of service, but not a failure of all protection.

### `Concepts/Risk Control.md` — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-234` (fact): An insurer benefits from providing risk control because improving the accounts it writes lowers their losses, supporting profitability and helping it retain good accounts.
- `discrm-an-234` (fact): There is no general legal requirement that insurers provide risk control to every policyholder; some jurisdictions mandate specific inspections such as boilers, and service level depends on account size and line.

### `Concepts/Workers Compensation Classification.md` — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-400` (fact): When several basic classifications apply and none has payroll assigned, the highest-rated basic classification governs; this is not the general rule for choosing the governing class.
