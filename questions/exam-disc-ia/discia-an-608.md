A hardware store's building is insured under the BPP with the causes of loss basic form. Which loss is covered?

- A) The store's own delivery truck backs into the loading dock wall
- B) Burglars break in overnight and carry away the cash register's contents
- C) A customer's car loses its brakes and crashes through the front window
- D) Rain blows in through an open window and soaks the drywall below it

## Explanation

The aircraft or vehicles peril covers physical contact of a vehicle with the described property or the building containing it, excluding vehicles the insured owns or that are operated in the course of its business. A customer's car crashing into the store is covered. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — The insured's own delivery truck is excluded from the aircraft or vehicles peril.
- **B** — Theft is not a basic peril, and money is not covered property under the BPP in any case. Vandalism would cover only building damage the burglars cause getting in or out.
- **D** — Windstorm does not cover interior damage by rain unless wind or hail first damages the roof or walls and the rain enters through that opening; an open window is not such damage.
