The CIA's June 2022 note on **whether a group of contracts qualifies for the [[Premium Allocation Approach]]**. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objectives C1 and C2.

## Why eligibility matters

The PAA is the simplified measurement of the **[[Liability for Remaining Coverage|liability for remaining coverage]]** — broadly, unearned premium less deferred acquisition costs, rather than the full [[General Measurement Model|general measurement model]] with a [[Contractual Service Margin|CSM]]. Most Canadian P&C business uses it, but it is an *option with conditions*, not a default. Failing the test forces an insurer onto the GMM for that group.

## The two gateways

1. **Coverage period of one year or less** — automatic eligibility, no further test.
2. **Otherwise**, the insurer must reasonably expect that the PAA LRC **"would not differ materially"** from the LRC the GMM would produce.

## The two things that most often break eligibility

- **The time value of money.** Where the coverage period is long and interest rates are material, the GMM LRC discounts while the PAA LRC (absent a significant financing component) does not — Appendix B quantifies the gap.
- **Variability in the fulfilment cash flows** relating to remaining coverage.
