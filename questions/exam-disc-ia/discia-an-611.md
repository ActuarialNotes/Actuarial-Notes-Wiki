A restaurant insures its building and business personal property under the BPP with the causes of loss special form. In one month, a server takes \$3,000 of wine home, and a cook who has just been fired smashes \$5,000 of kitchen equipment on his way out. What does the special form cover?

- A) Both losses, because the special form covers all risks of direct physical loss
- B) Neither loss, because the form excludes all dishonest or criminal acts of employees
- C) The equipment the cook smashed, but not the wine the server took
- D) The wine the server took, but not the equipment the cook smashed

## Explanation

The special form excludes dishonest or criminal acts, including theft, by the insured's employees. The exclusion states that it does **not** apply to acts of destruction by employees, but that theft by employees is not covered. The cook's destruction is therefore covered and the server's theft is not. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Open perils means everything not excluded or limited; employee theft is excluded.
- **B** — The dishonesty exclusion carves out acts of destruction by employees.
- **D** — Reverses the rule: destruction is covered and theft is excluded.
