Company Q invests $X$ at the end of each year for 25 years at an annual effective interest rate of 9%. Company R invests 100 at the end of each year for 25 years at an annual effective interest rate of 9%, but, at the end of each year, the interest earned is reinvested at an annual effective interest rate of 8%.

Immediately after the 25th payment, Company R's total investment, including the reinvested interest, has the same value as Company Q's investment.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 91.22
- B) 91.93
- C) 92.67
- D) 93.41
- E) 94.03

## Explanation

For Company Q the accumulated value is $Xs_{\overline{25}|0.09} = 84.7009X$.

For Company R, the deposits are made at the end of each year, so during year $k$ its balance at 9% is $100(k-1)$ and the interest credited at the end of year $k$ is $9(k-1)$. The interest stream is therefore $9, 18, \ldots, 216$ at the ends of years $2, \ldots, 25$ — an increasing annuity $9(Is)_{\overline{24}|}$ reinvested at 8%. Adding the 25 deposits of 100 themselves, R's accumulated value is

$$
\begin{align*}
100(25) + 9(Is)_{\overline{24}|0.08} &= 2500 + 9\cdot\frac{\ddot{s}_{\overline{24}|0.08} - 24}{0.08} \\
&= 2500 + 9\cdot\frac{72.10594 - 24}{0.08} \\
&= 7{,}911.92
\end{align*}
$$

Then

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= 7{,}911.92/84.7009 \\
  &= 93.41
\end{align*}
$$
