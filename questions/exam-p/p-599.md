A random variable is exponentially distributed with mean $\mu$.

Determine the difference between the 75th percentile and the 25th percentile of this distribution.

- A) $\dfrac{\ln 3}{\mu}$
- B) $\mu$
- C) $\mu \ln 2$
- D) $\mu \ln 3$
- E) $\mu^2$

## Explanation

For an exponential with mean $\mu$, the cdf is $P[X < x] = 1 - e^{-x/\mu}$. Let $a$ be the 75th percentile:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.75 &= 1 - e^{-a/\mu} \\
0.25 &= e^{-a/\mu} \\
   a &= -\mu \ln(0.25)
\end{align*}
$$

Similarly the 25th percentile is $b = -\mu \ln(0.75)$. The difference is

$$
\begin{align*}
a - b &= -\mu \ln(0.25) + \mu \ln(0.75) \\
      &= \mu\left[\ln(0.75) - \ln(0.25)\right] \\
      &= \mu \ln 3
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (C), $\mu \ln 2$, is the *median* of the distribution, $-\mu \ln(0.5)$ — the 50th percentile itself rather than the spread between the quartiles. Of the two $\ln 3$ forms, only (D) scales with $\mu$: choice (A) divides by $\mu$, which cannot be right dimensionally, since a difference of percentiles must carry the same units as $X$.
