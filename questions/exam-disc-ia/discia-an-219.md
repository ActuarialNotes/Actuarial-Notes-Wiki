An insurer group's prior-year direct earned premium in the lines covered by the Terrorism Risk Insurance Program was \$500,000,000. This year it pays \$180,000,000 of insured losses from certified acts of terrorism, and industry losses exceed the program trigger. The deductible is 20% of prior-year premium and the federal share is 80%. How much does the federal government pay the group?

- A) \$64,000,000
- B) \$80,000,000
- C) \$116,000,000
- D) \$144,000,000

## Explanation

The group retains its deductible, and the Treasury pays its share of losses above it:

$$
\begin{align*}
D &= 0.20 \times 500{,}000{,}000 \\
&= 100{,}000{,}000 \\
F &= 0.80\,(180{,}000{,}000 - 100{,}000{,}000) \\
&= \mathbf{64{,}000{,}000}
\end{align*}
$$

The group keeps \$116,000,000: the deductible plus its 20% co-share of the excess.

- **B** pays all the losses above the deductible, forgetting the insurer's 20% co-share.
- **C** is what the group keeps, not what the government pays.
- **D** applies the 80% federal share from the first dollar, ignoring the deductible.
