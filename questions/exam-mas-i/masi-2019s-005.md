You are given the following information about random variable X:

- The hazard rate function is:

$$
r(x) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{k^2}{3x}, & \text{for } x \geq 2 \\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

- The value of the cumulative distribution function at $x = 5$ is:

$$
F(5) = 0.936.
$$

Calculate the absolute value of $k$.

- A) Less than 1.5
- B) At least 1.5, but less than 2.5
- C) At least 2.5, but less than 3.5
- D) At least 3.5, but less than 4.5
- E) At least 4.5

## Explanation

The survival function is the exponential of minus the cumulative hazard. The hazard is zero below 2, so $S(x) = 1$ there, and for $x \geq 2$

$$
\begin{align*}
H(x) &= \int_2^x \frac{k^2}{3t}\,dt \\
&= \frac{k^2}{3}\ln\frac{x}{2}
\end{align*}
$$

which gives $S(x) = e^{-H(x)} = (x/2)^{-k^2/3}$. At $x = 5$ the survival probability is $S(5) = 1 - 0.936 = 0.064$:

$$
\begin{align*}
(2.5)^{-k^2/3} &= 0.064 \\
\frac{k^2}{3} &= \frac{-\ln 0.064}{\ln 2.5} \\
&= \frac{2.7489}{0.9163} \\
&= 3
\end{align*}
$$

So $k^2 = 9$ and $|k| = 3$ (indeed $2.5^{-3} = 0.064$ exactly).

This falls in $[2.5, 3.5)$, so the answer is **C**.
