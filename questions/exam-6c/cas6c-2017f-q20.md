The following information is available for a property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2016. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| Item | Capital (Margin) Required at Target |
|---|---|
| **Insurance Risk Margin** | |
| Premium liabilities | ? |
| Unpaid claims | ? |
| Catastrophes | 500 |
| Margin required for reinsurance ceded to unregistered reinsurers | ? |
| **Market Risk Margin** | |
| Interest rate risk | ? |
| Foreign exchange risk | 0 |
| Equity risk | 500 |
| Real estate risk | 40 |
| Other market risk exposures | 10 |
| **Credit Risk Margin** | 810 |
| **Operational Risk Margin** | ? |

**Unpaid Claims Margin**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Net claim liabilities | 56,416 |
| Provisions for adverse deviation (PfAD) | 3,069 |
| Average risk factor | 14% |
| Duration | 1.51 |

**Premium Liabilities Margin**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Net premium liabilities | 29,000 |
| PfAD | 3,000 |
| Average risk factor | 20% |
| Duration | 0.50 |

**Margin Required for Unregistered Reinsurance**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Unearned premiums ceded | 5,000 |
| Outstanding losses recoverable | 25,000 |
| Reinsurance receivable | 800 |
| Non-owned deposits | 20,000 |
| Letters of credit | 15,000 |

There is only one reinsurer.

**Assets**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Market value of bonds | 150,000 |
| Market value of other interest-sensitive assets | 0 |
| Duration of bonds | 1.0 |
| Risk factor | 1.25% |

**Operational Risk Margin**

| Item | Value | Risk Factor |
|---|---|---|
| Direct written premium in the last 12 months | 135,000 | 2.50% |
| Assumed written premiums in the last 12 months | 20,000 | 1.75% |
| Increase in gross written premiums in the last 12 months | 40,000 | n/a |
| Ceded written premiums in the last 12 months | 50,000 | 2.50% |

The correlation factor between asset risk margin and insurance risk margin is 50%.

The risk factor for premium growth beyond the threshold of 20% is 2.50%.

The risk factor applied to total capital required (before the operational risk margin and diversification credit) for the calculation of the operational risk margin is 8.50%.

## Part a (1.75 points)

Calculate the margin for insurance risk.

### Explanation
Premium liabilities:

$$\max\big(30\% \times (135{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 50{,}000),\ 29{,}000 - 3{,}000\big) \times 20\% = 6{,}300$$

Unpaid claims:

$$(56{,}416 - 3{,}069) \times 14\% = 7{,}468.58$$

Unregistered reinsurers:

- Receivable letters of credit $= 30\% \times (5{,}000 + 25{,}000) = 9{,}000$
- Required collateral to reduce to 0 the capital required $= (5{,}000 + 25{,}000) \times 1.15 + 800 = 35{,}300$
- Acceptable collateral $= 20{,}000 + 9{,}000 = 29{,}000$
- Max capital required $= (5{,}000 + 25{,}000) \times 15\% = 4{,}500$
- Capital required $= \min(35{,}300 - 29{,}000,\ 4{,}500) = 4{,}500$

$$\text{Insurance risk margin} = 6{,}300 + 7{,}468.58 + 500 + 4{,}500 = 18{,}768.58$$

Alternatively:

For unregistered reinsurance, start from the 15% margin and reduce it by acceptable collateral:

- 15% margin $= (\text{UP ceded} + \text{O/S losses recoverable}) \times 15\% = 4{,}500$
- LOC limited to $30\% \times (\text{UP ceded} + \text{O/S losses recoverable}) = 9{,}000$, so only 9,000 of the LOC can be used
- Acceptable collateral in excess of recoverable $= \max(20{,}000 + 9{,}000 - 5{,}000 - 25{,}000 - 800,\ 0) = \max(-1{,}800,\ 0) = 0$
- Insurance risk margin for unregistered reinsurance $= 4{,}500 - 0 = 4{,}500$
- Insurance risk margin $= 7{,}468.58 + 6{,}300 + 4{,}500 + 500 = 18{,}768.58$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to calculate the different components of the capital required for insurance risk.

Common mistakes include:

- Adding or not removing PfADs to net claim liabilities for the calculation of the capital required for unpaid claims.
- For the net premium liabilities, the average risk factor was not properly applied, i.e. Max(30%x NWP, Net Prem. Liab.) x 20% vs Max(30%x NWP, Net Prem. Liab. x20%)
- The limit on letters of credit was ignored.
- The reduction in capital required for unregistered reinsurance was not limited to a positive number. Many candidates applied the following formula:

  1.15 x (UEP+O/S losses recoverable) – (UEP + O/S losses recoverable + Reinsurance receivable – Non-owned Deposits – Letters of credit)

  This formula produced a capital required for unregistered reinsurance greater than the maximum capital of 15% x (UEP+O/S losses recoverable).

## Part b (1 point)

Calculate the margin for market risk.

### Explanation
Liabilities duration:

$$\frac{56{,}146 \times 1.51 + 29{,}000 \times 0.5}{56{,}146 + 29{,}000} = 1.1671 \quad \text{(as printed; the net claim liabilities are 56,416)}$$

| | Value | Duration | $-\Delta y$ | $+\Delta y$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | 150,000 | 1 | -1,875 | 1,875 |
| Liability | 85,416 | 1.1671 | -1,2460.10 (as printed) | 1,246.10 |
| Interest rate risk | | | | 628.90 |

$$\text{Market Risk Margin} = 628.90 + 0 + 500 + 40 + 10 = 1{,}178.90$$

Alternatively:

- Asset $\times$ Duration $\times$ Risk shock $= 150{,}000 \times 1 \times 1.25\% = 1{,}875$
- Premium Liab. $\times$ Duration $\times$ Risk shock $= 29{,}000 \times 0.5 \times 1.25\% = 181.25$
- Claims Liab. $\times$ Duration $\times$ Risk shock $= 56{,}416 \times 1.51 \times 1.25\% = 1{,}064.85$
- Interest rate risk $= \max(1{,}875 - 1{,}246,\ -1{,}875 - (-1{,}246)) = 629$
- Market risk margin $= 629 + 0 + 500 + 40 + 10 = 1{,}179$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to apply the interest rate shock test to calculate the capital required for interest rate risk. Candidates were also expected to know how to calculate the market risk margin by summing its components.
Common mistakes include:

- Adding or removing PfADs to net claim or premium liabilities.
- Applying the interest rate shock test on assets only.
- Not calculating the margin for market risk.
- Not including one or more of the following: equity risk, real estate risk, foreign exchange risk and other market risk exposures in calculating the margin for market risk.

## Part c (0.75 points)

Calculate the margin for operational risk.

### Explanation
$$\text{Cap} = 30\% \times (18{,}768.58 + 1{,}178.9 + 810) = 6{,}227$$

- Gross premium growth $= 40{,}000 / (135{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 40{,}000) = 34.8\%$
- Above the 20%: $40{,}000 - (135{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 40{,}000) \times 20\% = 17{,}000$

Uncapped operational risk margin:

$$8.5\% \times (18{,}768.58 + 1{,}178.9 + 810) + 135{,}000 \times 2.5\% + 20{,}000 \times 1.75\% + 50{,}000 \times 2.5\% + 17{,}000 \times 2.5\% = 7{,}164$$

$$\text{Operational Risk Margin} = \min(6{,}227,\ 7{,}164) = 6{,}227$$

Alternatively:

- Growth $= (135{,}00 + 20{,}000) / (135{,}000 + 20{,}000 - 40{,}000) = 1.3478$ (as printed; the direct written premium is 135,000)
- Growth in excess of 20% $= (1.3478 - 1.2)/(1.3478 - 1) \times 40{,}000 = 17{,}000$
- $CR_o = 18{,}769 + 1{,}179 + 810 = 20{,}758$
- Operational Risk Margin $= \min(30\% \times CR_o,\ 0.085 \times CR_o + 0.025 \times 135{,}000 + 0.0175 \times 20{,}000 + 0.025 \times 50{,}000 + 0.025 \times 17{,}000) = 6{,}227$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to calculate the margin for operational risk.

A common mistake was:

- Not calculating the growth in excess of 20% properly, either by using direct written premium rather than gross written premium or by calculating the growth on the premium at the end of the period rather than on the one at the beginning of the period. Examples of improper calculation include:
  - 40,000 – (135,000 x 1.2 – 135,000) = 13,000
  - 40,000 – (155,000 x 1.2 – 155,000) = 9,000
  - 40,000 – (135,000 – (135,000 - 40,000) x 1.2) = 19,000
  - 135,000 – (135,000 – 40,000) x 1.2 = 21,000

## Part d (0.75 points)

Calculate the insurance company's capital required at target.

### Explanation
$$\text{Diversification credit} = A + I - \left(A^2 + I^2 + 2 \times R \times A \times I\right)^{0.5}$$

- $A = 810 + 1{,}179 = 1{,}989$
- $I = 18{,}769$
- $R = 0.5$
- Diversification credit $= 920$

$$\text{Capital required at target} = 18{,}769 + 1{,}179 + 810 + 6{,}227 - 920 = 26{,}065$$

Alternatively:

$$\text{Capital required at target} = \text{Operational Risk Margin} + \left(A^2 + I^2 + 2 \times R \times A \times I\right)^{0.5} = 26{,}065$$

with $A = 810 + 1{,}179 = 1{,}989$ and $I = 18{,}769$.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to calculate the diversification credit and the capital required at target.
Common mistakes include:

- Using (A^2 + I^2 – 2xRxAxI)^0.5 instead of (A^2 + I^2 + 2xRxAxI)^0.5 in the diversification credit calculation.
- Dividing the capital required by 1.5. This gives the minimum capital required instead the one at target.
- Not including the credit risk margin within the calculation of A.
