A test of $H_0: \mu = 5{,}000$ at $\alpha = 0.05$ returns a p-value of 0.31, so the actuary fails to reject. What may be concluded?

- A) The mean is 5,000
- B) There is a 31% chance the mean is 5,000
- C) The mean differs from 5,000 by less than 5%
- D) The data are consistent with 5,000, which may still be wrong
