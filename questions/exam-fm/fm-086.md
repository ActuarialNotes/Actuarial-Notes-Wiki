A bank agrees to lend 10,000 now and $X$ three years from now in exchange for a single repayment of 75,000 at the end of 10 years. The bank charges interest at an annual effective rate of 6% for the first 5 years and at a force of interest $\delta_t = \dfrac{1}{t+1}$ for $t \ge 5$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 23,500
- B) 24,000
- C) 24,500
- D) 25,000
- E) 25,500

## Explanation

The accumulation factor from time 5 to time 10 under the force of interest $\delta_t = 1/(t+1)$ is:
$$
\begin{align*}
e^{\int_5^{10}\frac{1}{t+1}\,dt} &= e^{\ln(11)-\ln(6)} \\
                                 &= \frac{11}{6}
\end{align*}
$$

The equation of value at time 10:
$$\left[10{,}000(1.06)^5 + X(1.06)^2\right]\cdot\frac{11}{6} = 75{,}000$$
$$
\begin{align*}
(13{,}382.26 + 1.1236X)\cdot\frac{11}{6} &= 75{,}000 \\
1.1236X &= 27{,}526.83 \\
X &= 24{,}498.78 \approx 24{,}500
\end{align*}
$$
