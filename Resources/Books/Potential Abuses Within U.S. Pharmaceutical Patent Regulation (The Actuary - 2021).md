An article in The Actuary, February 2021, on how some pharmaceutical manufacturers have used patent laws to gain extended patent lifespans and deter legal challenges, which consumer advocates note increases costs to consumers by delaying generic entrants. It covers potential abuses of patents as well as proposed reforms to patent laws to address them.
