An annual policy is written January 1 for \$1,200. On July 1, an endorsement adds coverage for the rest of the term at an additional premium of \$300, recorded on July 1. Treating months as equal, how much premium has been earned on the policy by September 30?

- A) \$900
- B) \$975
- C) \$1,050
- D) \$1,200

## Explanation

Endorsement premium is recorded on the endorsement's effective date and earned over the remaining coverage period, here the six months from July 1.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Base} &= 1{,}200 \times \frac{9}{12} \\
&= 900 \\
\text{Endorsement} &= 300 \times \frac{3}{6} \\
&= 150 \\
\text{Earned} &= 900 + 150 \\
&= 1{,}050
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$900 ignores the endorsement.
- **B** — \$975 earns the endorsement over twelve months ($300 \times \tfrac{3}{12}$) although it covers only six.
- **D** — \$1,200 treats the endorsement premium as fully earned when it is written.
