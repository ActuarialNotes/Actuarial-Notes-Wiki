Two continuous random variables have joint density $f(x,y) = 8xy$ on the region $(0 < x < y < 1)$. Which conclusion follows?

- A) They are independent, because the density factors into a function of $x$ times a function of $y$
- B) They are dependent, because the range of one variable depends on the value of the other
- C) They are independent, because the density is positive everywhere on its support
- D) Independence cannot be determined without first computing the covariance

<!-- rationale: 1: factoring is necessary but not sufficient, the support must also be a rectangle · 2: positivity on the support says nothing about independence · 3: zero covariance would not imply independence either, and the support already settles it -->
