A deferred annuity is in the third year of its surrender charge period, when the charge is 5% of any amount withdrawn above a free withdrawal of 10% of the account value. The account value is \$80,000, and the owner withdraws \$20,000. What is the surrender charge?

- A) \$400
- B) \$600
- C) \$1,000
- D) \$3,600

## Explanation

The first 10% of the account value, \$8,000, can be taken without charge, so the charge applies only to the excess:

$$
\begin{align*}
SC &= 0.05\,(20{,}000 - 0.10 \times 80{,}000) \\
&= 0.05\,(12{,}000) \\
&= \mathbf{600}
\end{align*}
$$

- **A** charges 5% on the free amount, \$8,000, which is the part that is not charged.
- **C** charges 5% on the whole withdrawal, ignoring the free withdrawal.
- **D** charges 5% on the whole account value above the free amount, $SC = 0.05\,(80{,}000 - 8{,}000)$, as if the contract had been surrendered. A partial withdrawal is charged only on the amount taken out above the free amount.
