A March 2022 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report that introduces actuaries to decentralized finance (DeFi), an emerging financial system powered by blockchain technology. It explains what DeFi is, its key characteristics, applications, opportunities and risks, how DeFi might be adopted alongside the current financial system, and what it means for practicing and aspiring actuaries. A glossary and a brief history of DeFi are included as appendices.
