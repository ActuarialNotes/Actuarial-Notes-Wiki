A paper in Variance, Volume 01, Issue 01 (2007), that summarizes key results from the Report of the Casualty Actuarial Society Research Working Party on Risk Transfer Testing. The Working Party defined and described a structured process of elimination to narrow down the field of reinsurance contracts that have to be tested for risk transfer, and offered two metrics for gauging risk transfer that are superior to the standard "10-10" value-at-risk test: the expected reinsurer deficit (ERD) and right-tailed deviation (RTD). A related metric, the risk coverage ratio (RCR), is also described.
