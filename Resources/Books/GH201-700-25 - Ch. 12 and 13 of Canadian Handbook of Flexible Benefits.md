A Wiley handbook on flexible benefit programs in Canada, in its third edition (2007). The publisher describes it as offering everything organizations need to know about designing, implementing, communicating and administering a successful flexible benefits program, with up-to-date information on health care cost management, the legal aspects of flexible benefits, administration alternatives, retiree and executive flex programs, and special considerations for specific provinces.
