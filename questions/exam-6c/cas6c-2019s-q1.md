## Part a (0.75 points)

Identify three objectives of the Insurance Bureau of Canada.

### Explanation
- Study legislation
- Engage in market research
- Discuss general insurance

Other acceptable answers:

- Raise public awareness and understanding of P&C insurance and the risks
- Collect & analyze statistical / actuarial

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to understand the objectives of the Insurance Bureau of Canada.
Common errors included:

- Answering along the lines of affordability/availability and rate approval

## Part b (1 point)

Contrast the following two forms of insurance regulation:

- i. Guidelines
- ii. Legislation

### Explanation
- Guidelines are more flexible & adaptable than legislation
- Changes to legislation must go through the senate, house of commons and the royal approval, while guidelines don't need to go through these steps

Other acceptable answers:

- Guidelines are interpreted rules on how things should be done
- Legislation are hard rules on how to do things & not up for interpretation
- Guidelines are preferred over legislation as they are more flexible, don't need any regulatory approval & less likely to be misinterpreted in court
- Guidelines are less obtrusive

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to understand the difference between legislation and guidelines.

Common errors included:

- Failing to contrast or explaining why a contrast exists, for example:
    - Stating "guidelines need disclosure" without commenting on legislation
    - Stating "legislation is interpreted" without commenting on guidelines

## Part c (0.25 points)

Briefly describe how federal legislation protects Canadian insureds of foreign insurance companies.

### Explanation
- Forces minimum amount of assets held in Canada for recovery in insolvency

Alternatively:

- Foreign insurer has to vest assets > 5 million in Canadian fund subject to control of minister of finance

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected know how federal legislation protects Canadian insureds of foreign insurance companies.

Common errors included:

- Not relating the answer to assets held in Canada, for example:
    - Being licensed in Canada
