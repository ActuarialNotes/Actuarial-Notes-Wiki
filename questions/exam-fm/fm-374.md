A car dealership offers a 120-month loan for a blue car costing 30,000, with an annual nominal interest rate of 9% compounded monthly and level end-of-month payments.

The dealership also offers a loan for a red car costing 33,000, with the same interest rate and end-of-month payments as for the loan for the blue car.

Calculate the number of months needed to pay off the loan for the red car.

- A) 132
- B) 135
- C) 138
- D) 140
- E) 141

## Explanation

The monthly interest rate is $\dfrac{0.09}{12} = 0.0075$. The level payment $P$ on the blue car's loan is

$$
\begin{align*}
30{,}000 &= P\,a_{\overline{120}|0.0075} \\
P &= \frac{30{,}000}{78.94169} \\
  &= 380.03
\end{align*}
$$

The red car's loan is repaid with the same payment of 380.03 at the same rate, so its number of payments $n$ solves

$$
\begin{align*}
380.03\,a_{\overline{n}|0.0075} &= 33{,}000 \\
a_{\overline{n}|0.0075} &= 86.83525 \\
v^{n} &= 1 - 86.83525(0.0075) \\
      &= 0.348736 \\
n &= -\frac{\ln 0.348736}{\ln 1.0075} \\
  &= 140.98
\end{align*}
$$

That is 140 full payments and a smaller final payment at the end of month 141, so the loan takes 141 months to pay off.
