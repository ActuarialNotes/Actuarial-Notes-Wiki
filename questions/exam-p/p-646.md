A car rental insurance policyholder rents a car for seven days. For any day, the probability that the policyholder has no claims is a constant $r$. The occurrences of claims on different days are mutually independent.

Determine the probability that the policyholder has at least one claim during the seven-day rental period.

- A) $r^7$
- B) $(1-r)r^6$
- C) $7(1-r)r^6$
- D) $1 - r^7$
- E) $(1-r)^7$

## Explanation

"At least one claim" is awkward to count directly — it covers one claim day, two claim days, and so on — so take the complement instead:

$$
P[\text{at least 1 claim in 7 days}] = 1 - P[\text{no claims in 7 days}]
$$

The days are mutually independent and each is claim-free with probability $r$, so

$$
P[\text{no claims in 7 days}] = \left(P[\text{no claims on a given day}]\right)^7 = r^7
$$

and therefore

$$
P[\text{at least 1 claim}] = \mathbf{1 - r^7}
$$

Choice (E), $(1-r)^7$, is the probability of a claim on *every one* of the seven days — the far end of the range rather than its complement. Choice (C), $7(1-r)r^6$, is the probability of exactly one claim day, which is only the first term of the tail.
