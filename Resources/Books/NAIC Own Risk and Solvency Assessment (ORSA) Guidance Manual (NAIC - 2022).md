The NAIC's guidance manual, as of December 2022 and maintained by the Group Solvency Issues (E) Working Group of the Financial Condition (E) Committee, on reporting an insurer's Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA) as required by the Risk Management and Own Risk and Solvency Assessment Model Act (#505). It sets out the ORSA's two primary goals and what an insurer subject to the requirements is expected to do.
