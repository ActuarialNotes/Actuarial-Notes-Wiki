A loan of 100 is to have all principal and accrued interest paid at the end of five years. Interest accrues at an annual effective rate of 5% for the first two years and at a force of interest at time $t$ in years $(t > 2)$ of $\delta_t = \dfrac{1}{1+t}$.

Calculate the equivalent annual effective rate of discount for the five-year period.

- A) 10.9%
- B) 13.0%
- C) 14.6%
- D) 15.4%
- E) 17.1%

## Explanation

Accumulate the loan of 100 for two years at 5% and then from $t = 2$ to $t = 5$ under the force of interest:

$$
\begin{align*}
AV_5 &= 100(1+0.05)^{2}\exp\left(\int_{2}^{5}\frac{1}{1+t}\,dt\right) \\
     &= 100(1.1025)\exp\left[\left.\ln(1+t)\right|_{2}^{5}\right] \\
     &= 110.25\left(\frac{6}{3}\right) \\
     &= 220.50
\end{align*}
$$

The equivalent annual effective rate of discount $d$ is the level rate that discounts 220.50 at time 5 back to the 100 lent:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{100}{(1-d)^{5}} &= 220.50 \\
(1-d)^{5} &= \frac{100}{220.50} \\
          &= 0.453515 \\
1-d &= 0.853726 \\
d &= 0.1463
\end{align*}
$$
