Given the following information:

- The projected calendar year loss reserve payout as of December 31, 2012 is:

| Accident Year | Calendar Year Payments (\$000,000): 2013 | Calendar Year Payments (\$000,000): 2014 |
|---|---|---|
| 2012 | \$10 | \$10 |
| 2011 | \$10 | |

- Losses are paid at the end of each year.
- Liabilities are discounted using the bond yield curve rates.
- The asset portfolio is composed of:

| Asset | Market Value (\$000,000) |
|---|---|
| Cash | \$25 |
| Bonds | \$75 |

- The duration of cash is zero.
- The bond portfolio is composed of an equal investment in the following bonds:

| Time to Maturity (Years) | Coupon Rate | Face Value | Bond Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5% | \$1,000 | \$1,020 |
| 2 | 6% | \$1,000 | \$1,040 |

- Bond coupons are paid annually.

Calculate the duration gap of the market value surplus for the insurer as of December 31, 2012.

### Explanation
Solve for the rates from the bond prices:

$$\frac{1050}{1 + r_1} = 1020 \;\Rightarrow\; r_1 = 2.94\%$$

$$\frac{60}{1.0294} + \frac{1060}{(1 + r_2)^2} = 1040 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{1060}{(1 + r_2)^2} = 981.71 \;\Rightarrow\; r_2 = 3.91\%$$

Bonds:

| $T$ | PMT | PV | PV × T |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1110 | 1078.30 | 1078.30 |
| 2 | 1060 | 981.73 | 1963.46 |
| Total | | 2060.03 | 3041.76 |

$$\text{Duration} = \frac{3041.76}{2060.03} = 1.4766$$

Losses:

| $T$ | PMT | PV | PV × T |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20M | 19,428,793 | 19,428,793 |
| 2 | 10M | 9,261,585 | 18,523,170 |
| Total | | 28,690,378 | 37,951,963 |

$$\text{Duration} = \frac{37{,}951{,}963}{28{,}690{,}378} = 1.32228$$

Duration of assets, with cash at duration 0:

$$D_A = \frac{25(0) + 75(1.4776)}{100} = 1.1075$$

(the bond duration $1.4776$ is as printed).

Value of liabilities = 28,690,378, so the market value surplus is 100M - 28,690,378 = 71,309,622, and

$$D_{MVS} = \frac{1.1705(100\text{M}) - 1.3228(28{,}690{,}378)}{71{,}309{,}622} = 1.0209$$

(the asset duration $1.1705$ is as printed).

Duration gap = 1.0209 - 0 = 1.0209.
