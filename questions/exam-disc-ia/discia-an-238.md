An insurer domiciled and licensed in Nebraska wants to sell homeowners insurance to Kansas residents on an admitted basis. Which statement is correct?

- A) Its Nebraska license lets it write admitted business in any state
- B) It may write Kansas business only through surplus lines brokers
- C) The NAIC can license it to write in all states at once
- D) It needs a Kansas license, and Nebraska still leads its solvency oversight

## Explanation

An insurer must be admitted, that is licensed, in each state where it writes, apart from surplus lines business placed with nonadmitted insurers. To write admitted business in Kansas it needs a **Kansas license**, and it would be a foreign insurer there. Its state of domicile, **Nebraska**, still leads its solvency oversight.

- **A** ignores that each state licenses insurers for business in that state.
- **B** describes the nonadmitted route, which is not admitted business and generally requires that the cover be unavailable from admitted insurers.
- **C** treats the NAIC as a licensing body. The NAIC offers a uniform application form, but each state issues its own license.
