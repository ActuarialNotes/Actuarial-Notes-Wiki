The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries performing actuarial services with respect to the analysis of life or health cash flow risks. This standard (Doc. No. 219) was adopted in December 2025 and is effective for actuarial work performed on or after June 1, 2026.

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
-     - Applies to actuaries performing actuarial services with respect to the analysis of life or health cash flow risks.
-     - An actuary reviewing a cash flow analysis performed by another party follows the guidance to the extent practicable within the scope of the assignment.
- 1.3 Cross References
- 1.4 Effective Date
-     - Effective for actuarial work performed on or after June 1, 2026.
