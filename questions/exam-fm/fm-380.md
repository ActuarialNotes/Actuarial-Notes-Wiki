Annual payments of 240 are made at the end of each year to repay a loan of 3400. The payments are based on an annual effective interest rate of 4.5%. The loan is settled with a drop payment of $X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 1.61
- B) 4.53
- C) 8.83
- D) 12.48
- E) 13.04

## Explanation

The number of level payments of 240 that the loan supports at 4.5% solves

$$
\begin{align*}
240\,a_{\overline{n}|0.045} &= 3400 \\
a_{\overline{n}|0.045} &= 14.16667 \\
v^{n} &= 1 - 14.16667(0.045) \\
      &= 0.3625 \\
n &= -\frac{\ln 0.3625}{\ln 1.045} \\
  &= 23.05
\end{align*}
$$

So there are 23 full payments of 240, and the drop payment $X$ is paid at time 24:

$$
\begin{align*}
3400 &= 240\,a_{\overline{23}|0.045} + Xv^{24} \\
     &= 240(14.147775) + Xv^{24} \\
     &= 3395.466 + Xv^{24} \\
Xv^{24} &= 4.534 \\
X &= 4.534(1.045)^{24} \\
  &= 4.534(2.876014) \\
  &= 13.04
\end{align*}
$$
