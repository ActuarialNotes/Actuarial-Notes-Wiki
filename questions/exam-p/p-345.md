An actuary wishes to predict the size $W$ of a claim using a predictor $T$. Suppose that $W$ and $T$ are independent and normally distributed with the same mean and with variances 4 and 12, respectively.

Calculate $P[|W-T| < 1]$.

- A) 0.20
- B) 0.23
- C) 0.38
- D) 0.60
- E) 0.68

## Explanation

$W-T \sim N(0, 4+12) = N(0, 16)$, $\text{SD}=4$.

$$
\begin{align*}
P(|W-T|<1) &= P\!\left(-\frac{1}{4} < Z < \frac{1}{4}\right) \\
           &= 2\Phi(0.25)-1
\end{align*}
$$

$$\approx 2(0.5987)-1 = \mathbf{0.1974 \approx 0.20}$$
