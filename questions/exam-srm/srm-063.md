You have constructed the following regression tree predicting unit sales (in thousands) of car seats. The variable ShelveLoc has possible values Good, Medium, and Bad.

![Regression tree; each node shows its predicted value and the percentage of observations in it, and at every split the left branch is "yes" (the condition holds) and the right branch "no". Root: 7.5, 100%, split on ShelveLoc = Bad,Medium. Yes (left): node 6.8, 79%, split on Price >= 106. Its yes branch: node 6, 52%, split on ShelveLoc = Bad, with leaves 4.7 (15%) on the yes side and 6.6 (36%) on the no side. Its no branch: node 8.2, 27%, split on Age >= 55, with leaves 7.4 (16%) on the yes side and 9.4 (11%) on the no side. No (right) from the root: node 10, 21%, split on Price >= 110, with leaves 9.2 (14%) on the yes side and 12 (7%) on the no side.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ActuarialNotes/Actuarial-Notes-Wiki/main/Media/Attachments/srm-q63-car-seat-tree.png)

| Variable | Observed Value |
|---|---|
| ShelveLoc | Good |
| Price | 120 |
| Age | 57 |
| Advertising | 12 |

Determine the predicted unit sales (in thousands) for the above observation based on the regression tree.

- A) 4.7
- B) 6.6
- C) 7.4
- D) 9.2
- E) 9.4

## Explanation

For the first split, ShelveLoc = Good, the answer is no, and the branch to the right is taken. For the second split, Price = 120 ≥ 110, the answer is yes, and the branch to the left is taken. The node has a predicted value of 9.2.
