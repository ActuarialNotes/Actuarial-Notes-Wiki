On any given day, the number of available units, $X$, of a given commodity, and the market price per unit, $Y$, have joint probability function

$$
p(x,y) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{|y - 6x - 2|}{18}, & \text{for } x = 1, 2 \text{ and } y = 10, 12, 14 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Assume that all available units will sell at the market price per unit.

Calculate the expected revenue generated by the sale of all available units.

- A) $140/9$
- B) $144/9$
- C) $150/9$
- D) $156/9$
- E) $162/9$

## Explanation

Revenue is units times price, so the quantity wanted is $E[XY]$ — computed cell by cell, not as $E[X]E[Y]$.

Evaluate the joint function at all six cells:

| | $y=10$ | $y=12$ | $y=14$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x=1$ | 2/18 | 4/18 | 6/18 |
| $x=2$ | 4/18 | 2/18 | 0/18 |

They total $18/18 = 1$. Note $p(2,14) = 0$, since $|14 - 12 - 2| = 0$ — that cell drops out.

Now weight each cell by its product $xy$:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[XY] &= 10\left(\tfrac{2}{18}\right) + 12\left(\tfrac{4}{18}\right) + 14\left(\tfrac{6}{18}\right) \\
      &\quad + 20\left(\tfrac{4}{18}\right) + 24\left(\tfrac{2}{18}\right) + 28(0) \\
      &= \frac{20 + 48 + 84 + 80 + 48}{18} \\
      &= \frac{280}{18} \\
      &= \mathbf{\frac{140}{9}}
\end{align*}
$$

The absolute value is what makes the table asymmetric, and it is easy to drop a sign and get a negative entry — every probability must come out non-negative, and the row totals must reach 1, which is the check to run before computing anything.
