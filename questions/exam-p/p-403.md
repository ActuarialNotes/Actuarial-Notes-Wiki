Two components of a loss have joint density

$$f(x,y) = 24xy, \qquad x > 0,\ y > 0,\ x + y < 1$$

Calculate $P(X > 0.5)$.

- A) $1/16$
- B) $3/16$
- C) $5/16$
- D) $1/4$
- E) $1/2$

## Explanation

The support is the triangle bounded by the axes and the line $x + y = 1$. First find the marginal density of $X$; holding $x$ fixed, $y$ runs from $0$ to $1-x$.

$$
\begin{align*}
f_X(x) &= \int_0^{1-x} 24xy\,dy \\
       &= 24x\left[\frac{y^2}{2}\right]_0^{1-x} \\
       &= 12x(1-x)^2, \qquad 0 < x < 1
\end{align*}
$$

Now integrate over the required range:

$$
\begin{align*}
P(X > 0.5) &= \int_{0.5}^{1} 12x(1-x)^2\,dx \\
           &= 12\int_{0.5}^{1}\left(x - 2x^2 + x^3\right) dx \\
           &= 12\left[\frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{2x^3}{3} + \frac{x^4}{4}\right]_{0.5}^{1} \\
           &= 12\left[0.083333 - 0.057292\right] \\
           &= 12(0.026042) \\
           &= \mathbf{\frac{5}{16}} = 0.3125
\end{align*}
$$

Integrating $y$ from $0$ to $1$ instead of $0$ to $1-x$ ignores the constraint $x+y<1$ and gives a density that does not integrate to 1.
