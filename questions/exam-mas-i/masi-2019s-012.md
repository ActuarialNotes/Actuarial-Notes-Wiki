You are given the following information for a whole life annuity-due of 10,000 on (x) that is payable annually:

- $q_x = 0.010$
- $q_{x+1} = 0.012$
- Interest rate $i = 0.06$
- $\ddot{a}_{x+1} = 10.905$

Calculate the absolute change in the actuarial present value of this annuity due if $p_{x+1}$ is decreased by 0.01.

- A) Less than 900
- B) At least 900, but less than 925
- C) At least 925, but less than 950
- D) At least 950, but less than 975
- E) At least 975

## Explanation

Use the annuity-due recursion $\ddot{a}_x = 1 + v\,p_x\,\ddot{a}_{x+1}$. Changing $p_{x+1}$ changes $\ddot{a}_{x+1}$, and through it $\ddot{a}_x$, but not $\ddot{a}_{x+2}$ — so first back out $\ddot{a}_{x+2}$ from the given values, with $p_{x+1} = 1 - 0.012 = 0.988$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\ddot{a}_{x+1} &= 1 + v\,p_{x+1}\,\ddot{a}_{x+2} \\
\ddot{a}_{x+2} &= \frac{(10.905 - 1)(1.06)}{0.988} \\
&= 10.6268
\end{align*}
$$

Lowering $p_{x+1}$ by 0.01 (to 0.978) changes $\ddot{a}_{x+1}$ by

$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta\ddot{a}_{x+1} &= v\,(\Delta p_{x+1})\,\ddot{a}_{x+2} \\
&= \frac{-0.01}{1.06}(10.6268) \\
&= -0.10025
\end{align*}
$$

and $\ddot{a}_x$ by $v\,p_x$ times that, with $p_x = 1 - 0.010 = 0.99$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta\ddot{a}_x &= v\,p_x\,\Delta\ddot{a}_{x+1} \\
&= \frac{0.99}{1.06}(-0.10025) \\
&= -0.093633
\end{align*}
$$

For an annuity of 10,000 the actuarial present value falls by $10{,}000 \times 0.093633 = 936.3$.

This falls in $[925, 950)$, so the answer is **C**.
