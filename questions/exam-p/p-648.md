$X$ is a random variable with cumulative distribution function

$$
F(x) = \begin{cases}
0, & x < 0 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{x^2}{25}, & 0 \le x < 5 \\[4pt]
1, & x \ge 5.
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the second moment of $X$.

- A) $1.39$
- B) $3.33$
- C) $6.25$
- D) $12.50$
- E) $25.00$

## Explanation

Differentiate the cdf to get the density:

$$
f(x) = F'(x) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{2x}{25}, & \text{for } 0 \le x \le 5 \\[4pt]
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

The second moment is $E[X^2]$:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X^2] &= \int_0^5 x^2\left(\frac{2x}{25}\right) dx = \int_0^5 \frac{2x^3}{25}\,dx \\
       &= \left.\frac{2x^4}{100}\right|_0^5 \\
       &= \frac{2(625)}{100} \\
       &= \mathbf{12.50}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (B), 3.33, is the *first* moment $E[X] = 10/3$. Choice (A), 1.39, is the variance, $12.50 - (10/3)^2 = 1.389$. The "second moment" without qualification means $E[X^2]$ about the origin — the variance is the second moment about the *mean*, and here the two differ by a factor of exactly nine: $\tfrac{25}{2} \big/ \tfrac{25}{18} = 9$.
