This document outlines the retirement benefits (RET)-track exams and modules syllabuses. The Canadian Institute of Actuaries' syllabus for one of the five education tracks to its Fellowship (FCIA) designation: the track's 3 exams — each an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses, with the weight of each topic in parentheses and the level of each objective on the revised Bloom's taxonomy — and its two online modules.

## Retirement benefits track exams
- Exam F1RET: Pension Funding and Regulation
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Retirement benefits and pension mathematics (30%)
    - 3. Pension legislation (30%)
    - 4. Income tax legislation and regulation (30%)
    - 5. Professionalism and standards of practice relative to plan funding (10%)
- Exam F2RET: Pension Financial Reporting
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Accounting standards (30%)
    - 3. Reporting of pension, post-employment benefits, and post-retirement benefits for financial statement and proxy circular purposes (25%)
    - 4. Investments and asset allocation (20%)
    - 5. Pension governance and fiduciary duties (15%)
    - 6. Professionalism and standards of practice relative to plan accounting (10%)
- Exam F3RET: Pension Risk Management
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. ERM concept, framework, and process (15%)
    - 3. Risk categories and risk identification (15%)
    - 4. Investment risk management (25%)
    - 5. Risk management and financial economics (10%)
    - 6. Mathematics of financial risks (10%)
    - 7. Pension plan de-risking (25%)
