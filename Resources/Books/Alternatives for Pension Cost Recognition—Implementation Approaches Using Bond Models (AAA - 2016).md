An American Academy of Actuaries issue brief, dated December 2016, that extends an August 2015 brief on using discount rates from a full yield curve instead of a single aggregated discount rate to develop pension costs. It looks at developing a yield curve, and the associated spot rates, from a bond model, and explores five different approaches, each with different attributes to be considered. It is provided to educate actuaries on the issues involved and is not an actuarial standard of practice.
