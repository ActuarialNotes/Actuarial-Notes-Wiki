## Part a (1.25 points)

Identify the five conditions that must be met by a provincial health care program to be eligible for unreduced federal funding, as outlined in the Canada Health Act.

### Explanation
- Publicly administered
- Comprehensive
- Portable
- Universality
- Accessibility

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know the necessary conditions for a provincial health care system to receive unreduced funding by the federal government.

Candidates were expected to list or give a description of all five conditions from the text to get credit.
There were no common errors.

## Part b (1 point)

For each change below, identify which, if any, of the conditions from part a. above are not met.

- i. Introducing a per-visit dollar deductible
- ii. Privatizing the program
- iii. Removing extended health care services
- iv. Reverting coverage for out-of-province residents to their home province

### Explanation
- i. Accessibility
- ii. Public administration
- iii. All conditions met
- iv. Portability

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to state which criterion from part a., if any, was not met.
A common error was:

- Not identifying that all conditions in sub-part iii. were met
