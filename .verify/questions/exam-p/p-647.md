## [F-001] Explanation gives a wrong reason for the size of the shift from 1/6

- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, final paragraph
- claim: The bias only moves the answer from 0.167 to 0.204 because it acts on a single face
- evidence: The heads die reweights all six faces, not one: the explanation itself sets each odd face to 1/9 and each even face to 2/9. P[X=4] = p(2/9) + (1-p)(1/6) = 1/6 + p/18, so the move from 0.167 is p/18 = 0.666/18 = 0.037, driven by P[Heads] and the 2/9 vs 1/6 gap, not by the bias acting on a single face. SOA Exam P Sample Solutions (Aug 2026 rev.) Q647, p.184 derives P[X=4] = 0.666(2/9) + (1 - 0.666)(1/6) = 0.204 with no such claim. Answer and key (C) unaffected.
