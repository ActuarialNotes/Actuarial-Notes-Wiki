## [F-001] Correlation test superseded by Venter's 2020 erratum
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:claude-code
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:48Z/798e
- date: 2026-09-27

- locus: Part a, explanation and examiner report conclusion
- claim: The worked answer compares T = 2.321 with the one-tailed t-statistic for 0.90 at 2 degrees of freedom (1.89) and concludes the age-to-age factors are significantly correlated at the 10% level.
- evidence: Errata to Testing the Assumptions of Age-to-Age Factors (Venter, PCAS 1998), Version 1.0, January 31, 2020 (https://forum.casact.org/article/164288-testing-the-assumptions-of-age-to-age-factors/attachment/351760.pdf), amends page 833: because correlations can be positive or negative the test is two-tailed, and |T| is compared with the t-statistic for 0.95 at n - 2 degrees of freedom for significance at the 10% level. At 2 df that value is 2.920, and 2.321 < 2.920, so under the current syllabus reading the correlation is not significant. The question faithfully transcribes the 2014 paper and examiner's report, which predate the erratum.
- proposed_action: Keep the transcription; add a note after the examiner report that under the 2020 erratum the two-tailed critical value is 2.920 and the conclusion reverses.
