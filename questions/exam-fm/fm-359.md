A perpetuity pays $X$ at the end of the third year, $2X$ at the end of the sixth year, $3X$ at the end of the ninth year, etc.

At a 6% annual effective interest rate, the present value of the perpetuity is 655.56.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 2
- B) 17
- C) 20
- D) 33
- E) 39

## Explanation

The payments come every three years, so work in three-year periods. First calculate the three-year interest rate:

$$
\begin{align*}
j &= 1.06^{3} - 1 \\
  &= 0.191016
\end{align*}
$$

Measured in three-year periods, the payments $X, 2X, 3X, \ldots$ are made at the ends of periods $1, 2, 3, \ldots$, so this is an arithmetic increasing perpetuity-immediate. Its present value is $X\left[\dfrac{1}{j} + \dfrac{1}{j^{2}}\right]$:

$$
\begin{align*}
X\left[\frac{1}{0.191016} + \frac{1}{0.191016^{2}}\right] &= 655.56 \\
X(5.235164 + 27.406937) &= 655.56 \\
32.642101X &= 655.56 \\
X &= 20.08
\end{align*}
$$
