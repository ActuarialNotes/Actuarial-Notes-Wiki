## [F-001] Stem rewrites SOA's given dP/di = -700 as 'price decreases to -700 when the yield increases by 1%' and adds a spurious 'first-order Macaulay approximation'
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: major
- status: open
- locus: stem, lines 27-31
- claim: 'The yield to maturity is 8%. Using the first-order Macaulay approximation, the duration of the bond is calculated to be D. Given that the price decreases to -700 when the yield increases by 1%, calculate the Macaulay duration D. (Note: the price sensitivity dP/di ≈ -700 at the current yield.)'
- evidence: SOA Q30 (questions PDF p.15, page image): 'The current price of an annual coupon bond is 100. The yield to maturity is an annual effective rate of 8%. The derivative of the price of the bond with respect to the yield to maturity is –700. Using the bond's yield rate, calculate the Macaulay duration of the bond in years.' The vault (a) states a price that 'decreases to -700', impossible for a price of 100 and not what SOA gives; read as a finite change of 700 per 1% it implies dP/di = -70,000 and D = 756, not an option; (b) adds 'Using the first-order Macaulay approximation', which is the price-approximation formula P(i) ≈ P(i0)((1+i0)/(1+i))^D, not how D is obtained here - taking the finite reading P(9%) = 93 with that formula gives D = ln(0.93)/ln(1.08/1.09) = 7.89, again not an option; (c) drops 'an annual effective rate of', the basis of the (1+i) factor; (d) turns SOA's exact derivative into an approximation ('≈'). Only the parenthetical note carries SOA's given; with it, D = 700(1.08)/100 = 7.56 = SOA's key C (solutions PDF p.10), so the key remains reachable.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: Replace the stem paragraphs (lines 27-31) with SOA's wording verbatim: 'The current price of an annual coupon bond is 100. The yield to maturity is an annual effective rate of 8%. The derivative of the price of the bond with respect to the yield to maturity is –700. Using the bond's yield rate, calculate the Macaulay duration of the bond in years.' (whole-stem restoration - maintainer's call).
