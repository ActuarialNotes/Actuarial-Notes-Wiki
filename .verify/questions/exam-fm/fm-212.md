## [F-001] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 41 and 45-47
- claim: Math spans write 12,000, 1,200, 37,661.14 and 16,276.86 with a bare comma, which KaTeX typesets as punctuation ('12, 000').
- evidence: In TeX/KaTeX math a bare ',' is a punctuation atom followed by a thin space; the rest of the FM bank writes {,} (e.g. fm-211 383{,}404.42). Values themselves are right: 12000 x 1.1^12 = 37,661.14; s_12 @10% = 21.38428; difference 16,276.86 (matches SOA solutions p.54).
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Write the separators as {,} inside math.
- applied: true
