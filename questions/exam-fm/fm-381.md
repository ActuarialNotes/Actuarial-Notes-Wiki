You have decided to invest in a newly issued 20-year bond with annual coupons and the following characteristics:

- i) The price at issue is 1321.
- ii) The face amount is 1111.
- iii) The annual coupon rate is 10%.
- iv) The bond is callable for 1111 immediately after the payment of either the 18th or 19th coupon.

Calculate the minimum possible annual effective yield rate that you can earn.

- A) 7.881%
- B) 7.937%
- C) 7.985%
- D) 8.028%
- E) 8.065%

## Explanation

The bond is bought at a premium (the price of 1321 exceeds the redemption amount of 1111), so the yield is lowest when the bond is redeemed as early as possible. The minimum yield therefore occurs if the bond is called immediately after the 18th coupon, so $n = 18$.

The annual coupon is $0.10(1111) = 111.10$, and the minimum yield rate $i$ solves

$$1321 = 111.10\,a_{\overline{18}|i} + 1111v^{18}$$

Solving numerically (for example, with a financial calculator) gives

$$i = 0.07985$$

that is, a minimum annual effective yield rate of 7.985%.
