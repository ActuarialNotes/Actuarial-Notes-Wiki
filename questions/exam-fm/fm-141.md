Bond A and Bond B are both annual coupon, five-year, 10,000 par value bonds bought to yield an annual effective rate of 4%.

  i) Bond A has an annual coupon rate of $r\%$, a redemption value that is 10% below par, and a price of $P$.
  ii) Bond B has an annual coupon rate of $(r+1)\%$, a redemption value that is 10% above par, and a price of $1.2P$.

Calculate $r\%$.

- A) 5.85%
- B) 6.85%
- C) 7.85%
- D) 8.85%
- E) 9.85%

## Explanation

Bond A:
$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 10{,}000r \cdot a_{\overline{5}|0.04} + 9{,}000(1.04)^{-5} \\
  &= 44{,}518.22r + 7{,}397.34
\end{align*}
$$

Bond B:
$$
\begin{align*}
1.2P &= 10{,}000(r+0.01)a_{\overline{5}|0.04} + 11{,}000(1.04)^{-5} \\
&= 44{,}518.22(r+0.01) + 9{,}041.20 \\
&= 44{,}518.22r + 9{,}486.38
\end{align*}
$$

Subtracting the Bond A equation from the Bond B equation:
$$
\begin{align*}
0.2P &= 2{,}089.04 \\
P &= 10{,}445.20
\end{align*}
$$

Substituting back:
$$
\begin{align*}
10{,}445.20 &= 44{,}518.22r + 7{,}397.34 \\
r &= \frac{3{,}048}{44{,}518} \\
  &= 0.0685 \\
  &= 6.85\%
\end{align*}
$$
