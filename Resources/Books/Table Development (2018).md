![[Table Development (2018) - Cover.svg]]

A February 2018 Society of Actuaries paper, sponsored by its Experience Studies Executive Committee, documenting the process of creating experience-based tables for mortality, lapse, incidence, severity and other rates used to predict outcomes for life, health, annuity and pension products. It is intended to serve as a reference manual for the development of future tables, documenting common and alternative practices.
