![[IFRS 17 Insurance Contract Examples - Cover.svg]]

An Excel model listed in the Society of Actuaries' CP 321 and GH 201C syllabi, added to the CP 321 syllabus for the Nov 2026 sitting to give a numerical illustration of how [[IFRS 17|IFRS 17]] accounting functions in practice. According to the CP 321 course strategy guide, it demonstrates how the basics of IFRS 17 are applied to a simple renewable Term 10 product using the General Measurement Model (GMM) approach, and candidates should be prepared to replicate its mechanics in an assessment setting.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Outcomes 4b, 4d; listed without a chapter or section restriction.
> - [[Exam GH-201C (SOA)|Exam GH-201C]] — Outcomes 2e, 2f; listed without a chapter or section restriction.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> The model is distributed by the SOA with the course materials and no copy could be found online, so its worksheets and contents could not be read. This page records its citation and assignment from the syllabi and the CP 321 course strategy guide.
