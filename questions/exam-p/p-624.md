An insurance company offers two kinds of insurance: automobile insurance and homeowners insurance. This year the company's profit from automobile insurance is normally distributed with a mean of 400 and a standard deviation of 200. Also this year, the company's profit from homeowners insurance is normally distributed with a mean of $-100$ and a standard deviation of 500. The two profits are independent.

Calculate the probability that the company's overall profit this year is positive.

- A) $0.41$
- B) $0.59$
- C) $0.63$
- D) $0.67$
- E) $0.71$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the automobile profit and $Y$ the homeowners profit. Means always add:

$$
E(X + Y) = 400 - 100 = 300
$$

Because the two are independent, the *variances* add:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X + Y) &= 200^2 + 500^2 = 40{,}000 + 250{,}000 = 290{,}000 \\
\text{SD}(X + Y)  &= \sqrt{290{,}000} = 538.5165
\end{align*}
$$

A sum of independent normals is itself normal, so

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X + Y > 0] &= P\!\left[Z > \frac{0 - 300}{538.5165}\right] \\
             &= P[Z > -0.5571] \\
             &= \mathbf{0.7113}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (D), 0.67, adds the standard deviations instead: $200 + 500 = 700$ gives $P[Z > -0.4286] = 0.6659$. Adding standard deviations always overstates the spread of independent variables — 700 against the correct 538.5 — and a wider spread drags the probability of a positive total back toward 0.50.
