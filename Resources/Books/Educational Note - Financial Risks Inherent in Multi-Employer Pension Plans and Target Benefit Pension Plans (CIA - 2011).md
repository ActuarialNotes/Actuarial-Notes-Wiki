A May 2011 educational note of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries, prepared by its Task Force on MEPP/TBPP Funding, on the financial risks inherent in multi-employer pension plans (MEPPs) and target benefit pension plans (TBPPs). It gives actuaries guidance in addressing these risks, filling a gap in the actuarial literature on plans in which plan experience can have a direct effect on the benefits to plan participants. The CIA has archived it (May 14, 2024).

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — Outcome 2b; the whole note.

## 1 Introduction
- Under both MEPPs and TBPPs, contributions are fixed, and emerging plan experience may cause the benefit levels to be adjusted, upward for favourable experience and downward for unfavourable experience.
- The risks are typically borne by plan members through changes in benefit levels, not by the participating employers, and are spread across the membership rather than assumed by each member.
