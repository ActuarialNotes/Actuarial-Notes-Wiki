Bond A and bond B are both annual-coupon bonds.

Bond A is an $m$-year bond with face amount 1000 and price 800, where $m$ is a whole number. The coupon rate is half of the yield rate.

Bond B is a $3m$-year bond with the same face amount, coupon rate, and yield rate as bond A.

Calculate the price for bond B.

- A) 400
- B) 512
- C) 608
- D) 671
- E) 716

## Explanation

Let $i$ be the yield rate. The coupon rate is $i/2$, so each annual coupon is $1000(i/2)$. Using $i\,a_{\overline{m}|i} = 1 - v^{m}$, for bond A:

$$
\begin{align*}
800 &= 1000(i/2)\,a_{\overline{m}|i} + 1000v^{m} \\
    &= 500(1 - v^{m}) + 1000v^{m} \\
    &= 500 + 500v^{m} \\
v^{m} &= 0.6
\end{align*}
$$

Bond B has the same face amount, coupon rate and yield rate, and a term of $3m$ years. The price of bond B is:

$$
\begin{align*}
P_B &= 1000(i/2)\,a_{\overline{3m}|i} + 1000v^{3m} \\
    &= 500(1 - v^{3m}) + 1000v^{3m} \\
    &= 500(1 + v^{3m}) \\
    &= 500(1 + 0.6^{3}) \\
    &= 608
\end{align*}
$$
