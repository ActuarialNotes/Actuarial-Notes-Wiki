![[Managing and Evaluating Healthcare Intervention Programs (Duncan - 2014) - Cover.svg]]

A textbook on the financial management and evaluation of healthcare intervention programs, which its publisher describes as the first textbook devoted to the subject. The second edition is in four parts: an introduction to care management interventions, actuarial issues in care management interventions, practical applications, and wellness, integrated programs and other new developments.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-301 (SOA)|Exam GH-301]] — Disease Management; Ch. 3: Care Management Programs and Interventions (2a); Ch. 8: Understanding the Economics of Care Management Programs (2b); Ch. 9: Applying the Economic Model: The Example of Opportunity Analysis (2b); Ch. 11: The Use of Propensity Scoring in Program Evaluation (2b); Ch. 12: An Actuarial Method for Evaluating Care Management Outcomes, excluding Appendix 12.2-12.3 (2c); Ch. 13: Understanding Patient Risk and Its Impact on Chronic and Non-Chronic Member Trends (2d); Ch. 16: Testing Actuarial Methods for Evaluating Disease Management Savings Outcomes, excluding Appendices (2b).
