The following information is available from a property and casualty insurance company's December 31, 2013 P&C-1. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

**Liabilities and Equity (Page 20.20)**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Shares issued and paid | 900 |
| Contributed Surplus | 150 |
| Retained Earnings | 720 |
| Reserves | 50 |
| Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (Loss) | 200 |

**Accumulated Other Comprehensive Income (Loss) (Page 20.42)**

| Accumulated Gains (Losses) on: | Amount |
|---|---|
| Available for Sale: Loans | 10 |
| Available for Sale: Bonds and Debentures | 90 |
| Available for Sale: Equities | 45 |
| Other | 55 |
| Balance at End of Period | 200 |

| Line of Business | Net Written Premium | Net Unearned Premium | Net Unpaid Claims excluding PfAD | Margin on Unearned Premium | Margin on Unpaid Claims |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Property | 1,500 | 900 | 300 | 8% | 5% |
| Liability | 2,500 | 1,500 | 1,875 | 8% | 15% |

Assume that the following components of Capital Required have a non-zero minimum capital amount:

- Balance Sheet Assets
- Unearned Premiums/Unpaid Claims/Premium Deficiencies

Assume there is no Premium Deficiency.

## Part a (2 points)

Calculate the capital required for Balance Sheet Assets to achieve OSFI's supervisory ratio.

### Explanation
Capital available $=$ (Equity $-$ AOCI) $+$ (Accumulated gains/losses on bonds $+$ equities):

$$= (900 + 150 + 720 + 50) + (90 + 45) = 1820 + 135 = 1955$$

Capital required for unearned premium:

$$= 0.08 \times \left(\max(0.5 \times 1500,\ 900) + \max(0.5 \times 2500,\ 1500)\right) = 0.08 \times (900 + 1500) = 192$$

Capital required for unpaid claims $= 300 \times 0.05 + 1875 \times 0.15 = 296.25$

To meet supervisory target, Capital available / capital required $= 150\%$:

$$150\% = \frac{1955}{\text{Cap. Required for B/S assets} + 296.25 + 192}$$

**Cap. Required for B/S assets = \$815.08** (in \$000s)

### Examiner Report
- Candidates were expected to know how to calculate MCT based on P&C-1 information. It was also expected that candidates would be able to isolate the capital required for Balance Sheet Assets
- Candidates performed generally well on this question.
- One common mistake was to include the available for sale loans in the capital available.

## Part b (0.75 points)

In addition to Balance Sheet Assets, Unearned Premiums, Unpaid Claims and Premium Deficiencies, identify three other main components of the Minimum Capital Required formula.

### Explanation
- Interest Rate Risk

- Reinsurance ceded to unregistered reinsurers

- Catastrophes

Other accepted answers:

- Investment risk

- Structured Settlements, Letters of Credit, Derivates and Other Exposures

- Off-balance sheet risks

### Examiner Report
- Candidates are expected to know the main components of Minimum Capital Required.
- Many candidates got full marks on this part.
- The following answers were not given credit:
  - Inflation risk
  - Capital required for *registered* reinsurers
  - Hedging activities
