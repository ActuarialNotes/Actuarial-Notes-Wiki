Chapter 10 of OSFI's Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test (LICAT) guideline, dated January 1, 2025 (issued November 21, 2024), in the Capital Adequacy Requirements category. It describes the treatment of reinsurance in the determination of the LICAT ratios, collateral requirements for unregistered reinsurance, and the conditions necessary in order for an insurer to take credit for reinsurance. For calculating reinsurance credits on segregated fund guarantees, Restated Liabilities are used in lieu of Best Estimate Liabilities.

## 10.1 Definitions
- 10.1.1 [[Registered Reinsurance|Registered reinsurance]]
    - An arrangement is deemed to constitute registered reinsurance if it is conducted with a registered reinsurer, which OSFI considers a federally incorporated or foreign reinsurer authorized by order of the Superintendent, or a provincially/territorially regulated insurer approved by the Superintendent.
- 10.1.2 [[Unregistered Reinsurance|Unregistered reinsurance]]
    - An entity that is not a registered reinsurer is an unregistered reinsurer, and special purpose vehicles formed for the purpose of securitizing insurance risks are considered to be unregistered reinsurers.
    - Reinsurance with an unregistered reinsurer is treated as unregistered reinsurance unless it meets all of the conditions the section lists, in which case it is deemed registered reinsurance.
