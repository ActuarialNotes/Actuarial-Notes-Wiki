A March 2007 public policy paper of the American Academy of Actuaries on health premium deficiency reserves (PDRs). It was written to give insight into the testing for the need for a PDR, the manner in which a PDR, if needed, might be determined, and some details of its financial reporting; it describes practices the work group believes are commonly employed and is not a codification of generally accepted actuarial practice.
