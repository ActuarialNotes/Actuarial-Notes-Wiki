A Society of Actuaries Research Institute report on decentralized insurance alternatives, a fast-growing sector of decentralized finance (DeFi) in which blockchain-based platforms enable peer-to-peer risk sharing. Its purpose is to review the business models and operating designs of these platforms, which may have significant implications for traditional insurance and the actuarial profession. It focuses on products that cover risk events unique to DeFi, such as smart contract exploits and the de-pegging of stablecoins.
