A guide for practitioners that presents the mathematical and statistical framework necessary to develop stochastic models in any setting, insurance or otherwise, without requiring advanced mathematics or statistics. It covers techniques such as Monte Carlo simulation and lattice models, risk metrics such as Value at Risk and Conditional Tail Expectation, stochastic scenario generation for key risk factors, model calibration and validation, model review and communication of results, and case studies of life and non-life insurance companies. The monograph is sponsored by the International Actuarial Association; its author team is Milliman, Inc.
