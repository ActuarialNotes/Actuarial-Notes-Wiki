A white paper by the Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Risk & Security Working Group (AIRS) that provides its views on potential approaches to AI governance for financial services, including potential risks, risk categorization, interpretability, discrimination and risk mitigation, as applied to the financial industry. It is intended for discussion purposes only and is not a prescriptive roadmap for implementing AI/ML tools.
