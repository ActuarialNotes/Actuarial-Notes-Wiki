The Facility Association accomplishes its mission of ensuring automobile insurance availability through two types of risk sharing mechanism: risk sharing pools (RSP) and the Facility Association residual market (FARM).

## Part a (0.5 points)

Briefly describe the key purpose of each risk sharing mechanism: RSP and FARM.

### Explanation
Sample answers include the following:

- FARM: provide auto insurance to drivers who can't find it in the voluntary market. RSP:
allow insureds to cede risks on their own books for which they consider premiums to be inadequate
- RSP: allows insurers to write a riskier set of insureds and pool the costs to limit their losses.
FARM: is an insurer of last resort, ensuring that compulsory insurance can be sold to risks that no insurer will write

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know the purposes of the risk sharing pool and the Facility Association residual market.

A common error was:

- Not commenting on the transfer of risk when describing the purpose of the risk sharing mechanism. For example:
  - RSP allows insurer to accept risk from risky insured leading to greater availability
  - RSP ensures all PPV drivers have insurance coverage

## Part b (2 points)

Compare and contrast RSP and FARM with respect to:

- i. Rates and rules
- ii. Servicing carriers
- iii. Policyholder awareness
- iv. Treatment of losses in Ontario Private Passenger Automobile rate filing

### Explanation
Sample answers include the following:

**i) rates and rules**

- RSP: rates are insurer's own standard manual rates. FARM: uses FA rates
- RSP: use insurer's rates and rules, FARM: uses FA's rates and rules
- RSP: uses the ceding company's rates and rules, FARM: has its own rates and rules

**ii) servicing carriers**

- RSP: serviced by the ceding company, FARM: selected companies service policies
- RSP: policies/claims administered by member, FARM: policies/claims administered by servicing companies
- RSP: insurer services policies and claim wording, FARM: may have 3rd party to service policies and claim handling

**iii) policyholder awareness**

- RSP: policyholder is unaware, FARM: policyholder know they are a FARM risk
- RSP: not aware of assignment, FARM: aware of assignment

**iv) treatment of losses in Ontario Private Passenger Automobile Filing**

- RSP: include, FARM: excluded as it's not business of the insurer
- RSP: included in loss analysis, FARM: excluded from loss analysis

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know the difference between the risk sharing pool and the Facility Association residual market.

Common errors were:

- For i) rates and rules:
  - No common errors were made; candidates performed well on this question
- For ii) servicing carriers:
  - Stating that FA takes risks directly from agents and brokers
  - Stating that FARM is the servicing carrier
- For iii) policyholder awareness:
  - No common errors were made; candidates performed well on this question
- For iv) treatment of losses in Ontario PPA filing:
  - Stating that for RSP - ceded risks are negative direct business and negative losses
  - Stating that for RSP – insurer are responsible for 15% of the losses, cede 85%,
  - Stating that for FARM – 100% of losses are transferred to FARM
  - Stating that for FARM claims are all paid according to the participation ratio of voluntary insurer's premium

## Part c (1 point)

Identify four classes of business that determine a member's participation in the Facility Association results.

### Explanation
Any four of the following;

- PPA non-fleet, non-pool business
- Based on business not in the PPA non-fleet non-pool category  or in any risk sharing pool
- Business ceded to the pool in AB, NB, NS (or vehicles in a risk sharing pool except Ontario)
- Business ceded to the pool in Ontario or vehicles transferred to a risk sharing pool other than a risk sharing pool in AB, NB, NS or ON cat fund

- Vehicles in a catastrophic claim or uninsured motorist pool

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to identify classes of business that determine a member's participation in the Facility Association results.
Common errors were:

- Providing PPA, non-fleet as a class when it should have been non-pool, non-fleet PPA
- Stating fleet, commercial auto, motorcycle were classes of business
- Providing the formula for the participation ratio
