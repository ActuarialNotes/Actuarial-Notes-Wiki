On any given day, Alan will play either one or two games of billiards.

- If he plays one game today, he'll play one game tomorrow with probability 0.6
- If he plays two games today, he'll play two games tomorrow with probability 0.7
- His probability of winning each game is 0.6

It is now Monday and Alan played two games today.

Calculate Alan's expected number of wins two days from now on Wednesday.

- A) Less than 0.90
- B) At least 0.90, but less than 0.95
- C) At least 0.95, but less than 1.00
- D) At least 1.00, but less than 1.05
- E) At least 1.05

## Explanation

The number of games Alan plays each day is a two-state Markov chain (state 1 = one game, state 2 = two games):

$$
P = \begin{pmatrix} 0.6 & 0.4 \\ 0.3 & 0.7 \end{pmatrix}
$$

He is in state 2 on Monday, and Wednesday is two transitions away, so we need the second row of $P^2$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P^{(2)}_{2,1} &= (0.3)(0.6) + (0.7)(0.3) \\
&= 0.39 \\
P^{(2)}_{2,2} &= (0.3)(0.4) + (0.7)(0.7) \\
&= 0.61
\end{align*}
$$

Each game is won with probability 0.6, so the expected number of wins is 0.6 times the expected number of games:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[\text{wins on Wednesday}] &= 0.6\,\big[0.39(1) + 0.61(2)\big] \\
&= 0.6(1.61) \\
&= 0.966
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.95, 1.00)$, so the answer is **C**.
