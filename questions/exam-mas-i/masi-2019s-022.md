Let $X$ be a Bernoulli random variable with probability of success $q$. You want to perform the following hypothesis test:

- $H_0\colon q = 0.75$
- $H_1\colon q > 0.75$

We will reject $H_0$ in favor of $H_1$ if $\sum_{i=1}^{30} X_i \ge k$.

Calculate the smallest $k$ such that significance level of this test will be at most 0.02.

- A) Fewer than 27
- B) 27
- C) 28
- D) 29
- E) At least 30

## Explanation

The significance level is the probability of rejecting $H_0$ when it is true: $\Pr(S \ge k)$ where $S = \sum_{i=1}^{30} X_i \sim \text{Binomial}(30, 0.75)$. Raising $k$ shrinks the rejection region, so add up the upper tail from $S = 30$ downward until it passes 0.02, using

$$
\Pr(S = s) = \binom{30}{s}(0.75)^{s}(0.25)^{30-s}
$$

| $s$ | $\Pr(S = s)$ | $\Pr(S \ge s)$ |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 0.000179 | 0.000179 |
| 29 | 0.001786 | 0.001964 |
| 28 | 0.008631 | 0.010596 |
| 27 | 0.026853 | 0.037449 |

With $k = 27$ the significance level is 0.0374, above 0.02; with $k = 28$ it is 0.0106, at most 0.02. The smallest such $k$ is 28, so the answer is **C**.
