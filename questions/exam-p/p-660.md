A motorist decides not to renew a car insurance policy. The number of months until the motorist is charged for driving without insurance is exponentially distributed with mean 2.50.

Calculate the probability that the motorist lasts at least 2.50 months without being charged.

- A) $1 - e^{-0.4}$
- B) $e^{-1}$
- C) $1 - e^{-1}$
- D) $e^{-0.4}$
- E) $1 - e^{-6.25}$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the number of months until the motorist is charged. With mean 2.50 the rate is

$$
\lambda = \frac{1}{2.5} = 0.4
$$

The exponential survival function is $P(X > x) = e^{-\lambda x} = e^{-x/2.5}$, so

$$
P(X > 2.5) = e^{-\frac{1}{2.5}(2.5)} = \mathbf{e^{-1}}
$$

which is 0.368.

Choice (D), $e^{-0.4}$, uses the rate 0.4 as the exponent without multiplying by the elapsed time of 2.5 months — it is $P(X > 1)$, the probability of lasting one month. Choice (C), $1 - e^{-1}$, is the cdf: the probability of *being* charged within 2.50 months, the complement of what is asked.

Worth noting: any exponential has probability $e^{-1} = 0.368$ of surviving past its own mean, whatever that mean happens to be.
