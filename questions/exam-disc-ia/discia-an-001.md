A regional insurer's statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 are complete, unbiased and correctly prepared, but they do not reach the bank that lends to it until October 2026, after the bank has renewed the loan. Under FASB Concepts Statement No. 8, which qualitative characteristic do these statements most clearly lack?

- A) Timeliness
- B) Neutrality
- C) Comparability
- D) Verifiability

## Explanation

Timeliness means having information available to decision makers in time to be capable of influencing their decisions. Statements that arrive after the lending decision has been made cannot influence it, however good they are. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — neutrality is about bias in selecting or presenting information. The statements are described as unbiased.
- **C** — comparability is about seeing similarities and differences between entities or periods. Nothing suggests methods changed or differ from other insurers.
- **D** — verifiability means independent observers could agree the depiction is faithful. Correctly prepared figures can be verified; the problem is when they arrive.
