A toolkit of standards of internal control for mitigating risk in organizations of any size, published by Wiley in July 2019 in its Wiley Corporate F&A series. The publisher describes internal control as a process for assuring achievement of an organization's objectives in operational effectiveness, financial reporting and compliance; the book is organized by business process, after a first chapter of background on internal controls.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CFE-101 (SOA)|Exam CFE-101]] — CFE101-112-25: Internal Controls Toolkit, Doxey, Ch. 1, pp. 11-17 & 27-35; outcomes 1.2c, 3.2b, 3.2f.
