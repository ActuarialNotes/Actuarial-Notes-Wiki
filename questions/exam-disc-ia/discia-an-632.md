A building owner compares a named perils causes of loss form with the open perils special form. Which statement correctly describes how the two differ in deciding a claim?

- A) Under both forms the insured must prove that a listed peril caused the loss
- B) The special form covers every cause of loss, including flood and earthquake
- C) Under the special form a loss is covered unless the insurer shows an exclusion applies
- D) The named perils form covers more causes of loss than the special form does

## Explanation

A named perils form lists the causes it covers, so the insured must show that a listed peril caused the loss. The special form covers direct physical loss unless the loss is excluded or limited, so a loss is covered unless the insurer shows that an exclusion or limitation applies. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — That is the named perils burden only; the special form has no list of perils.
- **B** — Open perils is not all risks: earth movement and water (including flood) are excluded under every form.
- **D** — The special form covers every peril the named forms list and more, such as theft.
