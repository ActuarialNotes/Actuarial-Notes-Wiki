Jack inherited a perpetuity-due, with annual payments of 15,000. He immediately exchanged the perpetuity for a 25-year annuity-due having the same present value. The annuity-due has annual payments of $X$.

All the present values are based on an annual effective interest rate of 10% for the first 10 years and 8% thereafter.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 16,942
- B) 17,384
- C) 17,434
- D) 17,520
- E) 18,989

## Explanation

**PV of perpetuity-due** at mixed rates (first payment now, remainder discounted):

$$
\begin{align*}
PV_{\text{perp}} &= \left[\frac{1}{0.10} + \left(\frac{1}{0.08}-\frac{1}{0.10}\right)\frac{1}{1.10^{10}}\right](15{,}000) + 15{,}000 \\
                 &= 179{,}457.87
\end{align*}
$$

**PV of 25-year annuity-due** at same rates:

$$X\left(\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|0.10} + \frac{\ddot{a}_{\overline{15}|0.08}}{1.10^{10}}\right) = 179{,}457.87$$

$$
\begin{align*}
X\left(6.759 + \frac{9.244}{1.10^{10}}\right) &= 179{,}458 \\
X &= 17{,}384
\end{align*}
$$
