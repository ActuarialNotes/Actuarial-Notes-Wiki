Five claims are randomly selected from a group of fifteen different claims, which consists of five workers compensation claims, four homeowner claims and six auto claims.

Calculate the probability that the five claims selected consist of one workers compensation claim, three homeowner claims and one auto claim.

- A) $0.025$
- B) $0.036$
- C) $0.040$
- D) $0.150$
- E) $0.213$

## Explanation

The claims are drawn without replacement from a population split into three types, so this is the multivariate form of the hypergeometric distribution. Choose the required number from each type independently and divide by the number of ways to choose any five claims:

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= \frac{\binom{5}{1}\binom{4}{3}\binom{6}{1}}{\binom{15}{5}} \\
  &= \frac{(5)(4)(6)}{3003} \\
  &= \frac{120}{3003} \\
  &= \mathbf{0.040}
\end{align*}
$$

The three numerator factors must use up exactly five claims, which is the check that the counts were read onto the right categories. Note there is no multinomial coefficient in front: the combinations already treat the claims as an unordered set, so multiplying by an ordering factor would double count.
