Facility Association's 2026 bulletins to its Ontario Manual of Rules and Rates, the rates and rules of the [[Residual Market|residual market]] for automobile insurance in Ontario. Each bulletin announces an update FA is implementing for new business and renewals — revised rates with the overall change, rule changes, rate group tables — and most attach a summary of approved rule changes setting each rule's current wording beside its approved wording. The bulletins are distributed by the Servicing Carrier, to whom enquiries are addressed.

## Various Rule Changes effective January 1, 2027 (New Business and Renewals)
- October 2026: various rule changes in the Commercial and Driver's Policy sections, with a summary of approved rule changes (e.g. Rule 207.J rating class for truckmen, Rules 708 and 709 on the Driver's Policy).

## 2026 Private Passenger CLEAR Rate Group Tables effective December 1, 2026 (New Business and Renewals)
- August 2026: the 2026 Private Passenger CLEAR rate group tables, with a range of 11–42 for Accident Benefits rate groups.

## Revised PPV and CV Rate Change + Various Rule Changes effective October 1, 2026 (NB) and November 1, 2026 (Renewals)
- August 2026: revised Private Passenger Vehicle rates, an overall change of +8.7%, and revised Commercial Vehicle rates, an overall change of +8.6%; rates may vary with individual policy circumstances.
- Various rule changes in the Private Passenger, Commercial, Public and Driver's Policy sections, summarized with each change's premium impact on existing policies (beginning with the General Section's service fee schedule).

## Private Passenger Rule Changes Effective August 1, 2026 (New Business and Renewals)
- March 2026: a review of the FARM Private Passenger rules to simplify, streamline and harmonize [[Underwriting Guidelines|underwriting rules]] across all jurisdictions, with a summary of approved rule changes (including Rule 100, Filed Underwriting Rules).

## Accident Benefit Reform and Various Rule Changes effective July 1, 2026 (New Business and Renewals)
- March 2026: the new Ontario reform of [[Statutory Accident Benefits|accident benefits]] (O. Reg. 34/10, Statutory Accident Benefits Schedule); the new standard SABS endorsement (OPCF 47R and OEF 47R); Gender X (Class 04); a changed threshold for a non-chargeable accident; and rate pages updated for the accident benefit changes.

## Revised Interurban Rates effective June 1, 2026 (New Business and Renewals)
- March 2026: revised Interurban Vehicle rates, an overall change of −4.7%.
