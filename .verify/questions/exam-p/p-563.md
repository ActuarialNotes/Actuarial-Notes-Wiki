## [F-001] Explanation gives the reverse reason for the size of the cap's effect
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: ## Explanation, last paragraph
- claim: the reduction is modest here because β is over half the cap
- evidence: For an exponential, E[min(X,d)] = beta(1 - e^{-d/beta}), so the cap removes the fraction e^{-d/beta} of the mean. Here d/beta = 2.5 ln 2 = 1.733 and e^{-1.733} = 2^{-2.5} = 0.1768 (577.08 falls to 475.06). The fraction removed GROWS as beta/d grows (beta = d gives e^{-1} = 0.368; beta = 2d gives e^{-0.5} = 0.607), so beta being a large share of the cap makes the reduction larger, not modest. The reduction is modest because the cap sits 1.73 means above zero, i.e. only 17.7% of losses exceed it. The final value 475.06 and key D are unaffected (SOA solution Q563, solutions PDF p.156: 475.06).
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Replace the clause with the correct reason (the cap is 1.73 means out, so only P[X>1000] = 2^-2.5 = 0.177 of losses are capped) or delete it; human edit to explanation prose.
- applied: false
