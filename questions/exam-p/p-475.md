Let $X$ represent the number of defective parts in a shipment of four.

$$
P[X \geq x] = \frac{1}{4}\left(1 - \frac{x-2}{3}\right)^2, \qquad \text{for } x = 1, 2, 3, 4.
$$

Calculate $E(X)$.

- A) $0.83$
- B) $0.96$
- C) $1.39$
- D) $1.81$
- E) $1.83$

## Explanation

What is given is a **survival** function, not a probability mass function, so convert it first. Evaluate it at each value:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X \geq 1] &= 0.444, & P[X \geq 2] &= 0.250 \\
P[X \geq 3] &= 0.111, & P[X \geq 4] &= 0.028
\end{align*}
$$

Successive differences give the point probabilities, with $x = 0$ coming from the complement and $x = 4$ from the last survival value:

$$
\begin{align*}
p(0) &= 1 - 0.444 = 0.556 \\
p(1) &= 0.444 - 0.250 = 0.194 \\
p(2) &= 0.250 - 0.111 = 0.139 \\
p(3) &= 0.111 - 0.028 = 0.083 \\
p(4) &= 0.028
\end{align*}
$$

These sum to 1, a useful check that no value was dropped — note $X = 0$ is possible even though the given formula starts at $x = 1$.

$$
\begin{align*}
E(X) &= 0(0.556) + 1(0.194) + 2(0.139) + 3(0.083) + 4(0.028) \\
     &= \mathbf{0.833}
\end{align*}
$$

Alternatively, for a non-negative integer variable $E[X] = \sum_{x \geq 1} P[X \geq x]$, which sums the four given values directly to the same 0.833 with no conversion at all.
