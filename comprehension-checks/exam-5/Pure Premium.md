A book has 20,000 earned car-years, 1,400 ultimate claims and \$7,700,000 of ultimate loss and LAE. Average premium is \$550. Which statement is correct?

- A) The pure premium is \$5,500, so the book is running a 91% loss ratio
- B) The pure premium is \$385 and cannot be checked against the loss ratio without the expense provisions
- C) The pure premium is \$385, which is a 70% loss ratio against the \$550 average premium
- D) The pure premium is \$385, so frequency must be 0.07 and severity \$5,500
