Ordinary least squares is the best linear unbiased estimator by the Gauss–Markov theorem. Which assumption is NOT needed for that result?

- A) Errors follow a Normal distribution
- B) Errors have constant variance
- C) Errors are uncorrelated with one another
- D) The mean is linear in the parameters

<!-- rationale: 1–3: all three are Gauss–Markov conditions; normality is needed only for exact tests and intervals, which is the usual confusion -->
