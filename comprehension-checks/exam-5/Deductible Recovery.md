Under a large-deductible program the insurer pays each claim in full and then bills the insured for the deductible portion. How does that receivable differ from salvage and subrogation?

- A) It is collected at once, so it needs no development analysis
- B) It adds to gross losses instead of reducing net losses
- C) It is owed by the insured, so an insolvent policyholder may never repay it
- D) It is recovered from a third party rather than from the policyholder
