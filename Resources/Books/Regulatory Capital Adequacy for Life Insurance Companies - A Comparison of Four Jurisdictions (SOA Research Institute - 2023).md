A research report, dated July 2023, that introduces the concept of capital and compares four regulatory capital regimes for life insurers: the NAIC's Risk-Based Capital (United States), LICAT (Canada), Solvency II (European Union) and the Bermuda Insurance Solvency framework. Each regime's methodology is explained with key terms and formulas and illustrated by an example, and the regimes are compared on one model portfolio under a baseline and two alternative scenarios.
