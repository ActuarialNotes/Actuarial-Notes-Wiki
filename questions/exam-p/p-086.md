The amounts of automobile losses reported to an insurance company are mutually independent, and each loss is uniformly distributed between 0 and 20,000. The company covers each such loss subject to a deductible of 5,000.

Calculate the probability that the total payout on 200 reported losses is between 1,000,000 and 1,200,000.

- A) 0.0803
- B) 0.1051
- C) 0.1799
- D) 0.8201
- E) 0.8575

## Explanation

Let $X$ be a single loss, $X \sim U(0, 20{,}000)$. Let $Y$ be the payout, $Y = \max(0, X - 5000)$.
We first find the mean and variance of $Y$.
$$
\begin{align*}
E[Y] &= \int_{5000}^{20{,}000} (x - 5000) \frac{1}{20{,}000} dx \\
     &= \frac{(x-5000)^2}{40{,}000} \Big|_{5000}^{20{,}000} \\
     &= \frac{15{,}000^2}{40{,}000} \\
     &= 5{,}625
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\begin{align*}
E[Y^2] &= \int_{5000}^{20{,}000} (x - 5000)^2 \frac{1}{20{,}000} dx \\
       &= \frac{(x-5000)^3}{60{,}000} \Big|_{5000}^{20{,}000} \\
       &= \frac{15{,}000^3}{60{,}000} \\
       &= 56{,}250{,}000
\end{align*}
$$
$$
\begin{align*}
Var(Y) &= 56{,}250{,}000 - 5625^2 \\
       &= 56{,}250{,}000 - 31{,}640{,}625 \\
       &= 24{,}609{,}375
\end{align*}
$$

Let $S = \sum_{i=1}^{200} Y_i$. By the CLT, $S$ is approximately normal.
$$\mu_S = 200 \times 5625 = 1{,}125{,}000$$
$$\sigma_S^2 = 200 \times 24{,}609{,}375 = 4{,}921{,}875{,}000$$
$$\sigma_S = \sqrt{4{,}921{,}875{,}000} \approx 70{,}156.08$$

We want $P(1{,}000{,}000 < S < 1{,}200{,}000)$. The lower bound is $125{,}000/70{,}156 = 1.7817$ standard deviations below the mean and the upper bound is $75{,}000/70{,}156 = 1.0690$ standard deviations above it:
$$
\begin{align*}
P\left(\frac{1{,}000{,}000 - 1{,}125{,}000}{70{,}156} < Z < \frac{1{,}200{,}000 - 1{,}125{,}000}{70{,}156}\right) &= P(-1.7817 < Z < 1.0690) \\
&= P(Z < 1.0690) - [1 - P(Z < 1.7817)]
\end{align*}
$$
From the standard normal table (interpolating), $P(Z < 1.0690) = 0.8575$ and $P(Z < 1.7817) = 0.9626$, so
$$P = 0.8575 - (1 - 0.9626) = 0.8201$$
