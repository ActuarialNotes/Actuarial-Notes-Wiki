- locus: Explanation, RAROC/EVA set-up
- claim: The explanation computes RAROC = [(P-E)(1+y) - PV(L)]/Capital with no investment income on the capital and the discounted loss ratio taken as losses valued at year end, giving A 20%, B 23.6% (EVA A 0.199M, B 0.252M) and recommending B when capital is constrained.
- evidence: The vault matches the CAS exactly (report p.97 model solution 1 RAROC 20% / 23.6%; booklet-solutions p.49 and report p.97 EVA 198.9k / 251.6k; arithmetic re-run: A 1.90896/9.5 = 0.2009, B 1.06161/4.5 = 0.2359). Only considering investment income on the policy hold supplied funds'); the vault keeps the first and leaves the second implicit. The vault's own Risk-Adjusted Performance page defines NOI as including investment income on the allocated capital, which is the other reading (rank 4). Cummins 2000 (RMIR, Wiley) was not reachable to settle which convention the reading uses.

- proposed_action: Maintainer: decide whether the explanation should quote CAS model solution 1's second assumption ('Only considering investment income on the policy hold supplied funds', as printed) so the key's convention is visible, and whether Risk-Adjusted Performance needs a note that the CAS key excludes investment income on capital.
