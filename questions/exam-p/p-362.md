At a certain airport, 1/6 of all scheduled flights are delayed. Assume that flight delays are mutually independent events.

Use the normal approximation (with continuity correction) to calculate the probability that at least 40 of the next 180 flights are delayed.

- A) $0.011$
- B) $0.014$
- C) $0.018$
- D) $0.023$
- E) $0.029$

## Explanation

The number of delayed flights $X$ is binomial with $n = 180$ and $p = 1/6$. Its mean and standard deviation are

$$
\begin{align*}
\mu    &= np = 180\left(\frac{1}{6}\right) = 30 \\
\sigma &= \sqrt{np(1-p)} = \sqrt{180\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)\left(\frac{5}{6}\right)} = \sqrt{25} = 5
\end{align*}
$$

$X$ is a count, so approximating it by a continuous normal curve needs a **continuity correction**: the discrete event $X \geq 40$ covers the bar starting at 39.5, so the boundary moves from 40 to 39.5.

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X \geq 40] &\approx P\left[Z > \frac{39.5 - 30}{5}\right] \\
             &= P[Z > 1.90] \\
             &= \mathbf{0.0287}
\end{align*}
$$

Without the correction the boundary would be $(40-30)/5 = 2.00$, giving 0.0228 — choice (D). The correction matters here precisely because $\sigma = 5$ is small enough that half a unit is a tenth of a standard deviation.
