A five-page CAPSA guidance, dated May 2019, that outlines the expectations where an actuary wants to use a life insurance company's annuity quotation to determine the solvency or hypothetical wind-up liabilities of the portion of a pension plan assumed to be settled by purchase of annuities. CAPSA describes itself as a national inter-jurisdictional association of pension regulators. The guidance applies to solvency or hypothetical wind-up valuations and may not be appropriate for actual wind-up valuations.

## Introduction
- Typically the liabilities are valued with the annuity proxy interest rate and mortality assumption of the most recent CIA guidance, combined with a mortality improvement scale and generally no adjustment for sub- or super-standard mortality.
- Paragraphs 3240.05.1, 3240.17 and 3330.16 of the CIA Standards of Practice call for single premium annuity rates for benefits assumed to be settled by purchase of annuities, so an actual quotation might reflect a more accurate estimate.
- The actuary is expected to consider the quotation whether its premium is lower or higher than the liability from CIA guidance, and to apply the approach consistently.
- The date of quotation should coincide with the valuation date; a quotation dated within six months of the valuation date may be considered valid, with the liabilities adjusted as described at the end of the guidance.

## 1 Information to be included in the quotation by the life insurance company
- Date of quotation
- Date of first contemplated annuity quote pension payment for those receiving immediate pensions
- Premium amount split between immediate and deferred pensions
- Confirmation that the quotation covers all plan provisions specified in the request to quote
- Confirmation that all administration fees related to the purchase of annuities are included in the premium amount
- Any conditions that might prevent the life insurance company from acting on the quotation
- An authorized signature to support the fact that this is indicative of a bona fide annuity quotation

## 2 Information to be included in the actuarial report
- Name of the life insurance company
- Date of quotation
- Solvency or hypothetical wind-up liabilities related to the quotation, disclosed separately from other liabilities
- A summary of the demographic information provided to the life insurance company
- A description of the adjustment to the quotation, if the quotation date is after but less than six months following the valuation date
- Notional liabilities that would have resulted from the CIA guidance with no adjustments to the mortality rates
- The underlying adjustment to the unadjusted mortality table, or to the annuity proxy rate, needed to reproduce the liabilities in the quotation
- The actuary's or the plan administrator's certification that all plan provision information given to the life insurance company is true and correct

## Adjustment to quotation
- Where the quotation date is not the valuation date, the liabilities to be included equal A x B, where A is the ratio of the liability calculated with the annuity proxy rate at the valuation date to that at the date of the quotation, and B is the single premium amount at the date of the quotation.
