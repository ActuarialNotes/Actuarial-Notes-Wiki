The following information is available for the valuation of premium liabilities as at December 31, 2016. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Net unearned premium booked | 55,000 |
| Gross unearned premium booked | 60,000 |
| Expected reinsurance costs | 6,000 |
| Net undiscounted loss ratio | 98% |
| Expected ULAE | 2,500 |
| Discount rate | 3% |
| MfAD for claims development | 10% |
| MfAD for reinsurance ceded | 1% |
| MfAD for investment return rates | 0.5% |
| Gross present value of losses and ULAE | 53,000 |
| Maintenance expense as a % of gross premium | 3% |
| Unearned reinsurance commissions | 2,300 |

Cumulative accident year payment pattern:

| Age (Months) | % Paid |
|---|---|
| 12 | 30% |
| 24 | 100% |

Calculate the premium deficiency, if any.

### Explanation
All amounts in \$000s.

Discount factors, with claims paid at mid-year and adjusted to the average accident date of the premium liabilities:

| Age (Months) | Claims Avg Payment | Payment in Year | Discount Factor at 3% | Discount Factor at 2.5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 0.5 | 30% | 29.56% | 29.63% |
| 24 | 1.5 | 70% | 66.96% | 67.45% |
| Total | | | **96.52%** | **97.08%** |
| Average date of future accident year | | | 0.500 | 0.500 |
| Average date of premium liabilities | | | 0.333 | 0.333 |
| Discount factor for premium liabilities | | | $96.52\% \times 1.03^{(0.5-0.333)} = 97.00\%$ | $97.08\% \times 1.025^{(0.5-0.333)} = 97.48\%$ |

Adjusted net unearned premium = net unearned premium booked - expected reinsurance costs:

$$55{,}000 - 6{,}000 = 49{,}000$$

Net undiscounted loss & ULAE = (adjusted net unearned premium) $\times$ (net undiscounted loss & ALAE ratio) + ULAE:

$$49{,}000 \times 98\% + 2{,}500 = 50{,}520$$

- Net discounted loss & LAE $= 50{,}520 \times 0.97 = 49{,}004$ (PV @ 3%)
- Claims PfAD $= 49{,}004 \times 10\% = 4{,}900$
- Reinsurance PfAD $= (53{,}000 - 49{,}004) \times 1\% = 39$ (gross PV of loss & LAE less net discounted loss & LAE)
- Investment rate PfAD $= (0.9748 - 0.97) \times 50{,}520 = 243$ (PV @ 2.5% less PV @ 3%, on net undiscounted loss & LAE)
- **Net APV** $= 49{,}004 + 4{,}900 + 39 + 243 = 54{,}187$
- Maintenance expense $= 3\% \times 60{,}000 = 1{,}800$

Policy liabilities in connection with UPR = net APV + expected reinsurance costs + maintenance expense:

$$54{,}187 + 6{,}000 + 1{,}800 = 61{,}987$$

Maximum allowable DPAE = max(0, net unearned premium - policy liabilities in connection with UPR + unearned reinsurance commission):

$$\max(0,\ 55{,}000 - 61{,}987 + 2{,}300) = 0$$

Premium deficiency = max(0, policy liabilities in connection with UPR - unearned reinsurance commission - net unearned premium):

$$\max(0,\ 61{,}987 - 55{,}000 - 2{,}300) = \mathbf{4{,}687}$$

Note that there is no premium deficiency or DPAE for facility given that net unearned premium = premium liabilities.

Alternatively:

Timing the claims on unearned premium by interpolating the payment pattern:

| Age (Months) | Claims on UP Average Payment (in years) | Interpolated Cumulative Payment Pattern | Interpolated Incremental Payment Pattern | Discounted to valuation date @ 3% | Discounted to valuation date @ 2.5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 0.2929 | 44.5% | 44.5% | 0.4411 | 0.4418 |
| 24 | 1.2929 | 100% | 55.5% | 0.5342 | 0.5376 |
| Total | | | | **0.9754** | **0.9794** |

- Adjusted net unearned premium $= 55{,}000 - 6{,}000 = 49{,}000$
- Net undiscounted loss & ULAE $= 49{,}000 \times 98\% + 2{,}500 = 50{,}520$
- Net discounted loss & LAE $= 50{,}520 \times 0.9754 = 49{,}277$
- Claims PfAD $= 49{,}227 \times 10\% = 4{,}928$ (as printed; $49{,}277 \times 10\% = 4{,}928$)
- Reinsurance PfAD $= (53{,}000 - 49{,}277) \times 1\% = 37$
- Investment rate PfAD $= (0.9794 - 0.9754) \times 50{,}520 = 202$
- Net APV $= 49{,}277 + 4{,}928 + 37 + 202 = 54{,}444$
- Maintenance expense $= 3\% \times 60{,}000 = 1{,}800$
- Policy liabilities in connection with UPR $= 54{,}444 + 6{,}000 + 1{,}800 = 62{,}244$
- Maximum allowable DPAE $= \max(0,\ 55{,}000 - 62{,}244 + 2{,}300) = 0$
- Premium deficiency $= \max(0,\ 62{,}244 - 55{,}000 - 2{,}300) = \mathbf{4{,}944}$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to demonstrate how to calculate discount factor, how to calculate premium liabilities and maximum deferrable policy acquisition expense, and how to determine whether there is premium deficiency.
Common errors include:

- Discount factors: not calculating the average accident date associated with the premium liability properly and simply discounting to the middle of the year
- Net APV calculation: not including the ULAE in the calculation or not subtracting the expected reinsurance costs
- Maintenance expenses: discounting the maintenance expenses or not including the maintenance expenses in the premium liabilities calculation
