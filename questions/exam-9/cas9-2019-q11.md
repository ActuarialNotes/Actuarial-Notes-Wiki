Assume the following for a company that writes multiple lines of business:

- Beginning loss estimate is \$20,000
- Loss estimates have equal probability of developing adversely by 15% or favorably by 10% each year
- Beginning Assets are \$23,000
- Return on invested assets is 5% per year

## Part a (1.25 points)

Calculate the Expected Policyholder Deficit (EPD) Ratio at the end of year 2.

### Explanation
| Time | 0 | 1 | 2 | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Loss Estimate | \$20,000 | \$23,000 or \$18,000 | \$26,450, \$20,700 or \$16,200 | |
| Assets | \$23,000 | \$24,150 | \$25,358 | = \$24,150 * (1.05) |
| Expected Loss | \$20,000 | \$20,500 | \$21,013 | = 26,450 * .25 + 20,700 * .5 + 16,200 * .25 |
| EPD if liquidated | | \$0 | \$273 | = (26,450 - 25,358) * .25 |
| EPD Ratio | - | - | 1.3% | = 273 / 21,013 |

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to be able to calculate the expected policyholder deficit and understand the assumptions underlying this methodology.

Candidates were expected to calculate the EPD ratio.

Common mistakes included:

- Omitting the expected policyholder deficit ratio
- Incorrectly simulating the assets
- Incorrectly simulating the losses
- Using assets in the denominator of the EPD ratio
- Not multiplying by the correct or any probability in the numerator of the EPD ratio

## Part b (0.5 points)

Calculate the asset value needed at the end of year 2 to ensure a 5% EPD ratio.

### Explanation
(26,450 – A) * .25 = 21,013 * .05

A = 22,247

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to calculate the asset needed given a set EPD ratio.

The most common mistake was using an incorrect formula.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Explain why an EPD ratio capital requirement may exceed a rating agency capital requirement.

### Explanation
EPD Ratio doesn't consider diversification benefit, therefore rating agency capital would be lower, all else equal.

Alternatively:

If we assume rating agency is using a VaR approach it would be failing to consider the severity of loss whereas EPD considers severity of loss. This is a potential reason for EPD capital requirement could be higher than the rating agency requirement.

Alternatively:

Because rating agency looks at probability of default only whereas EPD includes severity of default in addition to probability of default.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know the advantages and disadvantages of using Expected Policyholder Deficit (EPD).

A common mistake was to confuse rating agency capital with regulatory capital (e.g. RBC).
