An article by Glenn M. Schultz and Frank J. Fabozzi in The Journal of Fixed Income, first published online on 12 November 2021. Key to the valuation of agency residential mortgage-backed securities is the modeling of voluntary prepayment and default behaviors of the borrowers in the mortgage pool. The authors propose a machine learning mortgage prepayment model using a boosted gradient classifier, trained at the loan level and generalized to the pool level, and contrast it with traditional modular prepayment models.
