![[Introduction to Probability Models (Ross - 2019) - Cover.svg]]

The [[Stochastic Processes|stochastic process]] reference on the MAS-I syllabus, covering learning objectives A1–A6. Chapter 5 is the heart of the MAS-I reading: the [[Exponential Distribution]], the [[Poisson Process]], and its nonhomogeneous, compound and mixed variants.

## 1 Introduction to Probability Theory

- Sample space and events, [[Axioms of Probability]]
- [[Conditional Probability]] and [[Independent Events|independence]]
- [[Bayes Theorem]]

## 2 Random Variables

- Discrete and continuous [[Random Variable|random variables]]
- [[Expected Value]], [[Variance]] and moment generating functions
- Limit theorems: [[Law of Large Numbers]] and the [[Central Limit Theorem]]
- [[Stochastic Processes]]

## 3 Conditional Probability and Conditional Expectation

- Conditional distributions, discrete and continuous
- Computing expectations and probabilities by conditioning
- [[Aggregate Loss Model|Compound random variables]] and their moments

## 4 Markov Chains

- [[Markov Chain|Chapman–Kolmogorov equations]] and classification of states
- Limiting probabilities and mean time in transient states
- Branching processes, time reversibility and Markov chain Monte Carlo

## 5 The Exponential Distribution and the Poisson Process

- The [[Exponential Distribution]] and its memoryless property
- The [[Poisson Process]]: counting processes, [[Interarrival Time|interarrival]] and waiting times
- Further properties: conditional distribution of arrival times, thinning
- Generalizations: the [[Nonhomogeneous Poisson Process]], the [[Compound Poisson Process]], and the [[Mixed Poisson Process]]

## 6 Continuous-Time Markov Chains

- Birth and death processes, transition probability functions
- Limiting probabilities, time reversibility, uniformization

## 7 Renewal Theory and Its Applications

- Distribution of $N(t)$ and limit theorems
- Renewal reward processes, regenerative processes
- Semi-Markov processes and the inspection paradox

## 8 Queueing Theory

- Preliminaries and cost equations
- Exponential and network models, the system M/G/1
- Multiserver queues

## 9 Reliability Theory

- Structure functions and system reliability
- Expected system lifetime and systems with repair

## 10 Brownian Motion and Stationary Processes

- Brownian motion, hitting times and variations
- Gaussian and stationary processes, white noise

## 11 Simulation

- General techniques for simulating continuous and discrete random variables
- Variance reduction techniques and statistical validation

## 12 Coupling

- A brief introduction to coupling arguments and their applications
