You are given the following bridge systems:

![Three systems built from identical components labelled P. System X: two P components in parallel (a box with one P on the top branch and one on the bottom branch), followed in series by a single P on the line leaving the box. System Y: a single P on the line entering the box, followed in series by two P components in parallel. System Z: three P components in parallel (top, middle and bottom branches of one box), with nothing in series](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ActuarialNotes/Actuarial-Notes-Wiki/main/Media/Attachments/masi-2019f-q5-bridge-systems.png)

- All three systems X, Y, and Z are built with independent and identical components, $P$
- The reliability of System $i$ is denoted as $r_i$, for $i = X, Y, Z$

Determine which of the following best describes the reliabilities of these systems.

- A) $r_X = r_Y > r_Z$
- B) $r_X < r_Y < r_Z$
- C) $r_X = r_Y = r_Z$
- D) $r_X = r_Y < r_Z$
- E) $r_X > r_Y > r_Z$

## Explanation

Let $p$ be the reliability of a single component $P$. A parallel block works if at least one of its components works; a series arrangement works only if every part works.

**System X** is a parallel pair in series with a single component, and **System Y** is a single component in series with a parallel pair. Series reliabilities multiply, and the order does not matter:

$$
r_X = r_Y = \left[1 - (1-p)^2\right] p = 2p^2 - p^3.
$$

**System Z** is three components in parallel:

$$
r_Z = 1 - (1-p)^3 = 3p - 3p^2 + p^3.
$$

Compare them:

$$
\begin{align*}
r_Z - r_X &= 3p - 5p^2 + 2p^3 \\
&= p(1-p)(3-2p)
\end{align*}
$$

which is positive for every $0 < p < 1$. Intuitively, Z needs any one of three components, while X and Y need a specific single component to work *and* one of two others.

So $r_X = r_Y < r_Z$, and the answer is **D**.
