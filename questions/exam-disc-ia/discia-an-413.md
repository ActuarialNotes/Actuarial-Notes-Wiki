A named insured's car, which carries other than collision coverage under an ISO PAP (PP 00 01 09 18), is stolen. She rents a car for \$40 a day for the 10 days until the police recover hers undamaged. How much does Part D pay for her transportation expenses?

- A) \$240
- B) \$300
- C) \$320
- D) \$400

## Explanation

After a total theft, transportation expenses are paid only for the period beginning **48 hours after the theft** and ending when the car is returned, at no more than **\$30 a day**, up to \$900, with no deductible. Two of the 10 days fall within the waiting period:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Paid} &= (10 - 2) \times \min(40,\ 30) \\
&= 240
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** pays \$30 a day for all 10 days, ignoring the 48-hour waiting period for theft.
- **C** applies the waiting period but pays the full \$40 rental rate, above the \$30 daily cap.
- **D** pays the whole rental bill.
