A debt is amortized with 60 equal monthly payments at an annual effective interest rate of 12%. The amount of principal in the third payment is 900.

Calculate the amount of principal in the 33rd payment.

- A) 668
- B) 900
- C) 1008
- D) 1195
- E) 1213

## Explanation

Monthly rate $j = 1.12^{1/12}-1 = 0.009489$.

Principal at payment $k$ grows by $(1+j)$ per period:
$$
\begin{align*}
P_{33} &= P_3 \cdot (1+j)^{30} \\
       &= 900(1.009489)^{30} \\
       &= 900(1.12)^{2.5} \\
       &= 900(1.327532) \\
       &= 1194.78
\end{align*}
$$
