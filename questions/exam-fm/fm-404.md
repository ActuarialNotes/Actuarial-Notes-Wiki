The following term structure of interest rates is based on available bond market data as of 01/01/2023:

| Term to maturity (years) | Spot Rate |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3.0% |
| 2 | 3.5% |
| 3 | 4.0% |

A three-year 1000 face amount bond with an annual coupon rate of 10% paid annually is issued on 01/01/2023.

Calculate the yield to maturity for this bond.

- A) 3.82%
- B) 3.86%
- C) 3.90%
- D) 3.94%
- E) 3.98%

## Explanation

The bond pays coupons of $0.10(1000) = 100$ at the ends of years 1 and 2, and $100 + 1000 = 1100$ at the end of year 3. Its market value is found by discounting each payment at the spot rate for its term:

$$
\begin{align*}
MV &= \frac{100}{1.03} + \frac{100}{1.035^{2}} + \frac{1100}{1.04^{3}} \\
   &= 97.087 + 93.351 + 977.896 \\
   &= 1168.33
\end{align*}
$$

The yield to maturity is the single annual effective rate $i$ at which the bond's payments have this present value:

$$
\begin{align*}
1168.33 &= 100\,a_{\overline{3}|i} + 1000v^{3} \\
i &= 3.94\%
\end{align*}
$$
