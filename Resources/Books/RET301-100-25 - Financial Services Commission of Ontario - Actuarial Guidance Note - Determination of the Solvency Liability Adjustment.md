A four-page actuarial guidance note, index number AGN-005, approved by the Superintendent of Financial Services, published on the FSCO website in July 2016 and effective December 10, 2015. It replaces AGN-002 and gives guidance on determining the average interest rates used for the solvency liability adjustment under Regulation 909 where the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Standards of Practice and Educational Notes change during the averaging period, for solvency valuations on or after September 30, 2015.

> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcome 1a (Assumptions); the whole note (the syllabus codes it RET301-100-25).

## Purpose of Guidance Note
- Regulation 909 permits a smoothing method in the solvency valuation; the solvency liability adjustment, defined in section 1.3, uses an interest rate that is the average of the market interest rates over the same period used to adjust the market value of assets, and the averaging period cannot exceed five years.

## Application of CIA Standards
- A report filed under the Pension Benefits Act must meet the requirements of the Act and the Regulation; under section 16 of the Regulation the actuary uses methods and assumptions consistent with accepted actuarial practice.
- For lump sum commuted values the actuary follows section 3500 of the CIA Standards: UP-94 with generational projection using scale AA before December 10, 2015, and the 2014 Canadian Pensioners Mortality Table with CPM Improvement Scale B (CPM2014Proj) on or after that date.
- For estimating group annuity purchase costs the actuary adheres to the CIA Educational Notes and periodic Educational Note Supplements.

## Acceptable Methodology
- The note describes a hypothetical plan: non-indexed benefits only, medium duration, a January 1, 2016 valuation date and a five-point averaging period using that date and the four previous anniversary dates.

## Pension benefits to be settled by lump sum transfer
- FSCO accepts applying the pension commuted value basis of section 3500 in effect on the valuation date as if it had been effective throughout the solvency liability adjustment period.
- For the January 1, 2016 example the five-point average is 2.5% for the select period and 3.9% for the ultimate period.

## Pension benefits to be settled by group annuity purchase
- The actuary uses the interest rates for group annuity purchase published in the CIA Educational Notes and Supplements applicable at each anniversary date, adjusted for any change in the base mortality table and improvement scale and for duration.
- For the January 1, 2016 example the average annuity proxy interest rate is 3.63%.
- The note does not preclude alternative smoothing methods that are appropriate for the plan; FSCO considers such submissions case by case.
