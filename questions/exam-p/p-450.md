$X$, $Y$, and $Z$ are three mutually independent Poisson random variables with common variance $\lambda$. Let $U = 100X + 150Y + 200Z$. The coefficient of variation for $U$ is 0.90.

Calculate $\lambda$.

- A) $0.44$
- B) $0.82$
- C) $1.22$
- D) $1.50$
- E) $2.25$

## Explanation

A Poisson distribution has mean equal to its variance, so each of $X$, $Y$, $Z$ has mean $\lambda$ as well.

The mean of $U$ scales each coefficient linearly:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[U] &= 100\lambda + 150\lambda + 200\lambda \\
     &= 450\lambda
\end{align*}
$$

The variance squares each coefficient, and independence lets the terms add:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(U) &= 100^2\lambda + 150^2\lambda + 200^2\lambda \\
              &= 72{,}500\lambda
\end{align*}
$$

The coefficient of variation is the standard deviation divided by the mean:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.9 &= \frac{\sqrt{72{,}500\lambda}}{450\lambda} \\
0.81 &= \frac{72{,}500\lambda}{202{,}500\lambda^2} \\
\lambda &= \frac{72{,}500}{(0.9)^2(450)^2} \\
        &= \mathbf{0.442}
\end{align*}
$$

One factor of $\lambda$ cancels, leaving a linear equation rather than a quadratic.
