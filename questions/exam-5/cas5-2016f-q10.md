(1 point)

A homeowners insurance company is considering utilizing number of vehicles in the household as an additional risk characteristic within its risk classification system.

Briefly discuss the appropriateness of adding this risk characteristic to the company's risk classification system using four considerations from the Actuarial Standard of Practice No. 12: Risk Classification (for All Practice Areas).

### Explanation
Causality – the number of vehicles does not seem to have an intuitive relationship to homeowners' losses, so this criteria may be violated Easy to verify – this would be easy to verify by checking vehicle records Existing Law – there is no current law which prohibits the use of number of vehicles in the risk classification system Objective – the number of vehicles is well defined and unambiguous.
