A random variable $X$ is exponentially distributed with 40th percentile equal to $4\ln\left(\dfrac{5}{3}\right)$.

Calculate the median of $X$.

- A) $5\ln\left(\dfrac{5}{3}\right)$
- B) $4\ln 2$
- C) $5\ln\left(\dfrac{25}{12}\right)$
- D) $4$
- E) $5$

## Explanation

For an exponential distribution with mean $\beta$, the $100q$th percentile is

$$
x_q = -\beta\ln(1-q)
$$

At the 40th percentile:

$$
\begin{align*}
x_{0.4} &= -\beta\ln(0.6) \\
        &= \beta\ln\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)
\end{align*}
$$

Matching this against the given $4\ln(5/3)$ gives

$$
\beta = 4
$$

The logarithm cancels outright — no numerical evaluation is needed. The median is the 50th percentile:

$$
\begin{align*}
x_{0.5} &= -\beta\ln(0.5) \\
        &= \mathbf{4\ln 2}
\end{align*}
$$

Numerically that is 2.77. The given form of the 40th percentile is deliberately written to make $\beta$ readable at sight — recognising $-\ln(0.6) = \ln(5/3)$ is the whole first step. Choice (D), 4, is $\beta$ itself, which is the mean rather than the median.
