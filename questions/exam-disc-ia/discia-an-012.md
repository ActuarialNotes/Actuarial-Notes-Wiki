During a year an insurer writes \$80 million of direct premium, assumes \$12 million of premium from other insurers under reinsurance treaties, and cedes \$25 million of premium to its reinsurers. What is its net written premium?

- A) \$55 million
- B) \$67 million
- C) \$92 million
- D) \$117 million

## Explanation

Assumed premium adds to the insurer's writings, and ceded premium reduces its written premium.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{NWP} &= 80 + 12 - 25 \\
&= 67
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$55 million leaves out the assumed premium, which is written premium of the assuming insurer.
- **C** — \$92 million is direct plus assumed, gross written premium before ceding.
- **D** — \$117 million adds the ceded premium instead of subtracting it.
