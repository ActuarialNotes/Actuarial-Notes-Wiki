## [F-001] Explanation's prose misstates when the interest is earned (9k on a 100k balance 'each year k')
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, line 43
- claim: 'Each year $k$, Company R earns interest of $100 \times 0.09 \times k = 9k$ on its $100k$ balance (prospective view) ... More precisely, R earns $100(0.09)$ on the first deposit, $100(0.09)(2)$ on the running total after year 2, etc.'
- evidence: Deposits are made at the END of each year, so during year k R's balance is 100(k-1) and the interest credited at the end of year k is 9(k-1), for k = 1..25: 0, 9, 18, ..., 216. That is why SOA (solutions PDF p.45) and the vault's own formula use 9(Is) over 24 periods at 8%, not 25. Read literally (year k = 1..25, interest 9k), the sentence gives 9(Is)_25 at 8% = 9(78.9544-25)/0.08 = 6069.87, R = 8569.87 and X = 101.18, which is not the key. The 'more precisely' sentence names the amounts but not the years in which they are credited, so it doesn't resolve the off-by-one. The displayed formula, its numbers (s-double-dot_24 at 8% = 72.1059, R = 7911.91) and the key D are correct.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: Reword the sentence to say that the interest credited at the end of year k is 9(k-1), on the balance of 100(k-1) built up by the first k-1 deposits (k = 2..25), so the interest stream is 9, 18, ..., 216 at times 2..25, i.e. 9(Is)_24. A maintainer writes the wording.
