A business pays \$2,500 of premium and three months later collects \$400,000 after a covered fire. Its neighbour pays the same premium every year for ten years and never has a claim. Which characteristic of insurance contracts does this contrast illustrate?

- A) Conditional
- B) Aleatory
- C) Indemnity
- D) Nontransferable

## Explanation

An aleatory contract depends on an uncertain event and transfers value unequally: an insured can pay premiums for many years without a covered loss, or pay a small premium for a short time and then collect for a large one. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — conditional refers to the events and duties that must be met before the insurer is obligated to pay; it does not describe the unequal exchange.
- **C** — indemnity limits each recovery to the insured's actual loss; the \$400,000 may be exactly that, but indemnity does not explain why the two businesses' exchanges differ.
- **D** — nontransferable concerns assigning the policy to someone else.
