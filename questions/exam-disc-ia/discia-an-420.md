An earthquake cracks an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured's foundation and ruptures a gas line, and the resulting fire burns the house and its contents. The insured has no earthquake endorsement. What does Section I cover?

- A) Nothing, because the loss was set in motion by an excluded earth movement
- B) The fire damage to the house and contents, but not the cracked foundation
- C) The fire damage to the house only, because contents are not covered for earthquake
- D) Everything, because fire is a covered peril for both the dwelling and the contents

## Explanation

Earth movement, including earthquake, is excluded however it combines with other causes, but the exclusion ends with an exception: **direct loss by fire, explosion or theft resulting from earth movement is covered**. The fire damage to the dwelling (open perils) and the contents (fire is a named peril) is covered; the foundation crack, caused by the earthquake itself, is not. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** applies the earth movement exclusion without its exception for ensuing fire.
- **C** — the contents' fire loss is covered by named peril 1, fire or lightning, so it is covered even though the earthquake started it.
- **D** pays the earthquake damage too; the crack is still excluded.
