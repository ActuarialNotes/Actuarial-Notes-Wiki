For a fully discrete 3-year endowment insurance of 1000 on (*x*), you are given:

(i) Expenses, payable at the beginning of the year, are:

| Year(s) | Percent of Premium | Per Policy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20% | 15 |
| 2 and 3 | 8% | 5 |

(ii) The expense policy value at the end of year 2 is –23.64

(iii) The gross annual premium calculated using the equivalence principle is *G* = 368.05

(iv) $G = 1000P_{x:\overline{3}|} + P^{e}$, where $P^{e}$ is the expense loading

Calculate $P_{x:\overline{3}|}$.

[Question 16 on the Spring 2014 exam]

- A) 0.290
- B) 0.295
- C) 0.300
- D) 0.305
- E) 0.310

## Explanation

Future expenses at $x + 2 = 0.08G + 5$

Expense load at $x + 2 = P^{e}$

$$\begin{align*}
-23.64 &= (0.08G + 5) - P^{e} \\
\Rightarrow P^{e} &= 58.08
\end{align*}$$

$$\begin{align*}
1000P_{x:\overline{3}|} &= 368.05 - 58.08 \\
&= 309.97
\end{align*}$$

The answer is **E**.
