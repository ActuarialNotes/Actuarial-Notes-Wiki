A customer purchases a lawnmower with a two-year warranty. The number of years before the lawnmower needs a repair is uniformly distributed on $[0, 5]$.

Calculate the probability that the lawnmower needs no repairs within 4.5 years after the purchase, given that the lawnmower needs no repairs within the warranty period.

- A) $0.10$
- B) $0.17$
- C) $0.44$
- D) $0.50$
- E) $0.60$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the time until repair, uniform on $[0,5]$. The warranty period is two years, so the conditioning event is $X > 2$ and the event of interest is $X > 4.5$.

For a uniform distribution, probabilities are just proportions of the interval:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 4.5] &= \frac{5 - 4.5}{5 - 0} = 0.1 \\
P[X > 2]   &= \frac{5 - 2}{5 - 0} = 0.6
\end{align*}
$$

Since $\{X > 4.5\}$ is contained in $\{X > 2\}$, their intersection is just $\{X > 4.5\}$, and

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 4.5 \mid X > 2] &= \frac{0.1}{0.6} \\
                      &= \frac{1}{6} \\
                      &= \mathbf{0.167}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (A), 0.10, is the unconditional $P[X > 4.5]$. Unlike the exponential distribution, the uniform is *not* memoryless — conditioning on survival past 2 leaves only 3 years of range, so the remaining lifetime is uniform on $[2,5]$ and the conditional probability is strictly larger than the unconditional one.
