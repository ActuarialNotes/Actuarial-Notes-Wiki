For its commercial property line last year, an insurer reports earned premium of \$50 million, incurred losses and LAE of \$33 million, and underwriting expenses of \$15.5 million. Using earned premium as the base for both ratios, what are the line's underwriting profit and combined ratio?

- A) A loss of \$1.5 million and a combined ratio of 103%
- B) A profit of \$1.5 million and a combined ratio of 66%
- C) A profit of \$1.5 million and a combined ratio of 97%
- D) A profit of \$17.0 million and a combined ratio of 97%

## Explanation

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{UW profit} &= 50 - 33 - 15.5 \\
&= 1.5 \\
\text{Combined ratio} &= \frac{33}{50} + \frac{15.5}{50} \\
&= 66\% + 31\% \\
&= 97\%
\end{align*}
$$

The line earned an underwriting profit of \$1.5 million, a 3% margin: one minus the combined ratio. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Gets the sign wrong: a combined ratio below 100% means an underwriting profit.
- **B** — Reports the loss ratio alone as the combined ratio, leaving out the expense ratio.
- **D** — Subtracts only the losses from premium, ignoring the underwriting expenses.
