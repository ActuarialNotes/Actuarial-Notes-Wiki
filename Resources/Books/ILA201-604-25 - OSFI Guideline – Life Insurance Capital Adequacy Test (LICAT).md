![[ILA201-604-25 - OSFI Guideline – Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test (LICAT) - Cover.svg]]

OSFI's Life Insurance Capital Adequacy Test (LICAT) guideline, which establishes standards, using a risk-based approach, for measuring specific life insurer risks and aggregating the results into a life insurer's regulatory required capital, and which defines the criteria for the qualifying regulatory available capital. It applies to federally regulated life insurers for annual reporting periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025 (issued November 21, 2024), and works with Guideline A-4 as the framework within which the Superintendent assesses whether a life insurer maintains adequate capital.
