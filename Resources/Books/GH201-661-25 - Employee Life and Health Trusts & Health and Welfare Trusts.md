A reading supplied by the Society of Actuaries for GH 201-C. According to the course strategy guide, it presents the key differences between Employee Life and Health Trusts (ELHTs) and Health and Welfare Trusts (HWTs), which are vehicles used by employers and benefit plan administrators to provide employee benefits.
