An insurer offers its commercial policyholders loss control surveys and safety training at no separate charge. Which reason is most consistent with the insurer's own interests?

- A) Improving the accounts it writes lowers their losses and helps it keep good accounts
- B) Risk control services let it charge every account the same rate whatever its hazards
- C) Insurers must provide risk control to every policyholder in every jurisdiction
- D) Risk control replaces the need for underwriters to evaluate submissions

## Explanation

An insurer pools many risks and holds capital against the part of their losses it can't predict. Risk control serves its profit goal by improving the accounts it writes and keeping the good ones, and the surveys also give underwriters the information they need to accept, modify or decline an account. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Risk control improves accounts but doesn't make them identical, and rates still depend on each account's classification and hazards.
- **C** — Some jurisdictions require particular inspections, such as of boilers, but there is no general requirement to provide risk control to every policyholder; the service level depends on the account's size and line.
- **D** — Risk control supports underwriting with information; it doesn't replace the underwriting decision.
