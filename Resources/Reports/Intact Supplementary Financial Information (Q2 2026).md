Intact Financial Corporation's unaudited supplementary financial information for the second quarter of 2026: a 25-page statistical package of consolidated and segment results by quarter. It reports premiums, underwriting ratios, catastrophe losses and prior-year claims development by segment and line of business, then the GAAP statements, investment portfolio, capital, shareholder information and the reconciliations of its non-GAAP measures. Figures are in millions of Canadian dollars unless noted, with pre-IFRS 17 nomenclature shown in italic where it applies. Intact publishes it as a PDF and an Excel workbook with the same title.

## Important Notes
- Non-GAAP and Other Financial Measures — the package uses GAAP measures alongside non-GAAP financial measures and ratios as defined in National Instrument 52-112, which have no standardized meaning under IFRS and may not be comparable to other companies' measures

- Operating direct premiums written, net premiums written and operating net underwriting revenue by segment (P&C Canada, P&C UK&I, P&C US) and, in Canada, by line of business (personal auto, personal property, commercial lines)
