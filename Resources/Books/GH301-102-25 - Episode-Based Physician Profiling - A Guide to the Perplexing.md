A perspective article in the Journal of General Internal Medicine (volume 23, issue 9, September 2008, pages 1521-1524). Its abstract describes episode-based profiling as a new "second generation" of physician profiling that measures physician practice at the "episode of care" level using claim and other administrative data sets, moving beyond legacy "first-generation" profiles based on population health and preventive services measures, and notes its advantages and its analytic and engagement limitations.
