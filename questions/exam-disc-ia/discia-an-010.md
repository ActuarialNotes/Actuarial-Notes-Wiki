An insurer writes a one-year policy with an annual premium of \$3,650, effective August 1, 2025. Using the daily pro rata method, what earned premium does this policy contribute to calendar year 2025?

- A) \$1,216.67
- B) \$1,368.75
- C) \$1,520.83
- D) \$1,530.00

## Explanation

The daily pro rata method computes the expired portion of each policy. From August 1 through December 31, 2025 the policy is in force for 153 of its 365 days.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{EP}_{2025} &= 3{,}650 \times \frac{153}{365} \\
&= 1{,}530.00
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$1,216.67 is $\tfrac{4}{12}$ of the premium, counting only September to December and dropping August.
- **B** — \$1,368.75 is the monthly pro rata result, $\tfrac{9}{24}$ earned, which assumes the policy was written mid-month. The question asks for the daily method.
- **C** — \$1,520.83 is $\tfrac{5}{12}$, a month count that treats every month as the same length.
