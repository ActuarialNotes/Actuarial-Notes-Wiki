## [F-001] Explanation says full immunization 'requires two conditions', omitting that asset cash flows must fall before and after the liability
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, line 41
- claim: Full immunization requires two conditions (present value matching and duration matching):
- evidence: Finan, §56 'Full Immunization and Dedication', PDF p.486, lists three full-immunization conditions for a single liability: (1) PV of assets = PV of liability, (2) modified duration of assets = modified duration of liability, (3) the asset cash flows occur before and after the liability cash flow. SOA's solution Q56 (solutions PDF p.16) only says 'Set up the following two equations in the two unknowns' and states no definition. The question's setup (A at 2, B at 6, liability at 4) satisfies (3), so the answer is unaffected, but the sentence teaches an incomplete definition of a syllabus term (syllabus PDF p.5, Topic 5 outcomes a and c).
- source_rank: 3
- proposed_action: Reword the lead sentence to name the third condition (one asset cash flow before and one after the liability) or to say the two equations are what solve for A and B. Authoring - maintainer's call.
