A Quebec automobile insurer writes a driver it considers a much higher risk than its rates assume. The insurer wants to keep the customer but not the underwriting result.

Which arrangement describes the *plan de répartition des risques* (P.R.R.) through which it can do this?

- A) The Facility Association writes the driver directly in its residual market at rates approved for that market
- B) The Société de l'assurance automobile du Québec takes over the driver's private property damage coverage
- C) The driver is placed in an uninsured automobile fund financed by a levy on all insurers in the province
- D) The insurer cedes the risk to a private reinsurer of its choice and keeps the commission it negotiates
- E) The insurer issues and services the policy itself and transfers the risk to a pool shared by the province's auto insurers

## Explanation

Dutil's study note *Facility Association* says the Facility Association does not operate in Quebec. There, the Groupement des Assureurs Automobiles (GAA) administers a risk sharing pool mechanism, the *plan de répartition des risques* (P.R.R.), which "essentially fulfills the same role" as the Facility Association's pools elsewhere. Dutil describes those pools this way. Member companies underwrite individual policies at their own rates and rules, then either keep the business or transfer it to the pool. The transfer is invisible to the consumer and the intermediary, and the pool's results are shared among all the insurers licensed to write automobile insurance in the province. The answer is **E**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — this is the Facility Association Residual Market (FARM), where the facility itself is the market. The Facility Association does not operate in Quebec, and the P.R.R. is a pool behind the insurer's own policy.
- **B** — the SAAQ is Quebec's public plan for bodily injury. It does not take over private property damage coverage.
- **C** — an uninsured automobile fund pays victims of uninsured drivers. Dutil notes that the Facility Association administers these funds in the four Atlantic Provinces. It is not a way to place a high-risk insured driver.
- **D** — private reinsurance is a voluntary market transaction with a chosen counterparty. The P.R.R.'s results are shared industry-wide, with no counterparty for the insurer to choose.
