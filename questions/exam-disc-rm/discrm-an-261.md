A commercial lines insurer's SWOT analysis finds: its policy systems are outdated and its expense ratio is high, and the market is softening as new competitors cut rates on small accounts. Which strategic option belongs to the weakness-threat (WT) quadrant?

- A) Withdraw from small, price-driven accounts where its high expenses leave it unable to compete
- B) Use its claims reputation to win large accounts that value service over price
- C) Use growth in a profitable line to fund replacing its policy systems
- D) Expand into a new region where demand is rising and competition is weak

## Explanation

A weakness-threat option limits exposure where a weakness meets a threat. Here the weakness, high expenses, meets the threat, rate cutting on small accounts, so withdrawing from those accounts is the WT option. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Uses a strength against a threat, an ST option.
- **C** — Uses an opportunity to fix a weakness, a WO option.
- **D** — Pursues an opportunity with no stated weakness or threat behind it, an SO or opportunity-led option.
