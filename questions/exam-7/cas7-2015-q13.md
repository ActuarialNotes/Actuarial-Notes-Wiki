Given the following information for a reinsurer as of December 31, 2014 (\$000):

| Accident Year | Earned Premium | Earned Risk Pure Premium | Adjusted Premium | Aggregate Reported Loss | Aggregate Loss Report Lag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 10,000 | 10,000 | 9,000 | 6,000 | 90% |
| 2012 | 11,500 | 11,000 | 12,000 | 5,000 | 70% |
| 2013 | 12,500 | 12,000 | 11,000 | 2,000 | 40% |
| 2014 | 14,000 | 13,000 | 13,000 | 4,000 | 30% |

## Part a (1.25 points)

Using the Stanard-Bühlmann Method, estimate the IBNR for all accident years combined.

### Explanation
All amounts in \$000.

| AY | Adjusted Premium | Aggregate Loss Report Lag | Reported Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 9,000 | 90% | 6,000 |
| 2012 | 12,000 | 70% | 5,000 |
| 2013 | 11,000 | 40% | 2,000 |
| 2014 | 13,000 | 30% | 4,000 |

Used-up premium is adjusted premium × lag:

$$\text{Used-up premium} = 9{,}000 \times 90\% + 12{,}000 \times 70\% + 11{,}000 \times 40\% + 13{,}000 \times 30\% = 24{,}800$$

$$\text{ELR} = \frac{6{,}000 + 5{,}000 + 2{,}000 + 4{,}000}{24{,}800} = \frac{17{,}000}{24{,}800} = 68.55\%$$

$$\text{IBNR} = \text{ELR} \times \text{Adjusted Premium} \times (1 - \text{Lag})$$

| AY | IBNR (\$ millions) |
|---|---|
| 2011 | 0.61695 |
| 2012 | 2.4678 |
| 2013 | 4.5243 |
| 2014 | 6.23805 |
| Total | 13.847 |

IBNR for all accident years combined is \$13.847 million.

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally performed well on this part. Common mistakes included using Earned Risk Pure Premium instead of Adjusted Premium, as well as calculating IBNR by subtracting the reported losses from the expected ultimate losses.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Identify and briefly explain the need for the modification made to earned risk pure premium to derive adjusted premium.

### Explanation
- The adjustment is to bring earned risk pure premium to the **current rate level** (on-level premium).
- The Stanard-Bühlmann method calculates a single ELR from all years, so the premiums must be on the same rate level for the loss ratios of each accident year to be comparable.

### Examiner Report
The bulk of candidates correctly identified Current Rate Level as the required adjustment.
Discussion of trend and other similar terms/concepts also earned credit. Candidates who mentioned adjusting for expenses and other similar terms/concepts did not receive any credit, as these adjustments are already included in the Earned Risk Pure Premium.
