You have decided to perform k-means clustering with K=2 on the following dataset and have already randomly assigned clusters as follows:

| Observation | $x_1$ | $x_2$ | Initial Cluster |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | 5 | 2 |
| 2 | 4 | 6 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 0 | 1 |
| 4 | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| 5 | 5 | 1 | 2 |
| 6 | 3 | 6 | 1 |
| 7 | 2 | 5 | 2 |

- The centroid of the initial cluster 1 is $x_1 = 3.667$, $x_2 = 3.000$
- The centroid of the initial cluster 2 is $x_1 = 4.00$, $x_2 = 4.250$

Calculate the Euclidean distance of Observation 5 from the final centroid of Cluster 2.

- A) Less than 1
- B) At least 1, but less than 2
- C) At least 2, but less than 3
- D) At least 3, but less than 4
- E) At least 4

## Explanation

Run the k-means loop to convergence: assign each point to the nearer centroid, recompute centroids, repeat.

**Iteration 1** — distances to $C_1 = (3.667, 3.000)$ and $C_2 = (4.00, 4.25)$:

| Obs | $(x_1, x_2)$ | $d$ to $C_1$ | $d$ to $C_2$ | Assigned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (5, 5) | 2.404 | 1.250 | 2 |
| 2 | (4, 6) | 3.018 | 1.750 | 2 |
| 3 | (3, 0) | 3.073 | 4.366 | 1 |
| 4 | (5, 3) | 1.333 | 1.601 | 1 |
| 5 | (5, 1) | 2.404 | 3.400 | 1 |
| 6 | (3, 6) | 3.073 | 2.016 | 2 |
| 7 | (2, 5) | 2.603 | 2.136 | 2 |

Observation 5 has moved from cluster 2 to cluster 1. New centroids:

$$
\begin{align*}
C_1 &= \left(\frac{3+5+5}{3},\ \frac{0+3+1}{3}\right) = (4.333,\ 1.333) \\
C_2 &= \left(\frac{5+4+3+2}{4},\ \frac{5+6+6+5}{4}\right) = (3.5,\ 5.5)
\end{align*}
$$

**Iteration 2** — reassigning against the new centroids leaves cluster 1 as $\{3, 4, 5\}$ and cluster 2 as $\{1, 2, 6, 7\}$. No point changes cluster, so the algorithm has converged and the final Cluster 2 centroid is $(3.5,\ 5.5)$.

**Distance from Observation 5** at $(5, 1)$ — note the question asks for its distance from the *Cluster 2* centroid even though it ended up in cluster 1:

$$
\begin{align*}
d &= \sqrt{(5-3.5)^2 + (1-5.5)^2} \\
  &= \sqrt{2.25 + 20.25} \\
  &= \sqrt{22.5} \\
  &= 4.743
\end{align*}
$$

This is at least 4, so the answer is **E**.
