A curated collection of readings designed to accompany the Society of Actuaries' Pre-Actuarial Foundations and Actuarial Science Foundations e-learning modules. Its opening note says that, unlike a traditional textbook, it does not follow a linear narrative: each article, excerpt or transcript is meant to be read as the modules prompt, reading by reading. The readings are drawn from excerpts of published books, articles, pieces written specifically for the modules and transcripts from conferences or meetings, and the SOA says their topics cover the role of actuaries, core financial risk concepts and how actuarial work informs business decisions.
