## Part a (0.75 points)

Identify three main areas on which Canadian insurance regulation is focused.

### Explanation
Solvency, Canadianization, creation of tax revenues (need to specify **tax** revenues), promote market integrity / improve contract form, promote intermediary honesty / competence.

Also allow "protection of the insured / consumer".

Alternatively:

1. Regulation regarding solvency of the insurer
2. Creating tax revenue
3. Promoting ownership of Canadian insures and local investment

## Part b (0.75 points)

Identify three duties of the audit committee.

### Explanation
Audit committee:

1. review the annual statement
2. review such returns of the company the superintendent may specify
3. require management to implement and maintain appropriate internal control procedures
4. review such investments and transactions that could adversely affect the well-being of the company...
5. meet with the auditor
6. meet with the actuary
7. meet with the chief internal auditor of the company

Alternatively:

1. Meet with actuary to discuss portion of annual report that relates to valuing policy liabilities
2. Review the annual report before its presented to the directors
3. Meet with auditor to discuss the fairness of the report
