An insurer cedes 25% of a book with \$20 million of written premium under a quota share with a 30% ceding commission. Losses on the book are \$12 million. Ignoring the reinsurer's own expenses and investment income, what is the reinsurer's underwriting result on the treaty?

- A) A loss of \$1.0 million
- B) A gain of \$0.5 million
- C) A gain of \$2.0 million
- D) A gain of \$3.5 million

## Explanation

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Ceded premium} &= 0.25 \times 20 \\
&= 5.0 \\
\text{Commission} &= 0.30 \times 5.0 \\
&= 1.5 \\
\text{Ceded losses} &= 0.25 \times 12 \\
&= 3.0 \\
\text{Result} &= 5.0 - 1.5 - 3.0 \\
&= 0.5
\end{align*}
$$

The reinsurer gains \$0.5 million. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Subtracts the commission twice.
- **C** — Forgets the ceding commission the reinsurer pays.
- **D** — Leaves out the reinsurer's share of the losses.
