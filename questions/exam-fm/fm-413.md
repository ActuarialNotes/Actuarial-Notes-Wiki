A graduate takes out a 60-month car loan at an annual nominal interest rate of 3% convertible monthly. The loan provides for a payment of 359.37 at the end of each month. The graduate failed to make the 25th and 26th payments. Beginning with the 27th month the graduate resumes payments of 359.37 at the end of each month, with a final balloon payment at the end of the 60th month made necessary by the two missed payments.

Calculate the outstanding loan balance immediately following the payment at the end of the 36th month.

- A) 8298
- B) 8361
- C) 8723
- D) 9036
- E) 9099

## Explanation

The effective rate per month is $i = 0.03/12 = 0.0025$. The payment of 359.37 is the level payment on a loan of 20,000, rounded to the cent. Hence the original loan amount is

$$
\begin{align*}
L &= P\,a_{\overline{60}|i} \\
  &= 359.37\,a_{\overline{60}|0.0025} \\
  &\approx 20{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

Using the retrospective approach, the outstanding loan balance after the 36th payment is the loan accumulated to time 36, less the payments actually made, accumulated to time 36. Those are all 36 scheduled payments except the 25th and 26th, which would have earned interest for 11 and 10 months:

$$
\begin{align*}
OLB_{36} &= L(1+i)^{36} - \left[P\,s_{\overline{36}|i} - P(1+i)^{11} - P(1+i)^{10}\right] \\
         &= 20{,}000(1.0025)^{36} - \left[359.37\,s_{\overline{36}|0.0025} - 359.37(1.0025)^{11} - 359.37(1.0025)^{10}\right] \\
         &= 21{,}881.028 - (13{,}519.701 - 369.377 - 368.456) \\
         &= 21{,}881.028 - 12{,}781.868 \\
         &= 9099.16
\end{align*}
$$
