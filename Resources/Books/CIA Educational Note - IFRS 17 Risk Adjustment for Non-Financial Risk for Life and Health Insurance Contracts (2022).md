A CIA educational note that summarizes some of the methods available to calculate the risk adjustment for non-financial risk and its corresponding confidence level under IFRS 17. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated June 30, 2022 (document 222090), it covers risk margins, the cost-of-capital approach and quantile techniques for measuring the risk adjustment, and the considerations for quantifying the confidence level associated with it.
