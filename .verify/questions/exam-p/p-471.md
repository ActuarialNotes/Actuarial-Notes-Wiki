## [F-001] Explanation attributes the 1:2:3 marginal ratio to the wrong cause
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, final paragraph
- claim: The marginal probabilities come out in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3, a consequence of the coefficient 2 on x being twice the coefficient on y.
- evidence: For p(x,y) proportional to ax + by + 1 on y = 0,1,2, summing over y gives 3ax + 3b + 3 = 3(ax + b + 1), which is proportional to 1:2:3 over x = 0,1,2 exactly when a = b + 1. Here a = 2, b = 1, so the ratio holds because 2 = 1 + 1, not because 2 = 2 x 1: with a = 4, b = 2 (x coefficient still twice the y coefficient) the marginal is proportional to 3, 7, 11. The marginal values 6/36, 12/36, 18/36 match the SOA solution table (Aug 2026 rev., solutions PDF p.131) and the recomputation; only this side remark is wrong.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Delete the causal clause (a consequence of ... coefficient on y), or restate it as: summing over y leaves 6x + 6, proportional to x + 1.
