A May 2025 Deloitte paper on how chief financial officers in the insurance sector can manage expenses through cost allocations. It outlines the challenges and leading practices for expense allocations in the insurance industry, emphasizing the need to address intricate allocation processes, a lack of transparency, and the need for robust IT infrastructure and talent.
