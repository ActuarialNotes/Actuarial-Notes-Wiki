A Geneva Association report (December 2020) on social inflation linked to liability risks. On a broad definition, social inflation refers to all the ways in which insurers' claims costs rise over and above general economic inflation; more narrowly, to legislative and litigation developments that affect insurers' legal liabilities and claims costs. The report covers its drivers and how insurers can respond.
