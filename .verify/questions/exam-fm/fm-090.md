## [F-002] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 46 and 52-54
- claim: Math spans write thousands separators as bare commas (500,000 / 127,535 / 128,000 / 417,466.36 / 125,227).
- evidence: In TeX math a bare comma is punctuation and is followed by a thin space, so e.g. $500,000$ renders '500, 000'. The numbers themselves match SOA's solution Q90 (solutions PDF p.25, image) and my recomputation.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: Write the separators as {,} inside math.
