A research report, dated November 2025, that introduces the concept of capital and key related terms and compares four regulatory capital regimes for life insurers. The regimes are the NAIC's Risk-Based Capital (United States), the Life Insurer Capital Adequacy Test (Canada), Solvency II (European Union) and the Bermuda Insurance Solvency Framework. It reports that they differ in risk taxonomy, diversification and aggregation methods and minimum capital thresholds, and that the US method is largely factor-based while the others are model-based.
