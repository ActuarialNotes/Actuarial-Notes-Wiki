A book from the W.E. Upjohn Institute for Employment Research in which John A. Turner identifies the current problems facing pension policy for U.S. employer-provided pension plans and recommends solutions to those problems, based on his examination of pension systems in other industrialized nations. The Upjohn Research Repository offers the full book as an open-access PDF.
