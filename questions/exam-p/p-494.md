Each of the twelve employees of a company independently has the same probability of being accident-free this year.

The expected number of accident-free employees is 9.

Calculate the variance of the number of accident-free employees this year.

- A) $0.75$
- B) $1.50$
- C) $2.25$
- D) $9.00$
- E) $12.00$

## Explanation

The count of accident-free employees is binomial with $n = 12$. Recover $p$ from the mean:

$$
\begin{align*}
9 &= np = 12p \\
p &= 0.75
\end{align*}
$$

Then apply the binomial variance formula:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X) &= np(1-p) \\
              &= 12(0.75)(0.25) \\
              &= \mathbf{2.25}
\end{align*}
$$

Equivalently, $\text{Var} = E[X](1-p) = 9(0.25)$, which skips recomputing $np$.

Choice (D), 9.00, is the mean mistaken for the variance — they coincide for a Poisson distribution but never for a binomial, where the variance is always the smaller by the factor $(1-p)$.
