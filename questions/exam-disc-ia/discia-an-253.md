After a hearing, a commissioner finds that an insurer's advertising misrepresented policy benefits on 12 occasions. The violations were not committed flagrantly in conscious disregard of the law. Under a state law that follows the penalty limits of the NAIC Unfair Trade Practices Act, what is the largest fine the commissioner may order?

- A) \$12,000
- B) \$100,000
- C) \$250,000
- D) \$300,000

## Explanation

For violations that are not flagrant, the model allows a penalty of up to \$1,000 for each violation, not to exceed \$100,000 in all:

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= \min(1{,}000 \times 12,\ 100{,}000) \\
&= \mathbf{12{,}000}
\end{align*}
$$

The commissioner also issues an order to cease and desist.

- **B** is the aggregate cap, which binds only above 100 violations.
- **C** is the aggregate cap for flagrant violations.
- **D** applies the flagrant rate of \$25,000 per violation to all 12 and ignores the cap.
