A textbook on insurance regulation in the United States by Karen Porter, published in 2008 by the Insurance Institute of America (The Institutes). The SOA's GI 302 syllabus assigns parts of seven of its chapters, citing them by chapter and page.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GI-302 (SOA)|Exam GI-302]] — Outcomes 2a-c: Chapter 2 (pp. 2.3-2.18), Chapter 3 (pp. 3.3-3.18) and Chapter 4 (excluding Exhibits 4-2 and 4-3); Outcomes 2d, 2e: Chapter 5 (pp. 5.3-5.7 and 5.10-5.11) and Chapter 6 (pp. 6.3-6.5, 6.11-6.12 and 6.14-6.17, ending at "Licensing Priority and Process" on p. 6.17); Outcome 2f: Chapter 11 (pp. 11.3-11.10, 11.22-11.23 from "Priority Examination Scheduling" to "Association Examinations", and 11.25-11.26 ending at "Risk-Based Capital") and Chapter 12 (pp. 12.5-12.17, from "Regulatory Control After Insolvency" to "Interstate Insolvency Remedies") (as the syllabus states it)
