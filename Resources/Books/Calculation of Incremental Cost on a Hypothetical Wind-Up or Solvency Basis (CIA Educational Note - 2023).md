A Canadian Institute of Actuaries educational note, dated April 2023 (document 223065), intended to assist actuaries in calculating a pension plan's incremental cost on a hypothetical wind-up or solvency basis, which the Standards of Practice require, with limited exceptions, in an external user report on the funded status or funding of a pension plan. It updates the note first issued on December 21, 2010 after the quinquennial review of the pension-specific standards (Part 3000). It was prepared by the Committee on Pension Plan Financial Reporting and approved for distribution on April 11, 2023.

## 1 Introduction
- Quotes paragraph 3260.08 of the Standards of Practice, which requires the report to give the incremental cost between the calculation date and the next calculation date for the defined benefit portion of the plan.
- Incremental cost
    - The present value, at the calculation date (time 0), of the expected aggregate change in the hypothetical wind-up or solvency liability between time 0 and the next calculation date (time t), adjusted upwards for expected benefit payments between time 0 and time t.
- Calculation methodology
    - Present value at time 0 of expected benefit payments between time 0 and time t, plus the projected liability at time t discounted to time 0, less the liability at time 0.
- Assumptions
- Additional considerations
- Incremental cost versus service cost
    - The service cost generally represents the expected change in liability due to service rendered during the period; the incremental cost represents the expected change in liability due to all factors other than expected benefit payments.
