A textbook offering a comprehensive introduction to models and techniques for the emerging field of actuarial finance. Wiley describes it as a clear, application-oriented guide that focuses on the mathematical models and techniques used in actuarial finance for the pricing and hedging of actuarial liabilities exposed to financial markets and other contingencies. It runs to 592 pages in three parts: an introduction to actuarial finance, binomial and trinomial tree models, and the Black-Scholes-Merton model.
