A **Fellow of the Casualty Actuarial Society (FCAS)** is an [[Associate of the Casualty Actuarial Society (ACAS)|Associate]] of the Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS) who has passed three further exams at the advanced end of the same material: reserving, ratemaking, and the capital and risk questions an insurer's management and regulator argue about. It is the senior of the CAS's two designations.

%%credential-path FCAS%%

The three fellowship exams are written-answer papers, sat less often in the year than the associateship exams, so the calendar is part of the planning.

## What a Fellow can do

Fellowship is the level at which the profession expects an actuary to sign for the numbers. It is the qualification associated with senior reserving, pricing and capital roles and, together with the local regulator's own requirements (such as [[Fellow of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries (FCIA)|CIA Fellowship]] in Canada), with holding an [[Appointed Actuary]] role and issuing a [[Statement of Actuarial Opinion]]. Fellows also hold the CAS's governance roles: standing for the Board and chairing the committees that write the syllabus and the research.
