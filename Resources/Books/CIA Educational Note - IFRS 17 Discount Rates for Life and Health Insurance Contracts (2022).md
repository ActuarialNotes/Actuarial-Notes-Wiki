A CIA educational note that provides guidance on setting the discount rates used to calculate the present value of estimates of future cash flows under IFRS 17. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated June 30, 2022 (document 222097), its first chapter illustrates considerations in developing an entity's discount curve for the Canadian market, and its second presents reference curves for liquid and illiquid insurance contracts and guidance on disclosures in the Appointed Actuary's Report.
