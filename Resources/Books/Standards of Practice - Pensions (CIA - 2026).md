Part 3000 of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries Standards of Practice, the practice-specific standards for pension plans, in the complete Standards of Practice published January 2026 (accession 226011). Part 1000 applies to work within its scope. Its sections apply to advice on the funded status or funding of a pension plan, to full or partial wind-up valuations, to financial reporting of a pension plan's costs and obligations, and to the computation of commuted values.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcomes 2a-2d (Methods); Standards of Practice – Pensions, sections 3100-3500 (CIA, Jan 2026).

## 3100 Scope
- Section 3200 applies to advice on the funded status or funding of a pension plan, except advice on a wind-up or on financial reporting of the plan's costs and obligations.
- Section 3300 applies to advice on the funded status or funding with respect to the wind-up, in full or in part, of a pension plan.
- Section 3400 applies to advice on financial reporting of a pension plan's costs and obligations in the employer's or the pension plan's financial statements.
- Section 3500 applies to advice on the computation of commuted values in the circumstances described in subsection 3510.
