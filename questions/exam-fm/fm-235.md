You are given the following information about Bond X and Bond Y:

i) Both bonds are 20-year bonds.

ii) Both bonds have face amount 1500.

iii) Both bonds have an annual nominal yield rate of 7% compounded semiannually.

iv) Bond X has an annual coupon rate of 10% paid semiannually and a redemption value $C$.

v) Bond Y has an annual coupon rate of 8% paid semiannually and a redemption value $C + K$.

vi) The price of Bond X exceeds the price of Bond Y by 257.18.

Calculate $K$.

- A) -380
- B) -88
- C) 0
- D) 235
- E) 250

## Explanation

Both bonds use the semiannual yield $j = 3.5\%$ over $n = 40$ periods, with $a_{\overline{40}|0.035} = 21.355072$ and $v^{40} = 0.252572$. Bond X's semiannual coupon is $0.05(1500) = 75$ and Bond Y's is $0.04(1500) = 60$:
$$
\begin{align*}
P_X &= 75a_{\overline{40}|} + Cv^{40} \\
P_Y &= 60a_{\overline{40}|} + (C+K)v^{40}
\end{align*}
$$

The redemption value $C$ cancels in the difference:
$$
\begin{align*}
P_X - P_Y = 257.18 &= 15a_{\overline{40}|} - Kv^{40} \\
Kv^{40} &= 15a_{\overline{40}|} - 257.18 \\
K(0.252572) &= 320.326 - 257.18 \\
&= 63.146 \\
K &= 250.01
\end{align*}
$$
