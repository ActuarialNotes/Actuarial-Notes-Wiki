A married couple are both insureds under an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11). After a burglary, the husband, without his wife's knowledge, inflates the claim with items they never owned. The same month a guest is injured at the house and sues both spouses. How do the concealment or fraud conditions apply?

- A) Neither spouse is covered for the burglary or the guest's suit
- B) Both spouses keep their coverage, because the wife was innocent
- C) Only the husband loses coverage, under both Section I and Section II of the policy
- D) No one is covered for the burglary; the wife is still covered for the suit

## Explanation

The two sections word the condition differently. Under **Section I** the insurer provides coverage to **no** insured if any insured has concealed or misrepresented a material fact, engaged in fraudulent conduct or made false statements relating to the insurance. Under **Section II** it does not cover **an insured** who did so, so the penalty falls only on the husband. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** applies Section I's rule to Section II as well.
- **B** ignores that Section I's condition bars every insured, innocent or not.
- **C** applies Section II's individual rule to Section I.
