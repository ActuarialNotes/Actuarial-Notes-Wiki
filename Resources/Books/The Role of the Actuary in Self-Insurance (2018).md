![[The Role of the Actuary in Self-Insurance (2018) - Cover.svg]]

A monograph, published by the Society of Actuaries in May 2018, presenting the work of the Self-Insurance Task Force formed in early 2017 for a strategic initiative of the SOA Health Section Council. Its goal is to explain the many ways in which actuaries are or could be involved in the self-insured employer health plan marketplace today and in the future.

> - [[Exam GH-101 (SOA)|Exam GH-101]] — Outcomes 3a, 3d; Sections 4, 5 and the Appendices, excluding 4.1.1-4.1.5, 4.4, 4.6.1-4.6.3 and Appendix D.
