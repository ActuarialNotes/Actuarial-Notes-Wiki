The number of tornadoes in a given year follows a Poisson distribution with mean 3.

Calculate the variance of the number of tornadoes in a year given that at least one tornado occurs.

- A) 1.63
- B) 1.73
- C) 2.66
- D) 3.00
- E) 3.16

## Explanation

Let $N\sim\text{Poisson}(3)$ be the number of tornadoes in a year.

$E[N|N\geq1]=3/(1-e^{-3})\approx3.157$, $E[N^2|N\geq1]=12/(1-e^{-3})\approx12.629$.

$\text{Var}(N\mid N\geq1)=12.629-3.157^2\approx\mathbf{2.66}$
