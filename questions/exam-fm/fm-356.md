At a force of interest $\delta_t = \dfrac{0.5}{5+0.5t},\ 0 \le t \le 6$ an investment of 1000 at time $t = 2$ will accumulate to $X$ at time $t = 6$. At an annual nominal rate of discount of 8% convertible quarterly, an investment of $Y$ will accumulate to $X$ at the end of two years.

Calculate $Y$.

- A) 1124
- B) 1129
- C) 1134
- D) 1138
- E) 1143

## Explanation

$X$ is 1000 accumulated from $t = 2$ to $t = 6$ under the force of interest:

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= 1000\exp\left(\int_{2}^{6} \frac{0.5}{5+0.5t}\,dt\right) \\
  &= 1000\exp\left[\left.\ln(5+0.5t)\right|_{2}^{6}\right] \\
  &= 1000\exp(\ln 8 - \ln 6) \\
  &= 1000\left(\frac{8}{6}\right) \\
  &= 1333.33
\end{align*}
$$

At an annual nominal rate of discount of 8% convertible quarterly, $Y$ accumulates over two years ($4 \times 2 = 8$ quarters) by the factor $\left(1 - \dfrac{0.08}{4}\right)^{-8}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
1333.33 &= Y\left(1 - \frac{0.08}{4}\right)^{-4(2)} \\
Y &= 1333.33(0.98)^{8} \\
  &= 1333.33(0.850763) \\
  &= 1134.35
\end{align*}
$$
