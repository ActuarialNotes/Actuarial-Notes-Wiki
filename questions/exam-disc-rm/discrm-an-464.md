Under the CAS's four types of risk for an enterprise (hazard, financial, operational and strategic), which of the following is a strategic risk for a furniture manufacturer?

- A) A shift in customer tastes away from its main product line
- B) A default by a large retail customer on its trade credit
- C) A windstorm that damages the roof of its main plant
- D) A failure of the payroll system that delays wages

## Explanation

The CAS's 2003 ERM overview lists customer wants among the sources of **strategic** risk, along with competition, reputational damage, demographic and social trends, technological innovation, capital availability, and regulatory and political trends. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Credit default is a **financial** risk.
- **C** — Windstorm and other natural perils are **hazard** risks.
- **D** — A failure of information technology in the firm's own operations is an **operational** risk.
