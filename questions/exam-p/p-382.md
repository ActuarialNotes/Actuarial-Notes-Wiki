A company's website consists of 30 pages. Five pages contain low graphical content, ten pages contain moderate graphical content, and fifteen pages contain high graphical content. Four pages are randomly selected from the website without replacement. Let:

$X$ = number of pages selected which contain moderate graphical content, and
$Y$ = number of pages selected which contain high graphical content.

Calculate the conditional variance of $Y$, given that $X = 3$.

- A) $0.1875$
- B) $0.2469$
- C) $0.5625$
- D) $0.7500$
- E) $1.3125$

## Explanation

Four pages are drawn in total. Given $X = 3$, three of them are moderate, so exactly **one** page remains to be accounted for — it is either low or high. That forces $Y \in \{0, 1\}$, making the conditional distribution of $Y$ a Bernoulli.

Only the split between the 5 low and 15 high pages matters for that last slot. Conditioning on $X=3$ means the remaining page was drawn from the 20 non-moderate pages, so

$$
\begin{align*}
P[Y = 1 \mid X = 3] &= \frac{15}{20} = 0.75 \\
P[Y = 0 \mid X = 3] &= \frac{5}{20} = 0.25
\end{align*}
$$

Formally, with $W$ the count of low-content pages, the conditional probability is a ratio of hypergeometric terms in which the moderate-page factor cancels:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[Y = 0 \mid X = 3] &= \frac{P[W = 1, X = 3]}{P[X = 3]} \\
                    &= \frac{\binom{5}{1}\binom{10}{3}\big/\binom{30}{4}}{\binom{20}{1}\binom{10}{3}\big/\binom{30}{4}} \\
                    &= \frac{5}{20} = 0.25
\end{align*}
$$

For a Bernoulli variable with success probability $p$, the variance is $p(1-p)$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(Y \mid X = 3) &= (0.75)(0.25) \\
                         &= \mathbf{0.1875}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (D) is the conditional *mean* $E[Y \mid X = 3] = 0.75$ rather than the variance. The work in this question is entirely in the first observation — that fixing $X=3$ leaves a single free page, collapsing $Y$ to two possible values.
