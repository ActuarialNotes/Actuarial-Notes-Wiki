## [F-001] Thousands comma inside math renders as a list separator
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-27
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: stem, line 1
- claim: uniform distribution on the interval $[0, 10,000]$
- evidence: SOA Q109, questions PDF p.48 (page image) prints the interval as plain text [0, 10,000]. Inside TeX math a comma is punctuation and takes a thin space after it, so $[0, 10,000]$ typesets as [0, 10, 000], which reads as three numbers. The value is right; only the typesetting misleads. 31 files in questions/exam-p put a thousands comma inside $...$.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: Write 10{,}000 inside math, or take the interval out of math as SOA prints it.
- applied: false
- fingerprint: 02275f0d6b84
