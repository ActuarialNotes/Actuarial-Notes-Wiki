## [F-001] Thousands commas inside inline math render with a thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 42 and 44
- claim: Inline math writes 100,000, 6,505.14 (twice) and 364,840 with a bare comma, which KaTeX typesets as punctuation ('100, 000').
- evidence: In TeX/KaTeX math a bare ',' is a punctuation atom followed by a thin space; the display block below already writes 100{,}000 and 364{,}840. Values are right: 100000/a_30 @5% (15.372451) = 6,505.14; s_30 @4% = 56.0849; product 364,840.1 (SOA solutions p.56: 364,841 unrounded).
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Write the separators as {,} inside math.
- applied: true
