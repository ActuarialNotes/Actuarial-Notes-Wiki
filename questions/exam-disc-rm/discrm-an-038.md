A homeowner has a mortgage on her house. She rents the basement apartment to a tenant, who has paid for and installed his own kitchen cabinets there. If fire destroys the house, which parties have a financial interest in property exposed to loss?

- A) Only the homeowner, because she owns the house
- B) The homeowner and the lender, but not the tenant
- C) The homeowner, the lender and the tenant
- D) Only the lender, until the loan is repaid

## Explanation

The owner is not the only party who can lose. A mortgagee loses when the collateral behind its loan is destroyed, and a tenant can lose the improvements he paid for and the use of the premises. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Leaves out the lender's interest in its collateral and the tenant's interest in his improvements.
- **B** — Leaves out the tenant, whose cabinets and use of the apartment are lost too.
- **D** — The homeowner keeps her interest in the house whether or not the loan is outstanding.
