An introductory textbook on the mathematics of pension funding and valuation, written by William H. Aitken of the University of Waterloo and first published in 1994. Its defining characteristic, in its preface, is a large number of worked examples: each unit is introduced with enough explanatory material to allow the reader to move quickly to problem solving, and the text adds discussion questions and exercises. It is organized into eight chapters, from an introduction to pension plans through the cost methods, gain and loss analysis, plan changes, options and assets, to an overview of the earlier material.
