A July 2019 public policy practice note from the Pension Committee of the American Academy of Actuaries, giving information on current and emerging practices in the selection of investment return assumptions based on anticipated future experience. It is built around Actuarial Standard of Practice No. 27, which provides guidance in selecting economic assumptions such as investment return, discount rates and compensation increases, and it addresses considerations when using arithmetic and geometric averages. It is not an actuarial standard of practice.
