The time to failure of an electrical appliance has an exponential distribution. The mean of this distribution exceeds its median by 3.80.

Calculate the variance of this distribution.

- A) $12$
- B) $14$
- C) $30$
- D) $153$
- E) $231$

## Explanation

Let $\lambda$ be the mean of $X$ and $m$ its median. From the survival function,

$$
0.5 = P[X \ge m] = e^{-m/\lambda} \implies m = \lambda \ln 2
$$

So an exponential's median is always $\ln 2 = 0.693$ times its mean, and the stated gap gives

$$
\begin{align*}
3.8 &= \lambda - \lambda \ln 2 = \lambda(1 - \ln 2) \\
\lambda &= \frac{3.8}{0.30685} = 12.3838
\end{align*}
$$

For an exponential the variance is the square of the mean:

$$
\text{Var}(X) = \lambda^2 = \mathbf{153.36}
$$

Choice (A), 12, is $\lambda = 12.38$ — the mean, which for an exponential also equals the standard deviation, but not the variance. That coincidence between mean and standard deviation is exactly what makes this a trap: the two differ from the variance by a factor of $\lambda$ itself.
