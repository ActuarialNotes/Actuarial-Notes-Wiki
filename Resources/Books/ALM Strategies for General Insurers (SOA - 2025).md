A Society of Actuaries study note (May 2025) titled "ALM in General Insurance". It combines two articles, both written in 2013, from the discontinued Financial Economics, Regulation and Law Module for the General Insurance track: one compares asset-liability management (ALM) for general insurance companies with ALM for life insurance companies, the other looks at ALM strategies for general insurance companies.
