A named insured carries Parts A, B and D of the ISO personal auto policy. She causes an accident that injures her own passenger, a friend, damages the other driver's car and damages her own car. Which pairing of loss and part is correct?

- A) Her own car's damage is paid under Part A, because she was at fault
- B) The other driver's car is paid under Part D, as damage to a non-owned auto
- C) Her friend's medical bills can be paid under Part B without proof of fault
- D) Her friend's medical bills are paid only under Part A, because she was at fault

## Explanation

Part B pays reasonable medical expenses of anyone occupying the covered auto, without regard to fault. Her friend may also claim against her under Part A, but the policy will not pay the same expense twice. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Part A pays others for damage the insured is liable for; damage to her own car is Part D, collision.
- **B** — a "non-owned auto" is one the insured is driving or has custody of. Damage she causes to another driver's car is property damage liability under Part A.
- **D** — fault makes Part A available to the friend, but it doesn't take away the no-fault Part B payment.
