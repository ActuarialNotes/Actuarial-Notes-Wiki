Given the following information for a general liability policy, determine the value of X that yields an experience modification of +4.5%.

- Effective period of the policy: January 1 to December 31, 2014.
- Expected loss ratio: 65.6%.
- Type of policy being rated: Claims-made.

### Explanation
The company subject loss cost is CSLC = 147,830, which falls in the \$145,184 – \$151,800 band, so $Z = 0.34$ (printed as ".034"), EER = 0.870 and MSL = 111,400.

For each policy year and subline, ARULL = CSLC × EER × LDF. The 2012 policy is claims-made, so no LDF applies to it:

| PY | Coverage | CSLC | EER | LDF | ARULL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | Prem/Ops | 32,160 | 0.870 | 0 | 0 |
| 2012 | Products | 6,679 | 0.870 | 0 | 0 |
| 2011 | Prem/Ops | 42,832 | 0.870 | 0.338 | 12,595 |
| 2011 | Products | 14,137 | 0.870 | 0.637 | 7,835 |
| 2010 | Prem/Ops | 38,695 | 0.870 | 0.198 | 6,666 |
| 2010 | Products | 13,327 | 0.870 | 0.528 | 6,122 |
| Total | | 147,830 | | | 33,218 |

$$\text{Mod} = Z \times \frac{AER - EER}{EER} \qquad 0.045 = 0.34 \times \frac{AER - 0.87}{0.87}$$

$$AER = 0.985147059 = \frac{102{,}718 + X + 33{,}218}{147{,}830}$$

$$X = 9{,}698$$

### Examiner Report
Common errors that resulted in partial credit:

- LDFs were used for the Claims Made (2012) Policy
- Did not multiply by EER in calculation of the ARULL
- In the formula for the Mod, some candidates used 1+Mod instead of just the mod itself on the left side of the equation
- Candidates did not do the entire first step to calculate the ARULL
- There were some candidates who did not explicitly calculate the AER, however if they arrived at the correct answer, they were given full credit. If the candidate did not explicitly calculate the AER, but got the wrong answer, additional deductions were taken since the steps were not clearly documented.
- Incorrect formulas
- Did not calculate includable losses correctly
- Some candidates wrote down the correct credibility to use somewhere on the page, but actually used something different in formulas and calculations
