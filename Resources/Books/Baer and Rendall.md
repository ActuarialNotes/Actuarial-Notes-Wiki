A casebook of Canadian insurance law — court decisions on the law of insurance in Canada — edited by Marvin G. Baer and James A. Rendall. This sixth edition was published by Carswell in Scarborough, Ontario, in 2000, and runs to lxi and 961 pages; its library record files it under the subject "Insurance law — Canada — Cases".

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] — objective A2; pp. 67–91, 93–100, 302–304, 518–529, 821–827 and 829–831, and candidates are responsible for Glenn v. Scottish Union and National Insurance Company Ltd. (Chapter 1), Fletcher v. MPIC (Chapter 8) and Dillon v. Guardian Insurance (Chapter 11); in the study kit.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> No copy of this casebook could be found online; the CAS supplies it in the Exam 6C study kit, and Google Books shows only its table of cases, table of concordance and first section, "History and Basic Theory of Insurance".
