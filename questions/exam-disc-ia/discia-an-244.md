A state regulates homeowners rates under a flex rating law, requiring prior approval of any rate change of more than 7% above or below the rates on file. An insurer plans a 5% increase in one territory and a 10% increase in another. Which changes need prior approval?

- A) Both, because every rate change in a flex rating state needs prior approval
- B) Neither, because flex rating means rates are not filed
- C) Only the 10% increase
- D) Only the 5% increase

## Explanation

Under **flex rating**, prior approval is required only for changes beyond a set percentage of the rates on file. A 5% change is inside the 7% band, so it takes effect without prior approval under the state's filing rules. A 10% change is outside it, so it needs **prior approval**.

- **A** describes a prior approval law for every change.
- **B** describes a no-file law, under which rates are not filed but the insurer keeps its supporting records for the commissioner.
- **D** reverses the band: the larger change is the one that needs approval.
