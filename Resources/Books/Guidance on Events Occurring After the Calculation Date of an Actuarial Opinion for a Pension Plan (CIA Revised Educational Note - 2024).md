A Canadian Institute of Actuaries educational note, dated May 15, 2024 (document 224065), intended to assist pension actuaries in valuing and reporting on events that occur after the calculation date, when the actuary has determined that the event makes the entity different. It was originally published in January 2007 and updated in January 2015; the 2024 update refers to the Standards of Practice effective December 1, 2022 and adds subsequent events in reporting a pension plan's costs and obligations in financial statements. It was approved for distribution on May 14, 2024.
