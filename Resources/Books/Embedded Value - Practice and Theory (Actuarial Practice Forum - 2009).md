![[Embedded Value - Practice and Theory (Actuarial Practice Forum - 2009) - Cover.svg]]

A paper in the March 2009 issue of the Society of Actuaries' Actuarial Practice Forum that introduces basic embedded value (EV) principles, concepts and formulas, aimed at the beginner student of EV. It describes embedded value as a financial measurement basis applied primarily to long-duration insurance business, and covers EV mechanics, assumptions, financial options and guarantees, the analysis of movement and disclosure, with an appendix on market-consistent embedded value.
