An August 2017 Society of Actuaries paper, written by Irina Pogrebivsky and sponsored by the SOA Pension Section Council, on applying credibility theory to the mortality assumption for pension plans. It aims to give a clear and concise overview of credibility theory, to describe and compare the standard approaches, and to show how they apply to adjusting a standard mortality table to fit a specific plan's experience.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 2d; the whole paper, which the SOA describes as important because credibility theory is used in the selection of many types of assumptions.
