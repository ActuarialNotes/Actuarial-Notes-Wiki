A research report by Raghu Ramachandran of S&P Dow Jones Indices and Aaron Sarfatti of Oliver Wyman, written by Oliver Wyman in the fall of 2017 for S&P DJI. It examines the properties of alternative volatility management solutions for guaranteed variable annuities from the perspective of both policyholders and guarantee manufacturers, with a focus on the role of volatility-indexed fees in a potential "next generation" solution. Its purpose is to give manufacturers a framework for weighing the trade-offs of each solution and the implications of certain features. The SOA supplies it as study note INV201-106-25.
