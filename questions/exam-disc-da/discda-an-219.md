Colorado's SB21-169 (C.R.S. §10-3-1104.9) restricts insurers' use of external consumer data and information sources, and of algorithms and predictive models that use them. Which of the following uses falls within the statute?

- A) A title insurer's use of public court records to find liens on a property
- B) A large manufacturer's commercial property insurer using satellite roof images
- C) A homeowners insurer's use of purchasing-habit data in underwriting
- D) A qualified surety company's use of contractors' financial statements for bonds

## Explanation

The statute defines external consumer data and information sources to include credit scores, social media habits, locations, purchasing habits, home ownership, educational attainment, occupation, licensures, civil judgments and court records, and it applies to insurance practices including underwriting. A homeowners insurer is within its scope. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — title insurance is expressly excluded from the section.
- **B** — insurers issuing commercial policies are excluded, except for businessowners and commercial general liability policies with annual premiums of \$10,000 or less; a large manufacturer's property policy is neither.
- **D** — bonds executed by qualified surety companies are expressly excluded.
