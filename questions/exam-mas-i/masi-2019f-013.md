An actuary is using the inversion method to simulate the waiting time until the $5^{\text{th}}$ event of a Poisson process with a rate $\lambda = 1$.

Five random draws from $U(0, 1)$ are provided below:

$$
0.2, 0.7, 0.8, 0.3, 0.5
$$

Calculate the simulated waiting time until the $5^{\text{th}}$ event.

- A) Less than 2.5
- B) At least 2.5, but less than 3.5
- C) At least 3.5, but less than 4.5
- D) At least 4.5, but less than 5.5
- E) At least 5.5

## Explanation

The waiting time until the $5^{\text{th}}$ event is the sum of five independent interarrival times, each exponential with mean $1/\lambda = 1$. Simulate each by inverting the exponential CDF $F(x) = 1 - e^{-x}$:

$$
u = 1 - e^{-x} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad x = -\ln(1-u).
$$

Applying this to the five draws:

$$
\begin{align*}
T_5 &= -\ln(0.8) - \ln(0.3) - \ln(0.2) - \ln(0.7) - \ln(0.5) \\
&= 0.2231 + 1.2040 + 1.6094 + 0.3567 + 0.6931 \\
&= 4.0864
\end{align*}
$$

(Here the complements $1-u$ are the same five numbers as the draws, so using $x = -\ln u$ instead gives the same total.)

This falls in $[3.5, 4.5)$, so the answer is **C**.
