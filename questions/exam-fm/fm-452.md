An investment project involves an outlay of 30 at time 0 and 40 at time 1, where time is measured in years. In return the investor will receive 60 at time 2 and 90 at time $n$, where $n \ge 3$.

Determine which one of the following statements about the annual effective yield rate is true.

- A) There is a unique yield rate, which is higher than 50%.
- B) There is a unique yield rate, which is lower than 50%.
- C) There are multiple yield rates, but all are higher than 50%.
- D) There are multiple yield rates, but all are lower than 50%.
- E) There are multiple yield rates with at least one lower than 50% and at least one higher than 50%.

## Explanation

Because the outlays (at times 0 and 1) all occur before the inflows (at times 2 and $n$), the cash flows change sign only once, so there is a unique yield rate.

To see which side of 50% it lies on, find the time of the final payment that would make 50% the yield rate:

$$
\begin{align*}
-30 - \frac{40}{1.5} + \frac{60}{1.5^{2}} + \frac{90}{1.5^{n}} &= 0 \\
-30 + \frac{90}{1.5^{n}} &= 0 \\
1.5^{n} &= 3 \\
n &= \frac{\ln 3}{\ln 1.5} \\
  &= 2.71
\end{align*}
$$

Since $2.71 < 3$, the final payment actually comes later, so at 50% its present value is less than 30 and the net present value is negative. For the later final payment to be worth enough, the yield rate must be lower: there is a unique yield rate, and it is lower than 50%.
