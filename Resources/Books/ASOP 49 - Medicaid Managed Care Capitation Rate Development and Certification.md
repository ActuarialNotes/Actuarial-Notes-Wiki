![[ASOP 49 - Medicaid Managed Care Capitation Rate Development and Certification - Cover.svg]]

The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries performing professional services related to Medicaid (Title XIX) and Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP, Title XXI) managed care capitation rates, including a certification on behalf of a state to meet the requirements of 42 CFR 438.6(c). It (Doc. No. 179) was adopted in March 2015 and is effective for actuarial communications issued on or after August 1, 2015.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201U (SOA)|Exam GH-201U]] — Outcomes 4b; pp. 1-12.

## Transmittal Memorandum

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
-     - Applies to actuaries performing professional services related to Medicaid managed care capitation rates, including certification on behalf of a state to meet 42 CFR 438.6(c), capitation rate bid or rate acceptance, and department of insurance capitation rate filing.
-     - Also applies to managed care capitation rates for the Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP); throughout the standard "Medicaid" also refers to CHIP.
- 1.3 Cross References
- 1.4 Effective Date
-     - Effective for actuarial communications issued on or after August 1, 2015.
