Accounting differences can concern recognition (whether an item appears), measurement (the amount) or presentation (where and how it is shown). Which difference between statutory accounting and GAAP is a difference in presentation?

- A) SAP nonadmits premium receivable more than 90 days past due
- B) SAP nets reinsurance recoverable on unpaid losses against loss reserves
- C) SAP expenses acquisition costs as incurred instead of deferring them
- D) SAP excludes furniture and equipment from the balance sheet

## Explanation

Netting the recoverable on unpaid losses against the reserve changes where the reinsurance appears, not whether it is counted: GAAP shows the gross liability with a reinsurance recoverable asset, while SAP shows the reserve net. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — nonadmitting an overdue receivable is a recognition difference: under SAP's recognition concept the asset is not counted in surplus.
- **C** — expensing rather than deferring acquisition costs is a recognition (timing) difference: GAAP recognizes an asset that SAP does not.
- **D** — excluding furniture and equipment is again recognition: such assets are nonadmitted because they could not be used to pay policyholders.
