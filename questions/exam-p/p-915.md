An insured's annual loss $X$ is uniformly distributed on $[0, b]$. Let $Y = X/3 + 4$. Given $E[Y] = 20$, calculate $b$.

- A) 48
- B) 96
- C) 120
- D) 144
- E) 192

## Explanation

$E[X] = b/2$ for $X \sim \text{Uniform}[0,b]$.

$$
\begin{align*}
E[Y] &= \frac{E[X]}{3}+4 \\
     &= \frac{b/2}{3}+4 \\
     &= \frac{b}{6}+4 \\
     &= 20
\end{align*}
$$

$$\frac{b}{6} = 16 \implies b = \mathbf{96}$$
