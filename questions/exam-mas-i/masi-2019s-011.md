You are given the following information:

- An annuity-due is issued to a 30-year-old that will pay 1 each year until either she dies or reaches age 50, whichever comes first.
- Mortality follows the Illustrative Life Table.
- Annual interest rate $i = 6\%$.

Calculate the actuarial present value of this annuity.

- A) Less than 10.0
- B) At least 10.0, but less than 10.5
- C) At least 10.5, but less than 11.0
- D) At least 11.0, but less than 11.5
- E) At least 11.5

## Explanation

The annuity pays 1 at the start of each year from age 30 to age 49 while she is alive — a 20-year temporary life annuity-due. Write it as the whole life annuity-due less the part that starts at age 50:

$$
\ddot{a}_{30:\overline{20}|} = \ddot{a}_{30} - {}_{20}E_{30}\,\ddot{a}_{50}
$$

From the Illustrative Life Table at $i = 6\%$: $\ddot{a}_{30} = 15.8561$, $\ddot{a}_{50} = 13.2668$, $\ell_{30} = 9{,}501{,}381$ and $\ell_{50} = 8{,}950{,}901$. The 20-year pure endowment factor is

$$
\begin{align*}
{}_{20}E_{30} &= v^{20}\,\frac{\ell_{50}}{\ell_{30}} \\
&= (1.06)^{-20}\,\frac{8{,}950{,}901}{9{,}501{,}381} \\
&= (0.31180)(0.94206) \\
&= 0.29374
\end{align*}
$$

so

$$
\begin{align*}
\ddot{a}_{30:\overline{20}|} &= 15.8561 - (0.29374)(13.2668) \\
&= 15.8561 - 3.8970 \\
&= 11.959
\end{align*}
$$

This is at least 11.5, so the answer is **E**.
