You apply 2-means clustering to a set of five observations with two features. You are given the following initial cluster assignments:

| Observation | X1 | X2 | Initial cluster |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| 2 | 0 | 4 | 1 |
| 3 | 6 | 2 | 1 |
| 4 | 5 | 2 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 | 6 | 2 |

Calculate the total within-cluster variation of the initial cluster assignments, based on Euclidean distance measure.

- A) 32.0
- B) 70.3
- C) 77.3
- D) 118.3
- E) 141.0

## Explanation

Cluster 1 holds observations 1, 2 and 3. Its means are $(1 + 0 + 6)/3 = 2.3333$ for $X_1$ and $(3 + 4 + 2)/3 = 3$ for $X_2$, and the sum of squared distances from that centroid is

$$
\begin{align*}
&(1-2.3333)^2 + (3-3)^2 + (0-2.3333)^2 + (4-3)^2 + (6-2.3333)^2 + (2-3)^2 \\
&= 22.6667.
\end{align*}
$$

Cluster 2 holds observations 4 and 5. Its means are $(5 + 1)/2 = 3$ for $X_1$ and $(2 + 6)/2 = 4$ for $X_2$, and the sum of squared distances from that centroid is

$$
\begin{align*}
&(5-3)^2 + (2-4)^2 + (1-3)^2 + (6-4)^2 \\
&= 16.
\end{align*}
$$

The within-cluster variation of a cluster, $W(C_k) = \frac{1}{|C_k|}\sum_{i, i' \in C_k}\sum_j (x_{ij} - x_{i'j})^2$, equals twice its sum of squared distances from the centroid (equation (10.12) in the first edition of *ISLR*). The total within-cluster variation is therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
W(C_1) + W(C_2) &= 2(22.6667 + 16) \\
&= 77.33.
\end{align*}
$$
