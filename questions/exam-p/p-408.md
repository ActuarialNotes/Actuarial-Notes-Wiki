A random variable $X$ follows a beta distribution with $\alpha = 2$ and $\beta = 2$.

Calculate $P(0.25 < X < 0.75)$.

- A) $0.500$
- B) $0.625$
- C) $0.688$
- D) $0.750$
- E) $0.844$

## Explanation

$$
\begin{align*}
B(2,2) &= \frac{\Gamma(2)\Gamma(2)}{\Gamma(4)} = \frac{1 \cdot 1}{6} \\
f(x) &= 6x(1-x), \qquad 0 < x < 1
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
P(0.25 < X < 0.75) &= 6\int_{0.25}^{0.75}\left(x - x^2\right) dx \\
                   &= 6\left[\frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_{0.25}^{0.75} \\
                   &= 6\left[0.140625 - 0.026042\right] \\
                   &= 6(0.114583) \\
                   &= \mathbf{0.688}
\end{align*}
$$

With $\alpha = \beta$ the beta is symmetric about $0.5$, which is a useful check: the answer must exceed the $0.50$ a uniform distribution would give, because the $\text{Beta}(2,2)$ density concentrates mass toward the centre.
