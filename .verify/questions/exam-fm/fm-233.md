## [F-002] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space ('100, 000')

- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Options A-E (lines 33-37) and Explanation line 47
