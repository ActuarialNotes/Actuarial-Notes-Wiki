A steel company issued a ten-year 1,000,000 face amount bond on March 1, 2002 with an annual nominal coupon rate of 8% paid semiannually.

On September 2, 2004, Company X paid 1,100,000 for this bond to yield an annual nominal rate of $i$ convertible semiannually.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 4.0%
- B) 5.5%
- C) 6.3%
- D) 8.0%
- E) 9.7%

## Explanation

The bond was issued March 1, 2002, so its semiannual coupons of $1{,}000{,}000(0.04) = 40{,}000$ are paid on March 1 and September 1. The purchase on September 2, 2004 is one day after the fifth coupon (September 1, 2004); as in SOA's solution, ignore that one day and value the bond just after the fifth coupon, with $10 - 2.5 = 7.5$ years (15 semiannual periods) remaining.

$$MV_5 = 1{,}100{,}000 = 40{,}000a_{\overline{15}|j} + 1{,}000{,}000(1+j)^{-15}$$

Using a financial calculator: $PV = -1{,}100{,}000$, $PMT = 40{,}000$, $FV = 1{,}000{,}000$, $N=15$:
$j = 3.153\%$, so $i^{(2)} = 2j = 6.306\% \approx 6.3\%$
