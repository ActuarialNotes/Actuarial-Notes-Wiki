The Autorité des marchés financiers' capital adequacy guideline for property and casualty ("damage") insurers authorized in Québec, January 2025 revision. It sets out the capital framework — a risk-based formula for target and minimum capital requirements, the [[MCT|Minimum Capital Test (MCT)]] — and defines the capital available to meet the minimum standard. The guideline applies to all insurers authorized to carry on P&C insurance activities in Québec (excluding self-regulatory organizations and reciprocal unions), on a consolidated basis, and this version takes effect on January 1, 2025, for fiscal years beginning on or after that date.

- 1.2 Risk-based capital adequacy
    - Capital requirements are set at a target confidence level of 99% of the expected shortfall (CTE 99%) over a one-year horizon, including a terminal provision.
    - Target capital is the sum of the capital required for [[Insurance Risk|insurance risk]], [[Market Risk|market risk]], [[Credit Risk|credit risk]] and [[Operational Risk|operational risk]], less the [[Diversification Credit|diversification credit]]; minimum capital required is target capital divided by 1.5.
    - The MCT ratio is capital available divided by minimum capital required.
- 1.3 General requirements
    - 1.3.1 Minimum ratio, intervention target ratio and internal capital target ratio
        - Insurers maintain, continuously and at a minimum, an MCT ratio of 100%; the AMF expects an intervention target ratio of 150% in its supervisory activities.
        - Each insurer sets an internal capital target ratio above the 150% intervention target ratio, reflecting its [[Risk Appetite|risk appetite]] and the results of sensitivity analyses, and taking into account at least residual credit, market and insurance risks, liquidity, concentration, regulatory and strategic risk, and access to market capital.
