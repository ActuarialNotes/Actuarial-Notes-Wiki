You are given the following information:

- Computer lifetimes are exponentially distributed with mean of 10 months.
- Computer A has been functioning properly for 12 months.

Calculate the probability that Computer A will function properly for at least 4 more months.

- A) Less than 0.64
- B) At least 0.64, but less than 0.66
- C) At least 0.66, but less than 0.68
- D) At least 0.68, but less than 0.70
- E) At least 0.70

## Explanation

The exponential distribution is memoryless: given that the computer has already lasted 12 months, its remaining lifetime is again exponential with mean 10, so the 12 months of past service do not matter.

$$
\begin{align*}
\Pr(T > 16 \mid T > 12) &= \frac{S(16)}{S(12)} \\
&= \frac{e^{-16/10}}{e^{-12/10}} \\
&= e^{-4/10} \\
&= 0.6703
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.66, 0.68)$, so the answer is **C**.
