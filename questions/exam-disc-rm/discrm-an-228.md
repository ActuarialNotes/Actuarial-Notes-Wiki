A car rental agency keeps about 150 new vehicles parked together on one open lot in a region known for hailstorms. For physical damage coverage, which concern and response is most appropriate for the underwriter?

- A) A hail catastrophe accumulation; manage it with the deductible and the choice of covered causes
- B) Collision frequency from renters; decline comprehensive and offer collision only
- C) Theft of individual vehicles; require an alarm on the lot and offer collision only
- D) Liability for renters' accidents; require the agency to buy a higher liability limit

## Explanation

Vehicles parked together create a catastrophe accumulation, because one hailstorm can damage all 150 at once. The underwriter's tools are the deductible and the choice between comprehensive and specified causes of loss, which together control how much of an accumulation loss the insurer takes. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Collision coverage doesn't cover hail at all, and declining comprehensive ignores the accumulation the lot creates.
- **C** — Theft matters, but an alarm doesn't address the larger exposure of 150 vehicles in one hail zone.
- **D** — Liability is a different coverage; the question is about damage to the agency's own vehicles.
