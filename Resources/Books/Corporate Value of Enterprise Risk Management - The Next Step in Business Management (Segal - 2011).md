A book synthesizing enterprise risk management (ERM) and value-based management, presenting ERM as a strategic business management approach. Its publisher calls it the first book to introduce this emerging approach, says it clarifies ERM as an approach that enhances strategic planning and other decision-making processes, and describes it as a practical guide for corporate Chief Risk Officers (CROs) and other business professionals seeking to implement ERM to protect and increase shareholder value.
