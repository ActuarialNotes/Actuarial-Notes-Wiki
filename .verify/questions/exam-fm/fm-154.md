## [F-001] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 48-49, 56-59 and 65
- claim: Math spans write thousands separators as a bare comma (112,955, 107,676, 107,533), which KaTeX sets as punctuation followed by a thin space ('112, 955').
- evidence: KaTeX treats ',' in math mode as punctuation (mpunct) and adds a thin space after it; '{,}' makes it an ordinary character. The digits themselves match SOA's solution PDF.
- source_rank: 5
