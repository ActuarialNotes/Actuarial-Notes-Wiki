Kelly purchases a 25-year annual coupon bond for 20,000 with an annual effective yield rate of $i$. The amount for the accumulation of discount in the first coupon is 50.

The bond's book value is 20,491.51 just after the 8th coupon payment and 21,263.74 just after the 16th coupon payment.

Calculate the bond's redemption value.

- A) 22,135
- B) 22,170
- C) 22,234
- D) 22,671
- E) 23,660

## Explanation

Each amount for accumulation of discount is $1+i$ times the one before, so the amount in the $t$th coupon is $50(1+i)^{t-1}$, and the total accumulation of discount over the first $t$ coupons (the increase in book value since purchase) is $50\,s_{\overline{t}|i}$. After the 8th coupon this total is $20{,}491.51 - 20{,}000 = 491.51$:

$$50\,s_{\overline{8}|i} = 491.51$$

Using a BA II Plus calculator, $i$ can be computed as 5.8099441%.

The same rate follows algebraically from the two book values. The accumulation of discount over the first 16 coupons is $21{,}263.74 - 20{,}000 = 1263.74 = 50\,s_{\overline{16}|i}$, and $(1+i)^{16} - 1 = \left[(1+i)^{8} + 1\right]\left[(1+i)^{8} - 1\right]$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{(1+i)^{16} - 1}{(1+i)^{8} - 1} &= \frac{1263.74}{491.51} \\
(1+i)^{8} + 1 &= 2.57114 \\
(1+i)^{8} &= 1.57114 \\
i &= 0.0581
\end{align*}
$$

Just after the 25th and last coupon, the book value equals the redemption value, so the redemption value is the purchase price plus the accumulation of discount over all 25 coupons:

$$
\begin{align*}
C &= 20{,}000 + 50\,s_{\overline{25}|0.058099441} \\
  &= 20{,}000 + 2670.91 \\
  &= 22{,}670.91
\end{align*}
$$
