A five-year loan has an annual nominal interest rate of 30%, convertible monthly. The loan is scheduled to be repaid with level monthly payments of 500, beginning one month after the date of the loan.

The borrower misses the thirteenth through the eighteenth payments, but increases the next six payments to $X$ so that the final 36 payments of 500 will repay the loan.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 1070
- B) 1075
- C) 1080
- D) 1150
- E) 1160

## Explanation

Monthly rate $j = 2.5\%$.

After 12 payments, the outstanding balance is $500a_{\overline{48}|0.025} = 13{,}886.58$.

This balance grows for 6 months (months 13–18, no payments): $13{,}886.58(1.025)^6 = 16{,}104.02$.

Wait — the solution says balance after year 1 is $500a_{\overline{48}|0.025} = 13{,}886.58$, which must equal the PV of the revised plan at that time:
$$
\begin{align*}
13{,}886.58 &= Xv^6 a_{\overline{6}|0.025} + 500v^{12}a_{\overline{36}|0.025} \\
          &= 4.74964X + 8{,}757.69
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= (13{,}886.58 - 8{,}757.69)/4.74964 \\
  &= 1{,}079.85 \approx 1080
\end{align*}
$$
