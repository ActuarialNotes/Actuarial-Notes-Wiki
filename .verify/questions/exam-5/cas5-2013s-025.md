- evidence: The examiner's report (PDF p.84) names exactly this as a common error: 'applying the ratio to 50% of case reserves and 100% of IBNR, despite the question clearly identifying the policy as being claims-made'. The two rank-1 parts of the same document conflict, so the key is not changed here.
