Let $X$ be a random variable with cumulative distribution function

$$
F(x) = \begin{cases}
0, & x < 0 \\[2pt]
\dfrac{1}{2}x^3, & 0 \leq x < 1 \\[2pt]
2x - \dfrac{x^2}{2} - 1, & 1 \leq x \leq 2 \\[2pt]
1, & x > 2.
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate $E(X)$.

- A) $3/8$
- B) $25/48$
- C) $2/3$
- D) $1$
- E) $25/24$

## Explanation

What is given is the **cumulative** distribution function, so differentiate each branch to get the density:

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{3}{2}x^2, & 0 \leq x < 1 \\[2pt]
2 - x, & 1 \leq x \leq 2 \\[2pt]
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Check the pieces meet: $F(1) = \tfrac{1}{2}$ from both branches, and $F(2) = 4 - 2 - 1 = 1$, so the function is continuous and reaches 1 as required.

Now integrate $xf(x)$ over each branch:

$$
\begin{align*}
\int_0^1 \frac{3}{2}x^3\,dx &= \frac{3}{8}x^4\bigg|_0^1 = \frac{3}{8} \\
\int_1^2 x(2-x)\,dx &= \left(x^2 - \frac{x^3}{3}\right)\bigg|_1^2 = \frac{4}{3} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3}
\end{align*}
$$

Adding:

$$
\begin{align*}
E(X) &= \frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{3} \\
     &= \frac{9 + 16}{24} \\
     &= \mathbf{\frac{25}{24}}
\end{align*}
$$

Choices (A) and (C) are the two pieces reported individually. The trap is integrating $xF(x)$ instead of $xf(x)$ — the differentiation step is easy to skip when the given function already looks integrable.
