A specialty insurer writes only commercial coverage for wineries and breweries. It charges more than general commercial insurers but offers coverage forms and risk control services designed for those businesses. Which generic business-level strategy does it follow?

- A) Cost leadership
- B) Focused differentiation
- C) Focused cost leadership
- D) Differentiation across the whole market

## Explanation

Porter's generic strategies combine the source of advantage, low cost or uniqueness, with the scope, broad or narrow. The insurer targets a narrow segment and competes on something unique rather than on price, which is focused differentiation. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A cost leader competes on low prices across a broad market; this insurer charges more.
- **C** — Focused cost leadership serves a narrow segment at the lowest cost, not at a premium price.
- **D** — Broad differentiation targets customers in general, while this insurer serves only one niche.
