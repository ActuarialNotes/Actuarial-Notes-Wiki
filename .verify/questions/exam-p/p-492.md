- claim: Choice (E) is the survival function rather than the cumulative one, and choice (A) is $0.8^x$ with the same error.
- evidence: Option (E) is F(x) = e^{-0.8x} (questions PDF p.199 page image). The survival function of X is P[X > x] = (e^{-lambda})^x = 0.8^x, which the explanation itself derives in its second display and which SOA Exam P Sample Solutions (Aug 2026 rev.) Q492, solutions PDF p.136 implies (F(x) = 1 - 0.80^x, key C). e^{-0.8x} is the survival function only of the wrong model with rate 0.8 (the complement of trap D): at x = 1 it gives e^{-0.8} = 0.449, not the stated 0.80. So (E) combines two errors (survival in place of CDF, and 0.8 read as the rate); calling it "the survival function" contradicts the derivation earlier on the page. The rest of the paragraph (A is the survival function, D reads 0.8 as the rate, true rate 0.2231, F(1) = 0.2) is correct.
