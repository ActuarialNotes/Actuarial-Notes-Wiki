A loan of amount $a_{\overline{4}|i}$ is repaid with payments of 1 at the end of each year for four years. The sum of the interest paid in the last two years is equal to $(1+i)^2$ times the sum of the principal repaid in the first two years.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 0.543
- B) 0.567
- C) 0.592
- D) 0.618
- E) 0.645

## Explanation

For a loan of $a_{\overline{n}|}$ repaid by level payments of 1, the principal repaid in payment $k$ is $P_k = v^{n-k+1}$ and the interest paid is $I_k = 1 - v^{n-k+1}$. Here $n = 4$.

Setting $I_3 + I_4 = (1+i)^2(P_1 + P_2)$:

$$(1-v^2)+(1-v) = (1+i)^2(v^4+v^3)$$

Since $(1+i)^2 v^2 = 1$, the right side is $v^2 + v$:

$$2-v-v^2 = v^2+v$$

$$2v^2+2v-2=0 \implies v^2+v-1=0$$

$$
\begin{align*}
v &= \frac{-1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \\
  &= 0.61803 \\
1+i &= \frac{1}{v} = 1.61803 \\
i &= 0.61803 \approx 0.618
\end{align*}
$$
