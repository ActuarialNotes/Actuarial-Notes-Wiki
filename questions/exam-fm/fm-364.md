Consider an amortization schedule for a loan at interest rate $i$ per period, $i > 0$, being repaid with payments of 1 at the end of each period for $n$ periods.

Determine which of the following statements about this schedule is true.

- A) The total interest paid equals $n - a_{\overline{n}|}$.
- B) The total interest paid equals $ia_{\overline{n}|}$.
- C) The total principal repaid equals $n - ia_{\overline{n}|}$.
- D) The principal repaid in payment $t$ equals $v^{n-t}$.
- E) The total payment amount equals $a_{\overline{n}|}$.

## Explanation

The borrower makes $n$ payments of 1, so the total amount paid is $n$. The initial loan amount is the present value of those payments, $a_{\overline{n}|i}$, and over the life of the loan all of it is repaid as principal. Every part of a payment that is not principal is interest, so the total interest paid is the total paid minus the loan amount.

The other statements fail: the total principal repaid is $a_{\overline{n}|i}$, the total payment amount is $n$, the principal repaid in payment $t$ is $v^{n-t+1}$, and $ia_{\overline{n}|i} = 1 - v^{n}$ is only the interest in the first payment.

The total interest paid equals $n - a_{\overline{n}|i}$.
