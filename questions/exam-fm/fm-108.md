You are given the following information about a company's liabilities:
- Present value: 9697
- Macaulay duration: 15.24
- Macaulay convexity: 242.47

The company decides to create an investment portfolio by making investments into two of the following three zero-coupon bonds: 5-year, 15-year, and 20-year. The company would like its position to be Redington immunized against small changes in yield rate.

The annual effective yield rate for each of the bonds is 7.5%.

Determine which of the following portfolios the company should create.

- A) Invest 3077 for the 5-year bond and 6620 for the 20-year bond.
- B) Invest 6620 for the 5-year bond and 3077 for the 20-year bond.
- C) Invest 465 for the 15-year bond and 9232 for the 20-year bond.
- D) Invest 4156 for the 15-year bond and 5541 for the 20-year bond.
- E) Invest 9232 for the 15-year bond and 465 for the 20-year bond.

## Explanation

Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ be the amounts invested in the 5-year, 15-year and 20-year zero-coupon bonds; one of them is 0. For zero-coupon bonds, the present value, Macaulay duration and Macaulay convexity of the assets are

$$x + y + z, \qquad \frac{5x + 15y + 20z}{x + y + z}, \qquad \frac{5^2x + 15^2y + 20^2z}{x + y + z}$$

Redington immunization needs the present values and the Macaulay durations to match, and the assets' convexity to exceed the liabilities':

$$x + y + z = 9697, \qquad \frac{5x + 15y + 20z}{x + y + z} = 15.24$$

Since 5 and 15 are both less than the required duration 15.24, $z$ cannot be zero. So try either the 5-year and 20-year bonds ($y = 0$) or the 15-year and 20-year bonds ($x = 0$).

**5-year and 20-year ($y = 0$):**

$$x = \frac{(20-15.24)(9697)}{20-5} = 3077.18 \qquad z = \frac{(15.24-5)(9697)}{20-5} = 6619.82$$

The Macaulay convexity of the assets is

$$\frac{5^2(3077.18) + 20^2(6619.82)}{9697} = 281.00$$

which exceeds the liabilities' 242.47, so this portfolio is Redington immunized: invest 3077 in the 5-year bond and 6620 in the 20-year bond.

**15-year and 20-year ($x = 0$):** the same two equations give $y = 9231.54$ and $z = 465.46$ (option E), but the convexity is $[15^2(9231.54) + 20^2(465.46)]/9697 = 233.40$, which is less than the liabilities' 242.47, so it fails.
