Given the following information as of December 31, 2016:

| Accident Year | Earned Premium | Cumulative Reported Loss: 12 Months | Cumulative Reported Loss: 24 Months | Cumulative Reported Loss: 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2014 | \$1,100,000 | \$450,000 | \$585,000 | \$614,250 |
| 2015 | 1,210,000 | 600,000 | 840,000 | |
| 2016 | 1,331,000 | 850,000 | | |

- Assume no further development after 36 months.

Calculate the ultimate losses for each accident year using each of the following methods:

- i. Collective loss ratio
- ii. Individual loss ratio
- iii. Benktander loss ratio
- iv. Optimal credible loss ratio

### Explanation
Incremental loss ratios by development period (sum of incremental losses over the sum of premium for the years that have reached that age):

$$m_1 = \frac{450 + 600 + 850}{1{,}100 + 1{,}210 + 1{,}331} = 0.5218$$

$$m_2 = \frac{135 + 240}{1{,}100 + 1{,}210} = 0.1623$$

$$m_3 = \frac{29.25}{1{,}100} = 0.0266$$

$$\sum m = 0.7107 = \text{ELR}$$

Percentage reported $p_k$ and unreported $q_k = 1 - p_k$:

| AY | $p_k$ | $q_k$ |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 | 1.000 | 0.0000 |
| 2015 | $(0.5218 + 0.1623)/0.7107 = 0.9626$ | 0.0374 |
| 2016 | $0.5218/0.7107 = 0.7342$ | 0.2658 |

**i. Collective:** $U = C_k + EP_k \times \text{ELR} \times q_k$

- 2014: $614{,}250 + 1{,}100{,}000 \times 0.7107 \times 0 = 614{,}250$
- 2015: $840{,}000 + 1{,}210{,}000 \times 0.7107 \times 0.0374 = 872{,}162$
- 2016: $850{,}000 + 1{,}331{,}000 \times 0.7107 \times 0.2658 = 1{,}101{,}431$

**ii. Individual:** $U = C_k / p_k$

- 2014: $614{,}250 / 1.0000 = 614{,}250$
- 2015: $840{,}000 / 0.9626 = 872{,}637$
- 2016: $850{,}000 / 0.7342 = 1{,}157{,}723$

**iii. Benktander:** $U = (1 - q_k^2)\,U_{ind} + q_k^2\,U_0$, with $U_0 = EP \times \text{ELR}$

- 2014: $614{,}250$
- 2015: $(1 - 0.0374^2) \times 872{,}637 + 0.0374^2 \times 1{,}210{,}000 \times 0.7107 = 872{,}619$
- 2016: $(1 - 0.2658^2) \times 1{,}157{,}723 + 0.2658^2 \times 1{,}331{,}000 \times 0.7107 = 1{,}142{,}760$

**iv. Optimal:** $Z = p/(p + \sqrt{p})$ — 2014: 0.500, 2015: 0.495, 2016: 0.461; $U = Z \times U_{ind} + (1 - Z) \times U_{coll}$

- 2014: $614{,}250$
- 2015: $0.495 \times 872{,}637 + (1 - 0.495) \times 872{,}162 = 872{,}397$
- 2016: $0.461 \times 1{,}157{,}723 + (1 - 0.461) \times 1{,}101{,}431 = 1{,}127{,}381$

Alternatively:

Working in reserves, with the same $m_k$ ($\sum m = 0.711$), $p$ and $q$: $R^{coll} = 0.711 \times q \times EP$, $R^{ind} = (q/p) \times \text{Cum. Loss}$, $R^{GB} = p \times R^{ind} + q \times R^{coll}$, $Z^* = p/(p + \sqrt{p})$, $R^* = Z^* R^{ind} + (1 - Z^*) R^{coll}$ (the $R^{GB}$ formula is printed as "$p \times R^{ind} + q + R^{coll}$" in the examiner's report).

| k | m | p | q | $R^{coll}$ | $R^{ind}$ | $R^{GB}$ | $Z^*$ | $R^*$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.522 | 1.000 | 0.000 | 0 | 0 | 0 | | 0 |
| 2 | 0.162 | 0.962 | 0.038 | 32,692 | 33,181 | 33,162 | 0.495 | 32,929 |
| 3 | 0.027 | 0.734 | 0.266 | 251,727 | 308,038 | 293,059 | 0.461 | 277,580 |

e.g. $p_2 = (0.711 - 0.027)/0.711$. Ultimates (reported loss + reserve):

| AY | $U^{coll}$ | $U^{ind}$ | $U^{GB}$ | $U^*$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2014 | 614,250 | 614,250 | 614,250 | 614,250 |
| 2015 | 872,692 | 873,181 | 873,162 | 872,929 |
| 2016 | 1,101,727 | 1,158,038 | 1,143,059 | 1,127,580 |

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to apply the basic mechanics of four credibility claims reserving methods.
Common errors include:

- Not including ultimate losses in the final answer.
- Applying the complement of credibility in both the Benktander and Optimal methods to the individual loss ratio method rather than the collective loss ratio method.
- Deriving loss development factors directly from the reported losses rather than through incremental loss ratios.
