A May 2021 report on hybrid retirement plans for state and local government employees. It treats a hybrid as an umbrella term for a wide range of designs, some defined benefit pensions with risk-sharing provisions and others blending defined benefit and defined contribution features, each trading off retirement benefits, risks and costs. It gives officials an overview of hybrid plan design, finds that some shifts to hybrids were made without evaluating their long-term implications, and concludes that not all hybrid plans are created equal.

> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — RET101-104-25; Outcomes 1a, 1b; 3a, 3b, 3c; 4a-4n. The SOA course strategy guide notes that much of the reading is in the context of U.S. public plans but gives an overview of almost the full range of plans that might be called hybrid.
