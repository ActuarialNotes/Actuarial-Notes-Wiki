An HO-3 insured's dog bites two people in the same week: a visiting neighbor and the insured's own 8-year-old daughter, who lives at home. Which statement about the homeowners policy is correct?

- A) Both are paid under Coverage F, because medical payments to others don't depend on fault
- B) Both are paid under Coverage E, because the insured is liable for the dog
- C) Neither is covered, because injury caused by an insured's animal is excluded
- D) The neighbor can be paid under Coverages E and F; the daughter under neither

## Explanation

Section II covers the neighbor: Coverage F pays her medical expenses without regard to fault, and Coverage E pays damages if the insured is liable. The daughter is excluded from both: Coverage F does not apply to regular residents of the household, and Coverage E excludes bodily injury to the named insured and resident relatives. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** misses Coverage F's exclusion of household residents.
- **B** misses Coverage E's exclusion of bodily injury to an insured.
- **C** — an insured's animal is not excluded under Section II; animals the insured owns appear only among the open perils exclusions of Section I.
