You are given the following annual sales totals for a department store.

| Year | Sales |
|:-:|:-:|
| 2013 | 400 |
| 2014 | 375 |
| 2015 | 410 |
| 2016 | 420 |
| 2017 | 410 |
| 2018 | 525 |

Calculate the sample lag 2 autocorrelation.

- A) Less than 0.00
- B) At least 0.00, but less than 0.05
- C) At least 0.05, but less than 0.10
- D) At least 0.10, but less than 0.15
- E) At least 0.15

## Explanation

The sample autocorrelation at lag 2 is the lag-2 sum of cross-products of deviations from the mean, divided by the total sum of squares:

$$
\hat\rho(2) = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{n-2}(x_t - \bar{x})(x_{t+2} - \bar{x})}{\sum_{t=1}^{n}(x_t - \bar{x})^2}
$$

The mean is $\bar{x} = 2540/6 = 423.33$, so the deviations are

$$
-23.33, \quad -48.33, \quad -13.33, \quad -3.33, \quad -13.33, \quad 101.67
$$

The denominator is the sum of their squares, 13,583.33. The numerator pairs each deviation with the one two years later:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Numerator} &= (-23.33)(-13.33) + (-48.33)(-3.33) + (-13.33)(-13.33) + (-3.33)(101.67) \\
&= 311.11 + 161.11 + 177.78 - 338.89 \\
&= 311.11
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat\rho(2) &= \frac{311.11}{13{,}583.33} \\
&= 0.0229
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.00, 0.05)$, so the answer is **B**.
