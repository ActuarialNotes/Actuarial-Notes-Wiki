Ontario Regulation 193/18, made under the Pension Benefits Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.8, in the consolidation in force from October 15, 2019 (last amendment O. Reg. 335/19). It prescribes the notices, contract terms, solvency ratio tests and records that apply when the administrator of a pension plan buys a pension, a deferred pension or an ancillary benefit from an insurance company under section 43.1 of the Act, and the election to have an actuarial cost certificate prepared after the purchase.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcomes 3a, 3b; the whole regulation, as study note RET301-109-25.

## 1 Interpretation
- Defines "date of the purchase", "date of the subsequent adjustment", "General Regulation" and "solvency ratio" (solvency assets plus letters of credit held in trust, over solvency liabilities, for defined benefits and ancillary benefits).

## 2 Notices required under subss. 43.1 (3) and (7) of the Act
- The notice to a former member states that a deferred pension has been purchased, the date of the purchase, the insurance company's policy and certificate numbers, and that the administrator intends to file an actuary's certificate of compliance with section 43.1.
- The notice to a retired member carries the matching statements for a pension.
- Both notices say whether the member would be, or may be, entitled to surplus if the plan is later wound up with a surplus.
- The spouse of a retired member receiving a share of the pension instalment under subsection 67.4 (1) of the Act also gets notice.

## 3 Requirements re contract to purchase a pension, etc.
- The contract must provide that money payable under it is not assigned or charged except as the Family Law Act permits, that a former member's pension is a joint and survivor pension unless waived under section 46 of the Act, and that the insurance company gives a certificate confirming the purchase.

## 4 Requirements re purchase of pension, etc.
- On the day after the date of the purchase, the plan's solvency ratio must be at least 1.0 if the most recently filed report showed at least 1.0, and otherwise at least the greater of 0.85 and the ratio in that report.
- If the ratio falls short, the employer must pay in the shortfall within 90 days after the date of the purchase.
- The administrator keeps a record of the purchase and provides the Superintendent with member details and a copy of the contract when the certificate is filed.

## 5 Requirements re original purchase under s. 43 of the Act
- Sets the solvency ratio tests for a subsequent adjustment to an original purchase under section 43, with the same 90-day employer payment.
- If no adjustment is required, the ratio in the most recently filed report must be at least 0.85.

## 6 Election re preparation of actuarial cost certificate
- After a purchase the administrator may elect to have an actuary prepare an actuarial cost certificate as of the date of the purchase.
- The employer begins payments under the certificate, and the administrator files it, within 90 days after the date of purchase.

## 7 Omitted
- Provides for the coming into force of the regulation.
