A bank issues a loan to be repaid by level end-of-year payments for ten years at an annual effective interest rate of 10%.

The bank invests these payments at an annual effective interest rate of 10% for the first four years and 7% for the next six years.

Calculate the bank's annual effective yield rate on this loan over the ten-year period.

- A) 7.90%
- B) 8.20%
- C) 8.33%
- D) 8.67%
- E) 9.10%

## Explanation

Let the loan be $L$. The bank's annual payment is $L/a_{\overline{10}|0.10}$.

The payments are reinvested at 10% for the first four years and 7% for the next six, so the first four payments accumulate to $s_{\overline{4}|0.10}$ per unit at time 4 and then grow at 7% for six years, while the last six accumulate at 7%. Accumulated value at time 10:
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{L}{a_{\overline{10}|0.10}}\left(s_{\overline{4}|0.10}(1.07)^6 + s_{\overline{6}|0.07}\right) &= \frac{L}{6.1446}\left(4.6410(1.5007) + 7.1533\right) \\
&= \frac{L}{6.1446}(6.9647 + 7.1533) \\
&= L\,\frac{14.1180}{6.1446} \\
&= 2.2976L
\end{align*}
$$

The bank's yield $j$ over the ten years makes the loan $L$ grow to that accumulated value:
$$
\begin{align*}
L(1+j)^{10} &= 2.2976L \\
j &= 2.2976^{1/10} - 1 \\
  &= 0.0867 = 8.67\%
\end{align*}
$$
