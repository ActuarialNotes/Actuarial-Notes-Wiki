A bond is priced at 950, giving an annual effective yield to maturity of 9%. At 9%, the derivative of the price of the bond with respect to the yield rate is $-4750$.

Calculate the Macaulay duration of the bond in years.

- A) 4.59
- B) 4.62
- C) 5.00
- D) 5.41
- E) 5.45

## Explanation

The price is $P = 950$. The modified duration is the negative of the derivative of the price with respect to the yield, divided by the price:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD} &= -\frac{P'}{P} \\
        &= -\frac{-4750}{950} \\
        &= 5
\end{align*}
$$

Modified duration with respect to an annual effective yield $i$ is the Macaulay duration divided by $1+i$, so the Macaulay duration is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC} &= (1+i)\,D_{MOD} \\
        &= (1.09)(5) \\
        &= 5.45
\end{align*}
$$
