A Guy Carpenter manual on enterprise risk management for property and liability insurers, written by nine specialists and published in 2007. It addresses both ERM theory and practical implementation issues: models and management frameworks, insurance hazard risks, financial risk models, operational risk and strategic risk, with loss distribution and loss reserve models, pricing cycle frameworks, reinsurance recoverables risk and timeline simulation as further topics.
