An annuity pays 1 at the end of each year as long as the annuitant is alive, for a maximum of 3 years. In each year, conditional on the annuitant being alive at the start of the year, the probability of surviving the year is 0.90. A payment of 1 made at the end of year $k$ has present value $0.95^k$.

Calculate the expected present value of the annuity payments.

- A) 1.90
- B) 2.10
- C) 2.21
- D) 2.30
- E) 2.50

## Explanation

The payment at the end of year $k$ is made only if the annuitant is alive then. Let $p_k$ be the probability of being alive at the end of year $k$; multiplying the conditional survival probabilities,

$p_1 = 0.90$, $p_2 = 0.90^2 = 0.81$, $p_3 = 0.90^3 = 0.729$.

That payment has present value $0.95^k$ if made and 0 otherwise, so

$$
\begin{align*}
E[\text{PV}] &= 0.95\,p_1 + 0.95^2\,p_2 + 0.95^3\,p_3 \\
             &= 0.95(0.90)+0.95^2(0.81)+0.95^3(0.729) \\
             &= 0.855+0.731+0.625 \\
             &= \mathbf{2.211 \approx 2.21}
\end{align*}
$$
