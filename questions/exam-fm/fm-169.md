A borrower takes out a loan to be repaid over 20 years. The first payment is 1102 payable at the end of the first month. Each subsequent monthly payment is five more than the previous month's payment.

Calculate the accumulated value of the payments at the end of 15 years using an annual effective interest rate of 6.5%.

- A) 442,031
- B) 443,525
- C) 445,578
- D) 447,287
- E) 448,547

## Explanation

The effective monthly rate is $j = 1.065^{1/12} - 1 = 0.0052617$. Only the first 180 payments (15 years) have been made by the end of 15 years. Payment $k$ is $1102 + 5(k-1) = 1097 + 5k$, so the payments are a level 1097 plus an increasing annuity of $5, 10, 15, \ldots$. The accumulated value is

$$
\begin{align*}
AV &= 1097s_{\overline{180}|0.0052617} + 5(Is)_{\overline{180}|0.0052617} \\
   &= 1097(298.733) + 5\cdot\frac{\ddot{s}_{\overline{180}|0.0052617} - 180}{0.0052617} \\
   &= 327{,}710 + 5\cdot\frac{300.3047 - 180}{0.0052617} \\
   &= 327{,}710 + 114{,}321 \\
   &= 442{,}031
\end{align*}
$$
