A paper from the 1997 Proceedings of the Casualty Actuarial Society (Volume LXXXIV) noting that financial pricing models are replacing traditional ratemaking techniques for property-liability insurers. It introduces the target total rate of return approach, the capital asset pricing model, the discounted cash flow technique and the option pricing model, all in an insurance context, with examples of each method and discussions of their advantages and weaknesses.
