Which of these claims against a hotel is covered by the hotel's CGL despite the aircraft, auto or watercraft exclusion (CG 00 01 04 13)?

- A) A guest struck by the hotel's own airport shuttle van on a public road
- B) A guest injured by a 20-foot motorboat the hotel borrowed from a supplier and was using, free of charge, to take guests around the lake
- C) A guest injured when a hotel employee drives a guest's car out of the hotel lot onto the highway to fetch supplies
- D) A pedestrian struck by a rented truck the hotel was using to move furniture between its two sites

## Explanation

Exclusion g removes injury arising out of the use of any aircraft, auto or watercraft owned or operated by, or rented or loaned to, any insured. One exception restores a watercraft the named insured does not own that is less than 26 feet long and not being used to carry persons or property for a charge. The borrowed 20-foot boat carrying guests free of charge fits. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** is an auto owned by the insured, which belongs to a business auto policy.
- **C** is an auto *operated by* an insured (the employee). The parking exception covers only parking a non-owned auto on, or on the ways next to, the insured's premises, not driving it on the highway.
- **D** is an auto rented to the insured, also excluded.
