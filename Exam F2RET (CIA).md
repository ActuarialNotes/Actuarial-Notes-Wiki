# Exam F2RET
**F2RET – Pension Financial Reporting** is an exam of the retirement benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques of financial reporting relevant to retirement benefits.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of pension plans.
>
> 3. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions and to develop appropriate proposals or recommendations relating to financial reporting of pension plans.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in the [[Financial Reporting Environment|financial reporting environment]] of [[Pension Plan|retirement benefits]]. (A1).

> [!example]- Accounting standards {30%}
>
> 1. Recognize, define, calculate, and interpret [[Financial-Statement Component|financial-statement components]]: [[Balance Sheet-profit-And-Loss Statement-change|balance sheet/profit-and-loss statement/changes]] in other [[Comprehensive Income-note|comprehensive income/notes]] to [[Financial Statements|financial statements]]. (B3)
> 2. Compare and contrast applicable [[Accounting Standards|accounting standards]]. (B4)
> 3. Evaluate recognition of [[Liability-accounting Expense|liability/accounting expense]]. (C5)
> 4. Apply [[Actuarial Cost Method|actuarial cost methods]]. (C3)
> 5. Analyze and decide on actuarial assumptions relative to [[Pension|pension]] and other [[Retiree Group Benefits|post-employment benefits]]. (C4)
> 6. Consider and apply allocation, attribution, and [[Vesting|vesting]]. (B4)
> 7. Recognize and evaluate benefit changes. ([[B5]])
> 8. Recognize and evaluate assumption changes. ([[B5]])
> 9. Evaluate recognition and [[Amortization|amortization]] of [[Past Service Cost|past service costs]]. (C5)
> 10. Evaluate recognition and [[Amortization|amortization]] of actuarial gains and losses. (C5)
> 11. Recognize and appraise curtailment, settlement, and [[Annuity Purchase|annuity purchase]]. (C5)
> 12. Recognize and calculate [[Termination Benefit|termination benefits]], [[Business Discontinuance|business discontinuance]], and a combination of both. (C5)
> 13. Differentiate and distinguish the accounting treatment applicable to shared-risk or [[Multi-Employer Pension|multi-employer pension]] and [[Defined Benefit Plan|benefit plans]]. (C4)
> 14. Recognize and apply [[IAS 19 Disclosure|IAS 19 disclosure]] requirements. (C3)
> 15. Recognize and apply [[FASB Disclosure|FASB disclosure]] requirements. (C3)
> 16. Recognize and apply IFRIC 14 adjustments and [[Asset Ceiling|asset ceiling]]. (C3)
> 17. Outline and apply the [[CIA-CICA Joint Policy Statement|CIA/CICA Joint Policy Statement]]. (C3)
> 18. Analyze and evaluate accounting impacts of transactions such as mergers, acquisitions, and purchase price adjustment. ([[C5]])

> [!example]- Reporting of pension, post-employment benefits, and post-retirement benefits for financial statement and proxy circular purposes {25%}
>
> 1. Describe, explain, and understand the different components of [[Executive Compensation|executive compensation]], such as: (B3)
>    - a. Salary.
>    - b. Short- and [[Long-Term Compensation Incentive|long-term compensation incentives]] ([[Performance Share Unit|performance share units]], [[Stock Option|stock options]], [[Stock Appreciation Right|stock appreciation rights]], [[Deferral Plan|deferral plans]]), [[Pension|pensions]], [[Group Benefit|group benefits]], [[Share Purchase Plan|share purchase plan]], perquisites, and [[Severance-change-In-Control Agreement|severance/change-in-control agreements]].
> 2. Define the purpose of [[Executive Compensation|executive compensation]] and explain its [[Governance Practice|governance practices]], including [[Proxy Circular|proxy circulars]]. (B2)
> 3. Interpret and apply the [[CPA Canada Handbook]]. (B3)
> 4. Interpret and apply IAS 19 and IFRIC 14. ([[C3]])
> 5. Understand the concept of accrual versus [[Cash Basis|cash basis]] for [[Pension Plan Asset|pension plan assets]]. (B2)
> 6. Differentiate [[Audited Financial Report|audited financial reports]] and [[Audited Financial Statement|audited financial statements]]. (C4)
> 7. Understand and use [[Audited Financial Statement|audited financial statements]] and reports: (C3)
>    - a. versus [[Financial Report|financial reports]] from custodians; and
>    - b. concerning how [[Residual Benefit|residual benefits]] are treated.

> [!example]- Investments and asset allocation {20%}
>
> 1. Assess the different types and combinations of [[Investment Vehicle|investment vehicles]] for providing [[Pension Plan|retirement benefits]] given the particulars of a [[Stakeholder's Financial Circumstance|stakeholder’s financial circumstances]], philosophy, industry, workforce, and [[Benefit Package|benefit package]]. (C5)
> 2. Distinguish the various strategies, approaches, and techniques used to manage [[Investment Policy Statement|retirement fund assets]]. (C5)
> 3. Assess a statement of [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policy]] including how environmental, social, and [[Corporate Governance|governance]] (ESG) factors should be integrated in the [[Investment Process|investment process]]. (C5)
> 4. Assess the potential effects of various investments and [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policies]] on stakeholders, including tax implications. (C5)
> 5. Describe the [[Regulatory Restriction|regulatory restrictions]] on, and guidelines for, [[Investment Policy Statement|retirement plan assets]]. (B2)
> 6. Solve for a measure of [[Investment Performance|investment performance]] relevant to a given benchmark. (C5)

> [!example]- Pension governance and fiduciary duties {15%}
>
> 1. Describe the roles of [[Plan Administrator|plan administrator]], board of trustees, and [[Pension Committee|pension committee]]. (B2)
> 2. Describe the role of [[Pension Advisory Authority|pension advisory authorities]]. (B2)
> 3. Define, describe, and interpret [[Benefit Right|benefit rights]]. (B2)
> 4. Identify, assess, and evaluate [[Funding Obligations-responsibility|funding obligations/responsibility]] for deficits. (C5)
> 5. Interpret, analyze, and assess the right to surplus. ([[D5]])
> 6. Recognize and apply [[Family Law|family law]] and marriage-breakdown requirements. (C3)
> 7. Recognize and interpret basic principles of [[Trust Law|trust law]]. (C3)
> 8. Consider and apply bankruptcy and [[Insolvency Legislation|insolvency legislation]]. (C3)
> 9. Consider and apply [[Employment Standards Legislation|employment standards legislation]]. (C3)
> 10. Consider and apply [[Human-Rights Legislation|human-rights legislation]]. (C3)
> 11. Consider and apply key [[Pension Case Law|pension case law]], jurisprudence, and [[Legal Opinion|legal opinions]] such as Monsanto, Indalex, [[McCarthy Tétrault Legal Opinionon|McCarthy Tétrault legal opinionon]] implications of [[Climate-Related Risk|climate-related risks]] for [[Canadian Pension Plan Fiduciary|Canadian pension plan fiduciaries]] and policymakers, etc. (C3)
> 12. Demonstrate an understanding of [[Regulator Guidance|regulator guidance]] and context for different approaches to [[Responsible Investment|responsible investment]] and, specifically, the integration of ESG factors in the [[Investment Process|investment process]]. (C3)
> 13. Demonstrate an understanding of the underlying issues that constitute factors within each of the ESG areas. ([[C3]])
> 14. Demonstrate an understanding of the [[ESG Market|ESG market]]: relevance, size, scope, key drivers and challenges, and [[Risk|risks]] and opportunities. (C3)

> [!example]- Professionalism and standards of practice relative to plan accounting {10%}
>
> 1. Apply the standards related to communications to [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsors]] and others with an [[Interest|interest]] in an actuary’s results. (C3)
> 2. Explain the [[Professional Conduct Guideline|professional conduct guidelines]]. (B3)
> 3. Explain relevant [[Qualification Standard|qualification standards]]. (B3)
> 4. Demonstrate compliance with requirements regarding an [[Actuarial Communication|actuary’s responsibilities]] to [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsors]], [[Plan Member|plan members]], etc. (D5)
> 5. Explain and apply all relevant [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|standards of practice]] related to valuing retirement and other [[Benefit Obligation|benefit obligations]]. (C3)
> 6. Recognize situations and actions that violate or [[Compromise Standard|compromise standards]] of [[Professional Conduct Guideline|professional conduct guidelines]]. (B2)
> 7. Recommend a course of action to repair a violation of the standards or [[Professional Conduct Guideline|professional conduct guidelines]]. (D5)
