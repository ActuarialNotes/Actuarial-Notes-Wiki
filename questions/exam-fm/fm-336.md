An actuary invests 1000 at the end of each year for 30 years. The investments will earn interest at a 4% annual effective interest rate and, at the end of each year, the interest will be reinvested at a 3% annual effective interest rate.

Calculate the accumulated value of the investment at the end of the 30-year period.

- A) 51,625
- B) 53,434
- C) 55,260
- D) 58,437
- E) 58,938

## Explanation

The deposits themselves accumulate to $30(1000) = 30{,}000$: the 4% interest is paid out at the end of each year and reinvested elsewhere, so the principal never grows.

The interest is the growing part. At the end of year $t$ the fund holds $t-1$ earlier deposits of 1000, so the 4% interest paid then is $40(t-1)$: 40 at the end of year 2, 80 at the end of year 3, and so on up to 1160 at the end of year 30. These 29 payments form an increasing annuity-immediate reinvested at 3%, whose accumulated value is $40(Is)_{\overline{29}|0.03}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
AV &= 30(1000) + 40(Is)_{\overline{29}|0.03} \\
   &= 30{,}000 + 40\cdot\frac{\ddot{s}_{\overline{29}|0.03}-29}{0.03} \\
   &= 30{,}000 + 40\cdot\frac{46.575416-29}{0.03} \\
   &= 30{,}000 + 40(585.8472) \\
   &= 30{,}000 + 23{,}433.89 \\
   &= 53{,}433.89 \approx 53{,}434
\end{align*}
$$
