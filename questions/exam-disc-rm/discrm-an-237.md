Under Florida's workers compensation audit rule for the voluntary market, which audit is required at the expiration of a policy for a florist (not a construction classification) with an estimated annual premium of \$14,000?

- A) None, because audits are required only for construction classifications
- B) A mail audit, which the employer completes and returns
- C) A physical audit, conducted every year
- D) A physical audit, conducted every other year

## Explanation

Florida's rule requires a final physical audit every year for policies with an estimated annual premium of \$10,000 or more, whatever the governing classification. Below \$10,000 a mail or physical audit is allowed, and new business with construction classifications needs a physical audit regardless of premium. The florist's \$14,000 premium calls for a physical audit each year. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Construction classifications trigger extra requirements, but every policy gets a final audit.
- **B** — Mail audits are permitted only where a physical audit is not required, which is below \$10,000 of premium for this class.
- **D** — The requirement is annual, not biennial; the biennial rule in the regulation is for per capita policies.
