The Canadian Association of Pension Supervisory Authorities' Communiqué for Spring 2023, a three-page newsletter. It announces CAPSA's new Executive Committee, Manitoba's and Newfoundland and Labrador's joining of the 2020 Agreement Respecting Multi-jurisdictional Pension Plans effective July 1, 2023, the 2023-2026 Strategic Plan, and planned consultations on the Capital Accumulation Plans guideline and an integrated Risk Management guideline.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcomes 3a, 3b; the whole communiqué, as study note RET301-113-25.

## CAPSA Welcomes New Executive
- Angela Mazerolle (New Brunswick) is elected Chair, with Leah Fichter (Saskatchewan) and Caroline Blouin (Ontario) confirmed as Vice-Chairs.

## Amendment to 2020 Agreement Respecting Multi-Jurisdictional Pension Plans
- CAPSA announced on May 11, 2023 that, effective July 1, 2023, Manitoba and Newfoundland and Labrador have joined the original governments party to the 2020 Agreement through an amendment.
- All jurisdictions in Canada that have [[Canadian Pension Legislation|pension legislation]], and all multi-jurisdictional pension plans in Canada, are then subject to the 2020 Agreement.
- The 2020 Agreement with the 2023 Amending Agreement replaces all prior agreements between individual governments.
- CAPSA is working on a Commentary Guide for the 2023 Amendment and administrative procedures.

## 2023-2026 CAPSA Strategic Plan Released
- CAPSA will focus on promoting harmonized regulatory guidance, standards and expectations, and on determining effective supervisory methods and approaches for regulators.

## Second Consultation Planned for CAP Guideline
- CAPSA will release a revised Guideline No. 3, Guidelines for Capital Accumulation Plans, for a second abbreviated consultation in spring 2023, targeting publication of the final guideline in 2023.

## CAPSA to Release Integrated Risk Management Guideline for Consultation
- An integrated Risk Management Guideline combining the current Risk Management, Cyber Risk, Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) Considerations, and Leverage and Effective Management of Associated Risks guidelines will be released for consultation in spring 2023.
- The integrated guideline also incorporates Investment Risk, Use of Third Parties and Target Benefits.
