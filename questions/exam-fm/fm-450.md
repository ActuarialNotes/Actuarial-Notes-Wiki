An electric company faces liabilities with present values 24,000 and 1,000, due 7.5 years from now and 20 years from now, respectively.

The company will purchase an $n$-year zero-coupon bond and a $2n$-year zero-coupon bond.

Determine which of the following expressions describes the set of all values of $n$ for which the company can achieve Redington immunization.

- A) $4 \le n \le 8$
- B) $5 < n < 7$
- C) $n = 5$ or $n = 7$
- D) $0 < n < 5$ or $n > 7$
- E) $4 \le n < 5$ or $7 < n \le 8$

## Explanation

Redington immunization requires the assets to match the liabilities in present value and in duration, and to have a greater convexity. The liabilities have present value $24{,}000 + 1000 = 25{,}000$ and duration

$$
\begin{align*}
D_L &= \frac{24{,}000(7.5) + 1000(20)}{25{,}000} \\
    &= 8
\end{align*}
$$

Once the durations are equal, the assets have the greater convexity exactly when their present-value-weighted average of the squared payment times is the greater. For the liabilities this is

$$\frac{24{,}000(7.5^{2}) + 1000(20^{2})}{25{,}000} = 70$$

Let $f$ be the fraction of 25,000 invested in the $n$-year bond. Each bond is a single payment, so the duration of the assets is $fn + (1-f)2n$ and their average squared time is $fn^{2} + (1-f)4n^{2}$.

For the durations to match,

$$
\begin{align*}
fn + (1-f)2n &= 8 \\
f &= 2 - \frac{8}{n}
\end{align*}
$$

Since $f$ must be between 0 and 1, $n$ must be between 4 and 8 (inclusive).

Substituting for $f$, the convexity condition is

$$
\begin{align*}
\left(2 - \frac{8}{n}\right)n^{2} + \left(1 - 2 + \frac{8}{n}\right)4n^{2} &> 70 \\
-2n^{2} + 24n &> 70 \\
n^{2} - 12n + 35 &< 0 \\
(n-7)(n-5) &< 0
\end{align*}
$$

This forces $n$ to lie strictly between 5 and 7, which is inside $4 \le n \le 8$, so the company can achieve Redington immunization exactly when $5 < n < 7$.
