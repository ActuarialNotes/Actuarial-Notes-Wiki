A McKinsey article of November 2020 on the new operating models leaders need in a uniquely severe global crisis to respond quickly to a rapidly shifting environment and sustain their organizations. It says uncertainty can be measured in magnitude and duration, that in a serious crisis uncertainty can reach extreme levels, and that the operating model must enable continuous learning and flexible responses as situations evolve.
