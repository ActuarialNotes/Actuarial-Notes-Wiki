LexisNexis Canada's sixth edition of Canadian Pensions and Retirement Income Planning provides a general perspective of legislative, professional, and administrative requirements for running pension and other retirement plans in Canada. It covers all types of retirement income plans, including defined benefit, defined contribution, RRSPs, and deferred profit sharing plans, with background material on minimum pension standards, actuarial valuations, and accounting standards to provide context to the tax rules.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 1a; Chapter 15 (Sections 1500 through 1515) only; the remaining material is background only and legislative requirements will not be tested.
