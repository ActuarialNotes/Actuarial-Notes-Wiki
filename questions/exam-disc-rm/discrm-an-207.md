A commercial account's manual premium is \$18,000. The insurer's guideline lets a line underwriter apply schedule credits or debits of up to 25%, and requires referral for anything more. The underwriter believes the account's housekeeping and safety program justify a 30% credit. What is the lowest premium the underwriter can quote without a referral?

- A) \$12,600
- B) \$13,500
- C) \$18,000
- D) \$23,400

## Explanation

The credit the underwriter may apply alone is capped at 25%:

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 18{,}000 \times (1 - 0.25) \\
&= 13{,}500
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**. To give the full 30% credit, the underwriter has to refer the account to someone with wider authority.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Applies the full 30% credit, beyond the underwriter's authority.
- **C** — Treats the account as if no discretionary pricing were allowed, which is typical of personal lines, not of this guideline.
- **D** — Applies 30% as a debit instead of a credit.
