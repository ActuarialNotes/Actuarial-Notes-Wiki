An underwriter's inspection report on a furniture store notes: (1) the building has a wood roof deck over brick walls; (2) the store refinishes furniture in a back room using solvents; (3) the town has a paid fire department 2 miles away; (4) a fireworks wholesaler occupies the building next door. Which pairing of facts with COPE elements is correct?

- A) (1) occupancy, (2) construction, (3) exposure, (4) protection
- B) (1) construction, (2) exposure, (3) protection, (4) occupancy
- C) (1) construction, (2) occupancy, (3) exposure, (4) protection
- D) (1) construction, (2) occupancy, (3) protection, (4) exposure

## Explanation

- **(1) Construction:** the materials of the building, here joisted masonry.
- **(2) Occupancy:** how the building is used, including the hazards of the insured's own operations.
- **(3) Protection:** the fire department and its distance are public fire protection.
- **(4) Exposure:** the hazard from neighboring property, here a fireworks wholesaler.

The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Mixes up construction and occupancy, and exposure and protection.
- **B** — Calls the insured's own refinishing work exposure. In COPE, exposure is the hazard from surrounding property; the insured's own operations are occupancy.
- **C** — Swaps protection and exposure: the fire department protects the building, and the neighbor exposes it.
