Harry borrows 10,000 from Sally and agrees to repay the five-year loan with equal payments at the end of each quarter. Interest on the loan is charged at an annual nominal rate of 6% convertible quarterly.

Immediately after the fourth payment, Harry and Sally get married and Sally forgives the remaining debt.

Calculate the total interest payments that Sally forgoes receiving by having forgiven the remaining debt.

- A) 582
- B) 951
- C) 1,088
- D) 1,270
- E) 1,769

## Explanation

The quarterly interest rate is $0.06/4 = 0.015$, and the loan is repaid with 20 quarterly payments of $P$:

$$
\begin{align*}
10{,}000 &= P\,a_{\overline{20}|0.015} \\
P &= \frac{10{,}000}{17.168639} \\
  &= 582.4574
\end{align*}
$$

The outstanding balance immediately after the fourth payment is the present value of the 16 remaining payments:

$$
\begin{align*}
OB_4 &= 582.4574\,a_{\overline{16}|0.015} \\
     &= 582.4574(14.131264) \\
     &= 8230.86
\end{align*}
$$

Had the loan continued, Harry would have made 16 more payments, totaling

$$16(582.4574) = 9319.32$$

Of that total, the outstanding balance of 8230.86 is principal, and the rest is the interest Sally forgoes:

$$9319.32 - 8230.86 = 1088.46$$
