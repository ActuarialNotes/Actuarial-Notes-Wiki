An annuity has the first monthly payment of 750 occurring in five years. Each payment thereafter is 1.0% greater than the preceding payment. The final payment occurs exactly 15 years after the first payment.

The annuity has a present value of $P$ based on an annual nominal interest rate of 7.2% compounded monthly.

Calculate $P$.

- A) 136,551
- B) 137,370
- C) 138,440
- D) 189,523
- E) 198,217

## Explanation

First, note that the monthly rate of interest is $\dfrac{0.072}{12} = 0.006$. Also, the first payment occurs 60 months out, and the last payment occurs $15(12) = 180$ months later, 240 months out. Thus, there are 181 payments in total: $750(1.01)^{k}$ at month $60 + k$ for $k = 0, 1, \ldots, 180$.

Then, the present value of the annuity is

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= \frac{750}{(1.006)^{60}}\left[1 + \frac{1.01}{1.006} + \ldots + \left(\frac{1.01}{1.006}\right)^{180}\right] \\
  &= \frac{750}{(1.006)^{60}}\sum_{k=0}^{180}\left(\frac{1.01}{1.006}\right)^{k} \\
  &= \frac{750}{(1.006)^{60}}\cdot\frac{\left(\dfrac{1.01}{1.006}\right)^{181} - 1}{\dfrac{1.01}{1.006} - 1} \\
  &= 523.8204\left(\frac{1.050851}{0.003976143}\right) \\
  &= 523.8204(264.2890) \\
  &= 138{,}440
\end{align*}
$$
