A Society of Actuaries study note, dated July 2019 and revised April 2025, that the SOA's ILA 101 course strategy guide says explains the concepts behind principle-based reserving. It is listed in the syllabus under the Introduction to Life and Annuity Valuation Concepts topic as a reading on the US statutory actuarial framework.
