A household's members drive two registered cars, own a 30-foot sailboat, rent a second house they own to a tenant, and keep a dog at home. Which of these personal liability exposures does the family's HO-3 Section II cover without an endorsement or another policy?

- A) Liability for a crash while driving one of the registered cars
- B) Liability for an injury caused by the sailboat
- C) Liability for a tenant's injury at the rented second house
- D) Liability for a visitor's injury from the dog at home

## Explanation

The HO-3 covers personal liability for bodily injury and property damage from an occurrence, including injury caused by an insured's animal on the insured location; nothing in Section II excludes the dog. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — motor vehicle liability for a registered vehicle is excluded; the cars belong on the personal auto policy's liability coverage.
- **B** — an owned sailing vessel is covered only if it is under 26 feet; a watercraft endorsement or a boat policy is needed.
- **C** — the exclusion for premises an insured owns or rents to others that aren't an insured location removes the rented house, unless it is added to the policy.
