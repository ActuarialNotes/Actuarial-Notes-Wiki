A model reproduces its training data almost exactly but forecasts new claims badly. In the decomposition of expected prediction error, which term has grown large?

- A) Variance — the fit is tracking noise peculiar to the training sample
- B) Bias — the model is too rigid to capture the underlying pattern
- C) The irreducible error, which flexible models inflate
- D) None of them; a perfect training fit means the error is zero
