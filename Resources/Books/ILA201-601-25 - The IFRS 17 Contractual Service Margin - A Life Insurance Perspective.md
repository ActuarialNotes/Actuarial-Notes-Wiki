A paper by the IFRS 17 CSM Working Party of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries, dated October 2020, that explores the technical, operational and commercial issues that arise in applying the contractual service margin (CSM) under IFRS 17, which it describes as the unearned profit that an entity expects to earn as it provides services. It aims to give readers a deeper understanding of the issues and opportunities that accompany the CSM from a life insurance perspective, and covers the CSM's basics, level of aggregation and recognition, measurement, transition, and KPIs and management information.
