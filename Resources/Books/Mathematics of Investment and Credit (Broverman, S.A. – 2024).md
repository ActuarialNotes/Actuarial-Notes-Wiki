![[Mathematics of Investment and Credit (Broverman, S.A. – 2024) - Cover.svg]]

A textbook giving a complete treatment of the theory of interest and its application to a wide variety of financial instruments. It emphasizes a direct-calculation approach. Per the author's preface, the first five chapters develop the traditional topics of compound interest and dated cashflow valuations; Chapters 6 to 9 cover subjects such as the term structure of interest rates, interest rate swaps and forward contracts, the foundation for the models used to value the derivative securities introduced in Chapter 10. The 8th edition expands the 7th edition's coverage of options, particularly the binomial model for valuing options.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam FM-2 (SOA)|Exam FM]] — a suggested text (the syllabus requires none); Chapter 1 (excluding 1.2.1 and 1.8); Chapter 2 (excluding 2.3.1.2, 2.4.2, 2.4.3 and 2.4.5); Chapter 3 (excluding 3.2.1, 3.2.2, 3.3 and 3.4); Chapter 4 (excluding 4.1.3 and 4.1.4; 4.4 is for background only); Chapter 5 (excluding 5.2, the investment year method portion of 5.3.1, and all of 5.3.2, 5.3.3 and 5.3.4); Chapter 6 (excluding 6.2 and 6.4); Chapter 7 (excluding 7.1.3 and 7.3). The Seventh Edition (2017) may be used with the same sections.
