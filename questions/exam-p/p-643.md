The number of times per year a motorist uses emergency roadside service is Poisson distributed with mean 2.

Calculate the probability that the motorist does not use emergency roadside service more than four times in a given year.

- A) $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)e^{-2}$
- B) $1 - \left(\dfrac{19}{3}\right)e^{-2}$
- C) $\left(\dfrac{19}{3}\right)e^{-2}$
- D) $1 - e^{-2}$
- E) $7e^{-2}$

## Explanation

"Not more than four times" is $X \le 4$, so sum the Poisson pmf with $\lambda = 2$ from 0 through 4:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X \le 4] &= e^{-2}\left[\frac{2^0}{0!} + \frac{2^1}{1!} + \frac{2^2}{2!} + \frac{2^3}{3!} + \frac{2^4}{4!}\right] \\
           &= e^{-2}\left[1 + 2 + 2 + \frac{4}{3} + \frac{2}{3}\right] \\
           &= \mathbf{7e^{-2}}
\end{align*}
$$

which is 0.947.

Choice (C), $(19/3)e^{-2}$, stops one term early: $1 + 2 + 2 + 4/3 = 19/3$ is $P[X \le 3]$. The phrase "not more than four" includes four, so the $k = 4$ term of $2/3$ belongs in the bracket — and adding it is what turns $19/3$ into the clean $7$.
