$X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_{100}$ is a random sample from a Bernoulli distribution with probability of success $q$.

You perform the hypothesis test:

$$
H_0\colon q = 0.5 \quad \text{vs} \quad H_1\colon q > 0.5
$$

The critical region is chosen to be $C = \left\{\sum_{i=1}^{100} x_i \ge 60\right\}$.

Calculate the probability of a Type I error.

- A) Less than 0.01
- B) At least 0.01, but less than 0.02
- C) At least 0.02, but less than 0.03
- D) At least 0.03, but less than 0.04
- E) At least 0.04

## Explanation

A Type I error is rejecting $H_0$ when it is true, so we need $\Pr\left(\sum X_i \ge 60\right)$ when $q = 0.5$.

Under $H_0$ the sum $S = \sum_{i=1}^{100} X_i$ is binomial with

$$
\begin{align*}
E[S] &= 100(0.5) \\
&= 50 \\
\operatorname{Var}(S) &= 100(0.5)(0.5) \\
&= 25
\end{align*}
$$

With $n = 100$, use the normal approximation with a continuity correction:

$$
\begin{align*}
\Pr(S \ge 60) &\approx \Pr\left(Z \ge \frac{59.5 - 50}{5}\right) \\
&= \Pr(Z \ge 1.9) \\
&= 1 - 0.9713 \\
&= 0.0287
\end{align*}
$$

(The exact binomial probability is 0.0284, and the approximation without a continuity correction gives $\Pr(Z \ge 2) = 0.0228$; all three land in the same range.)

This falls in $[0.02, 0.03)$, so the answer is **C**.
