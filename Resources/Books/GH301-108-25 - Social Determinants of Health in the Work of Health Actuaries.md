A study note in the Society of Actuaries' GH 301 course that serves as the main reading for the course's Social Determinants of Health topic. The SOA's course strategy guide says the topic is presented in an integrated manner with this study note, which provides context and guides the candidate to read specific sections of the supplemental sources: sections of two SOA research papers, a health in housing study example, and two papers on the use of SDOH and other variables in the Massachusetts Medicaid risk adjustment model.

> - [[Exam GH-301 (SOA)|Exam GH-301]] — Outcomes 5a-5d.
