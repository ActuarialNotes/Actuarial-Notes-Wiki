In an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured's home a corroded water supply pipe inside a wall bursts, soaking the walls, the floor and a sofa. Mold later grows inside the wall cavity. Which part of the loss is **not** covered?

- A) Repairing the corroded pipe itself
- B) Tearing out and replacing the wall to reach the pipe
- C) The hidden mold in the wall cavity
- D) The water-soaked sofa

## Explanation

The open perils coverage excludes rust, corrosion and wear and tear, but an exception restores loss *resulting from* an accidental discharge of water from a plumbing system, including tearing out and replacing part of the building to repair the system. The same exception says the insurer does not cover loss to the system from which the water escaped, so the pipe itself is not covered. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — tear-out and replacement needed to repair the system is expressly covered.
- **C** — mold hidden within the walls that results from an accidental discharge from a plumbing system is covered.
- **D** — accidental discharge or overflow of water from a plumbing system is named peril 12 for Coverage C.
