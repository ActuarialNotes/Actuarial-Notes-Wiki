A loan of 100,000 at an annual effective rate of 9.12% is repaid with level payments at the end of each month over 15 years. Immediately after the 59th payment is made, the outstanding balance is refinanced at an annual effective rate of 5.76%. The term of the refinanced loan is 20 years with level payments at the end of each month.

Calculate the interest portion of the first payment of the refinanced loan.

- A) 375
- B) 378
- C) 381
- D) 384
- E) 387

## Explanation

The payments are monthly, so find the monthly effective rate $j$ equivalent to 9.12% a year:

$$
\begin{align*}
1.0912 &= (1+j)^{12} \\
j &= 0.0072997
\end{align*}
$$

The original loan is repaid with 180 monthly payments of $P$:

$$
\begin{align*}
100{,}000 &= P\,a_{\overline{180}|0.0072997} \\
P &= 1000.0167
\end{align*}
$$

Immediately after the 59th payment, $180 - 59 = 121$ payments remain, and the outstanding balance is their present value:

$$
\begin{align*}
OB_{59} &= 1000.0167\,a_{\overline{121}|0.0072997} \\
        &= 1000.0167(80.17325) \\
        &= 80{,}174.59
\end{align*}
$$

This balance is the amount refinanced. The monthly effective rate $j'$ on the refinanced loan is equivalent to 5.76% a year:

$$
\begin{align*}
1.0576 &= (1+j')^{12} \\
j' &= 0.00467776
\end{align*}
$$

The interest portion of the first payment of the refinanced loan is one month's interest on the amount refinanced, so the 20-year term is not needed:

$$80{,}174.59(0.00467776) = 375.04$$
