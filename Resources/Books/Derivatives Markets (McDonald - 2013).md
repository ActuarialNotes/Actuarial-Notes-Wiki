A textbook for courses in options, futures and derivatives, in the Pearson Series in Finance. Pearson describes it as explaining how business students can use derivatives in corporations, presenting the mathematics accessibly and using a narrative to build intuition about pricing and use. The library record of the third edition gives xxviii and 948 pages, Boston, copyright 2013.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam ACIA-CAPSTONE (CIA)|ACIA Capstone Exam]] — A3.9 Introductory derivatives – forwards and futures (C, SLT, SST): Chapters 2-5 — alternate reading; A3.10 General properties of options (C, SLT, SST): Chapters 3-9 — alternate reading; A3.11 Binomial pricing models (C, SLT, SST): Chapters 10-11 — alternate reading; A3.12 Black-Scholes option pricing model (C, SLT, SST): Chapter 12 — alternate reading; A3.13 Option Greeks and risk management (C, SLT, SST): Chapter 12 — alternate reading.
