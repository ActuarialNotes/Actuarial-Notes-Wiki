Supported and kept: post-Lehman collateral 'settled on treasury money market funds' and the total return swap (Patel/PartnerRe 2015 p.5); offerings quote exceedence (attachment) and exhaustion probabilities and the expected loss, the spread read as a multiple of it (Froot NBER 7286 PDF pp.4-7: 576/63 = 9.1x; Braun & Kousky p.3; Exam 9 S2017 Q15); 'theory says the spread need only cover EL' (Froot p.3, p.7: a zero-beta risk earns the risk-free rate, so the theoretical spread is the EL); 'well above ... model uncertainty, limited capacity and the reinsurance cycle' (Patel pp.2-5: margin from uncertainty in estimated EL, reinsurance cycle and availability of capacity; Froot pp.16-18, 29: price cycle, estimation uncertainty, insufficient capital, ambiguity); principal lost = Principal x min(1,(I-A)+/(E-A)) is the proportional payout Braun & Kousky p.6 describe (loss rises with the excess over attachment, full loss at exhaustion).
