A special arithmetically increasing annuity-due will make 84 monthly payments. It provides payments of 100 today, 100 one month from now, 105 two months from now, 105 three months from now, and so on until the 83rd and 84th payments of 305 each are made.

The present value of this annuity based on an annual nominal rate interest rate of 4.8% compounded monthly is $P$.

Calculate $P$.

- A) 13,950
- B) 13,978
- C) 14,008
- D) 14,062
- E) 14,117

## Explanation

The payments come in equal pairs: 100 at months 0 and 1, 105 at months 2 and 3, and so on up to 305 at months 82 and 83. There are two blocks of 42 payments (paid every two months) increasing in an arithmetic progression: one block at months $0, 2, \ldots, 82$ and the other at months $1, 3, \ldots, 83$, each paying $100, 105, \ldots, 305$. Value them at the two-month effective rate

$$
\begin{align*}
j &= (1 + 0.048/12)^{2} - 1 \\
  &= 0.008016
\end{align*}
$$

Treating each block as an increasing annuity-immediate with first payment 100 and increases of 5, the value of each of these blocks two months before its first payment is

$$
\begin{align*}
V &= 100\,a_{\overline{42}|j} + 5\left(\frac{a_{\overline{42}|j} - 42v^{42}}{j}\right) \\
  &= 3554.1141 + 5\left(\frac{35.54114068 - 30.03429308}{0.008016}\right) \\
  &= 3554.1141 + 3434.9099 \\
  &= 6989.024
\end{align*}
$$

The first block starts today, so its value today is $V$ accumulated for two months, $V(1.008016)$. The second block starts in one month, so $V$ is its value one month ago and it is accumulated for one month, $V(1 + 0.048/12)$. The present value is then:

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 6989.024(1.008016) + 6989.024(1 + 0.048/12) \\
  &= 7045.048 + 7016.980 \\
  &= 14{,}062.03
\end{align*}
$$
