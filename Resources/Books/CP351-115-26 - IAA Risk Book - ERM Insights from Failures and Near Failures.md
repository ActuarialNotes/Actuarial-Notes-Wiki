A chapter of the IAA Risk Book, developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association in March 2023, on what can be learned from situations where inadequate enterprise risk management (ERM) processes led a company into trouble or failure. It reviews ten insurer case studies, from HIH (Australia) to Confederation Life (Canada), then the causes of such failures, leading indicators, and the high-level lessons and the actuarial role.
