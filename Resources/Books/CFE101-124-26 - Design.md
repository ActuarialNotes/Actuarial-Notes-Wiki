A Milliman briefing note, dated November 2020, on approaches to developing a robust suite of Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) and the wider considerations for risk reporting. It treats a KRI as an early warning indicator that a company's risk profile may deviate from its risk tolerance or risk limits in the future, to be designed on what is most useful and meaningful rather than on what is easily monitored.
