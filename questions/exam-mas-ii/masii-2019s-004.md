Below is a table with the physical damage claim experience of commercial auto fleets for three insured companies for the most recent period.

| Company | Number of vehicles | Mean claim amount per vehicle | Standard deviation of claim amount |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| X | 150 | 3130.20 | 579.62 |
| Y | 272 | 2278.53 | 139.10 |
| Z | 511 | 2950.50 | 472.08 |

Company X will have 175 vehicles in its fleet in the next period.

Calculate the Bühlmann credibility prediction of Company X's aggregate claim amount for the next period.

- A) Less than 500,000
- B) At least 500,000, but less than 515,000
- C) At least 515,000, but less than 530,000
- D) At least 530,000, but less than 545,000
- E) At least 545,000

## Explanation

The vehicle counts differ by company, so this is non-parametric empirical Bayes under the Bühlmann-Straub model with $m_X = 150$, $m_Y = 272$, $m_Z = 511$ and $m = 933$.

**Expected value of the process variance** — pool the within-company sample variances:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{v} &= \frac{\sum_i (m_i - 1)s_i^2}{\sum_i (m_i - 1)} \\
        &= \frac{149(579.62^2) + 271(139.10^2) + 510(472.08^2)}{149 + 271 + 510} \\
        &= \frac{168{,}959{,}808}{930} \\
        &= 181{,}677
\end{align*}
$$

**Overall mean** — exposure-weighted:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{\mu} &= \frac{150(3130.20) + 272(2278.53) + 511(2950.50)}{933} \\
          &= \frac{2{,}596{,}996}{933} \\
          &= 2783.49
\end{align*}
$$

**Variance of the hypothetical means** — the Bühlmann-Straub estimator:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{a} &= \frac{\sum_i m_i(\bar{x}_i - \hat{\mu})^2 - (r-1)\hat{v}}{m - \frac{\sum_i m_i^2}{m}} \\
        &= \frac{101{,}639{,}946 - 2(181{,}677)}{933 - \frac{357{,}605}{933}} \\
        &= \frac{101{,}276{,}592}{549.72} \\
        &= 184{,}233
\end{align*}
$$

**Credibility for Company X:**

$$
\begin{align*}
k &= \frac{\hat{v}}{\hat{a}} = \frac{181{,}677}{184{,}233} = 0.986 \\
Z_X &= \frac{150}{150 + 0.986} \\
    &= 0.9935
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Predicted mean per vehicle} &= 0.9935(3130.20) + 0.0065(2783.49) \\
                                  &= 3127.94
\end{align*}
$$

The fleet grows to 175 vehicles, so the predicted aggregate is

$$
175 \times 3127.94 = 547{,}390.
$$

This is at least 545,000, so the answer is **E**.
