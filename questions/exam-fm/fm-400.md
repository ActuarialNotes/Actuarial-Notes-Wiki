A ten-year loan of 1000 is repaid by level payments at the end of each month at an annual nominal interest rate of 8%, compounded monthly. The lender invests the repayments at an annual nominal interest rate of 6%, compounded monthly.

Calculate the lender's annual effective yield rate.

- A) 6.8%
- B) 6.9%
- C) 7.0%
- D) 7.1%
- E) 7.2%

## Explanation

The loan's monthly effective rate is $\dfrac{0.08}{12}$, so the level monthly repayment is

$$
\begin{align*}
R &= \frac{1000}{a_{\overline{120}|0.08/12}} \\
  &= \frac{1000}{82.42148} \\
  &= 12.13276
\end{align*}
$$

The lender invests each repayment at $\dfrac{0.06}{12} = 0.005$ per month, so the accumulated loan repayments at 6% at the end of ten years are

$$
\begin{align*}
AV &= 12.13276\,s_{\overline{120}|0.005} \\
   &= 12.13276(163.8793) \\
   &= 1988.31
\end{align*}
$$

The lender's annual effective yield rate $j$ is the rate at which the 1000 lent grows to this amount in ten years:

$$
\begin{align*}
1000(1+j)^{10} &= 1988.31 \\
j &= 0.0711
\end{align*}
$$
