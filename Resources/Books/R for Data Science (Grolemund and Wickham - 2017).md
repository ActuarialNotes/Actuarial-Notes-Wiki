The standard introduction to doing data science in R with the tidyverse: importing, tidying, transforming, visualizing and modelling data. It is a Domain A reading for [[Exam PCPA (CAS)|PCPA]], on getting a raw file into a shape a model can use (see [[Tidy Data]]). The CAS outline cites the 1st edition (2017), which the authors keep free online. A 2nd edition has since been published with a different chapter structure.
