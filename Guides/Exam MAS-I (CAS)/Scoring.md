Scaled **0–10, with 6 required to pass**. The pass mark is set before the sitting, so you are not competing against the other candidates in the room.

Since Fall 2022 there is **no penalty for a wrong answer**, so never leave a question blank. Until then a quarter of a question's points came off for each wrong answer ([CAS, 2022](https://www.casact.org/article/guessing-penalty-eliminated-beginning-fall-2022)).
