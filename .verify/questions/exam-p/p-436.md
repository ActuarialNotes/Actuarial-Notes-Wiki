## [F-001] Distractor note: 0.50 is not the answer if 1000 were the median

- severity: minor

- locus: Explanation, last paragraph
- claim: Choice (C), 0.50, would be right if 1000 were the median rather than the mean
- evidence: Recomputation (rank 5, falsifying): if 1000 were the median of an exponential, theta = 1000/ln 2 = 1442.7, and by memorylessness P[X > 1500 | X > 1000] = P[X > 500] = e^{-500/1442.7} = e^{-0.3466} = 0.7071 (= 2^{-1/2}), not 0.50. Nor does the long form give 0.50: P[X>1500]/P[X>1000] = e^{-1.5}/0.5 = 0.446 under the stated mean. The SOA solution (solutions PDF p.122) gives no distractor rationale. The keyed answer is unaffected: e^{-0.5} = 0.6065 = option D.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Delete the sentence about choice (C); the following clause about the mean sitting at the 63rd percentile (1 - e^{-1} = 0.632) is correct and can stay.
