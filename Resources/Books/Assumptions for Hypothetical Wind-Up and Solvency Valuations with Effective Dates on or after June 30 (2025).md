A Canadian Institute of Actuaries educational note, dated September 11, 2025 (document 225103), giving guidance on the assumptions to be used for hypothetical wind-up and solvency valuations for the second half of 2025 and the first half of 2026. It confirms the initial guidance for June 30, 2025 assumptions issued in the educational note supplement of August 1, 2025. It was prepared by the Committee on Pension Plan Financial Reporting and approved for distribution by the Actuarial Guidance Council on September 10, 2025.

## Summary
- Effective June 30, 2025, the cost of purchasing non-indexed or fully CPI-indexed annuities, before any adjustment for sub- or super-standard mortality, would be estimated from spreads and a mortality assumption given in a summary table.
- The table uses CPM2014 combined with improvement scale CPM-B (CPM2014Proj), with a spread of 120 bps over CANSIM V39062 for non-indexed pensions at low, medium and high duration, and 30 bps over CANSIM V39057 for fully CPI-indexed pensions.
