An insurer's guideline for small office buildings says:

- acceptable: protection class 1 to 6, sprinklered or under 20,000 square feet;
- acceptable only with a \$10,000 deductible: one property loss in the past three years;
- decline: two or more losses in the past three years.

A line underwriter's authority covers acceptable risks up to \$5,000,000 of insured value. A submission is a 15,000 square foot unsprinklered office in protection class 4, insured value \$3,000,000, with one water damage loss two years ago. What should the underwriter do?

- A) Offer coverage with the \$10,000 deductible
- B) Decline it, because the building has no sprinklers
- C) Refer it to a senior underwriter, because it has had a loss
- D) Accept it as submitted, because the loss was only water damage

## Explanation

The building passes the acceptability test: protection class 4 and under 20,000 square feet, so sprinklers are not required. The single loss moves it into the "acceptable with a modification" group, and the guideline names the modification. The value is within the underwriter's authority, so there is nothing to refer. The answer is **A**: accept with the modification.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Sprinklers are required only for buildings of 20,000 square feet or more.
- **C** — The guideline itself says how to handle one loss. Referral is for submissions beyond the underwriter's authority, and this one is within it.
- **D** — The guideline does not distinguish water damage from other losses, so accepting without the deductible ignores it.
