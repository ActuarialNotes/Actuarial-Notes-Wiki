![[Predictive Analytics and Machine Learning - Practical Applications for Actuarial Modeling (Nested Stochastic) (SOA Research Institute - 2023) - Cover.svg]]

A May 2023 report of the Society of Actuaries Research Institute that provides a practical guide, with concrete case studies, to using artificial intelligence and machine learning (AIML) to speed up life and annuity actuarial modeling. It targets the runtime challenge of "nested stochastic" calculations, in which inner-loop stochastic calculations are performed within an outer-loop projection scenario. The authors do not position AIML models as a replacement for traditional actuarial models but as a strategic capability that can dramatically reduce runtimes and computing costs.
