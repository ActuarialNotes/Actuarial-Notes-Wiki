- claim: 'Equality would be cash-flow matching.'
- evidence: SOA solutions PDF p.51 (image) says only: 'Answer E is false because the convexity of the assets must be greater than the convexity of the liabilities.' No source read equates equal convexity with cash-flow matching, and it is false (rank-5 counterexample): liabilities with PV 1 at t=1 and PV 1 at t=3 vs assets with PVs 0.25, 1.5, 0.25 at t=0, 2, 4 have the same total PV (2), the same PV-weighted Σt (4) and Σt² (10) — hence the same Macaulay/modified duration and the same convexity at that rate — yet no asset cash flow coincides with a liability. Equal PV, duration and convexity are necessary for a cash-flow match, not sufficient. Key E unaffected.
