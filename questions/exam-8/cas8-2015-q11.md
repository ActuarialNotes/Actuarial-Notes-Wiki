An underwriter and an actuary are discussing the effectiveness of the current experience rating plan. The following table contains experience from five experience rated risks (all of similar size):

| Risk | Manual Premium | Modified Premium | Actual Losses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 400,000 | 360,000 | 300,000 |
| 2 | 600,000 | 840,000 | 690,000 |
| 3 | 800,000 | 560,000 | 440,000 |
| 4 | 900,000 | 1,080,000 | 860,000 |
| 5 | 1,000,000 | 800,000 | 650,000 |

## Part a (1.5 points)

Evaluate whether the experience rating plan is effective or not and explain why.

### Explanation
For each risk, the mod is modified premium ÷ manual premium, the manual loss ratio is losses ÷ manual premium, and the modified loss ratio is losses ÷ modified premium:

| Risk | Mod | Manual LR | Modified LR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | .9 | 75% | 83.3% |
| 2 | 1.4 | 115% | 82.14% |
| 3 | .7 | 55% | 78.6% |
| 4 | 1.2 | 95.6% | 79.63% |
| 5 | .8 | 115% (as printed; risk 5 has actual losses of 650,000 on manual premium of 1,000,000) | 81.25% |

Sorted by mod:

| Risk | Mod | Manual LR | Modified LR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | .7 | 55% | 78.6% |
| 5 | .8 | 115% (as printed) | 81.25% |
| 1 | .9 | 75% | 83.3% |
| 4 | 1.2 | 95.6% | 79.63% |
| 2 | 1.4 | 115% | 82.14% |

There is good dispersion in the manual loss ratios as the mod increases, so the plan is good at identifying risks. There is no clear trend in the standard loss ratios, so the plan is good in correcting differences. Overall, the plan is effective.

### Examiner Report
Overall, candidates scored well on this subpart and successfully calculated the mods, manual loss ratios and modified loss ratios.  Most candidates were also able to use the calculated values to conclude that the experience rating plan was effective.   Comments on the manual loss ratio trends and the lack of trends in the modified loss ratios were required for full credit.

## Part b (1 point)

The underwriter argues that the modification factor for risk 4 is too high. Propose two additional pieces of information the actuary could request regarding risk 4 in order to support or disprove the underwriter's argument and explain why the information would be useful.

### Explanation
I would want risk and class characteristics as this would indicate if the mod was correcting for a poor class fit.  I would want the risk's loss history as this would indicate if a random large loss (or a few) is driving the high mod.

Alternatively:

Prior loss history that goes into the experience mod.  It is possible that risk 4 had one large, random loss that is increasing its mod but isn't actually predictive of future losses.  May want to limit large losses if this is the case.

Recent changes to risk 4 that could impact losses.  For example, if risk 4 just implemented a new return to work program the future losses would be expected to be lower than the experience suggests.

### Examiner Report
Candidates performed well on this subpart although not as well as they did on part a.  To obtain full credit, candidates needed to successfully identify two separate pieces of information and explain its impact on the mod.
