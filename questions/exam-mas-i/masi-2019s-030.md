You are given the following information about a linear model:

- $Y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_1 + \beta_2 X_2 + \varepsilon$

| Observed Y's | Estimated Y's |
|:---:|:---:|
| 2.441 | 1.827 |
| 3.627 | 3.816 |
| 5.126 | 5.806 |
| 7.266 | 7.796 |
| 10.570 | 9.785 |

- Residual Sum of Squares = 1.772

Calculate the $R^2$ of this model.

- A) Less than 0.6
- B) At least 0.6, but less than 0.7
- C) At least 0.7, but less than 0.8
- D) At least 0.8, but less than 0.9
- E) At least 0.9

## Explanation

$R^2 = 1 - \text{RSS}/\text{TSS}$, and the residual sum of squares is given, so only the total sum of squares of the observed $Y$'s is needed.

$$
\begin{align*}
\bar{y} &= \frac{2.441 + 3.627 + 5.126 + 7.266 + 10.570}{5} \\
&= \frac{29.030}{5} \\
&= 5.806
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{TSS} &= (-3.365)^2 + (-2.179)^2 + (-0.680)^2 + (1.460)^2 + (4.764)^2 \\
&= 11.3232 + 4.7480 + 0.4624 + 2.1316 + 22.6957 \\
&= 41.3610
\end{align*}
$$

(The squared differences between the observed and estimated $Y$'s do sum to 1.772, matching the given RSS.)

$$
\begin{align*}
R^2 &= 1 - \frac{1.772}{41.3610} \\
&= 1 - 0.0428 \\
&= 0.957
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.9, \infty)$, so the answer is **E**.
