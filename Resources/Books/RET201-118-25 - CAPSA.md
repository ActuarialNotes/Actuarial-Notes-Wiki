CAPSA Guideline No. 7, in the May 2021 edition, gives guidance on the development and adoption of funding policies for pension plans that provide defined benefits or target benefits. A funding policy is a document prepared by the plan sponsor of a single employer pension plan that outlines how the benefit commitments made in the plan documents are to be achieved; for a multi-employer or target pension arrangement it is the responsibility of the administrator. Some jurisdictions require a funding policy, and where none does, CAPSA encourages sponsors or administrators to follow the practice.
