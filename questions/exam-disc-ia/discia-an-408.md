A family insured under an unendorsed ISO PAP (PP 00 01 09 18) takes a summer road trip in its covered auto. Where on the trip is an accident within the policy territory?

- A) In Canada, on a highway in Ontario
- B) In Mexico, ten miles south of the U.S. border
- C) In Mexico, after crossing from California
- D) On a ferry between Mexico and Puerto Rico

## Explanation

The policy territory is the United States, its territories or possessions, Puerto Rico and **Canada**, and the policy also applies while the covered auto is transported between their ports. An accident in Ontario is covered. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** and **C** — Mexico is outside the territory, however close to the border; the ISO limited Mexico coverage endorsement (PP 03 21) is what adds it, for short trips near the border.
- **D** — the transport extension covers travel between the ports of the places in the territory, and Mexico is not one of them.
