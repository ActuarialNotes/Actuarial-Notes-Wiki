## [F-004] Thousands commas inside math render with a trailing thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, inline math on lines 50, 59 and 61
- claim: Numbers inside math spans are written with a bare thousands comma (11,000, 12,100, 10,803.57, 8,821.43, 19,820, 19,624.5), which KaTeX sets as punctuation and renders as e.g. '40, 000'.
