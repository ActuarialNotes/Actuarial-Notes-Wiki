A chapter of the IAA Risk Book on model governance, approved by the IAA's Insurance Regulation Committee (version 1.2, May 2023). It explores the meaning and function of model governance as it relates to insurance enterprises, and describes the fundamental concepts behind, and main components of, effective model risk management and model governance: a governance framework, a model inventory and model validation.
