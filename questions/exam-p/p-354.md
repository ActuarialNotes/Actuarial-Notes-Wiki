An insurance company insures male and female drivers. The probability that a randomly selected insured driver is male and has an accident is 0.30. The probability of an insured male driver having an accident is 0.50.

Calculate the probability that a randomly selected insured driver is female.

- A) $0.15$
- B) $0.40$
- C) $0.50$
- D) $0.60$
- E) $0.85$

## Explanation

Let $M$ be the event that the driver is male, $F$ that the driver is female, and $A$ that the driver has an accident. The two given probabilities are of different kinds: the first is a **joint** probability and the second a **conditional** one.

$$
\begin{align*}
P[M \cap A] &= 0.30 \\
P[A \mid M] &= 0.50
\end{align*}
$$

The definition of conditional probability, $P[A \mid M] = P[M \cap A] / P[M]$, rearranges to give $P[M]$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[M] &= \frac{P[M \cap A]}{P[A \mid M]} \\
     &= \frac{0.30}{0.50} \\
     &= 0.60
\end{align*}
$$

Every driver is either male or female, so

$$
\begin{align*}
P[F] &= 1 - P[M] \\
     &= 1 - 0.60 \\
     &= \mathbf{0.40}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (D) is $P[M]$ itself, the value produced one step before the answer. The question only asks for a complement after that, so the work is really in reading which of the two given numbers is conditional on what.
