Ontario's Pension Benefits Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P.8, in the e-Laws consolidation from July 1, 2026 (last amendment 2026, c. 2, Sched. 13). Its groups of sections run from interpretation and application, through registration and administration, record keeping and disclosure, membership, retirement, benefits, contributions, locking in and family law matters, to winding up, surplus, asset transfers between pension plans, conversions, the Pension Benefits Guarantee Fund, the Chief Executive Officer's orders and appeals, and the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario.
