An annuity-immediate has quarterly payments of 100, 200, 300, and 400 each year for ten years. At a nominal annual rate of 12% convertible quarterly, the present value of the annuity is $P$.

Determine the equation of value.

- A) $P = 100(Ia)_{\overline{4}|3\%}\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|12\%}$
- B) $P = 100(Ia)_{\overline{4}|3\%}\,a_{\overline{10}|12\%}$
- C) $P = 100(I\ddot{a})_{\overline{4}|3\%}\,a_{\overline{10}|12.55\%}$
- D) $P = 100(I\ddot{a})_{\overline{4}|3\%}\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|12\%}$
- E) $P = 100(Ia)_{\overline{4}|3\%}\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|12.55\%}$

## Explanation

First determine the present value of the four payments within a year. Then determine the present value of ten of these.

The quarterly effective rate is $\dfrac{0.12}{4} = 0.03$. Within each year, the payments of 100, 200, 300 and 400 come at the end of each quarter, so at the start of the year they are worth $100(Ia)_{\overline{4}|0.03}$. The annuity is therefore equivalent to ten annual payments of $100(Ia)_{\overline{4}|0.03}$, each at the start of a year: an annuity-due, valued at the annual effective rate

$$
\begin{align*}
i &= \left(1 + \frac{0.12}{4}\right)^{4} - 1 \\
  &= 0.12551
\end{align*}
$$

Hence,

$$P = \left[100(Ia)_{\overline{4}|0.03}\right]\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|0.12551}$$
