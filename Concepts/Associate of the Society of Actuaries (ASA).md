An **Associate of the Society of Actuaries (ASA)** is an actuary who has finished the basic education of the Society of Actuaries (SOA), the professional body for actuaries in life and health insurance, pensions and investments. An ASA is the first requirement for an [[Fellow of the Society of Actuaries (FSA)|FSA]].

%%credential-path ASA%%

The SOA's route shares its first two exams with the CAS's, so a candidate can choose between the [[Credential Path|societies' routes]] after starting.

The SOA revises this route from time to time and publishes transition rules for credit already earned. Check them before you plan a sitting order.
