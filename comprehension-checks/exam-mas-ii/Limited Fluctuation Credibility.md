The standard for full credibility is 1,082 claims. A class has produced 271 claims. What partial credibility does it earn?

- A) 0.25, the ratio of observed claims to the standard
- B) 0.06, the square of that ratio
- C) 1.00, since the class has a substantial body of data
- D) 0.50
