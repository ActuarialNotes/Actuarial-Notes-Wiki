Consider a 7-year loan to be repaid with equal payments made at the end of each year. The annual effective interest rate is 10%.

Calculate the Macaulay duration of the loan payments.

- A) 3.15
- B) 3.29
- C) 3.40
- D) 3.50
- E) 3.62

## Explanation

Let $x$ be the annual payment. Macaulay duration:
$$
\begin{align*}
D &= \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{7} k \cdot x(1.1)^{-k}}{\sum_{k=1}^{7} x(1.1)^{-k}} \\
  &= \frac{(Ia)_{\overline{7}|0.1}}{a_{\overline{7}|0.1}}
\end{align*}
$$

$$a_{\overline{7}|0.1} = 4.8684$$

$$
\begin{align*}
(Ia)_{\overline{7}|0.1} &= \frac{\ddot{a}_{\overline{7}|} - 7v^7}{0.1} \\
                        &= \frac{5.3553 - 7(0.5132)}{0.1} \\
                        &= \frac{5.3553 - 3.5924}{0.1} \\
                        &= 17.629
\end{align*}
$$

$$D = 17.629/4.8684 = 3.62$$
