## [F-001] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space

- author: agent:validate-v1

- claim: Math spans write thousands separators as bare commas (200,000 / 41,929.54 / 158,070.46).
- evidence: In TeX math a bare comma is punctuation and is followed by a thin space, so e.g. $200,000$ renders '200, 000'. The numbers themselves match SOA's solution Q89 (solutions PDF p.25, image) and my recomputation.
