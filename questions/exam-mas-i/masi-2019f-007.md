You are given the following information regarding a series system with two independent machines, X and Y:

- The hazard rate function, in years, for machine $i$ is denoted by $r_i(t)$
- $r_x(t) = \ln(1.06)$, for $x > 0$
- $r_y(t) = \frac{1}{20-t}$, for $0 < y < 20$
- Both machines are currently three years old

Calculate the probability that the system fails when the machines are between five and nine years old.

- A) Less than 0.305
- B) At least 0.305, but less than 0.315
- C) At least 0.315, but less than 0.325
- D) At least 0.325, but less than 0.335
- E) At least 0.335

## Explanation

A series system fails when its first machine fails, so the system survives to a given age only if both machines do. Condition each machine's survival on its being alive at age 3, using $S(t)/S(3) = \exp\left(-\int_3^t r(s)\,ds\right)$.

**Machine X** has a constant hazard, so it is exponential:

$$
\frac{S_X(t)}{S_X(3)} = e^{-(t-3)\ln 1.06} = 1.06^{-(t-3)}.
$$

**Machine Y** has hazard $1/(20-t)$, the uniform (de Moivre) law with limiting age 20:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{S_Y(t)}{S_Y(3)} &= \exp\left(-\int_3^t \frac{ds}{20-s}\right) \\
&= \frac{20-t}{17}
\end{align*}
$$

By independence, the system's survival to age $t$ given both are age 3 is the product:

$$
S(t) = 1.06^{-(t-3)} \cdot \frac{20-t}{17}.
$$

The system fails between ages 5 and 9 with probability

$$
\begin{align*}
S(5) - S(9) &= 1.06^{-2}\left(\frac{15}{17}\right) - 1.06^{-6}\left(\frac{11}{17}\right) \\
&= 0.78529 - 0.45615 \\
&= 0.32914
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.325, 0.335)$, so the answer is **D**.
