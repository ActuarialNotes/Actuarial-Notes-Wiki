Let $X$ be a random variable that is uniform on $[a, b]$. The probability that $X$ is greater than 8 is 0.60. The probability that $X$ is greater than 11 is 0.20.

Calculate the variance of $X$.

- A) $3.70$
- B) $4.69$
- C) $6.25$
- D) $7.24$
- E) $8.75$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution, an upper-tail probability is the proportion of the interval above the cutoff:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 11] &= \frac{b - 11}{b - a} = 0.2 \\
P[X > 8]  &= \frac{b - 8}{b - a} = 0.6
\end{align*}
$$

The first gives $b - a = 5(b-11)$. Substituting into the second:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.6\bigl[5(b-11)\bigr] &= b - 8 \\
3b - 33 &= b - 8 \\
2b &= 25 \\
b &= 12.5
\end{align*}
$$

Then $b - a = 5(12.5 - 11) = 7.5$, so $a = 5$.

The variance of a uniform distribution is the squared interval length over 12:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X) &= \frac{(12.5 - 5)^2}{12} \\
              &= \frac{56.25}{12} \\
              &= \mathbf{4.6875}
\end{align*}
$$

A quick check on the endpoints: 8 sits 40% of the way from 5 to 12.5 and 11 sits 80% of the way, matching the given tail probabilities of 0.60 and 0.20. Choice (C), 6.25, divides the squared length by 9 instead of 12.
