A research paper, dated August 2015, sponsored by the Society of Actuaries Pension Section Research Committee. It surveys current literature to help pension actuaries understand the corporate finance practices within which decisions on defined benefit pension plans are now made at an enterprise level. It identifies three corporate finance metrics that pension plans affect: corporate leverage, rating agency accounting adjustments, and the weighted average cost of capital.
