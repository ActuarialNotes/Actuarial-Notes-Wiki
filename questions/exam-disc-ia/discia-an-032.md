A retailer's commercial package policy contains a commercial property coverage part and a commercial general liability coverage part. Where would you find how many days' notice the insurer must give before cancelling the whole policy for nonpayment of premium?

- A) The Common Policy Conditions
- B) The Building and Personal Property Coverage Form
- C) The commercial property declarations
- D) The CGL coverage form's own conditions section

## Explanation

In a modular commercial package, conditions that apply to every coverage part, including cancellation, changes, premiums and transfer of rights, are in the Common Policy Conditions (IL 00 17), which give 10 days' notice for nonpayment and 30 days for any other reason. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — the BPP holds the property coverage part's insuring agreement, limits, deductible and loss conditions, not policy-wide cancellation.
- **C** — the declarations show facts that vary by insured, such as limits and locations, not the cancellation rules.
- **D** — the CGL form's conditions apply to the liability coverage part only; cancellation of the whole policy sits in the common conditions.
