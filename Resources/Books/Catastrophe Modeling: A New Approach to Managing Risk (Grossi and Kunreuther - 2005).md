An edited volume that analyzes how catastrophe models can be used for assessing and managing the risks of extreme events, in the Huebner International Series on Risk, Insurance and Economic Security. It focuses on natural disaster risk but also discusses the management of terrorism risk. Three catastrophe modeling firms, AIR Worldwide, EQECAT and Risk Management Solutions, examine the role of catastrophe modeling in rate setting, portfolio management and risk financing, using data from three model cities (Oakland, Long Beach and Miami/Dade County).

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 9 (CAS)|Exam 9]] — objectives A4–A5; Chapters 2-6 (excluding references at the end of each chapter) and including errata for Section 2.4, updated March 2021.
