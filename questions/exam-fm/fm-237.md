An annuity writer sells an annual perpetuity-immediate for 16,000. The first payment is 1,000 and the payments decrease by $r$% each year.

The annual effective yield rate is 5.7%.

Calculate $r$.

- A) 0.52
- B) 0.55
- C) 0.62
- D) 0.73
- E) 0.91

## Explanation

This is a geometrically decreasing perpetuity. The growth factor is $1 - r/100$. With yield $i = 5.7\%$:

$$
\begin{align*}
16{,}000 &= \frac{1{,}000}{i - (-r/100)} \\
       &= \frac{1{,}000}{0.057 - (-r/100)}
\end{align*}
$$

Payments decrease, so growth factor $= 1 - r/100 < 1$. The PV formula for a geometrically decreasing perpetuity-immediate:

$$
\begin{align*}
16{,}000 &= \frac{1{,}000}{i+r/100} \\
       &= \frac{1{,}000}{0.057+r/100}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
0.057 + r/100 &= 1{,}000/16{,}000 \\
              &= 0.0625 \\
r/100 &= 0.0625 - 0.057 \\
      &= 0.0055 \\
r &= 0.55
\end{align*}
$$
