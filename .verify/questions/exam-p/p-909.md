- severity: minor

- locus: whole question (learning_objective: Multivariate Random Variables)
- claim: Total claims $W = X + Y$ with independent exponentials (means 3000, 1500). Calculate $P[W > 3000]$.
- evidence: SOA Probability Exam syllabus Nov 2026, PDF p.4, Topic 3 outcome g: "Calculate probabilities for linear combinations of independent discrete random variables as well as for continuous normal random variables." (same in the Jan 2027 syllabus); outcome h covers only moments of linear combinations. Mitigating: the Topic 3 Learning Objective sentence mentions "discrete and continuous settings".
