At age 66 a retiree annuitizes under the life with 20-year period certain option, at \$1,200 a month. He dies 12 years after payments begin. Which statement is correct?

- A) Payments stop at his death, because a life annuity pays only while the annuitant is alive
- B) His beneficiary receives a lump sum equal to the premium paid less the payments made
- C) His beneficiary receives \$1,200 a month for the rest of the beneficiary's life
- D) His beneficiary receives \$1,200 a month for the remaining 8 years of the period certain

## Explanation

Under life with period certain, the insurer pays for as long as the annuitant lives and guarantees payments for the set period. He died 12 years into a 20-year period, so his beneficiary receives the regular payments for the remaining

$$
20 - 12 = \mathbf{8} \text{ years}
$$

If he had died after the 20 years, nothing would be paid after his death.

- **A** describes the life only option, which pays the highest income because nothing continues after death.
- **B** describes a refund of premium, which this option does not promise. A period certain guarantees a number of payments, not the return of what was paid in.
- **C** describes a joint and survivor option, which pays as long as either of two people lives.
