Replacing 8 territory bands with 220 individual postal codes drops training deviance from 2,400 to 1,950 but raises holdout deviance from 2,510 to 2,890. What happened?

- A) Bias rose faster than variance fell
- B) The irreducible error rose with the extra levels
- C) The model became too rigid for the data
- D) Variance rose faster than bias fell
