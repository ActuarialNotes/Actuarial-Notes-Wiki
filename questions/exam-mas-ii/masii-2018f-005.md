You are given:

- The number of claims incurred per year for each insured has mean $\theta$ and variance $2\theta$.
- $\theta$ is uniformly distributed from 0.05 to 0.25.
- The following table of claim experience for a company:

| Year | Number of Insureds | Number of Claims |
|------|--------------------|------------------|
| 1 | 200 | 15 |
| 2 | 250 | 20 |
| 3 | 225 | 25 |
| 4 | 250 | --- |

Calculate the estimated claim count for this company in Year 4 using the Bühlmann-Straub credibility approach.

- A) Less than 21
- B) At least 21, but less than 23
- C) At least 23, but less than 25
- D) At least 25, but less than 27
- E) At least 27

## Explanation

Each insured's annual claim count has hypothetical mean $\theta$ and process variance $2\theta$, with $\theta \sim U(0.05, 0.25)$. The structural parameters per insured are

$$
\begin{align*}
\mu &= E[\theta] \\
&= 0.15 \\
v &= E[2\theta] \\
&= 0.30 \\
a &= \operatorname{Var}(\theta) \\
&= \frac{(0.25 - 0.05)^2}{12} \\
&= 0.003333
\end{align*}
$$

In Bühlmann-Straub the exposure is the number of insured-years in Years 1–3, and the observed mean is claims per insured:

$$
\begin{align*}
m &= 200 + 250 + 225 \\
&= 675 \\
\bar{X} &= \frac{15 + 20 + 25}{675} \\
&= 0.08889 \\
k &= \frac{0.30}{0.003333} \\
&= 90 \\
Z &= \frac{675}{675 + 90} \\
&= 0.8824
\end{align*}
$$

The credibility-weighted frequency per insured, applied to Year 4's 250 insureds:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{\lambda} &= 0.8824(0.08889) + 0.1176(0.15) \\
&= 0.09608 \\
250\,\hat{\lambda} &= 24.02
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[23, 25)$, so the answer is **C**.
