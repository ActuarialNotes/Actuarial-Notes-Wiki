A journal article by Carolyn Kousky and Howard Kunreuther on the roles of the public and private sectors in insuring disaster losses. Its abstract says it synthesizes six papers and the findings of a November 2016 workshop, "Improving Disaster Financing: Evaluating Policy Interventions in Disaster Insurance Markets," which evaluated disaster insurance programs for flood, earthquake and terrorism losses, and suggests ways to improve public-private partnerships for disaster financing in risk communication, risk reduction and risk transfer. It concludes with a proposal for a comprehensive insurance program harnessing the benefits of both sectors.
