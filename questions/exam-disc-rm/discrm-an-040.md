A homeowner's dog bites a neighbor's child, and the child's parents claim damages for her medical bills and pain and suffering. Which describes the exposure and how an individual usually treats it?

- A) A property exposure, treated by the homeowners policy's property coverages
- B) A liability exposure, treated by the homeowners policy's liability section and an umbrella
- C) A liability exposure, treated by the personal auto policy
- D) A personal exposure, treated by the family's own health insurance

## Explanation

The claim alleges the homeowner is legally responsible for injury to another person, so it is a **liability** exposure; pets are one of an individual's personal liability exposures. The homeowners policy's liability section treats it, with an umbrella policy above it for larger claims. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Property coverages pay for damage to the homeowner's own property; the child's injury is a claim by someone else.
- **C** — The personal auto policy treats liability from owning and driving autos, not from pets.
- **D** — The family's health insurance covers its own members; the injured child belongs to the neighbor.
