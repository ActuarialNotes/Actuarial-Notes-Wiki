Consider two loans. Loan A has an initial principal of $P_0$ and an annual nominal interest rate of $i$, convertible monthly. Loan B also has an annual nominal interest rate of $i$, but the interest is convertible daily.

At the end of the first month, a payment of $m$ is made on Loan A, which includes one month of interest. The remaining balance on Loan A is then $P_1$.

Let $j$ be the monthly effective interest rate of Loan B, assuming there are 12 equal months in a year and 365 days in a year.

Determine which of the following represents $j$.

- A) $\left[1+\dfrac{1}{12}\left(\dfrac{P_1-P_0+m}{P_0}\right)\right]^{12}-1$
- B) $\left[1+12\left(\dfrac{P_1-P_0+m}{P_0}\right)\right]^{1/12}-1$
- C) $\left[1+\dfrac{12}{365}\left(\dfrac{P_1-P_0+m}{P_0}\right)\right]^{12}-1$
- D) $\left[1+\dfrac{365}{12}\left(\dfrac{P_1-P_0+m}{P_0}\right)\right]^{12/365}-1$
- E) $\left[1+\dfrac{12}{365}\left(\dfrac{P_1-P_0+m}{P_0}\right)\right]^{365/12}-1$

## Explanation

Let $I$ be the amount of interest in the first month. Then

$$
P_0 - m + I = P_1, \qquad I = P_1 - (P_0 - m)
$$

In the first month the interest $P_1 - (P_0 - m)$ was charged on a principal of $P_0$, so the effective monthly rate of Loan A is

$$
\frac{P_1 - (P_0 - m)}{P_0} = \frac{P_1 - P_0 + m}{P_0}
$$

The nominal annual rate for both loans is therefore $i = 12\left(\dfrac{P_1 - P_0 + m}{P_0}\right)$, so the effective daily rate of Loan B is

$$
\frac{i}{365} = \frac{12}{365}\left(\frac{P_1 - P_0 + m}{P_0}\right)
$$

A month is $365/12$ days, so the effective monthly rate of Loan B is

$$
j = \left[1 + \frac{12}{365}\left(\frac{P_1 - P_0 + m}{P_0}\right)\right]^{365/12} - 1
$$

which is answer E.
