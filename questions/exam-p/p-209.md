Let $X$ be a random variable with density function

$$f(x) = \begin{cases} 2e^{-2x}, & x > 0 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

Calculate $P[X \leq 0.5 \mid X \leq 1.0]$.

- A) 0.433
- B) 0.547
- C) 0.632
- D) 0.731
- E) 0.865

## Explanation

Integrate the density to get the distribution function:

$$F(x) = \int_0^x 2e^{-2y}\,dy = -e^{-2y}\Big|_0^x = 1 - e^{-2x}$$

The event $\{X \leq 0.5\}$ lies inside $\{X \leq 1.0\}$, so the conditional probability is a ratio of two values of $F$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X \leq 0.5 \mid X \leq 1.0] &= \frac{P[X \leq 0.5]}{P[X \leq 1.0]} = \frac{F(0.5)}{F(1.0)} \\
&= \frac{1 - e^{-1}}{1 - e^{-2}} = \frac{0.6321}{0.8647} \\
&= \mathbf{0.731}
\end{align*}
$$
