A report to the American Academy of Actuaries' Board of Directors by its Public Plans Practices Task Force, created to examine actuarial issues and practices related to public-sector retirement systems. It argues for the actuary to act as a risk advisor in the public plan sector and recommends initiatives to strengthen the management of public pension and post-retirement health plans through an actuarial application of emerging risk management techniques, organised around a risk management framework.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — RET101-111-25; Appendices B and C only (the remainder is background); Outcomes 2b. The SOA course strategy guide says the appendices discuss the structure and governance of public retirement plans in the U.S., some of it relevant to other countries.
