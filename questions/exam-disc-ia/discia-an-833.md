A contractor expects 5 premises and operations liability claims a year. With illustrative figures, 30% of claims result in a payment averaging \$40,000, and defending a claim costs \$8,000 on average whether or not it results in a payment. What is the expected annual cost of this liability loss exposure, including defense?

- A) \$60,000
- B) \$72,000
- C) \$100,000
- D) \$240,000

## Explanation

The cost of a liability exposure includes damages and defense costs, and defense is incurred on every claim, including those that end without a payment.

$$
\begin{align*}
E[L] &= 5\left(0.30 \times 40{,}000 + 8{,}000\right) \\
&= 5(12{,}000 + 8{,}000) \\
&= 100{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** leaves out defense costs: 5 claims times 0.30 times \$40,000.
- **B** charges defense only on the claims that result in a payment: 5 claims times 0.30 times \$48,000.
- **D** assumes every claim results in a payment: 5 claims times \$48,000.
