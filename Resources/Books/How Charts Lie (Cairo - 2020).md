A guide to reading charts critically: how they encode data, and the ways they mislead through design, data, uncertainty and the patterns they suggest. It is a Domain C reading for [[Exam PCPA (CAS)|PCPA]], on [[Data Visualization|presenting model results]] honestly. The CAS content outline cites the 2020 Norton edition. The Library of Congress records the first edition as \[2019\], with hardcover and paperback ISBNs.
