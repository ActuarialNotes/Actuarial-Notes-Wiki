## Tooling fixed in the run

- **`verify_record.py`: a replayed batch is now a no-op.** One agent re-applied its batch to
  test idempotency, and the recorder filed 34 "recurrences" of findings it had just resolved,
  with second resolutions and passes.
  - Those entries were never committed. They were trimmed from the 13 working-tree logs, with
    every dropped entry asserted to belong to the replay.
  - The recorder now treats a finding closed in the same run, a repeated resolution and a
    repeated pass as nothing to append. Replaying that batch now leaves all 13 logs
    byte-identical, pinned by `test_a_batch_applied_twice_leaves_every_log_byte_identical`.
- **`verify_paper.py`: the stem diff ignores LaTeX environment names.** Before the fix,
  `\begin{cases}` read as the word "cases".
- **Citation labels must not put a comma before the question number.** The app reads
  `SOA Exam P Sample Questions …, Q n` with `[^,]*`. Twelve passes were re-recorded with a
  comma-free label after `questionPublisher.test.ts` caught them.
