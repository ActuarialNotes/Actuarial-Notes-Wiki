A three-part series of articles on economic scenario generators (ESGs) in The Modeling Platform, the newsletter of the Society of Actuaries' Modeling Section, published in November 2019, August 2020 and July 2021. The series gives an overview of ESGs: the general motivation for stochastic modeling and its advantages and limitations, then the key factors underpinning ESGs, then a case study of the Academy Interest Rate Generator (AIRG), the most commonly used real-world ESG for US actuaries.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-312 (SOA)|Exam CP-312]] — Part I (Nov 2019), Part II (Aug 2020) and Part III (Jul 2021), each for outcome 2a (Topic 2).

## Part I Motivation for Stochastic Modeling
- What Is Stochastic Modeling?
- Advantages of Stochastic Modeling
    - 1. Measuring "Tail Risks"
    - 2. Enabling Market-Consistent Valuation
    - 3. Satisfying Internal and External Stakeholder Requirements
    - 4. Quantifying Asymmetric Responses and Path Dependency
- Limitations of Stochastic Modeling
    - 1. Relying on "Black Box" Assumptions
    - 2. Making Subjective Judgments
    - 3. Requiring Time-Intensive Processes
- Conclusion
