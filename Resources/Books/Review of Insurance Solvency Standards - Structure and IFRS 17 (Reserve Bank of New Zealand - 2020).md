A consultation document of the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, published 30 November 2020, on the first stage of its review of the insurance solvency standards. That stage addresses structural changes and issues needing immediate attention, including preparing the standards for [[IFRS 17]]; this document covers the issues that shape the standards' fundamental structure — their purpose and principles, their application across sectors and funds, the impacts of IFRS 17, a ladder of intervention framework and the structure of the solvency calculations.
