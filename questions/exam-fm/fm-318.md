A ten-year bond with a face amount of 1000, a redemption value of $C$, and an annual coupon rate of 7% paid semiannually is purchased at a discount to yield an annual nominal rate of 7.5%, convertible semiannually.

The accumulation of discount in the 12th coupon is 7.

Calculate $C$.

- A) 673
- B) 799
- C) 1193
- D) 1344
- E) 1540

## Explanation

Work in half-years. The semiannual coupon is $Fr = 1000(0.035) = 35$, the semiannual yield rate is $i = 0.075/2 = 0.0375$, and there are $n = 20$ coupons. Let $g = Fr/C$ be the modified coupon rate, so $Cg = Fr$.

For a bond bought at a discount ($Ci > Fr$), the accumulation of discount in coupon $k$ is $C(i-g)v^{n-k+1} = (Ci - Fr)v^{n-k+1}$. For the 12th coupon, $n - k + 1 = 20 - 12 + 1 = 9$:

$$
\begin{align*}
7 &= C(i-g)v^{9} \\
&= \left[Ci - Cg\right]\left(\frac{1}{1.0375}\right)^9 \\
&= (0.0375C - 35)(1.0375)^{-9}
\end{align*}
$$

Multiply through by $(1.0375)^9 = 1.392813$:

$$
\begin{align*}
7(1.0375)^9 &= 0.0375C - 35 \\
9.7497 &= 0.0375C - 35 \\
C &= \frac{44.7497}{0.0375} \\
&= 1193.33
\end{align*}
$$

So $C \approx 1193$.
