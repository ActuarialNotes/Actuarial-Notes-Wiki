A February 2024 report of the Society of Actuaries Research Institute on generative artificial intelligence, which builds on the core concepts of AI to generate new data or material such as text, images or computer code. It surveys where generative AI may emerge in actuarial work (general task automation, coding and model documentation, data enrichment, scenario development, claims and underwriting), the limitations actuaries must be cognizant of, and the architecture and deployment considerations before putting an application into a production process.
