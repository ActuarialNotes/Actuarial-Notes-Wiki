A MAPFRE Economics report on the progress of insurance solvency regulation toward risk-based systems, published by Fundación MAPFRE in March 2024. It analyzes the prudential regulations applicable to insurers and their groups in the United States, Latin America, Asia-Pacific and the European Union, and global initiatives, since the 2018 edition of the report, measuring progress with the Risk-Based Regulation Proximity Index (I-RBR). It closes with the institutional and market preconditions that must be met for further progress in implementing risk-based regulation in the insurance industry.
