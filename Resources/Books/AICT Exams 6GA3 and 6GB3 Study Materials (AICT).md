The Actuarial Institute of Chinese Taipei's lists of the study materials for its two non-life exams, 6GA3 and 6GB3, for the Fall 2026 sitting (August 28, 2026). The exams are 精算實務處理準則及會計實務 (Actuarial Standard of Practice and Accounting) and 保險法規與紀律規範 (Insurance Regulations and Discipline); each list (考試範圍, the exam scope) names every document examined, with its version. The AICT says the exams confirm that an actuary knows the relevant standards of practice and accounting practice (6GA3) and the insurance laws and its disciplinary rules (6GB3), and that passing both passes the CAS's Exam 6 – Taipei.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6T (CAS)|Exam 6T]] — the study materials are set and published by the Actuarial Institute of Chinese Taipei; the CAS's Exam 6-Taipei syllabus lists no readings of its own
