After a downpour, water ponds on a warehouse's flat roof and its weight causes part of the roof to fall in, ruining stock below. Wind did not damage the roof. Which statement is correct?

- A) The basic form covers it under windstorm, because rain accompanied the storm
- B) The broad form covers it under weight of snow, ice or sleet, which includes rain
- C) No causes of loss form covers it, because collapse is excluded under all three
- D) The broad form covers it through its collapse additional coverage, but not the basic form

## Explanation

The broad form adds an additional coverage for abrupt collapse of a building, and the weight of rain that collects on a roof is one of the listed causes. The basic form has no collapse coverage, and the loss fits none of its perils. The answer is **D**. (The special form also covers collapse caused by the weight of rain that collects on a roof.)

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Windstorm requires wind or hail damage; rain falling with a storm is not windstorm, and the basic form excludes interior rain damage unless the wind first opens the roof or walls.
- **B** — The broad form's peril is the weight of snow, ice or sleet. Rain is not one of them; its weight is covered only through the collapse additional coverage.
- **C** — Collapse is excluded under the special form except for the additional coverage and listed causes, and the broad form grants it as an additional coverage, so not all three forms deny it.
