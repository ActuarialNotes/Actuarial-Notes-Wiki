## 1 Application of subsection 3570 – Target Pension Arrangements
- Paragraph 3570.01 defines a target pension arrangement as a plan for which applicable legislation contemplates reducing accrued pensions while the plan is ongoing as one of the options for maintaining its funded status, where the reduction is not necessarily caused by the financial distress of the plan sponsor.
- Any plan meeting this definition is a target pension arrangement for purposes of Section 3500, whether or not it is required to be funded on a solvency basis or the reduction is subject to conditions such as regulatory approval.
- Section 3500 does not apply to commuted values under a target pension arrangement in the case of a full or partial wind-up; no standard of practice specifies the assumptions and method for those calculations.
