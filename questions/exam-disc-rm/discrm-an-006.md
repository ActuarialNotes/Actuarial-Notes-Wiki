A hospital board asks its new chief risk officer to start an enterprise risk management program. The CRO proposes four activities. Under the CAS's ERM framework, which should come first?

- A) Run workshops with department heads to list the risks that could affect the hospital
- B) Plot the risks the hospital already knows about on a frequency and severity map
- C) Set out the hospital's objectives, stakeholders and a common measure for judging risks
- D) Buy higher liability limits for the exposures behind the largest past claims

## Explanation

The CAS framework begins by **establishing the context**: the external context (the hospital's environment and stakeholders), the internal context (its objectives, strategies and governance) and the risk management context (the risk categories that matter and a common risk metric). Everything later is judged against it. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Workshops are a good way to identify risks, but identification is the second step; without the context there is no agreed basis for deciding what counts as a material risk.
- **B** — Mapping risks by frequency and severity belongs to assessing and prioritizing, which comes after identification and analysis.
- **D** — Buying limits is treatment, chosen before the risks are even identified or ranked; it reflects the traditional insurance-buying approach rather than the process.
