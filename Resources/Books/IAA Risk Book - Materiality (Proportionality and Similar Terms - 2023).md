A chapter of the IAA Risk Book, developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association (version 2.0, April 2023). It addresses the concepts of proportionality, materiality, cost-benefit and risk-focused, which allow exceptions to set templates and processes, by outlining their current definitions and discussing issues with their use and possible pitfalls.
