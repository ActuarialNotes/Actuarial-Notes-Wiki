Each day, an insurance claims adjuster is assigned five reports to complete. The total time required to complete all five reports is normally distributed with mean 7.5 hours and standard deviation 1.5 hours.

Calculate the probability that the claims adjuster will be finished in eight hours or less given that he is still working after seven and one-half hours.

- A) $0.13$
- B) $0.26$
- C) $0.37$
- D) $0.63$
- E) $0.74$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the time to complete the five reports. The conditioning event is $X \ge 7.5$, and the target is $X \le 8$, so the intersection is the band $7.5 \le X \le 8$:

$$
P[X \le 8 \mid X \ge 7.5] = \frac{P[7.5 \le X \le 8]}{P[X \ge 7.5]}
$$

Standardizing with $\mu = 7.5$ and $\sigma = 1.5$:

$$
= \frac{P\!\left[\dfrac{7.5 - 7.5}{1.5} \le Z \le \dfrac{8 - 7.5}{1.5}\right]}{P\!\left[Z \ge \dfrac{7.5 - 7.5}{1.5}\right]} = \frac{P[0 \le Z \le 0.333]}{P[Z \ge 0]}
$$

The conditioning point is the mean itself, so the denominator is exactly 0.5:

$$
\frac{0.13056}{0.5} = \mathbf{0.2611}
$$

Choice (D), 0.63, is the unconditional $P[X \le 8] = 0.6306$. Choice (A), 0.13, is the numerator alone. Knowing the adjuster is still working at the 7.5-hour mark discards the entire lower half of the distribution, which is why the conditional answer is far below the unconditional one.
