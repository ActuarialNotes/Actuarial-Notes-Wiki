Losses incurred by a policyholder follow a normal distribution with mean 20,000 and standard deviation 4,500. The policy covers losses, subject to a deductible of 15,000.

Calculate the 95th percentile of losses that exceed the deductible.

- A) 27,400
- B) 27,700
- C) 28,100
- D) 28,400
- E) 28,800

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the loss and $k$ the 95th percentile of losses that exceed the deductible, so $P(X>k)=0.05\,P(X>15000)$.

$P(X\leq15000)\approx0.1333$. Setting $P(X\leq k)=0.05(0.1333)+0.95=0.9567$: $k=20000+4500(1.72)\approx\mathbf{27700}$
