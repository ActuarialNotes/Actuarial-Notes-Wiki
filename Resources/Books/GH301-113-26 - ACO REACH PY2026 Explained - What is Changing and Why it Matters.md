A Milliman paper of 2025 that puts the Center for Medicare and Medicaid Innovation's (CMMI) performance year 2026 updates to the ACO REACH program into context. It describes each change and quantifies its estimated impact, assuming for each analysis that no other change is implemented, unless otherwise noted.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-301 (SOA)|Exam GH-301]] — Provider Contracting and Reimbursement, outcome 1d; the whole paper.

## Background
- In late May 2025 CMMI announced updates to the ACO Realizing Equity, Access, and Community Health (REACH) Model for performance year (PY) 2026, based on early findings from the PY2023 evaluation of the program.

## 1 Modify risk score growth constraints
- CMMI will apply an additional, one-sided 3% cap to risk score growth between 2019 and 2026 for Standard ACOs, after the existing 3% symmetrical cap and coding intensity factor (CIF).
- For High Needs ACOs the cap on the High Needs CIF rises from 1% to 2%, with an 8% cap on risk score growth (2023 to 2026) for new voluntarily aligned beneficiaries.
- Figure 1: Estimated PY2026 gross savings percent impact of additional risk score cap

## 2 Reduce the regional component of the benchmark for all REACH ACOs
- The weight of regional expenditures falls to 40% for Standard ACOs and 45% for High Needs or New Entrant ACOs.
- Figure 2: PY2025 and PY2026 regional weights in baseline adjustment
- Figure 3: Estimated PY2026 gross savings percent impact of regional weight update

## 3 Narrow the first risk corridors to 10% for REACH ACOs in the global risk option
- The first corridor, in which ACOs retain 100% of savings or losses, falls from 25% to 10% of the benchmark, lowering both risk and reward.
- Figure 4: Estimated percentage of ACOs impacted by risk corridor update

## 4 Increase quality withhold from 2% to 5% while proportionally increasing the high performers pool bonus
- The quality withhold, reduced to 2% from PY2023, will be 5% from PY2026; the high performers pool is funded by unearned withholds.
- Figure 5: Estimated PY2023 gross savings percent impact of quality withhold update

## 5 Update risk adjustment model
- The transition from the V24 to the V28 risk score model continues, with V28 weighted 33% in 2024, 67% in 2025 and 100% in 2026; no changes to the original plan.

## 6 Adjust PY2024 expenditures for significant, anomalous, and highly suspect billing
- CMMI will remove two HCPCS codes for urinary catheters and ostomy bags (A4353 and A5057) from PY2024 settlement calculations, from historical benchmark years and from future performance years in which 2024 serves as a benchmark year, affecting the PY2024 and PY2025 retrospective trend adjustments.

## What's next
- These changes generally increase pressure on REACH ACOs to decrease expenditures and increase quality of care, and the future of REACH beyond PY2026 is uncertain.
