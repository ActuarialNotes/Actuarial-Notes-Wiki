Loan A is a 96-month loan with an annual nominal interest rate of 7.2% compounded monthly. This loan is repaid with equal payments of $m$ at the end of each of the first 48 months followed by payments of $1.5m$ at the end of each of the last 48 months.

Loan B is also a 96-month loan with the same annual nominal interest rate compounded monthly. This loan is repaid with equal payments of $1.2m$ at the beginning of each of the first 48 months followed by payments of $0.9m$ at the beginning of each of the last 48 months.

Calculate the ratio of the amount of Loan A to the amount of Loan B.

- A) 1.127
- B) 1.133
- C) 1.145
- D) 1.152
- E) 1.190

## Explanation

The effective monthly interest rate is $i = 0.072/12 = 0.006$. Let $L_A$ be the amount of Loan A and $L_B$ the amount of Loan B. Each is the present value of its payments, with the last 48 payments deferred 48 months. We then have

$$
\begin{align*}
L_A &= m\,a_{\overline{48}|i} + 1.5m\,a_{\overline{48}|i}/(1+i)^{48} \\
L_B &= 1.2m\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{48}|i} + 0.9m\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{48}|i}/(1+i)^{48}
\end{align*}
$$

Since $\ddot{a}_{\overline{48}|i} = (1+i)\,a_{\overline{48}|i}$, the factor $m\,a_{\overline{48}|i}$ cancels from the ratio. Hence

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{L_A}{L_B} &= \frac{m\,a_{\overline{48}|i} + 1.5m\,a_{\overline{48}|i}/(1+i)^{48}}{1.2m\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{48}|i} + 0.9m\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{48}|i}/(1+i)^{48}} \\
                &= \frac{1 + 1.5/1.006^{48}}{(1.2 + 0.9/1.006^{48})(1.006)} \\
                &= \frac{1 + 1.5(0.750407)}{[1.2 + 0.9(0.750407)](1.006)} \\
                &= \frac{2.125611}{1.886619} \\
                &= 1.12668
\end{align*}
$$
