A new property and casualty insurance company started writing business on January 1, 2014. It has the following loss information as at December 31, 2014:

| | Paid Loss | Case Reserves | Incurred Loss | Ultimate Loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gross Layer | \$32,000,000 | \$14,000,000 | \$46,000,000 | \$60,000,000 |
| Ceded Layer | \$12,000,000 | \$6,000,000 | \$18,000,000 | \$26,000,000 |

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Discount rate (gross and ceded) | 5% |
| The gross unpaid claims discounted at 5% (excluding PfAD) | \$26,804,722 |
| The ceded unpaid claims discounted at 5% (excluding PfAD) | \$13,402,361 |
| MfAD for investment return rate (gross and ceded) | 1% |

Cumulative Accident Year Payment Pattern for all layers:

- Percentage paid by 12 months: 50%
- Percentage paid by 24 months: 80%
- Percentage paid by 36 months: 100%

Assume that all payments are made in the middle of the year. The same Margin for Adverse Deviation (MfAD) for claims development is used for both gross and ceded layers.

The following additional information is provided:

- The Unpaid Claims and Adjustment Expenses from page 20.20 are \$28,374,091.
- The Unpaid Claims and Adjustment Expenses Recoverable from Reinsurers from page 20.10 are \$12,377,727.
- The booked claim liabilities match the actuary's estimate.
- All business is ceded to a single reinsurer with an A.M. Best financial strength rating of C.

## Part a (2 points)

Calculate the MfAD for recovery from reinsurance ceded used to determine the Unpaid Claims and Adjustment Expenses Recoverable from Reinsurers.

### Explanation
- Undiscounted gross reserves = ultimate - paid $= 28\text{M}$
- Undiscounted ceded reserves = ultimate - paid $= 14\text{M}$

Discounted reserves plus PfADs (given):

- Gross: $28{,}374{,}091$
- Ceded: $12{,}377{,}727$

**Back out the MfAD for claims development from the gross.** The investment-return MfAD lowers the discount rate to $5\% - 1\% = 4\%$, so

$$28{,}374{,}091 = \text{discounted gross reserves} + \text{PfAD interest} + \text{PfAD claims} = \text{discounted gross reserves @ 4\%} + \text{MfAD}_{\text{claims}} \times 26{,}804{,}722$$

$$\text{Discounted gross reserves @ 4\%} = \frac{0.6 \times 28\text{M}}{1.04^{0.5}} + \frac{0.4 \times 28\text{M}}{1.04^{1.5}} = 27{,}033{,}855$$

Hence $\text{MfAD}_{\text{claims}} = 5\%$.

**Then the reinsurance MfAD from the net.** The ceded side is

$$\text{Discounted ceded reserves} + \text{PfADs} = \text{discounted ceded reserves @ 4\%} + \text{MfAD}_{\text{claims}} \times 13{,}402{,}361$$

and the net is gross - ceded:

$$\text{Discounted net reserves} + \text{PfADs} = 28{,}374{,}091 - 12{,}377{,}727 = 15{,}996{,}364$$

$$= \text{discounted net reserves @ 4\%} + \text{MfAD}_{\text{claims}} \times \text{discounted net reserves @ 5\%} + \text{MfAD}_{\text{reinsurance}} \times \text{discounted ceded @ 5\%}$$

$$= \frac{0.6 \times 14\text{M}}{1.04^{0.5}} + \frac{0.4 \times 14\text{M}}{1.04^{1.5}} + 0.05 \times (26{,}804{,}722 - 13{,}402{,}361) + \text{MfAD}_{\text{reinsurance}} \times 13{,}402{,}361$$

Hence $15{,}996{,}364 = 14{,}187{,}046 + \text{MfAD}_{\text{reinsurance}} \times 13{,}402{,}361$, so

$$\text{MfAD}_{\text{reinsurance}} = 13.5\%$$

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally did well on this part.  The most common errors were calculation errors.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Evaluate and briefly explain whether the calculated MfAD from part a. above is reasonable.

### Explanation
- It is the high end of the acceptable range (0 to 15%), but should be higher since all reinsurance is with one company, and that company is poorly rated
- Range for reinsurance MfAD is 0 to 15%.  The financial rating of AM best is low and thus expect the MfAD to be at a higher range.  Thus, 13.5% is acceptable.
- If there is calculation error in part a and the rationale stated in part b is reasonable, credit is given

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally did well on this part and would earn full marks as long as they showed that they understood the range and were able to provide a rationale to back up the calculated Mfad

Common errors included:
- not mentioning that Mfad is a range
- not providing an appropriate rationale

## Part c (0.5 points)

This company selected its ceded discount rate to be the same as its gross and net discount rates (its portfolio yield rate). List two other possible sources for the selection of the ceded discount rate.

### Explanation
- Risk free rate
- The rate selected by the reinsurer to discount the same liabilities

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally did well on this question
