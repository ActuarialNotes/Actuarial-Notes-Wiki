A textbook by Giuseppe Campolieti and Roman N. Makarov, published by CRC Press in 2014 (805 pages), in four parts: an introduction to pricing and management of financial securities, discrete-time modeling, continuous-time modeling, and computational techniques. The SOA supplies its Chapter 5, on single-period Arrow-Debreu models, as study note INV201-100-25.
