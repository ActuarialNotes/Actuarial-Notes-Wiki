A building worth \$800,000 at the time of loss is insured for \$500,000 under the BPP with an 80% coinsurance percentage and a \$1,000 deductible. A fire causes a \$64,000 loss. How much does the insurer pay?

- A) \$39,000
- B) \$49,000
- C) \$49,218.75
- D) \$63,000

## Explanation

Follow the coinsurance condition's four steps:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Step 1:}\ \ 0.80 \times 800{,}000 &= 640{,}000 \\
\text{Step 2:}\ \ 500{,}000 \div 640{,}000 &= 0.78125 \\
\text{Step 3:}\ \ 0.78125 \times 64{,}000 &= 50{,}000 \\
\text{Step 4:}\ \ 50{,}000 - 1{,}000 &= 49{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Divides the limit by the full value instead of 80% of it: $R = 500{,}000/800{,}000 = 0.625$, giving \$39,000.
- **C** — Applies the ratio after subtracting the deductible; the form multiplies the loss *before* the deductible.
- **D** — Ignores the penalty, though the limit is below the \$640,000 the condition requires.
