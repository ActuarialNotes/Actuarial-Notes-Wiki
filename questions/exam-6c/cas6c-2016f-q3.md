## Part a (0.5 points)

Describe the purpose of the Grid rating program in Alberta.

### Explanation
- The Grid rating program was developed to set maximum premiums that insurance companies can charge for basic coverage for any driving record. The Grid was also developed promote coverage availability and affordability.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to demonstrate knowledge about the Grid program in Alberta.

Candidates were expected to demonstrate knowledge of two elements: the Grid was developed to set maximum premiums for basic coverage for any given driving record.
A common error was mentioning only one of the two items. Partial credit was given.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Briefly describe the potential effects of eliminating the Grid rating program.

### Explanation
Any four of the following:

- More competitive rates for insured
- Less subsidization between risks
- Fewer rules for insurers to determine pricing
- Some insureds could have difficulty finding a carrier (availability issue)
- Drivers capped by the Grid may have some affordability issues
- The risk sharing pool may decrease as insurers may charge the appropriate premium
- Could put more pressure on the Facility Association residual market
- Could increase innovation since would insurers have the incentive to charge the appropriate premium
- Loss ratios may decrease because premium is not limited
- May have to revisit the all-comer rule

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to demonstrate that they can evaluate the effects of eliminating the Grid rating program.
A common mistake was not providing sufficient details in the potential effects (e.g. mentioning only one potential effect with no explanation).
