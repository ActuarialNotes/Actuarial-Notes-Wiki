For a 10-year deferred whole life annuity-due with payments of 100,000 per year on (70), you are given:

(i) Annual gross premiums of $G$ are payable for 10 years

(ii) First year expenses are 75% of premium

(iii) Renewal expenses for years 2 and later are 5% of premium during the premium paying period

(iv) Mortality follows the Standard Ultimate Life Table

(v) $i = 0.05$

Calculate $G$ using the equivalence principle.

[A modified version of Question 8 on the Fall 2017 exam]

- A) 64,900
- B) 65,400
- C) 65,900
- D) 66,400
- E) 66,900

## Explanation

$$\begin{align*}
G\ddot{a}_{70:\overline{10}|} &= 100{,}000\,{}_{10}E_{70}\,\ddot{a}_{80} + 0.05G\ddot{a}_{70:\overline{10}|} + 0.7G \\
7.6491G &= (100{,}000)(0.50994)(8.5484) + 0.05G(7.6491) + 0.7G \\
\Rightarrow G &= 66{,}383.54
\end{align*}$$

The answer is **D**.
