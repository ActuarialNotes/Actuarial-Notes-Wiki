Under SSAP No. 55, which cost is classified as adjusting and other (AO) expense rather than defense and cost containment (DCC)?

- A) Fees of an attorney defending the insured under the insurer's duty to defend
- B) Fees of an expert witness retained for the defense of a lawsuit
- C) Fees of an attorney retained to determine whether the policy covers the claim
- D) Surveillance of a claimant whose claimed injury is disputed

## Explanation

DCC covers defense, litigation and medical cost containment, including attorney fees incurred owing to a duty to defend, surveillance and the cost of experts. Attorney fees incurred in determining coverage, including litigation between the insurer and the policyholder, are AO. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — defense counsel under the duty to defend is the core DCC expense.
- **B** — the cost of engaging experts is listed as DCC.
- **D** — surveillance expenses are listed as DCC.
