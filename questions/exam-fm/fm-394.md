Given a force of interest $\delta_t = \dfrac{1}{t+8}$, where $t$ is measured in years, calculate the effective rate of interest during the 5th year.

- A) 7.4%
- B) 7.7%
- C) 8.0%
- D) 8.3%
- E) 8.7%

## Explanation

The 5th year runs from $t = 4$ to $t = 5$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
1 + i_5 &= e^{\int_{4}^{5} \frac{1}{t+8}\,dt} \\
        &= e^{\left.\ln(t+8)\right|_{4}^{5}} \\
        &= e^{\ln 13 - \ln 12} \\
        &= \frac{13}{12} \\
        &= 1.08333 \\
i_5 &= 8.3\%
\end{align*}
$$
