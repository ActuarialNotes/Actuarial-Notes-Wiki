The eLearning module that serves as the content for the Medical Data topic of the Society of Actuaries' GH 301 course. The SOA's course strategy guide says candidates read the module and the source material it references, most of which shows how to identify data sources, evaluate their quality and reliability, and adjust them for other actuarial purposes, and that the module notes whether or not each document is testable. The syllabus says module content is accessed through the SOA online portal registration.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-301 (SOA)|Exam GH-301]] — Outcomes 4a-4h: medical terms, diseases and treatment options, coding sets (CPT, ICD-10, DRG, Revenue Codes) and how they change, clinical policy bulletins, public and private data, study methods, and researching a question using multiple data sources.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> The module is available only through the SOA's online portal, so its content and section titles could not be read. This page records its citation and assignment from the syllabus and the SOA's course strategy guide.
