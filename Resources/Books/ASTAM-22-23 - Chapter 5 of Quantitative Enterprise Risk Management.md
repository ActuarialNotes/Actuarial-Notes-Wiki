Extreme Value Theory, the fifth chapter of Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management by Mary R. Hardy and David Saunders, reprinted by the Society of Actuaries as a study note for the Advanced Short-Term Actuarial Mathematics exam with the permission of Cambridge University Press. The chapter presents key results from extreme value theory (EVT) and shows how EVT can supplement traditional statistical analysis when the concern is the impact of very rare, very large losses.
