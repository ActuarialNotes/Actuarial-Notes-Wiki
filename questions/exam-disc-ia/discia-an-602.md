A garden center insures its business personal property under the BPP, with no Personal Property of Others limit and a 70% coinsurance percentage. A grass fire spreads from a neighboring lot. Which loss is covered under the business personal property limit?

- A) Garden furniture held for sale, stored in a rented locker two miles away
- B) Patio sets held for sale, displayed in the open 70 feet from the store
- C) A customer's riding mower left at the store for repair in the back room
- D) Planters held for sale aboard a barge docked at the river behind the lot

## Explanation

Business personal property is covered in or on the described building, or in the open or in a vehicle within 100 feet of the building or the premises, whichever distance is greater. Stock displayed 70 feet from the store is inside that zone. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Property two miles away is outside the 100-foot zone. The property off-premises extension might reach it, but extensions apply only when the coinsurance percentage is 80% or more, and here it is 70%.
- **C** — A customer's mower is personal property of others, which is covered only if a limit is shown for it; the personal effects and property of others extension is again unavailable at 70% coinsurance.
- **D** — Personal property while waterborne is property not covered.
