Under statutory accounting, which item is charged or credited directly to surplus rather than passing through statutory net income?

- A) Earned premium
- B) Incurred losses and loss adjustment expenses
- C) Net investment income
- D) The change in nonadmitted assets

## Explanation

Under the NAIC Preamble, statutory income excludes certain direct charges to surplus that are not attributable to the earnings process, and it names the change in nonadmitted assets as an example. The change in the provision for reinsurance is another direct charge to surplus. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A**, **B** and **C** — earned premium, incurred losses and LAE, and investment income are all part of the earnings process and pass through the statement of income.
