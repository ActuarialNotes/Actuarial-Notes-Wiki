The joint probability function of $X$ and $Y$ is given by

$$
p(x,y) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{-2x - 4y + xy + 8}{18}, & \text{for } x = 1, 2, 3 \text{ and } y = 0, 1 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate $E\left(\dfrac{Y}{X}\right)$.

- A) $0.102$
- B) $0.200$
- C) $0.241$
- D) $0.306$
- E) $0.722$

## Explanation

The expectation of a function of two variables is the sum of that function's value at each cell, weighted by the cell probability — no marginal or conditional distribution is needed.

Evaluate the joint function over the six cells:

| | $X = 1$ | $X = 2$ | $X = 3$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $Y = 0$ | 6/18 | 4/18 | 2/18 |
| $Y = 1$ | 3/18 | 2/18 | 1/18 |

These total $18/18 = 1$, confirming the function was read correctly.

Every cell in the $Y = 0$ row contributes $0/x = 0$, so only the second row matters:

$$
\begin{align*}
E\left(\frac{Y}{X}\right) &= \left(\frac{1}{1}\right)\left(\frac{3}{18}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{2}{18}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(\frac{1}{18}\right) \\
                          &= \frac{3}{18} + \frac{1}{18} + \frac{1}{54} \\
                          &= \frac{13}{54} \\
                          &= \mathbf{0.241}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (B), 0.200, is $E[Y]/E[X] = \tfrac{1}{3} \big/ \tfrac{5}{3}$. The expectation of a ratio is not the ratio of expectations, and evaluating cell by cell is the only reliable route.
