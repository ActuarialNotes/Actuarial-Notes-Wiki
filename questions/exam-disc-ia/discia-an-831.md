A hotel's CGL is asked to respond to three matters during the policy period: (1) a guest's medical bills after she slips in the lobby, paid without any claim that the hotel was at fault; (2) a lawsuit by a former tenant of a hotel-owned apartment who says the hotel locked him out of his rooms without legal process; (3) a guest's suit for burns from a defective hair dryer in her room. Which coverages of the CGL respond to (1), (2) and (3) respectively?

- A) Coverage A, Coverage B, Coverage C
- B) Coverage C, Coverage A, Coverage A
- C) Coverage B, Coverage B, Coverage A
- D) Coverage C, Coverage B, Coverage A

## Explanation

- **(1)** Medical expenses for bodily injury from an accident on the premises, paid regardless of fault, are Coverage C.
- **(2)** Wrongful eviction from, or wrongful entry into, premises a person occupies, by or for its landlord, is a personal and advertising injury offense, so Coverage B.
- **(3)** Bodily injury caused by an occurrence is Coverage A.

The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** treats the medical payment as a liability claim and the eviction as Coverage C.
- **B** treats the eviction as bodily injury or property damage; it is a listed offense.
- **C** places the medical payment under Coverage B, which covers offenses, not medical expenses.
