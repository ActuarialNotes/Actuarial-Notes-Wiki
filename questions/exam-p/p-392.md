The amount of time, in years, that a refrigerator functions before breaking down is a continuous random variable with density function

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases}
c(x-5), & \text{for } 5 \leq x \leq 8 \\
c(11-x), & \text{for } 8 < x \leq 11 \\
0, & \text{otherwise,}
\end{cases}
$$

where $c$ is a constant.

Calculate the probability that the refrigerator will function between six and eight years before breaking down.

- A) $0.222$
- B) $0.278$
- C) $0.333$
- D) $0.379$
- E) $0.444$

## Explanation

The density is a triangle, rising from 0 at $x=5$ to a peak at $x=8$ and falling back to 0 at $x=11$. Find $c$ by integrating over both pieces and setting the total to 1:

$$
\begin{align*}
c\left[\int_5^8 (x-5)\,dx + \int_8^{11}(11-x)\,dx\right] &= 1 \\
c\left[\frac{(x-5)^2}{2}\bigg|_5^8 - \frac{(11-x)^2}{2}\bigg|_8^{11}\right] &= 1 \\
c\left[4.5 + 4.5\right] &= 1 \\
c &= \frac{1}{9}
\end{align*}
$$

The interval from 6 to 8 lies entirely in the first piece, so only that branch is integrated:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[6 < X < 8] &= \frac{1}{9}\int_6^8 (x-5)\,dx \\
             &= \frac{(x-5)^2}{18}\bigg|_6^8 \\
             &= \frac{9 - 1}{18} \\
             &= \mathbf{0.444}
\end{align*}
$$

The two halves of the triangle each carry probability 0.5, and the interval $[6,8]$ covers most but not all of the rising half — so an answer just under 0.5 is the sanity check. Choice (C), 0.333, is the fraction of the *base* covered, $2/6$, which would be right only for a uniform density.
