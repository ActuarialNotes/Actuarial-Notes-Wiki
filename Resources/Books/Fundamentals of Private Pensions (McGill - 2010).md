![[Fundamentals of Private Pensions (McGill - 2010) - Cover.svg]]

The ninth edition of a text the Pension Research Council describes as, for five decades, the most authoritative on private pensions in the US. The revised and updated edition adds to past knowledge while providing new perspectives on the provision of retirement income, for academics and professionals working in pensions and pension management. The volume is organized into eight sections.
