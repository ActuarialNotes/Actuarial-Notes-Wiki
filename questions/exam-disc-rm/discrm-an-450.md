A regional property-casualty insurer that writes homeowners and auto policies in three states makes four proposals. Which one is a forward vertical integration strategy?

- A) Begin writing its existing homeowners policies in two neighboring states
- B) Buy the independent agencies that sell most of its policies to customers
- C) Buy a life insurer, an industry with few similarities to its own
- D) Lay off staff and close two regional offices to cut costs

## Explanation

Vertical integration is a corporate-level strategy in which a firm enters a new part of its value chain. Moving forward means entering a buyer's business, toward the customer: buying the agencies that sell the insurer's policies takes it into the distribution of its own product. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Selling existing products in new markets is market development, a concentration strategy within the same industry.
- **C** — Entering an industry with few important similarities to the existing one is unrelated diversification.
- **D** — Reducing the size of part of the operations is retrenchment, a strategy for getting smaller.
