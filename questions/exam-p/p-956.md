A loss severity $X$ follows a gamma distribution with mean 800 and variance 320,000.

Calculate $P(X > 800)$.

- A) $0.135$
- B) $0.271$
- C) $0.406$
- D) $0.594$
- E) $0.677$

## Explanation

First recover the parameters. Since $E[X] = \alpha\theta$ and $\text{Var}(X) = \alpha\theta^2$, dividing strips out $\alpha$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\theta &= \frac{\text{Var}(X)}{E[X]} \\
       &= \frac{320{,}000}{800} \\
       &= 400 \\
\alpha &= \frac{E[X]}{\theta} = \frac{800}{400} = 2
\end{align*}
$$

With integer $\alpha = 2$ and $x/\theta = 800/400 = 2$, use the gamma survival identity:

$$
\begin{align*}
P(X > 800) &= \sum_{k=0}^{1}\frac{e^{-2}2^k}{k!} \\
           &= e^{-2}(1 + 2) \\
           &= 3e^{-2} \\
           &= \mathbf{0.406}
\end{align*}
$$

Answer (D) is $1 - 0.406$, the probability the loss falls *below* the mean. Note that a gamma is right-skewed, so more than half its mass lies below its mean.
