A guide to the legislative, professional and administrative requirements for running pension and other retirement plans in Canada. In the publisher's description, the sixth edition (LexisNexis Canada, March 2017; 528 pages) covers all types of retirement income plans, including defined benefit, defined contribution, RRSPs and deferred profit sharing plans, with a comprehensive examination of Canada's tax rules for retirement income programs and background material on minimum pension standards, actuarial valuations and accounting standards.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcomes 3a, 3b (Regulations: Evaluation and Application): Ch. 1 Overview (section 105, sections 120-145) (3a), Ch. 2 and Ch. 3 (3a, 3b), Ch. 5-12 (3b), Ch. 14 and Ch. 16-22 (3b), Ch. 23 Pensions for the Highly Paid (3b); Outcomes 4a, 4b (Regulations: Plan Funding): Ch. 15 Pension Plan Valuation Concepts (excluding Section 1525) and Ch. 23 Pensions for the Highly Paid.
