A CIA educational note to assist actuaries in applying the Standards of Practice in the valuation of group life and health policy liabilities in life insurer financial statements prepared under Canadian GAAP. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated June 2010 (document 210034), it applies to employee, association and creditor group insurance, participating and non-participating, and to assumed group reinsurance on the same principles. It is educational guidance, not a Standard of Practice.
