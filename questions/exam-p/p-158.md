The number of days an employee is sick each month is modeled by a Poisson distribution with mean 1. The numbers of sick days in different months are mutually independent.

Calculate the probability that an employee is sick more than two days in a three-month period.

- A) 0.199
- B) 0.224
- C) 0.423
- D) 0.577
- E) 0.801

## Explanation

In a three-month period, the total number of sick days $N \sim \text{Poisson}(3)$ (sum of three independent Poisson(1) variables).

$$
\begin{align*}
P(N > 2) &= 1 - P(N \leq 2) \\
         &= 1 - e^{-3}\left(1 + 3 + \frac{9}{2}\right) \\
         &= 1 - 8.5e^{-3} \\
         &= 1 - 8.5(0.049787) \\
         &= 1 - 0.4231 \\
         &= \mathbf{0.577}
\end{align*}
$$
