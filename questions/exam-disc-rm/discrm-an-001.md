A printing company's risk management policy states: "After a major fire, the company must be able to resume at least some printing within 45 days." Which risk management objective does this statement express?

- A) Economy, a pre-loss objective
- B) Survival, a post-loss objective
- C) Continued growth, a post-loss objective
- D) Reduction of anxiety, a pre-loss objective

## Explanation

Resuming **at least partial operation within a reasonable time after a loss** is the survival objective, the first of the post-loss objectives. The statement concerns what must be true after the fire, so it is post-loss. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Economy is a pre-loss objective: preparing for potential losses in the most economical way, by comparing the costs of safety programs, insurance and other techniques. The statement says nothing about cost.
- **C** — Continued growth concerns the firm's ability to keep expanding through new products, markets or acquisitions after a loss, not restarting basic operations.
- **D** — Reduction of anxiety is about limiting the worry certain exposures cause before any loss occurs; the statement sets a target for recovery after one.
