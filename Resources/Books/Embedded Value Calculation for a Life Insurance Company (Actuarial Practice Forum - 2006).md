A paper in the Society of Actuaries' Actuarial Practice Forum (October 2006) that looks at the embedded value of a Canadian life insurance company. Its abstract says the principles behind the embedded value calculation hold for any company in any country. It examines how profits emerge from a life insurance company, the formulas behind the embedded value, how the assumptions can be determined, the movement of the embedded value from one year to the next, and its uses.
