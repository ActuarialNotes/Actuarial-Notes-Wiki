A Texas insurer writes personal auto and homeowners insurance. Which statement about its underwriting guidelines is consistent with the Texas Insurance Code?

- A) They are trade secrets that the insurer never has to disclose to the Department of Insurance
- B) They need to be filed only when the insurer first enters the state, not when they later change
- C) They may use any criterion the insurer chooses, provided its rates have been filed and approved
- D) They must be filed with the department and may not be unfairly discriminatory

## Explanation

For personal auto and residential property, Texas requires each insurer to file its underwriting guidelines with the Department of Insurance and to update the filing each time they change. The guidelines must be sound, actuarially justified or otherwise substantially commensurate with the risk, and may not be unfairly discriminatory. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Guidelines for other lines are confidential in Texas, but even they can be obtained by the department. Personal auto and homeowners guidelines must be filed.
- **B** — The filing must be updated whenever the guidelines change.
- **C** — Guidelines are regulated separately from rates. A filed rate does not make an unjustified or unfairly discriminatory acceptance rule lawful.
