- A) They rise on the recent diagonals, so ultimate losses come out overstated
- B) They are unaffected, since strengthening changes only timing and not the ultimate settlement value
- C) They fall on the recent diagonals, and selections blended down from them understate the older years' ultimate
- D) They fall, correctly reflecting genuinely lower future development for every accident year
