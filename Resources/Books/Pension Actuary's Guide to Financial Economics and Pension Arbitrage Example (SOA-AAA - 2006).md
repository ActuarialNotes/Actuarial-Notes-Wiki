![[Pension Actuary's Guide to Financial Economics and Pension Arbitrage Example (SOA-AAA - 2006) - Cover.svg]]

The Joint AAA/SOA Task Force's guide for actuaries and others to learn what financial economics says about how pension plans are designed, invested, accounted for and funded. It is not a statement about how actuaries should practice nor how pension plans should be operated. It was written before the U.S. Pension Protection Act of 2006 was enacted, and it refers readers to a Pension Arbitrage Example worksheet on the SOA website for its worked tax-arbitrage illustration.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 2b; the syllabus cites the guide together with the Pension Arbitrage Example.
