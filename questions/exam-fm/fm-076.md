Let $S$ be the accumulated value of 1000 invested for two years at a nominal annual rate of discount $d$ convertible semiannually, which is equivalent to an annual effective interest rate of $i$.

Let $T$ be the accumulated value of 1000 invested for one year at a nominal annual rate of discount $d$ convertible quarterly.

$$S/T = (39/38)^4$$

Calculate $i$.

- A) 10.0%
- B) 10.3%
- C) 10.8%
- D) 10.9%
- E) 11.1%

## Explanation

Express $S$ and $T$ in terms of $d$:

$$
\begin{align*}
S &= 1000(1-d/2)^{-4} \\
T &= 1000(1-d/4)^{-4}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{S}{T} &= \frac{(1-d/2)^{-4}}{(1-d/4)^{-4}} \\
&= \left(\frac{1-d/4}{1-d/2}\right)^4 \\
&= \left(\frac{39}{38}\right)^4
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{1-d/4}{1-d/2} &= \frac{39}{38} \\
39 - 39(d/2) &= 38 - 38(d/4) \\
d(39/2 - 38/4) &= 1 \\
d &= \frac{1}{19.5-9.5} \\
  &= 0.1
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
1+i &= (1-d/2)^{-2} \\
&= (0.95)^{-2} \\
&= 1.108 \\
i &= 10.8\%
\end{align*}
$$
