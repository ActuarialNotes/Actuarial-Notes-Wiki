A 1000-par value 30-year bond has an annual coupon rate of 7% paid semiannually. After an initial 10-year period of call protection, the bond is callable immediately following the payment of any of the 20th through the 59th coupons.

- i) If the bond is called before payment of the 40th coupon, the redemption value is 1250.
- ii) If the bond is called immediately after the payment of any of the 40th through the 59th coupons, the redemption value is 1125.
- iii) If the bond is not called, it will be redeemed at par.

To ensure that the bond will provide at least an annual nominal yield rate of 5% convertible semiannually, it must be assumed that the bond will be called or redeemed immediately after the payment of the $n$-th coupon.

Calculate $n$.

- A) 20
- B) 39
- C) 40
- D) 59
- E) 60

## Explanation

The semiannual yield rate is $j = 0.025$ and the semiannual coupon is $1000(0.035) = 35$. Whether the earliest or the latest call date in a redemption period is the worst case for the buyer depends on the **modified coupon rate** $Fr/C$ (coupon divided by redemption value) compared with $j$.

**First redemption period (after coupons 20–39, redemption 1250).** The modified coupon rate is $1000(0.035)/1250 = 2.80\%$, which is larger than the desired yield rate of 2.5%. If redeemed during this period the bond sells at a premium, so the worst case for the buyer is the earliest redemption, after the 20th coupon:

$$35a_{\overline{20}|0.025} + 1250(1.025)^{-20} = 35(15.5892) + 762.84 = 1308.46$$

**Second redemption period (after coupons 40–59, redemption 1125).** The modified coupon rate is $1000(0.035)/1125 = 3.11\%$, which is also larger than the desired yield rate, so the worst case is again the earliest redemption, after the 40th coupon:

$$35a_{\overline{40}|0.025} + 1125(1.025)^{-40} = 35(25.1028) + 418.98 = 1297.58$$

**Not called (redeemed at par after the 60th coupon):**

$$35a_{\overline{60}|0.025} + 1000(1.025)^{-60} = 35(30.90866) + 227.284 = 1309.09$$

The appropriate price is the lowest of these three, 1297.58, which relates to the bond being called after the **40th** coupon is paid.
