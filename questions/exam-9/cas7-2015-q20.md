Given the following information for an insurer's gross aggregate loss distribution:

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Expected value | \$20,000,000 |
| 90% VaR | \$50,000,000 |
| Limited expected value at \$50,000,000 | \$8,000,000 |

- The capital requirement is set at 90% TVaR.

The insurer is evaluating the impact of two reinsurance options from a reinsurer that has received a B credit rating from a widely recognized rating agency. Assume a 60% charge for reinsurance recoverable credit risk associated with this reinsurer.

Option 1

- Excess-of-loss reinsurance
- Attachment: \$50,000,000
- Limit: \$100,000,000
- Expected ceded loss: \$7,500,000

Option 2

- Quota share reinsurance
- 40% ceding percentage
- No ceding commission

## Part a (1.25 points)

Calculate the net capital requirement for the insurer if Option 1 is purchased.

### Explanation
With $\alpha = 0.9$:

$$TVaR_\alpha(X_{Gross}) = VaR_\alpha(X_{Gross}) + \frac{E[X_{Gross}] - E[X_{Gross} \wedge VaR_\alpha(X_{Gross})]}{1 - \alpha} = 50M + \frac{20M - 8M}{1 - 0.9} = 170M$$

The excess-of-loss layer attaches at the gross 90% VaR, so its whole expected ceded loss sits in the tail:

$$TVaR_\alpha(X_{Cede}) = VaR_\alpha(X_{Cede}) + \frac{E[X_{Cede}] - E[X_{Cede} \wedge VaR_\alpha(X_{Cede})]}{1 - \alpha} = \frac{7.5M}{1 - \alpha} = 75M$$

Only $(1 - \theta)$ of the ceded amount is credited, where $\theta = 0.6$ is the credit risk charge:

$$\text{Net capital requirement} = TVaR_\alpha(X_{Gross}) - (1 - \theta) \times TVaR_\alpha(X_{Cede}) = 170M - (1 - 0.6) \times 75M = 140M$$

Alternatively:

Work from the net position: $TVaR_\alpha(X_{Net}) = 50M + \dfrac{20M - 7.5M - 8M}{1 - 0.9} = 95M$, and add back the credit charge on the ceded amount: $95M + 0.6 \times 75M = 140M$.

### Examiner Report
Candidates struggled with this question. It was common that the candidates did not know the formulas used to calculate TVaR and the Net Capital Requirement. It was common that the candidates did not enter the proper values into the calculation of TVaR and the Net Capital

Requirement.

## Part b (0.75 points)

Calculate the net capital requirement for the insurer if Option 2 is purchased.

### Explanation
$$TVaR_\alpha(X_{Gross}) = 50M + \frac{20M - 8M}{1 - 0.9} = 170M$$

$$TVaR_\alpha(X_{Cede}) = 0.4 \times TVaR_\alpha(X_{Gross}) = 0.4 \times 170M = 68M$$

$$\text{Net capital requirement} = TVaR_\alpha(X_{Gross}) - (1 - \theta) \times TVaR_\alpha(X_{Cede}) = 170M - (1 - 0.6) \times 68M = 142.8M$$

### Examiner Report
Same comments as for part a above.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Briefly describe two challenges of assessing the impact of reinsurance on the insurer's risk profile.

### Explanation
- The complexity of reinsurance contracts makes their impact on losses harder to calculate — excess-of-loss recoveries do not have a linear relationship with the underlying risks.
- Some reinsurance contracts do not warrant a credit to the insurer's risk (they may be bought for other reasons, such as tax).

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally did well on this question.

## Part d (0.5 points)

Briefly describe two ways the insurer may be able to lower its reinsurance recoverable credit risk exposure.

### Explanation
- Reduce concentration: diversify the reinsurance program across many reinsurers in different regions.
- Choose reinsurers with good credit ratings to reduce default risk.
