A commercial package policy includes a commercial property coverage part with a mortgageholder shown in the declarations. The first named insured has not paid the premium, and the insurer decides to cancel. Under the Common Policy Conditions (IL 00 17) and the BPP's mortgageholders condition, what is the least notice the insurer must give?

- A) 30 days to the first named insured, and none to the mortgageholder
- B) 10 days to every named insured, and 30 days to the mortgageholder
- C) 10 days to the first named insured, and 10 days to the mortgageholder
- D) 30 days to the first named insured, and 10 days to the mortgageholder

## Explanation

Under IL 00 17, the insurer may cancel by mailing or delivering notice to the **first named insured** at least 10 days before the effective date for nonpayment of premium (30 days for any other reason). The BPP's mortgageholders condition separately requires written notice to the mortgageholder at least 10 days before cancellation for nonpayment (30 days for any other reason). The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Uses the 30-day period that applies to cancellation for reasons other than nonpayment, and forgets the mortgageholder's own notice right.
- **B** — Notice goes to the first named insured, not every named insured, and the mortgageholder's period for nonpayment is 10 days.
- **D** — Uses the 30-day period for the named insured, which is for reasons other than nonpayment.
