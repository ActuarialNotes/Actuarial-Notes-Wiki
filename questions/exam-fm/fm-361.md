A level monthly contribution of $X$ is required to purchase an annual perpetuity of 5000 that commences five years from today.

The contributions are made at the end of each month for 60 months. The last contribution is made at the same time as the first payment from the perpetuity.

The annual nominal interest rate is 12% compounded monthly.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 402
- B) 483
- C) 510
- D) 544
- E) 581

## Explanation

Value everything at the end of month 60 (five years from today), when the last contribution is made and the first perpetuity payment is due. The monthly interest rate is $0.12/12 = 0.01$, so the annual effective rate is $i = (1.01)^{12} - 1$.

At that date the contributions have accumulated to $X\,s_{\overline{60}|0.01}$, and the perpetuity, whose first payment is due immediately, is a perpetuity-due worth $\dfrac{5000}{d}$, where $d = \dfrac{i}{1+i}$ is the annual effective rate of discount:

$$
\begin{align*}
X\,s_{\overline{60}|0.01} &= \frac{5000}{d} \\
  &= \frac{5000}{i}(1+i) \\
  &= \frac{5000(1.01)^{12}}{(1.01)^{12} - 1} \\
  &= \frac{5000(1.12682503)}{0.12682503} \\
  &= 44{,}424.39
\end{align*}
$$

Since $s_{\overline{60}|0.01} = 81.66967$,

$$
\begin{align*}
81.66967X &= 44{,}424.39 \\
X &= 543.95
\end{align*}
$$
