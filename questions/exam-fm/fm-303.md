Two deposits are made into a fund: 300 at time 0 and $X$ at time 4. The force of interest for the fund is $\delta_t = \dfrac{t}{50}$, $t \ge 0$. The amount of interest earned from time 0 to time 10 is $4X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 145
- B) 173
- C) 181
- D) 192
- E) 201

## Explanation

The accumulation function: $a(t) = \exp\!\left(\int_0^t \frac{r}{50}\,dr\right) = \exp\!\left(\frac{t^2}{100}\right)$.

Balance at time 10:

$$
\begin{align*}
300\,a(10) + X\cdot\frac{a(10)}{a(4)} &= 300e^{1} + X\,e^{0.84} \\
                                      &= 815.48 + 2.31637X
\end{align*}
$$

Total deposits: $300 + X$. Setting interest earned equal to $4X$:

$$
\begin{align*}
(815.48 + 2.31637X) - (300 + X) &= 4X \\
515.48 + 1.31637X &= 4X \\
515.48 &= 2.68363X \\
X &= 192.08
\end{align*}
$$
