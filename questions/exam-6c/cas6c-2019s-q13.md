The following information is available for a federally regulated property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2018. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| **Market Risk Margin** | |
| Interest rate risk | ? |
| Foreign exchange risk | 400 |
| Equity risk | 11,000 |
| Real estate risk | 0 |
| Other market risk exposures | 0 |
| **Credit Risk Margin** | 3,100 |
| **Operational Risk Margin** | ? |

Calculate the Minimum Capital Test (MCT) ratio.

### Explanation
Discount the unpaid claims for accident year 2018 (100,000 undiscounted, paid one third a year at mid-year):

| Time | Payment | PV@3% | PV@2.5% |
|---|---|---|---|
| .5 | 100000*1/3=33333.33 | 32844 | 32924 |
| 1.5 | 33333.33 | 31887 | 32121 |
| 2.5 | 33333.33 | 30959 | 31338 |
| **Total** | **100000** | **95690** | **96383** |

$$\text{APV} = 96383 + 95690 \times 12\% = 107866$$

$$\text{Mac Duration} = \frac{32844 \times .5 + 31887 \times 1.5 + 30959 \times 2.5}{95690} = 1.4803$$

$$\text{Modified Duration} = 1.4803 / 1.03 = 1.4372$$

Unpaid claims and premium liabilities margins:

$$\text{Unpaid Claims Margin} = 95690 \times 10\% = 9569$$

$$\text{Premium Liabilities Margin} = \max\big((150000 - 4500) \times .3,\ 78000\big) \times 15\% = 11700$$

Assuming "Net premium liabilities duration" is the modified duration:

$$\text{Interest Rate Risk Margin} = 1.25\% \times \big|128000 \times 3.1 - (107866 \times 1.4372 + 80000 \times 1.8)\big| = 1222$$

$$\text{Insurance Risk Margin} = 9569 + 11700 = 21269$$

$$\text{Market Risk Margin} = 1222 + 400 + 11000 = 12622$$

Operational risk margin, and its cap:

$$\text{Operational Risk Margin Cap} = 30\% \times (21269 + 12622 + 3100) = 11097.3$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Operational Risk Margin} &= 150000 \times 2.5\% + 4500 \times 2.5\% + (150000 - 100000 \times 1.2) \times 2.5\% \\
&\quad + (21269 + 12622 + 3100) \times 8.5\% = 7757 \quad \text{(less than cap)}
\end{aligned}$$

Diversification credit, with $I = 21269$ and $A = 12622 + 3100 = 15722$:

$$\text{Div Credit} = I + A - \sqrt{I^2 + A^2 + 2 \times .5 \times I \times A} = 4836$$

$$\text{Total Margin Required} = 21269 + 15722 + 7757 - 4836 = 39912$$

$$\text{MCT} = \frac{82000}{39912 / 1.5} = 308.2\%$$

Alternate premium liabilities duration calculation — assuming the "Net premium liabilities duration" is the Macauley duration:

$$\text{Modified duration} = \text{Macauley duration} / 1.03 = 1.80 / 1.03 = 1.748$$

### Examiner Report
Using the information provided, candidates were expected to determine the total capital required to calculate the MCT ratio. Candidates were expected to calculate the margins required for unpaid claims, premium liabilities, and market risk. In addition, candidates were expected to calculate the capital required for operational risk and the diversification credit.

Common errors included:

- Calculation errors
- Using an incorrect payment pattern
- Miscalculating the claims development PfAD
    - Using a discount rate of 2.5%
    - Using an incorrect MfAD percentage
- Using an incorrect formula in calculating the discounted with PfAD amount
- Not using modified duration when calculating the duration
- In the interest risk formula
    - Using incorrect values
    - Not mentioning the absolute value component of the formula
- In the net premium liabilities margin calculation
    - Not excluding PfAD from the premium liabilities amount
    - Not considering the maximum between 30% of net written premiums and net premium liabilities
    - Using direct written premium instead of net written premiums in calculating the maximum
- Omitting components or calculation errors in the operational risk calculation
- Not dividing by 1.5 in final MCT calculation
