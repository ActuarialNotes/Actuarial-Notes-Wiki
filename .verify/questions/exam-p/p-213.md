- locus: ## Explanation
- claim: Using Poisson(2): $P(X=2)=2e^{-2}$, $P(X\leq2)=5e^{-2}$. Ratio $=2/5=\mathbf{0.40}$
- evidence: The stem (SOA questions PDF p.92) specifies 100 independent bulbs with defect probability 0.02, i.e. Binomial(100, 0.02).
