A company is considering options for a workers compensation policy with the following parameters:

| Item | Value | Applicable to: |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Premium | \$1,000,000 | |
| Expected Loss and ALAE | 900,000 | |
| Per Occurrence Deductible | 250,000 | Loss and ALAE |
| Aggregate Limit | 1,000,000 | Loss and ALAE |
| ULAE | 7.0% | Loss and ALAE |
| Loss Based Assessment | 5.0% | Loss and ALAE |
| General Overhead | 5.0% | Standard Premium |
| Credit Risk | 4.0% | Standard Premium |
| Acquisition Expense | 5.0% | Net Premium |
| Profit and Contingency | 2.5% | Net Premium |
| Tax and Assessment | 8.0% | Net Premium |

| Limit | ELPPF |
|---|---|
| \$250,000 | 0.20 |
| 500,000 | 0.09 |
| 750,000 | 0.06 |
| 1,000,000 | 0.03 |

| Entry Ratio | Insurance Charge |
|---|---|
| 1.11 | 0.18 |
| 1.25 | 0.13 |
| 1.39 | 0.09 |
| 1.50 | 0.06 |

## Part a (1 point)

Calculate the premium for a large dollar deductible (LDD) policy.

### Explanation
Entry Ratio = \$1M / 900K (1 - .20) = 1.39 which the table corresponds to an insurance charge of 0.09.

$$\text{LDD Premium} = \frac{900{,}000\,[0.20 + 0.09 + 0.07 + 0.05] + 1{,}000{,}000(.05 + .04)}{1 - .05 - .08 - .025} = 543{,}195$$

Alternatively:

$$\text{LDD Premium} = \frac{900{,}000\,[0.20 + 0.8\,(0.09) + 0.07 + 0.05] + 1{,}000{,}000(.05 + .04)}{1 - .05 - .08 - .025} = 524{,}024$$

### Examiner Report
The most common error candidates made was forgetting to use limited losses in the denominator of the entry ratio calculation. Since it was not specified whether the insurance charge would apply to limited or unlimited losses, both answers were accepted.

## Part b (0.75 points)

Identify three reasons why an employer might choose an LDD Plan.

### Explanation
A medium-sized employer may be allowed to purchase an LDD plan even if it doesn't qualify for self-insurance.

Alternatively: If the company already has a full coverage policy, it is already familiar with the insurer's service if it decides to purchase an LDD policy.

Alternatively: LDD still services all losses (even those below the deductible) and insured can be comfortable with an experienced insurer handling the claims.

Alternatively: Employer gets to keep savings from tax and assessments for premium from losses under the deductible

Alternatively: Cash flow benefits as employer will get to hold onto cash longer since insurer will pay losses first and then seek reimbursement from the employer.

Alternatively: There is a tax incentive since the insured can deduct a tax liability for an unpaid deductible but not on a liability for a loss reserve.

Alternatively: Give employer more control of losses under the large deductible.

### Examiner Report
A variety of answers were accepted if they were explained correctly.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Assume the following revised assumptions for an excess workers compensation (WC) policy:

- Profit and Contingency = -1.5%
- Tax and Assessment = 3%

Calculate the revised premium.

### Explanation
$$\text{Excess Premium} = \frac{900{,}000\,(0.20 + 0.09)(1.07) + 1{,}000{,}000(.05)}{1 - .05 - 0.03 + .015} = 352{,}160$$

Alternatively:

$$\text{Excess Premium} = \frac{900{,}000\,[0.20 + (0.80)\,0.09](1.07) + 1{,}000{,}000(.05)}{1 - .05 - .03 + .015} = 333{,}621$$

### Examiner Report
The most common errors were ULAE not being applied to the total excess loss and only to either the insurance charge or the losses above the per occurrence deductible. Some candidates also forgot to exclude credit risk or loss based assessments. Errors made on part A were not penalized further on part C.

## Part d (0.5 points)

Describe the difference in profit and tax assumptions for LDD and excess WC policies.

### Explanation
| | LDD Plan | Excess WC |
|---|---|---|
| Profit | Profit is higher since company competes on excess price as well as quality of service | Profit is lower since company competes only on price |
| Tax | Tax rate on premium is WC tax rate | Tax rate charged on premium is GL tax rate |

Alternatively:

Profit – More ability for investment income due to longer payout period on XS vs. LDD $\rightarrow$ profit can be lower

Tax – XS uses GL taxes whereas LDD uses WC – WC taxes and assessments are higher

Alternatively:

LDD policies compete mostly on service while XS compete on profit. Therefore CS (as printed) policies have lower profits than LDD.

Taxes are less on XS policies since they are not subject to certain tax based assessments while LDD is.

### Examiner Report
The majority of candidates were able to identify the differences between these two assumptions but we include a few varieties of acceptable answers above.
