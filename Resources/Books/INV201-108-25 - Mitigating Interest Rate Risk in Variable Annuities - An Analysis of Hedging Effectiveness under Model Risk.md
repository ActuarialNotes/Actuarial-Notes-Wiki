A paper by Maciej Augustyniak and Mathieu Boudreault in the North American Actuarial Journal (volume 21, issue 4, 2017, pages 502-525). It studies the effectiveness of dynamic delta-rho hedging strategies for mitigating interest rate risk in variable annuities with either a guaranteed minimum death benefit or a guaranteed minimum withdrawal benefit rider, under model risk. The SOA supplies it as study note INV201-108-25.
