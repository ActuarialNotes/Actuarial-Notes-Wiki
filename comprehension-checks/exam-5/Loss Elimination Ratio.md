On a book of 1,000 claims, 400 claims fall entirely below \$500 and together cost \$100,000; the other 600 exceed \$500. Total losses are \$1,500,000. What share of losses does a \$500 deductible remove?

- A) 40% — four claims in ten never reach the insurer at all
- B) 26.7% — the 400 small claims plus \$500 from each of the 600 larger ones
- C) 6.7% — only the \$100,000 of claims that disappear entirely is eliminated
- D) 73.3% — the deductible leaves the insurer paying only the excess above \$500
