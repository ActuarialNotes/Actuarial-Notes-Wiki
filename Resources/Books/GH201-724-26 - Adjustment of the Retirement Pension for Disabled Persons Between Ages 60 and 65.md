## Adjustment of the retirement pension for disabled persons between ages 60 and 65
- For persons deemed disabled by Retraite Québec’s medical advisors between the ages 60 and 65, the retirement pension is reduced by 0.3% to 0.4% for each month in which they received a retirement pension before age 65, or a disability pension between ages 60 and 65 before January 2024
- When those persons turn 65 their retirement pension is no longer reduced and they receive 100% of the pension
- Usually the retirement pension is reduced by 0.5% to 0.6% for each month a person who is not deemed disabled receives it before age 65, because the normal retirement age under the Québec Pension Plan is 65
- Two examples follow: one person receiving a disability pension since age 58 whose pension is partly replaced by the retirement pension in January 2024, and one receiving it since age 50 whose pension is partly replaced when she turns 60 in March 2024, with a reduction of 0.35% for each of 60 months, 21% in total, cancelled at 65
