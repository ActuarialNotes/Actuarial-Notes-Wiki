Which claim against an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured is **not** removed by the Section II business exclusion?

- A) A child injured at the paid day care the insured runs at home for four families, earning \$18,000 a year
- B) A client injured at the insured's home office, where she runs her full-time tax practice
- C) A customer hit by a stone from the mower of the insured's son, 16, who mows lawns alone for pay
- D) A customer injured at a craft fair booth the insured runs every weekend, earning \$9,000 a year

## Explanation

The business exclusion does not apply to an insured **under 21 in a part-time or occasional, self-employed business with no employees**. The son's lawn mowing fits that exception. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — paid home day care is a business when an insured receives more than \$2,000 in the 12 months before the policy period; only unpaid exchanges and care for a relative are exceptions.
- **B** — a trade, profession or occupation is a business whether full- or part-time.
- **D** — an activity for money earning more than \$2,000 is a business, however small.
