Over one year, a regional insurer's management (1) studies new competitors entering its market and its own claims capabilities; (2) decides to concentrate on small commercial accounts and compete on service; (3) reorganizes its underwriting teams around that segment; (4) reviews the segment's growth and loss ratio against its targets at year end. Which steps of the strategic management process are these, in order?

- A) Analysis, formulation, implementation, evaluation
- B) Formulation, analysis, evaluation, implementation
- C) Analysis, implementation, formulation, evaluation
- D) Evaluation, formulation, implementation, analysis

## Explanation

Studying competitors and the insurer's own capabilities is analysis of the external and internal environment. Choosing a segment and how to compete in it is formulation. Building the organization around the choice is implementation. Comparing results with targets is evaluation, which feeds the next round of analysis. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Puts formulation before the analysis it relies on.
- **C** — Reorganizing the underwriting teams carries out a strategy, so it can't come before the strategy is chosen.
- **D** — Treats the year-end review as the start, and the opening study of competitors as the end.
