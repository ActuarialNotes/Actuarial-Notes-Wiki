Vehicle age and vehicle value correlate at $-0.94$ in a severity model. Which of the following is NOT a consequence?

- A) Predictions within the observed range become biased
- B) The two coefficients become unstable across samples
- C) Standard errors on both coefficients inflate
- D) A coefficient can flip sign when the other term is added

<!-- rationale: 1–3: all genuine symptoms; the trap is assuming that unstable coefficients must mean unreliable fitted values, when prediction inside the data is unaffected -->
