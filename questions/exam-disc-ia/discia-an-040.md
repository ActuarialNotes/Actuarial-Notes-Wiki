A building worth \$800,000 at the time of loss is insured under the ISO BPP for \$480,000 with an 80% coinsurance clause and a \$2,500 deductible. A fire causes a \$120,000 loss. What does the insurer pay?

- A) \$69,500
- B) \$72,000
- C) \$87,500
- D) \$117,500

## Explanation

The BPP's four steps: the required insurance is value times the coinsurance percentage; the limit divided by that is the ratio; the ratio times the loss before the deductible; then subtract the deductible.

$$
\begin{align*}
0.80 \times 800{,}000 &= 640{,}000 \\
R &= \frac{480{,}000}{640{,}000} \\
&= 0.75 \\
\text{Payment} &= 0.75 \times 120{,}000 - 2{,}500 \\
&= 87{,}500
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$69,500 divides the limit by the full value, $\tfrac{480}{800} = 0.60$, ignoring the 80% percentage.
- **B** — \$72,000 makes the same mistake and also forgets the deductible.
- **D** — \$117,500 applies no penalty, although the limit is below the requirement.
