A March 2024 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, sponsored by the Health Section Council, on prescription drug affordability. It discusses an alternative to policy solutions: reimagining commercial pharmacy financing with a value-based reimbursement methodology that increases transparency, encourages competition, aligns stakeholder incentives and mitigates total cost of care (TCOC) increases.
