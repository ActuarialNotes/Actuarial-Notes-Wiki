## [F-003] Dependence argued from Y being a function of X
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T02:59Z/919b
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: explanation, dependence step
- claim: Y=X^2 is a function of X, so X and Y are clearly dependent (knowing X determines Y).
- evidence: Being a function of X does not by itself imply dependence (a constant function of X is independent of X). The SOA solution (p.73) shows dependence directly: P(X=0, Y=0) = P(X=0) = 1/3, while P(X=0)P(Y=0) = (1/3)(1/3) = 1/9. Replaced the step with that check.
