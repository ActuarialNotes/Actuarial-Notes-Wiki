## [F-003] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space ('14, 000')
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 43-46, 50, 53-55, 59, 61
- claim: The math spans write 14,000, 157,337, 330,527, 49,142, 49,202 and 206,540 with a bare comma.
- evidence: In TeX/KaTeX math a bare ',' is a punctuation atom followed by a thin space, so e.g. $14,000$ typesets as '14, 000'; the rest of the FM bank writes {,}. No digit is affected.

- proposed_action: Write the separators as {,} inside math.
