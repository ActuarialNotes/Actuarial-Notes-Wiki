A manufacturer's CGL runs from July 1, 2026 to July 1, 2027. On May 15, 2027 it buys all the shares of a small corporation that has no liability insurance. Until when does the corporation automatically qualify as a named insured under paragraph 3 of Who Is an Insured, if the policy is not renewed or endorsed?

- A) July 1, 2027
- B) August 13, 2027
- C) May 15, 2028
- D) It never qualifies, because it was acquired after the policy began

## Explanation

A newly acquired organization (other than a partnership, joint venture or LLC) in which the named insured has ownership or majority interest qualifies as a named insured, if no similar insurance is available to it, until the 90th day after acquisition **or the end of the policy period, whichever is earlier**.

$$
\begin{align*}
t_{\text{end}} &= \min(\text{May } 15 + 90 \text{ days},\ \text{Jul } 1, 2027) \\
&= \min(\text{Aug } 13, 2027,\ \text{Jul } 1, 2027) \\
&= \text{Jul } 1, 2027
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** applies the 90 days without the "whichever is earlier" cap.
- **C** treats the acquisition as starting a new annual period.
- **D** — paragraph 3 exists precisely for organizations acquired or formed during the policy period.
