A regional insurer's risk documents contain the four statements below. Which one is a **risk tolerance** rather than a statement of risk appetite?

- A) We accept moderate underwriting risk in order to grow our commercial lines
- B) We will not take on risks that could threaten our financial strength rating
- C) We pursue investment returns consistent with a conservative risk profile
- D) Average claim settlement time may vary from 30 days by no more than 5 days

## Explanation

COSO defines **risk appetite** as the amount of risk, on a broad level, an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of value. **Risk tolerance** is the acceptable level of variation relative to a specific objective, usually measured in the same units as the objective. A permitted range around a 30-day settlement target is tactical and measurable, so it is a tolerance. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A broad statement of how much underwriting risk the insurer accepts to grow is risk appetite.
- **B** — A limit on risks that could threaten the rating is a broad appetite statement, not a variation around one objective.
- **C** — A conservative investment profile states appetite in broad terms, with no measurable variation set.
