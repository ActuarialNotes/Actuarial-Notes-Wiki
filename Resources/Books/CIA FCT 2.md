The CIA's February 2025 year-specific guidance on **capital and [[FCT|financial condition testing]] reporting**. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objectives C4 and C5.

## What to take from the assigned sections

- **§2.2 — P&C regulatory capital for 2025.** The [[MCT|MCT]] changes in force for the year, and the [[FCT]]/[[ORSA]] consequences of them.
- **§3.2 and §4.3 — recent events.** The year's inflation, interest rate, catastrophe and reinsurance-market conditions, and what they imply for scenario selection. This is what makes a scenario *plausible* in a given year.
- **§3.3 — standardized stress tests and the OSFI/AMF FCT templates.** The regulators prescribe scenarios so that results are comparable across insurers; the actuary's own scenarios sit alongside, not instead.
- **Climate-related risk and technology/cyber risk** as FCT scenarios — the two risk categories the guidance has been extending into, connecting to [[OSFI Climate]] and [[IAA Climate]].
- **§4.2 — economic capital modelling** in the ORSA, and how the internal target relates to the [[Internal Target Capital Ratio|internal target]] under [[OSFI Target Capital|Guideline A-4]].
