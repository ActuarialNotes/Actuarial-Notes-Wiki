An insurer has \$900,000 of ultimate losses on \$1,250,000 of earned premium, against a permissible loss ratio of 60%. What does this say about rate adequacy?

- A) Rates are inadequate — the indicated increase is $\frac{0.72}{0.60} - 1 = +20\%$
- B) Rates are adequate — 72% is well below 100%, so premium covers losses
- C) Rates are inadequate — the indicated increase is $(0.72 - 0.60) = +12\%$
- D) Rates are inadequate, but the size can't be determined without the expense ratio
