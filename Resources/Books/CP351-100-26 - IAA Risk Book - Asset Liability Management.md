A chapter of the IAA Risk Book, developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association, that gives practical insights into asset liability management (ALM) techniques and practices for insurance companies. This is version 2.0, approved on 29 July 2025; version 1.0 was approved on 24 October 2016. It covers the definition and objectives of ALM, its scope of risks, governance and framework, the measurement of risk exposures and the execution of ALM strategies.
