A time series, $\{x_t\}$ is modeled as an AR(3) process given by:

$$
x_t = 4 + 0.45x_{t-1} + 0.25x_{t-2} + 0.05x_{t-3} + w_t
$$

where $w_t$ is white noise, with:

- $E(w_t) = 0$
- $\operatorname{Var}(w_t) = \sigma^2$

The most recent three observed values in this series are:

| t | $x_t$ |
|:---:|:---:|
| 2016 | 30 |
| 2017 | 23 |
| 2018 | 21 |

Calculate the ten-step-ahead forecast value, $x_{2028}$.

- A) Less than 12
- B) At least 12, but less than 16
- C) At least 16, but less than 20
- D) At least 20, but less than 24
- E) At least 24

## Explanation

Forecast an AR process by recursion: each future white-noise term is replaced by its mean, 0, and each future $x$ on the right-hand side by its own forecast. With 2016, 2017 and 2018 observed, the first step is

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{x}_{2019} &= 4 + 0.45(21) + 0.25(23) + 0.05(30) \\
&= 4 + 9.45 + 5.75 + 1.50 \\
&= 20.70
\end{align*}
$$

and the second uses that forecast in place of $x_{2019}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{x}_{2020} &= 4 + 0.45(20.70) + 0.25(21) + 0.05(23) \\
&= 4 + 9.315 + 5.25 + 1.15 \\
&= 19.715
\end{align*}
$$

Continuing the same way out to ten steps:

| Year | Forecast |
|:---:|:---:|
| 2019 | 20.700 |
| 2020 | 19.715 |
| 2021 | 19.097 |
| 2022 | 18.557 |
| 2023 | 18.111 |
| 2024 | 17.744 |
| 2025 | 17.440 |
| 2026 | 17.190 |
| 2027 | 16.983 |
| 2028 | 16.812 |

As a check, the coefficients sum to $0.45 + 0.25 + 0.05 = 0.75 < 1$, and the process mean is $4/(1 - 0.75) = 16$: the forecasts decay steadily from the last observations toward 16, as a stationary AR forecast should, but after ten steps they have not yet reached it.

$\hat{x}_{2028} = 16.81$ falls in $[16, 20)$, so the answer is **C**.
