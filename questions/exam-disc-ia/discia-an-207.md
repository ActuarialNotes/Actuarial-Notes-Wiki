A producer recommends a flexible premium variable deferred annuity to a 40-year-old client. Which statement in the producer's sales presentation is accurate?

- A) Because the annuity grows tax-deferred, the client should buy it inside her IRA to gain an extra tax advantage
- B) The producer must be licensed by the state insurance department and also registered with FINRA to sell it
- C) The account value is guaranteed against loss because annuities are protected by the FDIC
- D) Withdrawals of earnings before age 59 and a half are tax-free, since the money was invested after tax

## Explanation

Every annuity is regulated by the state insurance department, and the person who sells one must hold a state license. A variable annuity is also a security, so its seller must in addition be registered with FINRA as a representative of a member broker-dealer, and the buyer receives a prospectus.

- **A** is wrong because an annuity inside an IRA or 401(k) adds no further tax advantage: the plan already defers the tax.
- **C** is wrong twice: annuities are not guaranteed by the FDIC or any federal agency, and a variable annuity's subaccounts carry no guaranteed return.
- **D** is wrong because earnings are taxed as ordinary income when withdrawn, and a withdrawal before age 59 and a half may also incur a 10% tax penalty.
