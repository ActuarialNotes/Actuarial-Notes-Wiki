A March 2022 Society of Actuaries Research Institute paper on policyholder dividends on traditional participating life insurance in regulatory contexts similar to those in the United States and Canada, with a brief touch on annuity products. It was prepared for the SOA's educational curriculum, and its sponsor is the Individual Life and Annuities Track Curriculum Committee.
