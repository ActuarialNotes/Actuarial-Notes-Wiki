A risk control representative surveys a restaurant that has applied for coverage, finds that the kitchen hood lacks a fire suppression system, and recommends one be installed as a condition of coverage. The underwriter then accepts the account. Which describes how the insurer's functions cooperated?

- A) Risk control supplied information that let underwriting accept the account on modified terms
- B) Premium auditing verified the restaurant's actual exposures once the policy period had ended
- C) Claims handling used the restaurant's loss data to set the premium it would be charged
- D) Marketing and distribution set the rate the restaurant was charged for its coverage

## Explanation

Risk control gives underwriters the survey information they need to accept, modify or decline an account, and its recommendations reduce expected losses. Here the recommendation turned a possible decline into an acceptance with a condition. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Premium auditing verifies exposures after the policy period on auditable lines; the account has not yet been written.
- **C** — Claims data helps risk control see where losses occur, but no claims function was involved in this decision.
- **D** — Marketing and distribution bring in the business; rates are set by actuarial and underwriting, and the decision here was about acceptability.
