An essay on why statisticians and infographics designers value different things in a graphic: attention and novelty on one side, accurate comparison and model checking on the other. It closes with the advice to use two graphs rather than cram both jobs into one. It is a Domain A reading for [[Exam PCPA (CAS)|PCPA]] (see [[Data Visualization]], [[Exploratory Data Analysis]]). The CAS content outline gives the subtitle as "Different Goals, Different Views"; the paper itself reads "Different Goals, Different Looks." The version linked here is the author's manuscript dated 20 January 2012.
