# Resolve: Exam P open findings (312 files, 407 findings closed)

The follow-up to run `2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2`, which fact-checked all 811 Exam P files and left
373 findings open on 312 of them. This run fixed the content behind every one of those findings,
closed each with a logged resolution, and re-verified each file with a new pass bound to its new
bytes. Nothing in the Exam P scope is left with an open finding.

The fixes follow one written policy, applied the same way in every batch:

- **SOA questions** carry SOA's stem and options word for word, transcribed from the Aug 2026 PDF
  (page images for anything in math), with givens laid out so they render as separate lines. A
  question whose vault version was not SOA's question was re-transcribed, re-keyed to SOA's answer,
  and its explanation rewritten to follow SOA's official solution. Every answer was recomputed
  before it was recorded.
- **Distractor notes, side remarks and explanation steps** were corrected only where the right
  statement was checked arithmetically, and deleted otherwise.
- **Original questions** with inconsistent givens or no correct option got the smallest change
  that makes them well-posed; four-option questions got an option E built from a named error.
- **Concept pages** were corrected to what a cited source says; a claim with no source read in
  either run was deleted rather than kept.
- **Off-syllabus material** (continuous joint densities, probabilities for sums of non-normal
  continuous variables) is kept but flagged `off_syllabus: true`, per the Nov 2026 syllabus:
  outcomes 3a–e are discrete-only, 3g covers discrete or normal only.
