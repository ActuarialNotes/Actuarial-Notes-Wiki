A paper in the Proceedings of the Casualty Actuarial Society, Volume LXXXV (1998). Two well-known methods for calculating risk load, Marginal Surplus and Marginal Variance, are applied to output from catastrophe modeling software, with risk loads calculated for sample new and renewal accounts. A new concept, "renewal additivity", is introduced, which neither marginal method satisfies, together with a new method inspired by game theory that splits the mutual covariance between any two accounts evenly between them and is extended to a proportional sharing.
