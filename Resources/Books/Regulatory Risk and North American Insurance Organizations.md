![[Regulatory Risk and North American Insurance Organizations - Cover.svg]]

A research report, dated August 2014, on regulatory risk in the North American insurance company environment. It aims to identify regulatory structures and influences in North America, provide examples that illustrate regulatory risk, and suggest mitigation ideas for both insurers and their regulators. It was sponsored by the Casualty Actuarial Society, the Canadian Institute of Actuaries and the Society of Actuaries.
