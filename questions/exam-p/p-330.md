The sales for a product can be modeled by $Z = 4X - Y - 3$. $X$ and $Y$ are independent random variables with $\text{Var}(X)=2$ and $\text{Var}(Y)=3$.

Calculate $\text{Var}(Z)$.

- A) 5
- B) 11
- C) 29
- D) 32
- E) 35

## Explanation

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(Z) &= 16\,\text{Var}(X)+\text{Var}(Y) \\
              &= 16(2)+3 \\
              &= 32+3 \\
              &= \mathbf{35}
\end{align*}
$$
