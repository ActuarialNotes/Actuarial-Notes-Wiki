A March 2017 research report published by the Society of Actuaries, prepared by RGA Reinsurance Company for the Reinsurance Section, Product Development Section and Committee on Life Insurance Research. It extends the May 2016 conversion experience study by using predictive analytics on term conversion experience, and develops three models: a conversion rate model, a point-in-scale mortality model and a post-conversion lapse model.
