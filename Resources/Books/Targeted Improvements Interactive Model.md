![[Targeted Improvements Interactive Model - Cover.svg]]

An Excel workbook with models of deferred acquisition cost (DAC) and reserve calculations following FASB's Accounting Standards Update No. 2018-12 on targeted improvements to long-duration contract accounting, which the SOA syllabus lists as a reading. It replicates the examples in the ASU, adds a 20-year-term example for each, and sets questions with an answer key.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam ILA-201U (SOA)|Exam ILA-201U]] — Outcomes 1b; the interactive model, with no sheets or questions singled out (as the syllabus states it)

## Instructions
- Overview of the spreadsheet, expectations for candidates, the spreadsheet assignments (questions on the DAC example and on the reserve example) and tab descriptions.

## FASB DAC Guidance
- Key paragraphs from the ASU relevant to DAC.

## DAC Example
- DAC calculation under the group method that replicates the example provided in the ASU.

## 20 YT DAC
- An additional DAC example that lets users test persistency scenarios and toggle the valuation year.

## FASB Reserve Guidance
- Key paragraphs from the ASU on the calculation of the reserve for traditional non-participating long-duration insurance contracts.

## Reserve Example
- A reserve calculation example that replicates the example provided in the ASU.

## 20 YT Reserve
- An additional reserve example that lets users test mortality and lapse scenarios and toggle the valuation year.

## Answer Key
- Answers to the questions asked in the Instructions tab.
