(1 point)

Given the following:

| | |
|---|---|
| General Expenses | $225,000 |
| Written Premium | $3,750,000 |
| Earned Premium | $3,000,000 |
| Other Acquisition Expense | 8.0% |
| Commission | 12.0% |
| Taxes, Licenses & Fees | 3.0% |

| | |
|---|---|
| Projected Ultimate Loss and LAE Ratio | 62.0% |
| Target Underwriting Profit Ratio | 5.0% |

- All expenses are paid at policy inception.
- Commission and Taxes, Licenses & Fees are 100% variable.
- All other expense categories are 50% variable.

Calculate the indicated rate change assuming the data is fully credible.

### Explanation
The general expense ratio is taken against **written** premium, because all expenses are paid at policy inception; the other ratios are given.

| Category | Total % | Fixed % | Variable % |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Expenses | $225{,}000 / 3{,}750{,}000 = 6\%$ | 3% | 3% |
| Other Acquisition Expense | 8% | 4% | 4% |
| Commission & Brokerage | 12% | | 12% |
| Taxes, Licenses & Fees | 3% | | 3% |
| **Total** | | **7%** | **22%** |

General expense and other acquisition are 50% variable, so each splits evenly; commission and taxes are 100% variable.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Indicated rate change} &= \frac{0.62 + 0.07}{1 - 0.22 - 0.05} - 1 \\
                             &= \frac{0.69}{0.73} - 1 \\
                             &= -5.48\%
\end{align*}
$$
