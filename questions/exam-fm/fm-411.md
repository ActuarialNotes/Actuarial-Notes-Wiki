Jeremy purchases a perpetuity providing a payment of 1 at the end of each year. The perpetuity's modified duration is 15 years.

Calculate the Macaulay duration of this perpetuity.

- A) 14.00
- B) 14.46
- C) 15.07
- D) 15.94
- E) 16.00

## Explanation

The price of the perpetuity at annual effective rate $i$ is $P(i) = 1/i$, so $P'(i) = -1/i^{2}$ and the modified duration is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD} &= -\frac{P'(i)}{P(i)} \\
        &= \frac{1/i^{2}}{1/i} \\
        &= \frac{1}{i}
\end{align*}
$$

Setting $D_{MOD} = 1/i = 15$ gives $i = 1/15$. The Macaulay duration is the modified duration times $1+i$:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC} &= D_{MOD}(1+i) \\
        &= 15(1 + 1/15) \\
        &= 16
\end{align*}
$$
