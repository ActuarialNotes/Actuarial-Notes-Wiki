A comprehensive treatment of all aspects of group insurance in the United States and Canada, in its eighth edition, edited by Daniel D. Skwire with William F. Bluhm as founding editor. It addresses life and health insurance as well as government programs and more specialized forms of insurance, with emphasis on the actuarial aspects of the field, including pricing, regulation, underwriting, financial reporting and modeling; the eighth edition adds chapters on Group Insurance Reserves and Estimating Pharmacy Claim Costs and a greatly expanded treatment of the Affordable Care Act.
