SOA study note RET101-100-25, titled Framework for Future Defined Contribution Design, supplied for the SOA's RET 101 Retirement Plan Design course. The SOA course strategy guide says it is a new reading written specifically for RET 101, to help the candidate understand how the changes to DC design of the last 20 or so years fit into a broader effort to improve retirement outcomes for DC participants, and that it highlights remaining gaps and gives thoughts on how they might be closed.
