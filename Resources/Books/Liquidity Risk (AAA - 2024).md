A public policy practice note, dated February 2024, developed by the Liquidity Work Group of the American Academy of Actuaries' ERM/ORSA Committee. It updates the Academy's 2000 practice note on liquidity risk management and extends it from life insurers to health and property/casualty insurers through practice-area appendices. It discusses liquidity demands and sources, liquidity risk analysis and liquidity risk management, with a primary focus on events that cause significant unexpected liquidity stress. It is not an actuarial standard of practice.
