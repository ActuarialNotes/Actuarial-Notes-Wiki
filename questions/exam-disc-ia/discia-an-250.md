To win a sale, a life insurance agent offers to pay the client's first month's premium out of her own commission. Nothing in the policy mentions this. Under a state law based on the NAIC Unfair Trade Practices Act, the offer is most likely

- A) misrepresentation of the policy's terms
- B) defamation of a competing insurer
- C) boycott, coercion and intimidation
- D) a prohibited rebate

## Explanation

The Act prohibits offering, as an inducement to buy a policy, any **rebate** of premium or any valuable consideration not specified in the policy, except as state law expressly allows. Paying part of the premium for the client is such an inducement.

- **A** requires a false statement about the policy's benefits, terms or conditions, which the agent did not make.
- **B** requires a false or maliciously critical statement about an insurer's financial condition.
- **C** concerns concerted action restraining competition in the business of insurance.
