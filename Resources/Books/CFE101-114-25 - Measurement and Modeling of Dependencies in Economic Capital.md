A discussion paper presented to the Institute of Actuaries on 10 May 2010. It covers the measurement and modelling of dependency within economic capital models, from the variance-covariance matrix approach to copulas and causal models with feedback loops, together with data and model uncertainties and ways to communicate the impact of dependency to senior management. It is concerned with the aggregation part of an economic capital model, not the marginal risk distributions.
