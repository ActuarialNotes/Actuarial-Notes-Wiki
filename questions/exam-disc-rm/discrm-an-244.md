Which order of claims handling activities is correct?

- A) Acknowledge and assign; identify the policy; contact the insured; investigate and document; determine cause, liability and amount; conclude
- B) Acknowledge and assign; investigate and document; determine cause, liability and amount; identify the policy; contact the insured; conclude
- C) Contact the insured; determine cause, liability and amount; acknowledge and assign; identify the policy; investigate and document; conclude
- D) Identify the policy; conclude; acknowledge and assign; contact the insured; investigate and document; determine cause, liability and amount

## Explanation

The claim is acknowledged and assigned, the policy identified, the insured contacted, the loss investigated and documented, the cause of loss, liability and amount determined, and the claim concluded by payment or denial. Each activity answers a question the next one depends on. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Determines the amount before confirming that a policy was in force, which risks paying a claim the policy doesn't cover.
- **C** — Settles cause and amount before the claim is even assigned or investigated.
- **D** — Concludes the claim before anything is investigated.
