An August 2022 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, sponsored by the SOA's Individual Life and Annuity Curriculum Committee, on registered index-linked annuities (RILAs), a type of deferred annuity first introduced in 2010. It sets RILAs against fixed, fixed indexed and variable annuities, and covers rate setting and hedging, product mechanics, risk management, and reserves and regulation.
