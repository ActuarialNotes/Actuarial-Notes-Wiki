A McKinsey article of February 2018 on organizational design that is stable yet can respond quickly to threats and opportunities. It is one of a series based on Scott Keller and Mary Meaney's 2017 book Leading Organizations: Ten Timeless Truths, in which reorganizing to capture value quickly is one of ten basic issues facing leaders.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CFE-201 (SOA)|Exam CFE-201]] — outcomes 3c.

## Why is organizational redesign important?
- It is inevitable
- Redesigns can have a profound impact
- Only 23 percent get it right

## What are the big ideas?
- Think beyond lines and boxes
- Agility requires stability
- Nine golden rules

## Making it happen
- 1. Aspire
- 2. Assess
- 3. Architect
- 4. Act
- 5. Advance
