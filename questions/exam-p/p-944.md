Four players enter a single-elimination tournament: three from Team A and one from Team B. When two Team A players meet, each is equally likely to win. When the Team B player meets a Team A player, the Team B player wins with probability 1/3. The bracket is chosen randomly, and match results are independent.

Calculate the probability that a player from Team A wins the tournament.

- A) $2/3$
- B) $3/4$
- C) $8/9$
- D) $5/9$
- E) $7/9$

## Explanation

Whatever the bracket, the Team B player meets a Team A player in the semifinal, and the other semifinal is between two Team A players, so if the Team B player wins the semifinal they meet a Team A player again in the final.

The Team B player wins the tournament only by winning both matches. Match results are independent, so

$$
\begin{align*}
P(\text{B wins tournament}) &= P(\text{B wins semifinal}) \times P(\text{B wins final}) \\
                            &= \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} \\
                            &= \frac{1}{9}
\end{align*}
$$

Otherwise a Team A player wins:

$$P(\text{A wins}) = 1 - \frac{1}{9} = \mathbf{\frac{8}{9}}$$
