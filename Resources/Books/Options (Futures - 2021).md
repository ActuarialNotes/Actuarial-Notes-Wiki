A textbook on options markets, futures markets and the full range of derivative securities, in its 11th edition. Pearson describes it as bridging theory and practice across futures and forwards, hedging strategies, swaps, options trading strategies, binomial trees, Black-Scholes-Merton pricing, the Greek letters, volatility smiles, value at risk, exotic options and credit derivatives, covering the latest regulations and trends from overnight indexed swaps to the valuation of commodity derivatives.
