A 20-year non-callable bond that pays coupons annually has a face amount of 2000. The bond was bought at a price of 2300 and has an annual effective yield rate of 7%.

A 20-year callable bond with the same annual coupon rate and face amount is callable for 2000 at the end of the 18th or 19th year.

Calculate the maximum price of the callable bond that guarantees an annual effective yield of at least 7%.

- A) 2268
- B) 2276
- C) 2285
- D) 2293
- E) 2300

## Explanation

Let $r$ be the annual coupon rate. The non-callable bond's price at its 7% yield gives the annual coupon $2000r$:

$$
\begin{align*}
2300 &= 2000r\,a_{\overline{20}|0.07} + 2000v^{20} \\
     &= 2000r(10.59401) + 516.838 \\
2000r &= \frac{2300 - 516.838}{10.59401} \\
      &= 168.318
\end{align*}
$$

The bond is bought at a premium (its coupon rate, $\dfrac{168.318}{2000} = 8.42\%$, exceeds the 7% yield), so the call that is worst for the investor is the earliest possible one, at the end of year 18. The maximum price that guarantees a 7% yield is the price at 7% assuming the bond is called then:

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 168.318\,a_{\overline{18}|0.07} + 2000v^{18} \\
  &= 168.318(10.059087) + 591.728 \\
  &= 1693.125 + 591.728 \\
  &= 2284.85
\end{align*}
$$
