Under which distribution system does the producer, rather than the insurer, usually own the expirations, so that the producer can move an account to another insurer at renewal?

- A) The direct writer system
- B) The exclusive agency system
- C) The direct response system
- D) The independent agency and brokerage system

## Explanation

Independent agents and brokers represent several unrelated insurers and **own the expirations**, the records of their customers and the right to solicit them at renewal, so they can move an account to another insurer. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A direct writer sells through its own employees, and the insurer owns the expirations.
- **B** — Exclusive agents represent one insurer or group and typically have limited or no ownership of expirations.
- **C** — Direct response uses no producer; the insurer keeps the customer relationship entirely.
