A twelve-year 2500 face amount bond has an annual coupon rate of 4.84% paid quarterly and is redeemable for $C$. The book values of the bond following the 4th and 19th coupons are 2092.88 and 2316.30, respectively.

Calculate the amount for accumulation of discount in the last coupon.

- A) 7.77
- B) 13.10
- C) 22.97
- D) 30.91
- E) 31.55

## Explanation

Each quarterly coupon is $2500(0.0484)/4 = 30.25$. Fifteen coupons are paid between the 4th and the 19th, so the book value after the 4th coupon is the present value, at the quarterly yield rate $j$, of those 15 coupons and of the book value after the 19th coupon:

$$2092.88 = 30.25\,a_{\overline{15}|j} + 2316.30\,v^{15}$$

Solving with a financial calculator ($N = 15$, $PV = 2092.88$, $PMT = -30.25$, $FV = -2316.30$) gives $j = 2.06\%$, the quarterly yield rate.

The amount for accumulation of discount in a coupon is the interest earned on the preceding book value minus the coupon. Let $A_t$ be that amount in the $t$th coupon. For the 20th coupon,

$$
\begin{align*}
A_{20} &= 2316.30(0.0206) - 30.25 \\
       &= 47.71578 - 30.25 \\
       &= 17.46578
\end{align*}
$$

These amounts grow by the factor $1+j$ from one coupon to the next. A twelve-year bond with quarterly coupons has 48 coupons, so the last coupon comes 28 coupons after the 20th:

$$
\begin{align*}
A_{48} &= 17.46578(1.0206)^{28} \\
       &= 17.46578(1.769929) \\
       &= 30.91
\end{align*}
$$
