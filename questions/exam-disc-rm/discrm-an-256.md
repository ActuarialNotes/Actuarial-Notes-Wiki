A mutual insurer's board approves three statements: (1) "To be the insurer every small-town family trusts first"; (2) "We protect the homes, farms and cars of rural families through local agents who live where they do"; (3) "Raise our policy retention rate from 84% to 88% by the end of next year." Which classification is correct?

- A) (1) mission, (2) vision, (3) goal
- B) (1) goal, (2) mission, (3) vision
- C) (1) vision, (2) mission, (3) goal
- D) (1) vision, (2) goal, (3) mission

## Explanation

- **(1) Vision:** what the insurer hopes to become in the future.
- **(2) Mission:** why it exists and whom it serves, in the present.
- **(3) Goal:** a narrower aim serving the vision and mission, here specific, measurable and time-bound.

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Swaps vision and mission: the forward-looking aspiration is the vision.
- **B** — A target with a number and a date is a goal, not a vision.
- **D** — The description of whom the insurer serves and how is its mission, not a goal.
