Two insurers each report a 28% underwriting expense ratio. Insurer A writes through independent agents; Insurer B writes direct with heavy advertising. What does the equal ratio tell you about how each should load its rates?

- A) Nothing further — an equal expense ratio means both load rates identically
- B) Little, until each ratio is split into fixed and variable components, which enter the rate formula in different places
- C) Insurer B's rates must be higher, since advertising is a genuine cost while commission is a pass-through
- D) Both should load 28% on top of the pure premium to recover expenses
