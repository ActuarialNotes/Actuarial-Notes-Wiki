Credit Risk, the twelfth chapter of Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management by Mary R. Hardy and David Saunders, studies mathematical models for credit risk. It begins with an overview of credit-sensitive instruments and the types of credit risk faced by financial market participants, then covers the three key components of mathematical credit risk models: probability of default, loss given default and exposure at default. The SOA supplies the chapter as study note INV101-107-26.
