An IAA Risk Book chapter (March 2023, version 2.0), developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association. It covers basic information on general insurance liabilities that stakeholders may need to know when reviewing the financial accounts of a general insurer, written for a reader somewhat familiar with life insurance and annuities but unfamiliar with general insurance.
