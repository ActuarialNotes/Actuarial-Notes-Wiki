Chapter 12 of Reimagining Pensions: The Next 40 Years (Oxford University Press, 2016), edited by Olivia S. Mitchell and Richard C. Shea for the Pension Research Council. It explores defined ambition (DA) schemes that provide variable annuities to participants, based on Dutch occupational pension schemes in which participants, rather than an external sponsor, bear the balance sheet risks of pension funds. Its analysis addresses the key issues of a move from the defined benefit design towards DA schemes.

## Dutch Occupational Pensions
- Three-pillar system
- Occupational pensions
- Benefit entitlements in terms of annuities
- Payout conditional on performance
- Nominal liabilities computed with market interest rates

## Strengths of Dutch Occupational Plans
- Advanced risk management and protection against behavioral biases
- Protection against agency issues
- Pooling of idiosyncratic longevity risk and completion of financial markets

## Weaknesses of Dutch Occupational Plans
- Ambiguous risk profiles and liabilities for communication and investment
    - Lack of risk-bearing capital
    - Substantial mismatch risk due to macroeconomic shocks
    - Insufficient scope for tailor-made risk profiles gives rise to intergenerational conflict
    - Nominal cuts of pensions in payment due to materialized interest-rate risk
    - Inadequate communication about risk profile
    - Pension funds aren’t ‘walking the talk’: incomplete investment policy
- Lack of economic valuation and fair pricing: ambiguous property rights
    - Lack of economic valuation
    - Incomplete pension fund policies
    - Incomplete government policies
    - Intergenerational conflict about investment policy due to asymmetric contract
    - Non-transparent redistribution when selling and buying annuity units
    - Difficulties in introducing elements of individual choice

## Characteristics of DA Plans
- Employer as distribution platform for annuities rather than as risk sponsor
    - Pension entitlement as (deferred) annuity
    - Risk sharing with complete contract in mutual insurer yields variable annuities
    - Innovation in occupational pension schemes
    - Specific forms of risk sharing contracts
- Communication and risk management on basis of consumption frame
- Economic valuation

## Current Status of Pension Reform in the Netherlands
- Government’s response
- Companies are considering individual defined contribution

## Lessons of DA for DC Schemes in the United States
- ‘Consumption frame’ during the accumulation phase
- Dealing with longevity risk
- Variable annuities: smoothing investment risk
- Role of the employer to deal with behavioral and market imperfections
