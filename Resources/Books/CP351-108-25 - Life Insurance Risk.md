The second of two Milliman reports, dated July 2024, on how life insurers are responding to the "Age of Uncertainty", with a focus on the UK, European, U.S. and Bermudian markets. Paper 2 examines how insurers' capital, asset-liability management (ALM) and liquidity models and monitoring performed during the events of 2022-23, what the lessons for future improvements are, and the management implications for businesses looking forward.
