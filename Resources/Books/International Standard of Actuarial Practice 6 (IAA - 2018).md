A model standard of actuarial practice from the International Actuarial Association, adopted by the IAA Council on 1 December 2018. It provides guidance to actuaries performing actuarial services involving enterprise risk management (ERM) programs, including the ORSA, that are within the scope of regulations consistent with two of the IAIS Insurance Core Principles: Risk Management and Internal Controls (ICP 8) and Enterprise Risk Management for Solvency Purposes (ICP 16).
