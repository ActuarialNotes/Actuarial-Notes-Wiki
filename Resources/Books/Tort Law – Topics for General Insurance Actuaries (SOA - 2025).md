A Society of Actuaries study note, revised May 2025, on tort law for general insurance actuaries. Torts are civil wrongs, and tort law compensates a not-at-fault claimant and makes the at-fault defendant financially responsible; the note presents an overview of tort cost trends, tort reform, and mass torts and toxic torts.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GI-201 (SOA)|Exam GI-201]] — outcomes 3b-d and 3i; sections 1-5, excluding 5.1 and 5.5; of the table in 5.4 (pp. 35-37), candidates are only expected to be familiar with the cases BMW of North America v. Gore and State Farm Mutual Auto. v. Campbell
