In an insurance arrangement, the members, called subscribers, insure one another. A corporation manages the arrangement for them in return for a percentage of premium, handling underwriting, claims and investments. What type of insurer is this?

- A) A reciprocal exchange
- B) A stock insurer
- C) A mutual insurer
- D) A Lloyd's syndicate

## Explanation

In a **reciprocal exchange**, subscribers insure one another, and an attorney-in-fact, often a corporation, manages the exchange for a fee, usually a percentage of premium. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — A stock insurer is a corporation owned by shareholders, who supply its capital and receive its profit.
- **C** — A mutual insurer is owned by its policyholders, but it is a corporation run by a board they elect, not an exchange among subscribers managed by an attorney-in-fact.
- **D** — A Lloyd's syndicate underwrites risks with capital supplied by its members, inside the Lloyd's marketplace, and is run by a managing agent.
