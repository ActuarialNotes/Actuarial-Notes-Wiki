An accident year carries \$5,000,000 paid, \$3,000,000 of case reserves, and \$4,000,000 of IBNR. What are the ultimate and the unpaid amounts?

- A) Ultimate \$12,000,000 and unpaid \$4,000,000
- B) Ultimate \$12,000,000 and unpaid \$7,000,000
- C) Ultimate \$7,000,000 and unpaid \$7,000,000
- D) Ultimate \$8,000,000 and unpaid \$3,000,000
