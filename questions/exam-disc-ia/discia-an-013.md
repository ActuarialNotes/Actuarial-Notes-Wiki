On December 20, 2025, an insurer receives full payment of \$6,000 for an annual policy that takes effect January 1, 2026. How does the payment appear in the insurer's December 31, 2025 statutory statements?

- A) As 2025 written premium, all of it placed in the unearned premium reserve
- B) As 2025 written premium, of which the 11 days since payment are earned
- C) As an advance premium liability, outside written premium and the reserve
- D) As other income, because the insurer has not yet provided any coverage

## Explanation

Under SSAP No. 53, written premium is recorded as of the policy's effective date. Premium paid before that date is an advance premium: a liability, not considered income until due, and not included in written premium or the unearned premium reserve. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — the premium will be written on January 1, 2026, not when it is received.
- **B** — no coverage has been provided, so nothing can be earned; earning follows the coverage period, not the payment date.
- **D** — other income is where SSAP No. 53 places flat installment service charges, not premium for coverage.
