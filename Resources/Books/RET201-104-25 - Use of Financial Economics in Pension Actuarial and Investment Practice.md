A Society of Actuaries study note (code RET201-104-25) for the RET 201 course, titled Use of Financial Economics in Pension Actuarial and Investment Practice. The SOA's course strategy guide says it is intended to supplement the Pension Actuary's Guide to Financial Economics by discussing how the concepts of financial economics have been applied (or not) in practice.
