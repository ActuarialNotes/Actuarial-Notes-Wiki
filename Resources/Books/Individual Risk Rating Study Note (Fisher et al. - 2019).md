A CAS study note, dated October 31, 2019, that introduces the concepts and methods used to support excess, deductible and individual risk pricing. Its authors consolidate insights from several foundational papers, give examples beyond U.S.-based workers' compensation practice and add a few fresh insights; it covers experience rating, loss sensitive rating plans, aggregate excess loss cost estimation and cautions on pricing excess and aggregate loss.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 8 (CAS)|Exam 8]] — objectives B1–B6; Version 3, October 2019.
