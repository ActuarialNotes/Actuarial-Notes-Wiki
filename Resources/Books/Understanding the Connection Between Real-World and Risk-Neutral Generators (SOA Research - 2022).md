A research report, dated August 2022, on the difference between real-world and risk-neutral scenario generators, which lies in their treatment of the market price of risk. It explains when each type is appropriate and introduces the term "market-coherent" for a real-world generator calibrated to reproduce market prices. An Excel workbook accompanies the paper and values a simple equity put option with real-world and risk-neutral methods side by side.
