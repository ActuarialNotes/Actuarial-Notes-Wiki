A CIA educational note that provides a framework for disclosing sources of earnings for group life and health benefits, so that approach and disclosure are reasonably consistent across the industry. Dated October 2010 (document 210069) and prepared by the Committee on the Appointed/Valuation Actuary for life insurance company actuaries, it supplements the draft educational note Sources of Earnings: Determination and Disclosure (August 2004), recommends a methodology for companies that use a simplifying approximation under which claim incidence is not projected in the valuation,. An appendix compares the income statement and sources of earnings presentations.
