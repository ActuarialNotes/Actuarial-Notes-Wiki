On Main Street, a driver’s speed just before an accident is uniformly distributed on $[5, 20]$. Given the speed, the resulting loss from the accident is exponentially distributed with mean equal to three times the speed.

Calculate the variance of a loss due to an accident on Main Street.

- A) 525
- B) 1463
- C) 1575
- D) 1632
- E) 1744

## Explanation

Let $V$ be the speed and $L$ the loss. $V$ is uniform on $[5,20]$ and, given $V$, $L$ is exponential with mean $3V$, so $E[L\mid V]=3V$ and $\text{Var}(L\mid V)=(3V)^2=9V^2$.

$E[V]=12.5$, $\text{Var}(V)=15^2/12=18.75$, $E[V^2]=18.75+156.25=175$.

$E[\text{Var}(L|V)]=E[9V^2]=9\times175=1575$.

$\text{Var}(E[L|V])=\text{Var}(3V)=9\times18.75=168.75$.

$\text{Var}(L)=E[\text{Var}(L|V)]+\text{Var}(E[L|V])=1575+168.75=1743.75\approx\mathbf{1744}$
