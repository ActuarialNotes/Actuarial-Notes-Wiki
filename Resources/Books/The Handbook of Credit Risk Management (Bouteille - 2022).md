![[The Handbook of Credit Risk Management (Bouteille - 2022) - Cover.svg]]

A roadmap to credit risk management (CRM) written for students and busy professionals, consistent with a commonly implemented risk management framework. Divided into four sections (Origination, Credit Assessment, Portfolio Management, and Mitigation and Transfer), it explains why CRM is critical to the success of large institutions and why organizational structure matters. The second edition updates its data, charts and content and adds three chapters, on consumer finance, state and local credit risk, and sovereign risk.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam INV-101 (SOA)|Exam INV-101]] — outcomes 3a and 3b: Ch. 1 Fundamentals of Credit Risk (background only); Ch. 4 Measurement of Credit Risk; Ch. 6 Fundamental Credit Analysis; Ch. 7 Alternative Estimations of Credit Quality; Ch. 13 Credit Portfolio Management
