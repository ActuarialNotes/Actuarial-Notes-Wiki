The Financial Accounting Standards Board's Accounting Standards Codification Topic 715, as supplied by the Society of Actuaries as study note RET201-111-25 for the RET 201 course. The SOA's course strategy guide describes it as the text of the standard for U.S. GAAP retirement plan accounting; the study note leaves out the Glossary sections (715-XX-20) and the Implementation Guidance and Illustrations sections (715-XX-55), which are background only readings.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 3a-3d; excluding all subsections ending in 00, 20, 60 and 65, and 20-S00, 20-S50, 20-S55, 20-S99, 30-55, 60-55, 70-55 and 80-55.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> No copy of this study note is published; the SOA supplies it with the course study notes, which the syllabus says are not available electronically but may be purchased through the SOA online store. This page records its citation, what the SOA says about it, and its assignment from the syllabus.
