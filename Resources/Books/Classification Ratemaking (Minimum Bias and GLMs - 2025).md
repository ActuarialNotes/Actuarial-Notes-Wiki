A study note of the Education Committee of the Society of Actuaries, dated May 2025. It combines three articles written in 2013 for the discontinued General Insurance Applications Module of the General Insurance track: a review of classification ratemaking, a summary of the use of one-way relativities and the minimum bias method, and a look at the relationship between the minimum bias method and generalized linear models (GLMs).
