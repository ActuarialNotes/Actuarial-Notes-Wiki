An employer provides disability benefits to its employees for work-related and other injuries. The random variables $X$ and $Y$ denote the employer's annual expenditures for work-related and other injuries, respectively. An actuarial study reveals the following information about $X$ and $Y$:

- (i) The density of $X$ is $f(x) = \frac{1}{20\sqrt{5}} e^{\frac{-x}{20\sqrt{5}}}$, for $x > 0$.
- (ii) $\text{Var}(Y) = 12{,}500$.
- (iii) The correlation between $X$ and $Y$ is 0.20.

Calculate the variance of the employer's total expenditures for work-related and other injuries.

- A) 12,500
- B) 13,500
- C) 15,500
- D) 16,500
- E) 18,972

## Explanation

By (i), $X$ follows an exponential distribution with mean $20\sqrt{5}$ and variance $(20\sqrt{5})^2 = 2000$. Then, with (ii) and (iii),

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Cov}[X,Y] &= \sqrt{\text{Var}[X]}\,\sqrt{\text{Var}[Y]}\;\text{Corr}[X,Y] \\
                &= \sqrt{2000}\,\sqrt{12{,}500}\,(0.2) \\
                &= (5000)(0.2) \\
                &= 1000.
\end{align*}
$$

It follows that

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}[X+Y] &= \text{Var}[X] + \text{Var}[Y] + 2\,\text{Cov}[X,Y] \\
                &= 2000 + 12{,}500 + 2(1000) \\
                &= \mathbf{16{,}500}.
\end{align*}
$$
