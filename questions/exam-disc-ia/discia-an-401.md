A named insured's PAP (ISO PP 00 01 09 18) lists the family sedan. Which of these claims does Part A cover?

- A) The named insured, logged into a ride-hailing app and waiting for a request, rear-ends another car
- B) The named insured's daughter injures a pedestrian while driving a car her employer furnishes for her daily use
- C) The named insured's son, who lives at home, takes the sedan without asking and dents a parked car
- D) The named insured injures a coworker, who is his employee in his roofing business, while driving him to a job

## Explanation

Part A excludes an insured who uses a vehicle without a reasonable belief of being entitled to, but the exclusion **does not apply to a family member using a covered auto the named insured owns**. The son is a resident relative using the family sedan, so the property damage is covered. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — being logged into a transportation network platform as a driver is public or livery use under the 09 18 edition, whether or not a passenger is aboard.
- **B** — a vehicle furnished or available for a family member's regular use is excluded for that family member (exclusion B.3). The exception applies only to the named insured.
- **D** — bodily injury to the insured's own employee in the course of employment is excluded; only a domestic employee without workers compensation is an exception.
