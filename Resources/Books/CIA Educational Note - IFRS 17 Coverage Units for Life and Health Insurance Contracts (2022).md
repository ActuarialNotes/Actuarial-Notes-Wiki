A CIA educational note that gives guidance on the selection of coverage units, the quantity of service each contract provides, for amortizing the contractual service margin of insurance contracts within the scope of IFRS 17, for products typically sold in Canada. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated December 2022 (document 222175), it covers general considerations, individual life, annuities, accident and sickness, segregated funds and group insurance, and coverages combined within a group.
