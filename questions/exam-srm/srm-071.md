Determine which of the following statements regarding the effect of omitting important variables from the regression model specification is NOT true.

- A) It will result in underfitting.
- B) It will increase the width of prediction intervals.
- C) The coefficient estimates will remain unbiased.
- D) The estimate of the variance, 2s , is inflated.
- E) The total sum of squares remains unchanged.

## Explanation

Per Frees (page 197), the estimates will be biased.
