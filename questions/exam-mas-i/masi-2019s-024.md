You are given the following information:

- Random variable $X$ has an exponential distribution
- $\operatorname{Var}(X) = \frac{1}{9}$

Calculate the median of the distribution.

- A) Less than 0.15
- B) At least 0.15, but less than 0.20
- C) At least 0.20, but less than 0.25
- D) At least 0.25, but less than 0.30
- E) At least 0.30

## Explanation

An exponential distribution with mean $\theta$ has variance $\theta^2$, so the variance fixes the mean, and the median follows from the CDF $F(x) = 1 - e^{-x/\theta}$.

$$
\begin{align*}
\theta^2 &= \frac{1}{9} \\
\theta &= \frac{1}{3}
\end{align*}
$$

Set the CDF equal to one half:

$$
\begin{align*}
1 - e^{-m/\theta} &= 0.5 \\
m &= \theta \ln 2 \\
&= \frac{1}{3}(0.6931) \\
&= 0.2310
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.20, 0.25)$, so the answer is **C**.
