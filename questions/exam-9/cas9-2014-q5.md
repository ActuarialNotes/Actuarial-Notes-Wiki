Given the following information for constructing an investment portfolio:

| Asset | Expected Return | Standard Deviation |
|---|---|---|
| A | 8% | 20% |
| B | 10% | 25% |

- The risk-free rate is 2%.
- The correlation coefficient between assets A and B is -0.4.
- The investor has a risk aversion coefficient of 5.

Calculate the expected rate of return and standard deviation of the optimal complete portfolio.

### Explanation
**1. The optimal risky portfolio.** Work in excess returns, $E(R) = E(r) - r_f$, so $E(R_A) = 8\% - 2\% = 6\%$ and $E(R_B) = 10\% - 2\% = 8\%$. The covariance of A and B is

$$Cov(A,B) = \rho_{AB}\,\sigma_A\,\sigma_B = -0.4 \times 0.2 \times 0.25 = -0.02$$

The proportion of asset A in the optimal risky portfolio is

$$w_A^* = \frac{E(R_A)\sigma_B^2 - E(R_B)Cov(A,B)}{E(R_A)\sigma_B^2 + E(R_B)\sigma_A^2 - [E(R_A) + E(R_B)]Cov(A,B)}$$

$$= \frac{(8\% - 2\%)(25\%)^2 - (10\% - 2\%)(-0.02)}{6\% \times 25\%^2 + 8\% \times 20\%^2 - (6\% + 8\%)(-0.02)} = \frac{0.00535}{0.00975} = 54.87\%$$

and of asset B, $w_B^* = 1 - 54.87\% = 45.13\%$. Then

$$E(r_P) = w_A^* E(r_A) + w_B^* E(r_B) = 54.87\% \times 8\% + 45.13\% \times 10\% = 8.90\%$$

$$\sigma_P^2 = w_A^{*2}\sigma_A^2 + w_B^{*2}\sigma_B^2 + 2w_A^* w_B^* Cov(A,B)$$

$$= 54.87\%^2 \times 0.2^2 + 45.13\%^2 \times 0.25^2 + 2 \times 54.87\% \times 45.13\% \times (-0.02) = 0.014867$$

**2. The proportion in risky assets.** With utility $U = E(r) - \frac{1}{2}A\sigma^2$:

$$y^* = \frac{E(r_P) - r_f}{A\sigma_P^2} = \frac{8.9\% - 2\%}{5 \times 0.014867} = 92.86\%$$

**3. The optimal complete portfolio.**

$$E(r_C) = y^* E(r_P) + (1 - y^*)r_f = 92.86\% \times 8.90\% + (1 - 92.86\%) \times 2\% = 8.41\%$$

$$\sigma_C = y^*\sigma_P = 92.86\% \times \sqrt{0.014867} = 92.86\% \times 0.12193 = 0.1132$$
