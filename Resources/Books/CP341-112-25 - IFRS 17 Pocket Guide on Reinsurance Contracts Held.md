A July 2018 IFRS Foundation pocket guide, in the Supporting IFRS Standards series, on how IFRS 17 Insurance Contracts applies to reinsurance contracts held. It is a reference tool that also includes insights on implementing IFRS 17 from the discussions of the Transition Resource Group for IFRS 17. Under IFRS 17 a reinsurance contract held is accounted for as a standalone contract, independent of the accounting for the underlying insurance contracts, and the guide sets out the requirements and then what they mean for reinsurance contracts held, topic by topic.
