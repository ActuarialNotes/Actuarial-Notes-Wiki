The following information is available for a property and casualty insurance company. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

Paid during calendar year:

| Accident Year | Calendar Year 2012 | Calendar Year 2013 | Calendar Year 2014 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | 88,000 | 25,000 | 8,500 |
| 2013 | | 86,000 | 25,750 |
| 2014 | | | 92,500 |

Discounted Claim Liabilities at the end of each calendar year:

| Accident Year | Calendar Year 2012 | Calendar Year 2013 | Calendar Year 2014 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | 103,100 | 66,625 | 56,750 |
| 2013 | | 108,600 | 77,900 |
| 2014 | | | 126,600 |

Other information:

- Annual investment yield for calendar year 2013 is 3.0%.
- Annual investment yield for calendar year 2014 is 2.8%.

Calculate the discounted excess or deficiency ratio found on the P&C-1 page 60.41 for accident year 2012 as at December 31, 2014.

## Part a (1.25 points)

### Explanation
For a specific accident year:

$$\text{Investment Income from UCAE \& IBNR for CY } X = \frac{\text{Discounted Claim Liabilities CY } X + \text{Discounted Claim Liabilities CY } X{+}1}{2} \times \text{Annual investment yield for CY } X$$

(as printed — the calculation below averages the liabilities at the end of CY $X{-}1$ and CY $X$ and applies CY $X$'s yield).

For accident year 2012:

- II for CY 2013 $= \dfrac{103{,}100 + 66{,}625}{2} \times 3\% = 2{,}546$
- II for CY 2014 $= \dfrac{66{,}625 + 56{,}750}{2} \times 2.8\% = 1{,}727$

Amount of excess (deficiency) for AY 2012, using the accident year 2012 figures:

$$\text{UCAE CY 2012} - \text{Paid CY 2013} - \text{Paid CY 2014} + \text{II CY 2013} + \text{II CY 2014} - \text{UCAE CY 2014}$$

$$= 103{,}100 - 25{,}000 - 8{,}500 + 2{,}546 + 1{,}727 - 56{,}750 = 17{,}123$$

Ratio of excess (deficiency) $=$ amount of excess (deficiency) / UCAE CY 2012 (accident year 2012):

$$= 17{,}123 / 103{,}100 = 16.61\%$$
