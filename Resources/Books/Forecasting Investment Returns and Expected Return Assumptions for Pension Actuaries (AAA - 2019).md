A February 2019 public policy practice note from the Pension Practice Council of the American Academy of Actuaries, giving information on current and emerging practices in the selection of investment return assumptions based on anticipated future experience. It illustrates how actuaries select or recommend an expected investment return assumption, or assess capital market models from an outside party, for accounting and financial reporting, funding valuations and asset liability modeling. It is not an actuarial standard of practice.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 2a-2d; the whole note, which the SOA describes as the core overview of investment return, interest rate and discount rate assumptions.
