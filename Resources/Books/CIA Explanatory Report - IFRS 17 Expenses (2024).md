A CIA explanatory report, dated December 17, 2024 (document 224129), that provides information concerning expenses under IFRS 17 requirements. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and the Committee on Property and Casualty Insurance Financial Reporting, it compares IFRS 4 with IFRS 17 on expenses, sets out considerations on directly attributable expenses, classifies grey-area expenses, and suggests disclosures for the Appointed Actuary's report; its appendices cover other expense types and the conduct of an expense study.
