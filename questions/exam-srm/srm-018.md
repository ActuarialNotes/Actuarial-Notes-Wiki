For a simple linear regression model the sum of squares of the residuals is $\sum_{i=1}^{25} e_i^2 = 230$ and the $R^2$ statistic is 0.64.

Calculate the total sum of squares (TSS) for this model.

- A) 605.94
- B) 638.89
- C) 690.77
- D) 701.59
- E) 750.87

## Explanation

Since $R^2 = 1 - \dfrac{\text{RSS}}{\text{TSS}}$, the total sum of squares is the residual sum of squares divided by $1 - R^2$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{TSS} &= \frac{\text{Residual Sum of Squares}}{1 - R^2} \\
&= \frac{230}{1 - 0.64} \\
&= \frac{230}{0.36} \\
&= 638.89
\end{align*}
$$
