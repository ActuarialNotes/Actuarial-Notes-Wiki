## [F-001] Thousands commas inside math render with a stray space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 44-61
- claim: Math such as $15,000 + 45,000 = 60,000 = 79,860/1.10^3$ and the align blocks write thousands with a bare comma.
- evidence: In TeX/KaTeX a bare comma in math is punctuation followed by a thin space, so 15,000 renders as '15, 000'; the bank elsewhere writes 15{,}000 (e.g. fm-141). quiz/src/lib/vaultMath.ts only moves delimiters and never edits a LaTeX body, so it does not repair this. The numbers themselves check: 79,860/1.1^3 = 60,000; 60,000(3/1.10) = 163,636.36; (163,636 - 27,000)/45,000 = 3.0364 (recomputed).
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Write thousands separators as {,} inside math.
- applied: true
- fingerprint: 6eb5cf52ccf0
