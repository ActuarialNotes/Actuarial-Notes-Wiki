A 20-year 1000 face amount bond has an annual coupon rate of 6%, payable semiannually.

The bond is callable for 1060 at the end of the 10th year and for 1030 at the end of the 15th year.

Calculate the highest price for the bond that guarantees an annual nominal yield rate of 5%, convertible semiannually.

- A) 1115
- B) 1119
- C) 1126
- D) 1135
- E) 1144

## Explanation

The semiannual coupon is $1000(0.06/2) = 30$ and the semiannual yield rate is $0.05/2 = 0.025$. The bond may be redeemed after 10 years for 1060, after 15 years for 1030, or at maturity after 20 years for 1000. A price guarantees the yield only if it is no more than the price at 2.5% per half-year for every one of these redemption dates, so compute all three and take the smallest.

Redemption after 20 years (40 half-years):

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 30\,a_{\overline{40}|0.025} + 1000\,v^{40} \\
  &= 30(25.102775) + 1000(0.3724306) \\
  &= 753.08 + 372.43 \\
  &= 1125.51
\end{align*}
$$

Call after 15 years (30 half-years):

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 30\,a_{\overline{30}|0.025} + 1030\,v^{30} \\
  &= 30(20.930293) + 1030(0.4767427) \\
  &= 627.91 + 491.04 \\
  &= 1118.95
\end{align*}
$$

Call after 10 years (20 half-years):

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 30\,a_{\overline{20}|0.025} + 1060\,v^{20} \\
  &= 30(15.589162) + 1060(0.6102709) \\
  &= 467.67 + 646.89 \\
  &= 1114.56
\end{align*}
$$

The highest price that guarantees the yield is the smallest of the three, 1114.56.
