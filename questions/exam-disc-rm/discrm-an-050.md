Before preparing a quote, a producer visits a small manufacturer, reviews its operations and contracts, and points out a liability exposure created by a lease the owner had not considered. Which producer function is the producer performing?

- A) Prospecting for new customers
- B) Selling and binding coverage
- C) Reviewing loss exposures and needs
- D) Handling and settling claims

## Explanation

Producers review prospects' loss exposures and risk management needs, often acting as consultants on the customer's insurance program. Finding an exposure in a lease is that review. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Prospecting is finding new customers; the producer already has this one.
- **B** — Selling and binding come after the exposures are understood and a quote is accepted.
- **D** — Claims help applies after a loss; no loss has occurred.
