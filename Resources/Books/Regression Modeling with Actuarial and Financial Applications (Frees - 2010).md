![[Regression Modeling with Actuarial and Financial Applications (Frees - 2010) - Cover.svg]]

A text that gives budding actuaries and financial analysts a foundation in multiple regression and time series. Cambridge University Press says readers learn the techniques using data on the demand for insurance, lottery sales, foreign exchange rates and other applications, with no specific knowledge of risk management or finance presumed. Beyond the fundamentals it describes advanced statistical topics relevant to actuarial and financial practice, including the analysis of longitudinal, two-part (frequency/severity) and fat-tailed data. The book's website holds datasets, sample R and SAS scripts and tips on writing a statistical report.
