A Deloitte report, copyright 2018, on the principal findings of Deloitte Advisory's reinsurance administration survey of senior executives responsible for ceded reinsurance at 13 insurance companies in 2016–17. It considers the objectives of reinsurance, relationships with reinsurers, organizational capabilities, technology applications used, top pain points and emerging opportunities, with Deloitte Advisory's perspectives from client experience and discussions with industry executives.
