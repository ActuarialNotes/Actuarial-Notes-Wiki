A question-and-answer document of the Canadian Life and Health Insurance Association (CLHIA) on the protection of personal information. According to the GH 201-C course strategy guide, it outlines guidelines and best practices for handling and protecting personal information related to group benefit plans, with the focus on managing personal data such as medical information and claim details securely and in compliance with privacy laws and regulations.
