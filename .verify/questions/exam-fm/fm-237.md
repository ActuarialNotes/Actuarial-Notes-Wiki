## [F-002] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space ('16, 000')
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 46-47 and 55-56
- claim: The math spans write 16,000 and 1,000 with a bare comma.
- evidence: In TeX/KaTeX math a bare ',' is a punctuation atom followed by a thin space, so e.g. $14,000$ typesets as '14, 000'; the rest of the FM bank writes {,}. No digit is affected.
