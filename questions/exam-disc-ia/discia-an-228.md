A local newspaper reports, accurately, that a private citizen with no public role received psychiatric treatment last year. The fact was private, and its publication serves no public interest. Which tort is the citizen's best claim?

- A) Public disclosure of private facts
- B) Libel
- C) Slander
- D) False light

## Explanation

Invasion of privacy takes four forms, and **public disclosure of private facts** is the one that fits a true but private fact made public.

- **B** and **C** are defamation, which requires a **false** statement of fact. The report is true, and truth defeats defamation. (Libel is written and slander spoken.)
- **D** places someone in a misleading or false light. An accurate report does not do that.
