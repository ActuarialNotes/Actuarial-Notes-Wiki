The statistics reference on the MAS-I syllabus, covering learning objectives B1–B8 and parts of C1–C9. Chapters 4–9 carry the exam's estimation, testing and linear-model material.

## 1 Probability and Distributions

- Sets, [[Probability]] set functions and [[Conditional Probability]]
- [[Random Variable|Random variables]], discrete and continuous
- [[Expected Value]] and moment generating functions

## 2 Multivariate Distributions

- [[Joint Probability Function|Joint]], [[Marginal Probability Function|marginal]] and [[Conditional Probability Function|conditional]] distributions
- [[Covariance]] and [[Correlation]]
- [[Independent Random Variables|Independence]] and transformations
- [[Linear Combinations of Random Variables|Linear combinations]] of random variables

## 3 Some Special Distributions

- The binomial, [[Poisson Distribution|Poisson]] and related discrete distributions
- The [[Normal Distribution]], [[Gamma]], chi-square and beta distributions
- The multivariate normal distribution
- $t$ and $F$ distributions, mixture distributions

## 4 Some Elementary Statistical Inferences

- Sampling and [[Statistics|statistics]], [[Order Statistics]]
- [[Confidence Interval|Confidence intervals]] for means, differences and proportions
- Introduction to [[Hypothesis Testing]]
- Additional comments on tests, chi-square tests
- The method of Monte Carlo, bootstrap procedures
- Tolerance limits

## 5 Consistency and Limiting Distributions

- Convergence in probability and [[Consistency]]
- Convergence in distribution and the [[Central Limit Theorem]]
- Asymptotics for multivariate distributions

## 6 Maximum Likelihood Methods

- [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]]
- Rao–Cramér lower bound ([[Fisher Information]]) and [[Efficiency]]
- [[Likelihood Ratio Test|Maximum likelihood tests]]
- Multiparameter estimation and testing
- The EM algorithm

## 7 Sufficiency

- Measures of quality of estimators: [[Unbiasedness]], [[Mean Square Error]], [[Minimum Variance]]
- A [[Sufficient Statistic]] for a parameter and factorization ([[Sufficiency]])
- Properties of a sufficient statistic; Rao–Blackwell
- Completeness, uniqueness and the exponential class of distributions
- Minimal sufficiency and ancillary statistics

## 8 Optimal Tests of Hypotheses

- Most powerful tests and the [[Power of a Test|power function]]
- Uniformly most powerful tests
- [[Likelihood Ratio Test|Likelihood ratio tests]]
- Sequential probability ratio tests, minimax and classification procedures

## 9 Inferences About Normal Linear Models

- [[ANOVA]] and one-way analysis of variance
- Noncentral $\chi^2$ and $F$ distributions
- Multiple comparisons; two-way analysis of variance
- The general [[Linear Regression|linear model]] in matrix notation
- Quadratic forms and independence

## 10 Nonparametric and Robust Statistics

- Location models, sign and signed-rank procedures
- Mann–Whitney–Wilcoxon procedure
- Adaptive and robust methods, Huber's estimator

## 11 Bayesian Statistics

- [[Bayesian]] procedures and priors
- More Bayesian terminology and ideas
- Gibbs sampler and modern Bayesian methods

- Mathematical comments, R primer, statistical tables and lists of common distributions
