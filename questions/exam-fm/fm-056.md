Aakash has a liability of 6000 due in four years. This liability will be met with payments of $A$ in two years and $B$ in six years. Aakash is employing a full immunization strategy using an annual effective interest rate of 5%.

Calculate $|A - B|$.

- A) 0
- B) 146
- C) 293
- D) 586
- E) 881

## Explanation

Full immunization of a single liability has three conditions: the present values of the assets and the liability are equal, their durations are equal, and the asset cash flows fall before and after the liability cash flow. Here $A$ at time 2 and $B$ at time 6 bracket the liability at time 4, so the third condition holds; the first two give two equations in the two unknowns, written with values at time 4:

**PV match:**
$$A(1.05)^2 + B(1.05)^{-2} = 6000$$

**Duration match** (the derivative of the time-4 surplus with respect to $i$ is zero):

$$2A(1.05)^1 - 2B(1.05)^{-3} = 0$$

Solving simultaneously:
$$A = 2721.09,\quad B = 3307.50$$

$$|A - B| = 586.41$$
