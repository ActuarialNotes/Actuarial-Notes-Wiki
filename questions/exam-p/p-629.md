Let $Y$ be a discrete random variable with probability function

$$
p(y) = \begin{cases}
0.2, & \text{for } y = a \\
0.1, & \text{for } y = 1 \\
0.3, & \text{for } y = 3 \\
0.4, & \text{for } y = 10 \\
0, & \text{otherwise,}
\end{cases}
$$

where $a$ is a constant less than 0.

$E(Y^2) = 47.8$.

Calculate $\text{Var}(Y)$.

- A) $5.6$
- B) $11.8$
- C) $22.8$
- D) $31.8$
- E) $63.8$

## Explanation

Write out the second moment and solve for $a$:

$$
\begin{align*}
47.8 &= E(Y^2) = a^2(0.2) + 1^2(0.1) + 3^2(0.3) + 10^2(0.4) \\
     &= 0.2a^2 + 42.8 \\
5 &= 0.2a^2 \\
a^2 &= 25
\end{align*}
$$

The problem states $a < 0$, so $a = -5$. Then

$$
E(Y) = -5(0.2) + 1(0.1) + 3(0.3) + 10(0.4) = -1 + 0.1 + 0.9 + 4 = 4
$$

and

$$
\text{Var}(Y) = E(Y^2) - \left[E(Y)\right]^2 = 47.8 - 4^2 = \mathbf{31.8}
$$

Choice (B), 11.8, takes the positive root $a = +5$. That raises the mean to 6 and, since $E(Y^2)$ is unchanged, drops the variance to $47.8 - 36 = 11.8$. The sign of $a$ is invisible in $E(Y^2)$ — which is exactly why the problem has to state $a < 0$ separately.
