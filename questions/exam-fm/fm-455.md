Bond X, Bond Y, and Bond Z are each $n$-year bonds with face amount 1000. All three bonds are priced to yield a nominal annual rate of 6.0% convertible quarterly.

Bond X pays annual coupons of $R$, Bond Y pays semiannual coupons of 34.00, and Bond Z pays quarterly coupons of 15.00.

The price of Bond Y exceeds the prices of Bond X and Bond Z by 138.88 and 73.78, respectively.

Calculate $R$.

- A) 52.05
- B) 52.23
- C) 53.20
- D) 53.92
- E) 54.60

## Explanation

The quarterly yield rate is $0.06/4 = 0.015$, which equals Bond Z's quarterly coupon rate $15/1000$, so Bond Z sells at its face amount: its price is 1000. The price of Bond Y is therefore $1000 + 73.78 = 1073.78$.

The semiannual yield rate is

$$
\begin{align*}
j &= 1.015^{2} - 1 \\
  &= 0.030225
\end{align*}
$$

Pricing Bond Y over its $2n$ half-years,

$$1073.78 = 34\,a_{\overline{2n}|0.030225} + 1000(1.030225)^{-2n}$$

which a financial calculator solves as $2n = 30$, so $n = 15$.

The price of Bond X is $1073.78 - 138.88 = 934.90$, and the annual yield rate is $1.015^{4} - 1 = 0.06136$. Then

$$
\begin{align*}
934.90 &= R\,a_{\overline{15}|0.06136} + 1000(1.06136)^{-15} \\
       &= 9.62652R + 409.3165 \\
R &= \frac{934.90 - 409.3165}{9.62652} \\
  &= 54.60
\end{align*}
$$
