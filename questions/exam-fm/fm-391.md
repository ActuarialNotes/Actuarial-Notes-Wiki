A company is considering a project where it expects to pay and receive the following cash flows:

| End of year | Cash flow |
|---|---|
| 2 | Receives $X$ |
| 4 | Pays $Y$ |
| 6 | Receives $X$ |

When calculated using an annual effective rate of $i$ ($i > 0$), the present value of this project is equal to 0.

Determine $i$.

- A) $\dfrac{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}{2X}$
- B) $\dfrac{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}{2X} - 1$
- C) $\sqrt{\dfrac{-Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}{2X}} - 1$
- D) $\sqrt{\dfrac{2X}{-Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}} - 1$
- E) $\sqrt{\dfrac{2X}{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}} - 1$

## Explanation

With $v = \dfrac{1}{1+i}$, setting the present value of the project equal to 0 and dividing by $v^{2}$ gives a quadratic equation in $v^{2}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
Xv^{2} - Yv^{4} + Xv^{6} &= 0 \\
Xv^{4} - Yv^{2} + X &= 0
\end{align*}
$$

Solving with the quadratic formula, then inverting and taking the square root:

$$
\begin{align*}
v^{2} &= \frac{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}{2X} \\
(1+i)^{2} &= \frac{2X}{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}} \\
1+i &= \sqrt{\frac{2X}{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}} \\
i &= \sqrt{\frac{2X}{Y \pm \sqrt{Y^{2} - 4X^{2}}}} - 1
\end{align*}
$$
