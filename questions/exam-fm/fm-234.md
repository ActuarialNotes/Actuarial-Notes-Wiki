A bank issues two 20-year par-value bonds providing annual coupons. Each bond sells for the same price and provides an annual effective yield rate of 6.5%.

The first bond has a redemption value of 6000 and a coupon of 7.6% paid annually. The second bond has a redemption value of 7500 and a coupon of $r$% paid annually.

Calculate $r$.

- A) 5.6
- B) 5.9
- C) 6.1
- D) 6.7
- E) 7.2

## Explanation

At 6.5%, $a_{\overline{20}|0.065} = 11.0185$ and $v^{20} = 0.283797$.

**First bond.** The coupon is $0.076(6000) = 456$:
$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 456\,a_{\overline{20}|0.065} + 6000v^{20} \\
&= 456(11.0185) + 6000(0.283797) \\
&= 5024.44 + 1702.78 \\
&= 6727.22
\end{align*}
$$

**Second bond.** It sells for the same price, with coupon $\frac{r}{100}(7500)$:
$$
\begin{align*}
6727.22 &= \tfrac{r}{100}(7500)\,a_{\overline{20}|0.065} + 7500v^{20} \\
&= \tfrac{r}{100}(7500)(11.0185) + 2128.48
\end{align*}
$$

so the coupon is
$$\tfrac{r}{100}(7500) = \frac{6727.22 - 2128.48}{11.0185} = \frac{4598.74}{11.0185} = 417.37$$

and $r = 100 \times 417.37/7500 = 5.56 \approx 5.6$.
