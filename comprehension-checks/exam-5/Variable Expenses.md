An insurer shifts distribution from independent agents to direct-to-consumer, cutting commissions sharply. What happens to $V$ and to the permissible loss ratio?

- A) $V$ falls, and the permissible loss ratio rises to leave more room
- B) $V$ falls, and the permissible loss ratio falls along with it
- C) $V$ rises, and the permissible loss ratio falls in response
- D) Neither moves — distribution costs are fixed on a per-policy basis
