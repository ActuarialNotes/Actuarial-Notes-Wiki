The lifetime $X$ of an electronic component has density function

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases}
8xe^{-4x^2}, & \text{for } x > 0 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the median lifetime of the component.

- A) $0.173$
- B) $0.268$
- C) $0.416$
- D) $0.693$
- E) $0.833$

## Explanation

Let $m$ be the median, so the density integrates to 0.5 up to $m$. The substitution $u = 4x^2$ makes the integral immediate — $8xe^{-4x^2}$ is exactly $-\frac{d}{dx}e^{-4x^2}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\int_0^m 8xe^{-4x^2}\,dx &= \left. -e^{-4x^2} \right|_0^m = 0.5 \\
-e^{-4m^2} + 1 &= 0.5 \\
e^{-4m^2} &= 0.5
\end{align*}
$$

Taking logs and solving for $m$:

$$
m = \sqrt{\frac{-\ln 0.5}{4}} = \sqrt{\frac{0.6931}{4}} = \mathbf{0.4163}
$$

Choice (D), 0.693, is $-\ln 0.5$ left as-is, skipping both the division by 4 and the square root. Choice (A), 0.173, is $m^2 = 0.1733$ — the square root taken one step too few. This is a Rayleigh distribution, not an exponential: the exponent carries $x^2$, so the last two operations are what separate a median from an exponential-style answer.
