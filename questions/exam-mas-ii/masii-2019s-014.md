You are fitting a Linear Mixed Model to determine the effect of different preservatives on the shelf life of apples. You are given the following information:

- Each apple is cut into three pieces and treated with a different preservative.
- The measured between subject variance is 1.43 and the within subject variance is 0.87.

Determine which of the following matrices is the variance-covariance matrix for a randomly selected subject for the Implied Marginal Model.

- A) $\begin{bmatrix} 1.43 & 1.43 & 1.43 \\ 1.43 & 1.43 & 1.43 \\ 1.43 & 1.43 & 1.43 \end{bmatrix}$
- B) $\begin{bmatrix} 0.87 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.87 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0.87 \end{bmatrix}$
- C) $\begin{bmatrix} 2.30 & 1.43 & 1.43 \\ 1.43 & 2.30 & 1.43 \\ 1.43 & 1.43 & 2.30 \end{bmatrix}$
- D) $\begin{bmatrix} 0.87 & 1.43 & 1.43 \\ 1.43 & 0.87 & 1.43 \\ 1.43 & 1.43 & 0.87 \end{bmatrix}$
- E) $\begin{bmatrix} 2.30 & 2.30 & 2.30 \\ 2.30 & 2.30 & 2.30 \\ 2.30 & 2.30 & 2.30 \end{bmatrix}$

## Explanation

Each apple is a subject with a random intercept $u_i \sim N(0,\, \sigma_c^2 = 1.43)$ and three residuals $\varepsilon_{ij} \sim N(0,\, \sigma^2 = 0.87)$. Integrating the random effect out gives the implied marginal covariance

$$
\mathbf{V}_i = \mathbf{Z}_i \mathbf{D} \mathbf{Z}_i' + \mathbf{R}_i = \sigma_c^2 \mathbf{J}_3 + \sigma^2 \mathbf{I}_3,
$$

where $\mathbf{J}_3$ is the $3 \times 3$ matrix of ones. Element by element:

$$
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{Var}(Y_{ij}) &= \sigma_c^2 + \sigma^2 \\
                           &= 1.43 + 0.87 \\
                           &= 2.30 \\
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_{ij}, Y_{ik}) &= \sigma_c^2 = 1.43 \quad (j \neq k)
\end{align*}
$$

That is compound symmetry: 2.30 down the diagonal and 1.43 everywhere off it, so the answer is **C**.

(As a check, the intraclass correlation is $1.43/2.30 = 0.62$.)
