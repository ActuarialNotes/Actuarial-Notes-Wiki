## Concept pages — the 37 majors (formula- or definition-level)

Missing conditions (18):

- **Regularity conditions** were missing from statements about the MLE, Cramér–Rao and efficiency:
  Maximum Likelihood Estimation, Parameter Estimation, Minimum Variance, Efficiency.
- **Relative efficiency** was defined without requiring both estimators to be unbiased: Efficiency.
- **Normal-population conditions** were missing: Sample Variance, Unbiasedness, Sampling
  Distribution, Confidence Interval.
- **Same data and response** was missing as a condition for comparing models: AIC, BIC.
- **Dispersion φ** was missing from the analysis-of-deviance and likelihood-ratio statements:
  ANOVA, Likelihood Ratio Test.
- **Uniqueness** was missing from "an MLE is a function of a sufficient statistic": Sufficiency.
- **Uncorrelated errors** were missing from Gauss–Markov: Linear Regression.
- **The discrete-response caveat** was missing from deviance-residual normality: Model Fit.
- **The grouped-data condition** was missing from the deviance-vs-df overdispersion check: Logistic
  Regression.
- **The linear-predictor scale** was not given as a requirement for an offset: Offset Variable.

Wrong or missing content (9):

- **Life Annuity and Whole Life Insurance** were defined by continuous models the MAS-I reading
  never uses. They lacked the annual annuity-due, its recursion and the level premium the papers
  test.
- **Mean Square Error** gave only the estimator sense. Domain C uses MSE for prediction error.
- **Model Structure** left out the distribution and link, which the outline names as model
  structure.
- **Extended Linear Model** said only ELMs may carry interactions or transformations.
- **Logistic Regression** had no nominal (multinomial) or ordinal logistic regression, though both
  are assigned (ISLR §4.3.5, Dobson Ch 8) and tested.
- **Variable Selection** lacked best subset selection and the lasso.
- **Parameter Estimate Tables** gave p-values and intervals in z form for t-value tables.
- **Severity Distribution** said excess layers grow faster than inflation. That holds only for an
  unlimited layer above a fixed attachment; a capped layer can grow more slowly.

Influence and leverage conflated (3): Outlier, Residual Plot and Added Variable Plot each treated
influence and leverage as the same thing, or called large-residual points influential.

Ratemaking (2):

- **Control Variable** said a territory priced outside the model enters as a control variable; the
  Monograph enters it as an offset.
- **Tweedie Distribution** said frequency and severity share relativities; the Monograph says they
  move in the same direction.

Worked examples (4):

- **AIC** counted predictors, not parameters.
- **Loading Vector** had two loading vectors that were not orthogonal.
- **Model Structure**'s example decided the model on the individual p-values of interaction
  dummies.
- **Box Plot** used the upper whisker end as the maximum. Fixed in this run.

Other (1): **Aggregate Loss Model** said compound Poisson requires exponential severities. Fixed in
this run.

- **Interruptions:** two account usage limits stopped running agents. Each was relaunched with a
  note to check its log for entries already recorded, so nothing was recorded twice.
