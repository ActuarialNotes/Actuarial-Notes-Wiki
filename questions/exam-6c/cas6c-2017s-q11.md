Calculate the amounts in A to M in the Schedule 60.30 above.

### Explanation
All amounts are in \$000s. The company underwrites property reinsurance only, so there is no direct business.

**A** (Direct claims and adjustment expenses paid – current year) $= 0$, since the company underwrites property reinsurance only.

Incremental Paid Claims (Gross):

| Year | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 2,500 | 5,000-2,500 = 2,500 | 5,000 | 1,000 |
| 2014 | 3,700 | 1,800 | 3,500 | |
| 2015 | 2,800 | 3,200 | | |
| 2016 | 3,000 | | | |

**B** (Reinsurance assumed claims and adjustment expenses paid – current year) is the sum of the last diagonal of the incremental paid claims (gross) triangle:

$$B = 3{,}000 + 3{,}200 + 3{,}500 + 1{,}000 = 10{,}700$$

Incremental Paid Claims (Net):

| Year | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 1,250 | 2,500-1,250 = 1,250 | 2,500 | 500 |
| 2014 | 1,850 | 900 | 1,750 | |
| 2015 | 1,400 | 1,600 | | |
| 2016 | 1,500 | | | |

**D** (Net claims and adjustment expenses paid – current year) is the sum of the last diagonal of the incremental paid claims (net) triangle:

$$D = 1{,}500 + 1{,}600 + 1{,}750 + 500 = 5{,}350$$

**C** (Reinsurance ceded claims and adjustment expenses paid – current year) is the gross last diagonal less the net last diagonal:

$$C = (3{,}000 + 3{,}200 + 3{,}500 + 1{,}000) - (1{,}500 + 1{,}600 + 1{,}750 + 500) = 5{,}350$$

or $C = B - D = 10{,}700 - 5{,}350 = 5{,}350$.

Provision for Unpaid Claims (Including Unreported) and Adjustment Expenses + Discounting + PFAD (Gross) — each cell is the ultimate less the cumulative paid plus the effect of discounting and PFAD:

| Year | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 7,613 | 12,000-5,000+206 = 7,206 | 1,536 | 518 |
| 2014 | 6,395 | 4,632 | 1,024 | |
| 2015 | 8,323 | 5,662 | | |
| 2016 | 9,135 | | | |

**E** (Direct provision for unpaid claims (including unreported) and adjustment expenses – current year) $= 0$, since the company underwrites property reinsurance only.

**F** (Reinsurance assumed provision – current year) is the sum of the last diagonal of the gross provision triangle:

$$F = 9{,}135 + 5{,}662 + 1{,}024 + 518 = 16{,}339$$

Provision for Unpaid Claims (Including Unreported) and Adjustment Expenses + Discounting + PFAD (Net):

| Year | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 4,167 | 6,000-2,500+445 = 3,945 | 841 | 284 |
| 2014 | 3,500 | 2,536 | 561 | |
| 2015 | 4,556 | 3,099 | | |
| 2016 | 5,000 | | | |

**H** (Net provision – current year) is the sum of the last diagonal of the net provision triangle:

$$H = 5{,}000 + 3{,}099 + 561 + 284 = 8{,}944$$

**G** (Reinsurance ceded provision – current year) is the gross last diagonal less the net last diagonal:

$$G = (9{,}135 + 5{,}662 + 1{,}024 + 518) - (5{,}000 + 3{,}099 + 561 + 284) = 7{,}395$$

or $G = F - H = 16{,}339 - 8{,}944 = 7{,}395$.

**I** (Net provision for prior year-end) is the sum of the next-to-last diagonal of the net provision triangle, for accident year 2015 and prior:

$$I = 4{,}556 + 2{,}536 + 841 = 7{,}933$$

**J** (Net amount paid during the year for claims of prior years) is the sum of the last diagonal of the incremental paid claims (net) triangle for accident year 2015 and prior:

$$J = 1{,}600 + 1{,}750 + 500 = 3{,}850$$

or $J = D - 1{,}500 = 5{,}350 - 1{,}500 = 3{,}850$, removing accident year 2016's payment from the last diagonal.

**L** (Net provision for claims of prior years) is the sum of the last diagonal of the net provision triangle for accident year 2015 and prior:

$$L = 3{,}099 + 561 + 284 = 3{,}944$$

or $L = H - 5{,}000 = 8{,}944 - 5{,}000 = 3{,}944$, removing accident year 2016's provision.

**K** (Investment income on unpaid claims of prior years) is the investment yield times the mean of I and L:

$$K = 3\% \times \text{mean}(7{,}933;\ 3{,}944) = 178$$

**M** (Margin (deficiency)):

$$M = I - J + K - L = 7{,}933 - 3{,}850 + 178 - 3{,}944 = 317$$

Alternatively, working directly from the cumulative diagonals:

- $A = 0$ and $E = 0$, since the company underwrites reinsurance only.
- B = cumulative paid from the 2016 diagonal (gross) – cumulative paid from the 2015 diagonal (gross):

$$B = (3{,}000 + 6{,}000 + 9{,}000 + 11{,}000) - (2{,}800 + 5{,}500 + 10{,}000) = 10{,}700$$

- D = cumulative paid from the 2016 diagonal (net) – cumulative paid from the 2015 diagonal (net):

$$D = (1{,}500 + 3{,}000 + 4{,}500 + 5{,}500) - (1{,}400 + 2{,}750 + 5{,}000) = 5{,}350$$

- $C = B - D$
- F = gross ultimate – gross paid + gross effect of discounting and PFAD (using the 2016 diagonal):

$$F = (12{,}000 + 11{,}500 + 10{,}000 + 11{,}500) - (3{,}000 + 6{,}000 + 9{,}000 + 11{,}000) + (135 + 162 + 24 + 18) = 16{,}339$$

- H = net ultimate – net paid + net effect of discounting and PFAD (using the 2016 diagonal):

$$H = (6{,}000 + 5{,}750 + 5{,}000 + 5{,}750) - (1{,}500 + 3{,}000 + 4{,}500 + 5{,}500) + (500 + 349 + 61 + 34) = 8{,}944$$

- $G = F - H$
- I = net ultimate – net paid + net effect of discounting and PFAD (using the 2015 diagonal):

$$I = (5{,}500 + 5{,}000 + 5{,}750) - (1{,}400 + 2{,}750 + 5{,}000) + (456 + 286 + 91) = 7{,}933$$

- J = paid for years 2015 and prior (net, 2016 diagonal) – paid for years 2015 and prior (net, 2015 diagonal):

$$J = (3{,}000 + 4{,}500 + 5{,}500) - (1{,}400 + 2{,}750 + 5{,}000) = 3{,}850$$

- $K = 3\% \times \text{average}(I, L) = 3\% \times \text{average}(7{,}933;\ 3{,}944) = 178$
- L = net ultimate – net paid + net effect of discounting and PFAD (using the 2016 diagonal, years 2015 and prior only):

$$L = (5{,}750 + 5{,}000 + 5{,}750) - (3{,}000 + 4{,}500 + 5{,}500) + (349 + 61 + 34) = 3{,}944$$

- $M = I - J + K - L = 7{,}933 - 3{,}850 + 178 - 3{,}944 = 317$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to:

- Use different information in different types of triangles.
- Fill in schedule 60.30.

Common errors include:

- Assuming the company writes only direct business when the question specifies that the company is a reinsurer.
- Using the Paid triangles as incremental instead of cumulative as specified.
- Not treating Ultimate Claims as undiscounted (incurred claims and IBNR) but as including provisions.
- Not considering discounting and PFAD.
