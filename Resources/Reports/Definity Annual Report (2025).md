Definity Financial Corporation's annual report for 2025: letters from the Board Chair and the CEO, the MD&A and the audited consolidated financial statements. Definity describes itself as one of Canada's leading property and casualty insurers, with a multi-line and multi-channel business model; its MD&A, dated February 12, 2026, places it sixth among Canadian P&C insurers. The year's results are set against its acquisition of Travelers' Canadian P&C insurance business, which closed on January 2, 2026, adding about $1.5 billion of pro forma gross written premiums.
