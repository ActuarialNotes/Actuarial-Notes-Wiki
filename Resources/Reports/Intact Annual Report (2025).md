Intact Financial Corporation's annual report for the year ended December 31, 2025: the shareholder letters, then the MD&A and the audited consolidated financial statements. Intact describes itself as a global provider of property and casualty insurance, with $25.1 billion of 2025 operating direct premiums written — 69% in Canada, 19% in the UK and International (UK&I) and 12% in the US. The MD&A, dated February 10, 2026, is presented in millions of Canadian dollars and is to be read with the consolidated financial statements that follow it.

        - 8.1 Net Catastrophe (CAT) losses — current year CAT losses of $844 million in 2025 ($489 million of them in Canada), against $1,539 million in 2024
        - 8.2 CAT thresholds — a [[Catastrophe Loss|catastrophe claim]] is any one claim or group of claims, before reinsurance, from a single event, equal to or above a set threshold; from January 1, 2026 the thresholds are $15 million in Canada, £10 million in UK&I, US$7.5 million in the US and $20 million in aggregate across segments
        - 8.3 CAT guidance — $1.2 billion of annual CAT losses expected for 2026, net of reinsurance, about 75% of them in the Canadian segment
