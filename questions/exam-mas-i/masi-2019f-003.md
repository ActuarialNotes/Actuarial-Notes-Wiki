You are given the following information about an online retailer:

- Orders are placed on the website according to a homogeneous Poisson process with mean 50 per hour
- The number of items purchased in each order is independent and has the following distribution:

| Number of Items | Probability |
|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | 0.50 |
| 2 | 0.40 |
| 3 | 0.08 |
| 4 | 0.02 |

Calculate the variance of the total number of items purchased in a four-hour period.

- A) Less than 100
- B) At least 100, but less than 300
- C) At least 300, but less than 500
- D) At least 500, but less than 700
- E) At least 700

## Explanation

The total number of items is a compound Poisson process: a Poisson number of orders, each contributing an independent number of items $Y$. For $S(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{N(t)} Y_i$,

$$
\operatorname{Var}[S(t)] = \lambda t \, E[Y^2].
$$

Over four hours the expected number of orders is $\lambda t = 50(4) = 200$. The second moment of the items per order is

$$
\begin{align*}
E[Y^2] &= 1^2(0.50) + 2^2(0.40) + 3^2(0.08) + 4^2(0.02) \\
&= 0.50 + 1.60 + 0.72 + 0.32 \\
&= 3.14
\end{align*}
$$

So

$$
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{Var}[S(4)] &= 200(3.14) \\
&= 628
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[500, 700)$, so the answer is **D**.
