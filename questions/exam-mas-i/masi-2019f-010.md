Ann and Beatrice are playing a game with multiple rounds:

- At the end of each round, the loser pays 1 coin to the winner
- Each player begins with 5 coins and plays until one of them has no coins remaining
- Beatrice is more experienced at the game, and her probability of winning each round is 0.75
- Each round is independent

Calculate the probability that Beatrice has no coins remaining at the end of the game.

- A) Less than 0.01
- B) At least 0.01, but less than 0.03
- C) At least 0.03, but less than 0.05
- D) At least 0.05, but less than 0.07
- E) At least 0.07

## Explanation

This is the gambler's ruin problem with 10 coins in play. Beatrice ends with no coins exactly when Ann ends with all 10, so follow Ann's fortune: she starts at $i = 5$, wins each round with $p = 0.25$ and loses with $q = 0.75$. The probability of reaching $N = 10$ before 0 is

$$
P_i = \frac{1 - (q/p)^i}{1 - (q/p)^N}.
$$

Here $q/p = 3$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P_5 &= \frac{1 - 3^5}{1 - 3^{10}} \\
&= \frac{1 - 243}{1 - 59049} \\
&= \frac{242}{59048} \\
&= 0.0041
\end{align*}
$$

This is less than 0.01, so the answer is **A**.
