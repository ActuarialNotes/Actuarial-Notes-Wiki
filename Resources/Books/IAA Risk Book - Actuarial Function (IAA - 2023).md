A chapter of the IAA Risk Book on the actuarial function (AF), approved by the IAA's Insurance Regulation Committee (version 2.0, March 2023). It sets out some of the considerations when setting up, reorganizing and overseeing an AF: its unique role in risk oversight, its commercial and regulatory scope, expectations for its competencies, its position in the organization and internal structure, reliance on its competency, and the provision of effective actuarial oversight.
