# Resolve: Exam MAS-I open findings (155 files, 247 findings closed)

logged resolution, and re-verified each file with a new pass bound to its new bytes. It also wrote

The fixes follow the written policy the Exam P and FM resolve runs used:

- **Concept pages** were corrected to what a cited source says, re-reading the source in this run.
  A missing condition or formula was written in from the source. A claim no source read in either
  run supports was deleted rather than kept.
- **Questions** keep the paper's stem and options word for word, checked against the page images.
  Explanations were corrected step by step, with every number recomputed. No `answer:` changed:
  all 184 already matched CAS's final key.
- **Tags** changed only with a logged finding, never silently.

  and Spring 2019 Q28 (B, E). `answer:` holds one letter, so the app marks the other accepted
  letter wrong. This needs parser and grader support for more than one key.

  link it; the syllabus page doesn't.

  course page. It is cited for notation and section numbering, always beside a free source.
- **Tse 2nd edition.** A 2nd edition (2023) exists; the 2026 outline still cites 2009.

- **Unstable hashes.** NIST e-Handbook pages embed a per-request script, and LibreTexts embeds
