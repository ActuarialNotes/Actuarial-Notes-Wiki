The Society of Actuaries' third edition of its text on US generally accepted accounting principles for insurers, which significantly revises the second edition from 2006 (titled US GAAP for Life Insurers). It offers a comprehensive analysis of US GAAP, and its scope has expanded to cover property and casualty insurance. It is designed to assist actuaries, accountants and other professionals who help prepare and review insurance company financial statements submitted in accordance with GAAP.
