Given the following information:

| Accident Year | Cumulative Loss Payments, 12 Months | Cumulative Loss Payments, 24 Months | Cumulative Loss Payments, 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 1,500 | 2,700 | 3,450 |
| 2014 | 1,600 | 2,740 | |
| 2015 | 1,700 | | |

- Exposures and premium are constant across all accident years.
- There is no development beyond 36 months.

## Part a (2 points)

Calculate the total reserve indication as of December 31, 2015 using loss-ratio based payout factors and the Benktander method.

### Explanation
Premium is constant, so any premium can be assumed; take 5,000 per accident year. The loss-ratio payout increments are the average incremental payments in each development period divided by premium:

$$M_1 = \frac{(1{,}500 + 1{,}600 + 1{,}700)/3}{5{,}000} = 0.32$$

$$M_2 = \frac{(1{,}200 + 1{,}140)/2}{5{,}000} = 0.234$$

$$M_3 = \frac{750}{5{,}000} = 0.15$$

Expected loss ratio $= 0.32 + 0.234 + 0.15 = 0.704$, so the a priori ultimate is $U^0 = 0.704 \times 5{,}000 = 3{,}520$.

Payout factors:

$$p_1 = \frac{0.32}{0.704} = 0.455, \qquad q_1 = 1 - 0.455 = 0.545$$

$$p_2 = \frac{0.32 + 0.234}{0.704} = 0.787, \qquad q_2 = 1 - 0.787 = 0.213$$

Individual (chain-ladder) and collective (expected) reserves:

$$R_{2014}^{ind} = \frac{2{,}740}{0.787} \times 0.213 = 742, \qquad R_{2015}^{ind} = \frac{1{,}700}{0.455} \times 0.545 = 2{,}036$$

$$R_{2014}^{coll} = 3{,}520 \times 0.213 = 750, \qquad R_{2015}^{coll} = 3{,}520 \times 0.545 = 1{,}918$$

The Benktander reserve credibility-weights them with $Z = p$:

$$R_{2014}^{GB} = 742 \times 0.787 + 750 \times 0.213 = 743$$

$$R_{2015}^{GB} = 2{,}036 \times 0.455 + 1{,}918 \times 0.545 = 1{,}972$$

Total reserve $= 743 + 1{,}972 = 2{,}715$.

Alternatively:

Work in dollars rather than loss ratios. Average incremental payments are 1,600 (0–12), 1,170 (12–24) and 750 (24–36), so $U^0 = 1{,}600 + 1{,}170 + 750 = 3{,}520$, $p_1 = 1{,}600/3{,}520 = 0.455$ and $p_2 = 2{,}770/3{,}520 = 0.787$. Iterate the BF method twice:

- 1st iteration ultimate: 2014 $= 3{,}520 \times 0.213 + 2{,}740 = 3{,}490$; 2015 $= 3{,}520 \times 0.545 + 1{,}700 = 3{,}618$
- 2nd iteration ultimate: 2014 $= 3{,}490 \times 0.213 + 2{,}740 = 3{,}483$; 2015 $= 3{,}618 \times 0.545 + 1{,}700 = 3{,}672$

Benktander reserve $= 3{,}483 + 3{,}672 - 2{,}740 - 1{,}700 = 2{,}715$.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to produce loss ratio based payout factors and apply those factors in conjunction with the Benktander method. Many candidates struggled to produce loss ratio based payout factors and instead used a weighted average. Candidates also struggled to produce the appropriate a priori loss estimate. Many assumed a value of 3,450 since the oldest year was fully developed. However, this fails to recognize that payments for more recent years are emerging higher than corresponding payments for 2013. Once the payout factors and a priori estimate were derived, most candidates were able to compute the outstanding losses using the Benktander method.

## Part b (0.75 points)

Calculate the fifth-iteration Benktander method reserve indication for accident year 2015.

### Explanation
For AY 2015 the expected-cost (collective) reserve at the loss ratio of 70.4% is

$$R_{2015}^{EC} = 5{,}000 \times 70.4\% - 1{,}700 = 1{,}820$$

The $m$-th iteration reserve is $R^{(m)} = (1 - q^m)\,R^{ind} + q^m\,R^{EC}$, so with $m = 5$ and $q = 0.545$:

$$R^{(5)} = 2{,}036 \times (1 - 0.545^5) + 1{,}820 \times 0.545^5 = 2{,}025.6$$

Alternatively:

Keep iterating the AY 2015 ultimate from part a ($U = 1{,}700 + 0.545 \times U_{\text{previous}}$):

- 2nd iteration: $3{,}618 \times 0.545 = 1{,}918$; $1{,}918 + 1{,}700 = 3{,}672$
- 3rd iteration: $3{,}672 \times 0.545 = 2{,}001$; $2{,}001 + 1{,}700 = 3{,}701$
- 4th iteration: $3{,}701 \times 0.545 = 2{,}017$; $2{,}017 + 1{,}700 = 3{,}717$
- 5th iteration: $3{,}717 \times 0.545 = 2{,}026$; $2{,}026 + 1{,}700 = 3{,}726$

Reserve = 5th-iteration ultimate minus paid $= 3{,}726 - 1{,}700 = 2{,}726$ (as printed in the examiner's report).

### Examiner Report
Many candidates failed to understand that the Benktander method produces the second iteration reserve – not the first. Because of this, many candidates calculated the sixth iteration reserve and received partial credit.

## Part c (0.75 points)

Assuming $Var[U_i] = Var[U_i^{BC}]$, use Hürlimann's method for optimal credibility and minimum variance to calculate the reserve indication for accident year 2015.

### Explanation
With $Var[U_i] = Var[U_i^{BC}]$, Hürlimann's optimal credibility weight is

$$Z = \frac{p_1}{p_1 + \sqrt{p_1}} = \frac{0.455}{0.455 + \sqrt{0.455}} = 0.403$$

$$R_{2015} = Z \times R_{2015}^{ind} + (1 - Z) \times R_{2015}^{coll} = 0.403 \times 2{,}036 + 0.597 \times 1{,}918 = 1{,}966$$

### Examiner Report
The majority of candidates performed very well, receiving full credit.
