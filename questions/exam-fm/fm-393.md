An annual perpetuity-due pays 20,000 for the first five years and 50,000 thereafter.

At an annual effective interest rate of 3% for the first five years and $i$ thereafter, the present value of the perpetuity is 1,000,000.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 4.50%
- B) 4.75%
- C) 5.00%
- D) 5.50%
- E) 5.85%

## Explanation

The five payments of 20,000 at times 0 to 4 are valued at 3%. The payments of 50,000 from time 5 on form a perpetuity-due valued at $i$ as of time 5, which is then discounted to time 0 at 3%:

$$
\begin{align*}
1{,}000{,}000 &= 20{,}000\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{5}|0.03} + (1.03)^{-5}\,50{,}000\,\ddot{a}_{\overline{\infty}|i} \\
              &= 94{,}341.968 + (1.03)^{-5}\,50{,}000\,\frac{1+i}{i}
\end{align*}
$$

Solving for $i$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{50{,}000(1+i)}{i} &= (1{,}000{,}000 - 94{,}341.968)(1.03)^{5} \\
  &= 1{,}049{,}905.88 \\
\frac{1+i}{i} &= 20.99812 \\
\frac{1}{i} &= 19.99812 \\
i &= 0.0500
\end{align*}
$$
