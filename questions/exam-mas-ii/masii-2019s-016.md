Your company implements a new marketing strategy for agents. It is implemented under the direction of two marketing directors who each lead the agents in two different states. You build a Linear Mixed Model to model the policy growth over the next year after this new strategy is implemented. Below is the model output from this Linear Mixed Model.

**Solution for Fixed Effects**

| Effect | Location | Estimate | Standard Error |
|--------|----------|----------|----------------|
| Intercept | | 86.75 | 61.39 |
| Location | Rural | -272.71 | 63.82 |
| Location | Town | -179.56 | 47.62 |
| Location | City | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| Tenure | | -14.66 | 2.88 |
| Beginning Policies | | 0.33 | 0.07 |

**Solution for Random Effects**

| Effect | Director | State | Estimate | Standard Error Prediction |
|--------|----------|-------|----------|---------------------------|
| Intercept | D1 | | -21.66 | 32.39 |
| Intercept | D1 | S1 | 5.85 | 15.94 |
| Intercept | D1 | S2 | 1.89 | 15.93 |
| Intercept | D2 | | 21.66 | 32.39 |
| Intercept | D2 | S3 | -3.13 | 15.93 |
| Intercept | D2 | S4 | 7.10 | 15.92 |

Consider an agent with the following characteristics:

| Location | Tenure | Beginning Policies | Director | State |
|----------|--------|--------------------|----------|-------|
| Town | 2 | 921 | D1 | S2 |

Calculate the expected policy growth for this agent over this time period according to this Linear Mixed Model.

- A) Less than 140
- B) At least 140 but less than 150
- C) At least 150 but less than 160
- D) At least 160 but less than 170
- E) At least 170

## Explanation

The conditional prediction adds the fixed-effect contributions to the random effects of the agent's own director and state (states are nested within directors, so both levels apply).

**Fixed effects:**

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Intercept} &= 86.75 \\
\text{Location (Town)} &= -179.56 \\
\text{Tenure} &= -14.66 \times 2 = -29.32 \\
\text{Beginning Policies} &= 0.33 \times 921 = 303.93
\end{align*}
$$

**Random effects** for Director D1 and State S2 within D1:

$$
\begin{align*}
u_{D1} &= -21.66 \\
u_{D1,S2} &= 1.89
\end{align*}
$$

**Total:**

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{Y} &= 86.75 - 179.56 - 29.32 + 303.93 - 21.66 + 1.89 \\
        &= 162.03
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[160,\, 170)$, so the answer is **D**.
