A Bank Negara Malaysia special article on the concepts of general takaful and the operation of general takaful business in Malaysia. Its first part, "General Takaful: A Conceptual Framework" (the title the CAS cites it by), introduces takaful as Shariah-compliant insurance, its underlying contracts and how it differs from general insurance; the second gives the Malaysian industry at a glance, with figures to 2005; the third follows the operation of the business through its pre-operational prerequisites, the pre-contract stage and the post-contract management of the takaful fund.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6I (CAS)|Exam 6I]] — objectives A1–A2; pp. 2–5, 8–10, 14–17, 25–26.
