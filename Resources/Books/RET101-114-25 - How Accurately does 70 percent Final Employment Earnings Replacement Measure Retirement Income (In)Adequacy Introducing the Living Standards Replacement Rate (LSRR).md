An ASTIN Bulletin paper that uses Statistics Canada's LifePaths microsimulation model to test whether workers from the 1951-1958 Canadian birth cohort who reach roughly a 70% final employment earnings replacement rate keep their living standards after retirement. It finds the conventional measure has little predictive value, with a correlation of 0.11 to living standards continuity, and proposes the Living Standards Replacement Rate (LSRR) as a more accurate, understandable and consistent measure of retirement income adequacy.
