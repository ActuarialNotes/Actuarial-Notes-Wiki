A company is required to pay 500,000 ten years from now and 500,000 fifteen years from now. The company needs to create an investment portfolio using 5-year and 20-year zero-coupon bonds, so that, using a 7% annual force of interest, the present value and Macaulay duration of its assets match those of its liabilities.

Calculate the amount invested today in each bond.

- A) 211,631 for the 5-year bond and 211,631 for the 20-year bond
- B) 217,699 for the 5-year bond and 217,699 for the 20-year bond
- C) 223,852 for the 5-year bond and 199,410 for the 20-year bond
- D) 229,857 for the 5-year bond and 205,540 for the 20-year bond
- E) 248,293 for the 5-year bond and 174,969 for the 20-year bond

## Explanation

Let $x$ and $y$ be the amounts invested in the five-year and twenty-year bonds respectively. To match the present values:

$$
\begin{align*}
x + y &= 500{,}000e^{-0.07(10)} + 500{,}000e^{-0.07(15)} \\
      &= 248{,}293 + 174{,}969 \\
      &= 423{,}262
\end{align*}
$$

To match the Macaulay durations, note that the denominators of the durations for assets and liabilities are the same (the present values just matched), so the time-weighted present values must match. A zero-coupon bond's only payment is at its maturity, so:

$$
\begin{align*}
5x + 20y &= 500{,}000(10)e^{-0.07(10)} + 500{,}000(15)e^{-0.07(15)} \\
         &= 5{,}107{,}460
\end{align*}
$$

Subtracting five times the first equation, $5(423{,}262) = 2{,}116{,}310$, from the second:

$$
\begin{align*}
15y &= 2{,}991{,}150 \\
y &= 199{,}410 \\
x &= 423{,}262 - 199{,}410 \\
  &= 223{,}852
\end{align*}
$$
