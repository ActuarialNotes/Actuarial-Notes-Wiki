A McKinsey Quarterly article of June 2025 on operating models. It argues that strategy alone does not guarantee performance: McKinsey's research finds a 30 percent gap between strategy's full potential and what is delivered, attributed to shortcomings in operating models. It proposes "Organize to Value", a dynamic system of 12 elements that replaces the older 7-S framework, and draws on a survey of 2,000 executives.
