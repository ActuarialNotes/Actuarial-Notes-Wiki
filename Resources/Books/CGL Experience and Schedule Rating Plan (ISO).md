Insurance Services Office's multistate Commercial General Liability Experience and Schedule Rating Plan, as issued in Manual Notice GL-MU-2019-CGLES-001. The CAS content outline cites it in that form, says candidates are not required to memorize its details but will be expected to be able to use them on the examination, and says the entire manual will be provided both in the study kit and on the exam.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 8 (CAS)|Exam 8]] — objectives B5–B6; the entire manual, provided in the study kit and on the exam: candidates are not required to memorize the details but will be expected to be able to use them

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> No copy of this manual is published online; the CAS supplies the entire manual in the Exam 8 study kit and again on the exam. This page records its citation and assignment from the content outline.
