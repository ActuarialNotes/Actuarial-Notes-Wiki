Given the information below for a property and casualty insurance company:

| Line of Business | Net Unearned Premium Reserve (\$000s) | Selected Undiscounted Loss Ratio | Discount Factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Auto (excl Facility) | \$8,000 | 70% | 0.90 |
| Personal Property | \$4,000 | 65% | 1.05 |
| Facility | \$300 | 120% | 1.00 |

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Selected Maintenance Expense Ratio | 2.5% |
| Selected Internal Adjustment Expense Ratio | 10% |
| Selected Contingent Commission Ratio | 1% |
| Discount from Average Accident Date to the Evaluation Date | 0.98 |

Calculate the maximum deferred policy acquisition costs that the company can record on its financial statements.

### Explanation
Equity in unearned premium = UPR - losses on UPR (disc) - maintenance exp - internal adj exp - contingent comm:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{EQUP} &= (8{,}000 + 4{,}000 + 300) - (8{,}000 \times 0.7 \times 0.9 + 4{,}000 \times 0.65 \times 1.05 + 300 \times 1.2 \times 1) \\
&\quad - (8{,}000 + 4{,}000) \times 0.025 \times 0.98 - (8{,}000 \times 0.7 \times 0.9 + 4{,}000 \times 0.65 \times 1.05) \times 0.1 - (8{,}000 + 4{,}000) \times 0.01 \\
&= 2{,}979
\end{aligned}$$

Since EQUP is positive, no premium deficiency exists. So the maximum DPAC that can be recorded is $2979 + 0 = 2979$.

Alternatively, term by term:

- Unearned Premium $= 8{,}000 + 4{,}000 + 300 = 12{,}300$
- Unpaid claims discounted $= 8{,}000 \times 0.7 \times 0.9 + 4{,}000 \times 0.65 \times 1.05 + 300 \times 1.2 \times 1 = 8{,}130$
- Maintenance discounted $= (8{,}000 + 4{,}000) \times 0.025 \times 0.98 = 294$
- Internal Adjustment Expense $= (8{,}000 \times 0.7 \times 0.9 + 4{,}000 \times 0.65 \times 1.05) \times 0.1 = 777$
- Contingent Commission $= (8{,}000 + 4{,}000) \times 0.01 = 120$
- EQUP $= 2{,}979$
- Maximum DPAC $=$ EQUP $= 2{,}979$
