A 100,000 loan has an annual nominal interest rate of 8% convertible quarterly. The loan will be repaid with quarterly payments and the first payment is due three months from the date of the loan.

For the first five years each payment will be 2500. All payments thereafter will be 5000 except for a final balloon payment, which will be less than 10,000.

Calculate the balloon payment.

- A) 7920
- B) 8078
- C) 9056
- D) 9154
- E) 9237

## Explanation

The quarterly rate is $j = 8\%/4 = 2\%$. The outstanding balance at time 5 years, after 20 payments of 2500, is

$$
\begin{align*}
OB_{20} &= 100{,}000(1.02)^{20} - 2{,}500\,s_{\overline{20}|0.02} \\
        &= 148{,}594.74 - 2{,}500(24.29737) \\
        &= 148{,}594.74 - 60{,}743.42 \\
        &= 87{,}851.32
\end{align*}
$$

If the rest of the loan were repaid entirely with payments of 5000, the number of them, $m$, would satisfy

$$
87{,}851.32 = 5{,}000\,a_{\overline{m}|0.02}
$$

which gives $m = 21.86$ (BA II Plus: $I/Y = 2$, $PV = 87{,}851.32$, $PMT = -5{,}000$, $FV = 0$, CPT $N$). Since the final payment is a balloon, larger than 5000, use $m = 21$: there are 20 payments of 5000, and the 21st payment is $5{,}000 + B$, where

$$
\begin{align*}
87{,}851.32 &= 5{,}000\,a_{\overline{21}|0.02} + Bv^{21} \\
87{,}851.32 &= 85{,}056.05 + B(1.02)^{-21} \\
B &= 2{,}795.27(1.02)^{21} \\
  &= 4{,}236.70
\end{align*}
$$

So the balloon payment is $5{,}000 + 4{,}236.70 = 9{,}236.70$, which is less than 10,000. Answer E.

($B = 4{,}236.70$ is the balance left after 21 payments of 5000; paying it one quarter later instead would be a drop payment of $4{,}236.70(1.02) = 4{,}321.43$, less than 5000, which is not what the question describes.)
