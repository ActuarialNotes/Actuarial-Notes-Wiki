# Exam F2GB
**F2GB – Group Benefits Product Pricing** is an exam of the group benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques of pricing that are relevant to group insurance, including techniques to estimate the key components of a technical price.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of group insurance.
>
> 3. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions and to develop appropriate proposals or recommendations relating to pricing group insurance products.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in [[Group Benefit|group benefits]] and [[Health Insurance Pricing|health insurance pricing]]. (A1)

> [!example]- Experience studies and predictive modelling {20%}
>
> 1. Explain and apply the relevancy and limitations of actuarial [[Experience Study|experience studies]] available for [[Group Insurance|group insurance]] ([[Industry Table|industry tables]] and information: [[Life Mortality|life mortality]], waiver of [[Insurance Premium|premiums]], [[Long-Term Disability|long-term disability]] [LTD] termination, [[Rate Comparison|rate comparisons]], [[Expense Benchmark|expense benchmarks]], [[Life Insurance Market Research Association]], etc.). (C3)
> 2. Relative to 1., explain reasons for selecting applicable tables and their sources and characteristics: (D2)
>    - a. CIA, Krieger, GLTD, CPP, QPP.
>    - b. [[LTD Incidence Rate|LTD incidence rates]] (Have and Garand).
> 3. Compare and contrast the differences between the [[CIA's Predictive Analytics|CIA’s Predictive Analytics]] Model for [[Canadian Group Disability Termination Experience]] and the [[Group Long-term Disability Termination Study]]. (D5)
> 4. Evaluate the relevancy and limitations of [[Non-Actuarial Experience Study|non-actuarial experience studies]] available for [[Group Insurance|group insurance]], such as [[Régie De L'assurance Maladie Du Québec|Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec]] (RAMQ) data for [[Pharmacy Benefit|prescription drugs]]. (D5)
> 5. Analyze the sources, relevancy, and limitations of [[Trend|trends]] and [[Forecasts Study|forecasts studies]] available for [[Group Insurance|group insurance]] ([[Economic Forecast|economic forecasts]], [[Disability Incidence|disability incidence]], and [[Health-Trend Data|health-trend data]]) for the following: (C4)
>    - a. [[RAMQ Data|RAMQ data]] for [[Pharmacy Benefit|prescription drugs]].
>    - b. [[Economic Trends Published Quarterly|Economic trends published quarterly]] by [[Chartered Bank|chartered banks]].
>    - c. [[Annual Drug-Trend Study|Annual drug-trend studies]] produced by [[Pharmacy Benefits Manager|pharmacy benefits managers]].
>    - d. [[Annual Health-Care-Cost-Trend Study|Annual health-care-cost-trend studies]] by major [[Consulting Firm|consulting firms]].
> 6. Perform calculations for [[Claim|claims]] models for various types of coverage: (C3)
>    - a. Models for health and [[Dental Benefit|dental benefits]].
>    - b. Models for [[Stop-Loss Claim|stop-loss claims]].
> 7. Outline expense models for various types of coverage. ([[B2]])
> 8. Describe how [[Predictive Analytics|predictive modelling]] techniques can be used for [[Claims Analytics|claims analytics]]. (C3)

> [!example]- Credibility {10%}
>
> 1. Explain and outline the principles underlying the notion of [[Credibility|credibility]] in [[Group Insurance|group insurance]]. (D2)
> 2. Explain the variability of the notion of [[Credibility|credibility]] in the [[Group Insurance Market|group insurance market]]. (C2)
> 3. Analyze the factors affecting the [[Credibility|credibility]] of a particular group ([[Statistical Heterogeneity|statistical heterogeneity]], etc.). (B4)
> 4. Assess [[Utilization|utilization]] of [[Industry Statistics|industry statistics]] and [[Company Experience|company experience]]. (C5)
> 5. Develop and apply [[Credibility Formulae|credibility formulae]] to relevant calculations. (C4)
> 6. Evaluate [[Credibility|credibility]] in the context of predictive models (C5).
> 7. Compare traditional actuarial models with [[Predictive Analytics|predictive modelling]] techniques. (C4)

> [!example]- Capital requirements {15%}
>
> 1. Describe the principles underlying the determination of [[Regulatory Capital|regulatory capital]], in particular [[OSFI's Life Insurance Capital Adequacy|OSFI’s Life Insurance Capital Adequacy]] Test (LICAT), including the following: (C4)
>    - a. Identification of significant [[Risk Component|risk components]].
>    - b. Identification of [[Specialized Product LICAT|specialized product LICAT]] requirements; and
>    - c. Interpreting results from a regulatory perspective.
> 2. Outline and calculate the [[Capital|capital]] impacts of [[Funding Structure|funding structures]] (refund accounting with [[Claims Fluctuation Reserve|claims fluctuation reserves]], stop-loss, [[Large Amount Pooling|large amount pooling]], [[Hold-Harmless Agreement|hold-harmless agreements]], etc.). (C4)

> [!example]- Product pricing {25%}
>
> 1. Outline and apply the [[Underwriting Process|underwriting process]]. (C3)
> 2. Evaluate how [[Predictive Analytics|predictive analytics]] techniques can be used to estimate [[Trend|trends]]. (D5)
> 3. Identify [[Risk-Mitigation Strategy|risk-mitigation strategies]] (such as [[Pre-Existing Condition Exclusion|pre-existing condition exclusions]]). (B2)
> 4. Analyze the features, provisions, and [[Pricing Structure|pricing structures]] of [[Flexible Benefit|flexible benefits]]. (C4)
> 5. Identify, compare, and contrast [[Pricing Variable|pricing variables]] relevant to [[Group Insurance|group insurance]] by product ([[Demographic Variable|demographic variables]], variables that vary from one group to another, variables that vary from one [[Insurer|insurer]] to another, [[Economic Variable|economic variables]], etc.). (D5)
> 6. Apply relevant [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|standards of practice]]. (C3)
> 7. Create a [[Medical Cost Trend|medical-cost-trend experience]] analysis. (C6)
> 8. Develop and recommend assumptions. ([[C6]])
> 9. Calculate and recommend a [[Manual Rate|manual rate]]. (D6)
> 10. Apply [[Actual vs Expected Analysis|critical metrics]] to evaluate actual vs. expected results. (C3)

> [!example]- Profitability measures {10%}
>
> 1. Outline and calculate the following profitability measures: (C3)
>    - a. [[Return on Capital|Return on capital]].
>    - b. Return on equity.
>    - c. Percent of [[Insurance Premium|premium]].
>    - d. Percent of assets.
>    - e. Earnings per share.
>    - f. Types of embedded value (“traditional,” “European,” “[[Market Consistent|market consistent]]”).
> 2. Outline and explain the uses of embedded value. ([[B5]])
> 3. Outline and explain the sources of earnings. ([[B5]])
> 4. Perform benchmarking. ([[C5]])

>

> 2. Identify and analyze issues associated with self-insurance. ([[B4]])
> 3. Perform calculations relative to 1. and 2. above. ([[C3]])

> [!example]- Insurance company taxation {10%}
>
> 1. Describe the following [[Insurance Company Taxation Feature|insurance company taxation features]]: (B2)
>    - a. [[Tax Reserve|Tax reserves]].
>    - b. [[Investment Income Tax|Investment income tax]] (IIT).
>    - c. [[Income Tax|Income tax]].
>    - d. [[Claims Fluctuation Reserve|Claims fluctuation reserve]] (CFR) allowable; and
>    - e. [[Premium Tax|Premium taxes]].
> 2. Perform calculations relative to 1. above. ([[C3]])
