An article in The Actuary, February 2021, explaining how patents function in the pharmaceutical industry of the United States. It covers the two types of intellectual property protection for pharmaceutical inventions (patent exclusivity and the FDA's regulatory exclusivity), the economic justification of patents, a financial analysis of patents in the industry, and previous reforms of pharmaceutical patents.
