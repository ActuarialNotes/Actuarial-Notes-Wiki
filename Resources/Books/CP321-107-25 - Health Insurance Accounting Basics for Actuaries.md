A Society of Actuaries study note on the basics of health insurance accounting for actuaries. According to the SOA's course strategy guide for CP 321, it was written in 2018 and serves as the basic text for the insurer financial statements topic: it discusses financial concepts of date-of-service versus financial statement and of management reporting versus tax reporting, then describes GAAP versus statutory accounting, and its section 7 discusses IFRS.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Outcomes 4a-4d; Sections 4-7.

> No copy of this study note is published online; the SOA sells its study notes through its online store. This page records its citation and assignment from the syllabus and the course strategy guide.
