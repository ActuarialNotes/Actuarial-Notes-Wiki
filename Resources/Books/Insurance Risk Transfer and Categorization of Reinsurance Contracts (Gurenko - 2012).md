A World Bank Policy Research Working Paper, December 2012, that develops a simple, affordable and robust regulatory method to help insurance regulators categorize financial reinsurance contracts as reinsurance or as financial instruments. By reviewing real examples of different categorization methods, it explains how the proposed method standardizes such categorization, and it summarizes the existing literature on the subject.
