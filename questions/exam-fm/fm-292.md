An investor purchases a 30-year bond with a face amount of 1000 and annual coupon rate of 8% paid semi-annually. The bond is callable at its face value any time following the coupon payment occurring at the end of the 15th year.

The bond is bought at a premium based on an annual effective yield rate of 7%.

Calculate the purchase price of the bond.

- A) 1067
- B) 1104
- C) 1132
- D) 1141
- E) 1154

## Explanation

The bond is bought at a premium (its semiannual coupon of $1000(0.04) = 40$ exceeds $1000j = 34.41$ at the semiannual yield rate $j$ below), and a callable bond is priced assuming redemption at the date least favourable to the investor. For a premium bond redeemable at par that is the earliest possible call date: simultaneous with the coupon at the end of year 15. The cash flows are then semiannual coupons of 40 for 15 years (30 half-years) and 1000 at the end of year 15.

Semiannual yield rate:

$$1.07 = \left(1+\frac{i^{(2)}}{2}\right)^2 \implies j = \frac{i^{(2)}}{2} = 1.07^{1/2} - 1 = 0.034408$$

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 40a_{\overline{30}|0.034408} + 1000(1.034408)^{-30} \\
  &= 40(18.5292) + 1000(0.36245) \\
  &= 741.17 + 362.45 \\
  &= 1103.61 \approx 1104
\end{align*}
$$

Priced to maturity at year 30 instead, the bond would cost 1141.17 (option D); a buyer who paid that and saw the bond called at year 15 would earn less than 7%.
