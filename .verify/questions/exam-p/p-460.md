## [F-001] Explanation misattributes distractor D with false arithmetic (0.08/0.10 is 4/5, not 3/4)
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: major
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, final paragraph
- claim: Choice (D), 3/4, is $P[N=1]/P[N>0] = 0.08/0.10$ — the answer if the claim-amount information were ignored.
- evidence: Arithmetic: 0.08/0.10 = 0.8 = 4/5, which is not among the options (1/2, 3/5, 2/3, 3/4, 6/7 per questions PDF p.187 image). The value 3/4 is P[X <= 2000 | N = 1] = 1 - 500/2000, i.e. the conditional CDF for one claim that the explanation itself computes in its first display and that SOA solution p.128 writes as 0.08(3/4). A student reading the note learns a false equation and a wrong account of why D is tempting. The keyed answer E = 6/7 and the main derivation (0.06/0.07) are correct and agree with SOA.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Human rewrite of the distractor note: D (3/4) is P[X <= 2000 | N = 1] alone, forgetting to weight by the claim-count probabilities; ignoring the claim-amount information would give P[N=1 | N>0] = 0.08/0.10 = 4/5, which is not offered.
