A privately held insurer prepares financial statements under U.S. GAAP. Which source is authoritative GAAP for it?

- A) The FASB Accounting Standards Codification
- B) An Accounting Standards Update's basis for conclusions
- C) FASB Concepts Statement No. 8
- D) The SEC's rules and interpretive releases

## Explanation

Since FASB Statement No. 168, now Topic 105, the Codification is the source of authoritative GAAP recognized by the FASB for nongovernmental entities, and all of its content carries the same authority. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Accounting Standards Updates are not authoritative in their own right; they update the Codification and explain the change.
- **C** — FASB Concepts Statements are listed among nonauthoritative sources.
- **D** — the SEC's rules and interpretive releases are authoritative GAAP only for SEC registrants, which a privately held insurer is not.
