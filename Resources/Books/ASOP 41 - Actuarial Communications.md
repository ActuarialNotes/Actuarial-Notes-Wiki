The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries on actuarial communications, applying within any practice area. This revision (Doc. No. 120) was adopted in December 2010 to make the standard clearer and less confusing in wording and structure, and is effective for actuarial communications issued on or after May 1, 2011.

## Transmittal Memorandum
- The version then in effect had been in force for eight years and applied to all U.S. actuaries in all areas of practice; the ASB had received comments about a lack of clarity and confusion in its wording and structure.
- The ASB issued a first exposure draft in September 2008 and received twenty-three comment letters.

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
    - Applies to actuaries issuing [[Actuarial Communication|actuarial communications]] within any practice area; it does not apply to communications that include no actuarial opinion or other actuarial findings, such as brochures, fee quotes or invoices.
    - Where other guidance contains communication requirements additional to or inconsistent with this standard, those requirements supersede it.
- 1.3 Cross References
- 1.4 Effective Date

## 2 Definitions
- 2.1 Actuarial Communication
    - A written, electronic, or oral communication issued by an actuary with respect to actuarial services.
- 2.2 Actuarial Document
- 2.3 Actuarial Finding
- 2.4 Actuarial Report
    - The set of actuarial documents that the actuary determines to be relevant to specific actuarial findings that is available to an intended user.
- 2.5 Actuarial Services
- 2.6 Deviation
    - The act of departing from the guidance of an ASOP.
- 2.7 Intended User
- 2.8 Oral Communication
- 2.9 Other User
- 2.10 Principal
    - A client or employer of the actuary.
