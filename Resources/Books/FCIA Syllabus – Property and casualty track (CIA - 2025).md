This document outlines the property and casualty (PC)-track exams and modules syllabuses. The Canadian Institute of Actuaries' syllabus for one of the five education tracks to its Fellowship (FCIA) designation: the track's 3 exams — each an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses, with the weight of each topic in parentheses and the level of each objective on the revised Bloom's taxonomy — and its two online modules.

## Property and casualty track exams
- Exam F1PC: Pricing
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Regulations and applicable standards (15%)
    - 3. Data (10%)
    - 4. Catastrophe modelling (5%)
    - 5. Pricing – overall rate level indication (25%)
    - 6. Pricing – segmentation (25%)
    - 7. Pricing – large account pricing (5%)
    - 8. Pricing – reinsurance (10%)
    - 9. Portfolio analytics (5%)
- Exam F2PC: Estimating Unpaid Claims and Financial Reporting under IFRS 17 - Insurance Contracts
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Regulations and applicable standards (15%)
    - 3. Data (10%)
    - 4. Estimating unpaid claims – basic topics (25%)
    - 5. Estimation of unpaid claims – advanced topics (20%)
    - 6. IFRS17 financial reporting (30%)
- Exam F3PC: ERM, Economic Capital Modelling, and Stress and Scenario Testing
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. ERM concept, framework, and process (10%)
    - 3. Risk categories and risk identification (10%)
    - 4. Economic capital modelling and aggregation of risks (25%)
    - 5. Risk measurement and assessment (30%)
    - 6. Stress and scenario testing (10%)
    - 7. Capital management (15%)
