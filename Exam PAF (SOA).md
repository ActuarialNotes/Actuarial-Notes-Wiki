# PAF
Welcome to the Pre-Actuarial Foundations Module! As you embark on this journey of discovery, we hope you will learn many important concepts and skills that will help you in your future actuarial career. The concepts you will learn in this module include:

- What is the role of the professional actuary – past, present, and future?
- What do actuaries do in their day-to-day work?
- How do actuaries use soft skills such as emotional intelligence in their work?
- How do actuaries use emotional intelligence in leadership roles?

> [!question]- About the module
>
> This module also contains an End-of-Module Test, an Interactive Scenario, and an End-of-Module Assessment.
>
> **Module Objectives**
>
> - Explain what actuaries are
> - Describe what actuaries do
> - Explain how emotional intelligence benefits you as an actuary
> - Explain how emotional intelligence benefits you as a leader

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Role of the Professional Actuary
> The purpose of this topic is to provide a [[Brief History|brief history]] of the actuarial profession, to consider the future of the profession, to explore the role of [[Today's Actuary|today’s actuary]], to detail the [[Actuary's Skill Set|actuary’s skill sets]] and to review the [[Profession's Code|profession’s codes]] of conduct.
>
> 1. Describe important historical [[Events Influencing|events influencing]] the actuarial profession
> 2. Describe today’s and [[Tomorrow'|tomorrow's]] actuarial practice
> 3. Define “[[Actuary|actuary]]”
> 4. Identify the actuary’s knowledge, skills and [[Ability|abilities]]
> 5. Describe what an [[Actuary Contribute|actuary contributes]] as a professional

> [!example]- What Actuaries Do
> What do [[Actuary|actuaries]] do, day-to-day? How do they identify, assess, and manage [[Risk|risk]] in various areas of specialization or function? How do they reduce [[Financial Insecurity|financial insecurity]]? How do they protect individuals, companies, and other organizations from unforeseen or burdensome financial effects of random, [[Contingent Event|contingent events]]? And how do they communicate most effectively to bring about their desired results? [[Actuaries Focus|Actuaries focus]] on understanding the objectives of the [[Financial Security System|financial security system]]. They understand that, in their work, tradeoffs are often needed. There are [[Many Stakeholder|many stakeholders]] with [[Disparate Need|disparate needs]] associated with [[Modern Financial Security System|modern financial security systems]]. [[Actuary|Actuaries]] must work with the available products, plans, and programs to design policies to meet the [[Stakeholders' Need|stakeholders’ needs]], whether within a private, public, or [[Governmental Financial Security System|governmental financial security system]].
>
> 1. Define [[Risk|risk]].
> 2. Explain how [[Financial Security Systems Interact|financial security systems interact]] to [[Combat Financial Insecurity|combat financial insecurity]]
> 3. Explain ways in which [[Actuary|actuaries]] manage [[Risk|risk]]
> 4. Describe the [[Actuary's Contribution|actuary’s contributions]] within each area of practice
> 5. Identify [[Services Actuary|services actuaries]] provide for [[Financial Security System|financial security systems]]
> 6. Describe how [[Actuary|actuaries]] use the Actuarial Control Cycle to manage [[Risk|risk]]
> 7. Explain why it is crucial for [[Professional Actuary|professional actuaries]] to be [[Effective Communicator|effective communicators]]

> [!example]- Building Your Emotional Intelligence Skills
> [[Actuaries Need High Emotional Intelligence Skill|Actuaries need high emotional intelligence skills]], as well as [[Technical Skill|technical skills]], to be successful in their work. This section will [[Introduce You|introduce you]] to the topic of [[Emotional Intelligence]], the Reflect-Adapt-Relate model, and how [[Emotional Intelligence]] will [[Help You|help you]] to become a [[Better Actuary|better actuary]].
>
> 1. Define [[Emotional Intelligence|emotional intelligence]] and explain its importance to an [[Actuary's Success|actuary’s success]] in their career
> 2. Plan specific actions to improve [[Your Emotional Intelligence|your emotional intelligence]]
> 3. Use self-awareness techniques to identify [[Emotional Trigger|emotional triggers]]
> 4. Use adaptability approaches to manage [[Impulse|impulses]]
> 5. Use [[Social Awareness|social awareness]] techniques to [[Plan Simple Communication|plan simple communication]]
> 6. Recall the importance of identifying problems and proposing solutions in [[Problem-Solving Process|problem-solving processes]].
> 7. Explain the importance of [[Emotional Intelligence|emotional intelligence]] to an actuarial [[Leader's Success|leader’s success]] in their career
