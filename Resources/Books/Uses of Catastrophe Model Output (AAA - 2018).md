A July 2018 paper of the American Academy of Actuaries' Extreme Events and Property Lines Committee. It gives an overview of how catastrophe models have developed and demonstrates how catastrophe model output can be used in selected actuarial tasks, developing four use cases (ratemaking, loss mitigation, underwriting or risk selection, and reinsurance) for four types of event: hurricane wind, storm surge, inland flood, and tornado and straight-line wind.
