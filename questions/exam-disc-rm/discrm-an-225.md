An underwriter is evaluating employee theft coverage for a distributor. Which practice at the distributor most reduces the employee theft exposure?

- A) The employee who records cash receipts doesn't handle the cash
- B) The office has a safe and a monitored burglar alarm
- C) Payment instructions received by email are confirmed by a callback
- D) The cash on hand at the end of each day is kept below \$1,000

## Explanation

Employee theft is controlled mainly by the insured's internal controls. Separation of duties, so that no one both handles cash and records it, means a theft can't be hidden in the books by the person who committed it. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — A safe and an alarm address burglary and robbery by outsiders, not theft by employees with access.
- **C** — Callback verification addresses funds transfer fraud, where an outsider's instructions trick the business.
- **D** — Limiting cash on hand reduces robbery and burglary losses, but employees can steal property and funds other than cash.
