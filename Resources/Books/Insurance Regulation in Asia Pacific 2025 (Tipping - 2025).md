Norton Rose Fulbright's guide to ten common regulatory issues for insurance companies in 19 Asia Pacific jurisdictions, as at January 1, 2025. It is the eleventh edition. For each country it identifies the regulator, whether branches of foreign insurers are permitted, any restriction on foreign direct investment, the controller regimes and change-of-control approvals, the regulatory capital regime, group supervision, policyholder protection, portfolio transfers and the regulatory oversight of outsourcing.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6I (CAS)|Exam 6I]] — objective A1; only China (pp. 10–11), Hong Kong (pp. 12–14), Malaysia (pp. 30–31) and Singapore (pp. 41–42)
