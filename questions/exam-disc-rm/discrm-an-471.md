A landscaping company with a limited insurance budget ranks its coverages as essential, desirable or available. Which coverage is **desirable** insurance?

- A) Workers compensation, which its state requires it to carry
- B) Liability coverage that its client contracts require it to carry
- C) Coverage on a dump truck whose loss would strain the business but not bankrupt it
- D) Coverage for the occasional theft of hand tools worth a few dollars each

## Explanation

**Desirable** insurance protects against losses that may cause the firm financial difficulty but not bankruptcy. Losing the truck would be a serious setback that the company could survive. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Coverage required by law is **essential** insurance.
- **B** — Coverage required by contract is also **essential** insurance.
- **D** — Coverage for slight losses that would only be an inconvenience is **available** insurance.
