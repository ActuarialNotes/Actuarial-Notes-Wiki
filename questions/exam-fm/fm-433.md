Interest on a 20-year loan is charged based on an annual effective rate of 10.1%.

The loan is repaid by payments of 100 at the end of each of the years 1 through 5 and each of the years 11 through 20. During years 6 through 10 no payments are made.

Calculate the absolute value of the difference between the outstanding balance immediately after the payment in year 5 and the outstanding balance immediately after the payment in year 15.

- A) 0
- B) 142
- C) 144
- D) 234
- E) 378

## Explanation

Each outstanding balance is the present value, at 10.1%, of the payments still to be made.

Immediately after the payment in year 5, the remaining payments are 100 at the end of each of years 11 through 20, a 10-year annuity deferred 5 years:

$$
\begin{align*}
B_5 &= 100\,v^{5}a_{\overline{10}|0.101} \\
    &= 100(0.618107)(6.118259) \\
    &= 378.17
\end{align*}
$$

Immediately after the payment in year 15, the remaining payments are 100 at the end of each of years 16 through 20:

$$
\begin{align*}
B_{15} &= 100\,a_{\overline{5}|0.101} \\
       &= 100(3.781122) \\
       &= 378.11
\end{align*}
$$

The absolute value of the difference is

$$
\begin{align*}
|B_5 - B_{15}| &= |378.17 - 378.11| \\
               &= 0.06
\end{align*}
$$

which rounds to 0.
