An insurance company incorporated under the laws of Ontario, Canada holds a license from the New York Department of Financial Services and writes commercial property business there. From New York's point of view, how is the company classified?

- A) Alien and admitted
- B) Foreign and admitted
- C) Domestic and admitted
- D) Alien and nonadmitted

## Explanation

An **alien** insurer is one formed under the laws of another country, and an **admitted** insurer is one licensed in the state. The company was formed in Canada and holds a New York license, so it is alien and admitted there.

- **B** uses "foreign", which means an insurer domiciled in another U.S. state.
- **C** uses "domestic", which means domiciled in the state itself.
- **D** gets the license wrong: a nonadmitted insurer is not licensed in the state.
