An intermediate-level textbook covering the mathematics of pension funding and valuation, including a discussion of ancillary benefits, contributory plans, assets, and the selection of actuarial assumptions. This is the third edition, published by ACTEX in 2006; ACTEX describes it as a required reading for several SOA exams.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4 and 7 for outcomes 1b and 1c; Chapter 2 also for outcome 3d.
