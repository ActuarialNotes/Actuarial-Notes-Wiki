During the policy period a bicycle retailer runs an online advertisement. Four suits follow. Which one is covered under Coverage B of its CGL (CG 00 01 04 13)?

- A) A manufacturer alleges the retailer's own-brand bicycle infringes its patented gear design
- B) A customer alleges the advertised "lifetime-guaranteed" frame cracked within a year
- C) A competitor alleges the advertisement copies its trademarked slogan
- D) A supplier alleges the retailer breached their contract by not buying the agreed volume

## Explanation

Offense (g) in the definition of personal and advertising injury is infringing another's copyright, trade dress or slogan in the named insured's "advertisement". The intellectual property exclusion gives back infringement in the insured's advertisement of copyright, trade dress or slogan. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — patent infringement is not one of the seven offenses, and exclusion i removes it.
- **B** — exclusion g removes injury from goods failing to conform to a statement of quality or performance in the advertisement.
- **D** — a breach of contract is not an offense, and exclusion f removes injury arising out of a breach of contract.
