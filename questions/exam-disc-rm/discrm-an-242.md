After a hearing, a commissioner finds that an insurer committed 130 violations of the state's version of the NAIC Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Act, none of them flagrant or in conscious disregard of the act. The state uses the model act's penalty amounts. What is the largest monetary penalty the commissioner may impose?

- A) \$100,000
- B) \$130,000
- C) \$250,000
- D) \$3,250,000

## Explanation

For violations that are not flagrant, the model act allows up to \$1,000 per violation with an aggregate cap of \$100,000:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Penalty} &= \min(130 \times 1{,}000,\ 100{,}000) \\
&= 100{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **A**. The commissioner may also order the insurer to cease and desist.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Ignores the aggregate cap.
- **C** — Uses the \$250,000 cap that applies to flagrant violations.
- **D** — Applies the flagrant per-violation amount of \$25,000 with no cap.
