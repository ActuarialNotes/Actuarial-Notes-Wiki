A warehouse has had several forklift collisions, each recorded as "driver error." An analyst asks why each happened, and then why again, and finds that drivers rush because the shift schedule leaves too little time to load trucks before their departure times. Which assessment technique has the analyst used, and what should be treated?

- A) Trend analysis; the rising frequency of collisions over time
- B) Probability analysis; the expected cost of each collision
- C) Root cause analysis; the loading schedule
- D) Risk mapping; the drivers with the most collisions

## Explanation

Asking "why" repeatedly until the answer is something the organization can change is **root cause analysis**. "Driver error" is the symptom; the schedule is the root cause, and changing it prevents a family of collisions rather than only the last one. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Trend analysis examines how frequency and severity change over time; it would show the pattern, not its cause.
- **B** — Probability analysis describes outcomes with a distribution and expected value; it does not trace causes.
- **D** — A risk map plots risks by likelihood and impact. Targeting individual drivers treats the symptom.
