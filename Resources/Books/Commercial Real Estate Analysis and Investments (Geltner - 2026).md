A fully revised fourth edition that presents the foundations of real estate investment analysis with the rigor of general finance and economics, introducing market assumptions, valuation, financial analysis and development. Its topics include value concepts, mortgage analysis, financing alternatives, option value, leverage and risk analysis, and institutional and capital market trends, and the new edition addresses climate risks, alternative property types and the impact of technology on real estate as an asset class. The publisher lists chapters 16 to 29 as online chapters.
