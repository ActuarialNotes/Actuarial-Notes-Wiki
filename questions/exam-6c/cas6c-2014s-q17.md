| Accident Year (01) | Paid Loss Cumulative (03) | Total Undiscounted Unpaid Claims and Adjustment Expense (06) | Present Value of Unpaid Claims and Adjustment Expenses – Total (07) | PFAD Claims (08) | PFAD Reinsurance (10) | PFAD Interest Rate (11) |

| Accident Year (01) | Discounted Reserves including PFAD (12) | Earned Premium (13) | Investment Income from UPR (14) | Cumulative Investment Income from Unpaid Claim Reserve (15) | Loss Ratio Undiscounted (16) | Loss Ratio Discounted (17) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 637 | 4,500 | 12 | 13 | ?? | ?? |
| 2012 | 1,580 | 5,000 | 14 | ?? | ?? | ?? |

Additional information is also available:

- Investment Yield for 2012 = 4%
- Future income tax rate = 35%
- Net claims liabilities amount carried in the balance sheet = 2,500
- There are no reserves related to accident years prior to 2011

## Part a (1.75 points)

Calculate the Undiscounted and Discounted Loss Ratio (columns 16 and 17) for accident years 2011 and 2012.

### Explanation
Undiscounted loss ratios:

$$\text{2011 undiscounted LR} = \frac{1800 + 600}{4500} = 53.33\%$$

$$\text{2012 undiscounted LR} = \frac{1200 + 1500}{5000} = 54\%$$

Discounted loss ratios:

$$\text{2011 discounted LR} = \frac{1800 + 637 - 13}{4500 + 12} = 53.72\%$$

$$\text{Investment Income from Unpaid @ 12 month} = \frac{1580}{2} \times 4\% = 31.6$$

$$\text{2012 discounted LR} = \frac{1200 + 1580 - 31.6}{5000 + 14} = 54.8\%$$

### Examiner Report
- the candidate is expected to know the P&C 1 page 60.30
- the candidate would obtain full credit if they showed the formula and calculated answer correctly
- it is well answered, but some candidates missed the investment income, sometimes they forgot to add in investment income from UPR

## Part b (1.5 points)

Calculate the estimated effect of discounting the asset for future income taxes.

### Explanation
$$\text{PV factor} = \frac{591 + 2 + 1460 + 10}{600 + 1500} = 0.9824$$

$$\text{Estimated effect} = \{2500 - 0.95 \times \min(2500;\ 637 + 1500)\} \times 35\% \times (1 - 0.9824) = 2.426$$

(as printed: the result 2.426 is the one for the minimum taken on the APV reserve 637 + 1580 = 2217, as in the answer below)

Alternatively:

$$\text{APV reserve} = 637 + 1580 = 2217$$

$$\min(2500,\ 2217) = 2217$$

$$\text{PV factor} = \frac{591 + 2 + 1460 + 10}{600 + 1500} = 0.982$$

$$\text{Effect of discounting} = (2500 - 0.95 \times 2217) \times 35\% \times (1 - 0.982) = 2.48$$

### Examiner Report
- the candidate was expected to know C1
- the candidate will obtain full credit if they show the formula and calculate answer correctly
- it is well answered, but some candidates miss the present value factor, or forgot to apply the minimum to the reported reserves and the APV
