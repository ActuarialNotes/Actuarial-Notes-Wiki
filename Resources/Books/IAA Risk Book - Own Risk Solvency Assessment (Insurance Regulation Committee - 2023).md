An IAA Risk Book chapter (May 2023) from the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association. It explains the Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA) process and its principal component parts, how the different users interact, and how ORSA fits with other processes in an insurer and within groups.
