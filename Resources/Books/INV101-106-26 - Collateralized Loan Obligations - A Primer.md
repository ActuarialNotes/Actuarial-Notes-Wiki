An article on collateralized loan obligations (CLOs) in the Journal of Applied Corporate Finance, volume 34, issue 3 (September 2022), pages 35-50. The authors provide a first look at CLOs and their distinctive features as a securitization vehicle, describe their growth, and focus on two fundamental risks they pose: the reliability of credit ratings, and the systematic risk borne by CLO investors.
