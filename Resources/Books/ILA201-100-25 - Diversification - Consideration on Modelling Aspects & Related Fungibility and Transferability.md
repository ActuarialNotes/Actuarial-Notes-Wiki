A CRO Forum paper, dated October 2013, that supplements the Forum's 2005 paper on incorporating diversification in the solvency assessment of insurers. It adds analysis of practical considerations around modelling capabilities, principles for setting model correlations, calibrations and parameterisation, and clarification of fungibility and transferability, in the context of ORSA, stress testing, scenario analysis and risk appetites, to help insurers evaluate and justify their treatment of diversification.
