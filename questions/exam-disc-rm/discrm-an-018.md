In a state that regulates personal auto rates under a file-and-use system, when may an insurer begin charging a new rate?

- A) Only after the insurance commissioner approves it
- B) As soon as the rate is filed with the commissioner
- C) Only after the rate has been on file for a year without objection
- D) At any time, because no filing is required

## Explanation

Under **file-and-use**, an insurer may use a new rate as soon as it files it with the commissioner. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Approval before use is the **prior approval** system, the other common approach.
- **C** — No waiting period of a year is part of file-and-use; the rate may be used once filed.
- **D** — A system with no filing at all is not file-and-use, and most states regulate property-casualty rates for auto, property, liability and workers compensation.
