A paper that uses the won-lost record of baseball teams to examine and illustrate credibility concepts, an example analogous to the use of experience rating in insurance. It illustrates a situation where the phenomenon of shifting parameters over time has a very significant impact, and examines three criteria for selecting the optimal credibility: least squares, limited fluctuation and Meyers/Dorweiler.
