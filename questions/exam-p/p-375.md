The length of time, $T$, in months, taken by relatives to file for a death benefit has density function

$$
f(t) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{4\beta^4}{t^5}, & \text{for } t > \beta,\ 0 < \beta < 3 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the probability that the relatives of a policyholder will not file for the death benefit in the next four months, given that the policyholder died three months ago and the relatives have not yet filed for the death benefit.

- A) $\dfrac{81}{2401}$
- B) $\dfrac{256}{2401}$
- C) $\dfrac{81}{256}$
- D) $\dfrac{2401}{81}\left(\dfrac{81-\beta^4}{2401-\beta^4}\right)$
- E) $\dfrac{2401}{256}\left(\dfrac{256-\beta^4}{2401-\beta^4}\right)$

## Explanation

Integrate the density to get the distribution function, valid for $t \geq \beta$:

$$
\begin{align*}
F(t) &= \int_\beta^t 4\beta^4 r^{-5}\,dr \\
     &= -\beta^4 r^{-4}\Big|_\beta^t \\
     &= 1 - \left(\frac{\beta}{t}\right)^4
\end{align*}
$$

so the survival function has the clean form $P[T > t] = (\beta/t)^4$.

Three months have already passed without a filing, and the question asks about the next four, so the event is $T > 7$ conditional on $T > 3$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[T > 7 \mid T > 3] &= \frac{P[T > 7]}{P[T > 3]} \\
                    &= \frac{(\beta/7)^4}{(\beta/3)^4} \\
                    &= \left(\frac{3}{7}\right)^4 \\
                    &= \mathbf{\frac{81}{2401}}
\end{align*}
$$

The $\beta^4$ factors cancel, which is why the answer is a bare number rather than one of the $\beta$-dependent expressions in choices (D) and (E). That cancellation is the point: for this Pareto-type density the conditional survival probability depends only on the *ratio* of the two times. Choice (C), $81/256$, is $(3/4)^4$ — using the four-month window as the endpoint instead of the elapsed-plus-future total of 7.
