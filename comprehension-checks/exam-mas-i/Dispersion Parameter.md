A Poisson GLM returns a Pearson statistic of 2.5 per degree of freedom. What does that change about the fitted results?

- A) The coefficients shrink by a factor of $\sqrt{2.5}$
- B) The coefficients stay put; standard errors grow by $\sqrt{2.5}$
- C) Both coefficients and standard errors grow by 2.5
- D) Nothing, because the Poisson scale is fixed at 1
