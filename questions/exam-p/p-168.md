Claim amounts at an insurance company are independent of one another. In year one, claim amounts are modeled by a normal random variable $X$ with mean 100 and standard deviation 25. In year two, claim amounts are modeled by the random variable $Y = 1.04X + 5$.

Calculate the probability that a random sample of 25 claim amounts in year two average between 100 and 110.

- A) 0.48
- B) 0.53
- C) 0.54
- D) 0.67
- E) 0.68

## Explanation

Year-two claims: $Y = 1.04X + 5$.
$$
\begin{align*}
E[Y] &= 1.04(100) + 5 \\
     &= 109, \quad \text{SD}(Y) = 1.04(25) = 26
\end{align*}
$$

For a sample of $n = 25$: $\bar{Y} \sim N(109,\; 26^2/25)$, so $\text{SD}(\bar{Y}) = 26/5 = 5.2$.

$$
\begin{align*}
P(100 < \bar{Y} < 110) &= P\!\left(\frac{100-109}{5.2} < Z < \frac{110-109}{5.2}\right) \\
                       &= P(-1.731 < Z < 0.192) \\
                       &= \Phi(0.192) - \Phi(-1.731) \\
                       &\approx 0.576 - 0.042 \\
                       &= \mathbf{0.534 \approx 0.53}
\end{align*}
$$
