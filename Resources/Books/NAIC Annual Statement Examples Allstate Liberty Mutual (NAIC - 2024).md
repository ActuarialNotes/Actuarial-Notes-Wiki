Two property/casualty annual statements, in the NAIC's Association Edition form, that the companies post online: Allstate Insurance Company's Annual Statement for the year ended December 31, 2025 and the Combined Annual Statement of Liberty Mutual Insurance Company and its affiliated property and casualty insurers for the year ended December 31, 2024. Each carries the statement's financial pages, exhibits and schedules for the filing insurer or group.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6U (CAS)|Exam 6U]] — objective C1; NAIC Official Annual Statement Examples: Allstate and The Liberty Mutual Group, which candidates may use, or their own company's statements, as illustrations to better understand the annual statement but are not responsible for any company-specific data (as the content outline states it)
