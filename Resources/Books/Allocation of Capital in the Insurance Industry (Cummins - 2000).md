An article that discusses and critiques the methods proposed for allocating capital in financial institutions, with an emphasis on insurance. It covers the rationale for allocating capital by line of business, the implications of regulatory risk-based capital and the capital asset pricing model, the advantages and disadvantages of value-at-risk and insolvency put option criteria, and recently proposed methods of marginal capital allocation. It appeared in Risk Management and Insurance Review, Vol. 3, No. 1, Spring 2000, pp. 7-27.
