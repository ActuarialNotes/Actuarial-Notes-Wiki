# Exam F2FIE
**F2FIE – Valuation and Financial Considerations** is an exam of the finance, investments and ERM track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of the Valuation and Financial Considerations exam is to develop a candidate’s expertise to a level that allows for analysis in major areas of the investment practice. It builds on material covered in the Financial Products exam and earlier subjects and seeks to equip a student with the skills and broad working understanding of financial and investment markets.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA learning management system.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Investment policy and governance {10%}
>
> 1. Compare and differentiate the [[Interest|interests]] of key stakeholders. (B4)
> 2. Identify and analyze [[Principal|principal]] versus [[Agent Conflict|agent conflict]]. (B4)
> 3. Describe and analyze sources of [[Unethical Conduct|unethical conduct]] and explain the role of a fiduciary. (B4)
> 4. Describe and evaluate [[Governance Mechanism|governance mechanisms]] that attempt to address these conflicts. (B5)
> 5. Understand and evaluate the importance of an [[Organization's Culture|organization’s culture]] in [[Effectuating Governance|effectuating governance]]. (B5)
> 6. Explain and demonstrate how [[Corporate Governance|governance]] may be structured to [[Gain Competitive Advantage|gain competitive advantages]] and [[Efficiency|efficiencies]]. (C5)
> 7. Understand how ethics relate to [[Business Decision-Making|business decision-making]] and relate ethics in business to [[Personal Ethics|personal ethics]]. (D5)
> 8. Demonstrate an understanding of the concept of [[Investment Belief|investment beliefs]], including their application, in the following areas: (D5)
> 9. Establishment of an [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policy]].
> 10. Diversification and [[Portfolio Rebalancing|rebalancing]].
> 11. Strategic vs. [[Tactical Allocation|tactical allocation]].
> 12. Geographic and [[Sector Allocation|sector allocation]].
> 13. [[Currency]] management.
> 14. [[Liquidity]] management.
> 15. [[Credit Policy|Credit policy]].
> 16. Active vs. [[Passive Management|passive management]].
> 17. Alternatives and [[Derivatives Limit|derivatives limits]].
> 18. Demonstrate understanding of [[Regulators' Guidance|regulators’ guidance]] and context for different approaches to [[Responsible Investment|responsible investment]] and specifically, the integration of environmental, social, and [[Corporate Governance|governance]] (ESG) factors in the [[Investment Process|investment process]]. (C3)
> 19. Demonstrate an understanding of and analyze the underlying issues that constitute factors within each of the ESG areas. ([[C4]])
> 20. Demonstrate an understanding of and evaluate the [[ESG Market|ESG market]]: relevance, size, scope, key drivers and challenges, and [[Risk|risks]] and opportunities. (C5)

> [!example]- Asset allocation {15%}
>
> 1. Explain the impact of [[Asset Allocation Relative|asset allocation relative]] to various investor goals and [[Constraint|constraints]], including the impact due to liability calculation, [[Funding Rule|funding rules]] and regulations, and their results on the investor goals and attractiveness of different [[Asset Class|asset classes]]. (C2)
> 2. Propose and critique [[Asset Allocation|asset-allocation strategies]]. (D5)
> 3. Evaluate the significance of liabilities in the allocation of assets. ([[D5]])
> 4. Explain the purpose and impact of financing and leverage in the allocation of assets. ([[C2]])
> 5. Incorporate [[Risk Management Principle|risk management principles]] in [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policy]] and strategy, including [[Asset Allocation|asset allocation]]. (C6)
> 6. Discuss strategic-implementation choices in [[Asset Allocation|asset allocation]], including passive/active choices and vehicles for [[Implementing Passive|implementing passive]] and [[Active Mandate|active mandates]]. (C3)
> 7. Discuss strategic considerations in [[Rebalancing Asset Allocation|rebalancing asset allocations]]. (D5)
> 8. Describe and critique the use of [[Mean-Variance Optimization|mean-variance optimization]] in [[Asset Allocation|asset allocation]]. (D5)

> [!example]- Equities {20%}
>
> 1. Explain the nature and role of [[Equity Investment|equity investments]] within [[Portfolio|portfolios]] that may include other [[Asset Class|asset classes]]. (A2)
> 2. Understand and critique the basic concepts [[Surrounding Passive|surrounding passive]], active, and [[Semi-Active Enhanced Index Equity Investing|semi-active enhanced index equity investing]] as well as [[Factor-Based Investing|factor-based investing]], including managing [[Exposure Base|exposures]]. (B5)
> 3. Explain and critique the basic [[Active Equity Selection Strategy|active equity selection strategies]] including value, growth, and combination approaches. (B5)
> 4. Understand and calculate [[Equity Index|equity indices]] and their construction, including distinguishing among the [[Weighting Scheme|weighting schemes]] and their biases. (C3)
> 5. Identify and evaluate methods for [[Establishing Passive Exposure|establishing passive exposure]] to an [[Equity Market|equity market]]. (B5)
> 6. Compare and critique techniques for characterizing the [[Investment Style|investment style]] of an investor. (C5)
> 7. Recommend and justify, in a risk-return framework, the [[Asset Allocation|optimal portfolio allocations]] to a group of [[Investment Manager|investment managers]]. (C5)
> 8. Describe and critique the core-satellite approach to [[Portfolio|portfolio]] construction with a [[Completeness Fund|completeness fund]] to control overall [[Risk Exposure|risk exposures]]. (B5)
> 9. Explain and critique alpha and [[Beta Separation|beta separation]] as an approach to [[Active Management|active management]] and demonstrate the use of [[Portable Alpha|portable alpha]]. (B5)
> 10. Describe and critique the process of identifying, selecting, contracting with, and [[Overseeing Equity Manager|overseeing equity managers]]. (B5)
> 11. Distinguish between [[Active Share|active share]] and [[Active Risk|active risk]] and discuss how each measure relates to a [[Manager's Investment Strategy|manager’s investment strategy]]. (C5)

> [!example]- Advanced option pricing {20%}
>
> 1. Identify and differentiate the features of the [[Classic Short-Rate|classic short-rate]] models including the Vasicek and the Cox-Ingersoll-Ross models. (C4)
> 2. Understand and explain the terms “time-homogeneous models,” “[[Affine-Term Structure|affine-term structure]] models,” and “affine-coefficient models,” and explain their significance in the context of [[Short-Rate Interest|short-rate interest]] models. (D2)
> 3. Explain the dynamics of and motivation for the [[Hull-White Extension|Hull-White extension]] of the Vasicek model. (D5)
> 4. Explain the features of the Black-Karasinski model. ([[B2]])
> 5. Understand and explain the relationship between [[Market-Quoted Caplet Volatility|market-quoted caplet volatilities]] and model [[Volatility|volatilities]]. (B2)
> 6. Explain how [[Deterministic Shift|deterministic shifts]] can be used to fit any given [[Interest Rate Term Structure|interest rate term structure]] and demonstrate an understanding of the Cox-Ingersoll-Ross++ model. (C2)
> 7. Understand and explain the features of the G2++ model, including the motivation for more than one factor, calibration approaches, the pricing of [[Bonds|bonds]] and options, and the model’s relationship to the [[Two-Factor Hull-White|two-factor Hull-White]] model. (C3)
> 8. Describe and apply models that account for [[Negative Interest Rate|negative interest rates]], such as Black’s model and [[Shifted Log-Normal|shifted log-normal]]. (C3)
> 9. Explain the concept of [[Volatility Smile|volatility smile]] and some arguments for its existence. (B5)
> 10. Calculate the [[Hedging|hedge]] ratio for a [[Call Option|call option]] given the dependency of the [[Black-Scholes Volatility|Black-Scholes volatility]] on the underlying asset. (C3)
> 11. Compare and contrast “floating” and “sticky” [[Volatility Smile|volatility smiles]]. (C4)
> 12. Calculate the [[Risk-Neutral Density|risk-neutral density]] given [[Call Option Price|call option prices]]. (C3)
> 13. Identify several [[Stylized Empirical Fact|stylized empirical facts]] about smiles in a variety of [[Options Market|options markets]]. (B2)
> 14. Describe and contrast several approaches for [[Modelling Smile|modelling smiles]], including [[Stochastic Volatility|stochastic volatility]], [[Volatility Smile|local volatility]], [[Jump Diffusion|jump diffusions]], [[Variance Gamma|variance gamma]], and mixture models. (C4)
> 15. Describe and compare various issues and approaches for fitting a [[Volatility Surface|volatility surface]]. (C4)

>    - b. Agriculture and timberland.
>    - c. [[Green Asset|Green assets]].
>    - d. [[Longevity Bond|Longevity bonds]].
>    - e. [[Insurance-Linked Securities|Insurance-linked securities]] ([[Contingent Convertible|contingent convertibles]], [[Catastrophe Bond|catastrophe bonds]]).
>    - f. [[Hedge Fund|Hedge funds]].
>    - g. [[Venture Capital|Venture capital]].
>    - h. [[Derivative Contract|Derivative contracts]].
>    - i. [[Private Equity|Private equity]].
>    - j. [[Private Debt-credit|Private debt/credit]].
> 2. Understand and calculate benchmarks available to evaluate the performance of alternative [[Investment Manager|investment managers]]. (C3)
> 3. Describe the limitations of the benchmarks and evaluate how to circumvent these limitations. ([[B5]])
> 4. Understand and evaluate [[Investment Strategy|investment strategies]] and [[Portfolio|portfolio]] roles as well as their specific [[Construction-implementation Nuance|construction/implementation nuances]] that are characteristic of each alternative investment. (D5)
> 5. Perform a due diligence and [[Oversight Process|oversight processes]] and the impact of [[Negotiated Covenant|negotiated covenants]] on the [[Risk-Return Profile|risk-return profile]] for alternative investments. (C3)
> 6. Discuss how [[Hedge-Fund Strategy|hedge-fund strategies]] may be classified and evaluate the impact of an allocation to a [[Hedge-Fund Strategy|hedge-fund strategy]] in an [[Portfolio|investment portfolio]]. (C5)

> [!example]- Machine learning, neural networks, application of blockchain on insurance {10%}
>
> 1. Understand the [[Defining Feature|defining features]] of [[Blockchain Technology|blockchain technology]], [[Smart Contract|smart contracts]], and components of blockchain. (B2)
> 2. Discuss the applications of cryptocurrency and blockchain for insurance use cases such as [[Claims Processing|claims processing]], [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]] and swaps, tokenisations of [[Insurance Risk|insurance risk]], and the decentralized use of data. (B5)
> 3. Describe and evaluate the stages, [[Risk|risks]], and challenges to [[Blockchain Adoption|blockchain adoption]]. (C5)
> 4. Discuss examples and understand the characteristics of various supervised and [[Unsupervised Machine Learning|unsupervised machine learning]] methods. (B4)
> 5. Describe and evaluate various key areas of [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] practice for actuarial tasks - including model evaluation, [[Generalisation Error|generalisation error]] and [[Model Validation|model validation]], [[Feature Scaling|feature scaling]], regularisation, and [[Feature Engineering|feature engineering]]. (A5)
> 6. Distinguish between [[Neural Network|neural network]], [[Deep Learning|deep learning]] methods, and [[Reinforcement Learning|reinforcement learning]]. (B4)
> 7. Identify the steps required to process and analyze [[Big Data|big data]] in relation to the Actuarial Control Cycle (ACC). (C2)
> 8. Discuss and understand the steps and objectives of data collection, [[Data Cleaning|data cleaning]], and [[Model Validation|model validation]]. (C2)
