A chapter of the IAA Risk Book on distribution risks, approved by the IAA's Insurance Regulation Committee (version 2.0, March 2025). It explains what distribution risks are and how they can impact the business of insurance: risks to a distribution channel, to the performance of the insurer's business, to the insurer from the actions of a channel or the distribution process, and consumer protection and market conduct risks, with an appendix on types of distribution channels.
