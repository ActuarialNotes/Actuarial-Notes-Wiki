Under an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11), whose medical expenses does Coverage F, medical payments to others, pay?

- A) A jogger bitten by the insured's dog in a public park
- B) The insured's 10-year-old son, hurt falling from the family's trampoline
- C) The insured's mother, who lives with the family, hurt falling on the stairs
- D) The insured, hurt while repairing the roof of the house

## Explanation

Coverage F applies to a person off the insured location if the injury is caused by an animal owned by or in the care of an insured, without regard to fault. The jogger qualifies. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B**, **C** and **D** — Coverage F does not apply to the named insured or to regular residents of the household, other than residence employees. The son and the mother are residents, and the insured is "you". Their bills belong on health insurance.
