A commercial property policy requires the insured to give the insurer prompt notice of a loss and a signed, sworn proof of loss. In which category of policy provisions does this requirement belong?

- A) Declarations
- B) Insuring agreement
- C) Exclusion
- D) Conditions

## Explanation

Conditions qualify the insurer's promise by setting out duties, such as paying premium, reporting a loss promptly and submitting proof of loss; the insurer can be relieved of its obligation if the insured fails to comply in a way that matters. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — declarations hold the facts particular to the policy: insured, period, property, limits and premium.
- **B** — an insuring agreement states what the insurer promises to pay.
- **C** — an exclusion removes causes of loss, kinds of loss or property from the insuring agreement; a duty after loss removes nothing from coverage.
