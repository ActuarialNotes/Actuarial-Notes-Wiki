A chapter of the International Actuarial Association's Risk Book, developed and approved by the IAA Insurance Regulation Committee in May 2023 (version 2.0, written by Michael Eves). It outlines the key features of reinsurance arrangements, how they are priced and their potential impact on both the cedant and the reinsurer. The focus is on property and casualty reinsurance, with life and health reinsurance covered at a high level. It combines and replaces the earlier Non-proportional Reinsurance and Intra-Group Reinsurance Transactions chapters.
