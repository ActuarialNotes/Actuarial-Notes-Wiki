According to FASB Concepts Statement No. 8, to whom are general purpose financial reports prepared under GAAP primarily directed?

- A) Existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors
- B) State insurance regulators judging whether insurers are solvent
- C) Management, when it makes decisions about running the business
- D) Policyholders comparing insurers before buying coverage

## Explanation

The objective of general purpose financial reporting is to provide information useful to existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors in making decisions about providing resources to the entity. They cannot require information directly, so they are the primary users. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — regulators may find general purpose reports useful, but the reports are not primarily directed to them. Insurance regulators' framework is statutory accounting.
- **C** — management need not rely on general purpose reports because it can obtain the information it needs internally.
- **D** — members of the public are among the other parties who may find the reports useful, not the primary users.
