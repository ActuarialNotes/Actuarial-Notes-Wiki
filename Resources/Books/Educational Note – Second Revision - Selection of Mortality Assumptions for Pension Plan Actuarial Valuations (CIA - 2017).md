A December 2017 Canadian Institute of Actuaries educational note, prepared by the Committee on Pension Plan Financial Reporting, on selecting a best estimate mortality assumption for pension plan actuarial valuations. It reflects both the 2017 CIA MI Study and the 2014 CIA Canadian Pensioner Mortality (CPM) Study and replaces the educational note issued on March 27, 2014. It treats two key components separately: the base mortality table at the valuation date, and the future mortality improvement scale.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 2a-2d; the whole note, which the SOA describes as supplemental information on mortality assumption selection.
