![[International Standard of Actuarial Practice 5 (IAA - 2018) - Cover.svg]]

An IAA model standard of practice giving guidance to actuaries performing actuarial services that involve the use of enterprise risk models for insurers. Adopted by the IAA Council on 21 November 2016, with conformance changes adopted on 1 December 2018, it is intended to facilitate convergence in standards of practice for insurer enterprise risk models, to increase public confidence in actuarial services for ERM purposes, and to demonstrate the IAA's support for the IAIS in achieving effective insurer ERM practice.
