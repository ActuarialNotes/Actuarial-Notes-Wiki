A bank issues two bonds with the same annual effective yield rate. The first bond is a 10-year zero-coupon bond with a price of 3000 and a face amount of 5000. The second bond is a 15-year bond providing quarterly coupons, with a price of 1000 and a face amount of 950.

Calculate the annual coupon rate for the second bond.

- A) 5.24%
- B) 5.36%
- C) 5.58%
- D) 5.65%
- E) 5.76%

## Explanation

Let $j$ be the quarterly interest rate. The zero-coupon bond discounts 5000 over 10 years, or 40 quarters:

$$
\begin{align*}
3000 &= 5000v^{40} \\
v^{40} &= 0.6 \\
j &= 0.01285
\end{align*}
$$

Let $r$ be the quarterly coupon rate of the second bond, so it pays $950r$ at the end of each of 60 quarters and 950 at the end of 15 years. Pricing it at $j$:

$$
\begin{align*}
1000 &= 950r\,a_{\overline{60}|0.01285} + 950v^{60} \\
     &= 39{,}565.26r + 441.59
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
r &= \frac{1000 - 441.59}{39{,}565.26} \\
  &= 0.014114
\end{align*}
$$

This is the quarterly rate. The annual rate is

$$
\begin{align*}
4r &= 0.014114(4) \\
   &= 0.056456 \\
   &\approx 5.65\%
\end{align*}
$$
