A collector insures his classic convertible under ISO's coverage for damage to your auto (maximum limit of liability) endorsement, PP 03 08, at a scheduled amount of \$25,000 with a \$500 deductible. Which statement about how a loss to the car is settled is correct?

- A) A total loss always pays \$24,500, because the scheduled amount is an agreed value
- B) If the car's value rises above \$25,000, the payment for a total loss rises with it
- C) If the car's actual cash value falls to \$18,000, a total loss pays \$17,500
- D) A partial loss is paid at the scheduled amount less the deductible

## Explanation

PP 03 08 pays the **least** of the scheduled amount, the actual cash value and the cost to repair or replace, less the deductible. It is a ceiling, not an agreed value. With an ACV of \$18,000 a total loss pays:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Paid} &= \min(25{,}000,\ 18{,}000) - 500 \\
&= 17{,}500
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** treats the scheduled amount as an agreed value; a car worth less than its scheduled amount is paid its ACV.
- **B** — the scheduled amount caps the payment, so appreciation above it is not paid; the collector must raise the amount.
- **D** — a partial loss is paid at its repair cost, which is the least of the three amounts.
