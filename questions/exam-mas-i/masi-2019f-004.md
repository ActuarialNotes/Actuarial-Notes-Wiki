A building is powered by three generators with independent lifetimes, each following an exponential distribution with mean of one year. All three generators are started at the same time.

Let $T$ be the time in years between the first and last generator failure.

Calculate $E[T]$.

- A) Less than 0.8
- B) At least 0.8, but less than 1.2
- C) At least 1.2, but less than 1.6
- D) At least 1.6, but less than 2.0
- E) At least 2.0

## Explanation

$T$ is the gap between the first and third failures. Use memorylessness to break the lifetimes into the spacings between successive failures.

- While all three are running, failures occur at total rate $3$; the first failure comes after an exponential time with mean $1/3$.
- After the first failure the two survivors are "as good as new", so the second failure comes after a further exponential time with rate $2$, mean $1/2$.
- The last survivor then fails after a further exponential time with rate $1$, mean $1$.

$T$ is the sum of the last two spacings:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[T] &= \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{1} \\
&= 1.5
\end{align*}
$$

(Equivalently, $E[\max] - E[\min] = \left(\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1}{2} + 1\right) - \tfrac{1}{3} = 1.5$.)

This falls in $[1.2, 1.6)$, so the answer is **C**.
