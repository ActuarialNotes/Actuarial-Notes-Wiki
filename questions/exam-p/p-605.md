ElectroUSA is a store that sells portable music players. Ten percent of sales are Brand A and the rest are Brand B. The time, in years, until failure for Brand A is modeled by an exponential distribution with mean 2, while that for Brand B is modeled by an exponential distribution with mean 4.

A portable music player sold by ElectroUSA fails for the first time within three years of the purchase date.

Calculate the probability that it is Brand A.

- A) $0.08$
- B) $0.14$
- C) $0.47$
- D) $0.60$
- E) $0.78$

## Explanation

Let $T$ be the time until failure and $A$ the event that the player is Brand A. For an exponential with mean $\mu$, $P[T < 3] = 1 - e^{-3/\mu}$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
P[T < 3 \mid A] &= 1 - e^{-3/2} = 0.7769 \\
P[T < 3 \mid B] &= 1 - e^{-3/4} = 0.5276
\end{align*}
$$

Applying Bayes' theorem with the 10%/90% sales split:

$$
\begin{align*}
P(A \mid T < 3) &= \frac{(1 - e^{-3/2})(0.1)}{(1 - e^{-3/2})(0.1) + (1 - e^{-3/4})(0.9)} \\
                &= \frac{0.07769}{0.07769 + 0.47483} \\
                &= \mathbf{0.1406}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (A), 0.08, is the numerator alone — the *joint* probability $P(A \text{ and } T < 3) = 0.0777$. That is the probability that a randomly chosen sale is both Brand A and an early failure, not the probability of Brand A among the early failures; dividing by $P[T < 3] = 0.5525$ is what converts one into the other.
