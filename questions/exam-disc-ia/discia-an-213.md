A hotel buys an employment practices liability policy with no endorsements. Which claim does it cover?

- A) A guest's claim that a desk clerk sexually harassed her during her stay
- B) A rejected job applicant's charge of age discrimination before a government agency
- C) A housekeeper's claim for a back injury suffered lifting a mattress at work
- D) A class action by servers for unpaid overtime wages

## Explanation

EPLI covers claims by employees, former employees and **job applicants** alleging a wrongful employment practice, including discrimination, and a claim usually includes an administrative charge before a government agency. An applicant who was never hired can bring a covered discrimination claim.

- **A** is a third-party claim by a customer. It is covered only if third-party EPL has been added by endorsement.
- **C** is a workplace injury, which belongs to workers compensation and employers liability, not EPLI.
- **D** is a wage and hour claim, which EPLI usually excludes or covers only for defense under a sublimit.
