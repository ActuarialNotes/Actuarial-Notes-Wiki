A loan of 20,000 is to be repaid with ten increasing installments payable at the end of each year. Each installment will be 10% greater than the preceding installment. The annual effective interest rate on the loan is 9%.

Calculate the amount of principal in the second installment.

- A) 292
- B) 527
- C) 975
- D) 1435
- E) 1774

## Explanation

The installments $R_1, 1.10R_1, 1.10^2R_1, \dots$ form a geometric progression, so the loan is the present value of a geometrically increasing annuity-immediate at $i = 9\%$, $g = 10\%$:

$$
\begin{align*}
20{,}000 &= R_1 \cdot \frac{1-(1.10/1.09)^{10}}{0.09-0.10} \\
         &= R_1 \times 9.562485 \\
R_1 &= 2091.51
\end{align*}
$$

Second installment: $R_2 = 2091.51(1.10) = 2300.66$.

Outstanding balance just after the first installment (retrospective method):

$$
\begin{align*}
20{,}000(1.09) - R_1 &= 21{,}800 - 2091.51 \\
                     &= 19{,}708.49
\end{align*}
$$

The prospective method gives the same balance: the nine remaining installments, starting with $R_2 = 2300.657$, are worth $R_2\cdot\frac{1-(1.10/1.09)^{9}}{0.09-0.10} = 2300.657 \times 8.566462 = 19{,}708.49$.

Interest in the second installment: $19{,}708.49 \times 0.09 = 1773.76$.

Principal in the second installment:

$$
\begin{align*}
R_2 - \text{interest} &= 2300.66 - 1773.76 \\
                      &= 526.90 \approx 527
\end{align*}
$$
