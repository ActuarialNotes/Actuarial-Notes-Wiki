A ten-year 1000 face amount bond has an annual coupon rate of 6% paid semiannually. The bond is purchased at a premium, to yield an annual effective interest rate of 5%.

Calculate the amount of the amortization of premium in the 12th coupon.

- A) 3.85
- B) 3.95
- C) 4.00
- D) 4.10
- E) 4.26

## Explanation

The semiannual coupon is $Fr = 1000(0.03) = 30$, and the semiannual yield rate is

$$
\begin{align*}
j &= (1.05)^{1/2} - 1 \\
  &= 0.024695
\end{align*}
$$

The amortization of premium in the 12th coupon is the drop in book value from just after the 11th coupon, when 9 coupons remain, to just after the 12th, when 8 remain:

$$
\begin{align*}
B_{11} &= 1000v_j^{9} + 30\,a_{\overline{9}|j} \\
       &= 1042.35 \\
B_{12} &= 1000v_j^{8} + 30\,a_{\overline{8}|j} \\
       &= 1038.09
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
B_{11} - B_{12} &= 1042.35 - 1038.09 \\
                &= 4.26
\end{align*}
$$
