![[Canadian Institute of Actuaries Standards of Practice - Insurance (2024) - Cover.svg]]

The Standards of Practice that apply to actuarial work in Canada, in the complete version dated June 2024 (effective June 1, 2024; accession 224061). Responsibility for them vests in the Actuarial Standards Board (Canada), which approves the standards and changes to them through a process that includes consultation with the actuarial profession and other interested parties. Part 1000 holds the general standards; the practice-specific parts cover insurance (2000), pension plans (3000), actuarial evidence (4000), non-pension employee future benefit plans (6000), social security programs (7000) and enterprise risk management (8000).

> - [[Exam GH-201C (SOA)|Exam GH-201C]] — Reserving, outcomes 1d and 1g; sections 1400, 1510, 1700, 2100, 2200 and 2300 of the June 2024 Standards of Practice (the syllabus calls it "Standards of Practice - Insurance").
