The time series below shows study manuals sold by month by company XYZ:

| t | Month | Year | Study Manuals Sold |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Jan | 2016 | 52 |
| 2 | Feb | 2016 | 18 |
| 3 | Mar | 2016 | 16 |
| 4 | Apr | 2016 | 14 |
| 5 | May | 2016 | 16 |
| 6 | Jun | 2016 | 16 |
| 7 | Jul | 2016 | 18 |
| 8 | Aug | 2016 | 50 |
| 9 | Sep | 2016 | 20 |
| 10 | Oct | 2016 | 16 |
| 11 | Nov | 2016 | 18 |
| 12 | Dec | 2016 | 20 |
| 13 | Jan | 2017 | 60 |
| 14 | Feb | 2017 | 22 |
| 15 | Mar | 2017 | 17 |
| 16 | Apr | 2017 | 19 |
| 17 | May | 2017 | 21 |
| 18 | Jun | 2017 | 23 |
| 19 | Jul | 2017 | 25 |
| 20 | Aug | 2017 | 60 |
| 21 | Sep | 2017 | 28 |
| 22 | Oct | 2017 | 22 |
| 23 | Nov | 2017 | 24 |
| 24 | Dec | 2017 | 28 |

Calculate the monthly additive effect at time t = 13 using the centering approach.

- A) Less than 25
- B) At least 25, but less than 30
- C) At least 30, but less than 35
- D) At least 35, but less than 40
- E) At least 40

## Explanation

In an additive decomposition $x_t = m_t + s_t + z_t$, the trend $m_t$ is estimated by a moving average centred on $t$, and the additive effect at $t$ is what is left after removing it: $\hat{s}_t = x_t - \hat{m}_t$.

A 12-month average cannot be centred on a single month (12 is even), so the centering approach averages two adjacent 12-month windows. That puts half weight on the two end points, 6 months either side:

$$
\hat{m}_t = \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}x_{t-6} + x_{t-5} + \cdots + x_{t+5} + \tfrac{1}{2}x_{t+6}}{12}
$$

At $t = 13$ the window runs from $x_7 = 18$ to $x_{19} = 25$, and the eleven full-weight months $x_8$ through $x_{18}$ are

$$
50 + 20 + 16 + 18 + 20 + 60 + 22 + 17 + 19 + 21 + 23 = 286.
$$

So

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{m}_{13} &= \frac{\tfrac{1}{2}(18) + 286 + \tfrac{1}{2}(25)}{12} \\
&= \frac{307.5}{12} \\
&= 25.625
\end{align*}
$$

and the monthly additive effect is

$$
\hat{s}_{13} = x_{13} - \hat{m}_{13} = 60 - 25.625 = 34.375.
$$

This falls in $[30, 35)$, so the answer is **C**.
