IAA professional guidance on the governance of International Actuarial Work, approved by Council as non-binding guidelines to aid member associations. Produced by the IAA's Professionalism Committee to aid their consideration of professionalism issues, it defines International Actuarial Work and sets out principles member associations might apply to avoid or address inconsistency, duplication or gaps in its governance: qualification, codes and standards; continuing professional development; and disciplinary investigation and enforcement.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6I (CAS)|Exam 6I]] — objectives E3–E4; the whole document.

## 1 Definition of "International Actuarial Work"
- 1.1 International Actuarial Work is defined as work that is under the jurisdiction or regulation of one country, but is carried out by an actuary whose principal jurisdiction of practice or the jurisdiction of their professional membership is in a different country.
- 1.2 Its "governance" refers to the regulation and supervision of the actuary's conduct and work by member associations of the IAA.
- 1.3 It is broadly actuarial work in relation to which the law, standards or regulatory frameworks of more than one country, legal jurisdiction or IAA member association are relevant and material.
- 1.4 The principles that follow might be applied by IAA member associations to avoid or address inconsistency, duplication or gaps in the governance of International Actuarial Work.
