For a certain health insurance policy, losses are uniformly distributed on the interval $[0, 450]$. The policy has a deductible of $d$ and the expected value of the unreimbursed portion of a loss is 56.

Calculate $d$.

- A) 60
- B) 87
- C) 112
- D) 169
- E) 224

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the loss; the unreimbursed portion is $\min(X,d)$.

$E[\min(X,d)]=d-d^2/900=56\Rightarrow d^2-900d+50400=0\Rightarrow d=\mathbf{60}$ (verified: $60-3600/900=56$)
