![[How Does Where You Live Impact Your Health (SOA Research Institute - 2022) - Cover.svg]]

A March 2022 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, written for the Health Care Cost Trends Research Program, that considers how [[Social Determinants of Health|social determinants of health]] (SDOH) tied to where people live or work affect health. It presents a roadmap for developing SDOH "solutions", from data collection to analysis to integration with existing programs, and asks what payers and providers can do about the problem. It is the first of a two-part series; the companion report is the Institute's 2023 report on the incorporation of determinants of health into actuarial data and workstreams.
