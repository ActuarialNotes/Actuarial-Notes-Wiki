During a windstorm an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured leaves a window open. Rain blows in, damaging the drywall beneath the window and ruining an upholstered chair. The wind does not damage the house. What does Section I cover?

- A) Both the drywall and the chair, because windstorm is a covered peril
- B) Neither, because damage from rain is excluded as water damage
- C) The chair only, because Coverage C names windstorm as a peril
- D) The drywall only, because the dwelling has open perils coverage

## Explanation

The drywall is part of the dwelling under Coverage A, which covers any direct physical loss not excluded. Rain blowing in through an open window is not among the exclusions in the Coverage A and B perils or the Section I exclusions, so it is covered. The chair is personal property, covered only by named perils, and the windstorm peril covers rain inside a building **only if wind or hail first makes an opening in the roof or a wall**. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** applies the windstorm peril to the chair without its condition about an opening made by the wind.
- **B** — the water exclusion covers flood, surface water, sewer back-up and water below ground, not rain entering the house.
- **C** reverses the two bases: the chair fails the named peril, while the drywall is covered on an open perils basis.
