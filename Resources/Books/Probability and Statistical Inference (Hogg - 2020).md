![[Probability and Statistical Inference (Hogg - 2020) - Cover.png]]

A two-semester course text in probability and statistical inference that needs a good calculus background but no previous study of probability or statistics. Its first five chapters focus on probability — conditional probability, independence, Bayes' theorem, discrete, continuous and bivariate distributions, functions of random variables, the central limit theorem and Chebyshev's inequality — and the remaining four on statistical inference. The tenth edition adds more than 25 examples and 75 exercises, a section on the hypergeometric distribution and one on hypothesis tests for variances.
