A McKinsey article of February 2022 arguing that, as nonfinancial companies move from enterprise risk management to a resilience-based approach, their experience in nonfinancial risk can provide a model for banks. It notes that banks have led in developing approaches to financial risks, whereas nonfinancial risks arise from a bank's operations and are similar to those faced by companies outside the financial sector, and compares the two.
