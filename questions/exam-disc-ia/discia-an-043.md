A home is insured for \$350,000 under a homeowners policy with a \$1,000 deductible for all perils and a 3% hurricane deductible. A hurricane causes \$40,000 of covered damage. How much of the loss does the homeowner bear?

- A) \$1,000
- B) \$1,200
- C) \$9,500
- D) \$10,500

## Explanation

A percentage deductible is based on the home's insured value, not on the loss.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Deductible} &= 0.03 \times 350{,}000 \\
&= 10{,}500
\end{align*}
$$

The hurricane deductible replaces the dollar deductible for hurricane losses, so the homeowner bears \$10,500 and the insurer pays \$29,500. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$1,000 applies the ordinary deductible, which is used for other perils such as fire and theft.
- **B** — \$1,200 is 3% of the loss rather than of the insured value.
- **C** — \$9,500 credits the \$1,000 dollar deductible against the hurricane deductible; only one applies.
