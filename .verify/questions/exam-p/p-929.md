- claim: E[M^2] = \frac{k(n+1)(kn+k+n+1)}{(k+1)(k+2)} = \frac{3(n+1)(3n+4)}{20}
- evidence: From the stem pmf, P(M=m) = C(m-1,2)/C(n,3); m C(m-1,2) = 3C(m,3) and m(m+1)C(m-1,2) = 12C(m+1,4), so E[M] = 3(n+1)/4 and E[M(M+1)] = 3(n+1)(n+2)/5, giving E[M^2] = 3(n+1)(4n+3)/20 (general k: k(n+1)(kn+n+k)/((k+1)(k+2)), no "+1"). The correct 3(n+1)(4n+3)/20 gives 9 and 57/4; the page 3(n+1)(3n+4)/20 gives 7.8 and 12; the page general formula at k = 3, n = 3 gives 9.6. The two page forms also disagree with each other (at k = 3 the general one is 3(n+1)(4n+4)/20).
