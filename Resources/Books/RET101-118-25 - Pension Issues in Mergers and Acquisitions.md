A paper by Mitch Frazer of Torys LLP, delivered at the 13th International Pension and Employee Benefits Lawyers Association Conference, May 22-25, 2011. It identifies and analyzes the pension issues, obligations and risks that arise in corporate transactions, with a focus on Ontario-registered pension plans, noting that the structure of the deal directly affects the buyer's and the seller's pension obligations.
