A department store's annual sales, $x_t$, is modeled as an AR(3) process given by:

$$
x_t = 450 - 0.9x_{t-1} + x_{t-3} + w_t
$$

where $w_t$ is white noise, with:

- $E(w_t) = 0$
- $\text{Var}(w_t) = \sigma^2$

You are given the following historical annual sales totals for this store:

| Year | Sales |
|:-:|:-:|
| 2013 | 400 |
| 2014 | 375 |
| 2015 | 410 |
| 2016 | 420 |
| 2017 | 410 |
| 2018 | 525 |

Calculate the two-step-ahead forecast value, $x_{2020}$.

- A) Less than 400
- B) At least 400, but less than 450
- C) At least 450, but less than 500
- D) At least 500, but less than 550
- E) At least 550

## Explanation

Forecast recursively. Replace each future shock with its mean of 0, each future value with its forecast, and each known value with itself. The model uses lags 1 and 3 only, with no lag-2 term.

The one-step forecast uses $x_{2018} = 525$ and $x_{2016} = 420$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{x}_{2019} &= 450 - 0.9x_{2018} + x_{2016} \\
&= 450 - 0.9(525) + 420 \\
&= 397.5
\end{align*}
$$

The two-step forecast feeds that back in, together with $x_{2017} = 410$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{x}_{2020} &= 450 - 0.9\hat{x}_{2019} + x_{2017} \\
&= 450 - 0.9(397.5) + 410 \\
&= 502.25
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[500, 550)$, so the answer is **D**.
