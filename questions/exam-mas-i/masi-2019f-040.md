You have $p = 10$ independent variables and would like to select a linear model to fit the data using the following two procedures:

- Best Subset Selection (BSS)
- Forward Stepwise Selection (FSS)

Let $N_i$ be the maximum number of models fit by model selection procedure, $i$.

Calculate the ratio $\dfrac{N_{FSS}}{N_{BSS}}$.

- A) Less than 0.005
- B) At least 0.005, but less than 0.010
- C) At least 0.010, but less than 0.050
- D) At least 0.050, but less than 0.100
- E) At least 0.100

## Explanation

Best subset selection fits every possible model: each of the $p$ predictors is either in or out, so counting the null model,

$$
\begin{align*}
N_{BSS} &= 2^{p} \\
&= 2^{10} \\
&= 1024
\end{align*}
$$

Forward stepwise selection starts from the null model, and at step $k$ ($k = 0, \ldots, p-1$) it fits the $p - k$ models that add one of the remaining predictors:

$$
\begin{align*}
N_{FSS} &= 1 + \sum_{k=0}^{p-1}(p - k) \\
&= 1 + \frac{p(p+1)}{2} \\
&= 1 + \frac{10 \cdot 11}{2} \\
&= 56
\end{align*}
$$

The ratio is

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{N_{FSS}}{N_{BSS}} &= \frac{56}{1024} \\
&= 0.0547
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.050, 0.100)$, so the answer is **D**.
