## Part a (0.75 points)

According to MSA Research, identify three aspects of financial strength that are not captured in the OSFI regulatory solvency ratios and tests.

### Explanation
List of elements:

- subjective analysis of the company's market position
- prospects
- parental support
- sources of capital
- quality and sustainability of reinsurance support
- management quality
- sustainability of earnings

Alternatively:

- Parental support
- Subjective analysis of the competitive position
- Access to reasonable capital

### Examiner Report
For part a), candidates struggled to get full credit on this question. Many candidates tried to guess the answer or answered by listing some of the MSA ratios.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Given the following information:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Invested assets at Dec 31, 2011 | \$182,000 |
| Invested assets at Dec 31, 2012 | \$198,000 |

- Net Investment Income: \$20,000 for 2011 and 2012
- Other Comprehensive Income: \$0 in 2012.

Calculate the MSA Adjusted Investment Yield for 2012.

### Explanation
$$\text{Adjusted investment yield} = \frac{2 \times (\text{Net investment income} + \text{OCI})}{\text{Beginning of year assets} + \text{End of year invested assets} - \text{Net investment income} - \text{OCI}}$$

$$= \frac{2 \times (20{,}000 + 0)}{182{,}000 + 198{,}000 - 20{,}000 - 0} = 11.11\%$$

Alternatively:

$$Y = \frac{(\text{Net II} + \text{OCI}) \times 2}{\text{Inv Ass} + \text{Inv Ass} - (\text{Net II} + \text{OCI})} = 11.11\%$$

### Examiner Report
For part b), the majority of the candidates managed to get full credit. Many candidates failed to include the Other Comprehensive Income in the formula, which resulted in a small point deduction.
