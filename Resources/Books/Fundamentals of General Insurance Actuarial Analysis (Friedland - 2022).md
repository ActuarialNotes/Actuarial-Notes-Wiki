![[Fundamentals of General Insurance Actuarial Analysis (Friedland - 2022) - Cover.svg]]

A Society of Actuaries textbook by Jacqueline Friedland, in its second edition (2022), on general insurance actuarial analysis. It is built around the actuarial control cycle (define the problem, design a solution, monitor results) and aims to be a practical guide rather than a theoretical discourse, with examples embedded in the text and in case studies in the appendices. Its parts run from key concepts and data preparation through methods for estimating ultimate claims, financial reporting, trending, ratemaking and monitoring results.
