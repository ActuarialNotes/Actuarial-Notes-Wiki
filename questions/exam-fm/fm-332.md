An investor wants to accumulate 150,000 to purchase a business in ten years. The investor deposits 14,000 into an account at the beginning of each of years one through five. The investor deposits an additional amount $X$ at the beginning of years four and five to meet this goal. The account earns interest at an annual effective rate of 8%.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 2,113
- B) 2,592
- C) 5,958
- D) 9,595
- E) 11,574

## Explanation

Measure everything at the end of year 5, then accumulate to year 10.

The 14,000 deposits at the beginning of years 1–5 are a 5-year annuity-due, worth $14{,}000\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{5}|0.08} = 14{,}000(6.335929) = 88{,}703.01$ at the end of year 5.

The extra deposits $X$ at the beginning of years 4 and 5 are a 2-year annuity-due, worth $X\ddot{s}_{\overline{2}|0.08} = X(1.08 + 1.08^2) = 2.2464X$ at the end of year 5.

Both sums then earn interest for five more years, $(1.08)^5 = 1.469328$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\left(14{,}000\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{5}|0.08} + X\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{2}|0.08}\right)(1.08)^5 &= 150{,}000 \\
(88{,}703.01 + 2.2464X)(1.469328) &= 150{,}000 \\
88{,}703.01 + 2.2464X &= 102{,}087.48 \\
2.2464X &= 13{,}384.47 \\
X &= 5958.19
\end{align*}
$$

So $X \approx 5958$.
