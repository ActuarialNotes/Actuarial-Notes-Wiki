Claim counts are Poisson with a Gamma prior on the risk parameter. How does the posterior-mean estimate compare with the Bühlmann estimate?

- A) They coincide — for a conjugate pair the posterior mean is already linear in the data
- B) They differ — the Bühlmann answer is by construction only ever an approximation
- C) Bühlmann is larger, because it applies a credibility weight the other estimate lacks
- D) Only Bühlmann can be computed, since the other approach needs no prior distribution
