An insurer has the following book of insurance policies and claim experience as of December 31, 2016:

| Policy Number | Policy Effective Date | Policy Term (in Months) | Gross Written Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | January 1, 2015 | 12 | 12,000 |
| 2 | June 1, 2015 | 6 | 7,000 |
| 3 | August 1, 2015 | 12 | 10,000 |
| 4 | February 1, 2016 | 12 | 15,000 |
| 5 | May 1, 2016 | 6 | 8,000 |

| Claim Number | Accident Date | Claim Report Date | Gross Paid Claims | Gross Case Reserves | Reinsurance Recoveries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | September 1, 2015 | October 1, 2015 | 2,000 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | November 1, 2015 | January 1, 2016 | 3,000 | 1,000 | 500 |
| 3 | March 1, 2016 | March 1, 2016 | 1,000 | 2,000 | 0 |
| 4 | July 1, 2016 | September 1, 2016 | 4,000 | 0 | 1,000 |

## Part a (0.5 points)

Calculate the calendar year 2016 gross earned premium.

### Answer
27,583

### Explanation
Calendar year 2016 earns whatever portion of each policy's term falls inside 2016. The two policies that expired before 2016 began contribute nothing:

| Policy | Policy period | Months earned in 2016 | Earned |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1/1/2015 – 12/31/2015 | 0 — expired | 0 |
| 2 | 6/1/2015 – 11/30/2015 | 0 — expired (6-month policy) | 0 |
| 3 | 8/1/2015 – 7/31/2016 | 7 | $10{,}000 \times \tfrac{7}{12} = 5{,}833$ |
| 4 | 2/1/2016 – 1/31/2017 | 11 | $15{,}000 \times \tfrac{11}{12} = 13{,}750$ |
| 5 | 5/1/2016 – 10/31/2016 | 6 — its whole term | $8{,}000 \times \tfrac{6}{6} = 8{,}000$ |

$$\text{CY2016 gross earned premium} = 5{,}833 + 13{,}750 + 8{,}000 = 27{,}583$$

Note policy 5 earns its **full** premium in 2016: it is a six-month policy incepting and expiring within the year, so the denominator is its own 6-month term, not 12.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to determine which policies had premium earning in the period, determine the appropriate earning ratio, and calculate earned premium. Common errors included assuming all five policies had earnings in 2016, and prorating one or more policies incorrectly — for example applying a ratio of 5/12 to policy 3 instead of 7/12.

## Part b (0.25 points)

Calculate the gross unearned premium as of December 31, 2016.

### Answer
1,250

### Explanation
Unearned premium at a valuation date belongs to the unexpired portion of policies still in force. Only **policy 4** extends past December 31, 2016 — every other policy has expired by then (policy 5, a six-month policy written 5/1/2016, expired 10/31/2016).

Policy 4 runs 2/1/2016 – 1/31/2017, leaving **one month** unexpired:

$$15{,}000 \times \frac{1}{12} = 1{,}250$$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to determine which policies had unearned premium at the period end, determine the remaining policy term, and calculate the amount. Common errors included selecting the wrong policies and using incorrect proration terms.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Calculate the reported claims net of reinsurance recoveries for accident year 2016 as of December 31, 2016.

### Answer
6,000

### Explanation
Accident year 2016 is defined by the **accident date**, not the report date. Claim 2 is the trap: it was reported in January 2016 but occurred in November 2015, so it belongs to accident year 2015 and is excluded.

Reported claims are paid claims **plus case reserves**, less reinsurance recoveries:

| Claim | Accident Date | In AY2016? | Paid | Case | Recoveries | Net Reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9/1/2015 | No | — | — | — | — |
| 2 | 11/1/2015 | No — reported in 2016 but occurred in 2015 | — | — | — | — |
| 3 | 3/1/2016 | Yes | 1,000 | 2,000 | 0 | $1{,}000 + 2{,}000 - 0 = 3{,}000$ |
| 4 | 7/1/2016 | Yes | 4,000 | 0 | 1,000 | $4{,}000 + 0 - 1{,}000 = 3{,}000$ |

$$\text{AY2016 reported claims net of reinsurance} = 3{,}000 + 3{,}000 = 6{,}000$$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to determine which claims occurred within the accident year and calculate reported loss net of reinsurance. Common errors included using paid claims without adding the case reserves, including the wrong claims — most often claim 2, on the strength of its 2016 report date — and showing only the recoveries.
