A booklet of excerpts from The Institutes' Business Law for Insurance Professionals, prepared for the Society of Actuaries as a first edition by Institutes Custom Publishing; the SOA lists it as the former study note FRE-103-14. Candidates order it from The Institutes with the code SABE02. An SOA reading list of 2015 describes the booklet as including chapters on Contract Law: Insurance Applications, Tort Law and The International Legal Environment.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GI-201 (SOA)|Exam GI-201]] — outcomes 1j, 3a, 3f and 3i; Assignment 3, pp. 3.29-3.39 (begin on pp. 3.29 with "Financial Considerations in International Business")

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> No copy of this booklet is published online; The Institutes supplies it to candidates under code SABE02. This page records its citation and assignment from the syllabus.
