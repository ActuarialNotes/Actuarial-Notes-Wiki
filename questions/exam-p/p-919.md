Let $X$ be an exponentially distributed random variable with mean 2.

Calculate $E[e^{-X}]$.

- A) $1/3$
- B) $1/2$
- C) $2/3$
- D) $e^{-2}$
- E) $e^{-1/2}$

## Explanation

An exponential random variable with mean 2 has density $f(x) = \frac{1}{2}e^{-x/2}$ for $x > 0$. The expected value of a function of $X$ is that function integrated against the density:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[e^{-X}] &= \int_0^\infty e^{-x} \cdot \frac{1}{2}e^{-x/2}\,dx \\
          &= \frac{1}{2}\int_0^\infty e^{-3x/2}\,dx \\
          &= \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \\
          &= \mathbf{\frac{1}{3}}
\end{align*}
$$
