An October 2010 report to the Board of Directors of the American Academy of Actuaries from its Public Plans Practices Task Force (chair: David Sandberg), which was charged to examine objectively a broad range of actuarial issues and practices related to public-sector retirement systems. Its focus is public pension plans, and it applies risk management concepts to how current practices support or do not support the public interest.
