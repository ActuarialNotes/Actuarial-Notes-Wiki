# Exam F1RET
**F1RET – Pension Funding and Regulation** is an exam of the retirement benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main provisions, principles, regulation, professional standards, governance, and techniques of funding and administration applicable to retirement benefits.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of pension plans.
>
> 3. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions and to develop appropriate proposals or recommendations relating to the funding and administration of pension plans.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in the [[Funding|funding]] and [[Insurance Regulation|regulatory environment]] of [[Pension Plan|retirement benefits]]. (A1).

> [!example]- Retirement benefits and pension mathematics {30%}
>
> 1. Describe and analyze the features of [[Defined Benefit Plan|traditional defined benefit plans]] and [[Defined Contribution Plan|defined contribution plans]]. (C4)
> 2. Describe and analyze the structure of the following plan types: (C4)
>    - a. [[Defined Benefit Plan|Traditional defined benefit plans]].
>    - b. [[Defined Contribution Plan|Defined contribution]] and [[Defined Contribution Plan|savings plans]].
>    - c. [[Hybrid Plan|Hybrid plans]].
>    - d. [[Multi-employer Pension Plan|Multi-employer pension plans]].
>    - e. Other alternative [[Retirement Plan|retirement plans]] such as [[Target Benefit Plan|shared-risk plans]], [[Target Benefit Plan|target-benefit plans]], and jointly [[Sponsored Pension Plan|sponsored pension plans]].
> 3. Perform [[Going-Concern Valuation|periodic valuations]] of [[Going-Concern Valuation|ongoing plans]], calculating [[Normal Cost|normal cost]], actuarial liability, and gains and losses, using a variety of cost methods: (C3)
>    - a. [[Unit Credit|Unit credit]] and [[Projected Unit Credit Method|projected unit credit]].
>    - b. [[Entry Age Normal|Entry age normal]].
>    - c. [[Individual Level Premium|Individual level premium]].
>    - d. Aggregate.
>    - e. [[Attained Age Normal|Attained age normal]].
> 4. Compare and contrast methods described in 2. and determine their [[Appropriateness Factoring|appropriateness factoring]] in the [[Plan Sponsor's|plan sponsor’s]] objectives and regulatory requirements. (D5)
> 5. Calculate actuarially [[Equivalent Benefit|equivalent benefits]], [[Subsidized Benefit|subsidized benefits]], and other [[Ancillary Benefit|ancillary benefits]]. (C3)

> [!example]- Pension legislation {30%}
>
> 1. Describe and apply federal and [[Provincial Pension Benefit Act|provincial pension benefit acts]] and regulations. (B3)
> 2. Describe and apply the various [[Retirement Benefit Arrangement|retirement benefit arrangements]] available to a [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsor]]. (B3)
> 3. Describe and apply applicable [[Agreements Respecting Multi-Jurisdictional Pension Plan|agreements respecting multi-jurisdictional pension plans]] between [[Provincial Regulator|provincial regulators]]. (C5)
> 4. Recognize, define, interpret, apply, calculate, and evaluate [[Minimum Standard|minimum standards]] in respect of the following provisions of [[Registered Pension Plan|registered pension plans]]: (C5)
>    - a. [[Underwriting|Eligibility requirements]].
>    - b. [[Retirement Benefit Formula|Retirement benefit formula]] ([[Uniform Accrual|uniform accrual]] and integration with [[Government Benefit|government benefits]]).
>    - c. [[Employer Cost|Employer cost]] (50 [[Percent Cost Rule|percent cost rule]]).
>    - d. [[Interest]] credited on [[Member Contribution|member contributions]].
>    - e. [[Termination Benefit|Termination benefits]] such as [[Commuted Value|commuted values]].
>    - f. [[Death Benefit|Death benefits]].
>    - g. [[Spousal Protection|Spousal protection]].
>    - h. [[Vesting]] and [[Locking-In Provision|locking-in provisions]].
>    - i. Portability (LIFs, RIFs).
>    - j. [[Pension Credit Splitting|Pension credit splitting]] upon [[Marriage Breakdown|marriage breakdown]].
>    - k. [[Retirement Age|Retirement age]].
>    - l. [[Disclosure Requirements-privacy-right|Disclosure requirements/privacy/rights]] to information.
>    - m. Statement for members (annual or at retirement/termination).
>    - n. Indexation of [[Pension|pensions]].
>    - o. [[Decumulation]].
>    - p. [[Pension Fund Investment|Pension fund investment]] requirements.
>    - q. Remittance of contributions.
>    - r. [[Statutory Required Benefit|Statutory required benefits]] (such as [[Grow-In Benefit|grow-in benefits]] in Ontario).
> 5. [[Produce Funding|Produce funding]] requirements under applicable provincial and [[Federal Minimum Standard Legislation|federal minimum standard legislation]] considering: (C6)
>    - a. [[Going-concern Basis|Going-concern basis]].
>    - b. [[Solvency-hypothetical Wind-Up|Solvency/hypothetical wind-up]].
>    - c. [[Plan Termination|Plan termination]].
>    - d. [[Plan Merger and Spin-Off|Plan mergers]] and [[Plan Merger and Spin-Off|asset transfers]].
>    - e. [[Plan Conversion|Plan conversion]].
>    - f. [[Temporary Funding Relief|Temporary funding relief]] measures.
>    - g. Use of letters of [[Credit|credit]].
>    - h. Additional [[Mandated Liability|mandated liabilities]].
>    - i. [[Solvency Reserve Account|Solvency reserve accounts]].
>    - j. [[Stabilization Provision|Stabilization provisions]] and smoothing techniques.
>    - k. [[Canadian Pension Legislation|Plan amendments]].
>    - l. Restrictions on [[Benefit Payout|benefit payouts]].
> 6. Apply, analyze, and evaluate the following: (C5)
>    - a. [[Partial-full Plan Termination|Partial/full plan termination]].
>    - b. [[Plan Merger and Spin-Off|Plan mergers]] and [[Plan Merger and Spin-Off|asset transfers]].
>    - c. [[Plan Conversion|Plan conversion]].
>    - d. [[Canadian Pension Legislation|Plan amendments]].
>    - e. [[Surplus Ownership-contribution Holiday|Surplus ownership/contribution holiday]].
>    - f. [[Funding Relief|Funding relief]] measures.
>    - g. [[Frequency]] of [[Valuation|valuation]].
>    - h. Use of letters of [[Credit|credit]].
>    - i. Use of [[Solvency Reserves Account|solvency reserves account]].
> 7. Outline the following different [[Rate Regulation|filing requirements]] and reports, including: (B2)
>    - a. [[Annual Information Return]] (AIR).
>    - b. Actuarial [[Valuation Report]] and Actuarial Information Summary (AIS).
>    - c. [[Pension Benefits Guarantee Fund]] (PBGF) Assessment.
>    - d. Other provincially [[Legislated Form|legislated forms]] (e.g., [[Financial Statement]] and Form 8 – [[Investment Information Summary-Form|Investment Information Summary/Form]] 7 – Summary or [[Revised Summary]] of Contributions).
>    - e. Relevant policies and guidance from [[Pension Regulator|pension regulators]].
>    - f. [[Member Communication|Member communications]], including [[Annual Benefit Statement|annual benefit statements]], termination, and [[Retirement Election Form|retirement election forms]].

> [!example]- Professionalism and standards of practice relative to plan funding {10%}
>
> 1. Apply the standards related to communications to [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsors]] and others with an [[Interest|interest]] in an actuary’s results (i.e., participants, auditors, etc.). (C3)
> 2. Explain and apply the [[Professional Conduct Guideline|professional conduct guidelines]]. (B3)
> 3. Explain and apply relevant [[Qualification Standard|qualification standards]]. (B3)
> 4. Demonstrate compliance with requirements regarding the [[Actuarial Communication|actuary’s responsibilities]] to participants, [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsors]], etc. (B2)

> 7. Recommend a course of action to repair a violation of standards or [[Professional Conduct Guideline|professional conduct guidelines]]. (D6)
