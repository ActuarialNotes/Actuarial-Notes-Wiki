A loan of $X$ is repaid with level payments of $R$ payable at the end of each year for $n$ years.

You are given:

- i) The interest paid in year 1 is 797.50.
- ii) The principal repaid in year $n - 4$ is 865.
- iii) The principal outstanding at the end of year $n - 1$ is 1144.50.

Determine $X$.

- A) 9,500
- B) 10,000
- C) 10,500
- D) 11,000
- E) 11,500

## Explanation

The principal outstanding at the end of year $n-1$ is the present value of the one remaining payment:

$$
OB_{n-1} = R\,a_{\overline{1}|} = Rv = 1144.50
$$

The principal repaid in year $t$ is $Rv^{n-t+1}$, so in year $n-4$:

$$
P_{n-4} = Rv^{n-(n-4)+1} = Rv^5 = 865
$$

Dividing the two:

$$
\begin{align*}
v^4 = \frac{Rv^5}{Rv} &= \frac{865}{1144.50} \\
  &= 0.75579 \\
v &= 0.93240 \\
i &= 0.07251
\end{align*}
$$

The interest paid in year 1 is interest on the full loan, $I_1 = Xi = 797.50$, so from (i):

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= \frac{797.50}{i} \\
  &= \frac{797.50}{0.072506} \\
  &= 10{,}999.02
\end{align*}
$$

(Dividing by $i$ rounded to $0.07251$ gives $10{,}998.48$.) Either way $X \approx 11{,}000$, answer D.
