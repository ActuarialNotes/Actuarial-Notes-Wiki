The following information is available from the December 31, 2014 P&C-1 of a property and casualty insurance company. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

PREMIUMS AND CLAIMS (60.20) - Total all lines of business

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Premiums written less return premiums - Direct | 235,000 |
| Premiums written less return premiums - Assumed | 150,000 |
| Premiums written less return premiums - Ceded | 155,000 |
| Net unearned premiums at beginning of year | 105,000 |
| Net unearned premiums at period end | 115,000 |
| Claims incurred including adjustment expenses - Direct | 165,000 |
| Claims incurred including adjustment expenses - Assumed | 105,000 |
| Claims incurred including adjustment expenses - Ceded | 125,000 |

COMMISSIONS (80.10) - Total all lines of business

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total Gross Commissions (line 39) | 65,000 |
| Total Ceded Commissions (line 49) | 35,000 |

STATEMENT OF INCOME (20.30)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Acquisition Expenses - Taxes (line 12) | 7,000 |
| Acquisition Expenses - Other (line 14) | 8,500 |
| General Expenses (line 16) | 12,500 |
| Investment Operations - Income (line 32) | 14,000 |
| Investment Operations - Realized Gains (Losses) (line 33) | 4,500 |
| Investment Operations - Expenses (line 34) | 500 |
| Other Revenues (line 44) | 800 |

- The income tax rate is 22.0%.
- Assume there is no premium deficiency.

Calculate the following:

## Part a (0.75 points)

Net loss ratio

### Explanation
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Net Premium Earned} = {} & \text{Premiums written less return premiums (Direct)} \\ & + \text{Premiums written less return premiums (Assumed)} \\ & - \text{Premiums written less return premiums (Ceded)} \\ & + \text{Net unearned premiums at beginning of year} \\ & - \text{Net unearned premiums at period end} \\ = {} & 220{,}000 \end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Net Claims and Adjustment Expenses} = {} & \text{Claims incurred including adjustment expenses (Direct)} \\ & + \text{Claims incurred including adjustment expenses (Assumed)} \\ & - \text{Claims incurred including adjustment expenses (Ceded)} \\ = {} & 145{,}000 \end{aligned}$$

$$\text{Loss Ratio} = \frac{\text{Net Claims and Adjustment Expenses}}{\text{Net Premiums Earned}} = 65.9\%$$

### Examiner Report
Most candidates that didn't get full marks weren't able to compute the net earned premiums.
They either didn't consider the assumed or ceded premiums or didn't take into account the change in unearned premiums for the calendar year.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Net combined ratio

### Explanation
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Expenses} = {} & \text{Total Gross Commissions} - \text{Total Ceded Commissions} \\ & + \text{Acquisition Expenses (Taxes)} + \text{Acquisition Expenses (Other)} \\ & + \text{General Expenses} \\ = {} & 58{,}000 \end{aligned}$$

$$\text{Combined Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Claims and Expenses}}{\text{Net Premiums Earned}} = 92.3\%$$

Alternatively:

If the candidate allocated underwriting expenses to net written premiums:

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Underwriting/Acquisition Expenses} = {} & \text{Total Gross Commissions} - \text{Total Ceded Commissions} \\ & + \text{Acquisition Expenses (Taxes)} + \text{Acquisition Expenses (Other)} \\ = {} & 45{,}500 \end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned} \text{Net Written Premiums} = {} & \text{Premiums written less return premiums (Direct)} \\ & + \text{Premiums written less return premiums (Assumed)} \\ & - \text{Premiums written less return premiums (Ceded)} \\ = {} & 230{,}000 \end{aligned}$$

- Underwriting/Acquisition Expenses ratio $= 45{,}500 / 230{,}000 = 19.8\%$
- General Expenses ratio $= 12{,}500 / 220{,}000 = 5.7\%$
- Expenses ratio $= 19.8\% + 5.7\% = 25.5\%$
- Combined ratio $= 65.9\% + 25.5\% = 91.4\%$

### Examiner Report
Candidates needed to compute the expense ratio and add it to the net loss ratio to get the combined loss ratio. Common errors were:

- not including net commissions in the expense ratio
- including investment operations expenses or other revenues in the expense ratio.

## Part c (0.75 points)

Net income

### Explanation
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Investment Income} = {} & \text{Investment Operations (Income)} + \text{Investment Operations (Realized Gains (Losses))} \\ & + \text{Investment Operations (Expenses)} \\ = {} & 18{,}000 \end{aligned}$$

(as printed — the 18,000 deducts the investment expenses)

$$\text{Pre-tax income} = \text{Net Premium Earned} - \text{Total Claims and Expenses} + \text{Investment Income} + \text{Other Revenues} = 35{,}800$$

$$\text{Net Income} = \text{Pre-tax income} \times (1 - \text{tax rate}) = 27{,}924$$

### Examiner Report
Most candidates answered this question well. The most common errors were not applying the tax rate, forgetting to include an element in the expenses or investment income calculation and having the right formula without using the right number from what was given in the question.
