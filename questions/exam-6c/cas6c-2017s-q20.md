## Part a (0.5 points)

Define interest rate risk.

### Explanation
- Interest rate risk is the risk associated with a shift in the market yield curve and its resulting impact on the values of assets and liabilities

Alternatively:

- Interest rate risk is the risk arise from change of market value which could have impact on assets and liabilities

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to define interest rate risk, identify that it relates to loss from market changes in interest rates, and understand the relationship between changing interest rates and the value of assets and liabilities.
Common errors include:

- Not mentioning the relationship to assets or liabilities
- Stating that interest rate risk causes a mismatch between assets and liabilities

## Part b (0.5 points)

Contrast effective duration and modified duration.

### Explanation
Effective duration accounts for situations where the cash flows may change as a result of changes in interest rates. Modified duration does not.

Alternatively, by their formulas:

$$\text{Effective duration} = \frac{V^- - V^+}{2 \times V_0 \times \Delta y}$$

$$\text{Modified duration} = \frac{1}{1 + \text{Yield}} \times \frac{\sum t \times PVCF_t}{\text{Market Value}}$$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know the difference between effective duration and modified duration.
Common errors were providing an incorrect definition or formula for these items.

## Part c (1 point)

Calculate the margin required for interest rate risk at target based on a 125 basis point interest rate shock factor.

### Explanation
- Asset $= 900 \times 4.75 \times 1.25\% + 50 \times 25 \times 125\% + 50 \times 3 \times 125\% = 70.9375$ (125% as printed, for 1.25%)
- Liab $= 500 \times 2.5 \times 125\% + 100 \times 2.75 \times 125\% = 19.0625$ (125% as printed, for 1.25%)
- Margin required $= 70.9375 - 19.0625 = 51.875$

Alternatively, through the portfolio durations:

- Duration of rate sensitive assets $= (900 \times 4.75 + 50 \times 25 + 50 \times 3)/1{,}000 = 5.675$
- Duration of rate sensitive liabilities $= (500 \times 2.5 + 100 \times 2.75)/600 = 2.542$
- Effect of 125 bp increase $= \max(1{,}000 \times 5.675 \times .00125 - 600 \times 2.542 \times .0012,\ 0) = 51.87$ (factors as printed)
- Effect of 125 bp decrease $= \max(1{,}000 \times 5.675 \times -.00125 - 600 \times 2.542 \times -.0012,\ 0) = 0$ (factors as printed)
- Margin required $= \max(51.87,\ 0) = 51.87$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to be able to calculate the duration of interest rate sensitive assets and liabilities and margin required for an interest rate shock.
A common error was not applying the 125 basis point shock factor.
