You are given the following information about a sample, $X_1 \ldots X_n$:

- $X_i$'s are all mutually independent
- $X_i \sim \text{Gamma}(\alpha_i, \theta)$, for $i = 1, 2, \ldots n$
- $\alpha_i = \frac{1}{n}$ for all $i$
- $Y = \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i$

Calculate the probability that $Y > \theta$.

- A) Less than 0.2
- B) At least 0.2, but less than 0.4
- C) At least 0.4, but less than 0.6
- D) At least 0.6, but less than 0.8
- E) At least 0.8

## Explanation

Independent gamma variables with a common scale $\theta$ add by adding their shape parameters, so

$$
\begin{align*}
Y &\sim \text{Gamma}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \alpha_i,\ \theta\right) \\
&= \text{Gamma}\left(n \cdot \tfrac{1}{n},\ \theta\right) \\
&= \text{Gamma}(1, \theta)
\end{align*}
$$

A gamma with shape 1 is the exponential distribution with mean $\theta$, whatever the value of $n$. Therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
\Pr(Y > \theta) &= e^{-\theta/\theta} \\
&= e^{-1} \\
&= 0.3679
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.2, 0.4)$, so the answer is **B**.
