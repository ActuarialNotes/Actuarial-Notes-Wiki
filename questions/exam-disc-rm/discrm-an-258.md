An insurer's strategic plan lists four proposed goals. Which one best meets the SMART test (specific, measurable, aggressive, realistic and time-bound)?

- A) Become a better place to work for our claims and underwriting staff
- B) Grow written premium as fast as possible in every line we write
- C) Double commercial auto written premium within the next calendar month
- D) Cut average claim acknowledgment time from 6 days to 3 by June 30

## Explanation

A SMART goal is specific and measurable, aggressive but attainable, and has a deadline. Halving acknowledgment time by a stated date meets all five. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Neither specific nor measurable, and it has no deadline.
- **B** — Has no measure or deadline, and "as fast as possible in every line" is not a specific aim.
- **C** — Is measurable and time-bound but not realistic; goals with little chance of being reached discourage effort rather than motivate it.
