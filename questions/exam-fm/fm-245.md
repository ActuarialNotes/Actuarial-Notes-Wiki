At an annual effective interest rate of 5%, a 10-year annuity-immediate starting with an annual payment of 100 increases each year by 15% of the previous year's payment and has a present value of $X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 597
- B) 772
- C) 1040
- D) 1247
- E) 1484

## Explanation

Geometrically increasing annuity-immediate with first payment 100, growth rate $g = 15\%$, and interest rate $i = 5\%$. The $k$-th payment is $100(1.15)^{k-1}$ at time $k$, so

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= \sum_{k=1}^{10} 100(1.15)^{k-1}(1.05)^{-k} \\
  &= 100\cdot\frac{1-(1.15/1.05)^{10}}{0.05-0.15} \\
  &= 100\cdot\frac{1-2.48362}{-0.10} \\
  &= 100 \times 14.8362 \\
  &= 1483.62
\end{align*}
$$

$X \approx 1484$, answer E.
