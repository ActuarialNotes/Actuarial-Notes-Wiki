Four distinct integers are chosen randomly and without replacement from the first twelve positive integers. $X$ is the random variable representing the second smallest of the four selected integers, and $p$ is the probability function of $X$.

Determine $p(x)$ for $x = 2,3,\ldots,10$.

- A) $\frac{(x-1)(11-x)(12-x)}{495}$
- B) $\frac{(x-1)(11-x)(12-x)}{990}$
- C) $\frac{(x-1)(x-2)(12-x)}{990}$
- D) $\frac{(x-1)(x-2)(12-x)}{495}$
- E) $\frac{(10-x)(11-x)(12-x)}{495}$

## Explanation

For $X=x$: choose 1 from $\{1,\ldots,x-1\}$, include $x$, choose 2 from $\{x+1,\ldots,12\}$:
$$
\begin{align*}
p(x) &= \frac{\binom{x-1}{1}\binom{12-x}{2}}{\binom{12}{4}} \\
     &= \frac{(x-1)\cdot\frac{(12-x)(11-x)}{2}}{495} \\
     &= \frac{(x-1)(11-x)(12-x)}{990}
\end{align*}
$$
