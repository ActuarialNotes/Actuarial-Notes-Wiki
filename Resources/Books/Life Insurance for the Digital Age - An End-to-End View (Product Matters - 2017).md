An article by Nitin Nayak and Stephen Abrokwah of Swiss Re in the November 2017 issue (Issue 108) of Product Matters, the newsletter of the Society of Actuaries' Product Development Section. It gives an overview of innovative solutions supporting an end-to-end underwriting process for life insurance products, in response to the life insurance protection gap among middle market and millennial households.

## Introduction
- Life insurance ownership has declined over the past 30 years and is at a 50-year low, most pronounced among the middle market and millennial households.
- Independent and captive agents have migrated toward supporting mostly high net-worth individuals for larger face amount policies.
- Recommendations include educating customers, reducing friction and waiting times in the buying process, and improving the quality and speed of assessing and pricing mortality risk.

## Conclusion
- Insurers offering instant issue products with no human in the loop are currently limited to simplified-issue products, which limit coverage to lower face amounts at premium rates higher than traditional, fully underwritten products.
- The authors believe continuing improvements in prediction accuracy should allow accelerated underwriting programs to offer higher face amounts at premium rates closer to fully underwritten products.
