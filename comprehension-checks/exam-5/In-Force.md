A catastrophe manager needs to know the insurer's hurricane exposure along the Florida coast on 1 June. Which measure answers that, and why?

- A) Written exposure, since it counts every policy issued during the year
- B) Earned exposure, since it reflects coverage actually provided
- C) Any of them — over a full year the three converge
- D) In-force exposure, because it is a snapshot of what is actually on risk that day
