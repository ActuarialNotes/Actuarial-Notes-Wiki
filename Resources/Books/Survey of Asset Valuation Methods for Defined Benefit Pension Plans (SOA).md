A Society of Actuaries research report on the asset valuation methods used in valuations of defined benefit pension plans, from a survey run in 1998 by the Society's Committee on Retirement Systems Research. Asset valuation methods are classified into four groups and nine specific methods, and the report measures how frequently each is used for U.S. and Canadian plans, by size of plan. It is the first phase of a two-phase research project.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 1d; pp. 5-6 only, which list the types of asset valuation method.

## Executive Summary
- Asset valuation methods are classified into four groups and nine specific methods.
- Approximately 6,000 questionnaires were mailed out and 9,670 plans were included in the study: 9,026 U.S. plans, 612 Canadian plans and 32 other plans.
