A group of 18 patients is tested for diabetes. Each patient independently has a 0.15 probability of testing positive.

Calculate the mode of the number of patients in the group who test positive.

- A) $0$
- B) $1$
- C) $2$
- D) $3$
- E) $4$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the number of patients who test positive. Then $X$ is binomial with $n = 18$ and $p = 0.15$:

$$
P[X = i] = \binom{18}{i}(0.15)^i (0.85)^{18-i}
$$

The mode is the value of $i$ that maximizes this probability. The mean is $np = 18(0.15) = 2.7$, so evaluate the pmf around there:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X = 1] &= 0.1704 \\
P[X = 2] &= 0.2556 \\
P[X = 3] &= 0.2406
\end{align*}
$$

Binomial probabilities rise and then fall, so once the pmf turns over at $i = 2$ the search is finished:

$$
\text{mode} = \mathbf{2}
$$

Choice (D), 3, is the mean $np = 2.7$ rounded to the nearest integer. For a binomial the mode is $\lfloor (n+1)p \rfloor = \lfloor 19(0.15) \rfloor = \lfloor 2.85 \rfloor = 2$, which need not agree with the rounded mean — rounding the mean is a coincidence, not a rule.
