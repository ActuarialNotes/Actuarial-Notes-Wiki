A September 2013 paper, prepared by the Stanford Center on Longevity with the SOA Committee on Post-Retirement Needs and Risks, to help plan sponsor fiduciaries understand existing options and carry out due diligence on retirement income solutions for defined contribution plans. It educates human resources and financial professionals about the issues, solutions and processes of implementing, administering and communicating a retirement income program, and includes stochastic analyses and a discussion of fiduciary issues.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — Pp. 61-88 background only; Outcomes 1a, 1b; 2a, 2d; 3b. The SOA course strategy guide lists it with the readings on participants' decision to take distributions as an annuity or a lump sum.
