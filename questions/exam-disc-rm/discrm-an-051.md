Which statement about agents and brokers is accurate?

- A) A broker legally represents the insurer and can usually bind it.
- B) An agent's authority comes from the insurer, and agents often can bind coverage.
- C) An agent represents the buyer and shops the account among unrelated insurers.
- D) A broker can never also act as an agent of any insurer.

## Explanation

An agent acts for the insurer under an agency agreement, and an agent often has **binding authority**, the power to put coverage in force before the insurer issues the policy. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A broker legally represents the insured and usually cannot bind an insurer on its own.
- **C** — Representing the buyer and shopping the account is what a broker does.
- **D** — Many large firms act as brokers for some insurers and agents for others, and in about half the states brokers are required to be agents of the insurer.
