An insurance company writes insurance in three states. The company experiences the following aggregate loss distributions over the past eight years:

| State | Mean Annual Loss (in millions) | Standard Deviation of Annual Loss (in millions) |
|-------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| U | 147 | 72 |
| V | 57 | 27 |
| W | 54 | 24 |

Assume that the volume of business and the true underlying size of the average loss have not changed for these eight years and will continue to remain stable next year.

Calculate the estimate of next year's aggregate loss for State W using the nonparametric empirical Bayes method.

- A) Less than 60 million
- B) At least 60 million, but less than 65 million
- C) At least 65 million, but less than 70 million
- D) At least 70 million, but less than 75 million
- E) At least 75 million

## Explanation

Every state has the same $n = 8$ years of experience, so this is ordinary (equal-exposure) Bühlmann with $r = 3$ risks.

**Expected value of the process variance** — average the within-state sample variances:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{v} &= \frac{72^2 + 27^2 + 24^2}{3} \\
        &= \frac{5184 + 729 + 576}{3} \\
        &= 2163
\end{align*}
$$

**Overall mean:**

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{\mu} &= \frac{147 + 57 + 54}{3} \\
          &= 86
\end{align*}
$$

**Variance of the hypothetical means** — the between-state variance, less the process-variance contamination:

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{a} &= \frac{\sum_i (\bar{x}_i - \hat{\mu})^2}{r - 1} - \frac{\hat{v}}{n} \\
        &= \frac{61^2 + (-29)^2 + (-32)^2}{2} - \frac{2163}{8} \\
        &= \frac{5586}{2} - 270.4 \\
        &= 2522.6
\end{align*}
$$

**Credibility:**

$$
\begin{align*}
k &= \frac{2163}{2522.6} = 0.8575 \\
Z &= \frac{8}{8 + 0.8575} \\
  &= 0.9032
\end{align*}
$$

**Estimate for State W:**

$$
\begin{align*}
\hat{X}_W &= 0.9032(54) + 0.0968(86) \\
          &= 48.77 + 8.33 \\
          &= 57.1 \text{ million}
\end{align*}
$$

This is less than 60 million, so the answer is **A**.
