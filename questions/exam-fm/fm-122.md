A perpetuity-due with annual payments consists of ten level payments of $X$ followed by a series of increasing payments. Beginning with the eleventh payment, each payment is 1.5% larger than the preceding payment.

Using an annual effective interest rate of 5%, the present value of the perpetuity is 45,000.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 1,679
- B) 1,696
- C) 1,737
- D) 1,763
- E) 1,781

## Explanation

Payments are made at the start of each year, so payment $k$ is made at time $k-1$.

The present value of the ten level payments (times 0 to 9) is

$$X\ddot{a}_{\overline{10}|0.05} = 8.10782X$$

The eleventh payment, at time 10, is 1.5% larger than the tenth, so it is $1.015X$; the twelfth, at time 11, is $1.015^2X$, and so on. With $v = 1/1.05$, the present value of the remaining payments is a geometric series with ratio $1.015v$:

$$X\left(v^{10}(1.015) + v^{11}(1.015)^2 + \cdots\right) = X\frac{v^{10}(1.015)}{1 - 1.015v} = X\frac{1.015/1.05^{10}}{1 - 1.015/1.05} = 18.69366X$$

Then

$$45{,}000 = 8.10782X + 18.69366X = 26.80148X$$

so $X = 45{,}000/26.80148 = 1{,}679$.
