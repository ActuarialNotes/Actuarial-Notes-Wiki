A company’s annual profit, in billions, has a normal distribution with variance equal to the cube of its mean. The probability of an annual loss is 5%.

Calculate the company’s expected annual profit.

- A) 370 million
- B) 520 million
- C) 780 million
- D) 950 million
- E) 1645 million

## Explanation

The profit is $N(\mu,\mu^3)$ in billions, so $P(\text{loss})=\Phi(-\mu/\mu^{3/2})$:

$\Phi(-1/\sqrt\mu)=0.05\Rightarrow1/\sqrt\mu=1.645\Rightarrow\mu=(1/1.645)^2\approx0.370$ billion $=\mathbf{370}$ million
