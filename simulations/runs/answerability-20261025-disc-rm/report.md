### `Concepts/Insurance Producer.md` — 1 question(s)

- `discrm-an-050` (fact): In reviewing loss exposures and needs, a producer identifies exposures the customer may not have considered (such as liability exposures from contracts or leases) and often acts as a consultant on the customer's insurance program.
