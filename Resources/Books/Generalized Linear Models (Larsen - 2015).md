A CAS study note covering MAS-I learning objectives C1–C9. It is the syllabus's applied, insurance-facing treatment of the [[Generalized Linear Model]] — the practitioner's counterpart to the theory in [[An Introduction to Generalized Linear Models (Dobson - 2018)]].

**Available from:** the CAS study-note library.

## What it covers

- Why insurance data breaks ordinary [[Linear Regression]]: skewed [[Severity|severity]], non-negative counts, and variance that grows with the mean
- The three components of a [[Generalized Linear Model]]: the [[Exponential Family]] response, the linear predictor, and the [[Link Function]]
- Choosing a distribution and link for the job — [[Poisson Regression]] for [[Frequency|frequency]], [[Gamma]] for [[Severity|severity]], [[Logistic Regression]] for a binary response, and the [[Tweedie Distribution]] for pure premium
- The [[Dispersion Parameter]] and overdispersion
- Weights and the [[Offset Variable|offset]]: exposure entering a log-link model with its coefficient fixed at one
- [[Model Structure]]: [[Categorical Predictor|categorical, ordinal and continuous predictors]], base levels, banding and [[Interaction|interactions]]
- [[Control Variable|Control variables]] — kept in the model so the effects of interest are measured on like-for-like risks
- Reading the output: [[Parameter Estimate Tables|parameter estimate tables]], [[ANOVA]] tables, and turning coefficients into multiplicative rating relativities
- Model evaluation: [[Deviance]], [[AIC]], [[BIC]], [[R-Squared]], and the [[Likelihood Ratio Test]]
- Diagnostics: [[Residual Plot|residual plots]], [[QQ Plot|QQ plots]], [[Marginal Model Plot|marginal model plots]] and [[Added Variable Plot|added variable plots]]
- [[Variable Selection]] and validation, including [[Cross-Validation]] and the [[Bias-Variance Tradeoff]]

## Where it fits on the syllabus

Section C is 45–55% of the exam, and this note (with [[An Introduction to Generalized Linear Models (Dobson - 2018)]] and [[An Introduction to Statistical Learning (James et al. - 2021)]]) covers all of C1–C9.
