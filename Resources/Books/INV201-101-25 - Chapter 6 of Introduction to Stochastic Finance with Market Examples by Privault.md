A textbook, in its second edition, that presents an introduction to pricing and hedging in discrete and continuous-time financial models, emphasizing both analytical and probabilistic methods. It covers the basics of stochastic calculus for finance and the techniques required to model the time evolution of risky assets, with classical topics such as Black-Scholes pricing, American options, term structure modeling and change of numeraire, and special topics such as exotic options, stochastic volatility and jump processes. The SOA supplies its Chapter 6 as study note INV201-101-25.
