SOA study note RET101-106-25, titled Multi-Employer Plans, supplied for the SOA's RET 101 Retirement Plan Design course. The SOA course strategy guide says it discusses multi-employer plans in the U.S. and Canada, that the specific laws and regulations in those countries will not be tested, and that the general design concepts may be tested and applied in new situations.
