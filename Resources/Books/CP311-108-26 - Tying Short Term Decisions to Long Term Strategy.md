A McKinsey article based on a McKinsey Global Survey of 617 executives and managers on resource allocation, dated 20 May 2024 in its survey version. It reports that only about half of those surveyed say their companies effectively align budgets with corporate strategy, and that those whose organizations link budgets to strategy and take appropriate levels of risk are much more likely to report outperformance on revenue growth and return on capital.
