A limited liability company is the named insured on a CGL (CG 00 01 04 13). For which claim is the person described an insured?

- A) A member who injures a cyclist while driving to the beach on a Sunday with his family
- B) An employee who, while stocking shelves, knocks over and injures a customer
- C) An employee who, while stocking shelves, knocks over and injures a fellow employee
- D) A manager who injures a guest at a party in her own home

## Explanation

Employees are insureds for acts within the scope of their employment, so an employee injuring a customer at work is an insured. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — an LLC's members are insureds only with respect to the conduct of its business. A family outing is personal.
- **C** — employees are not insureds for bodily injury to a co-employee in the course of employment; that injury belongs to workers compensation.
- **D** — an LLC's managers are insureds only with respect to their duties as managers.
