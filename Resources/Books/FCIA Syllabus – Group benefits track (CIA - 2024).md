This document outlines the group benefits (GB)-track exams and modules syllabuses. The Canadian Institute of Actuaries' syllabus for one of the five education tracks to its Fellowship (FCIA) designation: the track's 3 exams — each an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses, with the weight of each topic in parentheses and the level of each objective on the revised Bloom's taxonomy — and its two online modules.

## Group benefits track exams
- Exam F1GB: Product Design and Group Benefits Environment
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Product design (25%)
    - 3. Benefit law and regulation (20%)
    - 4. Insurance company law and regulation (15%)
    - 5. Workers’ compensation (5%)
    - 6. Public and other benefit programs (15%)
    - 7. The U.S. healthcare model (10%)
    - 8. Other healthcare models (10%)
- Exam F2GB: Group Benefits Product Pricing
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Experience studies and predictive modelling (20%)
    - 3. Credibility (10%)
    - 4. Capital requirements (15%)
    - 5. Product pricing (25%)
    - 6. Profitability measures (10%)
    - 7. Financial arrangements in a group insurance plan (10%)
    - 8. Insurance company taxation (10%)
- Exam F3GB: Group Benefits Reserving
    - 1. Principal terms
    - 2. Reserving and claim liabilities (35%)
    - 3. Benefit taxation (15%)
    - 4. Employee benefits accounting (15%)
    - 5. Insurance company financial reporting (10%)
    - 6. Financial and capital management (10%)
    - 7. Asset and liability management (15%)
