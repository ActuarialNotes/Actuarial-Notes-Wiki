An HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured borrows a neighbor's laptop, which would cost \$1,400 to replace, and drops it. The insured's 15-year-old son, in a separate incident, deliberately breaks a classmate's \$300 phone. What does the damage to property of others additional coverage pay in total?

- A) \$0
- B) \$300
- C) \$1,000
- D) \$1,300

## Explanation

Damage to property of others pays, at replacement cost, up to \$1,000 per occurrence for property damage to others' property caused by an insured, whether or not the insured is legally liable. It excludes damage caused intentionally by an insured aged 13 or older. The laptop is paid at \$1,000; the phone is excluded. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** applies Coverage E's exclusion for property in the insured's care to the additional coverage, which exists precisely to pay such losses.
- **B** pays only the phone, reversing the two outcomes.
- **D** pays the phone too, missing the exclusion for intentional damage by an insured 13 or older.
