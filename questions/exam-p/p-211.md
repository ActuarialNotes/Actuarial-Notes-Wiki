A flood insurance company determines that $N$, the number of claims received in a month, is a random variable with $P[N=n]=\frac{2}{3^{n+1}}$, for $n=0,1,2,\ldots$. The numbers of claims received in different months are mutually independent.

Calculate the probability that more than three claims will be received during a consecutive two-month period, given that fewer than two claims were received in the first of the two months.

- A) 0.0062
- B) 0.0123
- C) 0.0139
- D) 0.0165
- E) 0.0185

## Explanation

Let $N_1$ and $N_2$ be the numbers of claims in the first and second months. $P(N_1<2)=\frac{2}{3}+\frac{2}{9}=\frac{8}{9}$.

$N_1=0$: $P(N_2>3)=\frac{1}{81}$; $N_1=1$: $P(N_2>2)=\frac{1}{27}$.

$$
\begin{align*}
P(\text{sum}>3,N_1<2) &= \frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{81}+\frac{2}{9}\cdot\frac{1}{27} \\
                      &= \frac{4}{243}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= \frac{4/243}{8/9} \\
  &= \frac{1}{54} \\
  &\approx \mathbf{0.0185}
\end{align*}
$$
