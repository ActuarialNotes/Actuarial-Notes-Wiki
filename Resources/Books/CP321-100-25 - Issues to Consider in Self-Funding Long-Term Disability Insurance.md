A study note, CP321-100-25, supplied by the Society of Actuaries for CP 321 under the title "Issues to Consider in Self-Funding Long-Term Disability Insurance". According to the course strategy guide, it has a couple of pages on underwriting and funding methods for disability income, like Chapter 29 of Skwire's Group Insurance, and is read with the group disability readings of the plan and product provisions and manual rates topics.

> - [[Exam CP-321 (SOA)|Exam CP-321]] — Outcomes 2e; the whole note.
