The 2025 Property and Casualty Practice Note of the American Academy of Actuaries, prepared by the Committee on Property and Liability Financial Reporting (COPLFR) of the Casualty Practice Council. COPLFR updates it annually to discuss changes in the NAIC Annual Statement Instructions—Property/Casualty regarding the Actuarial Opinion, the Actuarial Opinion Summary and the Actuarial Report. It is not an actuarial standard of practice and is not binding upon any actuary.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6U (CAS)|Exam 6U]] — objectives C5–C9; the 2025 practice note, excluding Appendix I.1.3 (NAIC Title SAO Instructions) and Appendix IV (SSAPs); the SSAPs reproduced in Appendix IV are separate readings (the content outline lists SSAPs 5R, 9, 53, 62R and 66 there as readings for tasks C1–C2).
> - [[Exam GI-302 (SOA)|Exam GI-302]] — Outcomes 4c; the 2025 practice note, excluding Appendix I Section I.3, Appendix II Section IV and Appendix IV (as the syllabus states it)
