The loss on an automobile policy is normally distributed with mean 2500 and standard deviation 500.

The premium $P$ for the policy is set such that the probability that a loss exceeds $P$ is 5%.

Calculate the amount by which $P$ exceeds the median loss.

- A) $822$
- B) $980$
- C) $2500$
- D) $3322$
- E) $3480$

## Explanation

A 5% chance of exceeding $P$ makes $P$ the 95th percentile of the loss, where $z = 1.6449$:

$$
P = 2500 + 1.6449(500) = 3322.45
$$

For a normal distribution the median equals the mean, so the median loss is 2500. The excess is

$$
3322.45 - 2500 = \mathbf{822.45}
$$

Choice (D), 3322, is the premium $P$ itself rather than the amount by which it exceeds the median. Notice that once the median is known to be the mean, the answer is just $1.6449\sigma = 822$ — the subtraction cancels the mean entirely, so the premium's absolute level never enters.
