A reading in the Society of Actuaries' GH 301 course on the MassHealth Social Determinants of Health risk adjustment model, Version 4.0. The syllabus assigns it, with the UMass report on the 2017 payment model, to the learning outcome on evaluating applications of SDOH-derived statistics in actuarial models. The course strategy guide describes these two papers as covering the use of SDOH and other variables in the Massachusetts Medicaid risk adjustment model.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-301 (SOA)|Exam GH-301]] — Outcomes 5d; pp. 1-9.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> No copy of this reading could be found outside the SOA's study materials, and the model's version 4.0 documentation is not among the files MassHealth lists for its risk adjustment methodology. This page records its citation and assignment from the syllabus.
