## Suggested edits

- **`Concepts/Expected Value of Process Variance.md`** — Bullet list, replace the 'Estimated from data by empirical Bayes' bullet
  - Change: - Estimated from data by [[Empirical Bayes Credibility|empirical Bayes]] as the pooled within-risk sample variance. If risk $i$ has $m_i$ exposure units with sample variance $s_i^2$ (Bühlmann-Straub, unequal exposures): $$\hat v = \frac{\sum_i (m_i-1)s_i^2}{\sum_i (m_i-1)}$$ With equal $n$ per risk this reduces to the plain average of the $s_i^2$.
  - Why: Fixes masii-2019s-q4, where the pooled-variance formula with unequal exposures was not on the page.
- **`Concepts/Variance of Hypothetical Means.md`** — New bullet after 'The overall mean is μ = E[μ(Θ)]'
  - Change: - **Estimating $a$ from data (Bühlmann-Straub, $r$ risks, exposures $m_i$, $m=\sum m_i$):** $\hat\mu = \sum m_i\bar x_i/m$ and $$\hat a = \frac{\sum_i m_i(\bar x_i-\hat\mu)^2-(r-1)\hat v}{m-\sum_i m_i^2/m}$$ Then $\hat k=\hat v/\hat a$ and $Z_i = m_i/(m_i+\hat k)$, using risk $i$'s own exposure, not a common $n$. See [[Empirical Bayes Credibility]].
  - Why: Supplies the VHM estimator and the per-risk Z used in masii-2019s-q4.
- **`Concepts/Empirical Bayes Credibility.md`** — Add a worked example callout at the end
  - Change: > [!example]- Bühlmann-Straub with Unequal Exposures {Example} > Three fleets have vehicles m = 150, 272, 511, means 3130.20, 2278.53, 2950.50, and SDs 579.62, 139.10, 472.08. Fleet X will have 175 vehicles next period. > > [!answer]- > > v̂ = 181,677; μ̂ = 2783.49; â = 184,233; k = 0.986; Z_X = 150/150.986 = 0.9935. > > Per-vehicle estimate = 0.9935(3130.20) + 0.0065(2783.49) = 3127.94. > > **Multiply by next period's exposure:** 175 × 3127.94 = 547,390. The credibility estimate is per exposure unit; scale it by the *future* exposure, not the past exposure.
  - Why: Shows the full empirical Bayes pipeline and the exposure scale-up step that probably produced answer A.
- **`Concepts/Variance of Hypothetical Means.md`** — End of bullet list
  - Change: - Contrast with [[Limited Fluctuation Credibility]], where $Z=\min\{1,\sqrt{n/n_{\text{full}}}\}$ and $n_{\text{full}}=(z_{(1+p)/2}/k)^2$. Halving the tolerance $k$ quadruples $n_{\text{full}}$ and so halves $Z$. Create this page if it does not exist.
  - Why: Gives a route from the Bühlmann pages to the limited fluctuation content needed for Statement III of masii-an-024.
- **`Concepts/Expected Value of Process Variance.md`** — Bullet 'Large v means...'
  - Change: Append: 'Contrast: a larger VHM $a$ (with $v$, $n$ fixed) lowers $k$ and **raises** $Z$. Do not mix up which variance lowers credibility.'
  - Why: Reinforces the direction-of-effect point tested by Statement II of masii-an-024.
