A technical paper of October 26, 2021 from the Center for Consumer Information and Insurance Oversight (CCIIO) of the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services on possible changes to the HHS-operated ACA risk adjustment models. It gives further detail and analyses on the model updates proposed in the 2022 Payment Notice, and information on HHS's ongoing evaluation of the cost-sharing reduction induced demand factors in the state payment transfer formula.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-301 (SOA)|Exam GH-301]] — Risk Adjustment, outcome 3a; Ch. 1, 5 & Appendix A.

## Overview
- The ACA established a permanent risk adjustment program to minimize the negative effects of adverse selection, transferring funds from issuers that enroll lower-than-average risk populations to issuers that enroll higher-than-average risk populations.
- Chapter 1 explains how the models currently work and the focus on improving predictive accuracy for certain subpopulations; Chapters 2 to 4 explore improvements for the lowest-risk enrollees, partial-year enrollees and the highest-risk enrollees; Chapter 5 gives the combined results.
