The aggregate loss experience of an insurer's book of business is described by the following distribution function:

$$F(x) = x^{0.25} \quad \text{where } 0 \le x \le 1$$

## Part a (1 point)

Derive an exposure curve from the above cumulative distribution function.

### Explanation
$$G(d) = \frac{\int_0^d 1 - F(x)\,dx}{\int_0^1 1 - F(x)\,dx} = \frac{\int_0^d 1 - x^{0.25}\,dx}{\int_0^1 1 - x^{0.25}\,dx} = \frac{x - 0.8x^{1.25}\Big|_0^d}{x - 0.8x^{1.25}\Big|_0^1} = \frac{d - 0.8 \cdot d^{1.25}}{1 - 0.8 \cdot 1^{1.25}}$$

$$= 5d - 4d^{1.25}$$

Alternatively:

$$F(x) = 1 - \frac{G'(x)}{G'(0)} = x^{0.25}$$

$$G'(0)\left(1 - x^{0.25}\right) = G'(x)$$

$$\frac{1}{G'(0)} = 0.2 = E(X) \qquad \Rightarrow G'(0) = 5$$

$$\Rightarrow G(x) = \int_0^d G'(x) = \int_0^d G'(0)\left(1 - x^{0.25}\right)dx = 5\int_0^d \left(1 - x^{0.25}\right)dx = 5d - 4d^{1.25} \text{ (as printed)}$$

### Examiner Report
Many candidates attempted to validate whether the given CDF was an exposure curve (ex. defining the properties required to be an exposure curve) rather (as printed) deriving an exposure curve from the given CDF which was what the item asked for.

Some candidates set up most of the integration correctly with either an error in the limits of integration, or did not complete solving it.

The majority of candidates received at least some credit for this item.

## Part b (1 point)

Given that the maximum possible loss is \$2,000,000, use the derived exposure curve in part a. above to determine the ratio of pure risk premium in the layer \$1,000,000 excess of \$500,000.

### Explanation
$$G\left(\frac{1.5M}{2M}\right) - G\left(\frac{0.5M}{2M}\right) = G(0.75) - G(0.25)$$

$$= \left(5 \cdot 0.75 - 4 \cdot 0.75^{1.25}\right) - \left(5 \cdot 0.25 - 4 \cdot 0.25^{1.25}\right)$$

$$= 0.4153$$

### Examiner Report
The vast majority of candidates identified the layers correctly. Regardless of the exposure curve defined in part a, work was followed through to determine partial or full credit. Candidates whose chosen exposure curve produced a final answer between 0 and 1, and followed through all steps correctly, received full credit.

The majority of candidates received full credit.
