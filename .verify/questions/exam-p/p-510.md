- claim: the answer must be at least E[Y] = 0.70 ... it must be at most E[X] + E[Y] = 1.20. Those two bounds alone leave only choice (C).
- evidence: From the table on questions PDF p.206: E[Y] = 1(0.16+0.11+0.03) + 2(0.06+0.09+0.05) = 0.70 and E[X] = 1(0.10+0.11+0.09) + 2(0.02+0.03+0.05) = 0.50, so the stated bounds are right, but they are non-strict (at least / at most), and the closed interval [0.70, 1.20] contains option B (0.70), C (0.87) and D (1.20). The main computation (0.87, matching SOA solutions PDF p.140) is unaffected.
