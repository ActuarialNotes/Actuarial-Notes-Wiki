A paper by Riccardo Rebonato in Quantitative Finance (volume 19, issue 5, pages 827 to 842), published online on 14 November 2018. It presents an approach to stress testing that is both practically implementable and solidly rooted in well-established financial theory, in a Bayesian-net context that can be extended to other settings. It shows how consistency and continuity conditions are satisfied, how a scenario's result can be cascaded from a small number of macrofinancial variables to the constituents of a granular portfolio, and how an approximate but robust estimate of a scenario's likelihood can be obtained.
