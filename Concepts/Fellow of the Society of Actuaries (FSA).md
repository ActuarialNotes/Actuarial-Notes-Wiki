## Fellow of the Society of Actuaries (FSA)

**FSA** is the fellowship credential of the **Society of Actuaries (SOA)** — the senior of its two designations. A Fellow has completed everything required of an [[Associate of the Society of Actuaries (ASA)|Associate]] and then specialised: the fellowship stage is where a candidate picks a practice area and is examined on the way that business is actually priced, reserved and regulated.

## Requirements

> [!answer]- The FSA pathway
> - **Every [[Associate of the Society of Actuaries (ASA)|ASA]] requirement**
> - **A two-course sequence (101 and 201) in one practice area**, plus **two further technical courses**
>     - The practice areas are Corporate Finance and ERM (CFE), Group and Health Insurance (GH), General Insurance (GI), Individual Life and Annuities (ILA), Investment (INV) and Retirement Benefits (RET); the two extra courses may come from another area or from the cross-practice (CP) catalogue.
> - **Decision Making and Communications (DMAC)**
>     - The course on writing and presenting actuarial work to the people who act on it.
> - **Fellowship Admissions Course (FAC)**
>     - The professionalism course that closes out the pathway.

Note the split with the Casualty Actuarial Society: general insurance is a *specialty track* inside the SOA's fellowship, while for the CAS it is the whole syllabus — see [[Fellow of the Casualty Actuarial Society (FCAS)|FCAS]].
