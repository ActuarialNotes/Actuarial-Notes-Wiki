An issues paper (June 2011) from the Credit Scoring Working Group of the Canadian Council of Insurance Regulators (CCIR). It sets out CCIR's understanding of insurers' use of credit-based insurance scores, the types of consumer risks that can potentially arise and the rules that currently exist in response to them, and seeks the views of consumers and the industry on those risks.
