The definitive guide to flexible benefit programs in Canada, in a third edition published in 2007 and edited by Robert J. McKay of Hewitt Associates. It offers everything organizations need to know about designing, implementing, communicating and administering a flexible benefits program, with new material on retiree flexible benefit programs, maintaining and revitalizing an existing plan, flex for executives, provincial considerations, case studies and flexible benefit developments in eight countries.
