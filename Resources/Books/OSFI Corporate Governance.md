## What the guideline sets out

- **The board** is responsible for the institution's business plan, strategy and **risk appetite**; it approves and oversees them, provides challenge, advice and guidance to senior management, and should be **independent** and provide objective oversight. The role of **Board Chair should be separate from the CEO**. Senior management implements the board's decisions.
- **Risk governance** — the institution should have a **Risk Appetite Framework**, a **Board Risk Committee** to oversee risk management, and a senior officer (**CRO** or equivalent) responsible for the risk management function.
- **The audit committee** — and its relationship with the **[[Appointed Actuary]]**, whose role the guideline points to [[OSFI AA|Guideline E-15]] for.

## Annex B — the Risk Appetite Framework

The assigned annex. The framework is the structure through which the board sets and the institution operates within its risk appetite:

- **Risk appetite** — the aggregate level and types of risk the institution is willing to accept in pursuit of its business objectives.
- **Risk limits** — the allocation of that appetite to businesses, products and risk types, at a granularity management can act on.
- **Risk capacity** — the maximum risk the institution *could* absorb given its capital, liquidity and operational capability. Appetite must sit inside capacity.
- **Roles and responsibilities** for setting, monitoring and enforcing the framework, and escalation when a limit is breached.

## Where it connects

Risk appetite is the hinge between governance and everything quantitative on this syllabus: the **[[Internal Target Capital Ratio|internal capital target]]** is where appetite becomes a number ([[OSFI Target Capital]]); **[[ORSA]]** is management's assessment against it; **[[FCT]]** tests whether it survives adverse scenarios; and the **Gross Underwriting Limit Policy** ([[OSFI Concentration]]) and the **Reinsurance Risk Management Policy** ([[OSFI Reinsurance]]) are both required to be consistent with it.
