A chapter of the IAA Risk Book, developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association (version 2.0, May 2023). It describes codes of professional conduct and professional standards of qualification and practice: the benefits of actuarial standards, how standard-setters and regulators interact, local and IAA model standards, and the enforcement of standards.
