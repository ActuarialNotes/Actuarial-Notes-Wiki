You are fitting a Linear Mixed Model to a set of 50 observations. Your model is of the form $Y_i = X_i\beta + Z_i u_i + \varepsilon_i$.

Your model includes 3 fixed covariates and 2 random covariates. The variance-covariance matrix of $u_i$ is $D$.

Determine the dimensions of the matrix $D$.

- A) 2 rows and 2 columns
- B) 3 rows and 3 columns
- C) 5 rows and 5 columns
- D) 50 rows and 50 columns
- E) The answer is not given by (A), (B), (C), or (D)

## Explanation

In the model $Y_i = X_i\beta + Z_i u_i + \varepsilon_i$, the vector $u_i$ holds subject $i$'s random effects — one entry per random covariate — and $D$ is its variance-covariance matrix. With $q$ random covariates, $u_i$ is $q \times 1$ and $D$ is $q \times q$.

The number of fixed covariates (3) sets the length of $\beta$, and the 50 observations set the dimensions of $Y$ and of the residual covariance matrix; neither affects $D$. With $q = 2$ random covariates, $D$ has 2 rows and 2 columns: the two random-effect variances on the diagonal and their covariance off it.

The answer is **A**.
