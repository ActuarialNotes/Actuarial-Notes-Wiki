A small manufacturer with a limited insurance budget sorts its possible coverages into essential, desirable and available insurance. Which coverage belongs in the essential category?

- A) Coverage for theft of small hand tools, which happens a few times a year
- B) Coverage for a storage shed whose loss would be only an inconvenience
- C) Workers compensation insurance, which state law requires it to carry
- D) Coverage for a loss that would cause financial difficulty but not bankruptcy

## Explanation

**Essential insurance** is coverage required by law or by contract, such as workers compensation, and coverage against losses that would threaten the firm's survival. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Small, frequent thefts are slight losses better retained and controlled; at most this is available insurance.
- **B** — A loss that would only be an inconvenience is the definition of available insurance.
- **D** — Losses that would cause financial difficulty but not bankruptcy are desirable insurance.
