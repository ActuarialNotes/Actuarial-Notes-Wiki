A note on enterprise risk management for life and non-life insurers, published by the International Actuarial Association on 31 March 2009. It offers a framework that is conceptually straightforward and built on practical principles that can be implemented in manageable steps, a series of building blocks to move from basic to advanced ERM. The note is intended to support the IAIS Standards and Guidance Notes for insurers by building understanding among actuaries and other risk management professionals of ERM practices and the challenges of implementing them.
