![[Economic Capital for Life Insurance Companies (SOA Research Paper - 2016) - Cover.svg]]

A research paper, dated October 2016 and sponsored by the SOA Committee on Finance Research, that addresses key questions on the calculation of economic capital (EC) for long-term life insurers. It discusses the drivers of economic capital, the development of an internal EC framework and the uses of EC within an insurance company, and is a refreshed edition of a 2008 EC report that can be read as a standalone document.
