An article in The Journal of Fixed Income, volume 2, issue 2 (September 1992), pages 29-44, on the interest rate risk exposure of interest rate-contingent claims. Its abstract observes that different types of instrument are affected differently by yield curve movements, and that understanding each instrument's exposure lets portfolio managers evaluate interest rate bets and control risk.
