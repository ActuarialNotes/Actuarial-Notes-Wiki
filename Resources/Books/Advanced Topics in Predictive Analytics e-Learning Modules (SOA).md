The e-Learning modules of the Society of Actuaries' Advanced Topics in Predictive Analytics (ATPA) course. The SOA says the modules provide support designed to enhance candidates' knowledge from the Statistics for Risk Modeling (SRM) and Predictive Analytics (PA) exams' learning objectives and readings, and clarify its expectations of a successful ATPA assessment submission. Candidates get access by registering for the ATPA assessment, and keep it until the submission deadline for their version.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam ATPA (SOA)|Exam ATPA]] — e-Learning Modules: all required resources are embedded in the e-Learning modules. The syllabus says its learning objectives and outcomes follow the four modules of the e-Learning support provided.

> [!note] Contents unavailable
> The modules are available only to candidates registered for the ATPA assessment, so their content and titles could not be read. This page records the citation and assignment from the syllabus and the SOA's description of the modules.
