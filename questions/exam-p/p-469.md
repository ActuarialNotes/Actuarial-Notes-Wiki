Random variable $X$ follows a uniform distribution with mean 12 and 75th percentile 18.

Calculate $\text{Var}(X)$.

- A) $24$
- B) $36$
- C) $48$
- D) $144$
- E) $192$

## Explanation

Let the distribution be uniform on $[a,b]$ with length $L = b - a$. A uniform distribution is symmetric, so its mean is the midpoint:

$$
\frac{a+b}{2} = 12
$$

Percentiles sit at proportional positions along the interval, so the 75th percentile is three-quarters of the way from $a$ to $b$. Measured from the *midpoint*, it lies a quarter of the length above:

$$
\begin{align*}
12 + \frac{L}{4} &= 18 \\
\frac{L}{4} &= 6 \\
L &= 24
\end{align*}
$$

so the interval is $[0, 24]$. The variance of a uniform distribution is the squared length over 12:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X) &= \frac{24^2}{12} \\
              &= \mathbf{48}
\end{align*}
$$

Working from the midpoint is what makes this quick: the offset of 6 is a quarter of the interval, not a half. Choice (D), 144, is $L^2/4 = 576/4$, and choice (B), 36, is $6^2$ — treating the distance from the mean to the 75th percentile as the standard deviation. For this distribution the true standard deviation is $\sqrt{48} = 6.93$.
