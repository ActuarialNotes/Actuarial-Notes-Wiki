A perpetuity-immediate has monthly payments that increase by 5% every 12 payments. The initial 12 payments are 500 each.

The present value of this perpetuity-immediate, using an annual effective interest rate of 8% is $X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 191,881
- B) 197,637
- C) 200,009
- D) 207,231
- E) 209,546

## Explanation

The monthly effective rate equivalent to 8% a year is

$$
\begin{align*}
j &= 1.08^{1/12} - 1 \\
  &= 0.00643403
\end{align*}
$$

The present value of the first year's payments, at the start of that year, is:

$$
\begin{align*}
500\,a_{\overline{12}|0.00643403} &= 500(11.512858) \\
  &= 5756.429
\end{align*}
$$

Each later year's twelve payments are 5% larger than the year before, so their value at the start of their year is 5% larger too: $5756.429$ at time 0, $5756.429(1.05)$ at time 1, $5756.429(1.05)^{2}$ at time 2, and so on. This perpetuity can therefore be thought of as a geometrically increasing perpetuity-due with first payment 5756.429, growing at 5% a year and valued at 8%. The present value is:

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= 5756.429\left[\frac{1}{0.08 - 0.05}\right](1.08) \\
  &= 5756.429(36) \\
  &= 207{,}231.44
\end{align*}
$$
