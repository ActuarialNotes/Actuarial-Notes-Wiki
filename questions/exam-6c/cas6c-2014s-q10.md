Describe the advantages and disadvantages of foreign participation in the Canadian insurance industry.

### Explanation
**Advantages**

- Increases competition which leads to more affordability
- Increases innovation as new ideas are brought in to better serve customers

**Disadvantages**

- Runs counter to goal of federal government to promote Canadian ownership
- Harder to verify financial resources of parent

Alternatively:

**Advantages**

- More competition resulting in more availability of insurance
- More competition allows premiums to be competitive, making them more affordable to customers

**Disadvantages**

- Foreign parent failure is the main cause of Canadian insolvency
- Domestic funds and profit are being transferred to foreign countries

### Examiner Report
Due to open ended nature of the question, most candidates were able to get at least partial points. In the advantages, some candidates simply stated increased competition without specifying the advantage this brings such as lower rates and increased availability; in such cases the answer wasn't accepted.
