A reference for designing and implementing employee benefits, covering everything from general objectives to costs. The seventh edition, published by McGraw Hill in 2011, brings it up to date on legislative changes such as the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act and focuses on health and group benefits: health benefits, life insurance, work/life programs, social insurance programs, financial management of group and health benefit plans, benefit administration, and issues such as retiree welfare benefits, small company benefits and multiemployer plans.
