Given the following information as of December 31, 2011:

| Accident Year | On-level Premiums | Cumulative Paid Loss | Fitted Paid Emergence Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2008 | \$1,300,000 | \$600,000 | 70% |
| 2009 | 1,200,000 | 350,000 | 45% |
| 2010 | 1,200,000 | 200,000 | 25% |
| 2011 | 1,300,000 | 75,000 | 10% |

- Parameter standard deviation: 300,000
- Process variance/mean scale parameter ($\sigma^2$): 10,000

## Part a (2 points)

Estimate the total loss reserve using the Cape Cod method.

### Explanation
The fitted emergence pattern is the percent *paid*, so used-up premium = on-level premium × % paid, and the Cape Cod (Stanard-Bühlmann) ELR is total paid loss over total used-up premium.

| AY | (1) On-level premium | (2) Paid loss | (3) % paid | (4) = (1) × (3) Used-up premium | (5) = (1) × ELR × [1 - (3)] Loss reserve |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2008 | 1,300,000 | 600,000 | 0.70 | 910,000 | 254,122 |
| 2009 | 1,200,000 | 350,000 | 0.45 | 540,000 | 430,053 |
| 2010 | 1,200,000 | 200,000 | 0.25 | 300,000 | 586,436 |
| 2011 | 1,300,000 | 75,000 | 0.10 | 130,000 | 762,367 |
| Total | | 1,225,000 | | 1,880,000 | 2,032,978 |

$$\text{ELR} = \frac{\sum (2)}{\sum (4)} = \frac{1{,}225{,}000}{1{,}880{,}000} = 0.6516$$

$$\text{Reserve} = \sum \text{ELR} \times (1 - \%\text{ paid}) \times \text{OLEP} = 0.6516 \times \left[0.30(1.3\text{M}) + 0.55(1.2\text{M}) + 0.75(1.2\text{M}) + 0.90(1.3\text{M})\right] = 2{,}032{,}978.72$$

### Examiner Report
The a. part of this question was a basic question on the Cape Cod methodology. Common errors included errors in calculation, and failure to recognize that the fitted emergence pattern was the % used (not the % unused).

## Part b (0.5 points)

Calculate the process standard deviation of the reserve estimate in part a. above.

### Explanation
$$\text{Process variance} = \sigma^2 \sum_i R_i = 10{,}000 \times 2{,}032{,}978 = 2.033 \times 10^{10}$$

$$\text{Process SD} = \sqrt{2.033 \times 10^{10}} = 142{,}583$$

### Examiner Report
The b. part requires the understanding that process variance = σ2 × mean and that standard deviation is the square root of the variance. Common errors included misunderstanding of these concepts, as well as arithmetic errors.

## Part c (1 point)

Calculate the total standard deviation and the coefficient of variation of the reserve estimate.

### Explanation
Total variance is process variance plus parameter variance (add variances, not standard deviations):

$$\text{Total SD} = \sqrt{2.033 \times 10^{10} + 300{,}000^2} = 332{,}159$$

$$\text{CV} = \frac{\text{Total SD}}{\text{Reserve}} = \frac{332{,}159}{2{,}032{,}978} = 0.163$$

### Examiner Report
The c. part requires the understanding that total variance is the sum of parameter and process variance, and that standard deviation is the square root of the variance. Common errors included adding the parameter standard deviation to the process deviation to find the total standard deviation, general misunderstanding of these concepts, as well as arithmetic errors.
