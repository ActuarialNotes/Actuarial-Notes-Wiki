An insurer filed the following information in support of a rate change to be effective October 1, 2015:

- All policies are semi-annual.
- The new rates will be in effect for one year.
- The indicated rate change from the prior rate review was 7%, with a target effective date of July 1, 2014.
- The implemented rate change from the prior rate review was 2.5%, with an effective date of October 1, 2014.
- The full credibility standard is 60,000 exposures over two years.
- The insurer uses the square root rule for partial credibility.
- The complement of credibility is selected using the trended present rates method.
- There have been no significant changes to paid loss patterns over the past four years.
- Variable expense ratio = 20%.
- Fixed expense ratio = 7%.
- LAE provision = 3% of loss.
- Profit and contingencies provision = 6%.
- Annual premium trend = 3%.
- Annual loss trend = 5%.
- All losses and subrogation/salvage recoveries are fully developed at 48 months.

| Calendar/Accident Year | Earned Exposures | On-Level Earned Premium ($000) | Gross Reported Loss Evaluated at December 31, 2014 ($000) | Ratio of Subrogation/Salvage Received to Paid Loss Evaluated at December 31, 2014 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 20,700 | 15,900 | 17,500 | 0.471 |
| 2014 | 23,200 | 17,500 | 18,800 | 0.314 |

Gross Reported Loss Development Factors:

| Accident Year | 12-24 | 24-36 | 36-48 | 48-Ult |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | 1.055 | 1.047 | 1.018 | 1.000 |
| 2011 | 1.052 | 1.043 | 1.020 | |
| 2012 | 1.057 | 1.048 | | |
| 2013 | 1.061 | | | |

Ratio of Subrogation/Salvage Received to Paid Loss Development Factors:

| Accident Year | 12-24 | 24-36 | 36-48 | 48-Ult |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | 1.012 | 1.006 | 1.006 | 1.000 |
| 2011 | 1.010 | 1.006 | 1.004 | |
| 2012 | 1.536 | 1.004 | | |
| 2013 | 1.500 | | | |

Calculate the indicated rate change and justify any assumptions made.

### Explanation
**Gross reported loss development.** The year-to-year variations look random, so select a straight average at each age:

| | 12-24 | 24-36 | 36-48 | 48-Ult |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Selected | 1.05625 | 1.046 | 1.019 | 1.000 |
| Age to ultimate | 1.126 (12 mos) | 1.066 (24 mos) | 1.019 (36 mos) | 1.000 |

**Salvage and subrogation development.** There appears to have been a large change at 12-24 months in 2012; 2013 is consistent with it, so select a 2-year average there on the assumption the change persists. The other ages are fairly stable — select all-year averages:

| | 12-24 | 24-36 | 36-48 | 48-Ult |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Selected | 1.518 | 1.005 | 1.005 | 1.000 |
| Age to ultimate | 1.534 (12 mos) | 1.1036 (24 mos) | 1.005 (36 mos) | 1.000 |

**Trend period.** Trend losses from the average accident date of each historical year (July 1) to the average accident date of the future period — the new rates are in effect from October 1, 2015 for one year on semi-annual policies, so the midpoint is July 1, 2016.

| AY | (1) Reported ($000) | (2) Ult LDF | (3) Trend | (4) = (1)(2)(3) Trended losses | (5) = 0.03 × (4) LAE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 17,500 | 1.066 | 1.053 | 21,593 | 648 |
| 2014 | 18,800 | 1.126 | 1.052 | 23,335 | 700 |

| AY | (6) S+S Ratio | (7) S+S LDF | (8) = (6)(7) Ult S+S Ratio | (9) = (4)(8) Ult S+S | (10) = (4)+(5)-(9) Net Trended Loss + LAE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 0.471 | 1.01036 | 0.476 | 10,276 | 11,965 |
| 2014 | 0.314 | 1.534 | 0.482 | 11,238 | 12,797 |

| AY | OLEP | Trend | Trended OLEP | Trended Loss + LAE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 15,900 | 1.033 | 17,374 | 11,965 |
| 2014 | 17,500 | 1.032 | 18,566 | 12,797 |
| **Total** | | | **35,940** | **24,762** |

$$\text{Loss Ratio} = \frac{24{,}762}{35{,}940} = 0.689$$

**Credibility.**

$$Z = \sqrt{\frac{20{,}700 + 23{,}200}{60{,}000}} = 0.855$$

**Indicated change on the insurer's own data**, with the fixed expense ratio added and the variable expense and profit provisions in the denominator:

$$\frac{0.689 + 0.07}{1 - 0.20 - 0.06} - 1 = 2.56\%$$

**Complement of credibility** — trended present rates. Trend from the target effective date of the last indication (July 1, 2014) to the current target date (October 1, 2015), a span of 1.25 years, and adjust for the shortfall between the 7% indicated and the 2.5% implemented:

$$\left(\frac{1.07}{1.025}\right)\left(\frac{1.05}{1.03}\right)^{1.25} = 1.069$$

**Credibility-weighted indicated change:**

$$0.855(1.0256) + (1 - 0.855)(1.069) - 1 = 3.2\%$$

Alternatively:

A second accepted approach develops each year's gross losses to ultimate, nets salvage and subrogation off the *ultimate* rather than off the trended figure, and then trends the net result:

| AY | Prem | Trend | Trended OLEP | Loss | CDF | Ult Gross Loss | S/S ratio | CDF | Ult S/S ratio | Ult Loss Net S/S |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 15,900 | ×1.033 | 17,374 | 17,500 | 1.065874 | 18,653 | 0.471 | 1.01033 | 0.4759 | 9,776 |
| 2014 | 17,500 | ×1.032 | 18,566 | 18,800 | 1.12556 | 21,161 | 0.314 | 1.5337 | 0.4816 | 10,969 |

| AY | Ult Loss Net S/S | Trend | Trended Net Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 9,776 | ×1.033 | 11,317 |
| 2014 | 10,969 | ×1.032 | 12,094 |

$$\text{Loss Ratio} = 0.6514$$

$$\frac{0.6514(1.03) + 0.07}{1 - 0.20 - 0.06} - 1 = 0.1273\%$$

$$\text{Credibility-weighted} = 0.855(0.001273) + (1 - 0.855)(0.0693) = 1.11\%$$
