Identify and briefly describe four considerations for defining the capital available of a company for the purpose of measuring capital adequacy.

### Explanation
- availability: the extent to which the capital element is fully paid in and available to absorb losses;

- permanence: the period for, and extent to which, the capital element is available;

- absence of encumbrances and mandatory servicing costs: the extent to which the capital element is free from mandatory payments or encumbrances;

- subordination: the extent to which and the circumstances under which the capital element is subordinated to the rights of policyholders and creditors of the insurer in an insolvency or winding-up

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to know the primary considerations that define the capital available for the purpose of measuring capital adequacy.

Most candidates were able to identify and describe at least 2 considerations.
