An office building classification is rated on area at \$30 per 1,000 square feet. The building measures 50 by 80 feet on the outside and has three floors above ground and a basement, 70% of which is used for storage and the heating plant. What is the general liability premium?

- A) \$120
- B) \$360
- C) \$480
- D) \$3,600

## Explanation

Area is the outside horizontal dimensions times the number of floors, including the basement, but any basement or floor at least half used for storage, building maintenance or heating is left out.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Area} &= 50 \times 80 \times 3 \\
&= 12{,}000 \\
\text{Premium} &= \frac{12{,}000}{1{,}000} \times 30 \\
&= 360
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Counts one floor only, $\frac{4{,}000}{1{,}000} \times 30$.
- **C** — Counts the basement, 16,000 square feet, though 70% of it is storage and heating.
- **D** — Applies the rate per 100 square feet instead of per 1,000.
