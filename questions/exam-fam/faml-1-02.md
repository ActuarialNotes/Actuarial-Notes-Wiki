Over the last 30 years, life insurance products and the management of the associated risks have radically changed and become more complex.

Determine which of the following is NOT a reason for this change.

- A) More sophisticated policyholders.
- B) More competition among life insurance companies.
- C) More computational power.
- D) More complex risk management techniques.
- E) Separation of the savings elements and the protection elements of life insurance products.

## Explanation

Insurers have an increased interest in combining savings and insurance products so Item E is false.

The other items are all true.
