The following deposits are made into a fund at the beginning of each year:

| Year | Deposit |
|---|---|
| 1 | 100 |
| 2 | 100 |
| 3 | 150 |
| 4 | 150 |
| 5 | 150 |

The fund earns an annual effective interest rate of 13%.

At the end of the tenth year, the fund is used to purchase a perpetuity-immediate with semiannual payments of $X$. The perpetuity earns an annual effective interest rate of 12%.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 99
- B) 102
- C) 105
- D) 180
- E) 204

## Explanation

Treat the deposits as 100 at the beginning of each of the five years plus an extra 50 at the beginning of years 3, 4 and 5. At the end of year 5 these are worth $100\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{5}|0.13} + 50\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{3}|0.13}$, and the fund then grows for five more years at 13% until the end of the tenth year:

$$
\begin{align*}
AV_{10} &= \left[100\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{5}|0.13} + 50\,\ddot{s}_{\overline{3}|0.13}\right](1.13)^{5} \\
        &= \left[100(7.322706) + 50(3.849797)\right](1.842435) \\
        &= (732.27 + 192.49)(1.842435) \\
        &= 924.76(1.842435) \\
        &= 1703.81
\end{align*}
$$

The perpetuity-immediate pays $X$ every half year, so it is valued at the semiannual effective rate $1.12^{1/2} - 1 = 0.0583005$. Its present value $\dfrac{X}{1.12^{1/2} - 1}$ must equal the fund:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{X}{1.12^{1/2} - 1} &= 1703.81 \\
X &= 1703.81(0.0583005) \\
  &= 99.33
\end{align*}
$$
