A perpetuity pays 10,000 at the beginning of each year. The present value of the payments calculated using an annual effective interest rate of 7% is $L$.

The annual effective interest rate decreases to 5% immediately after the perpetuity is purchased. Using the first-order modified duration approximation, the estimated present value of the payments is $M$.

Calculate $M - L$.

- A) 40,816
- B) 43,673
- C) 43,791
- D) 47,290
- E) 57,143

## Explanation

The perpetuity is a perpetuity-due of 10,000 a year, so at 7%

$$
\begin{align*}
L &= \frac{10{,}000}{0.07}(1.07) \\
  &= 152{,}857.14
\end{align*}
$$

Its Macaulay duration is the time-weighted present value divided by the present value. The payment at time 0 has weight 0, so the numerator is $(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|} = \dfrac{1}{i} + \dfrac{1}{i^{2}}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC} &= \frac{(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|}}{\ddot{a}_{\overline{\infty}|}} \\
        &= \frac{\dfrac{1}{0.07} + \dfrac{1}{(0.07)^{2}}}{\left(\dfrac{1}{0.07}\right)(1.07)} \\
        &= 14.2857
\end{align*}
$$

The modified duration is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD} &= \frac{14.285714}{1.07} \\
        &= 13.351135
\end{align*}
$$

The first-order modified approximation at 5% is $M = L\left[1 - (0.05 - 0.07)D_{MOD}\right]$:

$$
\begin{align*}
M &= 152{,}857.14\left[1 - (-0.02)(13.351135)\right] \\
  &= 193{,}673.47
\end{align*}
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
M - L &= 193{,}673.47 - 152{,}857.14 \\
      &= 40{,}816.33
\end{align*}
$$
