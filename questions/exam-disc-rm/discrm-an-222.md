An underwriter is setting up four general liability accounts. Which pairing of account and premium base follows the logic of ISO's general liability bases?

- A) An office building's owner rated on payroll, and a restaurant rated on area
- B) A roofing contractor rated on gross sales, and a restaurant rated on payroll
- C) A restaurant rated on area, and work let to subcontractors rated on payroll
- D) A roofing contractor rated on payroll, and an office building's owner rated on area

## Explanation

A premium base is chosen to track the exposure. A contractor is rated on payroll, which measures how much work it does; a store or restaurant on gross sales, which grow with the number of customers; a building owner on area; and work let to subcontractors on its total cost. Only **D** pairs both accounts with their usual bases.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A building owner's exposure grows with the building, not with its own payroll, and a restaurant is rated on sales.
- **B** — Reverses the contractor and the restaurant.
- **C** — Subcontracted work is rated on its total cost, because the insured's own payroll doesn't measure it.
