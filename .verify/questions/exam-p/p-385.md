- claim: Note also that $t = 14.4$ exceeds the mean 8.96, as it must, since more than half the computers have failed by then.
- evidence: With mean beta = 8.96284 (vault explanation and SOA Sample Solutions Q385, solutions PDF p.107), the exponential median is beta ln 2 = 6.2126 and F(beta) = 1 - e^(-1) = 0.6321. The conclusion is true but the stated reason conflates mean and median of a right-skewed distribution. Keyed answer D (14.42513, SOA Solution: D) unaffected.
