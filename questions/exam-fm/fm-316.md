A perpetuity-immediate with annual payments consists of ten level payments of $k$, followed by a series of increasing payments. Beginning with the eleventh payment, each payment is 200 larger than the preceding payment.

Based on an annual effective interest rate of 5.2%, the present value of the perpetuity is 50,000.

Calculate $k$.

- A) 34
- B) 86
- C) 163
- D) 283
- E) 409

## Explanation

The PV has two parts.

**Payments 1–10.** A level annuity-immediate: $k\,a_{\overline{10}|0.052} = 7.647284k$.

**Payments 11 onward.** The payments are $k+200, k+400, \ldots$ at times $11, 12, \ldots$ Valued at time 10 they are a level perpetuity-immediate of $k+200$ plus an increasing perpetuity-immediate of $0, 200, 400, \ldots$, whose value is $200/0.052^2$:

$$\text{PV at time 10} = (k+200)\left(\frac{1}{0.052}\right) + 200\left(\frac{1}{0.052^2}\right)$$

Discount by $v^{10} = 1.052^{-10} = 0.60234124$ and set the total equal to 50,000:

$$
\begin{align*}
50{,}000 &= k\,a_{\overline{10}|0.052} + v^{10}\left[(k+200)\left(\frac{1}{0.052}\right) + 200\left(\frac{1}{0.052^2}\right)\right] \\
&= 7.647284k + 0.60234124\left[19.230769k + 3846.153846 + 73{,}964.497\right] \\
&= 7.647284k + 11.583485k + 2316.697 + 44{,}551.867 \\
&= 19.230769k + 46{,}868.56
\end{align*}
$$

(The coefficient of $k$ is $a_{\overline{10}|} + v^{10}/0.052 = 1/0.052$, since together those are a level perpetuity of $k$.)

$$k = \frac{50{,}000 - 46{,}868.56}{19.230769} = \frac{3131.44}{19.230769} = 162.83$$

So $k = 163$.
