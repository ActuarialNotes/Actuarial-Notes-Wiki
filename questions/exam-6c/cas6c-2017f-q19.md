The following information is available for a property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2016. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Estimated undiscounted value of the commuted liability | 4,000 |
| Risk free rate | 2% |
| Required margin | 10% |
| Target capital to required capital ratio | 200% |
| Risk cost of capital | 10% |

Cumulative calendar year payment pattern:

| Age (Months) | % Paid |
|---|---|
| 12 | 50% |
| 24 | 100% |

Calculate the value of the commuted liability as at December 31, 2016.

### Explanation
| Row | Item | Total | 12 | 24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (1) | Payment Pattern | | 50% | 100% |
| (2) | Incremental Payment Pattern | | 50% | 50% |
| $(3) = (2) \times \text{Total}$ | Estimated Payment for each period | 4,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 |
| (4) | Payment duration | | 0.5 | 1.5 |
| (5) | Risk free rate | | 2% | 2% |
| (6) = PV of (3) @ 2% | PV of payment | **3,922** | 1,980 | 1,941 |
| (7) | Cash flow undiscounted future | | 4,000 | 2,000 |
| (8) | Required margin | | 10% | 10% |
| $(9) = (7) \times (8) \times 2$ | Regulatory capital at 200% | | 800 | 400 |
| (10) | Risk cost of capital | | 10% | 10% |
| $(11) = (9) \times (10)$ | Cost of capital | | 80 | 40 |
| (12) | Duration | | 1 | 2 |
| (13) | Discount rate | | 2% | 2% |
| (14) = PV of (11) @ 2% | Risk margin | **117** | 78.4 | 38.4 |

Claim payments are discounted from mid-year (row 4) and the cost of capital from year-end (row 12).

Commuted Value (total 3 + total 14) $= 4{,}038$ (as printed: the figure is the total of row (6), the discounted payments, plus the total of row (14)).

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to understand how to calculate the commuted value of liabilities including the present value of future payments and discounted cost of capital.
Common errors include:

- Discounting claim payments using the end-of-year assumption instead of the mid-year assumption without explicitly stating the assumption
- Discounting cost of capital using the mid-year assumption instead of the end-of-year assumption without explicitly stating the assumption
- Assuming amount of capital is equal to claim payments made during the year
