A three-year bond with a face value of 1000 pays coupons semiannually. The bond is redeemable at face value. It is bought at issue at a price to produce an annual yield rate of 10% convertible semiannually. If the term of the bond is doubled and the yield rate remains the same, the purchase price would decrease by 49.

Calculate the amount of a coupon.

- A) 37
- B) 46
- C) 54
- D) 63
- E) 74

## Explanation

Let $r$ = semiannual coupon rate. For the 3-year bond ($n=6$ periods):
$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 1000ra_{\overline{6}|0.05} + 1000(1.05)^{-6} \\
  &= 5075.692r + 746.215
\end{align*}
$$

For the 6-year bond ($n=12$ periods):
$$
\begin{align*}
P - 49 &= 1000ra_{\overline{12}|0.05} + 1000(1.05)^{-12} \\
       &= 8863.252r + 556.837
\end{align*}
$$

Subtracting:
$$
\begin{align*}
49 &= (5075.692r + 746.215) - (8863.252r + 556.837) \\
&= -3787.56r + 189.378
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
r &= \frac{189.378 - 49}{3787.56} \\
  &= \frac{140.378}{3787.56} \\
  &= 0.037 \\
\text{coupon} &= 1000(0.037) \\
              &= 37
\end{align*}
$$
