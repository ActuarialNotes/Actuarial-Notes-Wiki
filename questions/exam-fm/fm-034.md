Bill buys a 10-year 1000 par value bond with semi-annual coupons paid at an annual rate of 6%. The price assumes an annual nominal yield of 6%, compounded semi-annually.

As Bill receives each coupon payment, he immediately puts the money into an account earning interest at an annual effective rate of $i$.

At the end of 10 years, immediately after Bill receives the final coupon payment and the redemption value of the bond, Bill has earned an annual effective yield of 7% on his investment in the bond.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 9.50%
- B) 9.75%
- C) 10.00%
- D) 10.25%
- E) 10.50%

## Explanation

Because the yield rate equals the coupon rate, Bill paid 1000 for the bond. In return he receives $1000(0.06/2) = 30$ every six months, which accumulates to $30\,s_{\overline{20}|j}$, where $j$ is the semi-annual interest rate of the account. The redemption value of 1000 is paid at the end of 10 years.

Earning an annual effective yield of 7% on the 1000 means Bill has $1000(1.07)^{10}$ at the end of 10 years. The equation of value is:

$$1000(1.07)^{10} = 30\,s_{\overline{20}|j} + 1000 \implies s_{\overline{20}|j} = \frac{1967.15 - 1000}{30} = 32.238$$

Using a calculator to solve for the interest rate (N = 20, PV = 0, PMT = 30, FV = $-967.15$, CPT I/Y) produces $j = 0.0476$, and so

$$
\begin{align*}
i &= (1.0476)^2 - 1 \\
&= 0.0975 \\
&= 9.75\%
\end{align*}
$$
