At the end of each year for 60 years, Marilyn makes a deposit to a bank account that credits interest at an annual effective interest rate of $i$. She deposits 2 at the end of the first year, and each subsequent year her deposit increases by 2. At the end of 60 years, Marilyn uses the accumulated amount to purchase a 5-year annuity-immediate paying annually at the same annual interest rate of $i$, with a first payment of $X$ and each subsequent payment increasing by 5%.

Which of the following expressions represents a correct equation of value?

- I. $\dfrac{2\left(s_{\overline{60}|}-60\right)}{i} = \dfrac{X\left(1-(1.05v)^5\right)}{i-0.05}$
- II. $2(Ia)_{\overline{60}|} = Xv^{60}\left(1+1.05v+\ldots+(1.05v)^4\right)$
- III. $2\displaystyle\sum_{t=0}^{59}s_{\overline{60-t}|} = X\left(v+1.05v^2+\ldots+1.05^4v^5\right)$

- A) I only
- B) II only
- C) III only
- D) I, II, and III
- E) The correct answer is not given by (A), (B), (C), or (D).

## Explanation

Deposits of $2, 4, \ldots, 120$ at the ends of years 1 to 60 accumulate at time 60 to

$$
2(Is)_{\overline{60}|} = 2\sum_{t=0}^{59} s_{\overline{60-t}|} = \frac{2\left(\ddot{s}_{\overline{60}|} - 60\right)}{i}
$$

(the deposits are the sum of 60 level series of 2 a year: the series starting at the end of year $t+1$ runs to year 60 and accumulates to $2s_{\overline{60-t}|}$). At time 60 this buys a 5-year annuity-immediate whose payments $X, 1.05X, \ldots, 1.05^4X$ fall at times 61 to 65, worth at time 60

$$
X\left(v + 1.05v^2 + \cdots + 1.05^4v^5\right) = \frac{X\left(1-(1.05v)^5\right)}{i-0.05}
$$

**Statement I** should have an $\ddot{s}_{\overline{60}|}$ on the left: $\left(s_{\overline{60}|}-60\right)/i$ is not $(Is)_{\overline{60}|}$. Its right side is correct.

**Statement II** has an annuity-due rather than an annuity-immediate on the right: $Xv^{60}\left(1+1.05v+\cdots+(1.05v)^4\right)$ values payments at times 60 to 64, one year too early.

**Statement III** is correct: its left side is the accumulated value at time 60 and its right side is the value at time 60 of the annuity-immediate.

Only III is correct, answer C.
