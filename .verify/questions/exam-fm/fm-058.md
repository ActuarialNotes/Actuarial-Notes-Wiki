## [F-002] Explanation describes Redington immunization as PV and duration matching, omitting the convexity condition
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, line 41
- claim: Redington immunization requires PV matching and duration matching (first-order).
- evidence: Finan, §55 'Redington Immunization and Convexity', PDF p.480: 'immunization is achieved when the following three conditions are met: (1) P(i) = 0 ... (2) P'(i) = 0 ... (3) P''(i) > 0: The convexity of ... assets should be greater than the convexity of ... liabilities.' SOA's solution Q58 (solutions PDF p.17) sets only the PVs and first derivatives equal and states no definition. - source_rank: 3
- proposed_action: Add the convexity condition (asset convexity exceeds liability convexity), noting it is met because the asset times bracket the liability. Authoring - maintainer's call.
