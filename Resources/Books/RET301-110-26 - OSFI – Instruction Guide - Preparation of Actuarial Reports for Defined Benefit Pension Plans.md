OSFI's instruction guide setting out the reporting requirements of actuarial reports filed with OSFI for pension plans with defined benefit provisions under the Pension Benefits Standards Act, 1985. This version, modified 2024-11-15, updates the November 2023 guide for the maximum going concern discount rate and for negotiated contribution plans, and applies to actuarial reports with a valuation date on and after December 31, 2024.

- 2.8 Financial Position
    - 2.8.1 Going Concern Valuation
        - The report includes a balance sheet of going concern assets and liabilities at the valuation date and at the previous report's valuation date, with liabilities broken down by category of persons and shown separately for transfer deficiencies and buy-in annuities.
        - Notes to the balance sheet disclose the provision for future expenses and the provision for adverse deviations included in liabilities.
    - 2.8.2 Solvency Valuation
        - The report includes a balance sheet of solvency assets and liabilities, and shows the solvency ratio at both valuation dates.
        - The report shows the average solvency ratio used for funding purposes, the arithmetic average of the adjusted solvency ratios at the valuation date, the prior valuation date and the prior second valuation date.
        - Transfer deficiency payments are not considered special payments and are not included in the adjustments of prior solvency ratios.
- 2.9 Reconciliation of Financial Position

- 2.10 Funding Requirements
    - The report gives the rule for determining the current service cost, the current service cost at both valuation dates, and each schedule of going concern special payments and the schedule of monthly solvency special payments.
    - Pre-Existing Going Concern Special Payments
        - Pre-existing going concern special payments should not be eliminated or reduced unless their present value exceeds the going concern deficit.
    - Contribution Holidays
        - Employer contributions payable until the next report must be at least the employer current service cost minus the lesser of the going concern surplus and the amount by which solvency assets exceed 105% of solvency liabilities.
