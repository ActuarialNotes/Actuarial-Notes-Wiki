A book that introduces asset and liability management (ALM) in the context of banks and insurance companies. Although the strategy has a core of fundamental frameworks, models may vary between banks and insurance companies because of the different risks and goals involved; the authors compare and contrast these methodologies to draw parallels between their commonalities and divergences and so provide a deeper understanding of ALM in general. The SOA supplies Chapter 4 as a study note.
