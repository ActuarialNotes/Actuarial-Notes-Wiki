An investor borrows 13,650 at an annual effective interest rate of 7%. Level payments are made at the end of each year. The interest paid in the last payment is 65.42.

Calculate the principal repaid in the first payment.

- A) 44.50
- B) 45.00
- C) 45.50
- D) 46.00
- E) 46.50

## Explanation

For a loan repaid by $n$ level payments of $P$, the interest in payment $t$ is $P\left(1 - v^{n-t+1}\right)$. For the last payment, $t = n$:

$$
\begin{align*}
65.42 &= P\left(1 - v^{n-n+1}\right) \\
      &= P\left(1 - \frac{1}{1.07}\right) \\
P &= \frac{65.42}{1 - 1/1.07} \\
  &\approx 1000
\end{align*}
$$

The interest in the first payment is a year's interest on the full amount borrowed:

$$
\begin{align*}
I_1 &= 13{,}650(0.07) \\
    &= 955.50
\end{align*}
$$

so the principal repaid in the first payment is

$$
\begin{align*}
P_1 &= 1000 - 955.50 \\
    &= 44.50
\end{align*}
$$
