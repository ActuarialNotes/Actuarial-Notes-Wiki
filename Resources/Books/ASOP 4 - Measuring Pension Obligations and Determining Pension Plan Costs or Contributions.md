The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries measuring obligations under a defined benefit pension plan and determining periodic costs or actuarially determined contributions for such plans. This revised edition (Doc. No. 205) was adopted in December 2021 and is effective for actuarial reports issued on or after February 15, 2023 whose measurement date is on or after that date; it provides guidance for coordinating and integrating all of the elements of an actuarial valuation of a pension plan.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 1b and 3a-3d; the specific requirements of the standard are not tested, and it is included as an overview of the considerations that go into most pension valuations.

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
    - Gives guidance when measuring obligations under a defined benefit pension plan and determining periodic costs or actuarially determined contributions; other ASOPs address assumptions, asset valuation methods and assessment of risk.
- 1.2 Scope
    - Applies to measuring pension obligations, assigning their value to time periods, developing a cost allocation procedure or a contribution allocation procedure, determining the benefits supportable by specified cost or contribution levels, and projecting obligations, costs and contributions.
    - Does not apply to individual benefit calculations, individual benefit statement estimates, annuity pricing, nondiscrimination testing, or social insurance programs.
