# Exam F1GB
**F1GB – Product Design and Group Benefits Environment** is an exam of the group benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques of design that are relevant to group insurance.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of group insurance.
>
> 3. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions and to develop appropriate proposals or recommendations relating to pricing group insurance products.
>
> 4. Understand the Canadian health insurance environment.
>
> 5. Understand US and other health insurance environments.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in [[Group Benefit|group benefits]] and [[Health Insurance|health insurance]] design. (A1)

> [!example]- Product design {25%}
>
> 1. Analyze [[Benefits' Design Feature|benefits’ design features]] and provisions (life, [[Premium Waiver|premium waiver]], [[Accidental Death|accidental death]], and dismemberment [AD&D], disability, medical, drug, dental, [[Critical Illness Insurance|critical illness]] [CI], [[Long-Term Care Insurance|long-term care]] [LTC], [[Optional Benefit|optional benefits]], [[Individual Health Insurance|individual health insurance]]). (D4)
> 2. Analyze issues associated with benefit design and calculate, compare, and contrast [[Benefit-Coverage Level|benefit-coverage levels]] ([[Drug Formulary|drug formularies]], [[New Drug|new drugs]], [[Drug Substitution|drug substitution]] [generic or other], [[Preferred Provider Network|preferred provider networks]], [[Direct Payment|direct payment]], evolution of [[Dental-Fee Guide|dental-fee guides]], [[Emerging Illness|emerging illnesses]], [[Digital-virtual Product|digital/virtual products]], [[Health Care-wellness Spending Account|health care/wellness spending accounts]], etc.). (C4)
> 3. Analyze and calculate possible [[Participant Anti-Selection|participant anti-selection]] and its related impacts. (C4)
> 4. Understand the rationale underlying [[Risk Classification|risk classification]] for [[Individual Health Product|individual health products]]. (B2)
> 5. Evaluate the structure of [[Employee Benefit Plan|employee-benefit plans]] and products offered, and the rationale for offering these structures. (B5)
> 6. Recommend an [[Employee Benefit Plan|employee-benefit strategy]] considering an employer’s objectives. (C5)

> [!example]- Benefit law and regulation {20%}
>
> 1. Understand the effects of applicable legislation and regulation, including (but not limited to) privacy, [[Human Right|human rights]], and non-discrimination on the following: (B2)
>    - a. [[Allowable Benefit|Allowable benefits]] and exclusions.
>    - b. [[Defined Benefit Plan|Benefit plan]] design and pricing.
>    - c. [[Plan Administration|Plan administration]].
>    - d. [[Claim|Claims]] management and administration.
>    - e. [[Human Right|Human rights]] and non-discrimination.
> 2. Outline provisions of the [[Canadian Life]] and [[Health Insurance Association]] (CLHIA) guidelines and calculate impacts where applicable (definitions of groups, [[Takeover Rule|takeover rules]], coordination of benefits, disclosure). (C3)
> 3. Outline and apply applicable provisions of the [[Civil Code]] of Québec to [[Group Insurance|group insurance]]. (C3)
> 4. Outline and apply applicable provisions of other relevant laws and regulations to [[Group Insurance|group insurance]]. (C3)
> 5. Apply relevant [[Case Law|case law]] and interpret its implications. (C3)
> 6. Outline and apply provincially [[Mandated Benefit|mandated benefits]] ([[Prescription Drug Coverage|prescription drug coverage]] in Québec, etc.). (C3)
> 7. Compare and contrast the [[Main Difference|main differences]] between [[Québec Law|Québec law]] and the rest of Canada (designation of beneficiary, change of [[Insurer|insurers]], [[Civil Union|civil union]], etc.). (D5)
> 8. Outline and describe the [[Legal Aspect|legal aspects]] of self-insurance. (B2)

> [!example]- Insurance company law and regulation {15%}
>
> 1. Understand and apply the following legislation: (B3)
>    - a. Office of the Superintendent of [[Financial Institution|Financial Institutions]] ([[OSFI]]) guidelines.
>    - b. [[Insurance Companies Act]].
>    - c. [[Uniform Life Insurance Act]] and major variations.
>    - d. [[Uniform Health Insurance Act]] and major variations.
>    - e. [[Québec's Civil Code|Québec’s Civil Code]].
>    - f. Other [[Provincial Law|provincial laws]] that have an impact on insurance (regulations on insurance and other acts, laws on the sale and [[Probability Distributions|distribution]] of [[Insurance Policy|insurance products]], etc.).
> 2. Evaluate special issues ([[Group Creditor Insurance|group creditor insurance]], change of carrier, designation of beneficiary, etc.). (B5)
> 3. Identify and understand relevant [[Case Law|case law]] and its implications. (D2)

> [!example]- Workers’ compensation {5%}
>
> 1. Outline and evaluate the following: (B5)
>    - a. [[Social Insurance|Social insurance]] programs governed by [[Provincial Legislation|provincial legislation]], with emphasis on prevention of [[Workplace Injury|workplace injuries]], diseases, and fatalities.
>    - b. Association of [[Workers' Compensation Board|Workers’ Compensation Boards]] of Canada (AWCBC).
>    - c. Publicly insured programs vs. self-insured programs.
>    - d. Integration of [[Workers' Compensation|workers’ compensation]] programs with [[Private Group Insurance|private group insurance]] programs.
>    - e. Unique aspects of disability management when [[Disability Arise|disability arises]] from a cause covered under [[Workers' Compensation|workers’ compensation]].

> [!example]- Public and other benefit programs {15%}
>
> 1. Analyze the impact of following [[Public Plan|public plans]] on [[Group Insurance|group insurance]]: (C4)
>    - a. [[Canada-Québec Pension Plan|Canada/Québec Pension Plans]].
>    - b. Canada [[Health Act]].
>    - c. [[Provincial Medical|Provincial medical]] and [[Drug Plan|drug plans]].
>    - d. [[Provincial Automobile Plan|Provincial automobile plans]].
>    - e. [[Employment Insurance]].
>    - f. [[Québec Parental Insurance Plan]].
>    - g. [[Québec Public Prescription Drug Insurance Plan]].
>    - h. [[National Pharmacare Initiative|National pharmacare initiative]].
> 2. Evaluate how [[Group Health Insurance|private group insurance plans]] work within the framework of public programs in Canada. (D5)
> 3. Perform calculations under the plans mentioned in 1. above. ([[C3]])
> 4. Evaluate Government's role in [[Health-Care Policy|health-care policy]] and regulation. (D5)
> 5. Analyze financing and the flow of funds in the [[Health Care Insurance|Canadian health-care system]] and the role of the provider and government. (D4)
> 6. Describe variations in consumer behavior under the [[Canadian Health System|Canadian health system]]. (D2)

> [!example]- The U.S. healthcare model {10%}
>
> 1. Understand the operations of various types of (public, private, or both) health-care models applicable in the [[United State|United States]] and their financing: (B3)
>    - a. [[Health Maintenance Organization]] (HMO).
>    - b. [[Preferred Provider Organization]] (PPO).
>    - c. [[Group Health Insurance|Private Group Insurance Plans]].
>    - d. [[Affordable Care Act]] (ACA or Obamacare).
>    - e. [[Medicare]] and [[Medicaid]].
> 2. Differentiate the role of various players in the U.S. system (networks, employers, [[Insurer|insurers]], [[Actuary|actuaries]]) (B4)
> 3. Critique social programs in Canada and the [[United State|United States]], and discuss the value of the different systems. (B5)

> [!example]- Other healthcare models {10%}
>
> 1. Understand the various types of (public, private, or both) health-care models: (B2)
>    - a. Other countries of [[Interest|interest]] (such as the [[United Kingdom]] and China).
>    - b. Developing country for health-care models (such as India)
> 2. Compare and contrast the role of various stakeholders in these systems (networks, employers, [[Insurer|insurers]], [[Actuary|actuaries]]). (D5)
