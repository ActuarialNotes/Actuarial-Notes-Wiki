A March 2021 Society of Actuaries research report on peer-to-peer (P2P) insurance, a business model in which individuals pool their resources for mutual aid, built on blockchain and smart contract technology. It was written by researchers at Arizona State University and sponsored by the SOA Research Expanding Boundaries Pool. It describes a business use case on the Hyperledger Fabric blockchain platform and tests it with simulated examples for property and casualty and for life insurance.
