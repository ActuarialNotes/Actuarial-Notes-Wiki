A paper by Robert A. Painter and Dan Isaac of Conning Research & Consulting that introduces a practical approach to setting capital targets by considering the objectives of all of an insurance company's stakeholders. Because stakeholders define "risk" and "capital adequacy" differently, its multistakeholder approach produces capital indications across various key financial measures, time horizons and risk tolerances, and it introduces the "Financial Rating Risk Replication" technique, which maps distributions of regulatory and rating agency capital measures to observable financial rating transition matrices.
