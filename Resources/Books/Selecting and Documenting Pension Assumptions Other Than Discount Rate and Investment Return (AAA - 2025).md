A 2025 practice note from the Pension Committee of the American Academy of Actuaries, giving information on current and emerging practices in the selection and documentation of certain actuarial assumptions for measuring obligations of defined benefit pension plans. It does not cover the discount rate or investment return, replaces the June 2023 version, and incorporates material previously found in a separate Academy practice note on mortality assumptions. It is not an actuarial standard of practice.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 2a-2d; the whole note, which the SOA describes as the core overview of demographic assumptions.
