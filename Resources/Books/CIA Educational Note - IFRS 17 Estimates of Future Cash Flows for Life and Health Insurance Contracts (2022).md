A CIA educational note that provides information concerning the estimates of future cash flows in accordance with IFRS 17 requirements. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated June 30, 2022 (document 222085), it highlights the differences between IFRS 4 and IFRS 17, presents general considerations and the specific actuarial assumptions, and treats cash flows that vary with assumptions related to financial risk.
