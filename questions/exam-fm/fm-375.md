Two bonds have the same annual effective yield rate, $r$, where $r > 0$. The bonds have Macaulay duration of 5 years and 6 years with respect to $r$. One of the bonds has modified duration of 5.76 years while the other bond has modified duration of $d$ years.

Calculate $d$.

- A) 4.760
- B) 4.800
- C) 5.208
- D) 5.240
- E) 6.912

## Explanation

Modified duration is Macaulay duration divided by $1+r$. Because $r > 0$, each bond's modified duration is less than its Macaulay duration, so the modified duration of 5.76 (which exceeds 5) must belong to the bond with Macaulay duration 6. That bond gives the yield rate:

$$
\begin{align*}
5.76 &= \frac{6}{1+r} \\
1+r &= 1.041667 \\
r &= 0.041667
\end{align*}
$$

The other bond has Macaulay duration 5, so its modified duration is

$$
\begin{align*}
d &= \frac{5}{1.041667} \\
  &= 4.800
\end{align*}
$$
