A textbook on the economics and financing of health care, in its sixth edition (Wiley, February 2022). Wiley describes it as covering cost-benefit and cost-effectiveness analyses and the economic considerations driving the choices of physicians, hospitals and pharmaceutical companies, and the influence of public and private financial considerations on health care decision makers. The SOA lists selected sections of it as study note GH201-103-25.
