Let $X_1, X_2, X_3, X_4$ be independent observations of a random variable with mean 10 and standard deviation 3.

Calculate the variance of $3X_1 - X_2 - X_3 - X_4$.

- A) $27$
- B) $36$
- C) $54$
- D) $81$
- E) $108$

## Explanation

Each observation has variance $3^2 = 9$. For independent variables, the variance of a linear combination squares every coefficient — negative signs disappear:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(3X_1 - X_2 - X_3 - X_4) &= 3^2(9) + (-1)^2(9) + (-1)^2(9) + (-1)^2(9) \\
                                   &= 81 + 9 + 9 + 9 \\
                                   &= \mathbf{108}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (D), 81, is the $X_1$ term alone. Note the *mean* of this combination is $3(10) - 3(10) = 0$, yet the variance is far from zero — the coefficients cancel in the mean but reinforce in the variance, because squaring turns the minus signs positive. That contrast is exactly what the question is probing.
