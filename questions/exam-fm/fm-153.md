Krishna buys an $n$-year 1000 bond at par. The Macaulay duration is 7.959 years using an annual effective interest rate of 7.2%.

Calculate the estimated price of the bond, using the first-order Macaulay approximation, if the interest rate rises to 8.0%.

- A) 940.60
- B) 942.54
- C) 944.56
- D) 947.03
- E) 948.47

## Explanation

The bond is bought at par, so $P(0.072) = 1000$. The first-order Macaulay approximation with $D_{MAC} = 7.959$:

$$P(0.08) \approx P(0.072)\left(\frac{1.072}{1.08}\right)^{7.959} = 1000\left(\frac{1.072}{1.08}\right)^{7.959}$$

$$= 1000(0.9925926)^{7.959} = 1000(0.942542) = 942.54$$
