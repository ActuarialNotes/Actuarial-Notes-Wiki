A review that distinguishes AI Agents, modular systems driven by large language models for task-specific automation, from Agentic AI, systems marked by multi-agent collaboration, dynamic task decomposition, persistent memory and coordinated autonomy. It offers a conceptual taxonomy, a mapping of applications, and an analysis of the challenges of each paradigm, and proposes solutions and a roadmap for robust, scalable and explainable AI-driven systems. It is the arXiv preprint (2505.10468) of an article later published in Information Fusion.
