The modified duration of a ten-year zero-coupon bond with an annual effective yield rate of $i$ is 9.26 years.

Based on an annual effective interest rate of $i$, the Macaulay duration of a ten-year annuity-immediate with level annual payments is $D$.

Calculate $D$.

- A) 4.05
- B) 4.37
- C) 4.51
- D) 4.87
- E) 4.96

## Explanation

A zero-coupon bond's Macaulay duration is its term, 10 years, so its modified duration is $\dfrac{10}{1+i}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
9.26 &= \frac{10}{1+i} \\
i &= 0.079914
\end{align*}
$$

The annuity-immediate pays a level amount at times $1, 2, \ldots, 10$, so its Macaulay duration at $i = 0.079914$ is the present-value-weighted average of those times:

$$
\begin{align*}
D &= \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{10} t\,v^{t}}{\sum_{t=1}^{10} v^{t}} \\
  &= \frac{(Ia)_{\overline{10}|}}{a_{\overline{10}|}} \\
  &= \frac{32.70395}{6.71270} \\
  &= 4.8720
\end{align*}
$$
