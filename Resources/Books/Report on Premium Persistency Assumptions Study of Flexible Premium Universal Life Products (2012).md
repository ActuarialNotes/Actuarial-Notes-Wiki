A May 2012 report, prepared for the Society of Actuaries by Milliman authors and sponsored by its Product Development and Financial Reporting Sections and the Committee on Life Insurance Research. It summarizes a survey of flexible premium universal life (FPUL) companies about the premium persistency assumptions used in pricing and cash flow testing and for U.S. GAAP and IFRS purposes, with responses from 29 companies on 83 products.
