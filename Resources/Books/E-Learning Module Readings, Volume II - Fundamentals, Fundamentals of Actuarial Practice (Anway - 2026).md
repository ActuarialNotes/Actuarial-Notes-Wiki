A curated collection of readings designed to support and complement the Society of Actuaries' Fundamentals of Actuarial Practice e-learning course modules and help candidates prepare for its assessment. Its opening note says that, unlike a traditional textbook, it does not follow a linear narrative: each article, excerpt or transcript is meant to be read as the modules prompt, reading by reading. The readings are drawn from excerpts of published books, articles, pieces written specifically for the modules and transcripts from conferences or meetings, and are grouped by the course's modules.
