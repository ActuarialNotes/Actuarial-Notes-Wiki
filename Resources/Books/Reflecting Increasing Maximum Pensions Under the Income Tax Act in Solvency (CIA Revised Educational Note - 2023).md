## 1 Introduction
- The maximum accrual permitted for a defined benefit plan under the Income Tax Act is expected to increase every calendar year, raising the question whether a valuation as at December 31 should reflect the maximum in effect that day or the higher limit effective the following day.
- Lists the relevant Standards of Practice: general sections 1120 and 1430, and pension sections 3240.05, 3240.12, 3250.01 and 3310.06.
- Plan provisions fall into two broad categories: the maximum pension test applied at the time of pension commencement, or applied at the relevant calculation date.
- For the first category, values under a hypothetical wind-up or solvency valuation are not affected by whether the valuation is performed on December 31 or January 1.
- For the second category, a hypothetical wind-up valuation as at December 31 may reflect either the limit for that calendar year or the higher limit effective the following January 1, and both comply with the Standards of Practice.
- For an actual wind-up valuation the cut-off date is chosen as close as possible to the report date, and subsequent events are reported up to it.
