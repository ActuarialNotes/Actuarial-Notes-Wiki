An article in the Society of Actuaries' Health Watch, February 2019 (Issue 88), on the data-quality problems Medicare Advantage organizations (MAOs) face when preparing their Medicare Advantage bids. MAOs must consider influences both internal and external to the claims adjudication process as they review and use their data, including whether claims experience is consistent with the plan benefit package and provider contracts and reasonable compared to prior years and benchmarks.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201U (SOA)|Exam GH-201U]] — Outcomes 4a; the whole article.

## Internal Considerations
- Does the Data Contain the Required Elements?
- Is Claims Experience Consistent With Benefit Parameters and Provider Contracts?
- Is Claims Experience Reasonable Compared to Internal Expectations?

## External Considerations
- Is Claims Experience Reasonable Given External Benchmarks?
- Is Claims Experience Being Prepared to Comply with CMS Requirements for Bid Pricing?
