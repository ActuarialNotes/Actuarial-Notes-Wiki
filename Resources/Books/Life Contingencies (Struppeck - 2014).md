A short CAS study note covering MAS-I learning objectives A5–A6. "Life contingencies" is its own definition of the subject: survival models for human lives, and the cash flows that start or stop contingent on survival. Written for a casualty audience, so it stops well short of a full life-contingencies text.

**Available from:** the CAS study-note library (originally written for Exam S).

## What it covers

- [[Survival Model|Survival models]] for a single life: the survival function, the [[Hazard Rate]] (force of mortality), and the relationship between them
- The [[Life Table]]: $\ell_x$ and $d_x$, and the survival and death probabilities ${}_{t}p_x$, ${}_{t}q_x$ and ${}_{t|}q_x$ read off them
- Fractional-age assumptions between integer ages
- [[Joint Life]] statuses: the joint-life and last-survivor statuses built from two independent lives
- [[Whole Life Insurance]]: the actuarial present value of a benefit paid at death, $A_x = E[v^{K+1}]$
- The [[Life Annuity]]: payments continuing while a life survives, $\ddot{a}_x = \sum v^k\,{}_{k}p_x$, and its relationship to $A_x$
- Premiums and reserves at the level a casualty actuary needs
