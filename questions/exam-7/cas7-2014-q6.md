Given the following information from an insurance company's portfolio of policies with a \$500,000 deductible:

| Age-to-Age Loss and ALAE Development Factors | 12-24 | 24-36 | 36-48 | 48-60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited | 1.912 | 1.316 | X | 1.077 |
| Limited to \$500,000 | 1.900 | 1.300 | 1.125 | 1.050 |

| Months | 12 | 24 | 36 | 48 | 60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Limited Severity Relativities ($R^L$) | 0.980 | 0.974 | 0.962 | 0.944 | 0.920 |

## Part a (0.5 points)

Calculate the missing value, X, from the table above.

### Explanation
The limited LDF is the unlimited LDF adjusted by the change in the limited severity relativity:

$$LDF_t^L = LDF_t \times \frac{R_{t+1}}{R_t}$$

$$1.125 = LDF_t \times \frac{0.944}{0.962} \;\Rightarrow\; X = LDF_t = 1.146$$

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to provide a correct solution, but not via one and only one formula.
The most common mistake was selecting an incorrect limited severity relativity, be it simple column reference error or just misunderstanding the formula/concept.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Calculate the excess of \$500,000 36-48 month development factor.

### Explanation
The unlimited LDF is a weighted average of the limited and excess LDFs:

$$LDF_t = LDF_t^L \times R_t + (1 - R_t) \times XSLDF_t$$

$$1.146 = 1.125 \times 0.962 + (1 - 0.962) \times XSLDF \;\Rightarrow\; XSLDF = 1.690$$

Alternatively:

$$XSLDF = LDF_t \times \frac{1 - R_{t+1}}{1 - R_t} = 1.146 \times \frac{1 - 0.944}{1 - 0.962} = 1.6895$$

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to provide a correct solution, but not via one and only one formula.
The most common mistake was using an incorrect age for the LDF/relativity relationship.
