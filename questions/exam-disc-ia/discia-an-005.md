At year end an insurer's gross loss and LAE reserves are \$64 million, and \$15 million of that is recoverable from authorized reinsurers on unpaid losses. What amount does the insurer report as its loss and LAE reserve liability on the statutory balance sheet?

- A) \$15 million
- B) \$34 million
- C) \$49 million
- D) \$64 million

## Explanation

Statutory accounting reports loss reserves net of reinsurance: recoverables on unpaid case and IBNR losses and LAE are netted against the gross liability.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Reserve}_{\text{SAP}} &= 64 - 15 \\
&= 49
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$15 million is the ceded portion, not the insurer's liability.
- **B** — \$34 million nets the recoverable twice.
- **D** — \$64 million is the GAAP presentation, which shows the gross liability and the \$15 million recoverable as a separate asset.
