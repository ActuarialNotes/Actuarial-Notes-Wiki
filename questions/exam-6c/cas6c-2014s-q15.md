Given the information for a Canadian P&C insurer:

Summary of Assets which Support Policy Liabilities

| Description | Interest Rate | Maturity Date | Par Value | Market Value | Market Yield | Effective Market Yield | Modified Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BOND A | 3.10% | 18/6/40 | \$2,055,000 | \$2,638,691 | 1.82% | 1.83% | 20.13 |
| BOND B | 4.56% | 26/3/40 | \$1,080,000 | \$1,321,528 | 3.33% | 3.36% | 16.91 |
| BOND C | 4.99% | 30/10/37 | \$200,000 | \$247,497 | 3.57% | 3.61% | 15.57 |
| Total | | | \$3,335,000 | \$4,207,716 | | | |

| Accident Year | Gross Paid Loss (\$000s) | Gross Case Reserve (\$000s) | Gross Incurred Loss (\$000s) | Gross Ultimate Loss (\$000s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2010 | \$1,788 | \$0 | \$1,788 | \$1,788 |
| 2011 | \$1,025 | \$1,025 | \$2,050 | \$2,050 |
| 2012 | \$357 | \$2,023 | \$2,380 | \$2,380 |
| Total | \$3,170 | \$3,048 | \$6,218 | \$6,218 |

Cumulative Accident Year Payment Pattern

| Maturity | Percentage Paid |
|---|---|
| 12 | 15% |
| 24 | 50% |
| 36 | 100% |

Other information:

- Margin for adverse deviation for investment rate return: 25 basis points
- Margin for adverse deviation for claims development: 500 basis points
- All bonds are categorized as Available for Sale.

Assume that all payments are made in the middle of the year.

Using a discount rate calculated from the bond information given above, calculate the unpaid claims liability as at December 31, 2012.

### Explanation
Margins: $\text{MfAD}_{\text{int}} = 0.25\%$ and $\text{MfAD}_{\text{claims}} = 5\%$. Amounts in \$'000.

Bonds AFS: Valued at market value. Use effective market yield weighed by Market value and duration.

$$\text{Discount Rate} = \frac{(2{,}638{,}691 \times 20.13 \times 0.0183) + (1{,}321{,}528 \times 16.91 \times 0.0336) + (247{,}497 \times 15.57 \times 0.0361)}{(2{,}638{,}691 \times 20.13) + (1{,}321{,}528 \times 16.91) + (247{,}497 \times 15.57)} = 0.0235$$

Share of each accident year's unpaid claims paid in each future year (paid / unpaid):

| Unpaid Claims | AY 2010 | AY 2011 | AY 2012 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 0% | 50%/50% | 35%/85% |
| 24 | 0% | 0% | 50%/85% |
| 36 | 0% | 0% | 0% |

So AY 2011's unpaid 1,025 is all paid in the next year, and AY 2012's unpaid 2,023 is paid as 833 in the next year and 1,190 in the year after.

**AY 2010:** APV = 0

**AY 2011:**

$$PV = 1{,}025 \times (1.0235)^{-0.5} = 1{,}013.18$$

$$PV_{\text{with PfAD int}} = 1{,}025 \times (1.025 - 0.0025)^{-0.5} = 1{,}014.42$$

(as printed: the result 1,014.42 is the one for the margined rate 0.0235 - 0.0025)

$$PV_{\text{PfAD claims}} = 1{,}013.18 \times 5\% = 50.66$$

**AY 2012:**

$$PV = 833 \times (1.0235)^{-0.5} + 1{,}190 \times (1.0235)^{-0.5} = 1{,}972.68$$

$$PV_{\text{with PfAD int}} = 833 \times (1.025 - 0.0025)^{-0.5} + 1{,}190 \times (1.025 - 0.0025)^{-0.5} = 1{,}977.92$$

(as printed: the totals 1,972.68 and 1,977.92 discount the second payment, made a year later, for 1.5 years rather than 0.5, and at the rate 0.0235 - 0.0025)

$$PV_{\text{PfAD claims}} = 1{,}972.68 \times 5\% = 98.63$$

**Unpaid claims liability:**

$$APV = (1{,}014.42 + 50.66) + (1{,}977.92 + 98.63) = 3{,}141.63$$
