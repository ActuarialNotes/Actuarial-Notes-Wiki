An insurer has earned premium of \$800 million, incurred losses and loss adjustment expenses of \$560 million, underwriting expenses of \$248 million and investment income of \$72 million. Using earned premium for every ratio, what is its operating ratio?

- A) 70%
- B) 92%
- C) 101%
- D) 110%

## Explanation

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Combined ratio} &= \frac{560 + 248}{800} \\
&= 101\% \\
\text{Investment income ratio} &= \frac{72}{800} \\
&= 9\% \\
\text{Operating ratio} &= 101\% - 9\% \\
&= \mathbf{92\%}
\end{align*}
$$

The insurer loses money on underwriting but is profitable after investment income. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — 70% is the loss and LAE ratio alone, \$560 million divided by \$800 million.
- **C** — 101% is the combined ratio, which leaves out investment income.
- **D** — 110% adds the investment income ratio instead of subtracting it.
