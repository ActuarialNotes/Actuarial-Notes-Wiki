The following information is available for a property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2017. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Total Capital Available** | 59,400 |
| **Capital (Margin) Required at Target** | |
| Total Insurance Risk Margin | 26,700 |
| Total Market Risk Margin | 52,800 |
| Total Credit Risk Margin | 2,000 |
| Operational Risk Margin | ? |
| Less: Diversification Credit | ? |
| **Total Capital (Margin) Required at Target** | ? |

| | 2016 | 2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Premiums Written | 96,300 | 115,500 |
| Assumed Premiums Written | 11,000 | 20,400 |

The company cedes 30% of its direct written premium to a third party reinsurer. The company has no intra-group pooling arrangements.

## Part a (1.5 points)

Calculate the operational risk margin at target.

### Explanation
$$\text{Operational Risk Margin} = \min\left(30\% \times CR_0,\ (8.5\% \times CR_0 + 2.5\% \times P_w + 1.75\% \times P_a + 2.5\% \times P_c + 2.5\% \times P_\Delta) + \max(0.75\% \times P_{aig},\ 0.75\% \times P_{cig})\right)$$

Since there are no intra-group pooling arrangements, this simplifies to:

$$\text{Operational Risk Margin} = \min\left(30\% \times CR_0,\ 8.5\% \times CR_0 + 2.5\% \times P_w + 1.75\% \times P_a + 2.5\% \times P_c + 2.5\% \times P_\Delta\right)$$

- $CR_0$ = capital required before operational risk margin and diversification credit $= 26{,}700 + 52{,}800 + 2{,}000 = 81{,}500$
- $P_w = 115{,}500$
- $P_a = 20{,}400$
- $P_c = 115{,}500 \times 0.3 = 34{,}650$
- $P_\Delta$ = increase in GPW above 20% $= (115{,}500 + 20{,}400) - (96{,}300 + 11{,}000) \times 1.2 = 7{,}140$

$$\text{Operational Risk Margin} = \min\left(30\% \times 81{,}500,\ 8.5\% \times 81{,}500 + 2.5\% \times 115{,}500 + 1.75\% \times 20{,}400 + 2.5\% \times 34{,}650 + 2.5\% \times 7{,}140\right)$$

$$= \min(24{,}450,\ 11{,}216.75) = 11{,}216.75$$

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to calculate the operational risk margin.
Common errors included:

- Not knowing the appropriate factors to apply in the formula
- Not validating whether the increase in premium was higher than 20%

## Part b (0.75 points)

Calculate the diversification credit at target.

### Explanation
$$\text{Diversification credit} = A + I - \sqrt{A^2 + I^2 + 2 \times R \times A \times I}$$

- $A$ = credit risk + market risk $= 52{,}800 + 2{,}000 = 54{,}800$
- $I = 26{,}700$

$$\text{Diversification credit} = 54{,}800 + 26{,}700 - \sqrt{54{,}800^2 + 26{,}700^2 + 2 \times 0.5 \times 54{,}800 \times 26{,}700} = 9{,}534.11$$

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to calculate the diversification credit.
A common error was not knowing the formula.

## Part c (1 point)

Based on the answers from parts a. and b. above, calculate this company's MCT ratio and determine whether it meets two important thresholds for federally-regulated property and casualty insurance companies.

### Explanation
MCT Ratio = Total Capital Available / Total Minimum Capital Required

$$\text{Total Minimum Capital Required} = \frac{\text{Insurance risk margin} + \text{market risk margin} + \text{credit risk margin} + \text{operational risk margin} - \text{diversification credit}}{1.5}$$

$$= \frac{26{,}700 + 52{,}800 + 2{,}000 + 11{,}217 - 9{,}534}{1.5} = 55{,}455$$

$$\text{MCT Ratio} = 59{,}400 / 55{,}455 = 107.1\%$$

This does meet the minimum MCT ratio of 100% for federally regulated P&C insurers, but does not meet the supervisory target capital ratio of 150% established by OSFI.

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to calculate the MCT and assess if the company meets important thresholds.
A common error was not being able to identify the ratios numerically.

## Part d (0.75 points)

Fully describe the actions that insurance companies are required to take when their MCT ratio falls below their internal target ratio.

### Explanation
The insurer should inform OSFI and provide plans on how it expects to manage the risks and/or restore its Available Capital to its internal target within a relatively short period of time.

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to describe the actions required by a company if the MCT falls below the internal target ratio.
There were no common errors made by candidates on this part.
