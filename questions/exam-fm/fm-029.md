You are given the following information with respect to a bond:

(i) par value: 1000

(ii) term to maturity: 3 years

(iii) annual coupon rate: 6% payable annually

You are also given that the one, two, and three year annual spot interest rates are 7%, 8%, and 9% respectively.

The bond is sold at a price equal to its value.

Calculate the annual effective yield rate for the bond $i$.

- A) 8.1%
- B) 8.3%
- C) 8.5%
- D) 8.7%
- E) 8.9%

## Explanation

Using spot rates, the bond value is (same as Q28):

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= \frac{60}{1.07} + \frac{60}{1.08^2} + \frac{1060}{1.09^3} \\
  &= 926.03
\end{align*}
$$

The yield rate $i$ satisfies:

$$926.03 = 60a_{\overline{3}|i} + 1000(1+i)^{-3}$$

Solving numerically, $i = 8.9\%$.
