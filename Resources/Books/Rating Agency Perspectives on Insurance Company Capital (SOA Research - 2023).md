A research paper to aid students and practitioners in understanding how rating agencies affect the insurance industry, dated August 2023. It examines insurer credit ratings, including the rating types, the rating process and the rating scales that four major rating agencies use, and the quantitative capital adequacy tools each agency uses to compare available capital with required capital.
