- evidence: The standard increasing annuity-immediate is (Ia)_n = (a-double-dot_n - n v^n)/i (Finan §26 eq. 26.5, PDF p.235, image read); at 10%, (Ia)_20 = (1.1 x 8.51356 - 2.97287)/0.10 = 63.9205, not 55.407.
