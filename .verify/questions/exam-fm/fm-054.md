- claim: The stem gave only a coupon rate for each bond ('The only investments available are three bonds (par value 100, redeemed at par)' + table with header 'Yield-to-Maturity') and never said the coupons are annual or that Bond B is a zero-coupon bond.
- evidence: SOA Q54 (questions PDF p.25, page image): 'The only investments available to the company are the following three bonds. Bond A and Bond C are annual coupon bonds. Bond B is a zero-coupon bond.' and the table header 'Yield-to-Maturity (Annualized)'. The answer depends on the coupon frequency: with annual coupons Bond C pays 5 at t=1,2 and 105 at t=3, giving N_C = 100/105 = 0.95238, N_B = (102 - 4.7619)/100 = 0.97238, N_A = (99 - 4.7619)/107 = 0.88073 (key A); with semiannual coupons Bond C would pay 2.5 at t=0.5,...,2.5 and 102.5 at t=3, and the liabilities at t=1,2,3 could not be matched exactly at all. SOA's solution (solutions PDF p.16) uses 105 and 107, i.e. annual coupons.
