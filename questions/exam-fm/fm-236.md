A perpetuity-immediate with annual payments is priced at $X$ based on an annual effective interest rate of 10%. The amount of the first payment is 14,000. Each payment, from the second through the twentieth, is 4% larger than the previous payment. The 21st payment and each subsequent payment will be 1% larger than the previous payment.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 185,542
- B) 191,834
- C) 206,540
- D) 208,508
- E) 212,823

## Explanation

Part 1: PV of first 20 geometrically increasing payments (growth 4%, yield 10%):
$$
\begin{align*}
PV_1 &= 14{,}000\cdot\frac{1-(1.04/1.10)^{20}}{0.10-0.04} \\
     &= 14{,}000\cdot\frac{1-0.3276}{0.06} \\
     &= 14{,}000 \times 11.2067 \\
     &= 157{,}337
\end{align*}
$$

Part 2: PV of perpetuity starting at payment 21. Payment 21 $= 14{,}000(1.04)^{19}(1.01)$. Discounting to time 20:
$$
\begin{align*}
PV_{21\text{ onward at time }20} &= \frac{14{,}000(1.04)^{19}(1.01)}{0.10-0.01} \\
                                 &= \frac{14{,}000(2.1068)(1.01)}{0.09} \\
                                 &= 330{,}527
\end{align*}
$$

PV at time 0: $330{,}527(1.10)^{-20} = 330{,}527 \times 0.14864 = 49{,}142$

Total: $157{,}337 + 49{,}202 = 206{,}540$.
