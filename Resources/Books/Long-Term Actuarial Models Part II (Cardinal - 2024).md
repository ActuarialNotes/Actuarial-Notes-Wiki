A book by Timothy Cardinal, published by Cardinalis 1 LLC in 2024, that the Society of Actuaries' CP 312 Model Development and Governance syllabus assigns under its topic on cash flow models for long-term insurance business. The syllabus assigns its chapters on introduction to modeling, principle-based projections and nested methods, secondary guarantee universal life, fixed and fixed indexed annuities and long term health products, including their workbooks.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-312 (SOA)|Exam CP-312]] — Ch. 1: Introduction to Modeling (excluding search online exercises in section 1.5) (1a); Ch. 3: Principle-Based Projections and Nested Methods, Sections 3.1 to 3.2, including workbooks (1a, 1b); Ch. 6: Secondary Guarantee Universal Life, including workbooks (1a, 1b); Ch. 9: Fixed and Fixed Indexed Annuities, including workbooks (1a, 1b); Ch. 11: Long Term Health Products, Sections 11.1 to 11.5, including IDI workbooks (1a, 1b).
