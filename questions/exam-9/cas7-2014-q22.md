An insurer has decided to calculate risk-based performance metrics for its two lines of business. It has chosen a risk capital metric of 99th percentile VaR. Below is a summary of results (\$000,000) for the two lines of business.

| Line of Business | Expected Annualized Profit | Coefficient of Variation |
|---|---|---|
| A | 50 | 300% |
| B | 9 | 250% |

- $\Phi^{-1}(0.99) = 2.326$
- Income follows a normal distribution.
- The lines of business are independent.

## Part a (0.75 points)

Determine which line of business performs better using return on risk-adjusted capital.

### Explanation
Standard deviation = CV × mean: $\sigma_A = 3 \times 50 = 150$ and $\sigma_B = 2.5 \times 9 = 22.5$. Risk capital is the 99th percentile VaR of the normal income distribution, expected profit plus 2.326 standard deviations:

$$\text{VaR}_A = 50 + 2.326 \times 150 = 398.9, \qquad \text{RORAC}_A = \frac{50}{398.9} = 12.5\%$$

$$\text{VaR}_B = 9 + 2.326 \times 22.5 = 61.34, \qquad \text{RORAC}_B = \frac{9}{61.34} = 14.7\%$$

B has the higher return on risk-adjusted capital, so B performs better.

Alternatively:

Take the capital required as $z\sigma$ alone: $\text{capital}_A = 2.326 \times 150 = 348.9$ and $\text{capital}_B = 2.326 \times 22.5 = 52.34$, giving returns of $50/348.9 = 14.3\%$ and $9/52.34 = 17.2\%$ — B is still better.

### Examiner Report
Over half of the candidates got full credit for this part. A number of candidates incorrectly calculated allocated capital resulting in a response with negative allocated capital.

## Part b (0.75 points)

Calculate the return on risk capital and the risk-adjusted performance metric for the portfolio as a whole.

### Explanation
The lines are independent, so the portfolio variance is the sum of the variances:

$$\sigma_{\text{total}} = \sqrt{150^2 + 22.5^2} = 151.68$$

$$\text{VaR} = 50 + 9 + 2.326 \times 151.68 = 411.81, \qquad \text{Return} = \frac{59}{411.81} = 14.3\%$$

Alternatively:

With capital $= 2.326\,\sigma_{\text{total}} = 352.8$, the return is $59/352.8 = 16.7\%$.

### Examiner Report
A common error in part b was candidates calculating the portfolio allocated capital as a sum of the allocated capital for Lines A & B.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Allocate the risk capital to both portfolios using the proportional allocation method.

### Explanation
Allocate the portfolio capital in proportion to each line's stand-alone capital:

$$\text{A} = 411.81 \times \frac{398.9}{398.9 + 61.335} = 357\text{M}, \qquad \text{B} = 411.81 \times \frac{61.335}{398.9 + 61.335} = 55\text{M}$$

Alternatively:

$$\text{A} = 352.8 \times \frac{348.9}{348.9 + 52.34} = 306, \qquad \text{B} = 352.8 \times \frac{52.34}{348.9 + 52.34} = 46$$

### Examiner Report
A common error in part c was candidates not using the allocated capital by line to allocate the portfolio risk capital.

## Part d (1.25 points)

Calculate the allocation of capital using an incremental marginal approach and discuss an advantage this approach has over the proportional allocation method.

### Explanation
Treat each line as the last one in: its marginal capital is the portfolio capital less the capital of the other line alone.

- A: $411.81 - 61.335 = 350.475$
- B: $411.81 - 398.9 = 12.91$ (the report's sample prints this step as "= 15" before using 12.91)

The marginals sum to 363.385, so scale them to the portfolio capital:

$$\text{A} = 411.81 \times \frac{350.475}{363.385} = 397\text{M}, \qquad \text{B} = 411.81 \times \frac{12.91}{363.385} = 14.63\text{M}$$

This measures the marginal impact of each business unit, treating it as the last one in. Its advantage over proportional allocation is that it keeps the financial principle of comparing marginal profits with marginal costs: each unit is charged with the additional capital it requires, so growing the business with the highest return raises the return for the whole company.

Alternatively:

On the $z\sigma$ capital basis (portfolio capital 352.8):

| | Portfolio VaR | LOB VaR | Incremental Marginal VaR | % Allocated | Marginal VaR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LOB A | 352.8 | 348.9 | 300.5 | 99% | 348.3 |
| LOB B | 352.8 | 52.3 | 3.9 | 1% | 4.5 |
| Total | | | 304.4 | | 352.8 |

### Examiner Report
Many candidates did not attempt the calculation. Very few candidates got full credit for this part.
