You are given the following information about losses covered by an insurance company:

- Individual losses follow a lognormal distribution with $(\mu = 8,\ \sigma = 1)$
- For insurance payments, an ordinary deductible of 1,000 per loss applies
- Losses below the deductible are not reported to the insurance company

Calculate the mean payment made by the insurance company.

- A) Less than 3,900
- B) At least 3,900, but less than 4,200
- C) At least 4,200, but less than 4,500
- D) At least 4,500, but less than 4,800
- E) At least 4,800

## Explanation

Because losses below the deductible are never reported, the mean payment is a per-payment quantity: the mean excess loss $e(d) = E[X - d \mid X > d]$ at $d = 1{,}000$. For a lognormal, the conditional mean above $d$ has a closed form:

$$
E[X \mid X > d] = \frac{e^{\mu + \sigma^2/2}\,\Phi\left(\sigma - \dfrac{\ln d - \mu}{\sigma}\right)}{1 - \Phi\left(\dfrac{\ln d - \mu}{\sigma}\right)}
$$

With $\mu = 8$, $\sigma = 1$ and $\ln 1000 = 6.9078$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\ln d - \mu}{\sigma} &= -1.0922 \\
e^{\mu + \sigma^2/2} &= e^{8.5} \\
&= 4{,}914.77 \\
\Pr(X > 1000) &= \Phi(1.0922) \\
&= 0.86263
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X \mid X > 1000] &= \frac{4{,}914.77\,\Phi(2.0922)}{0.86263} \\
&= \frac{4{,}914.77 \times 0.98179}{0.86263} \\
&= 5{,}593.7
\end{align*}
$$

Subtract the deductible to get the payment:

$$
\begin{align*}
e(1000) &= 5{,}593.7 - 1{,}000 \\
&= 4{,}593.7
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[4{,}500, 4{,}800)$, so the answer is **D**.
