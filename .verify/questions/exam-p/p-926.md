## [F-001] Exponential waiting time asserted without a Poisson-process assumption
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: explanation, line 38
- claim: The waiting time until the first claim follows an exponential distribution with rate $\lambda$
- evidence: The stem (line 26) says only that the number of claims per year is Poisson; an exponential time to first claim follows from a Poisson process, which the stem does not state. SOA sample solution Q205 (solutions PDF pp.61-62) draws the same line: "the problem provides no information about the distribution of the time to subsequent accidents", and the Poisson count per period follows only "if the time to subsequent accidents has the same exponential distribution and the times are independent". The keyed answer is unaffected: the event {T1 > 1} is the event {no claims in year 1}, whose probability the stem gives as e^{-1.2} = 0.3012 (Poisson p0 = e^{-lambda}, SOA Tables for Exam C PDF p.14), option D.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: State in the stem that claims occur according to a Poisson process, or have the explanation argue from the event equivalence {T1 > 1} = {no claims in year 1}; consider linking Concepts/Poisson+Process.
