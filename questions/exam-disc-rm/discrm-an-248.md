Match each insurer's need to the function of reinsurance it calls for: (1) an insurer wants to stop writing in a state and transfer its remaining policies until they expire; (2) a fast-growing insurer's premium and unearned premium are outrunning its surplus; (3) a regional insurer wants help from its reinsurer's specialists in launching a cyber liability product.

- A) (1) withdrawal, (2) surplus relief, (3) services
- B) (1) catastrophe protection, (2) stability, (3) capacity
- C) (1) withdrawal, (2) capacity, (3) stability
- D) (1) surplus relief, (2) withdrawal, (3) services

## Explanation

- **(1) Withdrawal:** portfolio reinsurance or a 100% quota share carries the policies until they run off.
- **(2) Surplus relief:** reinsurance relieves the strain that growing premium and unearned premium put on surplus.
- **(3) Services:** reinsurers offer underwriting, claims and actuarial reviews and help with new products such as cyber liability.

The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Leaving a state is not a catastrophe, and helping with a product is not capacity.
- **C** — Expertise for a new product is a service, not stability, which is about smoothing results.
- **D** — Swaps surplus relief and withdrawal.
