The NAIC's guidance manual for an insurer, or an insurance group of which it is a member, on reporting its Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA), as required by the domestic state's version of the Risk Management and Own Risk and Solvency Assessment Model Act (#505). The syllabus cites the edition of December 2017; the manual is maintained by the NAIC's Group Solvency Issues (E) Working Group.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam ILA-201U (SOA)|Exam ILA-201U]] — Outcomes 3c; ILA201-802-25, the NAIC ORSA Guidance Manual, Dec 2017 (as the syllabus states it)

> [!note] Contents unavailable
