The **core financial statements** of the [[Canadian Annual Return]] — the P&C insurer's balance sheet, income statement and supporting statements, filed quarterly on forms approved by the [[Canadian Council of Insurance Regulators|CCIR]]. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objectives C1–C4. This is the return Domain C's "describe the elements of the Canadian Annual Return" questions are set on.

## The IFRS 17 shape

Four **liability roll-forwards** — the pages that did not exist before 2023 — are what makes this return an [[IFRS 17]] return:

- Two cuts of the same movement: **by measurement component** ([[Fulfilment Cash Flows|estimates of future cash flows]], [[Risk Adjustment for Non-Financial Risk|risk adjustment]], [[Contractual Service Margin|CSM]]) and **by remaining coverage and incurred claims** ([[Liability for Remaining Coverage|LRC]] / [[Liability for Incurred Claims|LIC]], with the [[Loss Component|loss component]] shown separately).
- The measurement-component roll-forward applies only to contracts **not** measured under the PAA, since a PAA group has no CSM to roll forward. The LRC/LIC roll-forward covers everything.
- The same pair is repeated for **reinsurance contracts held**, because IFRS 17 measures reinsurance held as a separate asset rather than netting it.
