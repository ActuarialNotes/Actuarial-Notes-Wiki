# PC Module 2
**PC Module 2 – Insurance on property and insurance against liability** is one of the two modules of the property and casualty track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the module
>
> The primary audience for the FCIA modules is individuals who desire to be admitted as Fellows of the CIA.
>
> Individuals seeking continuing professional development credit can also register for the FCIA modules.
>
> Each FCIA candidate through Pathways 1 and 2 is required to complete both FCIA modules, while Pathway 3 candidates will have to complete the second module. It is anticipated that FCIA module candidates will have six months from the date of module purchase to complete each FCIA module. As a general guideline, candidates should expect to spend approximately 100 hours completing all activities included in a module.

## Learning Objectives

### Part A – Insurance on property

> [!example]- Introduction to property insurance
>
> 1. Explain [[Property Insurance|property insurance]] and how it works.
> 2. Describe the elements of a [[Property Insurance Policy|property insurance policy]].
> 3. Describe the evolution of [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].
> 4. Describe who and what are covered in [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].
> 5. Identify and describe the development of [[Property Insurance Contract|property insurance contracts]] and key concepts, including [[Claim|claim]] concepts.

> [!example]- Legislation and general principles in common-law provinces and territories and Québec
>
> 1. Explain the legal context for [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].
> 2. Describe the legislation and its interaction with [[Property Policy|property policies]].
> 3. Describe the impact of [[Privacy Law|privacy laws]] in [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].

> [!example]- Perils and hazards
>
> 1. Define “[[Peril|peril]].”
> 2. Explain the scope of [[Property Insurance|property insurance]] including key concepts such as [[Fortuitous Event|fortuitous event]] and perils and [[Risk|risk]].
> 3. Identify and describe the [[Named Peril|named perils]] in [[Insurance Bureau of Canada]] [[Habitational Form|Habitational Forms]]: discussion.
> 4. Identify and describe [[Non-Standard Peril|non-standard perils]].
> 5. Describe and provide examples of [[Hazard|hazards]].

> [!example]- Anatomy of a property policy: Overview
>
> 1. Describe the structure and types of [[Personal Property Policy|personal property policies]].
> 2. Describe key [[Policy Form|policy forms]] and clauses in [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].
> 3. Explain how to read a [[Property Policy|property policy]].

> [!example]- Anatomy of a property policy: The homeowners form
>
> 1. Identify and describe the opening sections of [[Homeowner Form|homeowner forms]].
> 2. Describe [[Property Insurance Coverage|property insurance coverages]], including:
>    - a. Coverage A – [[Dwelling Building|dwelling building]].
>    - b. Coverage B – [[Detached Private Structure|detached private structures]].
>    - c. Coverage C – [[Personal Property|personal property]].
>    - d. Coverage D – additional [[Living Expense|living expense]].
> 3. Describe extensions, applications, and limitations of [[Coverage|coverage]].
> 4. Explain the basis of [[Claim Payment|claim payment]] in [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].

> [!example]- Anatomy of a property policy: Exclusions
>
> 1. Provide an overview of exclusions in a [[Property Policy|property policy]].
> 2. Describe exclusions for [[Property|property]] not insured.
> 3. Describe exclusions for loss or [[Damage|damage]] not insured.
> 4. Describe application of the [[Water-Damage Exclusion|water-damage exclusion]].

> [!example]- Endorsements and floaters
>
> 1. Describe and provide examples of [[Endorsement|endorsements]] in [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].
> 2. Describe and provide examples of floaters in [[Property Insurance|property insurance]].

> [!example]- Other property coverages
>
> 1. Describe [[Property Coverage|property coverage]] for condominiums.
> 2. Describe [[Property Coverage|property coverage]] for tenants.

> [!example]- Commercial property insurance
>
> 1. Describe the [[Named Peril|named perils]] and [[Broad-Form Peril|broad-form perils]] associated with [[Commercial Property Insurance|commercial property insurance]].
> 2. Describe exclusions of the [[Commercial Property Broad|commercial property broad]] form.
> 3. Explain limits of insurance of the [[Commercial Property Broad|commercial property broad]] form.

### Part B – Insurance against liability

> [!example]- Introduction to liability insurance
>
> 1. Describe liability imposed by [[Law|law]].
> 2. Describe [[Canadian Law|Canadian law]], including.
>    - a. Jurisdiction.
>    - b. Two systems of [[Civil Law|civil law]] in Canada.

> [!example]- Negligence: The ABC rule (common Law)
>
> 1. Describe [[Negligence|negligence]] in tort.
> 2. Describe the duty of care, including:
>    - a. [[Duty-to-your-neighbour Principle|Duty-to-your-neighbour principle]].
>    - b. [[Duty-of-care Legislation|Duty-of-care legislation]].
>    - c. [[Contractual Duty|Contractual duty]].
>    - d. [[Bailee's Duty|Bailee’s duty]] of care.
> 3. Describe breach of duty including foreseeability and the causal relationship between breach and [[Damage|damages]].

> [!example]- Defenses against negligence in common law
>
> 1. Identify and describe [[Defenses Against Negligence|defenses against negligence]], including:
>    - a. Common defenses.
>    - b. Defenses to [[Strict Liability|strict liability]].
>    - c. Defenses to [[Absolute Liability|absolute liability]].
> 2. Describe the effect of onus of [[Proof|proof]].

> [!example]- Québec: Extra-contractual liability
>
> 1. Describe [[Extra-Contractual Liability|extra-contractual liability]] in Québec.
> 2. Describe causal relationship between the fault and the [[Injury|injury]].
> 3. Explain injury or [[Damage|damage]] suffered.

> [!example]- Québec: Defenses against extra-contractual liability
>
> 1. Identify and describe defenses against [[Extra-Contractual Liability|extra-contractual liability]] in Québec.
> 2. Identify and describe defenses for manufacturers and distributors of [[Product|products]].
> 3. Explain [[Limitation Period|limitation periods]].

> [!example]- General legal principles
>
> 1. Describe legal concepts between parties in a [[Liability Lawsuit|liability lawsuit]].
> 2. Describe the [[Principal|principal]] and [[Agent|agent]] relationship in a [[Liability Lawsuit|liability lawsuit]].
> 3. Explain [[Municipal Liability|municipal liability]] and categories of [[Negligence|negligence]].
> 4. Describe [[Class Action|class actions]], including [[Limitation Period|limitation periods]] and [[Judgment Interest|judgment interest]].

> [!example]- Products liability and its effects
>
> 1. Describe [[Products Liability|products liability]], including burden of proof for [[Products Claim|products claims]] and [[Contract Theory|contract theory]] of [[Products Liability|products liability]].
> 2. Explain the effect of warnings and labels in [[Product Liability|product liability]].
> 3. Describe other defences to [[Product Liability|product liability]] including [[Contributory Negligence|contributory negligence]] and [[Intermediate Examination|intermediate examination]].

> [!example]- Standard commercial general liability policy
>
> 1. Identify and describe [[Insuring Agreement|insuring agreements]] and their exclusions in [[Commercial General Liability|commercial general liability]] ([[Commercial General Liability|CGL]]) policy, including:
>    - a. Section I – Coverages.
>    - b. Section II – Who is insured.
>    - c. Section III – Limits of insurance.
>    - d. Section IV – Conditions.
>    - e. Section V – Definitions.
> 2. Identify and describe common exclusions of the [[CGL Policy|CGL policy]].

> [!example]- Declarations page, other employer’s liability coverages
>
> 1. Describe the [[CGL Policy Declaration|CGL policy declarations]].
> 2. Identify and describe other [[Employer Liability Coverage|employer liability coverages]], including [[Workers' Compensation|workers’ compensation]] and [[Employer's Liability Insurance|employer’s liability insurance]].

> [!example]- Emerging trends in liability insurance
>
> 1. Describe [[Emerging Trend|emerging trends]] in [[Liability Insurance|liability insurance]], including:
>    - a. [[Cyber Insurance|Cyber risk]].
>    - b. The [[Sharing Economy|sharing economy]].
>    - c. The [[Autonomous Vehicle|autonomous vehicle]].
