A bakery's building is damaged by fire. Repairing it with materials of like kind and quality would cost \$250,000, and depreciation on the damaged parts is \$70,000. While the bakery is closed it loses \$60,000 of income, and it spends \$15,000 extra to rent a temporary kitchen for its key orders. Measuring the property damage at actual cash value, what is the total financial consequence of the fire?

- A) \$180,000
- B) \$255,000
- C) \$310,000
- D) \$325,000

## Explanation

The financial consequences of a property loss are the reduction in value, the income lost and the extra expense:

$$
\begin{align*}
\Delta V_{\text{ACV}} &= 250{,}000 - 70{,}000 \\
&= 180{,}000 \\
L &= 180{,}000 + 60{,}000 + 15{,}000 \\
&= \mathbf{255{,}000}
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$180,000 counts only the damage to the building and leaves out lost income and extra expense.
- **C** — \$310,000 values the damage at replacement cost and leaves out the extra expense.
- **D** — \$325,000 values the damage at replacement cost instead of actual cash value.
