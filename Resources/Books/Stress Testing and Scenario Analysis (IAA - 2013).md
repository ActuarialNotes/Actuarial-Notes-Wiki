An International Actuarial Association paper (July 2013) giving an actuarial perspective on scenario analysis and stress testing. It was produced and approved by the IAA's Insurance Regulation Committee. A scenario describes a consistent future state of the world over time, resulting from a plausible and possibly adverse set of events; a stress test assesses an extreme scenario, usually with a severe impact on the firm. The paper covers their uses, the types of scenarios, governance, formulation, risk dependencies and evaluation, with three case studies.
