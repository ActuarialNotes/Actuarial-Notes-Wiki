You plan to fit the following model:

$$
\begin{align*}
y &\sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda) \\
\lambda &\sim \text{Exponential}(5)
\end{align*}
$$

You have the following three data points:

$$
0 \qquad 1 \qquad 4
$$

You use Metropolis-Hastings, and a proposal distribution for $\lambda^{(k)}$ of:

$$
\lambda^* \sim \text{Exponential}\left(\lambda^{(k-1)}\right)
$$

Your current value of $\lambda$ is 4.2 and the proposed value is 4.3.

Calculate the probability of accepting this proposal.

- A) Less than 0.25
- B) Greater than 0.25, but less than 0.45
- C) Greater than 0.45, but less than 0.65
- D) Greater than 0.65, but less than 0.85
- E) Greater than 0.85

## Explanation

The proposal density depends on the current state, so it is **not** symmetric and the Hastings correction is required:

$$
\alpha = \min\left\{\frac{p(\lambda^*)\,L(\lambda^*)\,q(\lambda_{curr} \mid \lambda^*)}{p(\lambda_{curr})\,L(\lambda_{curr})\,q(\lambda^* \mid \lambda_{curr})},\ 1\right\}
$$

**Likelihood ratio.** With $\sum y_i = 5$ over $n = 3$ observations, $L(\lambda) \propto e^{-3\lambda}\lambda^{5}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{L(4.3)}{L(4.2)} &= e^{-3(4.3-4.2)}\left(\frac{4.3}{4.2}\right)^{5} \\
                      &= (0.7408)(1.1249) \\
                      &= 0.8333
\end{align*}
$$

**Prior ratio.** The exponential prior has mean 5, so $p(\lambda) = \frac{1}{5}e^{-\lambda/5}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{p(4.3)}{p(4.2)} &= e^{-(4.3-4.2)/5} \\
                      &= e^{-0.02} \\
                      &= 0.9802
\end{align*}
$$

**Proposal ratio.** The proposal is exponential with mean equal to the state it is centred on, $q(b \mid a) = \frac{1}{a}e^{-b/a}$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{q(4.2 \mid 4.3)}{q(4.3 \mid 4.2)} &= \frac{\frac{1}{4.3}e^{-4.2/4.3}}{\frac{1}{4.2}e^{-4.3/4.2}} \\
                                        &= \frac{4.2}{4.3}\,e^{\,4.3/4.2 \,-\, 4.2/4.3} \\
                                        &= (0.9767)(1.0482) \\
                                        &= 1.0237
\end{align*}
$$

**Combine:**

$$
\begin{align*}
r &= (0.8333)(0.9802)(1.0237) \\
  &= 0.8362
\end{align*}
$$

Since $r < 1$, the acceptance probability is 0.8362, which falls in $(0.65,\, 0.85)$, so the answer is **D**.
