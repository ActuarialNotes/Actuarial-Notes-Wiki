FSCO Policy S900-510, effective September 30, 2004, now kept by the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario as guidance PE0596ORG. It sets out the procedure for filing an application to distribute surplus to an employer on the full wind up of a pension plan under section 78 of the Pension Benefits Act and section 8 of Regulation 909, covering the notice of the application, the written agreements, the application itself and the member statement.

## General
- The onus is on the applicant to satisfy the Superintendent that the surplus application meets the requirements of the Pension Benefits Act and the Regulation.
- If information necessary for the Superintendent to approve a surplus application is missing, the Superintendent will not be able to consent.

## Distribution of Surplus to an Employer on Full Wind Up

### General Principles
- Under section 78 of the Act the employer must apply, and no surplus may be paid to it without the Superintendent's prior consent.
- An employer winding up a plan would generally not file a surplus application until the payment of basic benefits from the plan has been approved.
- Where surplus is distributed to the employer under a surplus sharing agreement, it is paid in cash after all other distributions have been made.
