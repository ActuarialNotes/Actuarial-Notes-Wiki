A Society of Actuaries study note (code RET201-107-25) for the RET 201 course, made up of the Introduction (A58), IFRS 1 (paragraphs 1-40 and Appendix A), IAS 19 and IFRIC 14. The SOA's course strategy guide describes it as the text of the standard for international retirement plan accounting. The IFRS Foundation describes IAS 19 as prescribing the accounting for all types of employee benefits except share-based payment.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-201 (SOA)|Exam RET-201]] — Outcomes 3a-3d; the Introduction (A58), IFRS 1 paragraphs 1-40 and Appendix A, IAS 19 and IFRIC 14.
