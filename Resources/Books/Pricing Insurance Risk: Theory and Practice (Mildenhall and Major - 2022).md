An account of how to determine the premium for a portfolio of non-hedgeable insurance risks and how to allocate it fairly to each portfolio component. The authors synthesize hundreds of academic research papers on the relationships between insurance risk, capital and premium, and connect the theory to practice: assessing the performance of business units, evaluating risk transfer options and optimizing portfolio mix. Part of the Wiley Series in Probability and Statistics.
