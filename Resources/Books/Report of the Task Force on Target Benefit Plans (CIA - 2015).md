A June 2015 report of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Task Force on Target Benefit Plans, set up in response to public interest in target benefit pension plans (TBPs). Its mandate was to recommend how TBPs should be managed, comment on certain regulatory aspects of such plans, and recommend whether the CIA should promote them and to what extent. It defines a TBP as a collective, pre-funded pension plan pooling both economic and demographic risks, with a predefined retirement income goal.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — Report of the Task Force on Target Benefit Plans, CIA, June 2015 (excluding sections 4, 5 and the appendices); Outcomes 1a, 1b; 2d; 3a, 3b, 3c. The SOA course strategy guide says it gives a good overview of target benefit plans as the term is defined in a Canadian regulatory context, and is assigned because their risk-sharing concepts could be applied in other countries.
