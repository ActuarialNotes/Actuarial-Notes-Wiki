![[ASOP 42 - Health and Disability Actuarial Assets and Liabilities Other than Liabilities for Incurred Claims - Cover.svg]]

The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries estimating or reviewing health benefit plan actuarial assets and liabilities, other than liabilities for incurred claims, when preparing or reviewing financial reports, claims studies, rates, or other actuarial communications as of a valuation date. This revised edition (Doc. No. 191) was adopted in March 2018 and is effective for work with a valuation date on or after August 1, 2018.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201U (SOA)|Exam GH-201U]] — Outcomes 1g; the standard excluding its Appendices.

## Transmittal Memorandum

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
-     - Complements ASOP No. 5, Incurred Health and Disability Claims.
- 1.2 Scope
-     - Applies to actuaries estimating or reviewing health benefit plan actuarial assets and liabilities, other than liabilities for incurred claims, on behalf of risk-bearing entities.
- 1.3 Cross References
- 1.4 Effective Date
-     - Effective for any actuarial work product with a valuation date on or after August 1, 2018.
