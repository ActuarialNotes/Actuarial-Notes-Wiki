You are told that $\theta$ represents the probability of a positive outcome for the random variable $X$. Accordingly, you specify the following model:

$$
\begin{align*}
X &\sim \text{Bernoulli}(\theta) \\
p(\theta) &\sim 1/\theta^2; \quad \theta > 0
\end{align*}
$$

You observe $X$ for one trial and note that the outcome is positive. You select the following proposal distribution for the Metropolis algorithm:

$$
\theta_{prop} =
\begin{cases}
\theta_{curr} + 0.1 & \text{; probability} = 0.5 \\
\theta_{curr} - 0.1 & \text{; probability} = 0.5
\end{cases}
$$

$\theta_{prop}$ and $\theta_{curr}$ represent the proposed and current parameter values.

The algorithm is seeded with an initial value for $\theta$ of 0.45.

Calculate the probability that after two iterations of the algorithm, $\theta$ is at 0.45.

- A) Less than 0.15
- B) Greater than or equal to 0.15 but less than 0.25
- C) Greater than or equal to 0.25 but less than 0.35
- D) Greater than or equal to 0.35 but less than 0.45
- E) Greater than or equal to 0.45

## Explanation

**The acceptance rule.** With one positive observation the likelihood is $\theta$, so the unnormalized posterior is

$$
p(\theta \mid x) \propto \theta \cdot \frac{1}{\theta^2} = \frac{1}{\theta}.
$$

The proposal is symmetric, so this is plain Metropolis:

$$
\alpha = \min\left\{\frac{1/\theta_{prop}}{1/\theta_{curr}},\ 1\right\} = \min\left\{\frac{\theta_{curr}}{\theta_{prop}},\ 1\right\}
$$

A **downward** move is always accepted; an **upward** move from $\theta$ to $\theta + 0.1$ is accepted with probability $\theta/(\theta+0.1)$.

**Iteration 1** from $\theta = 0.45$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P(\to 0.55) &= 0.5 \times \frac{0.45}{0.55} = 0.4091 \\
P(\to 0.35) &= 0.5 \times 1 = 0.5000 \\
P(\text{stay at } 0.45) &= 0.5 \times \left(1 - \frac{0.45}{0.55}\right) = 0.0909
\end{align*}
$$

**Iteration 2** — the ways to be at 0.45:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{from } 0.55: \quad &0.5 \times 1 = 0.5 \quad \text{(a downward move, always accepted)} \\
\text{from } 0.45: \quad &0.5 \times \left(1 - \tfrac{0.45}{0.55}\right) = 0.0909 \quad \text{(stay again)} \\
\text{from } 0.35: \quad &0.5 \times \frac{0.35}{0.45} = 0.3889 \quad \text{(an upward move)}
\end{align*}
$$

**Combine:**

$$
\begin{align*}
P(\theta = 0.45) &= (0.4091)(0.5) + (0.0909)(0.0909) + (0.5)(0.3889) \\
                 &= 0.2045 + 0.0083 + 0.1944 \\
                 &= 0.4073
\end{align*}
$$

This falls in $[0.35,\, 0.45)$, so the answer is **D**.
