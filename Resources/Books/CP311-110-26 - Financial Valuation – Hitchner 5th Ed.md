Chapter 10, Valuation Discounts and Premiums, of the fifth edition of Financial Valuation: Applications and Models, edited by James R. Hitchner (Wiley, November 2024), as the SOA study note CP311-110-26. Wiley describes the book as authoritative reference and practical guidance on the appropriate, defensible way to prepare and present business valuations, with contributions by 30 experts.
