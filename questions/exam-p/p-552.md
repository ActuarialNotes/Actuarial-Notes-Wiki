The distribution of the lifetime in years of a certain brand of lawnmower is uniform on the interval $[a, b]$, where $0 < a < b$.

Determine the 80th percentile of the lifetime in years of this brand of lawnmower.

- A) $a + 0.8b$
- B) $0.2a + 0.8b$
- C) $0.8a + 0.2b$
- D) $0.8(b-a)$
- E) $0.8(b+a)$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution on $[a,b]$, the cumulative distribution function is linear:

$$
F(x) = \frac{x - a}{b - a}
$$

Setting $F(x_{0.8}) = 0.8$ and solving:

$$
\begin{align*}
x_{0.8} - a &= 0.8(b-a) \\
x_{0.8} &= a + 0.8(b-a) \\
        &= \mathbf{0.2a + 0.8b}
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is a **weighted average** of the endpoints, with the weights summing to 1 — that is the structural check that eliminates most of the choices at a glance. Choice (D), $0.8(b-a)$, is the *distance* travelled from $a$ rather than the position itself, and would be negative-free only because it omits the starting point. Choice (C) has the weights on the wrong endpoints, placing the 80th percentile nearer $a$ than $b$.
