An insurer domiciled in Iowa writes personal auto insurance in Iowa, Illinois and Texas. Which regulator leads its periodic financial examination, and which reviews a rate increase for its Texas policies?

- A) The NAIC conducts the examination and reviews the Texas rate
- B) Iowa conducts the examination and reviews the Texas rate
- C) Iowa leads the examination, and Texas reviews the Texas rate
- D) The Federal Insurance Office leads the examination, and Texas reviews the rate

## Explanation

Solvency follows domicile: the **domiciliary state**, Iowa, leads the financial examination and other states rely on it. Rates and market conduct follow the policyholder: **Texas** regulates the rates charged on Texas policies under its own law, and Iowa has no say over them.

- **A** treats the NAIC as a regulator. It is the commissioners' association, writing model laws and running analysis tools, with no regulatory power of its own.
- **B** gives the domiciliary state control of another state's rates.
- **D** gives the Federal Insurance Office a supervisory role. It monitors the industry but does not regulate insurers.
