A management science textbook by Dimitris Bertsimas and Robert Freund, used in MIT Sloan's core MBA course of the same name. Its publisher describes it as teaching management science through real-world applications, using spreadsheets to model and solve problems and covering statistics, simulation, probabilistic modeling and optimization, with over thirty business cases and a capstone chapter on decision modeling in airlines, investment management and manufacturing.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-311 (SOA)|Exam CP-311]] — Ch. 5 Simulation Modeling: Concepts and Practice; Ch. 7 Linear Optimization; Ch. 8 Nonlinear Optimization; Ch. 9 Discrete Optimization (each for outcomes 3a-3c).
