A textbook by Runhuan Feng, published by CRC Press in 2018, that provides a resource for students and entry-level professionals to understand the fundamentals of industrial modeling practice for equity-linked insurance, and gives a glimpse of software methodologies for modeling and computational efficiency. The publisher describes it as a comprehensive and self-contained introduction to quantitative risk management of equity-linked insurance, with a review of both Monte Carlo simulation methods and non-simulation numerical methods. The SOA supplies Sections 1.2-1.3, 4.7 and 6.2-6.3 as study note INV201-105-25, formerly QFIQ-134-22.
