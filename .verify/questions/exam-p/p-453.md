## [F-001] Closing remark misstates the variance of a purely continuous payment
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: ## Explanation, final paragraph
- claim: The point mass at zero is what makes E[X^2] and E[X]^2 differ by only 6000p^2 rather than the 9600p^2 a purely continuous payment would give.
- evidence: A payment with no point mass at zero, i.e. p(Y-240) given Y > 240, is uniform on [0, 240p]: mean 120p, second moment (240p)^2/3 = 19,200p^2, variance 19,200p^2 - 14,400p^2 = 4800p^2. So a purely continuous payment gives 4800p^2, not 9600p^2, and the point mass raises the variance (4800p^2 to 6000p^2) rather than holding it down; 9600p^2 is E[X^2] itself, which would equal the variance only if E[X] = 0. The SOA solution (solutions PDF p.126) has no such remark and gets Var = 9600p^2 - (60p)^2 = 6000p^2, p = sqrt(2000/6000) = 0.57735, as the vault does. Rank-5 recomputation falsifies the sentence; keyed answer unaffected.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Delete the final sentence, or have a maintainer rewrite it (the continuous piece alone has variance 4800p^2; the point mass at zero raises it to 6000p^2).
