An office building's owner insures it under the BPP with limits shown for Building and Your Business Personal Property. A fire in the lobby damages four things. Which one is covered property?

- A) \$4,000 of cash kept in the building manager's safe
- B) The paved walkway and patio at the building's entrance
- C) The owner's licensed pickup truck parked by the entrance
- D) Lobby carpeting and the fire extinguishers that service the building

## Explanation

The Building item includes personal property the named insured owns and uses to maintain or service the building, and names fire-extinguishing equipment and floor coverings among the examples. The carpeting and extinguishers are therefore **Building** property. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Money is on the list of property not covered; a cash loss belongs to crime insurance.
- **B** — Bridges, roadways, walks, patios and other paved surfaces are property not covered.
- **C** — Vehicles licensed for use on public roads are property not covered (the exceptions are for vehicles the insured manufactures, processes, warehouses or holds for sale).
