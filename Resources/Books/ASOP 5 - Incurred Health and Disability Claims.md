The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries estimating or reviewing incurred claims under a health benefit plan, when preparing or reviewing financial reports, claims studies, rates, or other actuarial communications as of a valuation date. This revised edition (Doc. No. 186) was adopted in March 2017 and is effective for actuarial work products issued on or after September 1, 2017.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201U (SOA)|Exam GH-201U]] — Outcomes 1g; the standard excluding its Appendices.

## Transmittal Memorandum

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
-     - Applies to actuaries who estimate or review incurred claims under health benefit plans on behalf of risk-bearing entities, such as managed-care entities, self-funded employer plans, health care providers, government-sponsored plans or risk contracts, or government agencies.
-     - Does not give guidance on reserves such as policy reserves, premium reserves, or claim settlement expense reserves, and does not address interpretations of statutory or generally accepted accounting practices.
- 1.3 Cross References
- 1.4 Effective Date
-     - Effective for any actuarial work product covered by the scope issued on or after September 1, 2017.
