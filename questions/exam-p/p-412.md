Claims on a liability policy are independent and uniformly distributed on the interval $[0, 10]$. An auditor randomly selects three claims.

Calculate the probability that the maximum of the three claims is less than 7.

- A) $0.027$
- B) $0.081$
- C) $0.189$
- D) $0.343$
- E) $0.441$

## Explanation

The maximum of a set of values is less than 7 exactly when **every** value is less than 7 — that equivalence is what makes maximum order statistics easy.

For a single claim uniform on $[0,10]$,

$$
P[X < 7] = \frac{7 - 0}{10 - 0} = 0.7
$$

The three claims are independent, so the probabilities multiply:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[\max < 7] &= (0.7)^3 \\
            &= \mathbf{0.343}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (A), 0.027, is $(0.3)^3$ — the probability that all three *exceed* 7, which is the event that the **minimum** is greater than 7, not a statement about the maximum. Note that $P[\max < 7]$ and $P[\min > 7]$ are not complements: most outcomes have some claims on each side of 7.
