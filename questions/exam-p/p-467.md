Within a fleet of aircraft, planes are subject to mechanical inspection. For a randomly selected airplane, let $X$ denote the number of inspections in the past year and $Y$ the number of repairs. The joint probability function of $X$ and $Y$ is given by

$$
p(x,y) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{x - 2y - xy + 3}{18}, & \text{for } x = 1, 2, 3 \text{ and } y = 0, 1 \\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the expected number of repairs per inspection, $E\left(\dfrac{Y}{X}\right)$.

- A) $3/38$
- B) $11/108$
- C) $11/36$
- D) $1/2$
- E) $11/18$

## Explanation

Evaluate the joint function at all six cells:

| | $X = 1$ | $X = 2$ | $X = 3$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $Y = 0$ | 4/18 | 5/18 | 6/18 |
| $Y = 1$ | 1/18 | 1/18 | 1/18 |

They total $18/18 = 1$, confirming the function was read correctly.

The expectation of $Y/X$ sums that ratio over every cell, weighted by the cell probability. Each cell in the $Y = 0$ row contributes $0/x = 0$, so only the bottom row matters:

$$
\begin{align*}
E\left(\frac{Y}{X}\right) &= \left(\frac{1}{1}\right)\left(\frac{1}{18}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{18}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(\frac{1}{18}\right) \\
                          &= \frac{6 + 3 + 2}{108} \\
                          &= \mathbf{\frac{11}{108}}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (C), 11/36, drops the $1/18$ weights and averages the three ratios instead — that is $E[Y/X \mid Y=1]$ scaled wrongly. The three bottom-row cells happen to be equally likely, but each still carries only $1/18$ of the total probability, not $1/3$.
