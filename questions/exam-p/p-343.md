Let $X$ represent the number of defective parts in a shipment of five parts.

$$P[X \geq x] = \frac{1}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{\frac{x-1}{5}}\right), \quad x = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5$$

Calculate $E(X)$.

- A) 0.9
- B) 1.1
- C) 2.1
- D) 2.3
- E) 3.9

## Explanation

$X$ is never negative, so $P[X \geq 0] = 1$, and the formula gives the remaining tail probabilities:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X \geq 0] &= 1 \\
P[X \geq 1] &= \tfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{0}\right) = 0.5 \\
P[X \geq 2] &= \tfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{0.2}\right) = 0.27639 \\
P[X \geq 3] &= \tfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{0.4}\right) = 0.18377 \\
P[X \geq 4] &= \tfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{0.6}\right) = 0.11270 \\
P[X \geq 5] &= \tfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\sqrt{0.8}\right) = 0.05279
\end{align*}
$$

Each probability is the difference of consecutive tail probabilities, $p(x) = P[X \geq x] - P[X \geq x+1]$ (with $p(5) = P[X \geq 5]$):

$$
p(x) = \begin{cases}
0.5, & x = 0 \\
0.22361, & x = 1 \\
0.09262, & x = 2 \\
0.07107, & x = 3 \\
0.05991, & x = 4 \\
0.05279, & x = 5
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
E(X) &= \sum_{i=0}^{5} x_i\, p(x_i) \\
&= 0(0.5) + 1(0.22361) + 2(0.09262) + 3(0.07107) + 4(0.05991) + 5(0.05279) \\
&= 1.12565 \approx \mathbf{1.1}
\end{align*}
$$
