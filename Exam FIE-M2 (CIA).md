# FIE Module 2
**FIE Module 2 – Professional considerations in the investment environment** is one of the two modules of the finance, investments and ERM track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the module
>
> The primary audience for the FCIA modules is individuals who desire to be admitted as Fellows of the CIA.
>
> Individuals seeking continuing professional development credit can also register for the FCIA modules.
>
> Each FCIA candidate through Pathways 1 and 2 is required to complete both FCIA modules, while Pathway 3 candidates will have to complete the second module. It is anticipated that FCIA module candidates will have six months, from the date of module purchase, to complete each FCIA module. As a general guideline, candidates should expect to spend approximately 100 hours completing all activities included in a module.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Overview of the regulatory framework in Canada
>
> 1. Summarize the regulation applicable to the [[Insurance Market|insurance market]] in Canada.
> 2. Summarize the regulation applicable to the [[Investment Market|investment market]] in Canada.
> 3. Summarize the regulation applicable to the [[Retirement Market|retirement market]] in Canada.
> 4. Identify the major regulators of the [[Insurance Sector|insurance sector]] in Canada.
> 5. Identify the major regulators of the [[Investment Sector|investment sector]] in Canada.
> 6. Identify the major regulators of the [[Retirement Sector|retirement sector]] in Canada.
> 7. Summarize the activities of [[Insurance Regulator|insurance regulators]].
> 8. Summarize the activities of [[Investment Regulator|investment regulators]].
> 9. Summarize the activities of the [[Retirement Regulator|retirement regulators]].
> 10. Summarize the activities of [[Banking Regulator|banking regulators]].
> 11. Describe the goals of regulation and laws in the investment and [[Insurance Sector|insurance sectors]].
> 12. Explain how [[Regulation Protects Consumer|regulation protects consumers]] and investors.
> 13. Understand the compliance function in an [[Investment Management Organization|investment management organization]].

> [!example]- Canadian financial reporting
>
> 1. Understand the [[IFRS 17]] framework and contrast the [[General Measurement Model|general measurement model]] and building-block approach with the [[Variable Fee|variable fee]] approach for [[Insurance Contract Liability Measurement|insurance contract liability measurement]].
> 2. Calculate the components of [[Insurance Contract Liabilities|insurance contract liabilities]] under [[IFRS]] 17.
> 3. Evaluate profit and losses, including the [[Amortization|amortization]] of the [[Contractual Service Margin|contractual service margin]] (CSM) , under the [[IFRS 17]] framework, with use of [[Insurance Cash Flows|insurance cash flows]] that vary and do not vary with underlying items.
> 4. Discuss the methods of construction of [[Discount Rate|discount rates]] and [[Yield Curve|yield curve]] under [[IFRS 17]] including the estimation of the [[Illiquidity Premium|illiquidity premium]].
> 5. Describe how [[Climate-Related Risk|climate-related risks]] could impact the [[Valuation|valuation]] assumptions for pricing and reserving.
> 6. Determine key actuarial components of [[IFRS 17 Financial Statement|IFRS 17 financial statements]].
> 7. Calculate key [[Key Financial Measures|financial metrics]] and evaluate an [[Insurer's Financial Performance|insurer’s financial performance]] and strength based on such metrics.
> 8. Describe the [[Appointed Actuary's|Appointed Actuary’s]] role with respect to [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]].

> [!example]- Considerations in Canadian practice
>
> 1. Describe and explain the [[Insurance Market Stakeholder|insurance market stakeholders]], how they interact, and their needs.
> 2. Describe and explain the [[Investment Market Stakeholder|investment market stakeholders]], how they interact, and their needs.
> 3. Describe and explain the [[Banking Market Stakeholder|banking market stakeholders]], how they interact, and their needs.
> 4. Understand the implications of [[Replacing LIBOR|replacing LIBOR]] with alternative [[Reference Rate|reference rates]].
> 5. Understand and discuss the [[Latest Development|latest developments]] of environmental, social, and [[Corporate Governance|governance]] (ESG) investing in Canada.
> 6. Discuss the impacts and potential of [[Insurtech|insurtech]].
> 7. Discuss the application of blockchain and cryptocurrencies on [[Insurance|insurance]].
> 8. Explain the basic properties of a [[Smart Contract|smart contract]].
> 9. Understand centralization versus decentralization and the concept of [[Distributed Consensus|distributed consensus]] in the context of cryptocurrencies.
> 10. Understand the basics of [[Climate Science|climate science]] and its impact on the work of [[Actuary|actuaries]].
> 11. Describe the process to [[Conduct Climate-Scenario|conduct climate-scenario]] analysis for [[Financial Institution|financial institutions]].

> [!example]- Strategic decision-making
>
> 1. Analyze an [[Organization's External Environment|organization’s external environment]] and [[Internal Organization|internal organization]].
> 2. Describe and apply strategic management models, including [[Porter's Five Forces|Porter’s five forces]] and value-chain analysis.
> 3. Describe types of [[Business-Level Strategy|business-level strategies]] and recommend an appropriate [[Business-Level Strategy|business-level strategy]] for a given situation.
> 4. Explain the impact of [[Competitive Dynamics|competitive dynamics]] on strategic management.
> 5. Develop and apply [[Causal Loop Diagram|causal loop diagrams]] that model the [[Feedback Structure|feedback structure]] of [[Complex System|complex systems]].
> 6. Apply stocks and flows to [[Dynamic Modelling|dynamic modelling]].
> 7. Apply [[Dynamic Modelling|dynamic modelling]] to business decisions.
> 8. Understand what is meant by [[Strategic Thinking|strategic thinking]] and management process.
> 9. Determine [[Strategic Question|strategic questions]] related to [[Business Sustainability|business sustainability]] and [[Corporate Governance|corporate governance]].
> 10. Develop a [[Strategic Plan|strategic plan]].
> 11. Evaluate the impact of a [[Strategic Plan|strategic plan]] on the [[Financial Statement|financial statement]] and [[Capital Resource|capital resources]] of an entity.

> [!example]- Asset and portfolio management
>
> 1. Describe the [[Portfolio Management Process|portfolio management process]], and the role of [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policy]], the [[Investment Actuary|investment actuary]], and [[External Portfolio Manager|external portfolio managers]].
> 2. Describe and evaluate how a company's objectives, needs, and [[Constraint|constraints]] affect [[Investment Strategy|investment strategy]] and [[Portfolio|portfolio]] construction.
> 3. Describe the role of and significant considerations related to the design and function of [[Asset Allocation|asset-allocation strategies]].
> 4. Describe [[Fixed-Asset Portfolio|fixed-asset portfolio]] management methods, [[Immunization|immunization]] (including derivatives), [[Cash-Matching Strategy|cash-matching strategies]], and [[Convexity|convexity]].
> 5. Describe and assess alternative [[Portfolio|investment portfolios]] in the context of an [[Insurance Company Portfolio|insurance company portfolio]].
> 6. Describe and apply methods and processes for evaluating [[Portfolio Performance|portfolio performance]], including [[Performance Attribution|performance attribution]], sources of earnings analysis on [[Investment Income|investment income]], benchmarks, metrics, and [[Risk-Adjusted Performance Appraisal|risk-adjusted performance appraisals]].
> 7. Describe the principles of [[Liquidity Risk|liquidity risk]] management.
> 8. Describe [[Hedging|hedging]] against the cost of borrowing through [[Forward Rate|forward rate]], futures and perform related calculations.
> 9. Demonstrate understanding of [[Regulator Guidance|regulator guidance]] and context for different approaches to [[Responsible Investment|responsible investment]] and, specifically, the integration of ESG factors in the [[Investment Process|investment process]].
> 10. Demonstrate an understanding of the underlying issues that constitute factors within each of the [[ESG]] areas.
> 11. Demonstrate an understanding of the [[ESG Market|ESG market]]: relevance, size, scope, key drivers and challenges, and [[Risk|risks]] and opportunities.

> [!example]- Communication and organizational behaviour
>
> 1. Explain differences between good and [[Poor Communication|poor communication]] techniques and their implications.
> 2. Apply techniques to [[Structure Ideas|structure ideas]] logically.
> 3. Develop clear, [[Fact-Based Message|fact-based messages]] that can be communicated persuasively.
> 4. Explain the role of [[Cognitive Bias|cognitive biases]] on [[Making Suboptimal Individual|making suboptimal individual]] decisions.
> 5. Evaluate the role of organizational behavior on [[Organizational Decision-Making Process|organizational decision-making processes]] and efficacy.
> 6. Explain why communication is strategically important to [[Organization|organizations]].
> 7. Describe how information is communicated within [[Organization|organizations]].
> 8. Describe organizational and [[Individual Barrier|individual barriers]] to [[Effective Communication|effective communication]].
> 9. Identify the [[Risk|risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 10. Explain how to [[Overcome Communication Barrier|overcome communication barriers]] and [[Minimize Risk|minimize risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 11. Recognize the influence of culture and diversity on [[Communication|communication]].
> 12. Apply the [[CIA Standard|CIA Standards]] of Practice and Rules of [[Professional Conduct]] in [[Business Communication|business communication]].
