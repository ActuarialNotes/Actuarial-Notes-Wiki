# GB Module 2
**GB Module 2 – Professional considerations in the group benefits environment** is one of the two modules of the group benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the module
>
> The primary audience for the FCIA modules is individuals who desire to be admitted as Fellows of the CIA.
>
> Individuals seeking continuing professional development credit can also register for the FCIA modules.
>
> Each FCIA candidate through Pathways 1 and 2 is required to complete both FCIA modules, while Pathway 3 candidates will have to complete the second module. It is anticipated that FCIA module candidates will have six months from the date of module purchase to complete each FCIA module. As a general guideline, candidates should allocate approximately 100 hours to complete all activities included in a module.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Professional considerations
>
> 1. Outline the qualification requirements of the [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] in Canada.
> 2. Describe the role and responsibilities of the [[Appointed-valuation Actuary|appointed/valuation actuary]] in Canada.
> 3. Outline for which instances an [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] is required in Canada.
> 4. Describe the roles and responsibilities of [[Actuary|actuaries]] in [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance companies]], such as in the areas of [[Valuation|valuation]], pricing, [[Policy Illustration|policy illustration]], and [[Enterprise Risk Management|ERM]].
> 5. Outline qualification requirements under the U.S. [[Qualification Standard|qualification standard]] (USQS).
> 6. Outline requirements of the [[CIA Professional Standard|CIA professional standards]] that may inadvertently be breached in the [[Life Insurance|life insurance]] practice.
> 7. Identify and apply [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|actuarial standards of practice]] relevant to:
>    - a. [[Financial Reporting|Financial reporting]] and [[Valuation|valuation]].
>    - b. [[FCT|Financial condition testing]].
>    - c. Non-guaranteed elements.

> [!example]- Benefit law and regulation
>
> 1. Understand and apply the effects of applicable legislation and regulation, including (but not limited to) privacy, [[Human Right|human rights]], and non-discrimination, on the following:
>    - a. [[Human Right|Human rights]] and non-discrimination.
>    - b. Provisions of [[Canadian Life]] and [[Health Insurance Association]] (CLHIA) guidelines (definitions of groups, [[Takeover Rule|takeover rules]], coordination of benefits, disclosure).
>    - c. [[Provincial Health Plan|Provincial health plans]].
>    - d. Applicable provisions of the [[Civil Code]] of Québec to [[Group Insurance|group insurance]].
>    - e. Applicable provisions of other relevant laws and regulations to [[Group Insurance|group insurance]].
>    - f. Relevant [[Case Law|case law]] and its implications.
>    - g. Provincially [[Mandated Benefit|mandated benefits]] ([[Prescription Drug Coverage|prescription drug coverage]] in Québec, etc.).
>    - h. The [[Main Difference|main differences]] between [[Québec Law|Québec law]] and the rest of Canada (designation of beneficiary, change of [[Insurer|insurers]], [[Civil Union|civil union]], etc.).
>    - i. Legal and [[Tax Aspect|tax aspects]] of self-insurance.
> 2. Demonstrate understanding of [[Regulators' Guidance|regulators’ guidance]] and context for different approaches to [[Responsible Investment|responsible investment]] and specifically, the integration of environmental, social, and [[Corporate Governance|governance]] (ESG) factors in the [[Investment Process|investment process]].
> 3. Demonstrate an understanding of the underlying issues that constitute factors within each of the [[ESG]] areas.
> 4. Demonstrate an understanding of the [[ESG Market|ESG market]]: relevance, size, scope, key drivers and challenges, and [[Risk|risks]] and opportunities.
> 5. Describe examples of [[Public Health|public health]] programs and initiatives and [[Data Issues|data issues]] in the [[United State|United States]] and Canada.

> [!example]- Regulation and taxation of insurance
>
> 1. Explain why [[Solvency Regulation|solvency regulation]] is important.
> 2. Describe implications of [[Solvency|insolvency]].
> 3. Describe the [[Required Financial Reporting|required financial reporting]] requirements.
> 4. Explain the [[Regulatory Process|regulatory processes]] used to monitor [[Solvency|solvency]].
> 5. Explain how contracts are taxed to the owner of the [[Contract|contract]].
> 6. Describe [[Constraint|constraints]] on policy design imposed by [[Tax Law|tax laws]].
> 7. Describe the two major categories of differences between [[Pre-Tax Earning|pre-tax earnings]] and [[Taxable Earning|taxable earnings]].
> 8. Distinguish between the different forms of taxes paid by [[Canadian Company|Canadian companies]].
> 9. Understand the differences between federal and [[Provincial Taxation|provincial taxation]].

> [!example]- Canadian financial reporting
>
> 1. Apply the principles underlying the determination of LICAT, including the following:
>    - a. Identification of significant [[Risk Component|risk components]];
>    - b. Identification of [[Specialized-Product LICAT|specialized-product LICAT]] requirements;
>    - c. Interpreting results from a regulatory perspective; and
>    - d. Compare and contrast to other [[Regulatory Capital Regime|regulatory capital regimes]].
> 2. Describe and explain the regulatory and [[Tax Environment|tax environment]] for insurance and [[Annuities|annuities]] in Canada, specifically: effect on [[Product Development|product development]], reserving, pricing, and [[Business Practice|business practices]].
> 3. Describe key [[IFRS 17]] financial concepts for written and [[Ceded Coverage|ceded coverages]] and how they are calculated.
> 4. Describe [[IFRS 17 Valuation|IFRS 17 valuation]] methods.
> 5. Calculate [[IFRS 17 Liability|IFRS 17 liabilities]] for life and [[Annuity Contract|annuity products]] and their associated riders.

> [!example]- Risk management and mitigation
>
> 1. Outline the roles of [[Rating Agency|rating agencies]], analysts, and [[Regulators Together|regulators together]] with their methods and impact on [[Insurer|insurance companies]].
> 2. Calculate and evaluate [[Financial Conditions Testing|financial conditions testing]] ([[FCT]]).
> 3. Calculate and evaluate [[Stress Testing|stress testing]] ([[ORSA|own-risk and solvency assessment]], or [[ORSA]]).
> 4. Integrate climate-related considerations in the [[ORSA]], [[Stress Testing|stress testing]], and [[Add Climate|add climate]] scenario analysis where applicable.
> 5. Apply the applicable [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|actuarial standards of practice]].
> 6. Outline problems or recent problems faced by [[Insurer|insurance companies]].
> 7. Identify general sources of [[Risk|risk]] in a [[Group Insurance Line|group insurance line]] of business (strategic, operational, control, etc.).
> 8. Identify sources of [[Risk|risk]] in products, including (but not limited to) [[Mortality|mortality]], morbidity, and [[Lapse|lapse]].
> 9. Identify sources of [[Risk|risk]] in investments, including (but not limited to) [[Credit Risk|credit risk]], liquidity, and [[Asset-liability Matching|asset/liability matching]].
> 10. Identify other [[Risk|risks]], including operational, marketplace, competition and [[Expense Risk|expense risks]], [[Legislative Risk|legislative risk]], and [[Risk|risk]] from [[Government Plan Cost Shifting|government plan cost shifting]].
> 11. Outline the relation between [[Risk|risks]] (e.g., product, investments, and operational) and opportunities and influences on [[Firm Strategy|firm strategy]].
> 12. Understand the roles of [[Rating Agency|rating agencies]], analysts, and [[Regulators Together|regulators together]] with their methods and impact on [[Insurer|insurance companies]].

> [!example]- Risk management and reinsurance
>
> 1. Outline and describe the following concepts of [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]]:
>    - a. [[Risk Appetite|Risk appetite]].
>    - b. [[Risk Identification|Risk identification]].
>    - c. [[Risk Assessment|Risk assessment]].
>    - d. [[Risk Monitoring|Risk monitoring]].
>    - e. [[Risk Appetite|Risk limits]].
>    - f. [[Risk Mitigation|Risk mitigation]].
>    - g. [[Risk Capacity|Risk capacity]].
>    - h. [[Risk Tolerance|Risk tolerances]].
>    - i. [[Desired Risk Profile|Desired risk profile]].
>    - j. [[Risk]] objectives.
>    - k. [[Risk Reporting|Risk reporting]].
>    - l. [[Reinsurance]] types and uses.
>    - m. Management and mitigation of [[Catastrophic Claim|catastrophic claims]].
>    - n. [[Non-evidence Maximum|Non-evidence maximums]] (NEMs).
>    - o. [[Underwriting Guidelines|Underwriting guidelines]] (including unique aspects of [[Group Insurance Underwriting|group insurance underwriting]] such as [[Underwriting|underwriting]] on the employer and on the broker).
>    - p. [[Large Amount Pooling|Large amount pooling]].
>    - q. Assuris and [[Canadian Drug Insurance Pooling Corporation]] (CDIPC).
> 2. Explain the purpose and use of [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]] in the context of [[Group Insurance|group insurance]].

> [!example]- Communication and organizational behaviour
>
> 1. Explain differences between good and [[Poor Communication|poor communication]] techniques and their implications.
> 2. Apply techniques to [[Structure Ideas|structure ideas]] logically.
> 3. Develop [[Clear Fact-Based Message|clear fact-based messages]] that can be communicated persuasively.
> 4. Explain the role of [[Cognitive Bias|cognitive biases]] on [[Making Suboptimal Individual|making suboptimal individual]] decisions.
> 5. Evaluate the role of organizational behavior on [[Organizational Decision-Making Process|organizational decision-making processes]] and efficacy.
> 6. Explain why communication is strategically important to [[Organization|organizations]].
> 7. Describe how information is communicated within [[Organization|organizations]].
> 8. Describe organizational and [[Individual Barrier|individual barriers]] to [[Effective Communication|effective communication]].
> 9. Identify the [[Risk|risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 10. Explain how to [[Overcome Communication Barrier|overcome communication barriers]] and [[Minimize Risk|minimize risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 11. Recognize the influence of culture and diversity on [[Communication|communication]].
> 12. Apply [[CIA Standard|CIA Standards]] of Practice and Rules of [[Professional Conduct]] in [[Business Communication|business communication]].
