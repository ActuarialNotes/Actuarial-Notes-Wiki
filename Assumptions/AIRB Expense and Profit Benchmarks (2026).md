The industry benchmarks the AIRB will consider for Alberta automobile rate filings received on or after October 1, 2026, to be used only when an insurer lacks the data to support its own assumptions. The expense selections stay on the 2022 IFRS 4 expense ratio until an IFRS 17 metric is defined. The profit benchmark is the maximum pre-tax profit provision an insurer may use. The loss trend and loss development benchmarks are in the report's appendices and aren't transcribed here yet.
