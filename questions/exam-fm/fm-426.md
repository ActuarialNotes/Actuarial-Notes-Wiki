Company XYZ has a liability of 10,000 due in nine months. To pay this liability, they have an asset portfolio generating cash flows of $X$ in six months and $Y$ in one year. The portfolio is Redington immunized.

The annual effective interest rate is 2%.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 4926
- B) 4975
- C) 5000
- D) 5025
- E) 5074

## Explanation

Redington immunization requires the assets and the liability to have equal present values and equal durations, with the assets' convexity greater (which holds here, since the asset cash flows fall on either side of the liability). Matching the present values, and then the present values weighted by time (which, with the present values equal, matches the Macaulay durations), we have the system of equations

$$
\begin{align*}
Xv^{0.5} + Yv &= 10{,}000v^{0.75} \\
0.5Xv^{0.5} + Yv &= 7500v^{0.75}
\end{align*}
$$

Subtracting the second equation from the first eliminates $Y$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.5Xv^{0.5} &= 2500v^{0.75} \\
X &= 5000v^{0.25} \\
  &= 5000(1.02)^{-0.25} \\
  &= 4975.31
\end{align*}
$$
