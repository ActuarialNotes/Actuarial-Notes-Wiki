## Part a (0.5 points)

Explain why the asbestos litigation system has been an inefficient mechanism for providing compensation to victims of asbestos-related disease.

### Explanation
Either one of the following with description:

- Defense costs and plaintiff's attorney fees consumed a large part of total payments
- At least half of the total claims paid by defendants and their insurers is related to nonmalignant claims

Alternatively:

Because high part of the cost goes to lawyer rather than to compensate injured.

### Examiner Report
Candidates performed well on this part in general.

## Part b (1 point)

A proposed U.S. federal reform to solve the asbestos litigation crisis is the trust fund approach. Fully describe this approach.

### Explanation
Establish a no-fault trust from which claimants meeting asbestos exposure and medical criteria would have been compensated for their injuries

Alternatively:

Fair Act (no passed)

No-fault fund of 140B constituted of insurers, defendant and existing bankruptcy trust to compensate injured meeting medical and exposure criteria.

### Examiner Report
Some candidates spent a lot of effort in describing how the trust fund was formed. Candidates are expected to provide full explanation on how this no-fault trust fund compensates claimants and the underlying criteria for identifying claimants that are eligible for compensation.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Identify two concerns with the trust fund approach to asbestos litigation.

### Explanation
2 of the following:

- How many claims of various disease types will be filed?
- Will the medical and exposure criteria be appropriate to identify victims?
- Is funding adequate?
- Are awards appropriate?
- Is allocation of funding from various contributors adequate?
- Should the federal government contribute to the fund?

- Will the fund be operated efficiently?
- Will any statute withstand constitutional challenges?

Alternatively:

Would the fund is sufficient?

Would the medical criteria would be efficient to identify injured parties?

### Examiner Report
Candidates performed well on this part in general.
