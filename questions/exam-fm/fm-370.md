5000 is borrowed at an annual nominal rate of interest of $j$ convertible monthly. The loan is repaid with payments of 291.23 at the end of each quarter for 5 years.

Calculate $j$.

- A) 5.97%
- B) 6.00%
- C) 6.04%
- D) 6.09%
- E) 6.14%

## Explanation

Define $i'$ as the quarterly effective interest rate for the loan, and solve for $i'$ from the 20 quarterly payments:

$$291.23\,a_{\overline{20}|i'} = 5000$$

which gives (with a financial calculator) $i' = 1.5\%$. Then, solve for $j$ using the formula

$$
\begin{align*}
\left(1 + \frac{j}{12}\right)^{12} &= (1 + 0.015)^{4} \\
1 + \frac{j}{12} &= 1.015^{1/3} \\
                 &= 1.0049752 \\
j &= 12(0.0049752) \\
  &= 0.0597
\end{align*}
$$
