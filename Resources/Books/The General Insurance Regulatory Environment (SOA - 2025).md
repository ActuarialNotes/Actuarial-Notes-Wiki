A Society of Actuaries study note (May 2025) on how general insurance is regulated. It opens from the two main concerns of insurance regulation, the protection of consumers and the financial health of insurers, and goes on to the authority of regulators, licensing, the regulation of insurer activities and solvency and insolvency regulation.
