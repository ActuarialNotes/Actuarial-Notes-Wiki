A CAPSA guideline, published on 9 September 2024, that outlines regulators' views on the responsibilities of capital accumulation plan (CAP) sponsors, administrators, service providers and members, industry best practices in maintaining and administering a CAP, and the information that should be given to CAP members. It updates and replaces the 2004 Guidelines for Capital Accumulation Plans issued by the Joint Forum of Financial Market Regulators.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — RET101-123-26 (the SOA cites it as CAPSA Guideline No. 3: Guideline for Capital Accumulation Plans); Outcomes 1a, 1b; 4a-4n. The SOA course strategy guide says it covers DC and savings plan best practices, particularly the division of responsibilities and the information members need.
