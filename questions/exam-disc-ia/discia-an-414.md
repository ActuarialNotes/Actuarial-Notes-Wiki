A named insured's car, insured under an ISO PAP (PP 00 01 09 18) with other than collision coverage and a \$500 deductible, is stolen and never recovered. Without its modifications the car's actual cash value is \$20,000. It also carried \$4,000 of custom wheels and custom paint, which are "custom equipment" under the form. How much does Part D pay?

- A) \$19,500
- B) \$21,000
- C) \$21,500
- D) \$23,500

## Explanation

Part D excludes custom equipment except for the first \$1,500 of it, and the limit of liability caps custom equipment at \$1,500. The car itself is paid at its actual cash value, and the deductible comes off once:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Paid} &= 20{,}000 + 1{,}500 - 500 \\
&= 21{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**. An excess custom equipment endorsement would have covered the rest.

**Why not the others**

- **A** excludes the custom equipment entirely, missing the \$1,500 the form allows.
- **C** forgets the deductible.
- **D** pays all \$4,000 of custom equipment, ignoring the \$1,500 cap.
