An article from the September/October 2005 issue of HR Advisor (Thomson/West) by Heidi S. Hayden and Diane M. Pfadenhauer. It provides a step-by-step framework for the development of an Early Retirement Incentive Program (ERIP), a program that provides incentives to encourage employees approaching or at retirement age to retire voluntarily earlier than they otherwise might have. It explores the business and legal issues to consider and the steps of putting a program together: the release, eligibility, window, incentives, costs and announcement.
