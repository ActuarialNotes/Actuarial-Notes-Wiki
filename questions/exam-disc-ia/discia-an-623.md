A fire damages a shop insured under the BPP with the replacement cost optional coverage. The owner has not yet decided whether to rebuild and asks how the claim can be settled. Which statement is consistent with the optional coverage?

- A) The insurer must pay full replacement cost now, since depreciation is never deducted
- B) The owner must rebuild within 30 days or lose all coverage for the building
- C) Replacement cost applies to the shop's stock automatically, along with the building
- D) It may take actual cash value now and claim more after rebuilding, with notice in 180 days

## Explanation

The replacement cost option pays nothing on a replacement cost basis until the property is actually repaired or replaced, as soon as reasonably possible. The insured may make its claim on an actual cash value basis and still claim the additional replacement cost coverage later if it notifies the insurer of its intent within 180 days after the loss. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Replacement cost is not paid until the property is repaired or replaced; until then the settlement is at actual cash value.
- **B** — There is no 30-day rebuilding deadline in the option; the requirement is repair or replacement as soon as reasonably possible.
- **C** — The option does not apply to stock unless the declarations show the "Including Stock" option.
