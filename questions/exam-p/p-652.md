An insurance company offers basic and supplemental life coverages for small employee groups. For a group of size three, let $X$ be the number who choose basic life and let $Y$ be the number who choose supplemental life. The company models the probability distribution of $X$ and $Y$ using the joint probability function

$$
p(x, y) = \begin{cases}
\dfrac{x + 2y}{40}, & x = 0, 1, 2, 3, \; y = 0, \ldots, x \\[6pt]
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

Calculate the variance of $X$.

- A) $0.45$
- B) $0.67$
- C) $0.92$
- D) $6.16$
- E) $6.70$

## Explanation

Note the support: $y$ runs only from 0 up to $x$, so the row for a given $x$ has $x + 1$ terms. Summing each row gives the marginal:

$$
p(x) = \begin{cases}
0, & x = 0 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{1}{40} + \dfrac{3}{40} = \dfrac{4}{40}, & x = 1 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{2}{40} + \dfrac{4}{40} + \dfrac{6}{40} = \dfrac{12}{40}, & x = 2 \\[4pt]
\dfrac{3}{40} + \dfrac{5}{40} + \dfrac{7}{40} + \dfrac{9}{40} = \dfrac{24}{40}, & x = 3
\end{cases}
$$

(The $x = 0$ row contributes $p(0,0) = 0$.) Then

$$
\begin{align*}
E(X)   &= 0(0) + \tfrac{4}{40}(1) + \tfrac{12}{40}(2) + \tfrac{24}{40}(3) = 2.5 \\
E(X^2) &= 0(0) + \tfrac{4}{40}(1) + \tfrac{12}{40}(4) + \tfrac{24}{40}(9) = 6.7 \\
\text{Var}(X) &= 6.7 - (2.5)^2 = \mathbf{0.45}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (E), 6.70, is $E(X^2)$ with the subtraction of $\left[E(X)\right]^2$ omitted — and the pair 6.70/0.45 is the whole trap, since the two differ by the full $(2.5)^2$. The support restriction $y \le x$ is doing real work too: only $x + 1$ of the four $y$ values are admissible for each $x$, which is what makes the marginal weights $0, 4, 12, 24$ out of 40 rather than uniform rows.
