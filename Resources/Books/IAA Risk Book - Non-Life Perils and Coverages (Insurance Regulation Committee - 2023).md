An IAA Risk Book chapter (May 2023) describing non-life perils and insurance coverages, developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association. Non-life insurance is all types of insurance except life insurance, annuities and pension obligations; the chapter also covers methods other than insurance for managing the losses associated with non-life perils.
