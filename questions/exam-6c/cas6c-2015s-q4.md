## Part a (1.5 points)

Identify and describe two items that should be included in the design of a Risk Appetite Framework.

### Explanation
Candidates that identified the two items and described two elements for each received full credit:

- **Risk appetite statement**
  - Should reflect the aggregate level of risk
  - Should reflect type of risk that a FRFI is willing to accept in order to achieve its business objectives
  - Should be linked to the firm's short-term and long-term strategic, capital and financial plans
  - Should include qualitative and quantitative measures that can be aggregated and disaggregated
  - Should be forward looking
  - Should be normal and stressed scenarios
  - Should aim to be within the FRFI's risk capacity
- **Risk limit/Risk tolerance** is the allocation of the FRFI's risk appetite statement to
  - Specific risk categories
  - Business unit or platform level
  - Lines of business or product level
  - More granular levels, as appropriate

### Examiner Report
- The most common errors were:
  - Candidates described the risk appetite statement and the risk limit but neglected to identify them by name.
  - Candidates focused only on the risk appetite statement and did not mention risk limit.
  - Candidates mixed up the two items.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Identify two functions of the Chief Risk Officer.

### Explanation
Any two of the following received full credit:

- identifying, measuring, monitoring and reporting on the risks of a FRFI
- provide regular reports to the board, risk committee and senior management
- provide objective view to the risk committee and the board
- meet with risk committee or board on a regular basis
- should have processes and controls in place to assess the accuracy of any risk information or analysis provided by business lines
- Oversight the risk management of the company
- Implement the risk management policy of the company

### Examiner Report
- The most common error was stating that a CRO approves the risk appetite framework.
