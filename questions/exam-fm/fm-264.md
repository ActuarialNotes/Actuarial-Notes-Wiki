A ten-year bond paying annual coupons of $X$ has a face amount of 1000, a price of 450, and an annual effective yield rate of 10%.

A second ten-year bond has the same face amount and annual effective yield rate as the first bond. This second bond pays semi-annual coupons of $\dfrac{X}{2}$. The price of the second bond is $P$.

Calculate $P$.

- A) 439
- B) 442
- C) 452
- D) 457
- E) 461

## Explanation

From Bond 1:

$$
\begin{align*}
450 &= X\,a_{\overline{10}|0.10} + 1{,}000(1.10)^{-10} \\
450 &= 6.14457X + 385.54 \\
X &= \frac{450 - 385.54}{6.14457} \\
  &= 10.49
\end{align*}
$$

For Bond 2, the semiannual coupon is $X/2 = 5.245$. The semiannual yield rate $j$ satisfies $(1+j)^2 = 1.10$, so $j = 1.10^{1/2} - 1 = 0.048809$, and $(1+j)^{-20} = 1.10^{-10} = 0.385543$.

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 5.245\,a_{\overline{20}|0.048809} + 1{,}000(1.048809)^{-20} \\
  &= 5.245(12.5890) + 1{,}000(0.385543) \\
  &= 66.03 + 385.54 \\
  &= 451.57
\end{align*}
$$

SOA's solution rounds $X$ to 10.50 (coupon 5.25), which gives $P = 5.25\,a_{\overline{20}|0.048809} + 385.543 = 451.64$. Either way $P \approx 452$, answer C.
