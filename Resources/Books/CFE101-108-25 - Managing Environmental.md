The PSI ESG Underwriting Guide for Life & Health Insurance (Version 1.0), published in June 2022 by UNEP FI's Principles for Sustainable Insurance Initiative and co-sponsored and co-led by HSBC Life. It is described on its cover as the first ESG guide for the global life & health insurance industry, and it focuses on the underwriting portfolios of life & health insurers.

> - [[Exam CFE-101 (SOA)|Exam CFE-101]] — CFE101-108-25: Managing Environmental, Social and Governance Risks in Life & Health Insurance Business; outcomes 1.3a, 2.1a, 2.2h.
