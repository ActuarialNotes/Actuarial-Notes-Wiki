A paper dated 18 December 2023 in which the Bermuda Monetary Authority shares examples of approaches that regulators could take when confronted with private equity and alternative asset manager-owned and/or supported insurers. It outlines the risks such insurers present, including opacity and complex structures, conflicts of interest, higher proportions of illiquid assets, and misalignment of time horizons, and the supervisory and regulatory tools the Authority applies. It is for educational purposes only and not a promotion of such structures.
