Under the ISO Common Policy Conditions (IL 00 17), in which situation do the named insured's rights and duties under the policy pass to another person without the insurer's written consent?

- A) The named insured, a corporation, merges into another corporation
- B) The named insured sells the building and gives the buyer the policy
- C) The named insured, an individual, dies
- D) The named insured assigns the policy to its bank as loan security

## Explanation

The Common Policy Conditions provide that the named insured's rights and duties may not be transferred without the insurer's written consent, except on the death of an individual named insured, when they pass to the legal representative while acting within the scope of that role. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A**, **B** and **D** — each is a transfer by the named insured's own act, which needs the insurer's written consent. The insurer chose to insure this insured, which is why policies are nontransferable.
