## [F-001] Explanation miscounts the payment dates (250th payment placed in May 2030)

- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: major
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, final paragraph (line 57)
- claim: 'So there are 250 full payments plus a drop. The 250th payment is 250 months after July 31, 2009. Counting forward: 250 months = 20 years and 10 months after July 2009 = May 2030. The drop payment follows in the 251st month = May 31, 2030.'
- evidence: Payment k of the refinanced schedule falls k-1 months after the first payment on July 31, 2009, so the 250th payment is April 30, 2030 (249 months after) and the 251st (the drop) is May 31, 2030 (250 months after). The paragraph puts both the 250th and the 251st payment in May 2030, which contradicts itself, and its stated rule (payment k is k months after July 31, 2009) yields the off-by-one date — exactly distractor B (April 30, 2030) against key C, the distinction this question tests. SOA solution Q133 (solutions PDF p.36, image) counts differently and correctly: 'the final payment will be 251 months from June 30, 2009. This is 20 years and 11 months and so the final payment is May 31, 2030.' Recomputation: pmt 608.9664, balance 75,047.82 (75,048.24 with SOA's rounded payment), n = 250.62 -> 251 payments, last one May 31, 2030. The final date and key C are right.
