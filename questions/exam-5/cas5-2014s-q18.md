An insurance company has the following claims information as of December 31, 2013:

Cumulative Paid Claims ($000s)

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 1,000 | 1,500 | 1,815 |
| 2012 | 1,020 | 1,530 | |
| 2013 | 1,040 | | |

Cumulative Reported Claims ($000s)

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 1,100 | 1,650 | 1,815 |
| 2012 | 1,220 | 1,830 | |
| 2013 | 1,340 | | |

Case Outstanding Claims ($000s)

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 100 | 150 | 0 |
| 2012 | 200 | 300 | |
| 2013 | 300 | | |

Cumulative Open Claim Counts

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 1,000 | 1,100 | 1,155 |
| 2012 | 1,000 | 1,100 | |
| 2013 | 1,000 | | |

Cumulative Closed Claim Counts

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 900 | 1,080 | 1,155 |
| 2012 | 900 | 1,080 | |
| 2013 | 900 | | |

- No development occurs beyond 36 months.
- There are no partial payments.

Estimate ultimate claims for accident year 2013 using two reserving techniques that are consistent with a diagnostic review of the data.

## Explanation

**Diagnostic review.**

Average paid claim (cumulative paid ÷ cumulative closed counts):

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 1,111 | 1,389 | 1,571 |
| 2012 | 1,133 | 1,417 | |
| 2013 | 1,156 | | |

The year-to-year change down each column is about 2% — stable.

Ratio of closed to total claim counts:

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 0.474 | 0.485 | 0.5 |
| 2012 | 0.474 | 0.485 | |
| 2013 | 0.474 | | |

Consistent — the settlement rate is not changing.

Average case outstanding (case outstanding ÷ open counts):

| Accident Year | 12 Months | 24 Months | 36 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 0.1 | 0.136 | 0 |
| 2012 | 0.2 | 0.272 | |
| 2013 | 0.3 | | |

Very large year-to-year changes — above-normal inflation in case outstanding. Reported data is therefore distorted, but claim settlement and payments are stable, so the paid development technique works, and the claim counts are stable down each column, so a frequency-severity technique on developed counts and developed average paid claims also works.

**Technique 1 — paid claim development.**

Paid age-to-age factors (weighted average):

| Accident Year | 12-24 | 24-36 |
|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 1.5 | 1.21 |
| 2012 | 1.5 | |

Selected age-to-age: 1.5 and 1.21; age-to-ultimate at 12 months: $1.5 \times 1.21 = 1.815$.

$$\text{Ultimate} = 1{,}040 \times 1.815 = \$1{,}887{,}600$$

**Technique 2 — frequency-severity.**

Closed claim counts:

| Accident Year | 12 | 24 | 36 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 900 | 1,080 | 1,155 |
| 2012 | 900 | 1,080 | |
| 2013 | 900 | | |

Selected age-to-age (straight average): 1.2, 1.069, 1; age-to-ultimate at 12 months: 1.283.

$$\text{Ultimate counts} = 900 \times 1.283 = 1{,}155$$

Average paid age-to-age factors:

| Accident Year | 12-24 | 24-36 |
|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 1.25 | 1.131 |
| 2012 | 1.251 | |

Selected age-to-age (straight average): 1.25, 1.131, 1; age-to-ultimate at 12 months: 1.414.

$$\text{Ultimate severity} = 1.156 \times 1.414 = 1.635$$

$$\text{Ultimate claims} = 1{,}155 \times 1.635 = \$1{,}888{,}425$$
