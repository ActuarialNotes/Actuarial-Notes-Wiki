A CIA educational note that provides guidance related to the completion of a market consistent valuation for insurance contracts with financial guarantees within the scope of IFRS 17. Prepared by the Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting and dated June 30, 2022 (document 222073), it discusses stochastic modelling, how stochastic scenarios can be set to replicate market observable prices, adjustments to market prices, and items specific to segregated funds.
