An interview with Professor Ivo Vlaev of Warwick University, published by the BearingPoint Institute in 2016 (document BEI007-16-IVO). He makes the case for a behavioural approach to corporate strategic decision-making that joins a real-world understanding of complex human behaviours with data-driven modelling, and introduces MINDSPACE, a checklist drawn from behavioural economics that businesses can use to influence behaviours.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-311 (SOA)|Exam CP-311]] — CP311-112-26: How Behaviors Impact Your Corporate Decision-Making; outcomes 4a and 4b.

## In 30 Seconds
- Professor Ivo Vlaev makes the case for a behavioural approach to corporate strategic decision-making
- Strategic decisions make or break companies, yet most business leaders would admit that the quality of their strategic decisions is far from faultless
- A behavioural approach joins a real-world understanding of complex human behaviours with data-driven modelling
- MINDSPACE is an easy, practical checklist, drawn from behavioural economics, that businesses can use to influence behaviours

## Interview

## Dual Process Model of Behaviour: System 1 & System 2 Thinking
- System 1 is the mind's automatic, fast, intuitive approach to thinking
- System 2 is the mind's slower, conscious, more deliberate mode of thinking

## MINDSPACE Checklist for Decision Biases
- Messenger
- Incentives
- Norms
- Defaults
- Salience
- Priming
- Affect
- Commitments
- Ego
