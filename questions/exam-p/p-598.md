The profit $X$ on an insurance policy is normally distributed with $E(X^2) = 74$ and $P[X > 0] = 0.92$.

Calculate the variance of the profit on this policy.

- A) $24.88$
- B) $30.77$
- C) $34.50$
- D) $44.20$
- E) $62.86$

## Explanation

The second moment relates the two unknowns:

$$
E(X^2) = \mu^2 + \sigma^2 = 74
$$

The tail probability gives a second relation:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 0] &= P\!\left[Z > \frac{0 - \mu}{\sigma}\right] = 0.92 \\
\frac{0 - \mu}{\sigma} &= -1.405072 \\
\mu &= 1.405072\,\sigma
\end{align*}
$$

Substituting into the first equation:

$$
\begin{align*}
(1.405072\,\sigma)^2 + \sigma^2 &= 74 \\
2.974227\,\sigma^2 &= 74 \\
\sigma^2 &= \mathbf{24.88}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (E), 62.86, comes from writing the second moment as $\mu + \sigma^2 = 74$ — forgetting to square the mean. That mistake turns the equation into the quadratic $\sigma^2 + 1.405072\,\sigma - 74 = 0$, whose root gives $\sigma = 7.93$ and $\sigma^2 = 62.86$.
