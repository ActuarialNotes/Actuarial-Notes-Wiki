An **Associate of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries (ACIA)** is an actuary who has met the associate requirements of the [[Canadian Institute of Actuaries (CIA)]]. Since June 2021 the CIA has set its own modules and exam for it, and it also recognises associate-level credentials from other bodies. An Associate who completes an FCIA specialty track becomes a [[Fellow of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries (FCIA)|Fellow]].

## Coming from another body

Pathway 2 is for candidates with full or partial associate-level qualifications from another actuarial organization:

- an associate designation of the ASSA, CAS, IAAust, IFoA, SAI or SOA;
- CAS credit for the VEEs, Exams 1, 2, MAS-I, MAS-II and the DISCs;
- SOA credit for the VEEs, P, FM, FAM, ALTAM or ASTAM and SRM;
- or full qualification from another IAA member organization.

These candidates take ACIA Module 1 where it isn't already covered, ACIA Module 2, and the CIA Professionalism Workshop. Fully qualified actuaries also take Module 1 and the Capstone Exam.

A holder of a recognised fellow-level designation (Pathway 3) may also take the ACIA, but goes to the FCIA directly. The [[Credential Path|three societies' routes]] show which credentials count toward which.
