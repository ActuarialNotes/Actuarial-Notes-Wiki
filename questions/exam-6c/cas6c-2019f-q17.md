The following information is available for a federally regulated property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2018.

Exceedance probability curves:

| Percentile | West Canada | East Canada |
|---|---|---|
| 0.995 | 30,000 | 12,000 |
| 0.996 | 75,000 | 25,000 |
| 0.998 | 350,000 | 100,000 |
| 0.999 | 600,000 | 200,000 |

Reinsurance program:

| Loss Layer | % Ceded |
|---|---|
| 100,000 xs 50,000 | 50% |
| 150,000 xs 150,000 | 100% |

The following information is also available:

- The insurance company is phasing-in to the countrywide PML 500 requirement.
- No capital market financing is used by the company.
- The total capital & surplus of the company is 400,000.
- The company will use the maximum allowed of its capital & surplus to determine the earthquake reserves.
- The Earthquake Premium Reserve (EPR) is 25,000.

## Part a (2.5 points)

Calculate the earthquake reserves as at December 31, 2018.

### Explanation
East Canada PML 500 < West Canada PML 500 and East Canada PML 250 < West Canada PML 250, so East Canada PML 420 < West Canada PML 420.

$$\text{West Canada PML 420} = 0.68 \times \text{West Canada PML 500} + 0.32 \times \text{West Canada PML 250} = 0.68 \times 350{,}000 + 0.32 \times 75{,}000 = 262{,}000$$

$$\text{Countrywide PML 500} = \left(\text{East Canada PML 500}^{1.5} + \text{West Canada PML 500}^{1.5}\right)^{1/1.5} = \left(100{,}000^{1.5} + 350{,}000^{1.5}\right)^{1/1.5} = 384{,}784$$

Phasing in to the countrywide PML 500:

$$\text{Countrywide PML 2018} = \text{Countrywide PML 500} \times \frac{2018 - 2014}{8} + \max(\text{East Canada PML 420},\ \text{West Canada PML 420}) \times \frac{2022 - 2018}{8}$$
$$= 384{,}784 \times \tfrac{4}{8} + 262{,}000 \times \tfrac{4}{8} = 323{,}392$$

Financial resources $=$ capital & surplus allocated to EQ $+$ EPR $+$ reinsurance coverage $+$ capital market financing:

$$400{,}000 \times 10\% + 25{,}000 + (100{,}000 \times 50\% + 150{,}000) + 0 = 265{,}000$$

$$\text{ERC} = \text{Countrywide PML 2018} - \text{Financial resources} = 323{,}392 - 265{,}000 = 58{,}392$$

$$\text{Earthquake Reserves} = (\text{ERC} + \text{EPR}) \times 1.25 = (58{,}392 + 25{,}000) \times 1.25 = 104{,}240$$

Alternatively, compute the East Canada PML 420 rather than compare the two regions: $0.68 \times 100{,}000 + 0.32 \times 25{,}000 = 76{,}000$, less than West Canada's 262,000, and the rest follows as above.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to calculate the earthquake reserves.
Common errors included:

- Not adding the EPR into the financial resources
- Misestimating the PML 500 and PML 250 part of the PML 420 calculation

## Part b (0.5 points)

Identify two elements that earthquake exposure risk management policies and procedures should document.

### Explanation
- Nature and adequacy of financial resources
- Identification and assessment of PML factors

Alternatively:

- The risk appetite and the risk tolerance of the company
- The data management framework

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to identify two elements that the earthquake exposure risk management policies and procedures should document.
A common error included:

- Describing only one element
