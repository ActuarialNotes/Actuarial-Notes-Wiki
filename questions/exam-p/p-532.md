In a certain region, 5% of all banks will fail in the next five years. The FDIC insures 80% of the banks in the region. Only 3% of the banks in the region insured by the FDIC will fail in the next five years.

Calculate the probability that a bank in the region that fails in the next five years is insured by the FDIC.

- A) $0.02$
- B) $0.04$
- C) $0.06$
- D) $0.48$
- E) $0.60$

## Explanation

Let $F$ be failure and $D$ be FDIC insurance. The given quantities are

$$
P[F] = 0.05, \qquad P[D] = 0.80, \qquad P[F \mid D] = 0.03
$$

The conditional probability gives the joint one:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[F \cap D] &= P[F \mid D]P[D] \\
            &= (0.03)(0.80) = 0.024
\end{align*}
$$

Reversing the conditioning is then a single division:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[D \mid F] &= \frac{P[F \cap D]}{P[F]} \\
            &= \frac{0.024}{0.05} \\
            &= \mathbf{0.48}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (A), 0.02, is the joint probability 0.024 rounded, reported instead of the conditional one. The answer falls below the prior $P[D] = 0.80$ because FDIC-insured banks fail at a *lower* rate (3%) than banks overall (5%) — so among failures, the insured are underrepresented.
