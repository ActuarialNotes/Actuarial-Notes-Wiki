A paper by Robert W. Klein, in The Geneva Papers (2012, vol. 37, pp. 175–199), examining the economic principles that should govern insurance regulation. It argues that the rationale for regulation is the remediation of market failures — in insurance, chiefly asymmetric information problems and principal-agent conflicts — and uses these principles to assess current solvency, price and market conduct regulation in the United States and the EU and potential reforms.
