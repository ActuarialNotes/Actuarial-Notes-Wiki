You are given the following information about a perpetuity with annual payments:

i) The first 15 payments are each 2500, with the first payment to be made three years from now.

ii) Beginning with the 16th payment, each payment is $k$% larger than the previous payment.

iii) Using an annual effective interest rate of 3.5%, the present value of the perpetuity is 115,000.

Calculate $k$.

- A) 1.66
- B) 1.74
- C) 1.78
- D) 1.83
- E) 1.89

## Explanation

Write the growth rate as a decimal, $g = k/100$. The first payment is at time 3, so the 15 level payments fall at times 3 through 17 and the growing payments begin with the 16th payment, at time 18.

**The growing payments.** The 16th payment is $2500(1+g)$, the 17th $2500(1+g)^2$, and so on. Their value at time 17 is a geometric series:
$$
\begin{align*}
2500\left(\frac{1+g}{1.035} + \frac{(1+g)^2}{1.035^2} + \cdots\right) &= 2500\,\frac{\dfrac{1+g}{1.035}}{1 - \dfrac{1+g}{1.035}} \\
&= 2500\,\frac{1+g}{0.035-g}
\end{align*}
$$

**The level payments.** The 15 payments of 2500 are an annuity-immediate deferred 2 years:
$$2500\,v^2 a_{\overline{15}|0.035} = 2500(0.933511)(11.517411) = 2500(10.751626) = 26{,}879.07$$

**The equation of value.** With $v^{17} = 1.035^{-17} = 0.55720$:
$$115{,}000 = 2500\,v^2 a_{\overline{15}|0.035} + 2500\,v^{17}\,\frac{1+g}{0.035-g}$$

Divide by 2500 and multiply through by $0.035 - g$:
$$
\begin{align*}
46 &= 10.7516 + 0.55720\,\frac{1+g}{0.035-g} \\
46(0.035-g) &= 10.7516(0.035-g) + 0.55720(1+g) \\
1.61 - 46g &= 0.37631 - 10.7516g + 0.55720 + 0.55720g
\end{align*}
$$

Solve for $g$:
$$g = \frac{1.61 - 0.37631 - 0.55720}{46 - 10.7516 + 0.55720} = \frac{0.67649}{35.8056} = 0.01889$$

So each payment from the 16th on is 1.889% larger than the one before: $k = 1.89$.
