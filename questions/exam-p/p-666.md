Engineering specifications require that the probability a ball bearing produced at a manufacturing plant has diameter between 10.5 and 11.5 is at least 0.8. The diameters of these ball bearings are normally distributed with mean 11 and standard deviation $\sigma$.

Calculate the largest value of $\sigma$ for which the specification is met.

- A) $0.15$
- B) $0.39$
- C) $0.59$
- D) $1.60$
- E) $2.56$

## Explanation

Work at the boundary, where the probability is exactly 0.80:

$$
P[10.5 < X < 11.5] = 0.80
$$

The interval is centred on the mean of 11, so by symmetry the 0.20 left over splits into two tails of 0.10 each. That puts 11.5 at the **90th** percentile, not the 80th:

$$
P[X < 11.5] = 0.90
$$

Standardizing,

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{11.5 - 11}{\sigma} &= 1.28155 \\
\sigma &= \frac{0.5}{1.28155} = \mathbf{0.3902}
\end{align*}
$$

A larger $\sigma$ spreads the diameters further and pushes the probability below 0.80, so this boundary value is the largest one that meets the specification.

Choice (C), 0.59, uses $z = 0.84162$ — the 80th percentile — instead of the 90th, giving $0.5/0.84162 = 0.594$. That is the mistake of matching the $z$ to the 0.80 in the problem statement rather than to the tail area the symmetric interval actually leaves.
