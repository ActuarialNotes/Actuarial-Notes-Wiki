## Part a (0.25 points)

Briefly discuss the meaning of the term "risk capital."

### Explanation
Risk capital is the capital provided by equity holder to the company to cover the risk that liability may exceed the funds provided by policyholder in forms of loss reserves and UPR.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know the definition of risk capital.
A common error was giving the definition of other types of capital, such as economic capital

## Part b (2.25 points)

Determine which of the following results in a larger allocation of capital to Line B:

- i. Allocating the market value of equity using the Proportional Allocation based on 99.5% VaR Method, or
- ii. Allocating the risk capital using the Co-Measures Approach based on 99.5% Co-CTE.

### Explanation
**i. Proportional allocation based on 99.5% VaR.** The 99.5% VaR is the 6th worst observation of 1,000 (scenario 995) in each sorted table:

- Reserve VaR 99.5% = 850
- Line A VaR 99.5% = 1,500
- Line B VaR 99.5% = 900

$$\text{Line B allocation} = \frac{900}{850 + 1500 + 900} \times 2900 = 803.077$$

**ii. Co-measures based on 99.5% Co-CTE.** Average the 5 worst observations, where worst is based on the overall (total) distribution:

$$\text{Reserve Co-CTE} = \frac{800 + 500 + 500 + 100 + 300}{5} = 480$$

(as printed; the table's second-worst reserve value is 700)

$$\text{Line A Co-CTE} = \frac{1600 + 1500 + 1300 + 1400 + 1300}{5} = 1420$$

$$\text{Line B Co-CTE} = \frac{600 + 700 + 900 + 1100 + 900}{5} = 840$$

Line B allocation = 840, since the 99.5% CTE is used as the risk capital (instead of allocating the market value of equity).

Line B's allocation is higher under the Co-CTE approach in ii.

Alternatively:

Compare the shares each method gives Line B. Under i, Line B's share is $900/(900 + 1500 + 850) = 27.7\%$; under ii it is $840/2740 = 30\%$, where 2740 is the Co-CTE total ($480 + 1420 + 840$). Applied to the market value of equity, i gives $(900/3250) \times 2900 = 803.077$ and ii gives $(840/2740) \times 2900 = 889.051$. So ii allocates more to Line B.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to use the VaR and Co-CTE methods to find the correct allocated capital.
Common errors include:

- Using the wrong capital bases
- Selecting the wrong observations used for the methods

## Part c (0.5 points)

Explain one implication of allocating more capital to a line of business.

### Explanation
When measuring profitability with measures like RAROC, the line with more capital must generate more income to meet the target return.

Alternatively:

Allocating more capital will depress the line's RAROC, which could influence decision-making (downsize, exit the line) if it is below the cost of capital.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know implications of allocating more capital to a given line. There were no common errors to this part as the question was straight from the text and several approaches were accepted.
