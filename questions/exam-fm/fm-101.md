Cash flows are 40,000 at time 2 (in years), 25,000 at time 3, and 100,000 at time 4. The annual effective yield rate is 7.0%.

Calculate the Macaulay duration.

- A) 2.2
- B) 2.3
- C) 3.1
- D) 3.3
- E) 3.4

## Explanation

With $v = 1/1.07$:

$$
\begin{align*}
D &= \frac{2(40{,}000)v^2 + 3(25{,}000)v^3 + 4(100{,}000)v^4}{40{,}000v^2 + 25{,}000v^3 + 100{,}000v^4} \\
  &= 3.314
\end{align*}
$$
