![[Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management (Hardy - 2022) - Cover.svg]]

An introduction to enterprise risk management (ERM) that integrates quantitative and qualitative approaches and motivates key mathematical and statistical methods with real-world cases, both successes and failures. Its narrative runs from risk governance, through risk identification, risk modelling and risk mitigation, to the holistic topics of regulation, behavioural biases and crisis management. Worked examples and end-of-chapter exercises support the reader.

> - [[Exam CFE-101 (SOA)|Exam CFE-101]] — Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022: Ch. 13 Liquidity Risk (1.3a, 2.1a, 2.2h, 3.1a); Ch. 2 Risk Taxonomy (1.2c, 2.1a); Ch. 14 Model Risk and Governance (2.1a, 2.2f, 2.2g); Ch. 3 Risk Measures (2.2b, 3.3c); Ch. 10 Economic Scenario Generators (2.2g); Ch. 15 Risk Mitigation Using Options and Derivatives (3.2a, 3.2c); Ch. 18 Risk-Adjusted Measures of Profit and Capital Allocation, excluding section 18.6 (3.3b-3.3e), where the candidate is not responsible for demonstrating whether or how the fair allocation criteria are met for any of the capital allocation methods in section 18.4.
