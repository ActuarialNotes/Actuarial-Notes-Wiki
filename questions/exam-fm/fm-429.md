A bond with an annual coupon rate of 9% paid annually matures in ten years. The annual effective yield rate is 10%.

Calculate the modified duration of this bond.

- A) 6.27
- B) 6.32
- C) 6.47
- D) 6.89
- E) 7.58

## Explanation

Per 1 of face amount, the bond pays coupons of 0.09 at times 1 to 10 and 1 at time 10. Its Macaulay duration at 10% is the present-value-weighted average of the payment times:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC} &= \frac{0.09(Ia)_{\overline{10}|0.10} + 10v^{10}}{0.09\,a_{\overline{10}|0.10} + v^{10}} \\
        &= \frac{2.61323 + 3.85543}{0.55301 + 0.38554} \\
        &= \frac{6.46866}{0.93855} \\
        &= 6.8922
\end{align*}
$$

The modified duration is the Macaulay duration divided by $1+i$:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD} &= \frac{6.8922}{1.10} \\
        &= 6.2656
\end{align*}
$$
