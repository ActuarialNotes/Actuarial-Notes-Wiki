At year end an insurer estimates \$1,200,000 of earned but unbilled (EBUB) audit premium on its workers compensation policies. It holds \$400,000 of collateral specifically identifiable to those policies. Under SSAP No. 53, how much of the EBUB asset is nonadmitted?

- A) \$80,000
- B) \$120,000
- C) \$400,000
- D) \$800,000

## Explanation

Ten percent of EBUB in excess of collateral specifically held and identifiable on a per-policy basis is nonadmitted.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Nonadmitted} &= 0.10 \times (1{,}200{,}000 - 400{,}000) \\
&= 80{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **A**. The insurer must also accrue the commissions and premium taxes on the estimated premium, based on when it is earned.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — \$120,000 takes 10% of the whole estimate, ignoring the collateral.
- **C** — \$400,000 is the collateral, which reduces rather than measures the nonadmitted amount.
- **D** — \$800,000 is the whole amount above the collateral; only 10% of it is nonadmitted.
