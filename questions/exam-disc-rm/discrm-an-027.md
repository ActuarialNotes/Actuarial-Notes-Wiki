A company's risk identification team consists of the risk manager, the chief financial officer and a finance analyst who works from the same reports as the CFO. The risk manager can add one more member. Which addition would most improve the team's chance of finding exposures it would otherwise miss?

- A) A second finance analyst, to double-check the financial statements
- B) The plant's maintenance supervisor, who knows where equipment fails
- C) An outside auditor who reviews the same accounting records
- D) A senior executive whose views the team tends to follow

## Explanation

A team finds more only when its members see different parts of the organization. The maintenance supervisor brings knowledge of operations and equipment failures that no financial document shows. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A second analyst using the same reports shares the team's blind spots, so adds a person without adding a perspective.
- **C** — An auditor of the same accounting records again repeats the financial view.
- **D** — A dominant senior voice can narrow the list through deference and groupthink, one of the problems a facilitator must guard against.
