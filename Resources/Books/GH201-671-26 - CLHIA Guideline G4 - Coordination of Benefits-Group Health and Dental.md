A CLHIA guideline developed to foster consistency within the industry where a covered individual can submit a group health or group dental claim to more than one plan. It promotes consistency in determining the priority in which payments are made and outlines the minimum amount payable by each group plan, describing the order in which benefits are determined and how health or dental payments are coordinated. It applies to insured and uninsured plans, and the combined payment from all group plans for a particular item cannot exceed 100% of the eligible medical or dental expense. The copy read carries a 2016 copyright.

## 1 Introduction
- The Coordination of Benefits (COB) Guideline was developed to help foster consistency within the industry in situations where a covered individual can submit a group health or group dental claim to more than one plan.

## 2 Purpose
- It describes the order in which benefits are determined and how to coordinate health or dental payments from group plans available to the covered individual.
- The combined payment from all group plans for a particular item cannot exceed 100% of the eligible medical expense or eligible dental expense, and in some cases may be less than the submitted amount.

## 3 Scope
- It applies to both insured and uninsured plans that provide group health and dental benefits, and does not address out-of-country/out-of-province/territory coordination of benefits.
- Where the guideline conflicts with any applicable law, the law takes precedence to the extent of the conflict.
