- claim: Cummins says a $100 billion loss is well under 1% of the value of U.S. stocks and bonds; the SPV is bankruptcy-remote; investors gain exposures cut to the risk level they want; sum of class cash flows = pool cash flow - fees.

'Bankruptcy-remote', 'cut to the risk level they want' and the cash-flow identity appear in no source read; they belong to Coval, Jurek & Stafford (C6), which is a separate packet. Confirmed: pass-through securitization before tranching and Fannie/Freddie's retained credit risk (Exam 9 S2014 Q13 report pp.73-74), originator incentives and correlation assumptions and mortgage prepayment risk (Exam 9 S2015 Q14 pp.63-69), 5% risk retention (15 U.S.C. 78o-11(c)(1)(B)(i)), full collateralisation and multi-year price lock (GAO; Exam 9 S2017 Q15), complements reinsurance (Cummins abstract).
