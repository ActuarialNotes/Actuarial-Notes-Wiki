A research review, dated February 2017, that sets out definitions of systemic risk and applies them to the insurance industry. Written by Max J. Rudolph for the Society of Actuaries, it covers the drivers of systemic risk, historical insurer insolvencies, what could get an insurer into trouble, whether current resolution methods would work in a crisis, and how insurers differ from banks. It concludes that insurers are receivers of systemic risk rather than primary drivers of it.
