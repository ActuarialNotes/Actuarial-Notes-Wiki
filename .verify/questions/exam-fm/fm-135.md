- author: agent:validate-v1

- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, line 49
- claim: 'Since the coupon rate (3.5%) exceeds the yield (2.5%), the bond sells at a premium, making early call worst-case.'
- evidence: With redemption values of 1250 and 1125 (not par), whether the earliest call in a period is the worst case depends on Fr/C vs j, not Fr/F vs j. SOA solution Q135 (solutions PDF p.37, image): 'During the first redemption period the modified coupon rate is 1000(0.035)/1250 = 2.80% which is larger than the desired yield rate ... Key C and all three prices are unaffected.
