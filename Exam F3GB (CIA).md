# Exam F3GB
**F3GB – Group Benefits Reserving** is an exam of the group benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques for estimating reserves and claims-related expenses that are relevant to group insurance.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of group insurance.
>
> 3. Evaluate the considerations involved in selecting a best-estimate reserve.
>
> 4. Understand how reserves link to wider business processes (e.g., business planning, pricing, financial reporting, and capital setting).
>
> 5. Describe reporting requirements and calculate actuarial values for financial reporting.
>
> 6. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in the [[Valuation|valuation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]] and [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]] for [[Group Insurer|group insurers]]. (A1).

> [!example]- Reserving and claim liabilities {35%}
>
> 1. Outline the various uses of reserving ([[Life Insurance Company Financial Reporting|life insurance company financial reporting]], reporting to client on refund accounting, pricing, etc.) and evaluate their impact on the selection of methods and assumptions. (B5)
> 2. Apply the [[Canadian Asset Liability]] Method (CALM). (C3)
> 3. Evaluate the impact of [[IFRS 17]] on [[Group Insurance Reserving|group insurance reserving]]. (C5)
> 4. Apply the applicable [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|standards of practice]] (SOP). (C3)
> 5. Identify the applicable [[Educational Note|educational notes]]. (A2)
> 6. Understand, calculate, and evaluate provisions for [[Adverse Deviation|adverse deviations]] (PfADs). (C5)
> 7. Compare and contrast aggregate and seriatim approaches. ([[B5]])
> 8. Describe current tables/studies used in [[Valuation|valuation]] ([[CIA Experience|CIA experience]] analyses, [[Group Long-Term Disability|group long-term disability]] (GLTD), Krieger, [[SOA Table|SOA tables]], etc.). (B2)
> 9. Perform calculations for waiver of [[Insurance Premium|premiums]] (WP). (C3)
> 10. Perform calculations for LTD ([[Disabled Reserve|disabled reserves]]). (C3)
> 11. Perform calculations for non-traditional approaches (WP and [[LTD Reserve|LTD reserves]] based on [[Medical Prognosis|medical prognosis]], etc.). (C3)
> 12. Perform calculations for incurred but not reported ([[IBNR]]) [[Reserve|reserves]] (including the [[Chain Ladder Method|chain-ladder method]]). (C3)
> 13. Perform analysis on different reserving methods. ([[C4]])
> 14. Evaluate stochastic approaches to calculation of [[Group Insurance Reserve|group insurance reserves]]. (C5)
> 15. Evaluate [[Stop-Loss Reserves Reflecting Timing Mismatch|stop-loss reserves reflecting timing mismatch]] between [[Insurance Premium|premium]] and [[Claim|claims]]. (C5)
> 16. Explain the limitations and biases of the [[Claim Reserve|traditional valuation]] methods. (C2)
> 17. Evaluate [[Data Quality|data resources]] and appropriateness for calculating [[Reserve|reserves]]. (C5)
> 18. Describe the role and responsibilities of the [[Appointed-valuation Actuary|appointed/valuation actuary]]. (B2)

> [!example]- Benefit taxation {15%}
>
> 1. Describe the following [[Benefit Taxation Feature|benefit taxation features]] and perform related calculations: (C4)
>    - a. [[Income Tax|Income tax]] – federal (including relevant folios and [[Interpretation Bulletin|interpretation bulletins]]).
>    - b. [[Income Tax|Income tax]] – provincial (including differences with [[Federal Income Tax|federal income tax]]).
>    - c. [[Premium Sales Tax|Premium sales taxes]] and similar taxes.
>    - d. Other [[Premium Tax|premium taxes]].
> 2. Describe and perform calculations to evaluate the impact of taxation on: (C5)
>    - a. [[Claim|Claims]].
>    - b. [[Plan Design|Plan design]], including [[Flexible Benefits Plan|flexible benefits plans]].
>    - c. [[Plan Funding|Plan funding]] ([[Cross-Experience Rating|cross-experience rating]], [[Cost Sharing|cost sharing]], etc.).
> 3. Describe and perform calculations relative to [[Alternate Benefit Funding Structure|alternate benefit funding structures]] such as (C3):
>    - a. [[Administrative Service|Administrative services]] only (ASO).
>    - b. [[Employee Life|Employee life]] and [[Health Trust|health trusts]].

> [!example]- Employee benefits accounting {15%}
>
> 1. Compare [[Post-Retirement Benefit|post-retirement benefits]] vs. benefits while not actively at work. (B4)
> 2. Evaluate actuarial aspects of accounting for [[Employee Future Benefit|employee future benefits]]: (D5)
>    - a. Canadian accounting recommendations under IAS 19.
>    - b. CPA section 3462–3463 ([[Private Sector|private sector]] and not-for-profit).
>    - c. CPA 3250, and CPA 3255 ([[Public Sector|public sector]]) [[Plus non-Canadian|plus non-Canadian]] accounting recommendations (Accounting [[Standards Codification]] [ASC]) 715/712 – U.S. regarding [[Employee Future Benefit|employee future benefits]].
> 3. Calculate actuarial liabilities and other accounting items related to [[Employee Future Benefit|employee future benefits]]. (C3)
> 4. Apply [[CIA Practice-Specific Standard|CIA practice-specific standards]] to [[Employee Benefit|employee benefits]]. (C3)
> 5. Analyze the following issues related to [[Retiree Group Benefits|post-employment benefits]]: (D4)
>    - a. Why [[Employers Offer Post-Retirement|employers offer post-retirement]] and [[Retiree Group Benefits|post-employment benefits]].
>    - b. [[Cost Inflation|Cost inflation]].
>    - c. Selection.
>    - d. Reasonability of assumptions.

> [!example]- Insurance company financial reporting {10%}
>
> 1. Generate a basic [[Financial Statement|financial statement]] and its components, including the impact of [[IFRS]] 17. (C6)
> 2. Describe the following concepts and perform relevant calculations: (C3)
>    - a. Canada [[NAIC Annual Statement|Annual Statement]]: statements, key exhibits, and schedules.
>    - b. [[Life Insurance Company Taxation|Taxable income]].
>    - c. Fair-value accounting principles.
>    - d. International [[Accounting Standards|accounting standards]].
>    - e. Actuarial reviews of [[Reserve|reserves]].
> 3. Apply the [[IFRS 17]] general method approach, [[Variable Fee|variable fee]] approach, and [[Premium Allocation Approach|premium allocation approach]]. (C3)

> [!example]- Financial and capital management {10%}
>
> 1. Differentiate surplus management and earnings management ([[D4]]).
> 2. Describe creation of value from a [[Financial Economics|financial economics]] perspective (C4).
> 3. Relative to [[Economic Capital|economic capital]],
>    - a. analyze the significant [[Risk Component|risk components]]; (C4)
>    - b. select calculation methods appropriate to [[Stakeholders' Perspective|stakeholders’ perspectives]]; and (D3)
>    - c. describe how a company would implement an [[Economic Cap|economic cap]].(C5)
> 4. Evaluate the use of [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]] as a [[Capital|capital]] management technique. (D5)

> [!example]- Asset and liability management {15%}
>
> 1. Compare and contrast asset models. ([[D5]])
> 2. Compare and contrast [[Asset Allocation|asset allocation]] across the various lines of business of an [[Insurer|insurer]]. (D5)
> 3. Outline the selection process of assets for [[Backing Group Insurance Liability|backing group insurance liabilities]]. (C3)
> 4. Describe and apply the relationship between [[Interest Rate Guarantee|interest rate guarantees]] on [[Refund Arrangement|refund arrangements]] and selection of assets. (C3)
> 5. Compare and contrast the relationship between [[Asset-Liability Management|asset liability management]] ([[Asset-Liability Management|ALM]]) at the [[Corporate Level|corporate level]] and [[Asset-Liability Management|ALM]] at the [[Line-Of-Business Level|line-of-business level]]. (C4)
> 6. Analyze liability models related to [[Group Insurance|group insurance]]. (C4)
> 7. Interpret and develop appropriate presentation of results. ([[C6]])
> 8. Evaluate the impacts of [[Interest-Crediting Rate|interest-crediting rates]] on pricing for [[Embedded Value|new business]]. (C5)
> 9. Evaluate the impacts of [[Interest-Crediting Rate|interest-crediting rates]] on [[Valuation|valuation]] of liabilities. (C5)
> 10. Assess [[Risk Margin|risk margins]] related to the [[Rate of Return|rate of return]] of assets. (C5)
