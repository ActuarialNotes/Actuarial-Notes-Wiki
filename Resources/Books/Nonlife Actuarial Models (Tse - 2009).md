The [[Credibility Theory|credibility]] text on the [[Exam MAS-II (CAS)|MAS-II]] syllabus, supplying **Part III** for objectives A1–A2. The same book supplies loss-model material to [[Exam MAS-I (CAS)|MAS-I]] section B.

The book is organized in four parts — **I. Loss models**, **II. Risk and ruin**, **III. Credibility**, and **IV. Model construction and evaluation**. Only Part III is on the MAS-II syllabus.

## Part III — Credibility

### 6 Classical Credibility

- The [[Limited Fluctuation Credibility|limited fluctuation]] framework: choosing a probability level $p$ and a range parameter $k$
- The [[Full Credibility Standard|full credibility standard]] for [[Frequency|frequency]], [[Severity|severity]] and [[Aggregate Loss Model|aggregate loss]] — including the 1,082-claim benchmark and the $1 + \mathrm{CV}^2$ adjustment
- [[Partial Credibility]] and the square-root rule $Z = \sqrt{n/n_F}$
- Choosing the [[Complement of Credibility|complement of credibility]]

### 7 Bühlmann Credibility

- The greatest-accuracy (least-squares) framework and why it minimizes mean squared error rather than bounding a probability
- The structural parameters: $\mu$, the [[Expected Value of Process Variance]] $v$, and the [[Variance of Hypothetical Means]] $a$
- [[Bühlmann Credibility|Bühlmann]] $Z = n/(n+k)$ with $k = v/a$
- [[Bühlmann-Straub Credibility|Bühlmann-Straub]] for unequal exposures across periods

### 8 The Bayesian Approach

- Prior, posterior and [[Predictive Distribution|predictive]] distributions
- [[Conjugate Prior|Conjugate priors]]: Gamma-Poisson, Beta-Bernoulli, Normal-Normal
- [[Bayesian Credibility]] as the posterior mean, and its exact agreement with Bühlmann under a conjugate pair
- Loss functions and Bayes estimates

### 9 Empirical Implementation of Credibility

- [[Empirical Bayes Credibility|Non-parametric estimation]] of $\mu$, $v$ and $a$ from the data
- Semi-parametric estimation using a distributional assumption for the conditional model
- The unbiased correction to $\hat a$ and what to do when it comes out negative
