- A) AY 2023's is double — IBNR scales with reported losses
- B) AY 2023's is half — the more that has reported, the less remains to report
- C) They are identical — the IBNR term depends only on expected losses and the CDF
- D) AY 2023's is between equal and double — the method blends actual with expected
