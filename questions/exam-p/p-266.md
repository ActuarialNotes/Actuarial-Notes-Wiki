Claim amounts are independent random variables with probability density function

$$f(x) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{10}{x^2}, & \text{for } x > 10 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

Calculate the probability that the largest of three randomly selected claims is less than 25.

- A) $8/125$
- B) $12/125$
- C) $27/125$
- D) $2/5$
- E) $3/5$

## Explanation

The probability that a single claim is less than 25 is

$$P(X < 25) = \int_{10}^{25} 10x^{-2}\,dx = -\frac{10}{x}\Big|_{10}^{25} = 1 - \frac{10}{25} = \frac{3}{5}$$

The largest of the three claims is less than 25 exactly when all three are, and the claims are independent, so the probability that all three claims are less than 25 is

$$\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^3 = \mathbf{\frac{27}{125}}$$
