You are given the following results from a regression model.

| Observation number ($i$) | $y_i$ | $\hat{f}(x_i)$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 4 |
| 2 | 5 | 3 |
| 3 | 6 | 9 |
| 4 | 8 | 3 |
| 5 | 4 | 6 |

Calculate the sum of squared errors (SSE).

- A) –35
- B) –5
- C) 5
- D) 35
- E) 46

## Explanation

The sum of squared errors (SSE) is the sum of the squared differences between the observed values $y_i$ and the predicted values $\hat{f}(x_i)$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{SSE} &= \sum_{i=1}^{5} \left(y_i - \hat{f}(x_i)\right)^2 \\
&= (2-4)^2 + (5-3)^2 + (6-9)^2 + (8-3)^2 + (4-6)^2 \\
&= 4 + 4 + 9 + 25 + 4 \\
&= 46.
\end{align*}
$$

A sum of squares can never be negative, which rules out (A) and (B) immediately.
