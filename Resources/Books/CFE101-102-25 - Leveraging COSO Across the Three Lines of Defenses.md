A July 2015 paper by The Institute of Internal Auditors, commissioned by COSO, that relates the COSO Internal Control – Integrated Framework to the Three Lines of Defense Model. It aims to help organizations articulate and assign specific roles and responsibilities for internal control, so that there are neither gaps in addressing risk and control nor unnecessary duplication of effort.
