An Aon paper, first published in November 2013 and updated in June 2020, that sets out the case for introducing Collective Defined Contribution (CDC) plans in the UK: risk-sharing plans widely used in several other countries but not then permitted in the UK. It reports the authors' research into the design of CDC plans and their advantages and disadvantages compared with current pension arrangements, together with modelling of their historic and prospective performance.
