A Society of Actuaries study note on health reserves for the GH 201 courses. According to the course strategy guide, it overlaps with Skwire's Group Insurance chapter on short-term claim reserves but gives greater detail on reserving methods and non-IBNR reserves, also covers premium and non-claim reserves, and contains some math illustrating the reserve methods.
