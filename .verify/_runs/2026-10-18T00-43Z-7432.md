## Major finding (open): p-an-009 is off the current Exam P syllabus

p-an-009 asks for a conditional probability from a jointly continuous density, which needs a
continuous conditional density. The November 2026 syllabus's Topic 3 outcomes (a)–(e) are all
"for discrete random variables" (PDF p.4). Its suggested texts exclude continuous multivariate
material by name (PDF pp.5-6):

- Ross Ch.6 is limited to 6.1, 6.2, 6.3.3, 6.3.4, 6.4 and 6.6.
- Wackerly: "Chapter 5 (exclude continuous multivariate distributions…)".
- Hassett: 10.3.3 and 11.2.x "continuous" excluded.
- Asimow: "8.4 (exclude continuous)".
- Leemis: "Chapter 6 (exclude continuous…)".

The SOA sample set (pruned to the September 2022 syllabus, according to its cover) has no
continuous joint density: all 39 pages that mention "joint" are discrete.

The key itself is right: 0.75, recomputed. Proposed action: a writer replaces it, or the
maintainer marks it `off_syllabus: true`. Not applied: this is a new question or a scope
decision, not a clean-up fix. Continuous Random Variable keeps its question coverage through
