A practice resource document, dated November 2021, prepared by the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Committee on Enterprise Risk Management to help actuaries, risk practitioners, risk managers, executives and board members understand risk appetite and incorporate a risk appetite framework into their enterprise risk management program. It addresses key concepts, principles and objectives of an effective risk appetite framework, and how to turn them into risk appetite statements, limits and ongoing alignment processes.
