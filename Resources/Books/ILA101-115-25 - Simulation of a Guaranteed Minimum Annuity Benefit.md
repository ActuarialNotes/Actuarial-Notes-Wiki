A Society of Actuaries study note, credited in the syllabus to Freedman and dated 2019, on the simulation of a guaranteed minimum annuity benefit (GMAB). It is accompanied by an Excel model, Stochastic Simulation of a GMAB Option, whose worksheets are GMAB Liab, GMAB liab work, GMAB Liab plus Put, GMAB liab PLUS put work, GMAB_RN and GMAB_RN Work.
