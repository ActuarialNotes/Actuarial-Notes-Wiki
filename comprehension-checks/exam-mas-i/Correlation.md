Two variables have $\text{Var}(X) = 25$, $\text{Var}(Y) = 36$ and $\text{Cov}(X, Y) = -18$. What is the correlation?

- A) $-0.50$
- B) $-0.30$
- C) $-0.60$
- D) $+0.60$

<!-- rationale: 0: divides by the sum of the variances · 1: divides by the product of the variances · 3: drops the sign of the covariance -->
