A KPMG advisory white paper, copyright 2009, on how to put a quantifiable value on an enterprise risk management (ERM) program. It argues that good ERM programs enhance company value through reduced costs, decreased variability in financial results, enhanced market reputation and improved business decision-making, and it sets out an ERM program value model and KPMG's own ERM framework.
