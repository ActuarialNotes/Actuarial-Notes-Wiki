The ACIA modules are a core educational requirement toward ACIA designation. The Canadian Institute of Actuaries' syllabus for its Associate (ACIA) designation: the learning objectives of ACIA Modules 1 and 2, and the ACIA Capstone Exam's — an open-book, six-hour exam in a four-hour common section and a two-hour long- or short-term specialized section — with an appendix of the background knowledge it assumes and tables of background reading references.
