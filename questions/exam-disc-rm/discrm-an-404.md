Which pairing of a general liability account and the ISO premium base its classification normally uses is correct?

- A) A roofing contractor, rated on gross sales per \$1,000
- B) A hardware store, rated on payroll per \$1,000
- C) An apartment building owner, rated on admissions per 1,000
- D) A movie theater, rated on admissions per 1,000

## Explanation

Each ISO classification carries the premium base that best tracks its exposure: payroll for contracting and servicing, gross sales for manufacturing and mercantile risks, area or units for buildings, and admissions for theaters and events. A theater is the textbook admissions risk. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Contractors are rated on payroll, which measures how much work they do.
- **B** — Stores (mercantile classes) are rated on gross sales.
- **C** — An apartment building is rated on units, the separate living quarters, or area; admissions are for people admitted to events.
