Rhonda purchases a perpetuity providing a payment of 1 at the beginning of each year. The perpetuity's Macaulay duration is 30 years.

Calculate the modified duration of this perpetuity.

- A) 28.97
- B) 29.00
- C) 29.03
- D) 29.07
- E) 29.10

## Explanation

The payments of 1 are made at times $0, 1, 2, \ldots$, and the Macaulay duration is their present-value-weighted average time — the time-weighted present value divided by the present value:

$$
\begin{align*}
30 = MacD &= \frac{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} n v^n}{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} v^n} \\
&= \frac{(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|}}{\ddot{a}_{\overline{\infty}|}} \\
&= \frac{1/(di)}{1/d} \\
&= \frac{(1+i)/i^2}{(1+i)/i} \\
&= \frac{1}{i}
\end{align*}
$$

So $i = 1/30$. The modified duration is:

$$
\begin{align*}
ModD &= \frac{MacD}{1+i} \\
&= \frac{30}{1+\frac{1}{30}} \\
&= \frac{30 \cdot 30}{31} \\
&= 29.032
\end{align*}
$$
