Large claims arrive in a Poisson process at 1 per month. Three quiet weeks have just passed with no claim. What is the expected wait until the next one?

- A) One week, since three of the four weeks are used up
- B) One month, the same as at any other moment
- C) Slightly under a month, adjusted for the quiet spell
- D) It rises the longer the quiet spell runs
