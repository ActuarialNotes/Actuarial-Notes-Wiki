Four businesses each have an unendorsed CGL (CG 00 01 04 13). In each case a guest drinks too much on the premises and later injures a third party, who sues the business for contributing to the guest's intoxication. In which case does the liquor liability exclusion apply?

- A) An accounting firm that serves wine at its annual client reception
- B) A software company that allows staff to bring their own beer to a Friday social in its office
- C) A restaurant that sells beer and wine with meals
- D) A car dealer that hands out free champagne to buyers at a showroom launch

## Explanation

The liquor liability exclusion applies only if the named insured is *in the business of* manufacturing, distributing, selling, serving or furnishing alcoholic beverages. A restaurant selling beer and wine is in that business, so the exclusion applies to it. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** and **D** are host liquor exposures: businesses that serve alcohol incidentally, not as their business. The exclusion does not apply.
- **B** is expressly addressed by the form: permitting people to bring alcohol onto the premises for consumption there is not by itself the business of selling, serving or furnishing alcohol.
