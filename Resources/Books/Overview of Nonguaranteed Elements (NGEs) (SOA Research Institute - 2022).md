A November 2022 study note, sponsored by the SOA's Individual Life and Annuity Curriculum Committee, on nonguaranteed elements (NGEs): the numeric parameters of life insurance and annuity policies that are not fixed and guaranteed at issue. It sets out their history and types, the professional guidance and regulation that apply to them, international considerations and common practical issues, and closes with a case study.
