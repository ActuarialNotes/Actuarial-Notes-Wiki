Bank P offers a 3-year certificate of deposit that pays an annual effective interest rate of 4%. In addition, a bonus of 2% of the initial investment is paid at the end of the 3-year period.

Bank Q offers a 3-year certificate of deposit without any bonus.

Calculate the annual effective interest rate that Bank Q would have to offer to produce the same annual yield as the certificate from Bank P.

- A) 4.3%
- B) 4.4%
- C) 4.5%
- D) 4.6%
- E) 4.7%

## Explanation

Invest 1 in Bank P. After 3 years: $1.04^3 + 0.02 = 1.124864 + 0.02 = 1.144864$.

The annual effective rate $i$ for Bank Q satisfies $(1+i)^3 = 1.144864$:
$$
\begin{align*}
i &= 1.144864^{1/3} - 1 \\
  &= 0.04613 = 4.6\%
\end{align*}
$$
