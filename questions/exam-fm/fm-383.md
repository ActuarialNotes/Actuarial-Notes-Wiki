A company's liabilities are 20,000 today and 100,000 five years from today. The Macaulay duration of the company's liabilities with respect to the market annual effective yield rate is 3.70 years.

Calculate the modified duration of the company's liabilities, in years.

- A) 3.26
- B) 3.31
- C) 3.52
- D) 3.65
- E) 4.14

## Explanation

Let $i$ represent the effective market annual yield rate and $v = \dfrac{1}{1+i}$. The Macaulay duration is 3.70 years, which is equal to the present-value-weighted times of the liabilities. Therefore,

$$
\begin{align*}
3.70 &= \frac{20{,}000(0) + 100{,}000v^{5}(5)}{20{,}000 + 100{,}000v^{5}} \\
     &= \frac{25v^{5}}{1 + 5v^{5}}
\end{align*}
$$

Solving for $v$:

$$
\begin{align*}
3.70 + 18.5v^{5} &= 25v^{5} \\
3.70 &= 6.5v^{5} \\
v^{5} &= 0.569231 \\
v &= 0.893424 \\
1+i &= 1.11929
\end{align*}
$$

Modified duration equals Macaulay duration divided by $1+i$, so the modified duration is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD} &= \frac{3.70}{1.11929} \\
        &= 3.30567
\end{align*}
$$
