A Milliman article, dated 23 February 2024, on reinsurance "sidecars": vehicles formed by a sponsoring insurer with the support of third-party investors infusing capital and, in some cases, providing other services such as asset management. Sidecars have been a common structure in the U.S. property and casualty industry, and the article describes their growing use in the U.S. life and annuity industry, why they have grown in relevance, and considerations and next steps for sponsors and investors.

## What's driving the interest in reinsurance sidecars for the L&A industry?
- The life and annuity space is being reshaped by changing strategic imperatives that have led to a wave of transactions, underpinned by the entrance of new private capital, including leading asset managers.
- Sidecars provide a structure through which insurers can access third-party capital and new capital providers can support insurers in de-risking their balance sheets.
- Sidecars can serve strategies such as in-force block reinsurance, flow reinsurance for new business, and [[Pension Risk Transfer|pension risk transfer]].
