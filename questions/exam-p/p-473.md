Losses under a boat insurance policy are uniformly distributed on the interval $[0, 1]$. The policy has a fixed deductible.

The expected value of the claim payment on a given loss is 0.245.

Calculate the variance of the claim payment on a given loss.

- A) $0.020$
- B) $0.054$
- C) $0.062$
- D) $0.083$
- E) $0.114$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the loss and $d$ the deductible, with $0 < d < 1$. The payment is $Y = \max(0, X-d)$, a **mixed** random variable: it equals 0 with probability $d$, and is uniform on $[0, 1-d]$ with probability $1-d$.

**Recover $d$ from the given mean.** The point mass contributes nothing, and the uniform piece has mean $(1-d)/2$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.245 = E[Y] &= d(0) + (1-d)\left(\frac{1-d}{2}\right) \\
             &= \frac{(1-d)^2}{2}
\end{align*}
$$

so $(1-d)^2 = 0.49$ and, since $0 < d < 1$, $1 - d = 0.7$.

**Second moment.** The uniform piece on $[0, 1-d]$ has $E[Y^2 \mid Y > 0] = (1-d)^2/3$:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[Y^2] &= d(0)^2 + (1-d)\frac{(1-d)^2}{3} \\
       &= \frac{(1-d)^3}{3} = \frac{0.343}{3}
\end{align*}
$$

**Variance.**

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(Y) &= \frac{(1-d)^3}{3} - \left[\frac{(1-d)^2}{2}\right]^2 \\
              &= \frac{0.7^3}{3} - \frac{0.7^4}{4} \\
              &= 0.11433 - 0.06003 \\
              &= \mathbf{0.0543}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (E), 0.114, is $E[Y^2]$ with the squared mean never subtracted. The point mass at zero is what makes this different from an ordinary uniform variance: a uniform on $[0, 0.7]$ alone would have variance $0.7^2/12 = 0.041$.
