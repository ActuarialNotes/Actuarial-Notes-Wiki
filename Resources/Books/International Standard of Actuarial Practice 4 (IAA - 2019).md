A model standard of actuarial practice from the International Actuarial Association, adopted by the IAA Council on 21 November 2019. It provides guidance to actuaries performing actuarial services in connection with International Financial Reporting Standard 17 Insurance Contracts (IFRS 17), to increase intended users' confidence that the services are carried out professionally and with due care, that the results are relevant, clear and complete, and that the assumptions and methodology used are disclosed appropriately.
