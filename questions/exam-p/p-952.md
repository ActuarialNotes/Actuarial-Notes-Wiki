Two dependent losses have joint density

$$f(x,y) = 8xy, \qquad 0 < x < y < 1$$

Determine the marginal density of $Y$.

- A) $4y^3$ for $0 < y < 1$
- B) $4y$ for $0 < y < 1$
- C) $4y(1-y^2)$ for $0 < y < 1$
- D) $2y^2$ for $0 < y < 1$
- E) $8y$ for $0 < y < 1$

## Explanation

To obtain $f_Y(y)$, integrate $x$ out. Holding $y$ fixed on the sketch, $x$ ranges from $0$ up to $y$ — **not** from $0$ to $1$.

$$
\begin{align*}
f_Y(y) &= \int_0^y 8xy\,dx \\
       &= 8y\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^y \\
       &= \mathbf{4y^3}, \qquad 0 < y < 1
\end{align*}
$$

Check the result integrates to 1: $\int_0^1 4y^3\,dy = 1$. 

Choice (B) is what integrating $x$ from 0 to 1 produces, and it integrates to 2 — an immediate signal the region was wrong. Choice (C) is the marginal of $X$, namely $f_X(x) = \int_x^1 8xy\,dy = 4x(1-x^2)$.
