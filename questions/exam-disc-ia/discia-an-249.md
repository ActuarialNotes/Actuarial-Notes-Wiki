A state has enacted the NAIC Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Act. Which finding would most clearly support charging an insurer with an unfair claims settlement practice?

- A) One adjuster took three weeks to return a policyholder's call about a single claim
- B) After a reasonable investigation the insurer denied a claim, and the claimant disagrees with the result
- C) A market conduct examination shows the insurer routinely refuses claims without reasonable investigation
- D) A policyholder has sued the insurer under the Act for refusing her claim

## Explanation

The Act lists practices such as refusing to pay claims without conducting a reasonable investigation, but an act is an unfair claims practice only if it is committed **flagrantly and in conscious disregard** of the Act or with **such frequency as to indicate a general business practice**. A pattern found across files in a market conduct examination meets that test.

- **A** is a single lapse, not flagrant and not a general business practice.
- **B** is a denial after a reasonable investigation, which the Act does not prohibit.
- **D** misreads the Act, which states that it creates no private cause of action. It is enforced by the commissioner.
