A textbook on fixed income markets and the quantitative tools used to price and hedge in them, in its fourth edition, published by Wiley in September 2022. Its publisher describes it as presenting an overview of fixed income markets, explaining the conceptual frameworks and quantitative tool kits used in the industry for pricing and hedging, and examining a wide range of fixed income instruments and markets, from government bonds and interest rate swaps to corporate bonds, credit default swaps and mortgage-backed securities.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-351 (SOA)|Exam CP-351]] — Chapter 4, DV01, Duration, and Convexity, and Chapter 5, Key-Rate, Partial, and Forward-Bucket '01s and Durations (outcome 2a); Chapter 6, Regression Hedging and Principal Component Analysis (outcome 2b).
