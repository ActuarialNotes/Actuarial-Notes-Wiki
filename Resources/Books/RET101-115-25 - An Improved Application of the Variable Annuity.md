A February 2017 Global Risk Institute report by Stephen A. Eadie on variable annuities as a payout mechanism for pension plans, in which a "hurdle rate", the plan fund's targeted real rate of investment return, is compared with actual returns to adjust monthly pensioner payments each year. It assesses two potential improvements to the variable annuity for qualified US and registered Canadian pension plans, aiming at more stable increases to members' monthly pensions and better control of post-retirement risks.
