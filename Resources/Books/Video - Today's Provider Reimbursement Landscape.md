A short-form professional development video by Dan Pribe on modern approaches to healthcare financing. The Society of Actuaries describes it as focusing on value-based care and the shift from fee-for-service models to models in which providers share financial risk, discussing compensation approaches such as bundled payments, budget setting and quality incentives, and touching on the challenges of measuring quality and aligning incentives.
