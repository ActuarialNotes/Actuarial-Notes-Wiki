# Exam F3RET
**F3RET – Pension Risk Management** is an exam of the retirement benefits track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques of enterprise risk management (ERM) and investment risk management that are relevant to retirement benefits.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of pension plans.
>
> 3. Evaluate and propose recommendations related to the risk management of pension plans.
>
> 4. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in [[Enterprise Risk Management|ERM]]. (A1)

> [!example]- ERM concept, framework, and process {15%}
>
> 1. Apply the concept of [[Enterprise Risk Management|ERM]]. (D3)
> 2. Describe and understand the framework for [[Risk Management|risk management]] and control. (C2)
> 3. Describe the perspectives of [[Rating Agency|credit-rating agencies]]. (B2)
> 4. Reflect [[Data Issues|data issues]] in general and special considerations with respect to [[Enterprise Risk Management|ERM]]. (C3)
> 5. Demonstrate how to determine and articulate: (C3)
>    - a. [[Risk Appetite|Risk appetite]].
>    - b. [[Risk Identification|Risk identification]].
>    - c. [[Risk Assessment|Risk assessment]].
>    - d. [[Risk Monitoring|Risk monitoring]].
>    - e. [[Risk Appetite|Risk limits]].
>    - f. [[Risk Mitigation|Risk mitigation]].
>    - g. [[Risk Capacity|Risk capacity]].
>    - h. [[Risk Tolerance|Risk tolerances]].
>    - i. [[Desired Risk Profile|Desired risk profile]].
>    - j. [[Risk]] objectives.
>    - k. [[Risk Reporting|Risk reporting]].
> 6. Assess the implications of financial and other [[Risk|risks]] and opportunities for strategic planning. (C4)
> 7. Describe the [[Risk Management|risk management]] control cycle, including the relevance of [[External Influence|external influences]] and [[Emerging Risk|emerging risks]]. (C2)
> 8. Utilize methods to identify [[Risk|risks]] and their causes and implications. (D4)

> [!example]- Risk categories and risk identification {15%}
>
> 1. Explain what is meant by [[Risk|risk]] and uncertainty, including different definitions and concepts of [[Risk|risk]]. (B2)
> 2. Describe [[Risk Taxonomy|risk taxonomy]], including an awareness of how [[Individual Risk Model|individual risks]] might be categorized in different ways. (B1)
> 3. Describe common [[Risk|risks]] faced by [[Pension Plans Insurer|pension plans insurers]], including: (B2)
>    - a. [[Interest Rate Risk|Interest rate risk]].
>    - b. [[Credit Risk|Credit risk]].
>    - c. [[Market Risk|Market risk]].
>    - d. [[Liquidity Risk|Liquidity risk]].
>    - e. [[Mortality Risk|Mortality risk]].
>    - f. [[Operational Risk|Operational risk]].
>    - g. ESG and [[Climate-Related Risk|climate-related risks]].
>    - h. [[Foreign-exchange Risk|Foreign-exchange risk]].
>    - i. [[Regulatory Risk|Regulatory risk]].
>    - j. [[Political Risk|Political risk]].
>    - k. [[Demographic Risk|Demographic risk]].
>    - l. [[Strategic Risk|Strategic risk]].
>    - m. [[Asset-liability Duration Mismatch Risk|Asset/liability duration mismatch risk]].
> 4. Analyze the relationship between [[Systemic Risk|systemic risk]] vs. non-systemic and specific [[Risk|risk]] vs. concentration of [[Risk|risk]].

> [!example]- Investment risk management {25%}
>
> 1. Identify and assess the sources of [[Market Risk|investment risk]] applicable to [[Investment Policy Statement|retirement fund assets]]. (C3)
> 2. Evaluate the interaction of [[Investment Policy Statement|plan investments]] with [[Plan Design|plan design]], [[Valuation|valuation]], accounting, and [[Funding|funding]]. (C5)
> 3. Evaluate the interaction and relationship between [[Investment Policy Statement|plan investments]] and [[Valuation Assumptions-method|valuation assumptions/methods]]. (C5)
> 4. Evaluate how factors including [[Investment Policy Statement|cash-flow requirements]], various [[Plan Design|plan designs]], and various [[Economic Environment|economic environments]] affect setting [[Investment Strategy|investment strategy]]. (B5)
> 5. Apply and evaluate strategies and techniques for asset/liability management. ([[D5]])
> 6. Provide advice and analysis to [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsors]] regarding the mitigation of [[Pension Plan Risk|pension plan risks]]. (D6)
> 7. Given a context, interpret a statement of [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policy]] and evaluate if changes are required. (C5)
> 8. Given a context, interpret a statement of [[Funding Policy|funding policy]] and evaluate if changes are required. (C5)

> [!example]- Risk management and financial economics {10%}
>
> 1. Compare and analyze the [[Interest|interests]] of [[Plan Sponsor|plan sponsors]], employees, shareholders, taxpayers, and other stakeholders related to the financial management of a [[Retirement Plan|retirement plan]]. (C4)
> 2. Analyze how the [[Retirement Plan's|retirement plan’s]] financial and design [[Risk|risks]] integrate with the [[Sponsor's Risk Management Strategy|sponsor’s risk management strategy]]. (C4)
> 3. Analyze how the [[Retirement Plan Integrate|retirement plan integrates]] with the [[Sponsor's Overall Financial Position|sponsor’s overall financial position]]. (C4)
> 4. Understand and apply the principles of [[Financial Economics|financial economics]] with respect to [[Pension Plan Investing|pension plan investing]]. (C3)
> 5. Provide advice and analysis to stakeholders regarding the economic assumptions used in the [[Valuation|valuation]] of their [[Retirement Plan|retirement plans]]. (B6)
> 6. Provide advice and analysis to various stakeholders regarding the mitigation of [[Pension Plan Risk|pension plan risks]]. (B6)

> [!example]- Mathematics of financial risks {10%}
>
> 1. Understand how [[Predictive Analytics|predictive analytics]] are used in retirement applications. (C3)
> 2. Understand and analyze properties of [[Financial Time Series|financial time series]]. (C4)
> 3. Understand and analyze stochastic models. ([[C4]])
> 4. Analyze [[Stochastic Modelling|stochastic modelling]] from a regulatory requirement perspective. (C4)
> 5. Calculate and describe the concept of [[Duration|duration]], [[Convexity|convexity]], and [[Immunization|immunization]]. (B4)
> 6. Understand and apply [[Plausible Adverse Scenario|plausible adverse scenarios]]. (C3)

> [!example]- Pension plan de-risking {25%}
>
> 1. Design and construct [[Replication Portfolio|replication portfolios]] using available assets or products to [[Defray Risk|defray risk]]. (C6)
> 2. Apply [[Immunization Strategy|immunization strategies]] using [[Duration|duration]] and [[Convexity|convexity]]. (C3)
> 3. Identify, evaluate, and manage [[Longevity Risk|longevity risk]]. (B5)
> 4. Analyze, evaluate, and construct [[Glide Path|glide paths]] in order to de-risk a [[Pension Plan|pension plan]]. (C6)
> 5. Analyze, evaluate, and create margins in assumptions in order to de-risk a [[Pension Plan|pension plan]]. (C6)
> 6. Evaluate and apply change in [[Group Health Insurance|plan provisions]] in order to de-risk a [[Pension Plan|pension plan]]. (C6)
> 7. Consider, assess, and design [[Target-Benefit Pension Plan|target-benefit pension plans]] and [[Shared Risk Pension Plan|shared risk pension plans]]. (C6)
> 8. Apply the [[Process Relative|process relative]] to [[Annuity Purchase|annuity purchases]]: (D5)
>    - a. [[Annuities Buy-In|Annuities buy-ins]] versus buyouts.
>    - b. Legal requirements.
>    - c. Contribution requirements.
>    - d. Transfers in kind.
>    - e. [[Legal Contract Review|Legal contract reviews]].
>    - f. Coordination among [[Annuity Provider|annuity providers]].
>    - g. Conversion from [[Annuity Buy-In|annuity buy-in]] to buyout.
>    - h. Discharge of liabilities under applicable legislation.
> 9. Describe how [[Assuris Protection|Assuris protection]] can be optimized. (B2)
>    - a. Calculate and consider the impact of [[Ancillary Benefit|ancillary benefits]] (e.g., indexation, [[Survivor Benefit|survivor benefits]], etc.). (C4)
> 10. Apply legislative requirements to [[Plan Provisions Allowing Annuity Purchase|plan provisions allowing annuity purchases]]. (C4)
