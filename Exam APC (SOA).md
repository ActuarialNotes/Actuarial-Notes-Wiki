# APC

The **Associateship Professionalism Course (APC)** is the final requirement for the [[Associate of the Society of Actuaries (ASA)|ASA]] designation and the CERA credential. Candidates examine and discuss case studies based on actual business situations, and analyze and recommend solutions that align with the [[Code of Professional Conduct]], [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|Standards of Practice]], ethics and legal liability. There is no exam. A candidate takes the course after completing all other ASA requirements, and only by invitation, which comes from APC@soa.org.

> [!question]- About the course
>
> The APC is offered in person or virtually. The SOA listed the fee as $658 for the virtual course and $847 in person (read 2026-10-17). Every candidate scheduled for a particular APC cohort receives credit for the APC on the same day, at the end of the last week of the virtual sessions.
>
> A virtual APC runs over a month-long timeframe and has two components:
>
> - **An e-Learning module**, completed on one's own (estimated 3–4 hours), with approximately 14 days to complete it.
> - **A virtual meeting session**, a case discussion in which small groups of candidates and one facilitator meet on a virtual meeting platform (estimated 2–3 hours). Scheduling options span a two-week period. A participant absent for more than 10 minutes has to repeat the entire course.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- E-Learning Module

> 2. [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|Standards of Practice]]
> 3. [[Professional Ethics|Ethics]]

> 2. Analyze and recommend solutions that align with the [[Code of Professional Conduct]], [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|Standards of Practice]], ethics and [[Legal Liability|legal liability]].
