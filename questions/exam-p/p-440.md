Losses under a boat insurance policy are exponentially distributed. The median loss is 400.

Calculate the mean loss.

- A) $400$
- B) $446$
- C) $492$
- D) $533$
- E) $577$

## Explanation

For an exponential distribution with mean $\lambda$, the distribution function is $F(x) = 1 - e^{-x/\lambda}$. Set it to one half at the median:

$$
\begin{align*}
1 - e^{-400/\lambda} &= 0.5 \\
e^{-400/\lambda} &= 0.5 \\
-\frac{400}{\lambda} &= \ln 0.5 \\
\lambda &= \frac{400}{\ln 2} \\
        &= \mathbf{577.08}
\end{align*}
$$

The general relationship is $\text{median} = \lambda\ln 2 \approx 0.693\lambda$, so the mean is always about 1.44 times the median for an exponential distribution. The mean exceeds the median because the long right tail pulls it upward; choice (A), 400, sets them equal, as they would be for a distribution symmetric about its median — which the exponential is not.
