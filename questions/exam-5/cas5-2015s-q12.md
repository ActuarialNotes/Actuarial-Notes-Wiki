Describe two primary purposes of risk classification.

### Explanation
Enhance Fairness – By using risk classification, insureds are priced more closely to their expected losses for the policy.  Thus low risk insureds will pay a lower amount than higher risk insureds.  This way the low risk insureds will not subsidize the high risks and premium is equitable.

Alternatively:

Enhance Fairness.  Differences in prices reflect differences of the future expected loss.  Exposures in the same class with similar risks should not be charged significantly differently.
