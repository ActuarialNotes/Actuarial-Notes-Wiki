For a set of data with 40 observations, 2 predictors ($X_1$ and $X_2$), and one response (Y), the residual sum of squares has been calculated for several different estimates of a linear model with an intercept. Only integer values from 1 to 3 were considered for estimates of $\beta_0$ (the intercept), $\beta_1$ and $\beta_2$.

The grid below shows the residual sum of squares for every combination of the parameter estimates, after standardization:

**$\hat{\beta}_0 = 1$**

| $\hat{\beta}_1$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 1$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 2$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 3$ |
|:-:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 3,924 | 1,977 | 1,250 |
| 2 | 1,858 | 1,141 | 711 |
| 3 | 1,386 | 822 | 369 |

**$\hat{\beta}_0 = 2$**

| $\hat{\beta}_1$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 1$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 2$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 3$ |
|:-:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 3,949 | 1,822 | 1,174 |
| 2 | 1,907 | 1,187 | 717 |
| 3 | 1,363 | 711 | 349 |

**$\hat{\beta}_0 = 3$**

| $\hat{\beta}_1$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 1$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 2$ | $\hat{\beta}_2 = 3$ |
|:-:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 3,784 | 1,671 | 1,107 |
| 2 | 1,827 | 1,128 | 668 |
| 3 | 1,294 | 700 | 344 |

Let $\hat{\beta}_i^R$ be the estimate of $\beta_i$ using a ridge regression with budget parameter $s = 5$. Assume the intercept is not subject to the budget parameters.

Calculate the value of $\hat{\beta}_0^R + \hat{\beta}_1^R + \hat{\beta}_2^R$.

- A) Less than 6
- B) 6
- C) 7
- D) 8
- E) Greater than 8

## Explanation

In its budget form, ridge regression minimizes the RSS subject to $\beta_1^2 + \beta_2^2 \le s$. The intercept is left free. With $s = 5$, only these slope pairs $(\hat\beta_1, \hat\beta_2)$ are allowed:

- $(1, 1)$: $1 + 1 = 2 \le 5$
- $(1, 2)$ and $(2, 1)$: $1 + 4 = 5 \le 5$
- $(2, 2)$ gives $8 > 5$, and any pair containing a 3 gives at least 10, so every other pair is ruled out.

Read the RSS for the allowed pairs under each intercept (rows of the grid are $\hat\beta_1$, columns are $\hat\beta_2$):

| | $(1, 1)$ | $(1, 2)$ | $(2, 1)$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| $\hat\beta_0 = 1$ | 3,924 | 1,977 | 1,858 |
| $\hat\beta_0 = 2$ | 3,949 | 1,822 | 1,907 |
| $\hat\beta_0 = 3$ | 3,784 | 1,671 | 1,827 |

The smallest constrained RSS is 1,671, at $\hat\beta_0^R = 3$, $\hat\beta_1^R = 1$, $\hat\beta_2^R = 2$. The much smaller RSS values elsewhere in the grid (as low as 344) all need a slope of 3, which the budget does not allow.

So $\hat\beta_0^R + \hat\beta_1^R + \hat\beta_2^R = 3 + 1 + 2 = 6$, and the answer is **B**.
