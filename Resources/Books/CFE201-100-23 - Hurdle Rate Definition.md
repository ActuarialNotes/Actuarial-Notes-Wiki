A Corporate Finance Institute guide to the hurdle rate, also known as the minimum acceptable rate of return (MARR): the minimum required or target rate of return that investors expect to receive on an investment. The page says the rate is determined by assessing the cost of capital, the risks involved, current business expansion opportunities, rates of return for similar investments, and other factors that could directly affect an investment.
