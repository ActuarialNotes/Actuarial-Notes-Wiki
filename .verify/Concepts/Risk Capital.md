## [F-001] Merton-Perold pieces said always to sum to less than C
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-10-18T18:53Z/f1ee
- date: 2026-10-18
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: allocation methods, 'Merton–Perold marginal'
- claim: Merton–Perold marginal — C_i = C(all) - C(all but i). Correct for adding or dropping a whole line, but the pieces sum to less than C, leaving capital unallocated.
- evidence: Goldfarb's CAS study note p.33 Table 18 (sha256:b0ee2f32…): the with-and-without (incremental) 99% VaR amounts sum to 10,713,904 against total risk capital of 8,949,750 — more than C — and he notes 'the incremental amounts do not add up to the total capital'. Myers & Read (p.6 Table 1 Panel C, sha256:68145062…) have them fall short (35 + 46 + 58 = 139 < 150). Either direction occurs, so 'less than C' is not general (the page's own example, 40 + 80 = 120 < 140, is one case).
- source_rank: 3
- proposed_action: Say the pieces do not in general sum to C — they can fall short or exceed it.
- applied: true
