Each of bonds A, B, and C sells for 10,000 and has the same annual effective yield rate and term. The par values and coupon rates are shown below.

| | Bond A | Bond B | Bond C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Par Value | 20,000.00 | 10,835.58 | $X$ |
| Annual Coupon Rate | 0% | 4% | 3% |

Each bond is redeemed at par, and all coupons are paid annually.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 12,240
- B) 12,630
- C) 13,130
- D) 13,540
- E) 14,450

## Explanation

Bond A (zero-coupon): $10{,}000 = 20{,}000\,v^n \implies v^n = 0.5$.

Bond B (solving for $a_{\overline{n}|}$):

$$
\begin{align*}
10{,}000 &= 10{,}835.58(0.5 + 0.04\,a_{\overline{n}|}) \\
a_{\overline{n}|} &= \frac{10{,}000/10{,}835.58 - 0.5}{0.04} \\
                  &= \frac{0.922886-0.5}{0.04} \\
                  &= 10.5721
\end{align*}
$$

Bond C:

$$
\begin{align*}
10{,}000 &= X(0.5 + 0.03 \times 10.5721) \\
         &= 0.81716X \\
X &= 10{,}000/0.81716 \\
  &= 12{,}237.5 \approx 12{,}240
\end{align*}
$$
