A November 2024 Milliman white paper on the National Association of Insurance Commissioners' efforts to reform the Generator of Economic Scenarios (GOES) used in principle-based reserving in the United States. It uses the scenarios of the NAIC's second industry field test to look at VM-21 reserves and C-3 Phase II capital for variable annuities across a range of capital market sensitivities, with a detailed view of tail risk measures, key statistical observations and approaches to modeling future hedge strategies.
