The following information is given for a Canadian property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2014. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| Line of Business | Net Unearned Premium Reserve | Selected Loss Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Auto (excl. Facility Association) | 5,000 | 70% |
| Personal Property | 1,000 | 80% |
| Facility Association | 500 | 95% |

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Selected Maintenance Expense Ratio of Premium | 2.5% |
| Selected Internal Adjustment Expense Ratio of losses | 5.0% |
| Selected Contingent Commission Ratio of Premium | 6.0% |
| Unearned Commissions | 200 |
| Deferred Policy Acquisition Expenses | 1,100 |

Loss ratios are discounted according to accepted actuarial practice in Canada.

## Part a (2.25 points)

Determine the Deferred Policy Acquisition Expenses that the company will be allowed to book on its financial statements. Fully support your answer.

### Explanation
1. Expected claims and ALAE for voluntary business $= 5{,}000 \times 0.7 + 1{,}000 \times 0.8 = 4{,}300$
2. Expected claims and ALAE for Facility $= 500 \times 0.95 = 475$
3. Maintenance expense for voluntary business only $= (5{,}000 + 1{,}000) \times 2.5\% = 150$ (no maintenance expense for Facility business)
4. Internal Adjustment Expense $= 4{,}300 \times 5\% = 215$ (no IAE for Facility business)
5. Commission $= (5{,}000 + 1{,}000) \times 6\% = 360$ (no commission for Facility business)
6. EQUP = UEP - expected claims and ALAE for voluntary business and Facility - ME - IAE - Commission:

$$\text{EQUP} = (5{,}000 + 1{,}000 + 500) - 4{,}300 - 475 - 150 - 215 - 360 = 1{,}000$$

Maximum allowable DPAE = EQUP + Unearned commissions $= 1{,}000 + 200 = 1{,}200$

Since DPAE is $1{,}100 <$ allowable maximum DPAE, the company can book DPAE of 1,100 on its financial statements.

- Most candidates received full marks.
- Some candidates performed the calculation correctly, but failed to draw a conclusion as to determining the DPAE to be booked on the financial statements.
