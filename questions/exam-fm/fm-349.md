The annual force of interest is $\delta_t = \dfrac{2}{10-t}$, for $0 \le t < 10$, in which $t$ is measured in years.

Calculate the equivalent annual nominal discount rate compounded every two years for the period $2.0 \le t \le 2.4$.

- A) 20%
- B) 23%
- C) 27%
- D) 29%
- E) 34%

## Explanation

Accumulation factor from $t=2$ to $t=2.4$:
$$
\begin{align*}
\exp\!\left(\int_2^{2.4}\frac{2}{10-t}\,dt\right) &= \exp\!\left[-2\ln(10-t)\Big|_2^{2.4}\right] \\
                                                  &= \exp[-2\ln(7.6)+2\ln(8)] \\
                                                  &= \left(\frac{8}{7.6}\right)^2
\end{align*}
$$

Let $d^{(1/2)}$ be the equivalent annual nominal discount rate compounded every two years. Each two-year compounding period carries discount $d^{(1/2)}/0.5$, and the 0.4-year period is $0.5(0.4) = 0.2$ of one, so:

$\left(1-\dfrac{d^{(1/2)}}{0.5}\right)^{-0.5(0.4)} = \left(\dfrac{8}{7.6}\right)^2$

$\left(1-2d^{(1/2)}\right)^{-0.2} = \left(\dfrac{8}{7.6}\right)^2$

$$
\begin{align*}
1-2d^{(1/2)} &= \left(\frac{7.6}{8}\right)^{10} \\
             &= (0.95)^{10} \\
             &= 0.59874 \\
d^{(1/2)} &= \frac{1-0.59874}{2} \\
          &= 0.20063 \approx 20\%
\end{align*}
$$
