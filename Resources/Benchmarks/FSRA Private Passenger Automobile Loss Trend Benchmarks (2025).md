# FSRA Private Passenger Automobile Loss Trend Benchmarks (2025)

FSRA's guidance on the **benchmark loss trend rates and reform factors** it uses when it
reviews a private passenger automobile (PPA) major rate filing in Ontario. This edition is
built on the Ontario PPA industry experience reported to the General Insurance Statistical
Agency (GISA) as of **December 31, 2024**, and applies to rate filings submitted on or after
**December 11, 2025**. FSRA retained Oliver Wyman to derive the loss trend rates and reform
factors independently of FSRA.

## What the guidance sets out

- **Loss trend benchmarks, by coverage** — for each coverage, a *past trend* rate and a
  *range of future trend* rates. The future range is FSRA's range of reasonable future loss
  trends at the coverage level, representing the 95% confidence interval of the trend rate.
- **Reform factors, by coverage** — the adjustments that bring experience from before a
  reform onto the post-reform basis.
- **How FSRA uses them** — the benchmarks are what a major filing's selected trends and
  reform adjustments are reviewed against; a selection outside them needs its own support.

## How an actuary uses it

A pricing analysis for an Ontario auto rate filing takes the **trend rate** for each coverage
from this table — the past trend to bring experience to the present, and a selection within
the future range to project it to the average date of the proposed rates — and names the
edition (the data-as-of date) beside every trend it uses. A company trend that falls outside
the benchmark range is a judgment the filing has to defend.
