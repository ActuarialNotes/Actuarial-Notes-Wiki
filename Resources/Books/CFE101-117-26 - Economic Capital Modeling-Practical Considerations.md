A Milliman report, dated February 2025, that describes the key components and design choices in modeling economic capital and how they differ across jurisdictions. It treats whether to take a market-consistent or real-world approach to the balance sheet, which risks to include, the challenges in measuring different risk drivers, and the modeling choices that determine economic capital. Milliman states that the paper was commissioned and partially funded by the Society of Actuaries.
