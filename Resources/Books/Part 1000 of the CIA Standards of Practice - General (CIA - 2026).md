Part 1000 of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries Standards of Practice, the general standards that apply to actuarial work in Canada, in the complete Standards of Practice published January 2026 (accession 226011). Responsibility for the standards vests in the Actuarial Standards Board (Canada), and approval of standards and changes to them are made through a process that includes consultation with the profession and other interested parties. The existence of standards is not a substitute for professional judgment or consideration for the needs of the user when performing specific work.
