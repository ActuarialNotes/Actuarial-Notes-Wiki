The core [[Generalized Linear Model]] text on the MAS-I syllabus, covering learning objectives C1–C9. It develops the [[Exponential Family]], estimation and inference, and then works through the model families the exam asks about — Normal, binomial and Poisson.

## 1 Introduction

- Background and scope
- Notation and [[Linear Algebra|matrix]] conventions
- [[Probability Distributions]] and their [[Expected Value|expectations]]

## 2 Model Fitting

- Examples of fitted models
- Some principles of statistical modelling
- Notation and coding for explanatory variables ([[Categorical Predictor|categorical]] and continuous)

## 3 Exponential Family and Generalized Linear Models

- The [[Exponential Family]] of distributions
- Properties of distributions in the exponential family
- [[Generalized Linear Model]]s
- [[Link Function|Link functions]]

## 4 Estimation

- [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]] and the score statistic
- The Newton–Raphson method and iterative weighted least squares
- Sampling distribution for score statistics and for [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation|MLE]]s
- Log-likelihood ratio statistic

## 5 Inference

- Sampling distribution for likelihood ratio statistics
- [[Deviance]]
- [[Hypothesis Testing]]
- [[Residual Plot|Residuals]] and goodness-of-fit statistics

## 6 Normal Linear Models

- [[Linear Regression]] and simple linear models
- Multiple [[Linear Regression]]
- [[ANOVA]] and analysis of covariance
- General linear models

## 7 Binary Variables and Logistic Regression

- Probability distributions for binary data
- Generalized linear models for a binary response
- Dose response models and [[Logistic Regression]]
- Goodness of fit, [[Residual Plot|residuals]] and diagnostics

## 8 Nominal and Ordinal Logistic Regression

- Nominal [[Logistic Regression]]
- Ordinal [[Logistic Regression]]
- Comparing the two

## 9 Poisson Regression and Log-Linear Models

- [[Poisson Regression]]
- Examples of [[Poisson Regression]]
- Log-linear models and contingency tables

## 10 Survival Analysis

- [[Survival Model|Survival functions]] and [[Hazard Rate|hazard functions]]
- [[Censoring]] and the likelihood
- Proportional hazards models

## 11 Clustered and Longitudinal Data

- Repeated measures for Normal and non-Normal data
- Multilevel and mixed models ([[Linear Mixed Model]])
- Generalized estimating equations

## 12 Bayesian Analysis

- Priors, posteriors and [[Bayesian]] inference
- Distributions and hierarchical models

## 13 Markov Chain Monte Carlo Methods

- [[Markov Chain]] Monte Carlo, Gibbs sampling and Metropolis–Hastings
- Diagnostics of chain convergence

## 14 Example Bayesian Analyses

- Worked Bayesian analyses of the earlier data sets

## Appendix

- Postface, software notes and statistical tables
