- **Critical, fixed:** Period of Restoration F-001. The page said the ISO period includes building-code time. CP 00 30 10 12 F.3 excludes any increased period required by an ordinance or law. The bullet now transcribes the form.
- **Critical, open:** discrm-an-217 F-001. The key treats "the loss with protection working" as the PML and "sprinklers impaired" as the MFL. The National Alliance glossary and Korn (Risk & Insurance) define the PML with sprinkler impairment, and call the protection-working figure the NLE. IRMI is silent on protection assumptions. The stem needs rewriting.
- **Major, open:** Period of Restoration F-002. The first example counts code-upgrade weeks inside the period. Its 37-week answer survives, but the "paths tie" reasoning does not.

  - Underwriting Authority F-001 and Underwriting Policy F-001: practice claims that no source read this run supports.
