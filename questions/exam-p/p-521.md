An insurance company only sells auto insurance and property insurance. Total annual auto claims, $X$, follow a normal distribution with a mean of 15 and a standard deviation of 4. Total annual property claims, $Y$, follow a normal distribution with a mean of 20 and a standard deviation of 5.

The variables $X$ and $Y$ are independent.

Calculate the probability that the total overall claims, $X + Y$, is less than 45.

- A) $0.60$
- B) $0.71$
- C) $0.87$
- D) $0.94$
- E) $0.99$

## Explanation

A sum of independent normal variables is normal, so $X + Y$ needs only its mean and variance.

Means add:

$$
E[X+Y] = 15 + 20 = 35
$$

Variances add — the *standard deviations* do not:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X+Y) &= 4^2 + 5^2 = 41 \\
\text{SD}(X+Y)  &= \sqrt{41} = 6.40
\end{align*}
$$

Standardize:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X+Y < 45] &= P\left[Z < \frac{45 - 35}{6.40}\right] \\
            &= P[Z < 1.5617] \\
            &= \mathbf{0.9408}
\end{align*}
$$

Adding the standard deviations to get 9 instead of 6.40 would give $z = 1.11$ and a probability of 0.87, which is choice (C). Independent risks combine in quadrature: $\sqrt{41}$ is well below $4 + 5$, which is precisely the diversification benefit of writing two uncorrelated lines.
