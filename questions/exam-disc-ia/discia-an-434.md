An HO-3 with Coverage A of \$300,000 and Coverage C of \$150,000 carries ISO's earthquake endorsement (HO 04 54 10 00) with a 5% deductible and the exterior masonry veneer exclusion in force. An earthquake does \$60,000 of damage to the dwelling, of which \$10,000 is exterior brick veneer. How much does the insurer pay for the dwelling damage?

- A) \$27,500
- B) \$35,000
- C) \$45,000
- D) \$47,000

## Explanation

The earthquake deductible is the deductible percentage times the **greater** of the Coverage A and Coverage C limits, at least \$250. The excluded veneer's value is deducted before the deductible:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Deductible} &= 0.05 \times \max(300{,}000,\ 150{,}000) \\
&= 15{,}000 \\
\text{Paid} &= 60{,}000 - 10{,}000 - 15{,}000 \\
&= 35{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** bases the deductible on the sum of the Coverage A and C limits.
- **C** forgets the masonry veneer exclusion.
- **D** takes 5% of the loss as the deductible, rather than 5% of the larger limit.
