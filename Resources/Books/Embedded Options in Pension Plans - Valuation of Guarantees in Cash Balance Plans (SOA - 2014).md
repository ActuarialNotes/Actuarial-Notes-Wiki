![[Embedded Options in Pension Plans - Valuation of Guarantees in Cash Balance Plans (SOA - 2014) - Cover.svg]]

A January 2014 research report, sponsored by the SOA Pension Section and prepared by PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, that studies three embedded guarantees in cash balance plans: the return of participant pay credits at benefit commencement, the same with a fixed return, and a minimum annual interest rate credit. The first two are valued with a closed-form adaptation of the Black-Scholes formula and the third by Monte Carlo simulation under a Hull-White interest rate model. It is Part II of the SOA's research on embedded options in defined benefit pension plans.
