A Society of Actuaries study note of supplemental comments, dated March 2022, on the study note CP321-104-25, the American Academy of Actuaries' practice note Practices for Preparing Health Contract Reserves. According to the SOA's course strategy guide for CP 321, it comments on what has changed with ASU 2018-12, which was adopted after the Academy's note was written.
