Chapters 16 and 17 of Canadian Life & Health Insurance Law by H. E. Jones, supplied by the Society of Actuaries as a GH 201-C reading. According to the course strategy guide, it belongs to a set of readings that discuss the legal aspects surrounding group insurance plans in Canada.
