You are given the following table of spot rates to calculate the present value of a level four-year annuity-immediate:

| Length of investment in years | Spot rate |
|---|---|
| 1 | 4.25% |
| 2 | 4.50% |
| 3 | 4.75% |
| 4 | 5.00% |

Calculate the annual effective interest rate for this annuity.

- A) 4.52%
- B) 4.63%
- C) 4.74%
- D) 4.85%
- E) 4.87%

## Explanation

The annual effective interest rate for the annuity is the single rate $i$ at which the payments have the same present value as when each is discounted at the spot rate for its term. The size of the payment does not affect $i$, so suppose the level payment is 500:

$$
\begin{align*}
PV &= \frac{500}{1.0425} + \frac{500}{1.045^{2}} + \frac{500}{1.0475^{3}} + \frac{500}{1.05^{4}} \\
   &= 479.62 + 457.86 + 435.02 + 411.35 \\
   &= 1783.85
\end{align*}
$$

Solving for $i$:

$$
\begin{align*}
500\,a_{\overline{4}|i} &= 1783.85 \\
a_{\overline{4}|i} &= 3.5677 \\
i &= 4.74\%
\end{align*}
$$
