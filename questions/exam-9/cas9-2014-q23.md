A reinsurance contract is expected to make a single loss payment at the end of one year.

Given the following information:

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Loss distribution | Normal |
| Expected loss | \$4,000,000 |
| Standard deviation of loss | \$6,000,000 |
| Risk-free rate | 4% |
| Target yield | 12% |
| Standard deviation of yield rate | 15% |
| Normal Table z-Score at 99.9th Percentile | 3.0902 |

Using the swap method, calculate the assets required for the reinsurer to cover a loss at the 99.9th percentile.

### Explanation
The swap method requires both the loss safety constraint and the investment variance constraint on assets; the assets required are the larger of the two.

With $M_L = 4M$, $\sigma_L = 6M$, $r_f = 4\%$, $y = 12\%$, $\sigma_y = 15\%$ and $z = 3.0902$ at the 99.9th percentile (normal distribution), the loss safety level is

$$S_L = M_L + z\,\sigma_L = 4M + 3.0902(6M) = 22.5412M$$

**Loss safety constraint**

$$A \geq \frac{S_L - M_L}{1 + y} = \frac{22.5412M - 4M}{1.12} = 16{,}554{,}642$$

**Variance constraint**

$$A \geq \frac{\sigma_L}{\sigma_y} = \frac{6M}{.15} = 40{,}000{,}000$$

Choose the more restrictive constraint on assets: the assets required are **40,000,000**.

### Examiner Report
The candidates were expected to know how to use Kreps' swap method to calculate the assets a reinsurer needs to allocate to support writing a policy. Candidates generally performed well on this question, either getting full credit or having minor calculation error.

The most common errors made, aside from numerical calculation mistakes, were:

- Incorrect/omitted formula for the loss safety level, S
- Use of the risk free rate in the calculation of the assets for the loss safety constraint
- Calculating the risk loads for the loss safety/variance constraints, instead of the assets
- Adding the premium to the final allocated assets to give funds invested
- Selecting the loss safety constraint as dominant instead of the variance constraint

A small percentage of candidates appeared to be confused by the final sentence of the question, which asked "Using the swap method, calculate the assets required for the reinsurer to cover a loss at the 99.9th percentile." The swap method requires calculation of both the loss safety and investment variance constraints, and then using the higher of those assets. These candidates typically only calculated the loss safety constraint, or calculated both, and then indicated the question asked for the loss safety constraint as the answer.
