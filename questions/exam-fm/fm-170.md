Fund J begins with a balance of 20,000 and earns an annual effective interest rate of 6.5%. At the end of each year, the interest earned and an additional 1000 is withdrawn from the fund so that by the end of the 20th year, the fund is depleted.

The annual withdrawals of interest and principal are deposited into Fund K, which earns an annual effective rate of 8.25%. At the end of the 20th year, the accumulated value of Fund K is $X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 39,332
- B) 54,818
- C) 84,593
- D) 86,902
- E) 97,631

## Explanation

Fund J's balance at the start of year $k$ is $20{,}000 - 1000(k-1)$, so the withdrawal at the end of year $k$ is the interest earned plus 1000:

$$
\begin{align*}
W_k &= 1000 + 0.065\bigl(20{,}000 - 1000(k-1)\bigr) \\
    &= 1000 + 1300 - 65(k-1) \\
    &= 1000 + 65(21-k)
\end{align*}
$$

So Fund K receives 1000 at the end of each year and also receives interest payments of $1300, 1235, 1170, \ldots, 65$ — a decreasing annuity of $65 \times (20, 19, \ldots, 1)$. The accumulated value at 8.25% is

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= 1000s_{\overline{20}|0.0825} + 65(Ds)_{\overline{20}|0.0825} \\
  &= 1000(47.0491) + 65\cdot\frac{20(1.0825)^{20} - s_{\overline{20}|0.0825}}{0.0825} \\
  &= 47{,}049.1 + 65\cdot\frac{97.6311 - 47.0491}{0.0825} \\
  &= 47{,}049.1 + 65(613.115) \\
  &= 86{,}902
\end{align*}
$$
