An insurance company sells an automobile policy that pays 80% of any losses that exceed the deductible of 500. The loss amounts (in units of 1000) follow an exponential distribution with mean 6.

Calculate the probability that the policy payment on a randomly selected loss exceeds 5000.

- A) $0.024$
- B) $0.318$
- C) $0.325$
- D) $0.675$
- E) $0.682$

## Explanation

Work in units of 1000, matching the loss distribution: the deductible is 0.5 and the payment threshold is 5.

The payment on a loss $X$ exceeding the deductible is

$$
Y = 0.8(X - 0.5)
$$

Translate the event about $Y$ into one about $X$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.8(X - 0.5) &> 5 \\
X - 0.5 &> 6.25 \\
X &> 6.75
\end{align*}
$$

Now apply the exponential survival function with mean 6:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 6.75] &= e^{-6.75/6} \\
            &= e^{-1.125} \\
            &= \mathbf{0.3247}
\end{align*}
$$

Both adjustments matter and in this order: divide by the 80% coinsurance *first*, then add the deductible back. Reversing them — computing $0.8(5) + 0.5 = 4.5$ — gives $e^{-0.75} = 0.472$, and ignoring the coinsurance entirely gives $P[X > 5.5] = e^{-0.9167} = 0.400$. Neither is offered, but both are the natural slips.
