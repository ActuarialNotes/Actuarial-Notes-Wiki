Given the following:

| Claim ID | Accident Date | Transaction Date | Gross Amount Paid on Transaction Date (\$) | Gross Ending Case Outstanding (\$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | May 30, 2015 | December 24, 2015 | 1,000 | 550 |
| A | May 30, 2015 | August 1, 2016 | 500 | 225 |
| A | May 30, 2015 | June 1, 2017 | 725 | 0 |
| B | August 28, 2015 | August 29, 2015 | 300 | 1,050 |
| B | August 28, 2015 | February 6, 2016 | 600 | 375 |
| B | August 28, 2015 | June 14, 2016 | 450 | 150 |
| C | April 21, 2016 | April 25, 2016 | 1,200 | 575 |
| C | April 21, 2016 | March 3, 2017 | 700 | 250 |
| C | April 21, 2016 | December 1, 2017 | 200 | 0 |
| D | October 11, 2016 | October 12, 2016 | 400 | 900 |
| D | October 11, 2016 | May 17, 2017 | 800 | 625 |

| | |
|---|---|
| 60% | Quota share ceded percentage for reinsurance that applies to claims occurring in 2015. |
| \$1,500 | Per claim excess of loss retention for reinsurance that applies to claims occurring in 2016. |

## Part a (0.25 point)

Calculate calendar year 2015 reported claims, gross of reinsurance.

### Answer

\$2,900

### Explanation

Calendar year reported claims = payments made during the year + change in case outstanding during the year. Only claims A and B have 2015 transactions, and both case reserves start from zero:

$$\text{Paid} = 1{,}000 + 300 = 1{,}300$$

$$\Delta \text{Case} = (550 - 0) + (1{,}050 - 0) = 1{,}600$$

$$\text{CY 2015 Reported} = 1{,}300 + 1{,}600 = 2{,}900$$

### Examiner Report

Candidates were expected to understand the basic mechanics of case outstanding, paid claims, reported claims in relation to both Accident Year and Calendar Year.  The candidate was also expected to demonstrate basic knowledge of reinsurance.

Candidates were expected to understand how to derive total reported claims for a specific Calendar Year when given a list of paid and case outstanding claim data.

Common mistakes include:

- Calculation errors when summing paid and change in case
- Incorporating reinsurance calculations into the final answer

## Part b (0.75 point)

Calculate calendar year 2016 paid claims, net of reinsurance.

### Answer

\$2,220

### Explanation

Claims A and B occurred in 2015, so the 60% quota share applies and the company retains 40%. Claims C and D occurred in 2016 and are subject to a \$1,500 per-claim excess of loss retention.

| Claim | Gross Paid in CY 2016 | Ceded | Net Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 500 | $500 \times 0.60 = 300$ | 200 |
| B | $600 + 450 = 1{,}050$ | $1{,}050 \times 0.60 = 630$ | 420 |
| C | 1,200 | 0 (cumulative paid \$1,200 < \$1,500 retention) | 1,200 |
| D | 400 | 0 | 400 |
| **Total** | | | **2,220** |

### Examiner Report

Candidates were expected to understand how to derive total gross paid claims for a specific Calendar Year and then apply both quota share and excess of loss reinsurance where appropriate.

Common mistakes include:

- Calculation errors when summing net paid claims
- Forgetting to incorporate reinsurance and only calculating gross paid claims
- Multiplying gross paid claims by 0.6 rather than 0.4

## Part c (0.75 point)

Calculate calendar year 2016 reported claims, gross of reinsurance.

### Answer

\$3,400

### Explanation

Reported = paid during 2016 + change in case outstanding during 2016. Note that the case reserves on claims A and B *decrease* over the year as the payments are made.

| Claim | Paid in 2016 | Case at 12/31/2016 | Case at 12/31/2015 | Δ Case | Reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 500 | 225 | 550 | -325 | 175 |
| B | 1,050 | 150 | 1,050 | -900 | 150 |
| C | 1,200 | 575 | 0 | +575 | 1,775 |
| D | 400 | 900 | 0 | +900 | 1,300 |
| **Total** | 3,150 | | | +250 | **3,400** |

### Examiner Report

Candidates were expected to understand how to calculate reported claims for a specific Calendar Year.  Candidates did not receive full credit if they did not take into account change in case reserves for claims A and B.

Common mistakes include:

- Not including change in case reserves when calculating reported amounts for claims A and B.
- Unnecessarily applying reinsurance to paid or reported claims.
- Calculation errors in summing up total reported for claims A or B when taking case reserves into account.

## Part d (1 point)

Calculate calendar year 2017 reported claims, net of reinsurance.

### Answer

\$400

### Explanation

**Claim A** (2015, quota share): gross reported in 2017 is $725 + (0 - 225) = 500$. The company retains 40%:

$$500 \times 0.40 = 200$$

**Claim B**: no transactions in 2017, so \$0.

**Claim C** (2016, excess of loss): gross reported in 2017 is $(700 + 200) + (0 - 575) = 325$. Cumulative gross incurred reaches $1{,}200 + 700 + 200 = 2{,}100$, so total ceded is $2{,}100 - 1{,}500 = 600$; at the end of 2016 the incurred was $1{,}200 + 575 = 1{,}775$, already ceding $275$. The CY 2017 cession is therefore $600 - 275 = 325$:

$$325 - 325 = 0$$

**Claim D** (2016, excess of loss): gross reported in 2017 is $800 + (625 - 900) = 525$. Cumulative gross incurred is $400 + 800 + 625 = 1{,}825$, so ceded to date is $1{,}825 - 1{,}500 = 325$; at the end of 2016 the incurred was $1{,}300 < 1{,}500$, so nothing had been ceded. The whole \$325 is a CY 2017 cession:

$$525 - 325 = 200$$

$$\text{CY 2017 Reported, Net} = 200 + 0 + 0 + 200 = 400$$

### Examiner Report

Candidates were expected to understand how to calculate total reported claims in a given Calendar Year net of both Quota Share as well as Excess of Loss reinsurance.

Common mistakes include:

- Not applying or applying the wrong Quota Share to claim A.
- Not capping claims C and D at the 1,500 excess of loss limit.
- Incorrectly applying the 1,500 excess of loss limit on an aggregate claim basis.
