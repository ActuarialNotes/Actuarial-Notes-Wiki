A June 2020 Society of Actuaries report, sponsored by the Reinsurance Section and the Product Development Section, and a sequel to Life Reinsurance Treaty Construction (2013). It compiles and analyzes the recapture provisions of life reinsurance treaties from the perspectives of participants gathered by questionnaire, interviews and a roundtable, and says why those provisions matter to direct writers and to reinsurers.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-341 (SOA)|Exam CP-341]] — Outcomes 2a; the whole report.

## Report Usage

## 1 Executive Summary
- A recapture provision defines the obligation or an option of a cedant at a point in time or during a time period; provisions related to recapture may include changes in retention, insolvency, changes in legal control, changes in rating, changes in financial or capital ratios, and increases in premium rates.
