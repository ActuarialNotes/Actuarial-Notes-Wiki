A gardener models his strawberry ($S$) / blueberry ($B$) harvest with the following joint probability distribution.

| $S$ \ $B$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 0.07 | 0.06 | 0.06 | 0.05 | 0.01 |
| **2** | 0.07 | 0.10 | 0.08 | 0.05 | 0.03 |
| **3** | 0.04 | 0.05 | 0.06 | 0.05 | 0.04 |
| **4** | 0.01 | 0.02 | 0.02 | 0.07 | 0.06 |

The strawberry harvest is 2 units.

Calculate the conditional variance of the blueberry harvest.

- A) $1.23$
- B) $1.51$
- C) $2.00$
- D) $2.46$
- E) $6.06$

## Explanation

The $S = 2$ row is $0.07, 0.10, 0.08, 0.05, 0.03$, totalling $P[S = 2] = 0.33$. Dividing through:

$$
P(B \mid S = 2) = \begin{cases}
0.07/0.33 = 0.2121, & b = 1 \\
0.10/0.33 = 0.3030, & b = 2 \\
0.08/0.33 = 0.2424, & b = 3 \\
0.05/0.33 = 0.1515, & b = 4 \\
0.03/0.33 = 0.0909, & b = 5
\end{cases}
$$

The first two moments are

$$
\begin{align*}
E(B \mid S = 2) &= 1(0.2121) + 2(0.3030) + 3(0.2424) + 4(0.1515) + 5(0.0909) \\
                &= 2.60606 \\
E(B^2 \mid S = 2) &= 1(0.2121) + 4(0.3030) + 9(0.2424) + 16(0.1515) + 25(0.0909) \\
                  &= 8.30303
\end{align*}
$$

so

$$
\text{Var}(B \mid S = 2) = 8.30303 - (2.60606)^2 = \mathbf{1.5115}
$$

Choice (A), 1.23, is the conditional *standard deviation*, $\sqrt{1.5115} = 1.229$. The question asks for the variance, so 1.5115 is the finished answer — taking the root is one step too many.
