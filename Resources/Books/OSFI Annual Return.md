The **annual supervisory supplement** to the [[Canadian Annual Return]] — the exhibits OSFI needs once a year and does not collect quarterly. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objectives C1–C4. The syllabus assigns **pp. 10.60 and 60.45**.

## The two assigned pages

- **10.60 — Summary of selected financial data for five years.** The five-year trend of the insurer's key figures. It is where deterioration shows before it shows anywhere else, which is why a supervisor reads it first.
- **60.45 — Net ultimate claims and adjustment expenses: claims development.** The **claims development exhibit** — successive estimates of net ultimate claims for each accident year, restated at each subsequent valuation. This is the Canadian analogue of U.S. Schedule P (see [[CAS Financial Reporting]] ch. 15), and the return's principal check on [[Runoff|reserve adequacy]]: a row that steps consistently upward is adverse development.

## Points worth noticing

- **The discount rate pages (60.90, 60.95)** exist because [[IFRS 17]] lets each insurer choose its own curve; without disclosure, two insurers' liabilities are not comparable. See [[CIA Discount Rates]].
- **The reinsurance interrogatories (70.90) and the non-traditional risk mitigation exhibit (70.95)** are the return's [[Finite Reinsurance|finite reinsurance]] screen — the questions designed to surface arrangements that transfer less risk than their form suggests (see [[Freihaut and Vendetti]], [[OSFI Reinsurance]]).
- **Encumbered assets (10.42)** matter to solvency because a pledged asset is not available to policyholders in a wind-up.
