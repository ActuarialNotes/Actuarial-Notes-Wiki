A plumbing contractor's business auto liability losses have risen, and the underwriter learns that the contractor lets any employee drive its vans without checking their driving histories. Which condition of renewal most directly addresses the cause?

- A) A higher physical damage deductible on the vans
- B) Motor vehicle record checks and written standards for who may drive
- C) Switching physical damage from comprehensive to specified causes of loss
- D) A lower liability limit, so that each claim costs the insurer less

## Explanation

Driver selection is central to auto liability underwriting. Motor vehicle records show each driver's convictions and accidents, and written standards keep unfit drivers out of the vans, which also reduces the contractor's exposure for negligently entrusting a vehicle to an unfit driver. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — The deductible changes what the insurer pays for damage to the vans, not the liability to others.
- **C** — The choice of physical damage coverage doesn't affect liability claims at all.
- **D** — A lower limit caps the insurer's payment but leaves the cause, unscreened drivers, in place, and the contractor bears the difference.
