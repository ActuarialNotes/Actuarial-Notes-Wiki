Two borrowers obtain loans at the same time. Each loan is for the same amount and is repaid with level end-of-month payments.

The first borrower is charged a monthly effective interest rate of $i$ and makes payments of $P$ for $k$ months to pay off the loan, where $k$ is a positive integer.

The second borrower is charged a monthly effective interest rate of $j$ and makes payments of 120 for $5k$ months to pay off the loan.

You are given that $0 < j < 0.04$ and that $i = (1+j)^5 - 1$.

Determine which statement about $P$ is true.

- A) $400 < P \le 450$
- B) $450 < P \le 500$
- C) $500 < P \le 550$
- D) $550 < P \le 600$
- E) $600 < P \le 650$

## Explanation

Both loans are for the same amount $L$:
$$L = P\,a_{\overline{k}|i} = 120\,a_{\overline{5k}|j}$$

Since $1 + i = (1+j)^5$, discounting one period at $i$ is the same as discounting five periods at $j$: $v_i^{\,m} = v_j^{\,5m}$. Group the second borrower's $5k$ payments into $k$ blocks of five. The block ending at time $5m$ (in $j$-periods) is worth $120\,s_{\overline{5}|j}$ at that time, so
$$
\begin{align*}
120\,a_{\overline{5k}|j} &= 120\,s_{\overline{5}|j}\sum_{m=1}^{k} v_j^{\,5m} \\
&= 120\,s_{\overline{5}|j}\sum_{m=1}^{k} v_i^{\,m} \\
&= 120\,s_{\overline{5}|j}\,a_{\overline{k}|i}
\end{align*}
$$

Setting this equal to $P\,a_{\overline{k}|i}$:
$$P = 120\,s_{\overline{5}|j} = 120\cdot\frac{(1+j)^5 - 1}{j}$$

which does not depend on $k$. The factor $s_{\overline{5}|j}$ increases with $j$, from 5 as $j \to 0$ to $s_{\overline{5}|0.04} = 5.41632$ at $j = 0.04$. So for $0 < j < 0.04$,
$$600 < P < 120(5.41632) = 649.96$$

and $600 < P \le 650$.
