A CAS research paper on emerging practice in Asia in implementing the [[Premium Allocation Approach]] of [[IFRS 17]] for property and casualty insurers, and the challenges practitioners face. Supplemented by an industry survey of over 60 P&C insurers in territories already live with IFRS 17, including Hong Kong, Singapore and Malaysia, it covers PAA eligibility, current estimate liabilities, discounting, the risk adjustment, and the loss component and loss recovery. This is Part 1 of the paper.
