A basic introduction to [[IFRS 17]] Insurance Contracts with a focus on its key implications for property and casualty actuaries. IFRS 17 sets the principles for the recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure of insurance contracts, so that an entity gives relevant information that faithfully represents them. The note covers definitions, scope, the level of aggregation, the general measurement model and its variations, recognition and derecognition, and presentation, with an illustrative example of portfolio aggregation and of the LRC and LIC under the premium allocation approach, provided as an Excel workbook.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6I (CAS)|Exam 6I]] — objectives C2, D4–D5; candidates are responsible for the Excel illustrations.
