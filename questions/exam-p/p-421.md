The lifetimes of televisions of a certain model are exponentially distributed with a median of 2.7 years.

Calculate the 87.5th percentile of the lifetimes for these televisions.

- A) $3.08$
- B) $4.73$
- C) $8.10$
- D) $10.80$
- E) $19.68$

## Explanation

Recover the mean $\beta$ from the median:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.5 &= P[X < 2.7] = 1 - e^{-2.7/\beta} \\
\frac{2.7}{\beta} &= \ln 2 = 0.6931 \\
\beta &= 3.8953
\end{align*}
$$

Now solve for the 87.5th percentile $p$:

$$
\begin{align*}
0.875 &= 1 - e^{-p/3.8953} \\
0.125 &= e^{-p/3.8953} \\
\frac{p}{3.8953} &= -\ln(0.125) = 2.0794 \\
p &= \mathbf{8.10}
\end{align*}
$$

There is a shortcut worth seeing: $1 - 0.875 = 1/8 = (1/2)^3$, so the 87.5th percentile is exactly **three medians**, $3(2.7) = 8.1$. Each additional median of elapsed time halves the surviving fraction — the memoryless property expressed in percentiles. Choice (D), 10.80, is four medians, which is the 93.75th percentile, since $(1/2)^4 = 1/16$.
