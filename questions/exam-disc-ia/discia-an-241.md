Under a state law based on the NAIC Producer Licensing Model Act, which of these people needs an insurance producer license?

- A) An insurer's salaried claims adjuster who earns no commission
- B) An agency employee who quotes auto policies to callers, recommends limits and earns a commission per sale
- C) A salaried risk manager who advises her own employer on its insurance and earns no commission
- D) An insurer's salaried underwriter who decides whether to accept applications

## Explanation

No one may sell, solicit or negotiate insurance without a license for the line. Quoting policies, recommending limits and being paid a commission on each sale is selling, soliciting and negotiating, so the agency employee needs a **producer license**.

- **A** and **D** are insurer employees whose work is claims or underwriting and who receive no commission, which the model exempts from producer licensing. (A state may still require an adjuster license.)
- **C** is a salaried employee advising her own employer about its insurance, without selling or receiving a commission, which is also exempt.
