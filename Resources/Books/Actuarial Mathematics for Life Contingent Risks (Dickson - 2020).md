![[Actuarial Mathematics for Life Contingent Risks (Dickson - 2020) - Cover.svg]]

A textbook on the modern mathematics of life insurance, suitable for advanced undergraduate and graduate students of actuarial science, trainee actuaries preparing for professional examinations and life insurance practitioners. Cambridge University Press describes the third edition as substantially updated, with topics including multiple state models, cash-flow projection methods and option theory, numerous exam-style questions, and extensive use of Excel. It is a hardback of 782 pages with 300 exercises and 160 worked examples, and the publisher calls it the required text for the Society of Actuaries' LTAM exam.
