A ten-year mortgage is to be repaid with annual level payments at the end of the year. The mortgage is paid assuming an annual effective interest rate of 6%.

Calculate the Macaulay duration of this mortgage.

- A) 4.74
- B) 5.02
- C) 7.36
- D) 10.00
- E) There is insufficient information to calculate the Macaulay duration.

## Explanation

A mortgage repaid with level annual payments at the end of each year is a level annuity-immediate. With each payment equal to $P$, the Macaulay duration is the average of the payment times weighted by the present values of the payments. The payment $P$ cancels, so the amount of the mortgage is not needed:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC} &= \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{10} t\,P v^{t}}{\sum_{t=1}^{10} P v^{t}} \\
        &= \frac{(Ia)_{\overline{10}|0.06}}{a_{\overline{10}|0.06}} \\
        &= \frac{36.96241}{7.36009} \\
        &= 5.02201
\end{align*}
$$
