A named insured has Part B medical payments coverage on his PAP (ISO PP 00 01 09 18). In separate accidents during the year, each of these people is hurt. Whose medical expenses does his Part B pay?

- A) A pedestrian he strikes with his covered auto while turning at an intersection
- B) His wife, riding as a passenger on a friend's motorcycle when it crashes
- C) His son, who lives with him, hurt driving a car the son owns and insures separately
- D) His daughter, who lives with him, struck by a stranger's car while walking

## Explanation

Part B insures the named insured and family members while occupying, or as pedestrians struck by, a motor vehicle designed mainly for public roads, and anyone else only while occupying the covered auto. The daughter is a family member struck as a pedestrian, so her expenses are paid without regard to fault. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — a pedestrian the insured strikes is not an insured under his Part B; her claim is against his Part A liability coverage.
- **B** — Part B excludes injury while occupying a motorized vehicle with fewer than four wheels.
- **C** — Part B excludes injury to a family member in a vehicle, other than the covered auto, that the family member owns.
