In the risk management process described by ISO 31000 (2018), which activities make up risk assessment?

- A) Risk identification, risk analysis and risk evaluation
- B) Risk analysis, risk evaluation and risk treatment
- C) Establishing scope, context and criteria, then identifying risks
- D) Monitoring and review, together with recording and reporting

## Explanation

In ISO 31000, **risk assessment** is the umbrella for three activities: **risk identification, risk analysis and risk evaluation**. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Risk treatment is a separate element of the process that follows assessment: selecting and implementing responses.
- **C** — Scope, context and criteria is its own element, which tailors the process to the organization before assessment begins.
- **D** — Monitoring and review, and recording and reporting, are separate elements that run alongside the others.

Note that in the traditional four-step process, "assessment" means only measuring risks once identified, so it is worth reading which usage a question intends.
