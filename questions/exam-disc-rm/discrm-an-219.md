An underwriter is estimating the business income exposure of a pottery maker. Which fact bears on the **maximum length of the period of restoration**, rather than on the size of the loss during that period?

- A) Most of the year's sales come in the six weeks before the holidays
- B) Lost production could be made up with overtime after the plant reopens
- C) The custom kiln at the center of production has a nine-month lead time
- D) Most of the company's operating expenses would continue during a shutdown

## Explanation

The maximum period of restoration depends on how long the property takes to restore: the building's size and construction, permits and building codes, the season, and lead times for specialized machinery. A custom kiln with a nine-month lead time can set the critical path of the restoration. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Seasonality changes how much income is lost depending on when the loss strikes, not how long restoration takes.
- **B** — The ability to make up production afterwards reduces the loss, but it doesn't shorten the restoration.
- **D** — Continuing expenses are part of what is lost each month, so they affect the size of the loss.
