An article by four Boston Consulting Group authors, first published in MIT Sloan Management Review and dated March 10, 2025 on BCG's site. It opens by observing that politics increasingly generates surprises that reverberate in boardrooms around the world, argues that political risk differs from other risk factors, and offers a six-step approach to a political risk strategy.
