A contractor has a CGL policy with a \$1,000,000 each occurrence limit and a \$5,000,000 following form excess liability policy above it. A \$300,000 claim arises from a job abroad, outside the CGL's coverage territory, so the CGL does not cover it. Which statement about the excess policy is correct?

- A) It pays nothing, because it covers only what the underlying policy covers
- B) It pays the \$300,000 from the first dollar, because the underlying policy does not respond
- C) It pays \$275,000, after the insured retains a \$25,000 self-insured retention
- D) It requires the CGL to pay, because buying the excess layer extends the CGL's territory

## Explanation

A following form excess policy is subject to the same terms as the underlying policy, so it covers a claim only if the underlying policy does. The CGL excludes this claim, so the excess policy pays nothing, and the contractor bears it.

- **B** and **C** describe an umbrella, which also covers some claims the underlying policies exclude, above a self-insured retention. An excess policy only adds limits.
- **D** reverses the relationship: the excess policy follows the underlying policy's terms, it does not change them.
