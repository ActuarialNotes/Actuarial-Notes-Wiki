Part 6000 of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries Standards of Practice (publication 223179), the practice-specific standards for an actuary's advice on non-pension employee future benefit plans, effective February 1, 2024. It covers advice on funding or funded status (Section 6200), full or partial wind-up valuations (Section 6300) and financial reporting of non-pension employee future benefits costs (Section 6400), each with its reporting requirements for an external user report.

## 6100 Scope
- Part 1000 applies to work within the scope of Part 6000.
- Section 6200 applies to advice on funding, funded status, financial position or financial condition; Section 6300 to advice on the wind-up, in full or in part, of a non-pension employee future benefit plan; Section 6400 to advice on financial reporting of a plan's costs and obligations.
- Sections 6200 through 6400 apply to plans providing benefits such as long-term employee benefits, short-term employee benefits that accumulate or vest, post-employment life insurance or health care, and termination benefits, whether funded or not.

## 6200 Advice on the Funding, Funded Status, Financial Condition or Financial Position of a Non-Pension Employee Future Benefit Plan
- 6210 General
    - The advice should take account of the circumstances affecting the work; the actuary should select an actuarial cost method and, where applicable, an asset valuation method consistent with them.
    - The advice should take account of the plan's benefit provisions at the calculation date (a pending amendment may be reflected) and of all relevant data, including historical benefit utilization experience.
    - For a plan pre-funded in some manner, the actuary should select best estimate assumptions, or best estimate assumptions modified to incorporate margins for adverse deviations to the extent required by the terms of an appropriate engagement.
- 6220 Advice on Funding or Funded Status
- 6230 Reporting: External User Report
    - The report should describe significant terms of the engagement, include the calculation date, the report date and the next calculation date, and describe the sources of membership data, plan provisions, assets and historical benefit utilization.
    - The report should provide four statements of opinion, in the same section and in a stated order, and be sufficiently detailed to enable another actuary to examine the reasonableness of the valuation.

## 6300 Full or Partial Wind-Up Valuation
- 6310 General
    - The advice should assume the plan is being wound up at the calculation date and take account of subsequent events up to the cut-off date.
    - Assets, if any, should be valued at liquidation value.
    - The actuary should select an explicit assumption regarding the expenses of wind-up unless expenses are expected not to be paid from the plan's assets.
- 6320 Reporting: External User Report
    - If a previous external user report was prepared with respect to the wind-up, the actuary should describe and quantify the gains and losses between the prior calculation date and the calculation date.

## 6400 Financial Reporting of Non-Pension Employee Future Benefits Costs
- 6410 General
    - The actuary should use assumptions and methods for the value of assets, if any, and non-pension employee future benefit obligations that are appropriate to the basis of financial reporting in the financial statements and consistent with the terms of an appropriate engagement and the circumstances affecting the work.
- 6420 Reporting: External User Report
