Twelve independent bakeries agree that whenever one of them suffers an equipment breakdown, all twelve will share the repair cost equally. No insurer is involved. Which description fits this arrangement?

- A) It is insurance, because it combines pooling with risk transfer
- B) It is risk transfer without pooling, because each loss leaves the bakery that suffers it
- C) It is avoidance, because no single bakery bears a breakdown alone
- D) It is pooling without transfer of the risk to a third party

## Explanation

Insurance requires two elements: **pooling**, which spreads losses across the group and makes them more predictable, and **transfer** of the risk to a third party, the insurer. Here the members pay each other a share of every loss, which is pooling, but no outside party takes on the risk; the members still bear all the losses among themselves. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Without a third party accepting the risk, the arrangement lacks the transfer element of insurance.
- **B** — The cost is shared among the members, not shifted to an outside party, so it is pooling rather than transfer.
- **C** — Avoidance gives up the activity; the bakeries keep their equipment and its exposure to breakdown.
