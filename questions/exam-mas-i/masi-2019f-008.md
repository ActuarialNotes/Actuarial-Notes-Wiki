You are given:

- Mary plays a game repeatedly
- Each game ends with her either winning or losing
- Mary's chances of winning her next game depends on the outcome of the prior game
  - $P[\text{Winning after a win}] = \min(80\%, P[\text{Winning prior game}] + 10\%)$
  - $P[\text{Winning after a loss}] = 40\%$
- Mary just played her $10^{\text{th}}$ game and lost.

Calculate the probability that Mary will lose her $13^{\text{th}}$ game.

- A) Less than 40%
- B) At least 40%, but less than 45%
- C) At least 45%, but less than 50%
- D) At least 50%, but less than 55%
- E) At least 55%

## Explanation

The chance of winning depends on the whole current winning streak, not just the last result: after a loss it is 40%, and each further consecutive win adds 10 points (capped at 80%). So after a loss the next game is won with probability 0.4, after one win following a loss with 0.5, and after two straight wins with 0.6.

Game 10 was lost, so game 11 is won with probability 0.4. Trace games 11 and 12 and the resulting chance of **losing** game 13:

| Game 11 | Game 12 | Probability of path | $P(\text{win } 13)$ | $P(\text{lose } 13)$ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| W | W | $0.4 \times 0.5 = 0.20$ | 0.6 | 0.4 |
| W | L | $0.4 \times 0.5 = 0.20$ | 0.4 | 0.6 |
| L | W | $0.6 \times 0.4 = 0.24$ | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| L | L | $0.6 \times 0.6 = 0.36$ | 0.4 | 0.6 |

$$
\begin{align*}
P(\text{lose } 13) &= 0.20(0.4) + 0.20(0.6) + 0.24(0.5) + 0.36(0.6) \\
&= 0.080 + 0.120 + 0.120 + 0.216 \\
&= 0.536
\end{align*}
$$

The trap is treating this as a two-state win/loss chain with $P(\text{win} \mid \text{win}) = 0.5$ throughout: that misprices the W–W path and gives 0.556.

This falls in $[50\%, 55\%)$, so the answer is **D**.
