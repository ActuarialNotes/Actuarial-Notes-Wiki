A better practice guide, tabled as Report 28 of 2020-21 and dated 14 June 2021, developed by the Western Australian Office of the Auditor General to promote better practice principles for preparing the financial statements of the state's public sector entities. It sets out the reporting framework, seven principles for better practice financial statement preparation, the challenges facing smaller entities and a toolkit of fifteen tools. The first tool is an example risk analysis for financial statements, which sets out four risk assessment steps.
