The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries providing a written statement of actuarial opinion on property/casualty loss, loss adjustment expense, or other reserves. This revision (Doc. No. 213, adopted March 2024, effective October 1, 2024) renames the standard to add "other reserves" and incorporates and expands the disclosures that the NAIC Property/Casualty Annual Statement Instructions require.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6U (CAS)|Exam 6U]] — objective C7; the standard as revised in March 2024 (the content outline cites it as an online publication).

## Transmittal Memorandum
- The ASB adopted ASOP No. 36 in March 2000 and revised it in 2010 to remove guidance redundant with ASOP No. 43 and to keep the two consistent.
- When the standard was revised in 2010 the NAIC required the actuary to comment only if there was a significant risk of material adverse deviation; the NAIC now requires the actuary always to comment on it.

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
    - Applies to a written [[Statement of Actuarial Opinion|statement of actuarial opinion]] on the loss, loss adjustment expense, or other reserves of an insurance company or other property/casualty risk financing system that is prepared to comply with the NAIC Property/Casualty Annual Statement Instructions, is otherwise prescribed by applicable law, or is represented by the actuary as complying with the standard.
    - "Other reserves" include retrospective reinsurance premium reserves, unearned premium reserves for property/casualty long-duration contracts and for extended reporting endorsements, premium deficiency reserves and contingent commission reserves.
