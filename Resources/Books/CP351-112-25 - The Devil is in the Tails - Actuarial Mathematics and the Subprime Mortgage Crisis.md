A paper, published in ASTIN Bulletin 40(1) in 2010, that answers the criticism of mathematics and mathematical models after the 2007-2008 financial crisis through a discussion of actuarial models used to price credit derivatives, focusing on the Gaussian copula model and its drawbacks. It gives a synopsis of the crisis and a brief introduction to common credit derivatives, takes a closer look at risk management issues in part of the insurance industry, and recounts the events at American International Group during the crisis.
