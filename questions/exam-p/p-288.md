For a pregnant woman, a certain test will give the outcome "not pregnant" with probability 0.10. For a non-pregnant woman, the test will give the outcome "pregnant" with probability 0.20. Of women who take the test, 20% are pregnant.

Calculate the probability that a woman is pregnant, given her test outcome is "pregnant."  

- A) 0.10
- B) 0.20
- C) 0.50
- D) 0.53
- E) 0.90

## Explanation

$P(\text{positive} \mid \text{pregnant}) = 1 - 0.10 = 0.90$.

$P(\text{positive} \mid \text{not pregnant}) = 0.20$.

By Bayes' Theorem:

$$
\begin{align*}
P(\text{pregnant} \mid \text{positive}) &= \frac{0.90 \times 0.20}{0.90 \times 0.20 + 0.20 \times 0.80} \\
                                        &= \frac{0.18}{0.18+0.16} \\
                                        &= \frac{0.18}{0.34} \\
                                        &\approx \mathbf{0.529 \approx 0.53}
\end{align*}
$$
