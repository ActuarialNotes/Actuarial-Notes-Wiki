A chapter of the IAA Risk Book, developed and approved by the Insurance Regulation Committee of the International Actuarial Association (version 1.0, February 2020). It builds on prior work by the IAA and the CRO Forum with a focus on the appropriate application of the stress testing process, covering definitions, purposes and process, governance, limitations and examples of stress tests and scenario analyses.
