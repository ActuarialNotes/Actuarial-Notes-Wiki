Losses follow a memoryless distribution with mean 1,000. Each loss is insured and subject to a deductible of 500.

Calculate the average insurance payment made on losses that exceed the deductible.

- A) Less than 500
- B) At least 500, but less than 700
- C) At least 700, but less than 900
- D) At least 900, but less than 1100
- E) At least 1100

## Explanation

The only continuous memoryless distribution is the exponential, so losses are $X \sim \text{Exponential}(\theta = 1000)$. On a loss that exceeds the deductible, the payment is the excess $X - 500$, and memorylessness says that, given $X > 500$, this excess has the same distribution as $X$ itself:

$$
\Pr(X - 500 > y \mid X > 500) = \frac{e^{-(500+y)/1000}}{e^{-500/1000}} = e^{-y/1000}.
$$

So the payment per payment is again exponential with mean 1,000:

$$
E[X - 500 \mid X > 500] = 1000.
$$

This falls in $[900, 1100)$, so the answer is **D**.
