A homeowner with an unendorsed HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) has a finished basement, and twice in recent years heavy rain has made the municipal sewer back up into neighbors' basements. Which endorsement would most directly treat this exposure?

- A) Water back-up and sump discharge or overflow coverage
- B) Ordinance or law increased amount of coverage
- C) Personal property replacement cost
- D) Specified additional amount of insurance for Coverage A

## Explanation

Section I's water exclusion removes water that backs up through sewers or drains or overflows from a sump. ISO's water back-up and sump discharge or overflow endorsement (HO 04 95) adds that cause of loss back, up to a stated amount that is separate from the Coverage A to D limits. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** raises the amount available for building code costs after a covered loss; it doesn't make a sewer back-up covered.
- **C** changes how covered contents losses are valued, not which causes are covered.
- **D** adds dwelling insurance for a covered loss that exceeds the limit; the back-up loss would still be excluded.
