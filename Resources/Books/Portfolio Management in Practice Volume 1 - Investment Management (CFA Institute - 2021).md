![[Portfolio Management in Practice Volume 1 - Investment Management (CFA Institute - 2021) - Cover.svg]]

A comprehensive overview of investment management for students and industry professionals, and the first volume in the CFA Institute's Portfolio Management in Practice series. It explores the full portfolio management process, with coverage of forming capital market expectations, the principles of the asset allocation process, determining investment strategies within each asset class, and integrating considerations specific to high net worth individuals or institutions into chosen strategies.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam INV-101 (SOA)|Exam INV-101]] — Ch. 11 Overview of Equity Portfolio Management, sections 1-2 only (outcome 1b) and as background only (outcome 2g); Ch. 5 Overview of Asset Allocation, sections 2-8 excluding subsection 6.3 (outcomes 2a, 2c); Ch. 6 Principles of Asset Allocation, sections 2-3 and 5-7 (outcome 2d); Ch. 9 Overview of Fixed-Income Portfolio Management (outcome 2e); Ch. 10 Liability-Driven and Index-Based Strategies, sections 7-9 (outcome 2f); Ch. 12 Passive Equity Investing (outcome 2g); Ch. 13 Active Equity Investing: Strategies (outcome 2h); Ch. 19 Portfolio Performance Evaluation (outcomes 2i, 2j, 2k); Ch. 7 Asset Allocation with Real-World Constraints, section 6 only (outcome 2l)
