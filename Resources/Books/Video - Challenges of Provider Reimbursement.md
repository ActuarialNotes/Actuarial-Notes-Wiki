A short-form professional development video by Dan Pribe on the complexities of healthcare financing and insurance in the United States. The Society of Actuaries describes it as highlighting how hospital methodologies in charges can lead to confusion about medical costs, and as exploring how insurers strive to keep their products affordable, ensure access to care, and align costs with the value of care.
