## Part a (0.5 points)

Describe the concept of materiality.

### Explanation
An omission, understatement or overestimate is material if it will affect the user's decision making or reasonable expectation

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally performed well on this question

## Part b (0.5 points)

Identify two considerations in the determination of materiality.

### Explanation
Candidates would receive credits if they mentioned anything related to the characteristics of the entity and anything related to users' intent/situation/purpose. Sample answers include:

- Size of the company
- User of the work

### Examiner Report
- Candidates were typically able to provide one consideration
- Common error is that candidates typically omit one of the two considerations or providing the same considerations in different wordings.
