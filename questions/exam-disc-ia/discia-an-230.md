Every vehicle of one model has its fuel tank placed where it can rupture in a moderate rear-end collision, and a safer location was available at little extra cost. A driver is burned when his car of that model is hit from behind. What kind of defect does the car have?

- A) A manufacturing defect
- B) A design defect
- C) A warning defect
- D) No defect, because the car was built as designed

## Explanation

A **design defect** affects every unit of a product, judged by whether the product is more dangerous than an ordinary consumer would expect or by the risk-utility test, which weighs the cost of a reasonable alternative design against the harm it would prevent. Every car of the model has the tank in the same place, and a cheap safer placement existed.

- **A** is a single unit that departs from its intended design, such as a mis-welded joint. Here the cars match their design.
- **C** is a failure to give adequate instructions or warnings. The problem is the tank's placement, not the information supplied.
- **D** confuses conformity with the design with safety of the design. A product built exactly as designed can still be defective in design.
