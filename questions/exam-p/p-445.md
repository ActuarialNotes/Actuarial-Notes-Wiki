In a study of driver safety, drivers were categorized according to three risk factors. For each risk factor, exactly 1200 drivers exhibited that risk factor, and exactly 420 among them exhibited only that risk factor. There were exactly 320 drivers who exhibited all three risk factors and 480 who exhibited none of the three risk factors.

Calculate the number of drivers in the study.

- A) $1740$
- B) $2290$
- C) $2750$
- D) $3440$
- E) $4080$

## Explanation

Work with the seven regions of a three-circle Venn diagram. Five are given directly: each of the three "only" regions is 420, the centre is 320, and 480 sit outside all three circles. What remain are the three **exactly-two** regions.

Each risk factor's total of 1200 is made up of its "only" region, the centre, and the two pairwise regions touching it. Writing $X$, $Y$, $Z$ for those pairwise regions:

$$
\begin{align*}
420 + 320 + X + Y &= 1200 \\
420 + 320 + X + Z &= 1200 \\
420 + 320 + Y + Z &= 1200
\end{align*}
$$

Every equation gives the same pair sum, $460$. Subtracting any two shows the three unknowns are equal, so

$$
X = Y = Z = 230
$$

Now add all seven disjoint regions:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Total} &= \underbrace{3(420)}_{\text{only one}} + \underbrace{3(230)}_{\text{exactly two}} + \underbrace{320}_{\text{all three}} + \underbrace{480}_{\text{none}} \\
             &= 1260 + 690 + 320 + 480 \\
             &= \mathbf{2750}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (E), 4080, adds the three 1200s to the 480 with no overlap correction at all; choice (D), 3440, corrects only for the centre, $3(1200) - 2(320) + 480$, and never removes the pairwise overlaps. Summing the seven disjoint regions sidesteps that bookkeeping entirely.
