A guide, titled TACCESS: An Advisor's Guide to Understanding How Taxes Impact Group Insurance Benefits in Canada, supplied by the Society of Actuaries as a GH 201-C reading. The course strategy guide groups it with sections 12.1-12.4 of McKay's Canadian Handbook of Flexible Benefits as readings that give detailed information on provincial taxes affecting group insurance and on how employees are taxed on group benefits provided by their employers.
