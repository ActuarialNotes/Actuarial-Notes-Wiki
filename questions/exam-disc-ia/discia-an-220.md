A grocery clerk mops an aisle and leaves it wet with no warning sign. A customer slips but catches herself on a shelf and is not hurt in any way. Angry, she sues the store for negligence. Which element of negligence will she fail to prove?

- A) Duty
- B) Breach
- C) Causation
- D) Damages

## Explanation

Negligence requires actual harm. The store owed its customer a duty of care and arguably breached it by leaving a wet floor unmarked, but a near miss with no injury is not actionable, so the claim fails on **damages**.

- **A** fails because a store owes its customers, who are invitees, a duty of reasonable care.
- **B** fails because leaving a known hazard without a warning is the kind of conduct a reasonable store would avoid.
- **C** asks whether the breach caused the harm, but there is no harm for it to cause. The missing element is the harm itself.
