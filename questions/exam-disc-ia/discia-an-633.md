An underwriter is reviewing a commercial property policy built on the BPP (CP 00 10). Where in the policy are the exclusions that decide whether a windstorm or flood loss to covered property is covered?

- A) In the BPP's Property Not Covered section
- B) In the Commercial Property Conditions form
- C) In the BPP's Loss Conditions, under Valuation
- D) In the Causes of Loss form shown in the declarations

## Explanation

The BPP describes covered property, additional coverages, extensions, limits, deductible and conditions, but its sections on covered causes of loss and on exclusions and limitations simply refer to the applicable Causes of Loss form shown in the declarations, basic, broad or special. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Property Not Covered lists kinds of property, such as money or land, not causes of loss.
- **B** — The Commercial Property Conditions cover matters such as fraud, other insurance and the coverage territory, not perils.
- **C** — Valuation sets how a covered loss is measured, not whether a cause of loss is covered.
