A Moody's Investors Service report on the rising use of offshore reinsurance by life insurers. Trade-press coverage dates it to 30 May 2023 and reports that Moody's treats the shift of business offshore as a credit negative for the life insurance sector, because offshore vehicles face lighter regulation and tend to hold less capital than onshore entities.
