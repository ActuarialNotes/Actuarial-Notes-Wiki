# Exam F3BNK
**F3BNK – Banking Applications** is an exam of the finance, investments and ERM track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway, the banking option in place of Exam F3FIE.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of exam F3BNK is to expose candidates to key principles of risk management and banking practice and the application thereof, including governance and strategy setting and the principles of actuarial practice in solving problems, and to produce coherent advice and recommendations in the management of a banking operation.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA learning management system.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in the [[Banking Industry|banking industry]]. (A1)

> [!example]- Operations of a banking institution {15%}
>
> 1. Discuss the role of [[Banking Institution|banking institutions]] in economies: (C4)
>    - a. To [[Facilitate Loan-Deposit Transformation|facilitate loan-deposit transformation]].
>    - b. To [[Facilitate Investment|facilitate investments]] by firms and to [[Enable Growth|enable growth]] and [[Job Creation|job creation]].
>    - c. To [[Facilitate Local|facilitate local]] and [[International Trade|international trade]].
> 2. Discuss the implication of the different types of banks on the overall [[Economic Cycle|economic cycle]], including: (D4)
>    - a. [[Central Banks-reserve Bank|Central banks/reserve banks]].
>    - b. [[Lending Bank|Lending banks]] funded by [[Customer Deposit|customer deposits]].
>    - c. [[Lending Bank|Lending banks]] funded by [[Wholesale Deposit|wholesale deposits]].
>    - d. [[Investment Bank|Investment banks]].
>    - e. [[Universal Bank|Universal banks]].
>    - f. [[Community Banks-mutual Bank|Community banks/mutual banks]].
>    - g. [[Development Bank|Development banks]].
> 3. Demonstrate what constitutes the [[Main Risk|main risks]] for various activities carried out by banks, including: (C2)
>    - a. [[Retail Banking Activity|Retail banking activities]] and various products offered.
>    - b. [[Corporate Banking Activity|Corporate banking activities]] and various products offered.
>    - c. [[Investment Banking Activity|Investment banking activities]] and various products offered.
>    - d. Features and pricing of [[Banking Product|banking products]].
> 4. Demonstrate how to mitigate a [[Bank's Risks Exposure|bank’s risks exposure]] regarding its different sources of revenue, including: (D3)
>    - a. [[Net Interest Income|Net interest income]] from [[Banking Book|banking book]] operations.
>    - b. [[Non-interest Income|Non-interest income]] from [[Banking Book|banking book]] operations.
>    - c. [[Trading Income|Trading income]] from [[Trading Book|trading book]] operations.
> 5. Quantify the impact of a [[Bank's Cost Base|bank’s cost base]] on [[Trading Book|trading book]] and [[Banking Book Product Line|banking book product lines]], including: (B5)
>    - a. [[Operational Expense|Operational expenses]].
>    - b. Cost of [[Credit|credit]].
>    - c. [[Cost of Capital|Cost of capital]].
>    - d. Cost of liquidity.
>    - e. Tax.
> 6. Produce a [[Bank's Financial Statement|bank’s financial statements]] and [[Balance Sheet|balance sheet]]. (C6)
> 7. Analyze the main types of [[Capital|capital]] that must be held by a bank, including: (B4)
>    - a. [[Regulatory Capital|Capital requirements]] based on [[Risk-Weighted Asset|risk-weighted assets]].
>    - b. [[Regulatory Capital|Regulatory capital]].
>    - c. [[Economic Capital|Economic capital]] requirements.
>    - d. Available [[Capital|capital]] or [[Book Capital|book capital]].
> 8. Determine and analyze the various sources of funds that banks use to fund their operations, including: (B4)
>    - a. Deposit taking.
>    - b. [[Wholesale Markets Funding|Wholesale markets funding]].
>    - c. [[Central Bank Funding|Central bank funding]].
>    - d. [[Retained Earning|Retained earnings]].
> 9. Derive similarities and differences between the main types of financial and [[Non-Financial Risk|non-financial risks]] faced by a bank vs. a traditional actuarial practice area (i.e., [[Pension|pension]], insurance, etc.). (D4)
> 10. Explain how [[Actuary|actuaries]] can play a key role in a banking operation. (D5)

> [!example]- The banking regulatory framework {20%}
>
> 1. Describe the [[Main Provision|main provisions]] of the [[Canadian Bank Act]] and its regulations. (B2)
> 2. Describe a typical [[Corporate Governance Structure|corporate governance structure]] of a banking operation, including: (C2)
>    - a. Board of directors, [[Board Committee|board committees]], roles, and responsibilities.
>    - b. [[Executive Committee|Executive committee]] and senior management, roles, and responsibilities.
>    - c. Various [[Committees Existing|committees existing]] within a bank and their roles and responsibilities.
> 3. Outline the key elements of the Basel I framework and [[Perceived Shortcoming|perceived shortcomings]] of the Basel I accord. (C2)
> 4. Understand the [[Basel II]] framework and discuss the implications of [[Basel II]] for banking operations. (D5)
> 5. Understand the [[Basel III]] framework and discuss the implications of [[Basel III]] for banking operations. (D5)
> 6. Outline the roles of [[Central-reserve Bank|central/reserve banks]] in managing and [[Regulating Banking|regulating banking]] operations. (B2)
> 7. Outline the evolution of the [[Basel Regulation|Basel regulations]]. (B2)
> 8. Discuss how failure to implement key principles for [[Effective Risk Data Aggregation|effective risk data aggregation]] and [[Risk Reporting|risk reporting]] under Basel framework may lead to major losses. (D4)
> 9. Understand the various [[International Legislation|international legislation]], [[Guidance Note|guidance notes]], and [[Accounting Standards|accounting standards]] under which [[Banks Operate|banks operate]], including: (B2)
>    - a. [[Legislation Governing Company|Legislation governing companies]].
>    - b. [[Legislation Governing Bank|Legislation governing banks]].
>    - c. Directives and [[Guidance Note|guidance notes]] issued by the [[Basel Committee]] on [[Banking Supervision]].
>    - d. [[Central-reserve Bank|Central/reserve banks]] and other local and [[International Regulatory Body|international regulatory bodies]].
>    - e. Canadian and international [[Accounting Standards|accounting standards]].

> [!example]- Risk management in a banking operation {25%}
>
> 1. Discuss the role of [[High-Level Risk|high-level risk]] management in a banking operation (D4).
> 2. Discuss the role of an [[ERM Risk|ERM risk]] management framework in banking. (D4)
> 3. Discuss the [[Root Cause|root cause]] of major [[Financial Failure|financial failures]] of the past. (D4)
> 4. Discuss the impact on banking operations of an aggregation of [[Risk|risk]], including (D4):
>    - a. Diversification.
>    - b. Concentration.
>    - c. [[Correlation]].
> 5. Derive similarities and differences between the process of [[Controlling Risk|controlling risks]] in a banking operation vs. a traditional actuarial practice area (i.e., [[Pension|pension]], insurance, etc.), including but not limited to: (D5)
>    - a. Setting loan-sanctioning criteria.
>    - b. Setting [[Risk Appetite|risk appetite]], tolerance, and limits.
>    - c. Allocation of [[Risk-Based Capital|risk-based capital]].
> 6. Demonstrate how [[Risk Modelling|risk modelling]] may be used to identify and measure the different types of [[Risk|risks]] in a banking operation, including: (C3)
>    - a. [[Frequency]], [[Severity|severity]], and [[Exposure Modelling|exposure modelling]] for both [[Credit|credit]] and [[Operational Risk|operational risk]].
>    - b. [[Statistical Modelling|Statistical modelling]] for [[Portfolio|portfolio]] management.
>    - c. [[Survival Model|Survival models]] for [[Credit Risk|credit risk]] management.
>    - d. [[Market-implied Probability|Market-implied probability]] of default and [[Survival Curve|survival curves]]:
>       - i. Default and [[Survival Curve|survival curves]].
>       - ii. [[Closed-form Analytical Approximation|Closed-form analytical approximations]] vs. [[Monte Carlo Simulation|Monte Carlo simulation]].
>       - iii. [[Emerging Modelling|Emerging modelling]] techniques
>    - e. [[Asset-liability Modelling|Asset/liability modelling]] for [[Balance Sheet|balance-sheet]] management.
>    - f. Control cycle for all models.
>    - g. [[Cash Flow|Cash-flow]] models for budgeting and [[Balance Sheet|balance-sheet]] management.
> 7. Discuss the nature, impact, and [[Risk-Mitigation Procedures Relative|risk-mitigation procedures relative]] to: (D4)
>    - a. [[Operational Risk|Operational risk]].
>    - b. [[Liquidity Risk|Liquidity risk]].
>    - c. [[Credit Risk|Credit risk]] — standardized approach.
>    - d. [[Credit Risk|Credit risk]] — [[Internal Ratings-Based|internal ratings-based]] approach.
>    - e. Settlement and [[Counterparty Risk|counterparty risk]].
>    - f. [[Credit Valuation|Credit valuation]] adjustment (CVA) [[Risk|risk]].
>    - g. [[Market Risk|Market risk]].
>    - h. [[Climate-change Risk|Climate-change risk]].
> 8. Derive similarities and differences in how [[Risk|risks]] in a banking operation may be mitigated and/or managed vs. a traditional actuarial practice area (i.e., [[Pension|pension]], insurance etc.), including but not limited to: (D5)
>    - a. Positions.
>    - b. [[Credit Hedging|Credit hedging]].
>    - c. [[Securitization]].
>    - d. Dealing with [[Central Counterparty Clearing House|central counterparty clearing house]] (CCP).
