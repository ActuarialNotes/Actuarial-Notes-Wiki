A report to the Board of Directors of the American Academy of Actuaries from its Public Plans Practices Task Force, created to examine a broad range of actuarial issues and practices related to public-sector retirement systems. It discusses the need for the actuary to function as a risk advisor in the public plan sector and recommends ways actuaries can influence the operation of public plans so as to strengthen their risk management, through a risk evaluation, management and reporting framework.
