The loss $L$ due to a boat accident is exponentially distributed.

Boat insurance policy A covers up to 1 unit for each loss. Boat insurance policy B covers up to 2 units for each loss.

The probability that a loss is fully covered under policy B is 1.9 times the probability that it is fully covered under policy A.

Calculate the variance of $L$.

- A) 0.1
- B) 0.4
- C) 2.4
- D) 9.5
- E) 90.1

## Explanation

Let $\lambda$ be the rate of $L$, so $F(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x}$ and $\text{Var}(L)=1/\lambda^2$. A loss is fully covered under A if $L\le1$ and under B if $L\le2$, so $F(2)=1.9F(1)$:

$(1-e^{-2\lambda})=1.9(1-e^{-\lambda})\Rightarrow e^{-\lambda}=0.9\Rightarrow\lambda=\ln(10/9)\approx0.10536$.

$\text{Var}(L)=1/\lambda^2=1/(0.10536)^2\approx\mathbf{90.1}$
