A company has 100 insureds. Each insured, independently of the others, is high-risk with probability 0.60 and low-risk with probability 0.40. A high-risk insured files a claim in a year with probability 0.20; a low-risk insured, with probability 0.10. No insured files more than one claim in a year, and given the risk types, insureds file claims independently. Let $N$ be the total number of claims filed in a year.

Calculate the standard deviation of $N$.

- A) 3.41
- B) 3.56
- C) 3.67
- D) 3.76
- E) 3.87

## Explanation

Let $T_i$ be the risk type of insured $i$ and $N_i$ the number of claims (0 or 1) that insured files, so $N = N_1 + \cdots + N_{100}$ with the $N_i$ independent. Given $T_i$, $N_i$ is Bernoulli with variance $q(1-q)$.

By the law of total variance, $\text{Var}(N_i) = E[\text{Var}(N_i|T_i)]+\text{Var}(E[N_i|T_i])$, where

$E[\text{Var}(N_i|T_i)] = 0.60(0.2)(0.8)+0.40(0.1)(0.9) = 0.096+0.036 = 0.132$

$E[N_i] = 0.60(0.2)+0.40(0.1) = 0.16$

$\text{Var}(E[N_i|T_i]) = 0.60(0.2)^2+0.40(0.1)^2-(0.16)^2 = 0.024+0.004-0.0256 = 0.0024$

For 100 independent insureds:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(N) &= 100(0.132)+100(0.0024) \\
              &= 13.2+0.24 \\
              &= 13.44
\end{align*}
$$

$$\text{SD}(N) = \sqrt{13.44} \approx \mathbf{3.67}$$
