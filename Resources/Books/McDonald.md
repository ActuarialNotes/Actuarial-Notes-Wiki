A text on the life insurance laws of Canada's common law provinces by Bruce R. McDonald, published by the Life Underwriters Association of Canada. The library record for its 1995 edition, published in Don Mills, Ontario, describes one volume in various pagings; an earlier edition appeared in 1992.
