A July 2020 KPMG publication on IFRS 17 Insurance Contracts, produced by the KPMG International Standards Group. This 2020 edition provides an overview of the revised standard and how it may affect insurers' financial statements. It considers the requirements of IFRS 17 as published by the Board in June 2020 and refers to selected other standards in issue at 1 July 2020.
