An insurance company is planning to upgrade its current claims system. The project is expected to take several months, during which time the claims system will be periodically offline. In addition, much of the design and installation of the new system will be outsourced to a third party.

## Part a (0.5 points)

Describe what is meant by "operational risk."

### Explanation
Operational risk is the risk of loss due to the failure of people, processes or systems, or due to external events. It includes legal risk, but excludes reputational, strategic and systematic risk.

Alternatively:

The risk of loss due to failed processes, people or systems, or from external events. This includes legal risk but generally excludes reputational or strategic risk.

## Part b (1.5 points)

Identify two potential sources of operational risk this project entails and suggest a way to mitigate each one.

### Explanation
- **Business disruption and system failures** — the claims system will sometimes be offline, so the claims department can't do its job of serving customers. Mitigate by having a back-up system in place for when the new system is down.
- **External fraud** — since the project is outsourced, a third party may have access to the company's data and could hack its computer systems. Mitigate by requiring passwords to access certain information and heightening security.

Alternatively:

1. **Business disruption / systems failures**, due to the system being offline. Run two settings in parallel — one for development and one for regular use — until the new system is ready, and make sure any outages occur late at night.
2. **Execution, delivery and process management.** There is a risk the vendor won't deliver the system, or that it won't function properly. Mitigate by ensuring clarity and communication between your own IT personnel and the offshore personnel, so that expectations of the new system are clear; also fund for this risk in case customer claims aren't processed accurately.

### Examiner Report
Candidates generally selected two operational risks and provided valid means of mitigation.
Some candidates included reputational risk in their solutions where the definition of operational risk excludes this risk.  A few candidates included operational risks that were not relevant to the example given in the problem.  While grading we were considerate that "operational risk" can have numerous examples depicting it.
