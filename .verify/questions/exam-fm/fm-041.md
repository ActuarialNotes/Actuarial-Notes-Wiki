- evidence: SOA Q41 solution (solutions PDF p.12) draws a different conclusion from 40/1200 > 0.03: 'for early redemption the earliest redemption should be evaluated' — i.e. among the call dates the earliest (period 30) gives the lowest price. The explanation's two computations and its conclusion are right.
