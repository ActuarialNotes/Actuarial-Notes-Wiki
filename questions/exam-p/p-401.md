The joint density of two random variables is

$$f(x,y) = c(x+y), \qquad 0 < x < y < 1$$

and $f(x,y) = 0$ otherwise.

Calculate $c$.

- A) $1$
- B) $3/2$
- C) $2$
- D) $3$
- E) $4$

## Explanation

The support is the triangle **above** the line $y = x$ inside the unit square. Sketch it first: for each $x$ between 0 and 1, the variable $y$ runs from $x$ up to 1.

$$
\begin{align*}
1 &= \int_0^1\!\!\int_x^1 c(x+y)\,dy\,dx \\
  &= c\int_0^1 \left[xy + \frac{y^2}{2}\right]_{y=x}^{y=1} dx \\
  &= c\int_0^1 \left(x + \frac{1}{2} - x^2 - \frac{x^2}{2}\right) dx \\
  &= c\int_0^1 \left(x + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{3x^2}{2}\right) dx \\
  &= c\left[\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\right] \\
  &= \frac{c}{2}
\end{align*}
$$

Therefore $c = \mathbf{2}$.

Using the limits $0$ to $1$ for $y$ — treating the support as the whole unit square — gives $c = 1$, answer (A). The inner limits must be read off the sketch.
