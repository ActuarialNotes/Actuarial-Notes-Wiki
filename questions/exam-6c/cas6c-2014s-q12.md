## Part a (0.5 points)

Greater efficiency is an argument in favour of government insurance programs. Explain how the cost savings claimed for such programs may be overstated.

### Explanation
The claim handling and administration will likely be done by other departments and such costs may be excluded from calculating the "cost savings" involved in the program. For example advertising of the insurance provided be the government may be overlooked if it is bundled into the costs of another department.

### Examiner Report
Many candidates received no credit on this part.

## Part b (1.5 points)

Identify and briefly describe three reasons, other than efficiency, for government participation in insurance.

### Explanation
- Mandatory insurance: to make sure everyone has coverage that is mandatory, like auto insurance
- Social benefit: provide benefit to the society as a whole, like healthcare
- Filing needs not met by private insurance: when private companies won't insure certain risks, like terrorism

Alternatively:

- Filling needs unmet by private insurance. For example: private insurers don't have financial capacity to provide terrorism insurance
- Compulsory purchase of insurance: in order to fulfil social purpose, residual market is setup to ensure every driver has insurance
- Convenience: government can easily pass legislation to raise fund in order so setup a new program

### Examiner Report
The answer is one of key learning objectives of the exam so a large majority of students was well prepared and obtained most of points.
