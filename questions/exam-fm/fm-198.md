A ten-year 1000 par value bond with coupons paid annually at an annual rate of $r$ is callable at par at the end of the 6th, 7th, 8th, or 9th year. The price of the bond is 1023.

If the bond is called in the worst-case scenario for the bond investor, the resulting annual effective yield rate, $i$, is 96% of $r$.

Calculate $i$.

- A) 4.41%
- B) 7.46%
- C) 8.36%
- D) 10.56%
- E) 14.32%

## Explanation

Since price > par, the bond is at a premium and the worst case for the investor is the earliest call (year 6). At call time 6 with yield $i = 0.96r$:

$$
\begin{align*}
1023 &= 1000r \cdot a_{\overline{6}|i} + 1000(1+i)^{-6} \\
     &= \frac{1000r}{i}\left[1-(1+i)^{-6}\right] + 1000(1+i)^{-6}
\end{align*}
$$

$$= \frac{1000}{0.96}\left[1-(1+i)^{-6}\right] + 1000(1+i)^{-6} = \frac{1000}{0.96} - \frac{1000}{0.96}(1+i)^{-6} + 1000(1+i)^{-6}$$

$$
\begin{align*}
1023 &= \frac{1000}{0.96} - 1000\left(\frac{1}{0.96}-1\right)(1+i)^{-6} \\
     &= 1041.67 - 41.67(1+i)^{-6}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
(1+i)^{-6} &= \frac{1041.67-1023}{41.67} \\
           &= 0.448 \\
i &= 0.448^{-1/6}-1 \\
  &= 0.1432 \\
  &= 14.32\%
\end{align*}
$$
