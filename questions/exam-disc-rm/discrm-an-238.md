After a hailstorm, a homeowner hires an adjuster who, for a fee, prepares and negotiates the claim against the homeowner's insurer. What kind of adjuster is this?

- A) A public adjuster
- B) An independent adjuster
- C) A staff claims representative
- D) A third-party administrator

## Explanation

A public adjuster represents the insured against the insurer for a fee. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — An independent adjuster works for an adjusting firm that handles claims for insurers, often after a catastrophe, and is paid by the insurer.
- **C** — A staff claims representative is the insurer's own employee.
- **D** — A third-party administrator handles claims for self-insurers and sometimes for insurers, not for an individual claimant.
