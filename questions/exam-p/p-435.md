A large city police department is conducting an analysis of the annual number of car accidents in the city. The department hires an actuary who models the annual number of car accidents using an exponential distribution with a variance of 7225.

Calculate the median minus the mean of this distribution.

- A) $-2217$
- B) $-26$
- C) $0$
- D) $26$
- E) $2217$

## Explanation

An exponential distribution has variance equal to the square of its mean, so

$$
\text{mean} = \sqrt{7225} = 85
$$

Find the median $M$ from the survival function:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > M] &= e^{-M/85} = 0.5 \\
-\frac{M}{85} &= \ln 0.5 \\
M &= 85\ln 2 = 58.92
\end{align*}
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{median} - \text{mean} &= 58.92 - 85 \\
                            &= \mathbf{-26.08}
\end{align*}
$$

The sign is the substance of the question. For an exponential distribution the median is always $\ln 2 \approx 0.693$ times the mean, so the median is *below* the mean by about 30% — the long right tail pulls the mean up. Choice (C), 0, would hold for a symmetric distribution such as the normal, and choice (D) has the right magnitude with the comparison reversed.
