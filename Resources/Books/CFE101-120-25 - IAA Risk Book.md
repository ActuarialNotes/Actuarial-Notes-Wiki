A chapter of the International Actuarial Association's Risk Book, developed and approved by the IAA Insurance Regulation Committee (version 2.0, approved 29 July 2025, written by Charles Gilbert). It gives practical insights into asset liability management (ALM) techniques and practices for insurance companies. It covers the objectives of the ALM function, the sources of risk in its scope, governance and framework, the measurement of risk exposures and the execution of ALM strategies.
