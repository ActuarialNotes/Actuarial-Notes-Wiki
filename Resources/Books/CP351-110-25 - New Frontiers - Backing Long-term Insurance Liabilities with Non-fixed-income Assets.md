A TD Asset Management commentary on how Canadian life insurers can back long-term insurance liabilities when the structure of the Canadian fixed income market limits their hedging options. It argues that a carve-out strategy, which backs liability cash flows beyond a chosen horizon with non-fixed-income assets such as equities, real estate or infrastructure, may generate returns by compensating for the limited availability of long-term credit, and illustrates it with a case study.
