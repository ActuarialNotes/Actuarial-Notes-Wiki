A June 2021 report for the Society of Actuaries' Aging & Retirement Strategic Research Program, written by Deloitte Consulting LLP. It updates a 2014 paper on the continually evolving pension risk transfer landscape, discussing pension risk transfer in depth and identifying the key de-risking actions, alternatives and triggers that impact pension risk transfer strategies.
