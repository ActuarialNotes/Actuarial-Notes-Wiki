Before any disruption, a manufacturer qualifies a second supplier for its key component and keeps three weeks of safety stock. Which of the four resilience capabilities do these measures mainly build?

- A) Anticipate
- B) Adapt
- C) Recover
- D) Absorb

## Explanation

Buffers that limit the immediate damage of a disruption, such as redundancy (a second supplier) and slack resources (safety stock), build the capacity to **absorb**. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Anticipating means scanning for threats and knowing critical dependencies; identifying the single-supplier dependency is anticipation, but the stock and second supplier are buffers.
- **B** — Adapting means changing how the organization works once conditions change, such as redesigning processes.
- **C** — Recovering means bringing operations back to normal through plans and resources after the event.
