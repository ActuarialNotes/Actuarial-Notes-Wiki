The NAIC Insurance Regulatory Information System (IRIS) is a collection of analytical solvency tools and databases that give state insurance departments an integrated approach to screening and analyzing the financial condition of the insurers operating in their states. The 2025 edition of this manual is designed to help state insurance departments and the public understand the IRIS ratios for property/casualty and life/accident and health insurers.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6U (CAS)|Exam 6U]] — objectives A2, C3 and C7; the 2025 manual, Section II, Property/Casualty Ratios, pp. 5-26 (as the content outline states it)

## I The System
- Introduction
    - IRIS is intended to help state insurance departments target resources to the insurers in greatest need of regulatory attention, and is not intended to replace each department's own solvency monitoring efforts such as financial analyses or examinations.
- IRIS Ratio Application
    - The application generates key financial ratio results from insurers' statutory annual financial statements; a ratio outside its usual range is not necessarily considered adverse, and the ratios are not in themselves indicative of adverse financial conditions.
- Limitations
- Merged Insurers
- Branded Risk Classifications

## II Property/Casualty Ratios
- Ratio Ranges
- Overall Ratios
    - Ratio 1 Gross Premiums Written to Policyholders' Surplus
    - Ratio 2 Net Premiums Written to Policyholders' Surplus
    - Ratio 3 Change in Net Premiums Written
    - Ratio 4 Surplus Aid to Policyholders' Surplus
- Profitability Ratios
    - Ratio 5 Two-Year Overall Operating Ratio
    - Ratio 6 Investment Yield
    - Ratio 7 Gross Change in Policyholders' Surplus
    - Ratio 8 Change in Adjusted Policyholders' Surplus
- Liquidity Ratios
    - Ratio 9 Adjusted Liabilities to Liquid Assets
    - Ratio 10 Gross Agents' Balances (in collection) to Policyholders' Surplus
- Reserve Ratios
    - Ratio 11 One-Year Reserve Development to Policyholders' Surplus
    - Ratio 12 Two-Year Reserve Development to Policyholders' Surplus
    - Ratio 13 Estimated Current Reserve Deficiency to Policyholders' Surplus

## III Life/Accident & Health Ratios
- Ratio Ranges
- Overall Ratios
    - Ratio 1 Net Change in Capital and Surplus
    - Ratio 2 Gross Change in Capital and Surplus
    - Ratio 3 Net Income to Total Income (Including Realized Cap. Gains & Losses)
- Investments
    - Ratio 4 Adequacy of Investment Income
    - Ratio 5 Nonadmitted to Admitted Assets
    - Ratio 6 Tot. Real Estate and Total Mortgage Loans to Cash & Invested Assets
    - Ratio 7 Total Affiliated Investments to Capital and Surplus
- Surplus Relief
    - Ratio 8 Surplus Relief
- Change in Operations
    - Ratio 9 Change in Premium
    - Ratio 10 Change in Product Mix
    - Ratio 11 Change in Asset Mix
    - Ratio 12 Change in Reserving Ratio
