A February 2026 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, written by authors from Ernst & Young LLP, on the use of international reinsurance by U.S. life and annuity insurers. It aims to give the industry one resource that centralizes information about the jurisdictions a U.S. insurer could consider when setting up an international reinsurance arrangement, and to outline why U.S. insurers seek such reinsurance and the reserving and capital frameworks those jurisdictions use. It covers Bahamas, Barbados, Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Gibraltar, Guernsey, Hong Kong, Ireland, Isle of Man, Jersey, Luxembourg, Malta, Puerto Rico and Singapore.
