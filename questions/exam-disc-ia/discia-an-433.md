A guest injured at an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured's home wants compensation quickly. Under the Section II conditions, which statement is correct?

- A) The guest may sue the insurer directly for Coverage E damages as soon as the injury occurs
- B) If both the named insured and her husband are sued, Coverage E pays up to its full limit for each of them separately
- C) The guest can't sue the insurer under Coverage E until a judgment or signed agreement fixes the obligation
- D) A Coverage F payment to the guest is an admission that the insured was negligent

## Explanation

Under the suit against us condition, no one may join the insurer as a party to an action against an insured, and no action under Coverage E may be brought against the insurer until the insured's obligation has been determined by final judgment or by an agreement signed by the insurer. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** contradicts that condition.
- **B** — severability applies the insurance separately to each insured but does not increase the limit for one occurrence.
- **D** — payment under Coverage F is expressly not an admission of liability by an insured or the insurer.
