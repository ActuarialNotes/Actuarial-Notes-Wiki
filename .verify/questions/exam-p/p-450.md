- locus: ## Explanation, final paragraph (distractor note for D)
- claim: Choice (D), 1.50, treats the coefficient of variation as variance over mean rather than *standard deviation* over mean, which drops the square root and changes the power of lambda that survives.
- evidence: Var(U)/E(U) = 72,500 lambda / (450 lambda) = 161.1 for every lambda, so setting variance-over-mean equal to 0.9 has no solution and cannot yield 1.50. 1.50 is instead 1/sqrt(0.442) = 1.504 (equivalently sqrt(2.262), where 2.262 = 1/0.442 is option E). The SOA solution (solutions PDF pp.125-126, Solution: A, lambda = 72,500/(0.9 x 450)^2 = 0.442) gives no distractor rationale; this rests on rank-5 recomputation, which can falsify the sentence. Keyed answer and main derivation unaffected.
- source_rank: 5
