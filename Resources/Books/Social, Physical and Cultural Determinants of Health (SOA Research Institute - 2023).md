![[Social, Physical and Cultural Determinants of Health (SOA Research Institute - 2023) - Cover.svg]]

An April 2023 Society of Actuaries Research Institute report, sponsored by the Health Care Cost Trends Committee, that gives actuaries and others working with risk, data, analytics or program evaluation in health care considerations and frameworks for using determinants of health (DOH), sometimes more narrowly defined as social determinants of health, in data and actuarial work streams. It covers data resources, the changing DOH ecosystem, the measurement and evaluation of DOH interventions, approaches in actuarial and analytics work streams, and ethical and practical considerations.
