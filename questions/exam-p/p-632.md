A farmer purchases a five-year insurance policy that covers crop destruction due to hail. Over the five-year period, the farmer will receive a benefit of 30 for each year in which hail destroys the farmer's crop, subject to a maximum of three benefit payments. The probability that hail will destroy the farmer's crop in any given year is 0.5, independent of any other year.

Calculate the expected benefit that the farmer will receive over the five-year period.

- A) $45$
- B) $52$
- C) $66$
- D) $68$
- E) $75$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the number of years in which the crop is destroyed, so $X$ is binomial with $n = 5$ and $p = 0.5$. The benefit is $30\min(X, 3)$ — the cap collapses $X = 3$, $4$ and $5$ into the same payment of 90.

The binomial probabilities are

$$
\begin{align*}
P(X = 0) &= 0.03125 & P(X = 3) &= 0.31250 \\
P(X = 1) &= 0.15625 & P(X = 4) &= 0.15625 \\
P(X = 2) &= 0.31250 & P(X = 5) &= 0.03125
\end{align*}
$$

so $P(X \ge 3) = 0.31250 + 0.15625 + 0.03125 = 0.5$, and

$$
\begin{align*}
E[\text{benefit}] &= 30\left[0(0.03125) + 1(0.15625) + 2(0.31250) + 3(0.5)\right] \\
                  &= 30\left[0.15625 + 0.62500 + 1.5\right] \\
                  &= \mathbf{68.4375}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (E), 75, ignores the cap: $30\,E(X) = 30(5)(0.5) = 75$. The cap binds exactly half the time here — $P(X \ge 3) = 0.5$ — so leaving it out overstates the benefit by a full 6.6.
