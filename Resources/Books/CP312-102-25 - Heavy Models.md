A working paper of the Proxy Model Working Party, presented to the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries in London on 24 February 2014. It looks at the types of proxy model available to practitioners and suggests a basic framework for "replicating formula" proxies, within which the options in design and implementation are discussed. It closes with four specific proxy models in greater detail, two of which are the subject of a case study, leading to a result on the distinction between risk scenario accuracy and risk distribution accuracy.
