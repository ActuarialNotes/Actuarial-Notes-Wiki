Wilma takes out a loan of 5,000,000 to start a new business. She will repay the loan with level semiannual payments of 303,244.53, with the first payment due in six months.

The loan payments are based on an annual nominal interest rate of 6.5% convertible semiannually.

Immediately after the tenth payment, the outstanding loan balance is $X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 1,483,350
- B) 2,350,407
- C) 2,916,667
- D) 3,367,821
- E) 4,330,426

## Explanation

The semiannual interest rate is $0.065/2 = 0.0325$. The outstanding loan balance can be calculated using either the prospective approach or the retrospective approach, although the retrospective approach is easier, since it does not require the number of payments $n$. Retrospectively, the balance is the loan accumulated for 10 half-years minus the accumulated value of the 10 payments made:

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= 5{,}000{,}000(1.0325)^{10} - 303{,}244.53\,s_{\overline{10}|0.0325} \\
  &= 6{,}884{,}471.52 - 3{,}516{,}650.34 \\
  &= 3{,}367{,}821.18
\end{align*}
$$
