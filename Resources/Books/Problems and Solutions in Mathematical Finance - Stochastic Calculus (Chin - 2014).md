![[Problems and Solutions in Mathematical Finance - Stochastic Calculus (Chin - 2014) - Cover.svg]]

Volume 1 of a four-volume Wiley Finance set of problems and solutions in mathematical finance. It introduces the basic stochastic calculus concepts required for the study of mathematical finance through a large number of worked examples, which build the foundation for the more practically oriented problems of the later volumes. It is written mainly for students, industry practitioners and those involved in teaching in the field.
