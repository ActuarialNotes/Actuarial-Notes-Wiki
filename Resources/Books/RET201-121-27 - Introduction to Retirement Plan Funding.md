A study note, RET201-121-27, written specifically for the Society of Actuaries' RET 201 course. According to the course strategy guide it is the core reading describing funding practices, written to be independent of the jurisdiction or type of plan and to cover a broad range of actuarial approaches rather than just those allowed or required by local laws.
