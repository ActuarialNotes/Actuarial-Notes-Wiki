A Canadian Institute of Actuaries educational note, dated April 2023 (document 223077), intended to assist actuaries in selecting an appropriate best estimate discount rate for a going concern funding valuation of a pension plan. Earlier notes on the subject were issued on December 21, 2010 and December 7, 2015; this one is updated for the changes to Part 3000 of the Standards of Practice effective December 1, 2022. It was prepared by the Committee on Pension Plan Financial Reporting and approved for distribution on April 11, 2023.

## 1 Introduction
- Quotes the Standards of Practice paragraphs 3230.01, 3230.02, 3230.03 and 3260.02 for a going concern valuation and paragraph 1710.01 of the general standards.
- A best estimate discount rate is determined with reference to unbiased measurements and other information and without a margin for adverse deviations.
- Two distinct approaches: one based on the expected future investment return on the plan's assets, and one based on the yields of investment grade debt securities that would reasonably match projected benefit cash flows.
- Approaches to selection of best estimate discount rates
- Basing the best estimate discount rate on expected future investment returns
    - The building block approach
    - Determining the best estimate of expected future investment returns for various asset classes
    - Investment policy
    - Rebalancing and diversification
    - Value added returns from active management
    - Alternative asset classes
    - Use of leverage
    - Discount rates that vary by membership group
    - Stochastic methodology
- Discount rate based on fixed income yields
- Expenses
- Rounding
- Tax-sheltered status of assets
- Reporting
- Illustrative example – building block method
    - The example sets a best estimate discount rate of 5.5% per annum from a 2.5% long-term Government of Canada bond return, asset-class risk premia, a 0.60% diversification effect and a 0.25% allowance for investment expenses.
- Illustrative example – reflecting an asset mix glide-path
