- locus: explanation, opening paragraph and statements I-II
- claim: The data pull toward 2 and the prior pulls toward 7, which is why the current posterior mode of 2.1 sits just above the sample mean ... lowering the mean from 7 to 5 weakens the upward pull.
- evidence: With a Poisson likelihood and an exponential prior (Gamma alpha=1, rate 1/7) the posterior is Gamma(1+n ybar, n+1/7) (BDA3 PDF pp.53-54), mode n ybar/(n+1/7) = 2n/(n+0.143) < 2: an exponential prior is decreasing and pulls the mode down, not toward its mean. Taking 2.1 as given, CAS's key E ('I only') holds and the vault's answer matches; recomputed without the stated 2.1, the uniform(0,2) prior gives mode 2 > 2n/(n+1/7), which would make III also increase the mode. Statement I's argument (alpha=7 adds lambda^6) is sound.
