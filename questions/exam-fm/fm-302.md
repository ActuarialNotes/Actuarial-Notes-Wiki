Susan receives annual payments from a 20-year annuity-immediate. The payment in year 1 is 100 and in each succeeding year the payment is 90% of the prior year's payment.

Upon receipt of each payment, Susan invests the payment in a savings account earning interest at a 3% annual effective rate.

Calculate the balance in the savings account immediately after Susan invests the last annuity payment.

- A) 696
- B) 717
- C) 739
- D) 1296
- E) 1335

## Explanation

The payments are $100, 100(0.9), \dots, 100(0.9)^{19}$ at the ends of years 1 to 20: a 20-year geometric annuity-immediate with $g = -10\%$. The balance just after the last deposit is their accumulated value at 3%, which is the present value times $(1.03)^{20}$ (SOA's solution):

$$FV = 100\left[\frac{1-\left(\frac{0.9}{1.03}\right)^{20}}{0.03+0.10}\right](1.03)^{20}$$

$$
\begin{align*}
FV &= 100\left[\frac{1-0.0673140}{0.13}\right](1.806111) \\
   &= 717.4507 \times 1.806111 \\
   &= 1295.80 \approx 1296
\end{align*}
$$

Equivalently, accumulate each payment to year 20 directly:

$$FV = 100\left[(1.03)^{19} + 0.9(1.03)^{18} + \cdots + (0.9)^{19}(1.03)^0\right] = 100\cdot\frac{(1.03)^{20}-(0.9)^{20}}{1.03-0.9}$$

$$
\begin{align*}
FV &= 100\cdot\frac{1.806111-0.121577}{0.13} \\
   &= 1295.80
\end{align*}
$$

The present value, 717.45, is option B; the question asks for the balance at year 20.
