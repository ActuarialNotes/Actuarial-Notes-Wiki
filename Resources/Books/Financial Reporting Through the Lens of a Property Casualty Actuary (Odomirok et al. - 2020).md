A text, prepared by EY for the Casualty Actuarial Society, on financial reporting and taxation as they affect reserving and statutory reporting. It replaced a number of earlier syllabus readings with a single publication focused on the financial accounting and taxation topics of particular relevance to the property/casualty actuary, and this 2020 version brings the Annual Statement for Fictitious Insurance Company up to 2018.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6U (CAS)|Exam 6U]] — objectives A2, C1–C7 and C9–C10; Edition 5, excluding Part VII (the Canadian introduction and Chapters 27–29) and Appendix II, including errata (as the content outline states it)
