A reinsurer is offering a ceding company a two-year aggregate stop loss with the following terms:

- The treaty is effective on 1/1/2016 and expires on 12/31/2017.
- The treaty will cover aggregate losses between a 65% and a 70% loss ratio for accident years 2016 and 2017 separately.
- Premium is paid at the beginning of each year and all losses are paid at the end of the year incurred.

Additionally, the reinsurance treaty has the following termination provisions:

- The ceding company has the option to terminate the contract at the end of 2016 and receive a profit commission from the reinsurer calculated as follows:
  - Profit commission = [35% \* 2016 Ceded Premium] – [2016 Ceded Loss], subject to a minimum of 0.
- If the contract is not terminated at the end of 2016, the ceding company will again have the option to terminate the contract at term expiration and receive a profit commission from the reinsurer calculated as follows:
  - Profit commission = [35% \* Full Term Ceded Premium] – [Full Term Ceded Loss], subject to a minimum of 0.
- The reinsurer cannot terminate the contract at any time.

The reinsurer has simulated five trials of the ceding company's loss ratio subject to the aggregate stop loss as follows (values in millions):

| Simulation | Accident Year 2016: Loss Ratio | 2016: Loss Amount | Accident Year 2017: Loss Ratio | 2017: Loss Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 73.9% | 517 | 68.0% | 510 |
| 2 | 64.8% | 454 | 67.9% | 509 |
| 3 | 66.8% | 468 | 65.5% | 491 |
| 4 | 65.6% | 459 | 65.3% | 490 |
| 5 | 65.1% | 456 | 58.7% | 440 |
| Mean | 67.2% | 471 | 65.1% | 488 |

| | 2016 | 2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Subject Premium | 700 | 750 |
| Ceded Premium | 9.8 | 10.5 |

For each simulation, assume that the ceding company will only terminate the contract if the profit commission payable is greater than zero.

## Part a (1 point)

Calculate the expected profit commission payable at the end of 2016.

### Explanation
In each simulation, the 2016 ceded loss is the loss ratio in the 65%–70% layer times the 700M subject premium, and the profit commission is floored at zero:

| Simulation | Ceded Premium | Ceded Loss | Profit Commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9.8M | 0.05 × 700M = 35M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 35M) = 0 |
| 2 | 9.8M | 0 | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 0) = 3.43M |
| 3 | 9.8M | 0.018 × 700M = 12.6M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 12.6M) = 0 |
| 4 | 9.8M | 0.006 × 700M = 4.2M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 4.2M) = 0 |
| 5 | 9.8M | 0.001 × 700M = .7M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – .7M) = 2.73M |

Expected profit commission payable at end of 2016 = (0 + 3.43M + 0 + 0 + 2.73M) / 5 = 1.232M

Alternatively, working from the loss amounts: the loss amount at 65% is 700M × (.65) = 455M and at 70% is 700M × (.7) = 490M.

| Simulation | Ceded Premium | Ceded Loss | Profit Commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9.8M | 490M – 455M = 35M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 35M) = 0 |
| 2 | 9.8M | 0 | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 0) = 3.43M |
| 3 | 9.8M | 468M – 455M = 13M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 13M) = 0 |
| 4 | 9.8M | 459M – 455M = 4M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 4M) = 0 |
| 5 | 9.8M | 456M – 455M = 1M | Max(0, (.35 × 9.8M) – 1M) = 2.43M |

Expected profit commission payable at end of 2016 = (0 + 3.43M + 0 + 0 + 2.43M) / 5 = 1.172M

### Examiner Report
To receive full credit, candidates were expected to calculate the ceded losses and profit commission by simulation.  The expected profit commission should be calculated as an average profit from all 5 simulations.

The most common errors were:

- Calculating ceded losses by multiplying the ceded loss ratios by loss instead of premium
- Obtaining the average ceded loss for all simulations and determining the profit commission based on that.

## Part b (2.75 points)

Calculate the expected profit commission for the full term of the contract.

### Explanation
Simulations 2 and 5 have a positive profit commission at the end of 2016, so the ceding company terminates them then and collects it. The other three run the full term, with 2017's ceded loss added to 2016's:

| Simulation | Terminate 2016? | Total Ceded Premium | 2017 Ceded Loss | Total Ceded Loss | Profit Commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No | 20.3M | 0.03 × 750M = 22.5M | 35M + 22.5M = 57.5M | Max(0, (.35 × 20.3M) – 57.5M) = 0 |
| 2 | Yes | - | - | - | 3.43M |
| 3 | No | 20.3M | 0.005 × 750M = 3.75M | 12.6M + 3.75M = 16.35M | Max(0, (.35 × 20.3M) – 16.35M) = 0 |
| 4 | No | 20.3M | 0.003 × 750M = 2.25M | 4.2M + 2.25M = 6.45M | Max(0, (.35 × 20.3M) – 6.45M) = 0.655M |
| 5 | Yes | - | - | - | 2.73M |

Expected profit commission for the full term contract = (0 + 3.43M + 0 + 0.655M + 2.73M) = 1.363M (as printed — the sum is averaged over the 5 simulations)

Alternatively, working from the loss amounts: the loss amount at 65% is 750M × (.65) = 487.5M and at 70% is 750M × (.7) = 525M.

| Simulation | Terminate 2016? | Total Ceded Premium | 2017 Ceded Loss | Total Ceded Loss | Profit Commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No | 20.3M | 510M – 487.5M = 22.5M | 35M + 22.5M = 57.5M | Max(0, (.35 × 20.3M) – 57.5M) = 0 |
| 2 | Yes | - | - | - | 3.43M |
| 3 | No | 20.3M | 491M – 487.5M = 3.5M | 13M + 3.5M = 16.5M | Max(0, (.35 × 20.3M) – 16.5M) = 0 |
| 4 | No | 20.3M | 490M – 487.5M = 2.5M | 4M + 2.5M = 6.5M | Max(0, (.35 × 20.3M) – 6.5M) = 0.605M |
| 5 | Yes | - | - | - | 2.43M |

Expected profit commission for the full term contract = (0 + 3.43M + 0 + 0.605M + 2.43M) = 1.293M (as printed — the sum is averaged over the 5 simulations)

### Examiner Report
To receive full credit, candidates must have been able to determine which simulations would have terminated after the 2016 year. Partial credit was given for candidates that correctly applied the ceded premium, ceded loss, and profit commission formulas but failed to accurately determine which simulations were terminated in 2016.

The most common errors were:

- Incorrectly determining which simulations were terminated in 2016.
- Calculating ceded loss amounts by treating 2016 and 2017 as a combined policy year.
- Using only 2017 ceded premium and loss to determine profit commission.
- Calculating ceded losses by multiplying the ceded loss ratios by loss instead of premium.
- Obtaining the average ceded loss for all simulations and determining the profit commission based on that.
- Not including the profit commission from the 2016 terminated policies in the calculation of full term expected profit commission.
