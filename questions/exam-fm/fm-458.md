Investment A provides cash flows of 1000, 3000, and $X$ at the end of years two, three, and five, respectively.

Investment B provides cash flows of 1000, 3000, and $X$ at the end of years three, four, and seven, respectively.

The modified durations of Investment A and Investment B are 4 years and $Y$ years, respectively, each based on an annual effective interest rate of 10%.

Calculate $Y$.

- A) 5.48
- B) 5.52
- C) 5.56
- D) 5.58
- E) 5.60

## Explanation

The modified duration is $D_{MOD} = -\dfrac{P'(i)}{P(i)}$. For Investment A,

$$
\begin{align*}
P_A(i) &= 1000(1+i)^{-2} + 3000(1+i)^{-3} + X(1+i)^{-5} \\
P_A(0.1) &= 3080.3907 + 0.6209213X \\
-P_A'(i) &= 2000(1+i)^{-3} + 9000(1+i)^{-4} + 5X(1+i)^{-6} \\
-P_A'(0.1) &= 7649.7507 + 2.8223697X
\end{align*}
$$

Setting the modified duration of Investment A equal to 4:

$$
\begin{align*}
4 &= \frac{7649.7507 + 2.8223697X}{3080.3907 + 0.6209213X} \\
X &= \frac{4(3080.3907) - 7649.7507}{2.8223697 - 4(0.6209213)} \\
  &= \frac{4671.8121}{0.3386845} \\
  &\approx 13{,}794
\end{align*}
$$

For Investment B,

$$
\begin{align*}
P_B(i) &= 1000(1+i)^{-3} + 3000(1+i)^{-4} + 13{,}794(1+i)^{-7} \\
P_B(0.1) &= 9878.86 \\
-P_B'(i) &= 3000(1+i)^{-4} + 12{,}000(1+i)^{-5} + 7(13{,}794)(1+i)^{-8} \\
-P_B'(0.1) &= 54{,}545.12
\end{align*}
$$

so

$$
\begin{align*}
Y &= \frac{54{,}545.12}{9878.86} \\
  &= 5.5214
\end{align*}
$$
