A geometric perpetuity-due provides annual payments. The first payment is 1, and each subsequent payment is 2% less than the previous payment. Let $M$ be the price of this perpetuity using an annual effective interest rate of 8%.

The price should have been calculated using an annual effective interest rate of 7%. Let $P$ be the correct price of this perpetuity and let $E$ be the estimate of $P$ based on the initial price, $M$, using the first-order Macaulay approximation.

Calculate $\dfrac{E-P}{P}$, the percentage error of the estimate.

- A) $-1.48\%$
- B) $-1.16\%$
- C) $-0.92\%$
- D) $-0.49\%$
- E) $-0.26\%$

## Explanation

The present value of the geometric perpetuity-due, at annual effective interest rate $i$ and with payment growth rate $r = -0.02$, is

$$
\begin{align*}
P(i) &= \frac{1+i}{i - r} \\
     &= \frac{1+i}{i + 0.02}
\end{align*}
$$

Its derivative is $P'(i) = \dfrac{(i + 0.02) - (1+i)}{(i+0.02)^{2}} = \dfrac{0.02 - 1}{(i+0.02)^{2}}$, so the Macaulay duration is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC}(i) &= \frac{(1+i)\left(-P'(i)\right)}{P(i)} \\
           &= \frac{(1+i)\left[\dfrac{1 - 0.02}{(i+0.02)^{2}}\right]}{\dfrac{1+i}{i+0.02}} \\
           &= \frac{0.98}{i + 0.02}
\end{align*}
$$

The first-order Macaulay approximation of the present value at an interest rate $i$ near an initial interest rate $i_0$ is

$$
\begin{align*}
E &= P(i_0)\left(\frac{1+i_0}{1+i}\right)^{D_{MAC}(i_0)} \\
  &= \left(\frac{1+i_0}{i_0 + 0.02}\right)\left(\frac{1+i_0}{1+i}\right)^{\frac{0.98}{i_0 + 0.02}}
\end{align*}
$$

Using $i_0 = 0.08$ (so $M = P(0.08) = 1.08/0.10$) and $i = 0.07$:

$$
\begin{align*}
E &= \left(\frac{1.08}{0.08 + 0.02}\right)\left(\frac{1.08}{1.07}\right)^{\frac{0.98}{0.08 + 0.02}} \\
  &= 10.8\left(\frac{1.08}{1.07}\right)^{9.8} \\
  &= 11.830839
\end{align*}
$$

The correct price is

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= \frac{1.07}{0.07 + 0.02} \\
  &= 11.888889
\end{align*}
$$

so the percentage error is

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{E - P}{P} &= \frac{11.830839 - 11.888889}{11.888889} \\
                &= -0.004883 \\
                &= -0.49\%
\end{align*}
$$
