A February 2017 educational note supplement of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Committee on Life Insurance Financial Reporting. It reviews methods for taking into account the impact on mortality of selective lapsation at renewal of renewable term insurance products, summarizes Society of Actuaries surveys and studies of level premium term products, and presents alternate methods for the renewal mortality assumption, compared with available experience. The CIA has since archived it and replaced it with document 225110.
