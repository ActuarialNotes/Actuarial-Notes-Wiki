A study of fire damage claims is conducted. Claim amounts are mutually independent and each claim amount exceeds 1000 with probability 0.20.

Calculate the probability that at least one of three randomly selected claim amounts exceeds 1000.

- A) $0.472$
- B) $0.488$
- C) $0.512$
- D) $0.528$
- E) $0.600$

## Explanation

The number of claims exceeding 1000 is binomial with $n = 3$ and $p = 0.2$.

"At least one" spans three separate cases, so use the complement — none of the three exceeds 1000:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[N > 0] &= 1 - P[N = 0] \\
         &= 1 - (0.8)^3 \\
         &= 1 - 0.512 \\
         &= \mathbf{0.488}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (C), 0.512, is $P[N = 0]$, the complement reported instead of the answer. Choice (E), 0.600, is $3(0.20)$ — adding the three individual probabilities, which overcounts the outcomes where more than one claim exceeds 1000. That sum is always an upper bound, and here it overstates the answer by about 0.11.
