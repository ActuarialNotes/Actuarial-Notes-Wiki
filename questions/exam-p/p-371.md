A random variable $X$ is normally distributed with mean 5 and standard deviation 2.

Calculate the probability that $8X - X^2 < 1$.

- A) $0.007$
- B) $0.076$
- C) $0.082$
- D) $0.917$
- E) $0.925$

## Explanation

The event is a quadratic inequality, so solve it for $X$ before touching the normal distribution. Rearranging $8X - X^2 < 1$ gives

$$
X^2 - 8X + 1 > 0
$$

The roots of $X^2 - 8X + 1 = 0$ are

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= \frac{8 \pm \sqrt{64 - 4}}{2} \\
  &= 4 \pm \sqrt{15} \\
  &= 0.127017 \text{ and } 7.872983
\end{align*}
$$

An upward-opening parabola is positive **outside** its roots, so the event is $\{X < 0.127017\} \cup \{X > 7.872983\}$ — two tails, not an interval. Standardize each boundary with $Z = (X-5)/2$:

$$
\begin{align*}
z_1 &= \frac{0.127017 - 5}{2} = -2.4365 \\
z_2 &= \frac{7.872983 - 5}{2} = 1.4365
\end{align*}
$$

The two tails are disjoint, so their probabilities add:

$$
\begin{align*}
P\bigl[8X - X^2 < 1\bigr] &= P[Z < -2.44] + P[Z > 1.44] \\
                          &= 0.0073 + 0.0749 \\
                          &= \mathbf{0.0822}
\end{align*}
$$

Carrying the unrounded $z$-values instead gives 0.0828; either way the answer is (C).

Choices (D) and (E) are the complement — the probability of the *inside* interval, which is what you get by solving the inequality in the wrong direction. Choice (A) is the lower tail alone, dropping the much larger upper one.
