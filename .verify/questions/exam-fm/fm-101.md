## [F-001] Thousands commas inside math render with a trailing thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, align block (line 40)
- claim: Numbers inside math spans are written with a bare thousands comma (2(40,000)v^2 … 100,000v^4, six numbers), which KaTeX sets as punctuation and renders as e.g. '40, 000'.
- evidence: KaTeX treats ',' in math as punctuation (mpunct) and adds a thin space after it; quiz/src/lib/vaultMath.ts only moves delimiters and does not touch commas. The vault's own convention elsewhere (e.g. fm-104 '20{,}000') is '{,}'. Cosmetic; no value changes.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Write thousands separators in math as '{,}'.
- applied: true
