A jury awards a plaintiff \$40,000 for medical expenses, \$25,000 for lost wages, \$10,000 for damage to her car, \$120,000 for pain and suffering and \$200,000 in punitive damages. What are her total compensatory damages?

- A) \$75,000
- B) \$185,000
- C) \$195,000
- D) \$395,000

## Explanation

Compensatory damages are the special (economic) damages plus the general (non-economic) damages. Punitive damages are awarded on top of them, to punish and deter, and are not compensatory:

$$
\begin{align*}
S &= 40{,}000 + 25{,}000 + 10{,}000 \\
&= 75{,}000 \\
D &= 75{,}000 + 120{,}000 \\
&= \mathbf{195{,}000}
\end{align*}
$$

- **A** is the special damages alone, leaving out pain and suffering.
- **B** drops the car repair. Property damage is a special damage.
- **D** adds the punitive award, which is not compensation.
