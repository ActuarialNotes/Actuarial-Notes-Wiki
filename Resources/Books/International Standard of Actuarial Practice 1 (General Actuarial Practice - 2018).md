A model standard of actuarial practice from the International Actuarial Association, adopted by the IAA Council on 18 November 2012 and revised on 1 December 2018 (minor correction 16 April 2019). It is a general standard that gives actuaries guidance when performing actuarial services, so that intended users can have confidence that the services are carried out professionally and with due care, that the results are relevant, clear and complete, and that the assumptions and methodology used are disclosed appropriately.
