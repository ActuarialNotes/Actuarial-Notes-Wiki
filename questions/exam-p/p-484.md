An insurance company has customer service operations in Denver, Philadelphia, and Salt Lake City.

Employee salaries in Denver are uniformly distributed from 25 to 90. Employee salaries in Philadelphia are uniformly distributed from 45 to $x$. Employee salaries in Salt Lake City are uniformly distributed from 10 to $x/3$.

The 40th percentile of Denver salaries is equal to the 20th percentile of Philadelphia salaries.

Calculate the median of Salt Lake City employee salaries.

- A) $12.5$
- B) $17.5$
- C) $25.0$
- D) $35.0$
- E) $60.0$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution on $[a,b]$, the $100q$th percentile sits a fraction $q$ of the way along, at $(1-q)a + qb$.

**Denver's 40th percentile** is a known number:

$$
0.6(25) + 0.4(90) = 15 + 36 = 51
$$

**Philadelphia's 20th percentile** carries the unknown:

$$
0.8(45) + 0.2x = 36 + 0.2x
$$

Setting them equal:

$$
\begin{align*}
36 + 0.2x &= 51 \\
x &= 75
\end{align*}
$$

**Salt Lake City** is then uniform on $[10, 75/3] = [10, 25]$. A uniform distribution is symmetric, so its median is the midpoint:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{median} &= \frac{10 + 25}{2} \\
              &= \mathbf{17.5}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (C), 25.0, is the upper endpoint $x/3$ rather than the midpoint. The chain runs Denver → Philadelphia → Salt Lake City, so each city's parameters must be resolved in order; only the last step uses the symmetry of the uniform distribution.
