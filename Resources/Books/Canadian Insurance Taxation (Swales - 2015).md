A LexisNexis Canada book on Canadian insurance taxation, in its 4th edition, co-written by Jason Swales and Erdem Erinc. Its publisher describes it as covering the tax rules that are unique to businesses in the insurance industry.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201C (SOA)|Exam GH-201C]] — Regulation & Taxation, outcome 3c; Ch. 4: Income for Tax Purposes – General Rules (pp. 37-38 [excluding "Imputed Interest Benefit on Real Property"] and 43-47) and Ch. 9: Investment Income Tax (pp. 135-142).
> - [[Exam ILA-201I (SOA)|Exam ILA-201I]] — Advanced Product Management, outcome 4d; Ch. 4: Income for Tax Purposes – General Rules, Ch. 5: Investment Income and Ch. 9: Investment Income Tax.
