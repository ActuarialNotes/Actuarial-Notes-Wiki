## SSAP No. 29 Prepaid Expenses
- Scope of Statement
    - The statement establishes statutory accounting principles for the accounting for prepaid expenses, and does not address deferred policy acquisition costs and other underwriting expenses, income taxes and guaranty fund assessments.
- Summary Conclusion
    - A prepaid expense is an amount paid in advance of receiving future economic benefits anticipated by the payment; prepaid expenses are reported as nonadmitted assets, charged against unassigned funds (surplus), and amortized against net income as the estimated economic benefit expires.
    - In accordance with the reporting entity's written capitalization policy, prepaid expenses less than a predefined threshold are expensed when purchased.
- Disclosures
    - The financial statements disclose if the written capitalization policy and its predefined thresholds changed from the prior period, and the reasons for the change.
