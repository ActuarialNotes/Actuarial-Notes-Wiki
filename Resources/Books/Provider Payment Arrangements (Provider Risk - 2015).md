An October 2015 report prepared by Milliman for the Society of Actuaries on payment reform, in which providers and payers share financial risk to align incentives toward quality care at more affordable prices. It introduces a framework of four risks (utilization, technical, insurance and performance), defines eight payment models, walks through the pricing process, and works ten case studies.

## Glossary of Terms and Abbreviations

## I Executive Summary
- Payment reform is organization-specific, and a framework of four risks is introduced: utilization risk, technical risk, insurance risk and performance risk.
- Eight payment models are defined: fee-for-service, global capitation, shared savings, DRG/case rates, bundled payments, reference pricing, provider excess loss reinsurance and pay-for-performance.
- Ten case studies illustrate the general pricing process.
- Success in provider payment arrangements comes down to good holistic risk management by the payment reform team.

## II Introduction
- The report was prepared for public educational purposes for a multidisciplinary audience of providers, health insurers, health actuaries, policymakers and those pursuing an actuarial career.
- The main body has four sections: the risks and payment models, general pricing implications, ten case studies, and best practices and key takeaways.
