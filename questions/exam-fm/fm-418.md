A credit card charges an annual continuously compounded interest rate of 18%, plus a fee of 20 at the end of each year. These fees accrue interest once they are charged.

A borrower, upon receiving the credit card, immediately purchased a piano for 1500 with the card.

The borrower then made a payment of $X$ at the end of every month. At the beginning of the third year, the borrower's credit card balance was 200.

Determine which of the following is an equation of value that can be used to solve for $X$.

- A) $1500 + 20e^{-0.18} + 20e^{-0.36} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} e^{-0.015k} = 200e^{-0.36}$
- B) $1500 + 20e^{-0.18} + 20e^{-0.36} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} e^{-0.015k} = 200$
- C) $1500 + 20e^{0.18} + 20e^{0.36} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} e^{0.015k} = 200e^{0.36}$
- D) $1500 + \dfrac{20}{1.18} + \dfrac{20}{(1.18)^{2}} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} \dfrac{20}{(1.18)^{\frac{k}{12}}} = \dfrac{200}{(1.18)^{2}}$
- E) $1500 + \dfrac{20}{1.18} + \dfrac{20}{(1.18)^{2}} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} \dfrac{20}{(1.18)^{\frac{k}{12}}} = 200$

## Explanation

Take present values at time 0, discounting at the force of interest $\delta = 0.18$, so that an amount at time $t$ years is multiplied by $e^{-0.18t}$.

The amount charged to the card was 1500 immediately. The annual fees were 20 after 1 year and 20 after 2 years. The monthly payments were $X$ after $\dfrac{k}{12}$ years, for each whole number $k$ from 1 to 24 inclusive. Then to make the final balance 0, the final payment would have been 200 after 2 years.

Therefore, the present value of the charges equals the present value of the payments:

$$
\begin{align*}
1500 + 20e^{-0.18(1)} + 20e^{-0.18(2)} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} e^{-0.18\left(\frac{k}{12}\right)} - 200e^{-0.18(2)} &= 0 \\
1500 + 20e^{-0.18} + 20e^{-0.36} - X\sum_{k=1}^{24} e^{-0.015k} &= 200e^{-0.36}
\end{align*}
$$
