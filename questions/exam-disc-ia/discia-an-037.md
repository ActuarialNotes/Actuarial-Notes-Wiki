A house with a full replacement cost of \$300,000 is insured under an HO-3 for \$250,000, with a \$500 deductible. A kitchen fire causes damage that costs \$40,000 to repair with materials of like kind and quality; depreciation on the damaged part is 30%. The insured completes the repairs for \$40,000. What does the insurer pay?

- A) \$27,500
- B) \$32,833
- C) \$39,500
- D) \$40,000

## Explanation

The HO-3 pays replacement cost without deduction for depreciation when the insurance is at least 80% of the dwelling's full replacement cost.

$$
\begin{align*}
0.80 \times 300{,}000 &= 240{,}000 \\
\text{Payment} &= 40{,}000 - 500 \\
&= 39{,}500
\end{align*}
$$

The \$250,000 limit meets the \$240,000 requirement, so the repair cost is paid in full less the deductible. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$27,500 pays actual cash value, $40{,}000 \times 0.70 - 500$, which applies only if the insurance were well below the requirement.
- **B** — \$32,833 applies a proportion of $\tfrac{250}{300}$, as if the requirement were 100% of replacement cost.
- **D** — \$40,000 forgets the deductible.
