## [F-001/R] Correction applied
- entry_type: resolution
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-10-18T18:41Z/4d03
- date: 2026-10-18
- resolves: F-001
- status: resolved
- note: AAL clause confirmed: Humphreys (CAS E-Forum 2021) p.16 defines the AEP A(x) as the survival function of the annual aggregate S and writes E[S] = integral of A(x) dx — the area under the AEP curve is the expected annual loss, the AAL of p.2 (sum p_i X_i, the errata's Table 2.1 form). Capital bullet restated: 'supports little leverage and needs high margin ratios' and 'the main reason insurers buy reinsurance' are in no source read and were cut; it now says catastrophes produce losses correlated in space and time that break the law of large numbers (IIASA RR-00-20 pp.3-4), that in the highest layers a reinsurer's diversification advantage becomes negligible and it backs each dollar of exposure with a dollar of capital (Braun & Kousky p.6), and that insurers transfer it through reinsurance and choose which accumulations to hold (IIASA p.4: insurers must deliberately select the dependent risks they cover). Also restated this run: 'a changing climate shifts the hazard itself' to 'alters future catastrophe losses' (IAIS presentation p.8).
