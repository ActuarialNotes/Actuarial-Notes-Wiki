A first course in probability for students with a basic calculus background who are mostly interested in its applications to risk management. The authors name insurance, finance, economics and the health sciences as those areas, claim complete coverage of the SOA Exam P syllabus, and favour a practical, intuitive style over formal proofs. The third edition revises the text throughout, enhancing or adding the bivariate normal distribution, probability generating functions, the coefficient of variation, order statistics and the correlation coefficient.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam P-1 (SOA)|Exam P]] — a suggested text (the syllabus requires none); Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5; Chapter 6: 6.1, 6.2.1; Chapter 7; Chapter 8 (exclude 8.5, 8.6, 8.7); Chapter 9 (exclude 9.2, 9.3, 9.4, 9.6); Chapter 10 (exclude 10.2, 10.3.2, 10.3.3 continuous, 10.4.2); Chapter 11 (exclude 11.1.4, 11.1.5, 11.2.3 continuous, 11.2.5 continuous, 11.2.8, 11.3).
