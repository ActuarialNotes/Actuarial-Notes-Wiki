You are given the following information about the waiting time until a certain number of events occur:

- The underlying events follow a homogenous Poisson process
- $T_n$ is the time until the $n^{\text{th}}$ event occurs
- $E[T_2] = 2$

Calculate the variance of $T_{10}$.

- A) Less than 4
- B) At least 4, but less than 8
- C) At least 8, but less than 12
- D) At least 12, but less than 16
- E) At least 16

## Explanation

In a homogeneous Poisson process with rate $\lambda$, the interarrival times are independent exponentials with mean $1/\lambda$, so the waiting time until the $n^{\text{th}}$ event is a sum of $n$ of them — a gamma distribution with

$$
E[T_n] = \frac{n}{\lambda}, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(T_n) = \frac{n}{\lambda^2}.
$$

The given mean pins down the rate: $E[T_2] = 2/\lambda = 2$, so $\lambda = 1$. Then

$$
\operatorname{Var}(T_{10}) = \frac{10}{1^2} = 10.
$$

This falls in $[8, 12)$, so the answer is **C**.
