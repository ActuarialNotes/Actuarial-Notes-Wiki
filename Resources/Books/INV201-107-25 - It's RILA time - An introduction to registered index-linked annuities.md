A paper by Thorsten Moenig, published in the Journal of Risk and Insurance in 2022, that introduces registered index-linked annuities (RILAs) to the academic literature. It describes the products' key features, develops a general pricing model and derives the providers' hedging strategy by decomposing their liabilities into short-term European options. The SOA supplies it as study note INV201-107-25.
