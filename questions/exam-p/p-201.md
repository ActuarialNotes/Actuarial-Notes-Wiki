A group of 100 patients is tested, one patient at a time, for three risk factors for a certain disease until either all patients have been tested or a patient tests positive for more than one of these three risk factors. For each risk factor, a patient tests positive with probability $p$, where $0 < p < 1$. The outcomes of the tests across all patients and all risk factors are mutually independent.

Determine an expression for the probability that exactly $n$ patients are tested, where $n$ is a positive integer less than 100.

- A) $\left[1-3p^2(1-p)\right]^{n-1}\left[3p^2(1-p)\right]$
- B) $\left[1-3p^2(1-p)-p^3\right]^{n-1}\left[3p^2(1-p)+p^3\right]$
- C) $\left[1-3p^2(1-p)-p^3\right]\left[3p^2(1-p)+p^3\right]^{n-1}$
- D) $n\left[1-3p^2(1-p)-p^3\right]^{n-1}\left[3p^2(1-p)+p^3\right]$
- E) $3\left[(1-p)^{n-1}p\right]^2\left[1-(1-p)^{n-1}p\right]+\left[(1-p)^{n-1}p\right]^3$

## Explanation

The number of risk factors a patient tests positive for is binomial with 3 trials and probability $p$, so a patient tests positive for more than one of them with probability
$$
\begin{align*}
s &= \binom{3}{2}p^2(1-p) + \binom{3}{3}p^3 \\
  &= 3p^2(1-p) + p^3
\end{align*}
$$

Exactly $n<100$ patients are tested when each of the first $n-1$ patients tests positive for at most one factor and patient $n$ tests positive for more than one. The patients are independent, so the number tested is geometric with success probability $s$:
$$
\begin{align*}
P(\text{exactly } n \text{ tested}) &= (1-s)^{n-1}s \\
  &= \left[1-3p^2(1-p)-p^3\right]^{n-1}\left[3p^2(1-p)+p^3\right]
\end{align*}
$$
