A study note, Version 1.3a, written by Mary R. Hardy for the Education Committee of the Society of Actuaries and formatted for SOA Course GI 301, Further Topics in General Insurance. Its author's objective is to present both deterministic and some stochastic methods and models for estimating outstanding claims liabilities, in a form suitable for both classroom teaching and self-study, balancing rigour and accessibility. It uses the R package ChainLadder extensively in Chapters 4 and 5.
