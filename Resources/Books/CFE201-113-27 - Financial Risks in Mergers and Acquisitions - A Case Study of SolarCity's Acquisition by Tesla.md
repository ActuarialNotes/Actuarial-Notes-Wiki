A paper in Frontiers in Business, Economics and Management (Vol. 11, No. 3, 2023) that appraises Tesla's 2016 acquisition of SolarCity. Using quarterly data from Tesla and a mixed approach, it ascertains the changes in various aspects of Tesla's financial risk, and finds that liquidity risk and solvency risk characterize the post-acquisition period, with solvency ratios appearing to improve over time.
