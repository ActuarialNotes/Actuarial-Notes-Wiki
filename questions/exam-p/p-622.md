Let $X$ be a random variable representing the time required to fix a flat tire on a car.

You are given the following information:

i. $X$ is uniform on $[a, b]$.
ii. The 50th percentile of $X$ is 16.36.
iii. The standard deviation of $X$ is 7.63.

Calculate $\dfrac{b}{a}$.

- A) $1.83$
- B) $2.12$
- C) $4.58$
- D) $6.62$
- E) $9.41$

## Explanation

For a uniform distribution the 50th percentile is the midpoint, so

$$
\frac{a + b}{2} = 16.36 \implies a = 32.72 - b
$$

The variance is $(b-a)^2/12$, so the standard deviation is $(b - a)/\sqrt{12}$:

$$
\frac{b - a}{\sqrt{12}} = 7.63 \implies b - a = 7.63\sqrt{12} = 26.431095
$$

Substituting the first equation into the second:

$$
\begin{align*}
b - (32.72 - b) &= 26.431095 \\
2b &= 59.151095 \\
b &= 29.5755
\end{align*}
$$

and then $a = 32.72 - 29.5755 = 3.1445$. Therefore

$$
\frac{b}{a} = \frac{29.5755}{3.1445} = \mathbf{9.4056}
$$

Choice (A), 1.83, reads statement (iii) as a *variance* rather than a standard deviation, giving $b - a = \sqrt{12(7.63)} = 9.569$ and a ratio of 1.83. Notice how sensitive the ratio is: the midpoint is fixed at 16.36, so a wider spread pushes $a$ toward zero and the ratio $b/a$ climbs steeply.
