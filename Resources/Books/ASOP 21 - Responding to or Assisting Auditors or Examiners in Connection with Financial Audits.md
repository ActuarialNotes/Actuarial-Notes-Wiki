The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries performing actuarial services while responding to or assisting auditors or examiners in connection with a financial audit, financial review, or financial examination. This revised edition (Doc. No. 183) was adopted in September 2016 and is effective for fiscal periods beginning on or after December 15, 2016.

> - [[Exam GH-201U (SOA)|Exam GH-201U]] — Outcomes 2e; the standard excluding its Appendices.

## Transmittal Memorandum

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
-     - Applies to actuaries acting as a responding actuary or a reviewing actuary in connection with a financial audit or financial review under generally accepted auditing standards, or a financial examination for oversight of an entity's financial condition.
-     - Does not apply to services in connection with filings such as rate filings, tax returns, or the schedules of actuarial information filed with the Form 5500, nor to the audit, review, or examination of contract performance.
- 1.3 Cross References
- 1.4 Effective Date
-     - Effective for actuarial work in connection with a financial audit, financial review, or financial examination for fiscal periods beginning on or after December 15, 2016.
