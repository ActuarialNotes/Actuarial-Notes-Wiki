A gas explosion destroys the house next door to an HO-3 (HO 00 03 05 11) insured. The insured's house is undamaged, but the fire department and city order the street closed and bar residents from their homes for three weeks. The family's living costs rise by \$150 a day while they stay in a hotel. How much does Coverage D pay?

- A) \$0
- B) \$1,050
- C) \$2,100
- D) \$3,150

## Explanation

Coverage D's civil authority provision applies when a civil authority prohibits use of the residence premises because of direct damage to neighboring premises by a peril insured against, here explosion. It pays additional living expense, but for **no more than two weeks**:

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Paid} &= 14 \times 150 \\
&= 2{,}100
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** assumes Coverage D needs a covered loss to the insured's own home; the civil authority provision is the exception.
- **B** pays for one week only.
- **D** pays for the full three weeks, past the two-week limit.
