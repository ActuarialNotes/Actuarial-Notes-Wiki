A paper submitted by Paulette Tino and Edward Sypher and published as the whole of The Pension Forum, Volume 14, Number 1 (September 2002). It describes a variety of methods, called asset valuation methods, used to set the value of a plan's assets at a figure other than fair market value when calculating the required contributions for a plan subject to ERISA's minimum funding requirements, and discusses the statutory and regulatory requirements for those methods under ERISA.
