As part of business continuity management, a company estimates how quickly the cost of losing its order-processing activity would grow over hours and days, whatever caused the outage, and which resources the activity depends on. What is this analysis?

- A) A risk assessment
- B) A tabletop exercise
- C) A root cause analysis
- D) A business impact analysis

## Explanation

The **business impact analysis** lists activities and their dependencies and measures how the impact of losing each grows over time, regardless of cause. It ranks activities for recovery and sets the basis for recovery objectives. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A risk assessment judges which threats could cause an interruption and how likely they are, the question the BIA deliberately sets aside.
- **B** — A tabletop exercise tests a plan by discussion; it comes after the plan exists.
- **C** — Root cause analysis works back from a loss to its cause, which is not what is being measured.
