Under the ISO homeowners form (HO 00 03), which statement about settling a property damage claim is correct?

- A) The insurer may elect to repair or replace, by notice within 30 days of the proof of loss
- B) Loss is payable 30 days after the fire, whether or not a proof of loss is filed
- C) The insurer must pay full replacement cost before any repairs are made
- D) Payment always goes to the insured, even when a mortgagee is named

## Explanation

If the insurer gives written notice within 30 days after receiving the insured's signed, sworn proof of loss, it may repair or replace any part of the damaged property with property of like kind and quality. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — loss is payable 60 days after the insurer receives the proof of loss and the parties agree, a final judgment is entered or an appraisal award is filed.
- **C** — the HO-3's replacement cost settlement is tied to the cost actually spent to repair or replace; the form does not require full replacement cost to be paid first.
- **D** — the insurer pays the insured unless someone else is named or legally entitled; a named mortgagee is paid with the insured as interests appear.
