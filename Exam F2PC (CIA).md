# Exam F2PC
**F2PC – Estimating Unpaid Claims and Financial Reporting under IFRS 17 - Insurance Contracts** is an exam of the property and casualty track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques for estimating unpaid claims and claims-related expenses (hereafter referred to simply as “claims”) that are relevant to P&C insurance (including reinsurance).
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of P&C insurance.
>
> 3. Evaluate the considerations involved in selecting a best estimate of unpaid claims.
>
> 4. Understand how estimating unpaid claims links to wider business processes (e.g., business planning, pricing, financial reporting, and capital setting).
>
> 5. Describe IFRS 17 - Insurance Contracts (hereafter referred to as “IFRS 17”) and calculate actuarial values for financial reporting under IFRS 17.
>
> 6. Create appropriate proposals and recommendations related to estimates of unpaid claims and IFRS 17 financial reporting in P&C insurance business.
>
> 7. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgement to assess the implications of possible actions.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA’s learning management system. The ranges of weights (expressed in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of exams administered. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in the estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]] for P&C [[Insurer|insurers]]. (A1)

> [!example]- Regulations and applicable standards {15%}
>
> 1. Apply legislation (e.g., the [[Insurance Companies Act]]) and regulations (e.g., Office of the Superintendent of [[Financial Institutions Guideline|Financial Institutions guidelines]]) relevant to the [[Appointed Actuary's Valuation|Appointed Actuary’s valuation]] for [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]] purposes. (A3)
> 2. Apply actuarial [[Professional Responsibilities of the Actuary|professionalism requirements]] applicable to the estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]]. (B3)

> [!example]- Data {10%}
>
> 1. Differentiate the types of data (including [[Claim|claims]] and [[Exposure Base|exposures]]) and information required for estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]], their sources, and main uses. (A4)
> 2. Build [[Development Triangle|development triangles]]. (C3)
> 3. Differentiate the types of data and information required for [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]], their sources, and main uses. (A4)
> 4. Apply the concepts of [[Homogeneity|homogeneity]] and [[Credibility|credibility]] of data in estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and for [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]]. (B3)
> 5. Understand the implications to data requirements of different characteristics of insurance (e.g., [[Long Tail|long tail]] vs. [[Short Tail|short tail]], [[Low Frequency-high Severity|low frequency/high severity]] vs. [[High Frequency-low Severity Line|high frequency/low severity lines]] of business). (B2)
> 6. Organize data by [[Calendar Year|calendar year]], [[Accident Year|accident year]], [[Policy Year|policy year]], [[Underwriting Year|underwriting year]], and [[Report Year|report year]], and understand when each is appropriate. (C3)
> 7. Evaluate the influence of the [[Insurer's Internal Environment|insurer’s internal environment]] (such as changes in operations or information technology) on data used for estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and [[IFRS]] 17. (B5)
> 8. Evaluate the influence of [[External Environment|external environments]] (such as economic and judicial decisions) on data used for estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and [[IFRS]] 17. (B5)
> 9. Understand the possible causes of [[Data Error|data errors]] and the effects of [[Inadequate Data|inadequate data]] and information. (B2)
> 10. Create appropriate [[Data-Validation Process|data-validation processes]]. (C6)

> [!example]- Estimating unpaid claims – basic topics {25%}
>
> 1. Describe the purpose of estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for P&C [[Insurer|insurers]], including:
>    - a. The reasons for calculating estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and how these might influence the approach (such as [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]], financial planning and analysis, and pricing) (A2); and
>    - b. The key stakeholders and their [[Interest|interests]] in the results of estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (A2)
> 2. Use [[Development Triangle|development triangles]] (including [[Claim|claims]], [[Allocated Loss Adjustment Expense|allocated loss-adjustment expense]], counts, ratios, and [[Average Value|average values]]) as a diagnostic tool. (D4)
> 3. Analyze the range of general issues that can affect estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] using triangle-based techniques and identify how to deal with such issues. (D4)
> 4. Calculate and evaluate estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] using the following techniques: (C5)
>    - a. Development;
>    - b. Expected [[Claim|claim]];
>    - c. [[Bornhuetter-Ferguson Method|Bornhuetter-Ferguson]];
>    - d. [[Cape Cod]];
>    - e. Benktander;
>    - f. [[Frequency Severity|Frequency severity]]; and
>    - g. Berquist-Sherman.
> 5. For each of the above techniques:
>    - a. Understand the assumptions and mechanics. (C2)
>    - b. Analyze assumption changes and determine their influence on the results of estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (C4)
>    - c. Evaluate the technique when used with varying [[Data Quality|data quality]] and completeness and in different situations. (D5)
>    - d. Describe the strengths and limitations of each technique and the impact of limitations on results. (D2)
>    - e. Select the appropriate technique and assumptions given specific circumstances. (D5)
> 6. Apply processes to integrate more complex factors potentially relevant for the estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] of a given [[Claims Portfolio|claims portfolio]].
>    - a. Reflect the circumstances under which [[Claims Period|claims periods]] defined by [[Underwriting Period|underwriting period]], [[Accident Period|accident period]], and [[Reporting Period|reporting period]] approaches may be used. (C3)
>    - b. Distinguish between incurred but not yet reported (IBNYR) and incurred but not enough reported (IBNER) components of an estimate of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (C3)
>    - c. Apply and evaluate the [[Earned Premium|earned premium]] concept in various contexts of the estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (C5)
>    - d. Understand the drivers of [[Exposure Base|exposure]] changes for [[Claims Portfolio|claims portfolios]] (such as shifts in types of [[Exposure Base|exposures]] or [[Seasonality|seasonality]]) and adjust projection techniques to allow for such changes in estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (D4)
>    - e. Apply an allowance for [[Claims Inflation|claims inflation]] in the estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (C3)
>    - f. Measure and respond appropriately to changes in the [[Internal Environment|internal environment]] (such as changes in [[Claims-Processing System|claims-processing systems]] or the [[Mix of Business|mix of business]]) and the [[External Environment|external environment]] (such as judicial decisions). (D4)
>    - g. Execute on the concepts of a best estimate and a [[Risk|risk]] adjustment. (D6)
> 7. Determine whether to [[Discount|discount]] for [[Investment Income|investment income]] and adjust for [[Adverse Deviation|adverse deviation]] and calculate such adjustments to a best estimate of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (D5)

> [!example]- Estimation of unpaid claims – advanced topics {20%}
>
> 1. Describe, calculate, and evaluate approaches to allow for various other items in an [[Actuary's Estimation|actuary’s estimation]] of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]], including:
>    - a. Salvage and [[Subrogation Recovery|subrogation recovery]] items; (C5)
>    - b. [[Claims-management Expense|Claims-management expenses]] (including allocated and [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|unallocated loss-adjustment expenses]]) and other costs; (C5)
>    - c. [[Latent Claim|Latent claims]] and potential [[Claims Feature|claims features]] that have not manifested in the [[Reported Claims Data|reported claims data]]; and (C5)
>    - d. [[Discounted Cash Flow|Discounted cash flows]] such as [[No-Fault Automobile Accident Benefit|no-fault automobile accident benefits]] and other [[Recurring Benefit|recurring benefits]]. (C5)
> 2. Calculate estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]].
>    - a. Understand [[Reinsurance Cover|reinsurance cover]] considerations when [[Differentiating Losses-Occurring Basis|differentiating losses-occurring basis]] and a [[Risks-Attaching Basis|risks-attaching basis]] and considerations for [[Catastrophe Cover|catastrophe covers]]. (B2)
>    - b. Evaluate the approaches to estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for [[Reinsurance Contracts Held|reinsurance contracts held]] by analyzing [[Claims Gross|claims gross]] and net of [[Reinsurance Held|reinsurance held]] vs. [[Claims Gross|claims gross]] of [[Reinsurance Held|reinsurance held]] and [[Claim|claims]] ceded to [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]]. (C5)
>    - c. Calculate and evaluate estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for [[Reinsurance Held|reinsurance held]] using appropriate techniques and assumptions that reflect the characteristics of [[Reinsurance Contracts Held|reinsurance contracts held]] (e.g., contract terms and conditions, [[Data Availability|data availability]]). (C5)
>    - d. Determine the effect on relevant [[Earned Premium|earnings patterns]] and estimation of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] of [[Non-Annual Cover|non-annual covers]], [[Non-Uniform Risk|non-uniform risks]], common [[Renewal Date|renewal dates]], [[Unclosed Business|unclosed business]], adjustment [[Insurance Premium|premiums]], [[Risks Attaching|risks attaching]] at [[Future Date|future dates]], [[Reinstatements|reinstatement premiums]], and other [[Loss-Responsive Mechanism|loss-responsive mechanisms]]. (C3)
>    - e. Calculate and evaluate estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for various [[Layer of Insurance|layers]] of [[Claim|claims]]. (C5)
> 3. Develop estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] using [[Credibility|credibility]] models (C3), including:
>    - a. Testing the results for reasonableness; and (C4)
>    - b. Describing the strengths and weaknesses of such methods. (B2)
> 4. Evaluate [[Stochastic Processes|stochastic processes]] for estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]].
>    - a. Describe the likely sources of uncertainty in estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (B2)
>    - b. Describe the uses of stochastic techniques for estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (B2)
>    - c. Describe the following types of stochastic techniques, including data required, key assumptions, and mechanics: (B2)
>       - i. Analytic; and
>       - ii. Simulation-based.
>    - d. Differentiate Mack, bootstrapping, and [[Markov Chain Monte Carlo|Markov chain Monte Carlo]] approaches for estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and quantifying uncertainty in such estimates. (C4)
>    - e. Describe the features of aggregate [[Aggregate Loss Model|claims distributions]], as well as the inherent [[Risk Correlation|risk correlations]] and diversification that apply. (C2)
>    - f. Extend a frequency-severity technique to include stochasticity. (C3)
>    - g. Describe the issues, advantages, and disadvantages of each of the techniques, including sensitivity to assumptions. (C2)
>    - h. Describe the approach to aggregating the results of [[Stochastic Estimate|stochastic estimates]] of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] across [[Multiple Line|multiple lines]] of business and discuss methods of [[Correlation|correlation]]. (C2)
> 5. Describe machine-learning approaches with [[Individual Claim Case Reserve|individual claim case reserves]], including the advantages and challenges with such approaches. (C2)
> 6. Evaluate the results of estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for adequacy and reasonableness. (D5)
>    - a. Understand how alternative estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] can arise and highlight some of the professional issues in resolving them. (D2)
>    - b. Describe the factors an [[Actuary|actuary]] should consider and evaluate the typical diagnostics used in assessing the reasonableness of the results of estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (D4)
>    - c. Describe the factors an [[Actuary|actuary]] should consider in assessing the reasonableness of changes in the results of estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] over time. (D2)
>    - d. Analyze current experience to monitor performance and recommend estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] for [[Interim Valuation|interim valuations]]. (D4)
> 7. Communicate an estimate of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]].
>    - a. Describe what is meant by a “[[Best-Estimate Reserve|best-estimate reserve]].” (A2)
>    - b. Describe the uses, advantages, and disadvantages of estimating ranges of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] using the following approaches: (B2)
>       - i. Stochastic models;
>       - ii. Scenario tests; and
>       - iii. Use of alternative sets of assumptions.
>    - c. Defend the issues to be considered when [[Communicating Range|communicating ranges]] in estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] and uncertainties. (D5)

> [!example]- IFRS17 financial reporting {30%}
>
> 1. Describe [[IFRS 17]] – Accounting for [[Insurance Contract|Insurance Contracts]] including its purpose, scope, classification of contracts, and [[Contract Boundary|contract boundaries]]. (A2)
> 2. Apply both [[IFRS 17 Measurement|IFRS 17 measurement]] approaches: (C3)
>    - a. General measurement approach; and
>    - b. [[Premium Allocation Approach|Premium allocation approach]].
> 3. Reflect considerations related to the [[Level of Aggregation|level of aggregation]] when accounting for business under [[IFRS 17]] including determination of the unit of account and setting of [[Portfolio|portfolios]] and groups of contracts. (C3)
> 4. Estimate [[Future Cash Flow|future cash flows]] for the liability for remaining coverage and the [[Liability for Incurred Claims|liability for incurred claims]] under both the general measurement approach and the [[Premium Allocation Approach|premium allocation approach]]. (C4)
> 5. Determine [[Discount Rate|discount rates]] using the [[Top Down|top down]] and bottom-up approaches referred to in [[IFRS]] 17. (C3)
> 6. Determine [[Risk|risk]] adjustment for [[Non-Financial Risk|non-financial risk]], including:
>    - a. Reflect the criteria for and measurement of the [[Risk|risk]] adjustment under the general measurement approach and the [[Premium Allocation Approach|premium allocation approach]]. (C4)
>    - b. Apply multiple methods, including: (C4)
>       - i. Quantile;
>       - ii. [[Cost of Capital|Cost of capital]]; and
>       - iii. Margin.
>    - c. Adjust for the role of diversification and allocation in the determination and the application of the [[Risk|risk]] adjustment. (C4)
>    - d. Determine the [[Risk|risk]] adjustment for [[Reinsurance Contracts Held|reinsurance contracts held]]. (C4)
> 7. Determine key actuarial components of [[IFRS 17 Financial Statement|IFRS 17 financial statements]], including:
>    - a. [[Contractual Service Margin|Contractual service margin]]; (C4)
>    - b. Liability for remaining coverage; (C4)
>    - c. [[Liability for Incurred Claims|Liability for incurred claims]]; (C4)
>    - d. [[Risk]] adjustment for [[Non-Financial Risk|non-financial risk]]; (C4)
>    - e. Discounting; (C4)
>    - f. [[Onerous Contract|Onerous contracts]] (C4); and
>    - g. [[Loss Component|Loss component]] (C4);
> 8. Describe the components ([[Financial Statements|financial statements]], key exhibits, and schedules) of the [[Canadian Regulatory Return|Canadian regulatory return]] (P&C-1, P&C-2 and P&C-3) and how they interrelate. (B2)
> 9. Calculate key [[Key Financial Measures|financial metrics]] and evaluate an [[Insurer's Financial Performance|insurer’s financial performance]] and strength based on such ratios. (D5)
> 10. Describe the [[Appointed Actuary's|Appointed Actuary’s]] role with respect to [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]]. (B2)
