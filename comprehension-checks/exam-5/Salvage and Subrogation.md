An insurer totals a vehicle and sells the wreck for scrap. Separately, it recovers what it paid out from the at-fault driver's carrier. Which recovery is which?

- A) The scrap proceeds are salvage; the recovery from the other carrier is subrogation
- B) The scrap proceeds are subrogation; the recovery from the other carrier is salvage
- C) Both count as salvage, since both reduce the net loss
- D) Both count as subrogation, since both involve recovering paid amounts

<!-- rationale: 1: swaps the pair outright · 2: collapses them on the shared effect of reducing net losses · 3: collapses them on the shared mechanism of recovering money already paid -->
