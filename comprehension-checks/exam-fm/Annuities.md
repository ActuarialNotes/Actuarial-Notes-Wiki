Two streams each pay \$3,000 in total: stream X pays \$1,000 at the end of each of years 1–3, while stream Y pays the whole \$3,000 at the end of year 3. At a positive interest rate, how do their present values compare?

- A) X is worth more — its payments are discounted over shorter periods
- B) They are equal — the same total is paid either way
- C) Y is worth more — a single payment escapes being discounted three separate times
- D) It depends entirely on whether payments fall at the start or the end of each period
