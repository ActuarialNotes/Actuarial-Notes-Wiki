**Part III — SAP in the U.S.: fundamental aspects of the annual statement** (chapters 6–15, covering the statutory balance sheet, income statement, capital and surplus account, notes, general interrogatories, the five-year historical data exhibit, the schedules, **Schedule F** and **Schedule P**) is **excluded**. ## What chapters 1–5 supply

- **Why an actuary should care about accounting.** The actuary's estimate is an input to financial statements read by regulators, investors, [[Rating Agency|rating agencies]] and policyholders; the frame it is presented in determines what it means. Chapter 2 is the argument the rest of Domain C assumes.
- **The primary financial statements** — balance sheet, income statement, statement of changes in equity, cash flow statement — and what each is for.
- **Key accounting concepts** — accrual versus cash accounting, recognition and measurement, the going-concern assumption, conservatism, matching, [[Materiality|materiality]], and above all the different **purposes** of the reporting frameworks: statutory accounting exists to measure solvency, GAAP/IFRS to measure performance for investors, and tax accounting to measure taxable income. The same insurer's "surplus," "equity" and "capital" differ across them, and that is by design.

## Chapters 21, 25 and 27

Chapter 21 (**measurement tools** for the financial health of P&C insurers, in Part V) provides the analytical vocabulary — the ratios and diagnostics — that [[MSA Legend]] and [[Feldblum]] both assume. The assigned later chapters 25 and 27 sit outside Part III's U.S. statutory material.

## Reading a U.S.-framed text on a Canadian exam

The book is written to U.S. statutory accounting. What transfers is the **structure of the reasoning**: what a financial statement is for, how a reserve estimate becomes a reported number, and how a solvency framework differs from a performance framework. Where the book says *Annual Statement*, read [[Canadian Annual Return]]; where it says *Schedule P*, read page 60.45 of the [[OSFI Annual Return|PC3]]; where it says *risk-based capital*, read [[MCT]].
