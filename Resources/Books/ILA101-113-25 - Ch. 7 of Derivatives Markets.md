Interest Rate Forwards and Futures, the seventh chapter of Derivatives Markets by Robert L. McDonald, third edition, in the book's Part Two on forwards, futures and swaps. The publisher describes the book as giving business students a solid understanding of derivatives concepts and instruments, and of how they are used in corporations, by linking theories and concepts together with an engaging narrative that emphasises the core economic principles underlying the pricing and uses of derivatives.
