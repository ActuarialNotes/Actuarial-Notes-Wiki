A Harvard Business Review article of October 2005 (reprint R0510G) on why change programmes depend on hard factors as well as the soft issues of culture and motivation. Studying change initiatives at 225 companies, the authors found a consistent correlation between the outcomes of change programs and four hard factors, which they called DICE: project duration, integrity of performance, commitment, and the additional effort required of those affected. The DICE framework scores a project on each; the lower the score, the more likely the project will succeed.
