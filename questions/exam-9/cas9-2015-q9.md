A company entered into a 10-year currency swap.

Given the following information:

- The company receives 10% per annum in Mexican Pesos.
- The company pays 6% per annum in US Dollars.
- LIBOR = 5.0% United States Dollar (USD).
- LIBOR = 8.0% Mexican Peso (MXN).
- LIBOR Rates are per annum with continuous compounding.
- The principals are 10 million in US Dollars and 120 million in Mexican Pesos.

There are four years left on the swap and the current exchange rate is 1 USD to 11.5 MXN.

Determine the value of the swap in US Dollars.

### Explanation
**Method 1.** The company receives the pesos and pays out dollars. Discount each currency's cash flows at its own LIBOR, continuously compounded:

| $t$ | Payments USD (\$M) | Discount at 5.0% | Discounted payments (\$) | Receives MXN (M) | Discount at 8.0% | Discounted receipts (MXN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.6 | 0.9512 | 570,738 | 12 | 0.9231 | 11,077,396 |
| 2 | 0.6 | 0.9048 | 542,902 | 12 | 0.8521 | 10,225,725 |
| 3 | 0.6 | 0.8607 | 516,425 | 12 | 0.7866 | 9,439,534 |
| 4 | 10.6 | 0.8187 | 8,678,546 | 132 | 0.7261 | 95,851,673 |
| Total | | | 10,308,611 | | | 126,594,329 |

The current value to the company is 126,594,329 / 11.5 - \$10,308,611 = \$699,592, where 11.5 is the current exchange rate.

**Method 2.** Payments in millions; the numbers shown are unrounded.

| (1) $t$ | (2) Payments USD | (3) Receives MXN | (4) Pesos conversion factor | (5) Receives in \$ = (3) × (4) | (6) Net payment (\$) = (5) - (2) | (7) Discounted at 5% (\$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.6 | 12 | 0.084387 | 1.012639 | 0.412639 | 0.392514 |
| 2 | 0.6 | 12 | 0.081893 | 0.982711 | 0.382711 | 0.346291 |
| 3 | 0.6 | 12 | 0.079472 | 0.953667 | 0.353667 | 0.304404 |
| 4 | 10.6 | 132 | 0.077124 | 10.180304 | -0.419696 | -0.343618 |
| Total (\$M) | | | | | | 0.699592 |

Column (4) $= e^{(0.05 - 0.08)t} / 11.5$, the factor that converts column (3) from pesos to dollars.

Due to different rounding methods in intermediate steps, different final values would be arrived at, and accepted as correct, given the rounding used. The range of answers receiving full credit was \$695,000 to \$710,000.

### Examiner Report
The candidates were expected to lay out the future payment patterns, discount at the appropriate rates, and convert the pesos into dollars from the company's perspective.

Candidates did well overall with most candidates obtaining full credit or making one error.

Common mistakes included:

- misinterpreting the payment patterns to go from t = 0-3, 0-4, 1-10 or 7-10
- missing the final exchange of principal at t=4
- not using continuous compounding to discount
- miscalculating the payment patterns
- calculating the final step backwards from the Company's perspective, and getting -699,592
- calculation errors

In doing Method 2 above:

- miscalculating column (4), usually getting the exponent (.05-.08) backwards
- not discounting by 5% as a final step (Col 7, above)
