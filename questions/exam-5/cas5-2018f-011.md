Given the following for a large deductible commercial general liability policy:

| | |
|---|---|
| \$500,000 | Per occurrence deductible |
| 90.0% | Loss elimination ratio for a \$500,000 deductible |
| 10.0% | ALAE/ground up loss ratio |
| \$2,000,000 | Ground up loss estimate |
| \$100,000 | Fixed expenses |
| 12.0% | Variable expenses as % of premium |
| 4.0% | Underwriting profit as % of premium |
| 3.0% | Deductible processing cost as a % of losses below the deductible |
| 1.0% | Credit risk as a % of losses below the deductible |
| 7.0% | Additional risk margin as a % of excess losses |

- The insurer will handle all claims, including those that fall below the deductible.
- The insurer will make the payments on all claims and will seek reimbursement for amounts below the deductible from the insured.
- The deductible is for loss only.
- All ALAE is paid by the insurer.

## Part a (1.75 points)

Calculate the premium for the large deductible policy.

### Answer

\$697,619

### Explanation

Split the ground-up losses using the loss elimination ratio. The insured reimburses the losses below the deductible, so only the **excess** losses are a loss cost to the insurer:

$$\text{Losses below deductible} = 2{,}000{,}000 \times 0.90 = 1{,}800{,}000$$

$$\text{Excess losses} = 2{,}000{,}000 \times (1 - 0.90) = 200{,}000$$

All ALAE is paid by the insurer, on ground-up losses, so it is not reduced by the deductible:

$$\text{ALAE} = 2{,}000{,}000 \times 0.10 = 200{,}000$$

The deductible-specific charges are stated as percentages of the losses **below** the deductible, since that is the amount the insurer fronts and must recover from the insured:

$$\text{Deductible processing} = 0.03 \times 1{,}800{,}000 = 54{,}000$$

$$\text{Credit risk} = 0.01 \times 1{,}800{,}000 = 18{,}000$$

The additional risk margin applies to the excess layer:

$$\text{Risk margin} = 0.07 \times 200{,}000 = 14{,}000$$

These are all fixed dollar costs, so they belong in the numerator; only variable expenses and the profit provision go in the denominator:

$$P = \frac{200{,}000 + 200{,}000 + 100{,}000 + 54{,}000 + 18{,}000 + 14{,}000}{1 - 0.12 - 0.04} = \frac{586{,}000}{0.84} = 697{,}619$$

### Examiner Report

Candidates were expected to calculate the premium for a large deductible policy by correctly applying the loss elimination ratio and then including the appropriate additional costs and expenses.

Common mistakes included omitting fixed expenses, ALAE or the excess loss from the final calculation; calculating the excess loss as (ground up loss × LER) or (ground up loss − \$500,000 per occurrence deductible) rather than ground up loss × (1 − LER); using the wrong loss base for the credit risk and deductible processing charges; putting credit risk, the deductible processing fee or the risk margin in the denominator; using losses below the deductible rather than excess losses in the premium; and including only the ALAE associated with the excess layer, when the question states that all ALAE is paid by the insurer.
