A 2018 participants' report of a joint study sponsored by the Society of Actuaries and LIMRA, covering 2015 experience. It examines how variable annuity owners use their guaranteed living benefits (GLWBs, GMWBs, GMIBs and GMABs), drawing on over 4.9 million contracts issued during or in force as of 2015 from 20 participating insurers, and is an update of earlier investigations conducted since 2006.
