You are given an interest rate $i$, a real rate of interest $i'$, and an inflation rate $r$. All rates are annual effective rates.

Determine $i$, in terms of $i'$ and $r$.

- A) $i' + r$
- B) $i' + r + i'r$
- C) $i' + r - i'r$
- D) $(1+i') - (1+i')(1-r)$
- E) $i' + r - 2i'r$

## Explanation

The real rate of interest $i'$ is the rate left after inflation is taken out of the interest rate, $1 + i' = \dfrac{1+i}{1+r}$. Multiplying through by $1+r$ and expanding:

$$
\begin{align*}
(1+i')(1+r) &= 1+i \\
1 + i' + r + i'r &= 1+i \\
i &= i' + r + i'r
\end{align*}
$$
