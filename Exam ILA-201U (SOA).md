# Exam ILA-201U
**ILA 201-U – Valuation and Advanced Product and Risk Management, U.S.** is an exam of the Society of Actuaries' Fellowship (FSA) pathway. Its syllabus covers the Nov 2026/Mar 2027/Jul 2027 sittings.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- U.S. Financial Reporting Requirements {50–60%}
> The candidate will understand and apply U.S. [[Valuation Principle|valuation principles]] and methods applicable to [[Life Insurance|individual life insurance]] and [[Annuity Contract|annuity products]] issued by U.S. [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance companies]].
>
> 1. Describe, apply and evaluate the appropriate [[Valuation|valuation]] methods, requirements, and techniques and related under the [[Statutory Accounting Principles|US statutory]] framework, including the calculation of [[Principles-Based Reserving|principles-based reserves]] and [[Formulaic Reserve|formulaic reserves]]
> 2. Describe, apply and evaluate the appropriate [[Valuation|valuation]] methods, requirements, and techniques and related under the [[GAAP|US GAAP]] framework, including the calculation of [[Reserve|reserves]] and related items (e.g., DAC)
>
> **Readings:**

> [!example]- Capital Management {5–20%}
> The candidate will understand how to explain and apply the methods, approaches and tools of [[Regulatory Capital|financial capital]] management for [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance company]] under a US regulatory framework.
>
> 1. Explain and calculate [[Regulatory Capital|required capital]] under a US regulatory framework
> 2. Describe the purpose and application of [[Economic Capital|economic capital]]
> 3. Explain and evaluate the respective perspectives of regulators, investors, [[Policyholder|policyholders]] and [[Insurer|insurance company]] management regarding the role and determination of [[Capital|capital]]
> 4. Explain and apply methods in [[Capital|capital]] management
>
> **Readings:**
> - Statutory Valuation of Individual Life and Annuity Contracts, Volume II, Claire, D., Lombardi, L. and Summers, S., 5th Edition, 2018 (2a)
>     - Ch. 29: Risk-Based Capital
> - Economic Capital for Life Insurance Companies, SOA Research Paper, Oct 2016 (Sections 2 & 6) (2b)
> - A Multi-Stakeholder Approach to Capital Adequacy, Conning Research, Actuarial Practice Forum (2c)
> - ILA201-800-25: Theory of Risk Capital in Financial Firms (2d)

> [!example]- Management and Evaluation of Life Insurance Risks {5–20%}
> The candidate will understand types of [[Insurance Risk|life insurance risks]], the impacts of diversification, [[Crediting Rating Agency Framework|crediting rating agency frameworks]], and the assessment of [[Risk|risk]] management.
>
> 1. Analyze the impact of [[Risk Diversification|risk diversification]], including considerations for modeling and offsets between [[Mortality|mortality]] and [[Longevity Risk|longevity risk]]
> 2. Understand the role and framework used by [[Rating Agency|credit rating agencies]] for evaluating [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance companies]]
> 3. Explain and understand the use and application of the [[ORSA|Own Risk Solvency Assessment]] ([[ORSA]]) report
>
> **Readings:**
> - ILA201-801-25: Diversification of Longevity and Mortality Risk (3a)
> - ILA201-100-25: Diversification: Consideration on Modelling Aspects & Related Fungibility and Transferability, CRO, Oct 2013, pp. 1-18 (3a)
> - Rating Agency Perspectives on Insurance Company Capital, SOA Research Institute, Aug 2023 (excluding Appendices) (3b)
> - ILA201-802-25: NAIC Own Risk and Solvency Assessment (ORSA) Guidance Manual, National Association of Insurance Commissioners, Dec 2017 (3c)

> [!example]- Advanced Product Management {15–25%}
> The candidate will understand the fundamentals of value creation and [[In-Force Management|inforce]] management techniques for life and [[Annuity Contract|annuity products]].
>
> 1. Describe and evaluate [[In-Force Management|fundamental strategies]] for enhancing value and profitability through [[In-Force Management|active in-force]] and operational management
> 2. Recommend and justify changes to non-guaranteed elements and [[Policyholder Dividend|policyholder dividends]]
> 3. Describe, evaluate, and apply the [[Economic Value|economic value creation]] framework
> 4. Describe and apply the methods and principles of embedded value for an [[Embedded Value|insurance enterprise]]
> 5. Describe and calculate [[Reserve|reserves]] under [[US Company Tax Rule|US Company Tax rules]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - Evolving Strategies to Improve Inforce Post-Level Term Profitability, Product Matters, Feb 2015, pp. 23-28 (4a)
> - ILA201-101-25: Life in-force Management: Improving Consumer Value and Long-Term Profitability (4a)
> - Mechanics of Dividends, SOA Research Institute, Mar 2022 (4b)
> - ILA201-102-25: Economics of Insurance: How Insurers Create Value for Shareholders, pp. 4-31 (4c)
> - Embedded Value: Practice and Theory, Actuarial Practice Forum, Mar 2009 (4d)
> - FAQ on Certain Insurance Reserves Held by Insurance Companies for the Purpose of Determining U.S. Taxable Income after the Passage of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017, AAA, Dec 2021 (4e)
