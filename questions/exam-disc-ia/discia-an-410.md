A city resident sold her car and now owns no vehicle, but she rents cars several weekends a year and sometimes borrows her brother's. She wants personal auto liability, medical payments and uninsured motorists coverage that follows her. Which ISO endorsement fits?

- A) Extended non-owned coverage for vehicles furnished for regular use (PP 03 06)
- B) Joint ownership coverage (PP 03 34)
- C) Miscellaneous type vehicle endorsement (PP 03 23)
- D) Named non-owner coverage (PP 03 22)

## Explanation

Named non-owner coverage is written for an individual who owns no auto. It makes the person named in its schedule "you", provides liability, medical payments and uninsured motorists coverage, and treats only a newly acquired auto (for 14 days) as a covered auto. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — PP 03 06 is added to a PAP that already insures an owned car, to cover a vehicle furnished for the insured's *regular* use, such as a company car. Her rentals and borrowings are occasional.
- **B** — joint ownership coverage is for people who own a car together.
- **C** — the miscellaneous type vehicle endorsement insures owned motorcycles, motor homes, ATVs and similar vehicles.
