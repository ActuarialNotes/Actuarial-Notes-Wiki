![[Statutory Valuation of Individual Life and Annuity Contracts (Volume II - 2018) - Cover.svg]]

A textbook on the basic principles of the statutory valuation of individual life insurance and annuity contracts in the United States, including experience studies, model governance and risk-based capital. The fifth edition is divided into two volumes; Volume I focuses primarily on basic principles, the valuation process, model governance and formula-based reserves, and Volume II on principle-based reserves, experience studies and risk-based capital. It was written both for practicing actuaries and for those considering a career in financial reporting, product development or risk management.
