A text designed for teaching an introductory course in property/casualty insurance topics, giving an overview of the core principles and methodologies that are the building blocks of the property/casualty actuarial discipline. Its preface says the fifth edition incorporates the supplemental material to the fourth edition on the statistical foundations of the reserving methods, updates the terminology (using "reported losses" to avoid confusion with the accounting terminology of incurred losses), reflects industry changes over the past seven years and adds some exercises.
