## [F-001] '(as printed)' marks the wrong figure

- author: agent:validate-v1

- claim: The explanation says 216,377 is '(East^1.5 + West^1.5) raised to the power 1/1.5' and marks the phased figure 132,042 '(as printed)', i.e. as CAS's slip.
