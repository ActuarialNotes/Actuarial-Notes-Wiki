A producer licensed and in good standing in Georgia wants to sell to clients in Alabama, while continuing to live in Georgia. Under the NAIC Producer Licensing Model Act, what does Alabama require?

- A) She must pass Alabama's licensing examination, as a new resident applicant would, before selling there
- B) She must move to Alabama first, because the model lets a state license only its own residents
- C) Nothing, because a resident license in good standing in Georgia is valid in every other state
- D) She gets a nonresident license on application and fees, if Georgia reciprocates for Alabama residents

## Explanation

A nonresident receives a **nonresident producer license** if she is licensed as a resident and in good standing in her home state, applies and pays the fees, and her home state awards nonresident licenses to the other state's residents on the same basis. No further examination is needed.

- **A** applies the resident examination, which the model does not require of a licensed nonresident.
- **B** ignores nonresident licensing altogether.
- **C** overstates the home-state license. Each state licenses producers for business in that state, though it does so on reciprocal terms.
