An insurer has business reinsured through an excess of loss reinsurance arrangement and a stop loss limit reinsurance arrangement.

| Policy Year | Gross Ultimate Claims Estimate ($000) | Net of Excess of Loss Reinsurance — Net Ultimate Claims Estimate ($000) | Net of Excess of Loss Reinsurance — Claims as of December 31, 2014: Reported ($000) | Net of Excess of Loss Reinsurance — Claims as of December 31, 2014: Paid ($000) | Stop Loss Limit ($000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | 1,650 | 1,475 | 1,450 | 1,200 | 1,500 |
| 2013 | 1,800 | 1,750 | 1,600 | 1,200 | 1,500 |
| 2014 | 2,000 | 1,900 | 1,400 | 1,000 | 1,500 |

## Part a (0.75 points)

Calculate the IBNR for policy years 2012 through 2014 net of all reinsurance.

### Explanation
Net of *all* reinsurance means the excess of loss cover first, then the stop loss limit. Each policy year's net ultimate and net reported claims are therefore capped at the $\$1{,}500$k stop loss limit (all figures in $\$000$):

| Policy Year | (1) Net Ultimate After Stop Loss = min(net ult XOL, stop) | (2) Reported After Stop = min(reported net XOL, stop) | IBNR = (1) - (2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | $\min(1{,}475,\ 1{,}500) = 1{,}475$ | $\min(1{,}450,\ 1{,}500) = 1{,}450$ | $25$ |
| 2013 | $\min(1{,}750,\ 1{,}500) = 1{,}500$ | $\min(1{,}600,\ 1{,}500) = 1{,}500$ | $0$ |
| 2014 | $\min(1{,}900,\ 1{,}500) = 1{,}500$ | $\min(1{,}400,\ 1{,}500) = 1{,}400$ | $100$ |

$$\text{Total IBNR net of all reinsurance} = \$125{,}000$$

Note that capping the reported claims as well as the ultimate is what keeps policy year 2013 from producing a negative IBNR.

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to know how to calculate IBNR while taking into account the reinsurance agreements.
Most candidate who answered this part scored well. A common mistake was to forget to apply the stop loss limit and ending up with a negative IBNR for 2013.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Calculate the unpaid claims for policy years 2012 through 2014 net of all reinsurance.

### Explanation
Unpaid claims use the same capped ultimate, less claims paid (also net of all reinsurance):

| Policy Year | (3) Net Ultimate After Stop Loss | (4) Paid After Stop = min(paid net XOL, stop) | Unpaid = (3) - (4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 | $1{,}475$ | $1{,}200$ | $275$ |
| 2013 | $1{,}500$ | $1{,}200$ | $300$ |
| 2014 | $1{,}500$ | $1{,}000$ | $500$ |

$$\text{Total unpaid claims net of all reinsurance} = \$1{,}075{,}000$$

### Examiner Report
The candidate was expected to know how to calculate the unpaid claims while taking into the reinsurance agreements.
Candidate's performance was mixed on this part. A common mistake was using Net Ultimate Claims Estimate instead of the Net Ultimate Claims Cost (after applying the Stop Loss Limit) when calculating the Net Unpaid Claims.
