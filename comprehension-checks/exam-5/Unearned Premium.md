An insurer writing only annual policies reports written premium of \$30M in 2024 against \$24M in 2023, with the reserve rising from \$12M to \$15M. What does the \$3M increase in that reserve tell you?

- A) The insurer strengthened its provision for claims that have occurred but not been reported
- B) Earned premium in 2024 exceeded written premium by \$3M
- C) \$3M of premium was returned to policyholders on cancellations during the year
- D) Coverage worth \$3M more than a year ago has been sold but not yet provided, so earned premium is \$27M
