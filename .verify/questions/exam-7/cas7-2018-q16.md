- note: Maintainer's D10 decision (25 Oct): stays served. Part a (2.5 of 3) builds SB IBNR (Clark 2003 (sha256:1db0baa8...) PDF p.7 (printed 47): the Cape Cod method, expected loss = Premium x ELR x [G(y)-G(x)], exposure base 'usually onlevel premium'; PDF p.12: the MLE ELR = sum of losses / sum of premium x [G(x_t)-G(x_t-1)], 'equivalent to the Cape Cod Ultimate'), chain ladder IBNR, credibility-weights them, and projects six months' expected emergence. Patrik's own convention is Z = selected factor x lag. The stem gives only 'the selected credibility factor is 0.50', and CAS lists not multiplying it by the lags as a mistake, so that step's credit rests on Patrik. The rest of part a and part b (0.5, reasons for the difference) do not. Most points rest on current readings.
