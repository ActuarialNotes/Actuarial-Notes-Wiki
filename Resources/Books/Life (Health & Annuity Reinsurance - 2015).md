A textbook on life, health and annuity reinsurance by John E. Tiller, Jr. and Denise Fagerberg Tiller, published by ACTEX Publications as its fourth edition in 2015. It is the principal text of the SOA's CP 341 Advanced Life Reinsurance, which assigns most of its chapters, and ILA 101 assigns three chapters of it for its reinsurance outcome.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-341 (SOA)|Exam CP-341]] — Ch. 1: Basic Terms and Concepts (1a); Ch. 2: Automatic Reinsurance (1b, 1c); Ch. 3: Facultative Reinsurance (1b, 1c); Ch. 4: Basic Methods of Reinsurance (1b, 1c); Ch. 5: Advanced Methods of Structures of Reinsurance (1b, 1c); Ch. 7: Reinsurance of Inforce Risks (1d-1g); Ch. 17: Nonproportional Reinsurance (1d-1g); Ch. 19: Annuity Reinsurance (1d-1g); Ch. 8: The Reinsurance Treaty (2a); Ch. 9: Risk Transfer Considerations (2b); Ch. 10: Insolvency and Reinsurance (2c, 2d); Ch. 20: Captives (2c, 2d); Ch. 11: U. S. Regulation of Reinsurance (3a); Ch. 12: Canadian Regulation of Reinsurance (3b); Ch. 13: U.S. Statutory Accounting (3c); Ch. 14: U.S. GAAP Accounting (3c); Ch. 15: U.S. Tax Considerations (3c); Ch. 16: Canadian Accounting and Tax Considerations (3d); Ch. 23: Reinsurance Administration (4a); Ch. 24: Managing Reinsurance (4b).
> - [[Exam ILA-101 (SOA)|Exam ILA-101]] — Ch. 1: Basic Terms and Concepts (pp. 3-16 & 22) (5e); Ch. 4: Basic Methods of Reinsurance (5e); Ch. 5: Advanced Methods and Structures of Reinsurance (5e).
