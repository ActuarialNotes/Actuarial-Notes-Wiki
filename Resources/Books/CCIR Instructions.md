The **[[Canadian Council of Insurance Regulators|CCIR]]** instructions and forms for the P&C insurance return — the detailed, line-by-line rules for completing the [[Canadian Annual Return]]. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objective C1.

## Scope on the syllabus

Candidates are responsible for the detailed instructions **for the pages listed in [[OSFI Core Return]], [[OSFI Annual Return]], [[OSFI Quarterly Return]] and [[OSFI MCT|OSFI MCT/BAAT Return]]** — not for the whole document. Within it, four sections apply:

| Section | Date modified (per OSFI's site) |
|---|---|
| **I — Introduction** | 2024-09 |
| **III — Definitions** | 2025-07 |
| **IV — Detailed instructions** | 2025-07 |
| **V — Jurisdictional requirements** | 2024-06 |

> [!note]- Which version to use
> The content outline directs candidates to **the instructions for the 2024 returns linked on the CAS website** with permission for educational purposes, and states that candidates **will not be penalised if their answers are based on the final version** of the paper.

## Why the instructions are a CCIR document and not an OSFI one

Solvency regulation of federally incorporated insurers is [[OSFI]]'s, but insurance is regulated **provincially** as well (see [[Federal-Provincial Jurisdiction]]), and a national insurer would otherwise file a differently-shaped return to each provincial [[Superintendent of Insurance|Superintendent]]. The **CCIR** — the association of Canadian insurance regulators — approves one set of forms and instructions that every jurisdiction accepts. Section **V, jurisdictional requirements**, is where the residual provincial differences live; that section is the reason the document exists.

## What is in the assigned sections

- **I — Introduction.** Who files, on what basis (consolidated and non-consolidated; Canadian company versus foreign branch), the filing deadlines and the certification requirements.
- **III — Definitions.** The controlled vocabulary the return uses. Under [[IFRS 17]] this section did a great deal of work: "premium" is no longer the income statement's top line, and terms like [[Insurance Revenue|insurance revenue]], [[Insurance Service Expenses|insurance service expenses]] and [[Liability for Remaining Coverage|LRC]]/[[Liability for Incurred Claims|LIC]] have to be defined identically for every filer or the datapoints are not comparable.
- **IV — Detailed instructions.** Line by line: what belongs in each cell, how it is derived, and which cells must cross-foot to which. This is what makes the return's **datapoint addressing** (statement–page–row–column) work — the addressing that [[MSA Legend]]'s ratio formulas are written in.
- **V — Jurisdictional requirements.** Additional provincial and territorial filing requirements.
