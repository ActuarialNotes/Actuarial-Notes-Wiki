![[Financial Enterprise Risk Management (Sweeting - 2017) - Cover.svg]]

A guide to enterprise risk management for financial institutions that contains the tools needed to build and maintain an ERM framework. It discusses the internal and external contexts within which risk management must be carried out and covers a range of qualitative and quantitative techniques used to identify, model and measure risks. The second edition (2017) adds new content on Bayesian networks, expanded coverage of Basel III and a revised treatment of operational risk.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CFE-101 (SOA)|Exam CFE-101]] — Financial Enterprise Risk Management, Sweeting, Paul, 2nd Edition, 2017: Ch. 14 Quantifying Particular Risks (1.3a); Ch. 8 Risk Identification (1.3a, 2.1a, 2.1b); Ch. 16 Responses to Risk (3.1b, 3.2a, 3.2c).
