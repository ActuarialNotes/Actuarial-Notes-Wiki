An insurance company's annual profit is normally distributed. The probability that the annual profit exceeds 3.50 is 0.3264 and the probability that it exceeds 3.62 is 0.2743.

Calculate the variance of the insurance company's annual profit.

- A) $0.44$
- B) $0.64$
- C) $0.80$
- D) $1.25$
- E) $1.56$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the annual profit, $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$. Each stated probability gives one equation in $\mu$ and $\sigma$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 3.50] = 0.3264 &\implies \frac{3.50 - \mu}{\sigma} = 0.449876 \\
P[X > 3.62] = 0.2743 &\implies \frac{3.62 - \mu}{\sigma} = 0.599860
\end{align*}
$$

Dividing the second by the first eliminates $\sigma$:

$$
\frac{3.62 - \mu}{3.50 - \mu} = \frac{0.599860}{0.449876} = 1.33339
$$

Solving for $\mu$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\mu &= \frac{3.50(1.33339) - 3.62}{1.33339 - 1} \\
    &= 3.140061
\end{align*}
$$

Back-substituting,

$$
\begin{align*}
\sigma   &= \frac{3.62 - 3.140061}{0.599860} = 0.800085 \\
\sigma^2 &= \mathbf{0.6401}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (C), 0.80, is $\sigma$ rather than $\sigma^2$. The question asks for the variance, and here $\sigma$ is close enough to 1 that squaring changes the answer far less than usual — which is exactly why the two values both appear as options.
