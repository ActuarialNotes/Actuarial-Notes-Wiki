John took out a 20-year loan of 85,000 on July 1, 2005 at an annual nominal interest rate of 6% compounded monthly. The loan was to be paid by level monthly payments at the end of each month with the first payment on July 31, 2005.

Right after the regular monthly payment on June 30, 2009, John refinanced the loan at a new annual nominal rate of 5.40% compounded monthly, and the remaining balance will be paid with monthly payments beginning July 31, 2009. The amount of each payment is 500 except for a final drop payment.

Calculate the date of John's last payment.

- A) July 31, 2022
- B) April 30, 2030
- C) May 31, 2030
- D) April 30, 2031
- E) May 31, 2031

## Explanation

The original monthly payment, at $j = 0.06/12 = 0.005$, is

$$\frac{85{,}000}{a_{\overline{240}|0.005}} = \frac{85{,}000}{139.5808} = 608.97$$

By July 1, 2009 there have been 4 years of payments (48 payments, the last on June 30, 2009), leaving $16 \times 12 = 192$ payments. The outstanding balance is

$$608.97\,a_{\overline{192}|0.005} = 608.97(123.23803) = 75{,}048.26$$

The new monthly rate is $0.054/12 = 0.0045$. The number of remaining payments after refinancing is determined from

$$75{,}048.26 = 500\,a_{\overline{n}|0.0045} = 500\cdot\frac{1 - 1.0045^{-n}}{0.0045}$$

$$0.67543 = 1 - 1.0045^{-n}$$

$$n = -\frac{\ln(0.32457)}{\ln(1.0045)} = 250.62$$

So there are 250 payments of 500 and a smaller drop payment, 251 payments in all. The first is on July 31, 2009, one month after June 30, 2009, so payment $k$ falls $k$ months after June 30, 2009. The final payment is 251 months from June 30, 2009, which is 20 years and 11 months, so the final payment is **May 31, 2030**. (The 250th payment, the last full one, is on April 30, 2030.)
