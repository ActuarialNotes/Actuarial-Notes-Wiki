The **Causes of Loss Special Form** (ISO CP 10 30) is the open perils option of the commercial property program: when "Special" is shown in the declarations, covered causes of loss means direct physical loss unless the loss is excluded or limited in the policy. Attached to a coverage form such as the [[Building and Personal Property Coverage Form|BPP]], it covers every cause the [[Causes of Loss Basic and Broad Forms|basic and broad forms]] name and more, theft included.

The program's causes of loss forms come in basic, broad and special versions, and the form shown in the declarations decides which perils are covered. Under the special form, a loss is covered when:

1. It is direct physical loss to covered property.
2. No exclusion applies, or an exception to the exclusion gives the coverage back.
3. No limitation applies.

The burden shifts to the insurer: a loss is covered unless the insurer shows that an exclusion or limitation applies ([[Causes of Loss]]). The reading of the form therefore lies in its exclusions, its limitations and the exceptions that give coverage back.

> [!example]- Under which form is a burglary of a store's stock covered? {Example}
> - Theft is not one of the perils the basic or broad form names, so neither covers it.
> - The special form covers direct physical loss unless it is excluded or limited, and theft by outsiders is not excluded.
> - The burglary is covered only under the special form, subject to its theft special limits.

## Specified causes of loss

Many exceptions in the form give coverage back only when a *specified cause of loss* results. The form defines these as:

1. Fire, lightning and explosion.
2. Windstorm or hail, and smoke.
3. Aircraft or vehicles, riot or civil commotion, and vandalism.
4. Leakage from fire-extinguishing equipment.
5. Sinkhole collapse and volcanic action.
6. Falling objects, and weight of snow, ice or sleet.
7. Water damage.

Water damage here includes accidental discharge from a cracked system on the premises (other than a sump) and, if caused by wear and tear, from a broken off-premises municipal water or sewer pipe.

## Exclusions

The exclusions fall in three groups:

1. **Exclusions B.1 (regardless of any other cause).** The same as the named perils forms: ordinance or law, earth movement, governmental action, nuclear hazard, utility services, war and military action, water, and fungus, wet rot, dry rot and bacteria. Under this form, volcanic eruption that results in fire, building glass breakage or volcanic action is covered, and fungus that results in a specified cause of loss is covered.
2. **Exclusions B.2.** These include:
   - artificially generated electrical current (ensuing fire covered), and explosion of the insured's steam boilers;
   - delay, loss of use or loss of market;
   - wear and tear, rust and corrosion, decay, latent defect, settling and cracking, infestation and mechanical breakdown, with an ensuing specified cause of loss or building glass breakage covered;
   - continuous seepage or leakage over 14 days or more, and freezing of plumbing unless the insured did its best to keep heat on or drained the system;
   - dishonest or criminal acts, including theft, by the insured, its partners, officers, employees or anyone it entrusts the property to (but destruction by employees is covered, while employee theft is not);
   - voluntarily parting with property because of a fraudulent scheme;
   - rain, snow, ice or sleet to personal property in the open;
   - collapse, except under the collapse additional coverage or when caused by a specified cause of loss, building glass breakage, weight of rain on a roof or weight of people or property;
   - release of pollutants unless itself caused by a specified cause of loss, and neglect to protect property after a loss.
3. **Exclusions B.3.** Weather conditions (only when they combine with a B.1 cause), acts or decisions of any person or body, and faulty planning, design, workmanship, materials or maintenance. For each, a covered cause of loss that results is covered.

An exception to an exclusion restores coverage for the loss it describes. Many exclusions (wear and tear, faulty workmanship, mechanical breakdown) end with an exception for a resulting covered or specified cause of loss. The form then pays for the damage the resulting cause does, and the excluded cause itself stays excluded.

> [!example]- Which of these restaurant losses does the special form cover? {Example}
> A restaurant's building is insured under the special form:
>
> - (a) Rain enters through roof flashing that has worn out over years and soaks the ceilings.
> - (b) A dishwasher pipe cracks and floods the kitchen floor in one afternoon.
> - (c) A night manager steals the week's supply of wine.
> - (d) A disgruntled cook smashes the dining-room windows.
> - (e) A wiring error by the electrician who renovated the kitchen starts a fire.
>
> > [!answer]-
> > - (a) is not covered. Wear and tear is excluded, and rain damage to the interior is limited unless a covered cause first damages the roof or walls.
> > - (b) is covered. Sudden discharge from a cracked appliance is not excluded, and it is a specified cause of loss (water damage). The cost to repair the cracked pipe itself is not paid, but tearing out to reach it is.
> > - (c) is not covered. Theft by an employee is excluded as a dishonest act, and it is a [[Commercial Crime Insurance|crime insurance]] exposure.
> > - (d) is covered. The dishonesty exclusion does not apply to acts of destruction by employees.
> > - (e) The fire is covered. Faulty workmanship is excluded, but fire is a covered cause of loss that results from it. Denying the fire because faulty wiring started it misreads the exception.

## Limitations

The form does not pay for loss:

1. to steam boilers or water heaters from conditions inside them;
2. to a building's interior or contents from rain, snow, sleet, ice, sand or dust unless the roof or walls are first damaged by a covered cause of loss or the damage is from thawing on the building;
3. for theft of building materials not attached to the building, except materials held for sale;
4. for property missing with only an inventory shortage as evidence, or property transferred on unauthorized instructions.

Animals, fragile articles such as statuary and porcelain (not glass or containers of stock), and builders' machinery and tools away from the premises are covered only against the specified causes of loss or building glass breakage.

## Theft special limits

In any one occurrence of theft, unless the declarations show more: \$2,500 for furs and fur garments; \$2,500 for jewelry, watches, precious stones and metals and bullion, except jewelry and watches worth \$100 or less per item; \$2,500 for patterns, dies, molds and forms; and \$250 for stamps, tickets (including lottery tickets held for sale) and letters of credit. Property outside these categories is paid in full, and for each category in one occurrence the theft payment is

> $$\text{Paid}_k = \min(L_k,\ \ell_k)$$

- $L_k$ is the theft loss to property in category $k$.
- $\ell_k$ is the special limit for category $k$.
- The special limits are part of, not in addition to, the limit of insurance.

> [!example]- Ignoring the deductible, what does the special form pay for this theft? {Example}
> A department store's business personal property is insured under the BPP with the special form. In one night, burglars take:
>
> | Property | Value ($) |
> | :--- | ---: |
> | Jewelry, items worth more than \$100 each | 6,800 |
> | Watches, each worth \$100 or less | 1,200 |
> | A sheet of stamps | 600 |
> | Fur coats | 3,000 |
> | Other clothing | 5,000 |
>
> > [!answer]-
> > - Theft is covered because nothing excludes it.
> > - The jewelry is capped at $\min(6{,}800,\ 2{,}500) = \$2{,}500$.
> > - The watches are worth \$100 or less each, so they are outside the jewelry limit and paid in full, \$1,200.
> > - The stamps are capped at $\min(600,\ 250) = \$250$, and the furs at $\min(3{,}000,\ 2{,}500) = \$2{,}500$.
> > - The other clothing has no special limit and is paid in full, \$5,000.
> > - The form pays $2{,}500 + 1{,}200 + 250 + 2{,}500 + 5{,}000 = \$11{,}450$, and the store bears the other \$5,150 of the \$16,600 theft.

## Additional coverages

*Collapse* pays for abrupt collapse caused by hidden decay or hidden insect or vermin damage not known to the insured, or by defective construction materials or methods during construction (or afterwards, if a specified cause, building glass breakage or the weight of people, property or roof rain contributes). It excludes buildings in danger of collapse or standing though cracked. *Limited fungus coverage* pays up to \$15,000 per 12-month period for fungus, rot or bacteria resulting from a specified cause of loss other than fire or lightning. Neither increases the limit.

> [!trap] Treating open perils as all risks
> The B.1 exclusions are the same under every form. Flood and earth movement are excluded even under the special form, so a loss the special form covers that the broad form does not is theft, not flood.
