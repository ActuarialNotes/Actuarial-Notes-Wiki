The following information is available for an insurance policy:

- The expected loss amount for the policy is \$500.
- The standard deviation of the loss is \$3,000.
- The loss safety level is set at \$10,000.
- The target investment return is 12%, with a standard deviation of 30%.
- The risk-free interest rate is 3%.
- No taxes or expenses are associated with the policy.

Using the swap technique, calculate the policy premium.

### Explanation
Given $\mu_L = 500$, $S = 10{,}000$, $\sigma_L = 3{,}000$, $y = 0.12$, $\sigma_y = 0.3$ and $r_f = 0.03$; assume all losses are paid at time $t = 1$.

In the swap technique, with allocated assets $A$, the fund $F = P + A = \dfrac{\mu_L}{1 + r_f} + R + A$ satisfies

$$(1 + y)A = (1 + r_f)F - \mu_L$$

There are two constraints; choose the one giving the larger assets $A$.

- Safety constraint: $A_s = \dfrac{S - \mu_L}{1 + y} = \dfrac{10{,}000 - 500}{1.12} = 8{,}482.14$
- Variance constraint: $A_v = \dfrac{\sigma_L}{\sigma_y} = \dfrac{3{,}000}{0.3} = 10{,}000$

Use $A_v$ to calculate the risk load and premium:

$$(1 + y)A = (1 + r_f)(P + A) - \mu_L$$

$$1.12(10{,}000) = 1.03(P + 10{,}000) - 500 \quad\Rightarrow\quad P = 1{,}359.22$$

Alternatively:

Compute the risk load first, from the larger (variance) assets:

$$R = A\left(\frac{y - r_f}{1 + r_f}\right) = 10{,}000\left(\frac{0.12 - 0.03}{1.03}\right) = 873.86$$

$$P = R + \frac{\mu_L}{1 + r_f} = 873.786 + \frac{500}{1.03} = 1{,}3599.2 \text{ (as printed)}$$

### Examiner Report
Overall, the candidates did well on this question, but there were a few common mistakes.  The most common mistakes involved using the wrong interest rates in the denominator of the Safety Constraint calculation (using the risk free rate instead of the target investment return) or the Risk Load calculation (using the target investment return instead of the risk free rate).  Another common mistake involved the discounting of the expected loss in the Premium calculation – candidates either did not discount at all or discounted using the target investment return instead of the risk free rate.
