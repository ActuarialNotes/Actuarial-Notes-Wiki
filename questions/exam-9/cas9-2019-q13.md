An insurer has catastrophe exposure to three independent perils: water, wind, and earthquake.

| Event | Probability | Water loss (\$millions) | Wind loss (\$millions) | Earthquake loss (\$millions) | Total loss (\$millions) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.565 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 0.242 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| 3 | 0.100 | 0 | 7 | 0 | 7 |
| 4 | 0.043 | 3 | 7 | 0 | 10 |
| 5 | 0.030 | 0 | 0 | 30 | 30 |
| 6 | 0.013 | 3 | 0 | 30 | 33 |
| 7 | 0.005 | 0 | 7 | 30 | 37 |
| 8 | 0.002 | 3 | 7 | 30 | 40 |
| Total | 1.000 |  |  |  |  |

The assigned capital (\$millions) by event for 3 layers is provided:

| Event | \$3 million excess \$0 layer | \$4 million excess \$3 million layer | \$3 million excess \$7 million layer | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 0.207 | 0.622 | 0.968 | 1.797 |
| 6 | 0.090 | 0.269 | 0.419 | 0.778 |
| 7 | 0.034 | 0.104 | 0.161 | 0.299 |
| 8 | 0.014 | 0.041 | 0.065 | 0.120 |

Using the Capital Allocation by Percentile Layer methodology with a 98.0% VaR capital requirement, determine how much capital should be allocated to the earthquake peril.

### Explanation
VaR (98) = at event 5 since 0.565 + 0.242 + 0.1 + 0.043 + 0.03 = 0.98

| Event | 0-3 Layer Cap | 3-7 Layer Cap | 7-10 Layer Cap | 10-30 Layer Prob (CEP) | 10-30 Layer Cap | Total | EQ Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 0.207 | 0.622 | 0.968 | 0.6 | 12 | 13.797 | 1 |
| 6 | 0.090 | 0.269 | 0.419 | 0.26 | 5.2 | 5,978 (as printed) | 0.909 |
| 7 | 0.034 | 0.104 | 0.161 | 0.1 | 2 | 2.299 | 0.811 |
| 8 | 0.014 | 0.041 | 0.065 | 0.04 | 0.8 | 0.92 | 0.75 |

Prob (5) for 10-30 layer = 0.03 / (0.03 + 0.013 + 0.005 + 0.002) = 0.6

Capital (5) for 10-30 layer = 0.6 * (30 – 10) = 12

EQ Share (6) = 30/33 = 0.909

$$\text{EQ capital total} = 13.797 * 1 + 5.978 * 0.909 + 2.299 * 0.811 + 0.92 * 0.75 = 21.786\text{M}$$

Alternatively:

1) 98% VaR: Select 30M as required capital level.

| Event | Probability | Cumulative Prob | Loss Amount (\$M) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.565 | 0.565 | 0 |
| 2 | 0.242 | 0.807 | 3 |
| 3 | 0.100 | 0.907 | 7 |
| 4 | 0.043 | 0.950 | 10 |
| 5 | 0.030 | 0.980 | 30 |
| 6 | 0.013 | 0.993 | 33 |
| 7 | 0.005 | 0.998 | 37 |
| 8 | 0.002 | 1.000 | 40 |

2) Assign Capital by Event for 20 xs 10 layer.

| Event | Conditional Exceedance Prob. | Capital in Layer | Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 0.03/(0.03+0.013+0.005+0.002)=0.6 | 20 | 0.6 * 20 = 12 |
| 6 | 0.26 | 20 | 5.2 |
| 7 | 0.10 | 20 | 2 |
| 8 | 0.04 | 20 | 0.8 |

3) Allocate Event Capital to Earthquake.

| Event | Layer (20 xs 10) | Lower Layers Capital (Given) | Total Event Allocation | Earthquake % | Earthquake Capital Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 12 | 1.797 | 13.797 | 30/30 = 100% | 13.797 |
| 6 | 5.2 | 0.778 | 5.978 | 90.91% | 5.435 |
| 7 | 2 | 0.299 | 2.299 | 81.08% | 1.864 |
| 8 | 0.8 | 0.12 | 0.92 | 75.00% | 0.690 |

Final Answer = 21.786

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to calculate the amount of allocated capital to the Earthquake peril exposure using the percentile layer methodology.

Common mistakes included:

- Selecting the wrong capital requirement for 98% VaR.
- Omitting the capital for the lower layers or using the wrong numbers from the provided table (3 xs 7 layer for example).
- Only allocating the capital in the lower layers to Earthquake (i.e. not calculating anything for the 20 xs 10 layer)
