A Canadian Institute of Actuaries educational note, approved on May 9, 2023 (document 223084), giving actuaries guidance on determining commuted values for pension plans that are not covered under subsection 3570 of the Practice-Specific Standards. A separate educational note covers subsection 3570. It updates the note first issued on August 13, 2020 after the quinquennial review of Part 3000, aligns its references with the revised Section 3500 effective December 1, 2022, and carries over the content on calculating unisex mortality rates from the April 2006 educational note.
