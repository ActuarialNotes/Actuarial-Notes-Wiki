The Actuarial Standards Board's guidance to actuaries performing actuarial services that involve discounting property/casualty claim estimates to a present value. This revision (Doc. No. 209) was adopted in June 2023, addresses scope gaps with other ASOPs and reintroduces guidance on risk margins, and is effective for any actuarial work product covered by its scope issued on or after December 1, 2023.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam 6U (CAS)|Exam 6U]] — objectives C6 and C7; the standard as adopted in June 2023 (the content outline cites it as an online publication).

## Transmittal Memorandum
- The ASB first adopted ASOP No. 20 in April 1992; the 2011 revision removed guidance on risk margins, and this revision reintroduces and expands it.
- The revision also covers discount rates provided by others (requested by the principal, or supplied by investment managers or finance departments), and adds guidance on reliance, documentation and disclosures.

## 1 Purpose, Scope, Cross References, and Effective Date
- 1.1 Purpose
- 1.2 Scope
    - Applies to actuaries discounting claim estimates, which include unpaid claim estimates and future claim estimates (associated with prospective risk transfer or risk retention), for all classes of entities.
    - For unpaid claim estimates the actuary refers to ASOP No. 43; for future claim estimates associated with prospective risk transfer or risk retention, to ASOP No. 53.
    - Does not address whether the actuary should use discounted claim estimates, or include a risk margin, in a specific circumstance.

- 1.4 Effective Date
