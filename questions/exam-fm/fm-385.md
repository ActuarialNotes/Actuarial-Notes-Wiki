A three-year 1000 face amount bond pays coupons of $X$ quarterly. It is bought at a price to yield an annual nominal rate of 8% convertible quarterly. If the amount of each coupon were doubled, the purchase price would have to increase by 500 for the bond to maintain the same yield rate.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 20
- B) 24
- C) 31
- D) 40
- E) 47

## Explanation

The quarterly yield rate is $\dfrac{0.08}{4} = 0.02$ and there are $4(3) = 12$ quarterly coupons, so the price is

$$P = X\,a_{\overline{12}|0.02} + 1000v^{12}$$

With each coupon doubled, the price is 500 higher at the same yield:

$$500 + P = 2X\,a_{\overline{12}|0.02} + 1000v^{12}$$

Subtract the first equation from the second:

$$
\begin{align*}
500 &= X\,a_{\overline{12}|0.02} \\
    &= X(10.57534) \\
X &= 47.28
\end{align*}
$$
