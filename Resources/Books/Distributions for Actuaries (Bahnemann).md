![[Distributions for Actuaries (Bahnemann) - Cover.svg]]

A brief exposition of the standard probability distributions and their applications by property/casualty actuaries. The focus is on the use of parametric distributions fitted to empirical claim data to solve standard actuarial problems, such as the creation of increased limit factors, the pricing of deductibles, and the evaluation of the effect of aggregate limits. It is the second monograph in the CAS Monograph Series, published in 2015.
