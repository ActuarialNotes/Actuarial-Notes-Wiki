An NAIC Capital Markets Bureau special report, prepared by analysts Jennifer Johnson and Jean-Baptiste Carelus, on U.S. insurers owned by private equity firms. It reports their number as of July 2024, their total cash and invested assets at year-end 2023, and the asset types, bond types and credit quality of those investments. It covers year-end 2023 filings, compared with 2022 and 2021.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam CP-341 (SOA)|Exam CP-341]] — Outcomes 1h; the whole report.

## Executive Summary
- The number of [[Private Equity-Backed Insurer|private equity (PE)-owned]] U.S. insurers identified by the NAIC Capital Markets Bureau remained at 137 as of July 2024, compared to July 2023, but was up slightly from 132 at year-end 2022.
- Total cash and invested assets for the 137 PE-owned insurers increased 13.5% to $605.7 billion in book/adjusted carrying value (BACV), representing 7.1% of total U.S. insurers' total cash and invested assets.
- Bonds and mortgages remained the two largest asset types, at 63% and 17% of total cash and invested assets.
- Asset-backed and other structured securities (nontraditional bonds) were 29% of total bonds, compared with 12% for the overall U.S. insurance industry.
- Over 90% of all bond investments carried NAIC 1 and NAIC 2 designations.
