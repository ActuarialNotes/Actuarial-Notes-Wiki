You are building a Linear Mixed Model with longitudinal observations. The observations within a subject exhibit positive auto correlation. The variance of the residuals is 0.647.

Determine which of the following covariance matrices exhibits the first-order autoregressive structure.

- A) $\begin{pmatrix} 0.647 & 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.000 \\ 0.000 & 0.647 & 0.000 & 0.000 \\ 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.647 & 0.000 \\ 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.647 \end{pmatrix}$
- B) $\begin{pmatrix} 0.647 & 0.541 & 0.000 & 0.000 \\ 0.541 & 0.647 & 0.541 & 0.000 \\ 0.000 & 0.541 & 0.647 & 0.541 \\ 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.541 & 0.647 \end{pmatrix}$
- C) $\begin{pmatrix} 0.793 & 0.146 & 0.146 & 0.146 \\ 0.146 & 0.793 & 0.146 & 0.146 \\ 0.146 & 0.146 & 0.793 & 0.146 \\ 0.146 & 0.146 & 0.146 & 0.793 \end{pmatrix}$
- D) $\begin{pmatrix} 0.647 & 0.391 & 0.102 & 0.037 \\ 0.391 & 0.647 & 0.391 & 0.102 \\ 0.102 & 0.391 & 0.647 & 0.391 \\ 0.037 & 0.102 & 0.391 & 0.647 \end{pmatrix}$
- E) $\begin{pmatrix} 0.647 & 0.518 & 0.415 & 0.332 \\ 0.518 & 0.647 & 0.518 & 0.415 \\ 0.415 & 0.518 & 0.647 & 0.518 \\ 0.332 & 0.415 & 0.518 & 0.647 \end{pmatrix}$

## Explanation

The first-order autoregressive, AR(1), structure gives every observation the same variance $\sigma^2$ and makes the covariance decay geometrically with the distance between measurement occasions:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon_{ti}, \varepsilon_{t'i}) = \sigma^2 \rho^{\lvert t - t' \rvert}, \qquad \sigma^2 = 0.647.
$$

So the diagonal must be 0.647 and the off-diagonals must be $0.647\rho$, $0.647\rho^2$, $0.647\rho^3$ moving away from it, all positive.

- **A** is the independence (diagonal) structure: no autocorrelation at all.
- **B** is a banded (Toeplitz) structure: lag-1 covariance 0.541, but the lag-2 and lag-3 covariances drop straight to zero instead of decaying.
- **C** is compound symmetry: every pair has the same covariance 0.146, and the diagonal is 0.793, not the residual variance 0.647.
- **D** decays, but not geometrically. The lag-1 correlation is $0.391/0.647 = 0.604$, which would require a lag-2 covariance of $0.647(0.604)^2 = 0.236$, not 0.102.
- **E** decays geometrically. The lag-1 correlation is $0.518/0.647 = 0.80$, and

$$
\begin{align*}
0.647(0.8)^2 &= 0.414 \approx 0.415 \\
0.647(0.8)^3 &= 0.331 \approx 0.332
\end{align*}
$$

Matrix E is AR(1) with $\rho = 0.8$, so the answer is **E**.
