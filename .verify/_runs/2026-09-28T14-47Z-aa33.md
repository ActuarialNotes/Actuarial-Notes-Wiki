  p.63 (the increment term (a₂₀ − 20v²⁰)/i).

  is correct.
- **Concepts, fixed by deleting the contradicted claim:** Amortization Schedule said interest portions

against face instead of redemption value; Full Immunization omits duration matching; Asset-Liability

Term of Loan misattributes the drop/balloon distinction.

Redington Immunization.

Amortization (46), Bond Amortization, Nominal Discount Rate, Geometric Increasing Perpetuity, Spot Rates
