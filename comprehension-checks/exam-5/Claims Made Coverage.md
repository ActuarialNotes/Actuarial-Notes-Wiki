A policy in force for 2024 carries a retroactive date of 1 January 2020. A loss occurring in 2021 is first reported to the insurer in 2024. Does this policy respond?

- A) No — the loss happened in 2021, so the 2021 policy is the one that responds
- B) No — the retroactive date bars anything occurring before the policy period
- C) Only if an extended reporting period was purchased
- D) Yes — the trigger is the report date, and the occurrence post-dates the retroactive date
