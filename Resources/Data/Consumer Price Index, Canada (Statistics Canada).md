# Consumer Price Index, Canada (Statistics Canada)

Statistics Canada's **Table 18-10-0004-01**, *Consumer Price Index, monthly, not seasonally adjusted*. The series read here are for Canada as a whole, from 2000-01 to 2026-08.

> [!info] In Actuarial Tools
> The **Price Index** data source reads these series and gives the annualised change between two periods; it can send the window to **Trend Fit** or the annualised change to **Trend Factor**, as a selected trend. See [[Inflation]] and [[Loss Trend]].

## Series read

- All-items, Canada (2002=100), not seasonally adjusted (monthly, from 2000-01)
- Passenger vehicle insurance premiums, Canada (2002=100), not seasonally adjusted (monthly, from 2000-01)
- Passenger vehicle parts, maintenance and repairs, Canada (2002=100), not seasonally adjusted (monthly, from 2000-01)
- Health care, Canada (2002=100), not seasonally adjusted (monthly, from 2000-01)
- Shelter, Canada (2002=100), not seasonally adjusted (monthly, from 2000-01)
