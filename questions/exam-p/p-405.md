A monthly state lottery picks five distinct integers from 1 to 30 and selects one of the five to be the bonus number. An entry consists of five distinct integers from 1 to 30 with one of the numbers designated as the bonus number.

Each month there are 100,000 entries. Each entry that matches the five distinct numbers is awarded 50,000. If the bonus number is also matched, an additional 250,000 is awarded to that entry. Nothing is awarded for matching fewer than five numbers.

Calculate the expected payout from the lottery in a month.

- A) $70{,}172$
- B) $77{,}190$
- C) $84{,}731$
- D) $100{,}000$
- E) $175{,}431$

## Explanation

An entry is a choice of five numbers **and** a designation of which one is the bonus, so the number of distinct entries is

$$
\binom{30}{5} \times 5 = 142{,}506 \times 5 = 712{,}530
$$

Matching all five numbers has probability $1/\binom{30}{5}$. Given that, the bonus designation matches with probability $1/5$ and misses with probability $4/5$:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[\text{all five and bonus}] &= \frac{1}{\binom{30}{5}}\cdot\frac{1}{5} = \frac{1}{712{,}530} \\
P[\text{all five, no bonus}] &= \frac{1}{\binom{30}{5}}\cdot\frac{4}{5} = \frac{4}{712{,}530}
\end{align*}
$$

An entry matching all five *and* the bonus collects $50{,}000 + 250{,}000 = 300{,}000$; one matching all five without the bonus collects 50,000. Expected payout per entry, multiplied by the 100,000 entries:

$$
\begin{align*}
E[\text{payout}] &= 100{,}000\left[300{,}000\left(\frac{1}{712{,}530}\right) + 50{,}000\left(\frac{4}{712{,}530}\right)\right] \\
                 &= 100{,}000\left[\frac{300{,}000 + 200{,}000}{712{,}530}\right] \\
                 &= \mathbf{70{,}172}
\end{align*}
$$

The bonus award is *additional*, so an entry matching all five and the bonus takes 300,000 rather than 250,000 — using 250,000 there gives 63,155.

Choice (E), 175,431, is $100{,}000 \times 250{,}000/\binom{30}{5}$: paying the bonus prize on every five-number match, as though the bonus designation were always right. Choice (B), 77,190, double counts in the other direction — it awards 50,000 to every five-number match *and* the bonus package on top. The 1/5 and 4/5 split of the five-number match is what keeps the two prize levels from overlapping.
