A Canadian P&C insurer is preparing the statement of profit or loss in its IFRS 17 core financial statement return.

Which of the following items is reported within insurance service expenses?

- A) Acquisition expenses that are not directly attributable to any portfolio of insurance contracts
- B) The unwinding of the discount on the liability for incurred claims
- C) The allocation of reinsurance premiums paid under reinsurance contracts held
- D) Interest revenue on bonds held at amortized cost
- E) The amortization of insurance acquisition cash flows deferred in the liability for remaining coverage

## Explanation

OSFI's *Insurance Service Result* page (60.25 of the Supervisory Quarterly Return) breaks insurance service expenses into four columns: incurred claims and other insurance service expenses, **amortization of insurance acquisition cash flows**, losses and reversal of losses on onerous contracts, and adjustments to liabilities for incurred claims. The CIA's educational note on the LRC shows the same in its worked example: the 100 of acquisition cash flows amortized in the year sits inside "Total Insurance Service Expenses." The answer is **E**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — costs that are not directly attributable to the portfolio are accounted for outside the insurance service result, as "other expenses" (CIA educational note on the LRC, section 5.5). In the core return they fall under general and operating expenses.
- **B** — the unwinding of the discount comes from the time value of money, so it is insurance finance income or expense, reported in the net investment result.
- **C** — the allocation of reinsurance premiums is part of the separate line *net expenses from reinsurance contracts held*. IFRS 17 does not net reinsurance into the gross expense line.
- **D** — interest revenue on financial assets is investment income, a separate line of the net investment result.
