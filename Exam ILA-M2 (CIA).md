# ILA Module 2
**ILA Module 2 – Professional considerations in the life insurance environment** is one of the two modules of the individual life and annuities track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the module
>
> The primary audience for the FCIA modules is individuals who desire to be admitted as Fellows of the CIA.
>
> Individuals seeking continuing professional development credit can also register for the FCIA modules.
>
> Each FCIA candidate through Pathways 1 and 2 is required to complete both FCIA modules, while Pathway 3 candidates will have to complete the second module. It is anticipated that FCIA module candidates will have six months from the date of module purchase to complete each FCIA module. As a general guideline, candidates should expect to spend approximately 100 hours completing all activities included in a module.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Professional considerations
>
> 1. Outline the qualification requirements of the [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] in Canada.
> 2. Describe the role and responsibilities of the [[Appointed-valuation Actuary|appointed/valuation actuary]] in Canada.
> 3. Outline for which instances an [[Appointed Actuary|appointed actuary]] is required in Canada.
> 4. Describe the roles and responsibilities of [[Actuary|actuaries]] in [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance companies]], such as in the areas of [[Valuation|valuation]], pricing, [[Policy Illustration|policy illustration]], and [[Enterprise Risk Management|ERM]].
> 5. Outline qualification requirements under the U.S. [[Qualification Standard|qualification standard]] (USQS).
> 6. Outline requirements of the [[CIA Professional Standard|CIA professional standards]] that may inadvertently be breached in the [[Life Insurance|life insurance]] practice.
> 7. Identify and apply [[Actuarial Standards of Practice|actuarial standards of practice]] relevant to:
>    - a. [[Financial Reporting|Financial reporting]] and [[Valuation|valuation]].
>    - b. [[FCT|Financial condition testing]].
>    - c. Non-guaranteed elements.

> [!example]- Overview of the regulatory framework in Canada
>
> 1. Summarize the regulation applicable to the [[Insurance Market|insurance market]] in Canada.
> 2. Summarize the regulation applicable to the [[Investment Market|investment market]] in Canada.
> 3. Identify the major regulators of the [[Insurance Sector|insurance sector]] in Canada.
> 4. Identify the major regulators of the [[Investment Sector|investment sector]] in Canada.
> 5. Summarize the activities of [[Insurance Regulator|insurance regulators]].
> 6. Summarize the activities of [[Investment Regulator|investment regulators]].
> 7. Describe the goals of regulation and laws in the investment and [[Insurance Sector|insurance sectors]].
> 8. Explain how [[Regulation Protects Consumer|regulation protects consumers]] of [[Insurance Policy|insurance products]] and investors in [[Insurer|insurance companies]].
> 9. Understand the compliance function in an [[Investment Management Organization|investment management organization]].

> [!example]- Regulation and taxation of insurance
>
> 1. Explain why [[Solvency Regulation|solvency regulation]] is important.
> 2. Describe implications of [[Solvency|insolvency]].
> 3. Describe the [[Financial Reporting|financial-reporting]] requirements.
> 4. Explain the [[Regulatory Process|regulatory processes]] used to monitor [[Solvency|solvency]].
> 5. Explain how contracts are taxed to the owner of the [[Contract|contract]].
> 6. Describe [[Constraint|constraints]] on policy design imposed by [[Tax Law|tax laws]].
> 7. Describe the two major categories of differences between pre-tax and [[Taxable Earning|taxable earnings]].
> 8. Distinguish between the different forms of taxes paid by [[Canadian Company|Canadian companies]].
> 9. Understand the differences between federal and [[Provincial Taxation|provincial taxation]].

> [!example]- Canadian financial reporting
>
> 1. Apply the principles underlying the determination of LICAT, including the following:
>    - a. Identification of significant [[Risk Component|risk components]];
>    - b. Identification of [[Specialized-Product LICAT|specialized-product LICAT]] requirements;
>    - c. Interpreting results from a regulatory perspective; and
>    - d. Compare and contrast to other [[Regulatory Capital Regime|regulatory capital regimes]].
> 2. Describe and explain the regulatory and [[Tax Environment|tax environment]] for insurance and [[Annuities|annuities]] in Canada, specifically: effect on [[Product Development|product development]], reserving, pricing, and [[Business Practice|business practices]].
> 3. Describe key [[IFRS 17]] financial concepts for written and [[Ceded Coverage|ceded coverages]] and how they are calculated.
> 4. Describe [[IFRS 17 Valuation|IFRS 17 valuation]] methods.
> 5. Calculate [[IFRS 17 Liability|IFRS 17 liabilities]] for life and [[Annuity Contract|annuity products]] and their associated riders.

> [!example]- Asset and portfolio management
>
> 1. Describe the [[Portfolio Management Process|portfolio management process]] in an [[Insurer|insurance company]] and the role of [[Investment Policy Statement|investment policy]], the [[Investment Actuary|investment actuary]], and [[External Portfolio Manager|external portfolio managers]].
> 2. Describe and evaluate how a company's objectives, needs, and [[Constraint|constraints]] affect [[Investment Strategy|investment strategy]] and [[Portfolio|portfolio]] construction.
> 3. Describe the role of, and significant considerations related to the design and function of, [[Asset Allocation|asset-allocation strategies]].
> 4. Describe [[Fixed-Asset Portfolio|fixed-asset portfolio]] management methods, [[Immunization|immunization]] (including derivatives) and [[Cash-Matching Strategy|cash-matching strategies]], and [[Convexity|convexity]].
> 5. Describe and assess alternative [[Portfolio|investment portfolios]] in the context of an [[Insurance Company Portfolio|insurance company portfolio]].
> 6. Describe and apply methods and processes for evaluating [[Portfolio Performance|portfolio performance]], including [[Performance Attribution|performance attribution]], sources of earnings analysis on [[Investment Income|investment income]], benchmarks, metrics, and [[Risk-Adjusted Performance Appraisal|risk-adjusted performance appraisals]].
> 7. Describe the principles of [[Liquidity Risk|liquidity risk]] management in the context of [[Insurance Company Portfolio|insurance company portfolio]] management.
> 8. Describe how an [[Insurer|insurance company]] can [[Hedging|hedge]] against the cost of borrowing through [[Forward Rate|forward rate]], futures and perform related calculations.
> 9. Demonstrate understanding of [[Regulators' Guidance|regulators’ guidance]] and context for different approaches to [[Responsible Investment|responsible investment]] and, specifically, the integration of ESG factors in the [[Investment Process|investment process]].
> 10. Demonstrate an understanding of the underlying issues that constitute factors within each of the [[ESG]] areas.
> 11. Demonstrate an understanding of the [[ESG Market|ESG market]]: relevance, size, scope, key drivers and challenges, and [[Risk|risks]] and opportunities.

> [!example]- Communication and organizational behaviour
>
> 1. Explain differences between good and [[Poor Communication|poor communication]] techniques and their implications.
> 2. Apply techniques to [[Structure Ideas|structure ideas]] logically.
> 3. Develop clear, [[Fact-Based Message|fact-based messages]] that can be communicated persuasively.
> 4. Explain the role of [[Cognitive Bias|cognitive biases]] on [[Making Suboptimal Individual|making suboptimal individual]] decisions.
> 5. Evaluate the role of organizational behavior on [[Organizational Decision-Making Process|organizational decision-making processes]] and efficacy.
> 6. Explain why communication is strategically important to [[Organization|organizations]].
> 7. Describe how information is communicated within [[Organization|organizations]].
> 8. Describe organizational and [[Individual Barrier|individual barriers]] to [[Effective Communication|effective communication]].
> 9. Identify the [[Risk|risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 10. Explain how to [[Overcome Communication Barrier|overcome communication barriers]] and [[Minimize Risk|minimize risks]] of [[Ineffective Communication|ineffective communication]].
> 11. Recognize the influence of [[Unconscious Biase|unconscious biases]], culture, and diversity on communication.
> 12. Apply the [[CIA Standard|CIA Standards]] of Practice and Rules of [[Professional Conduct]] in [[Business Communication|business communication]].
