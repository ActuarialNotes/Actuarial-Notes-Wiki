A Society of Actuaries study note on group and health reinsurance. According to the GH 201-C course strategy guide, the reading focuses on group reinsurance: reinsurance covers a portion of high-severity claims so the insurer remains solvent over time, and lets an insurer stabilize its financial results, giving more predictable earnings and the ability to write more business without holding disproportionately high levels of capital.
