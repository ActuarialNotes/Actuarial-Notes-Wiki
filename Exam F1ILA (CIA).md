# Exam F1ILA
**F1ILA – Finance and Valuation** is an exam of the individual life and annuities track of the Canadian Institute of Actuaries' Fellowship (FCIA) pathway.

> [!question]- About the exam
>
> The aim of this exam is for candidates to develop the following skills:
>
> 1. Understand the main principles and techniques for estimating reserves and claims-related expenses (hereafter referred to simply as “claims”) that are relevant to life insurance.
>
> 2. Apply these principles and techniques within the context of life insurance.
>
> 3. Evaluate the considerations involved in selecting a best estimate reserve.
>
> 4. Understand how estimating reserves links to wider business processes (e.g., business planning, pricing, financial reporting, and capital setting).
>
> 5. Describe International Financial Reporting Standard 17 - Insurance Contracts (hereafter referred to as IFRS 17) and calculate actuarial values for financial reporting under IFRS 17.
>
> 6. Create appropriate proposals and recommendations related to reserves and IFRS 17 financial reporting in the life insurance business.
>
> 7. Evaluate hypothetical scenarios, including using judgment to assess the implications of possible actions.
>
> Exams are administered as an open-book, four-hour exam requiring analysis in the context of a problem and submission of written responses to specific questions. Candidates will complete the exam through the CIA learning management system. The ranges of weights (in parentheses) attached to the examination topics further below apply to most of the administered exams. Candidates should also recognize that questions often cover multiple learning objectives, including communication.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Principal terms {0%}
>
> 1. Define [[Principal|principal]] terms used in the [[Valuation|valuation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]] and [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]] for [[Life Insurer|life insurers]]. (A1)

> [!example]- Regulations and applicable standards {15%}
>
> 1. Apply legislation (e.g., the [[Insurance Companies Act]]) and regulations (e.g., Office of the Superintendent of [[Financial Institutions Guideline|Financial Institutions guidelines]]) relevant to the [[Appointed Actuary's Valuation|Appointed Actuary’s valuation]] for [[Financial Reporting|financial-reporting]] purposes. (A3)
> 2. Apply actuarial [[Professional Responsibilities of the Actuary|professionalism requirements]] applicable to the [[Valuation|valuation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]] and [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]]. (B3)

> [!example]- Data {10%}
>
> 1. Differentiate the types of data and information required for [[Valuation|valuation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]], their sources, and main uses. (A4)
> 2. Differentiate types of data and information required for [[IFRS 17 Financial Reporting|IFRS 17 financial reporting]], their sources, and main uses. (A4)
> 3. Analyze the implications to data requirements for different [[Life Insurance Product Feature|life insurance product features]] (e.g., term vs. [[Whole Life Insurance|whole life]] vs. [[Universal Life|universal life]], [[Annuities|annuities]], [[Accumulation Guarantee|accumulation guarantees]]). (B4)
> 4. Evaluate the [[Insurer's Internal Environment|insurer’s internal environment]] (such as changes in operations or information technology [IT]) on data used for [[Valuation|valuation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]]. (B5)
> 5. Analyze the influence of [[External Environment|external environments]] on data used for [[Valuation|valuation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]] and [[IFRS]] 17. (B4)
> 6. Evaluate the possible causes of [[Data Error|data errors]] and the effects of [[Inadequate Data|inadequate data]] and information. (B5)
> 7. Create appropriate [[Data-Validation Process|data-validation processes]]. (C6)

> [!example]- Principles of valuation - basic topics {15%}
>
> 1. Analyze the purpose of reserving for [[Life Insurer|life insurers]], including: (A4)
>    - a. The reasons for calculating [[Reserve|reserves]] and how these might influence the approach (such as [[Financial Reporting|financial reporting]], financial planning and analysis, and pricing); and
>    - b. The key stakeholders and their [[Interest|interests]] in the [[Reserves Held|reserves held]] and reported.
> 2. Calculate and evaluate [[Reserve|reserves]] using appropriate methods. (C5)
> 3. For methods in 2.:
>    - a. Generate the assumptions and mechanics. (C6)
>    - b. Analyze assumption changes and determine their influence on the results of [[Reserve|reserves]]. (C4)
>    - c. Critique the technique with varying [[Data Quality|data quality]] and completeness and in different situations. (D5)
>    - d. Evaluate the strengths and limitations of each technique and the impact of limitations on results. (D5)
>    - e. Select the appropriate technique and assumptions given specific circumstances. (D5)
> 4. Apply processes to integrate more complex factors potentially relevant for the estimation of [[Reserve|reserves]] of a given [[Claims Portfolio|claims portfolio]].
>    - a. Select the appropriate technique and assumptions given changes to the internal and [[External Environment Like|external environment like]] changes in [[Underwriting Practice|underwriting practices]], public-health measures, and [[Economic Condition|economic conditions]]. (C3)
> 5. Describe the role of [[Valuation|valuation]] assumptions with a focus on the following: (C4)
>    - a. Regular assumptions vs. one-time adjustments.
>    - b. Economic and non-economic assumptions.
>    - c. Understand their mechanics, such as how to construct and use select and [[Ultimate Table|ultimate tables]]. (B2)
>    - d. Analyze assumption changes, determine their influence on the results, and describe techniques for a [[Controlled Implementation Process|controlled implementation process]]. (B4)
>    - e. Explain how assumptions are impacted by impacts to the [[Insurance Environment|insurance environment]] such as legislative changes or changes to availability of [[Underwriting Data|underwriting data]]. (B2)
> 6. Determine whether to adjust for [[Adverse Deviation|adverse deviation]] and calculate such adjustments to a best estimate of [[Reserve|reserves]]. (D5)

> [!example]- Principles of valuation – advanced topics {25%}
>
> 1. Describe, calculate, and evaluate approaches to allow for various other items in an [[Actuary's Estimation|actuary’s estimation]] of [[Reserve|reserves]], including: (C5)
>    - a. [[Claims-management Expense|Claims-management expenses]] (including allocated and [[Unallocated Loss Adjustment Expenses ULAE|unallocated loss-adjustment expenses]]) and other costs.
>    - b. [[Latent Claim|Latent claims]] and potential [[Claims Feature|claims features]] that have not manifested in the [[Reported Claims Data|reported claims data]].
>    - c. [[Late Reported Claim|Late reported claims]] and potential [[Claims Feature|claims features]] that have not manifested in the [[Reported Claims Data|reported claims data]].
> 2. Critique margins for [[Adverse Deviation|adverse deviations]], including their purpose, how they are set, and the [[Financial Consequence|financial consequences]] of setting them inappropriately. (B5)
> 3. Calculate estimates of [[Reserve|reserves]] for [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]].
>    - a. Compare the approaches to calculating [[Reserve|reserves]] for [[Reinsurance Contracts Held|reinsurance contracts held]] by analyzing [[Claims Gross|claims gross]] and net of [[Reinsurance Held|reinsurance held]] vs. [[Claims Gross|claims gross]] of [[Reinsurance Held|reinsurance held]] and [[Claim|claims]] ceded to [[Reinsurance|reinsurance]]. (C4)
>    - b. Calculate [[Reserve|reserves]] for [[Reinsurance Held|reinsurance held]] using appropriate techniques and assumptions that reflect the characteristics of [[Reinsurance Contracts Held|reinsurance contracts held]] (e.g., contract terms and conditions, [[Data Availability|data availability]]). (C3)
>    - c. Calculate [[Reserve|reserves]] for various [[Layer of Insurance|layers]] of [[Claim|claims]]. (C3)
>    - d. Explain the consequences and calculate the effect on both ceding and assuming companies with respect to the following under various structures: (C3)
>       - i. [[Risk Transfer|Risk transfer]] and its effect on [[Regulatory Capital|regulatory capital]] requirements (including impact on [[Minimum Regulatory Capital|minimum regulatory capital]] requirements);
>       - ii. [[Cash Flow|Cash flow]];
>       - iii. [[Financial Statements|Financial statements]]; and
>       - iv. Tax and [[Reserve Credit|reserve credit]] requirements.
> 4. Evaluate [[Stochastic Processes|stochastic processes]] for estimating [[Reserve|reserves]].
>    - a. Evaluate the likely sources of uncertainty in estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (B5)
>    - b. Evaluate the uses of stochastic techniques for estimating [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (B5)
>    - c. Evaluate the following types of stochastic techniques, including data required, key assumptions, and mechanics: (B5)
>       - i. Analytic; and
>       - ii. Simulation-based.
>    - d. Evaluate the issues, advantages, and disadvantages of each of the techniques, including sensitivity to assumptions. (C5)
>    - e. Evaluate the approach to aggregating the results of [[Stochastic Estimate|stochastic estimates]] of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]] across [[Multiple Line|multiple lines]] of business and discuss methods of [[Correlation|correlation]]. (C5)
> 5. Evaluate the results of [[Reserve|reserves]] for adequacy and reasonableness. (D5)
> 6. Generate alternative estimates of [[Reserve|reserves]] and highlight some of the professional issues in resolving them. (D6)
> 7. Describe the factors an [[Actuary|actuary]] should consider and the typical diagnostics used in assessing the reasonableness of the results of estimates of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]. (D2)
> 8. Critique the reasonableness of changes in the results of [[Reserve|reserves]] over time by describing the factors an [[Actuary|actuary]] should consider. (D5)
> 9. Analyze current experience to monitor performance and recommend [[Reserve|reserves]] for [[Interim Valuation|interim valuations]]. (D4)
> 10. Communicate an estimate of [[Unpaid Claims|unpaid claims]]:
>     - a. Describe what is meant by a [[Best-Estimate Reserve|best-estimate reserve]]. (B2)
>     - b. Describe the uses, advantages, and disadvantages of estimating ranges of [[Reserve|reserves]] using the following approaches: (B2)
>        - i. Stochastic models.
>        - ii. Scenario tests; and
>        - iii. Use of alternative sets of assumptions.
>     - c. Understand the issues to be considered when [[Communicating Range|communicating ranges]] in estimates of [[Reserve|reserves]] and uncertainties. (D2)
> 11. Compare and analyze [[Valuation|valuation]] methods in Canada and the [[United State|United States]]. (B4)

> 2. Analyze how [[Insurance Contract|insurance contracts]] are measured using the: (B4)
>    - a. General measurement approach;
>    - b. [[Variable Fee|Variable fee]] approach; and
>    - c. [[Premium Allocation Approach|Premium allocation approach]].
> 3. Reflect considerations related to the [[Level of Aggregation|level of aggregation]] when accounting for business under [[IFRS]] 17, including determination of the unit of account and setting of [[Portfolio|portfolios]] and groups of contracts. (C3)
> 4. Estimate [[Future Cash Flow|future cash flows]] for the liability for remaining coverage and the [[Liability for Incurred Claims|liability for incurred claims]] under the general measurement approach, [[Variable Fee|variable fee]] approach, and the [[Premium Allocation Approach|premium allocation approach]]. (C4)
> 5. Create [[Discount Rate Curve|discount rate curves]] both using the top-down and bottom-up approaches referred to in [[IFRS]] 17. (C6)
> 6. Determine [[Risk|risk]] adjustment for [[Non-Financial Risk|non-financial risk]], including:
>    - a. Reflect the criteria for and measurement of the [[Risk|risk]] adjustment under the general measurement approach, [[Variable Fee|variable fee]] approach, and the [[Premium Allocation Approach|premium allocation approach]]. (C4)
>    - b. Apply multiple methods, including: (C4)
>       - i. Quantile;
>       - ii. [[Cost of Capital|Cost of capital]]; and
>       - iii. Margin.
>    - c. Adjust the role of diversification and allocation in the determination and the application of the [[Risk|risk]] adjustment. (C4)
>    - d. Determine the [[Risk|risk]] adjustment for [[Reinsurance Contracts Held|reinsurance contracts held]]. (C4)
> 7. Determine key actuarial components of [[IFRS 17 Financial Statement|IFRS 17 financial statements]], including:
>    - a. [[Contractual Service Margin|Contractual service margin]]; (C4)
>    - b. Liability for remaining coverage; (C4)
>    - c. [[Liability for Incurred Claims|Liability for incurred claims]]; (C4)
>    - d. [[Risk]] adjustment for [[Non-Financial Risk|non-financial risk]]; (C4)
>    - e. Discounting; (C4)
>    - f. [[Loss Component|Loss component]]; (C4)
>    - g. [[Onerous Contract|Onerous contracts]]; (C4)
