A Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City Economic Review article of 20 June 2024 that estimates the cost of guaranteeing against stablecoin losses using standard option pricing methods as applied to bank deposit insurance. For four major (unnamed) stablecoins it estimates the annualized cost of a 90-day guarantee at between nearly zero and almost $0.13 per dollar of stablecoins.
