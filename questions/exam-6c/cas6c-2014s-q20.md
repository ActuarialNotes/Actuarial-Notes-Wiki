Identify and briefly explain the impact on the Minimum Capital Test (MCT) if each of the following actions is performed by a company.

## Part a (0.5 points)

Reinvests the cash from matured government bonds into common shares.

### Explanation
Common shares are riskier, so the balance sheet factor applied to it will be greater, meaning higher capital required. It should lower the MCT ratio.

### Examiner Report
- For this question, candidates were expected to know that common shares have a higher capital required for balance sheet assets than government bonds.
- Candidates scored really well on this question.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Increases exposure to liability lines of business.

### Explanation
Liability lines have long payout period, which means that unpaid claims liabilities will increase. The capital required factor for unpaid claims is usually higher for liability lines, so the capital required will increase, which should lower the MCT ratio.

### Examiner Report
- For this question, candidates were expected to know that liability lines of business have a higher capital required for unpaid claims factor.
- Candidates scored really well on this question.

## Part c (0.5 points)

Increases the deferred commission amount.

### Explanation
If the deferred commission, which is an asset, is increased, it should mean an increase in equity. It will then increase the capital available, which will produce a higher MCT ratio.

Alternatively:

Decrease MCT as capital required for assets will increase.

### Examiner Report
- For this question, candidates needed to know the impacts caused by an increase in deferred commissions.
- Candidates had difficulty with this question.
- This question was challenging because a change in deferred commissions will have multiple impacts (expenses, underwriting income, net income, surplus, capital available, capital required).

## Part d (0.5 points)

Increases the reinsurance quota share percentage with an unaffiliated registered reinsurer.

### Explanation
Unaffiliated registered reinsurer balances have low factors applied. So, the decrease of liability (unpaid claims and unearned premium) should lower the capital required, producing a higher MCT ratio.

Alternatively:

MCT decreases since capital required increases from the credit factor for unaffiliated registered reinsurance.

### Examiner Report
- For this question, candidates were expected to know the impacts caused by an increase in quota share reinsurance with an unaffiliated reinsurer.
- This question was challenging in the way that there was not a lot of assumptions given in the question and that the impacts are dependent on those assumptions. This resulted in candidates needing to give a more general answer on this question.

## Part e (0.5 points)

Increases the reinsurance quota share percentage with an unregistered reinsurer. A letter of credit is held in the same amount of the increase of ceded unpaid losses and unpaid premium reserve (UPR).

### Explanation
Not all the letter of credit amount produces a credit for the margin of unregistered reinsurer. Not easy to predict impact: unpaid claims and unearned premiums will be lower, but there will be capital required for the fact that the reinsurer is unregistered. Most likely, the MCT will be lower.

Alternatively:

Will increase capital required for reinsurance ceded to unregistered reinsurers. Decrease MCT.

### Examiner Report
- For this question, candidates were expected to know the impacts caused by an increase in quota share reinsurance with an unregistered reinsurer. They also needed to understand the calculation of capital required for unregistered reinsurers.
- This question was challenging in the way that there was not a lot of assumptions given in the question and that the impacts are dependent on those assumptions. This resulted in candidates needing to give a more general answer on this question.
