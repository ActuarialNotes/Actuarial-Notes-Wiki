Volume 2 of the Handbooks in Finance's Handbook of Asset and Liability Management, edited by Stavros A. Zenios and William T. Ziemba and published in 2007. It portrays asset and liability management (ALM) in practice, in contrast to Volume 1, which addresses the theories and methodologies behind the models. In original articles, practitioners and scholars describe and analyze models used in banking, insurance, money management, individual investor financial planning, pension funds and social security.

> - [[Exam CP-351 (SOA)|Exam CP-351]] — Chapter 13, Stochastic Programming Models for Strategic and Tactical Asset Allocation - A Study from Norwegian Life Insurance, and Chapter 18, A Scenario Approach to ALM; outcome 3b.
