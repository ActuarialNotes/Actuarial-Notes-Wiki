Four business auto submissions have the same number of vehicles and similar loss records. Which presents the most severe liability exposure for the underwriter?

- A) Sedans used by sales representatives within one metropolitan area
- B) Light vans delivering flowers within a 30-mile radius
- C) Pickup trucks used by landscapers on local job sites
- D) Heavy tractor-trailers hauling chemicals on interstate routes

## Explanation

Business auto liability underwriting looks at vehicle type and weight, since a heavy truck causes far more severe injuries than a car; at what the vehicles carry, especially hazardous cargo; and at the radius of operation, since long-distance driving means more miles, fatigue and unfamiliar roads. The tractor-trailers combine all three. The answer is **D**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Light vehicles on local routes carry the least severity of the four.
- **B** — Light vans with a short radius and harmless cargo are a modest exposure.
- **C** — Pickups are lighter than tractor-trailers and stay local.
