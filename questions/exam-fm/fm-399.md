Jennifer paid $X$ for a ten-year 10,000 face amount bond at issue with an annual coupon rate of 5% paid annually. She immediately reinvests the coupons at an annual effective interest rate of 8%. The annual effective yield on her investment of $X$ is 8.5% over the ten-year period.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 7626
- B) 7704
- C) 7987
- D) 8068
- E) 8155

## Explanation

Accumulate values to time ten. The coupons are $0.05(10{,}000) = 500$ a year, reinvested at 8%, and the bond pays its face amount of 10,000 at time ten. An annual effective yield of 8.5% on the investment of $X$ means that $X$ accumulated at 8.5% equals what Jennifer holds at time ten:

$$
\begin{align*}
X(1.085)^{10} &= 500\,s_{\overline{10}|0.08} + 10{,}000 \\
X &= \frac{500\,s_{\overline{10}|0.08} + 10{,}000}{1.085^{10}} \\
  &= \frac{7243.28 + 10{,}000}{1.085^{10}} \\
  &= \frac{17{,}243.28}{2.260983} \\
  &= 7626.45
\end{align*}
$$
