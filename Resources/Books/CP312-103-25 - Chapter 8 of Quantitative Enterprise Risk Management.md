Market Risk Models, the eighth chapter (pages 227 to 261) of Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management by Mary R. Hardy and David Saunders of the University of Waterloo, published by Cambridge University Press in 2022. The chapter takes in the empirical behaviour of equity prices, geometric Brownian motion, the independent lognormal model, the GARCH model and regime-switching models, and then fitting models to returns data and choosing a model.
