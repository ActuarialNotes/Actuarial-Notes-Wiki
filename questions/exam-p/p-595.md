The check-out times for customers at a grocery store are modeled by a continuous uniform distribution between 1.1 and 8.6 minutes. The store would like to advertise that "no more than 5% of customers require more than $y$ minutes to check out."

Calculate the minimum value for $y$.

- A) $1.475$
- B) $7.125$
- C) $8.170$
- D) $8.225$
- E) $8.545$

## Explanation

Let $X$ be the check-out time, uniform on $[1.1, 8.6]$. Requiring at most 5% of customers to exceed $y$ makes $y$ the 95th percentile:

$$
P[X < y] = \frac{y - 1.1}{8.6 - 1.1} = 0.95
$$

Solving,

$$
\begin{align*}
y - 1.1 &= 0.95(7.5) \\
        &= 7.125 \\
      y &= \mathbf{8.225}
\end{align*}
$$

Choice (B), 7.125, is that intermediate quantity — 95% of the interval's *width*. A uniform percentile is measured from the left endpoint, so the lower limit 1.1 has to be added back.
