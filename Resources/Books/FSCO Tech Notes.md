The companion to [[FSCO Private Auto]]: what the regulator expects **technically** behind each item of an Ontario auto rate filing. Read for [[Exam 6C (CAS)|Exam 6C]] objective A1; the syllabus assigns **Sections A–F, pp. 1–14**.

## Structure

- **A. Introduction** · **B. Requirement to file** · **C. Filing documentation** · **D. Reviewing rate adequacy**
- **E. Major filings** — 23 numbered technical notes, keyed to the Section 4 items of the filing guidelines
- **F. Rate filing checklist for major filings**
- *Exhibit 1 — criteria for simplified filings*

## Why it is on the syllabus

These notes are where the Exam 5 ratemaking toolkit meets Canadian [[Rate Regulation|rate regulation]]: every standard technique — development, trend, on-levelling, credibility, profit provision — reappears here as something a regulator will test the support for. A common exam task is to say what a filing is missing, or why the regulator would reject a particular selection.
