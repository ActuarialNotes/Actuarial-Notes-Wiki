Which of the following is a speculative risk for a manufacturer?

- A) A fire that could destroy its main warehouse
- B) A claim from a visitor injured on its premises
- C) Launching a new product line that could succeed or fail
- D) The theft of trucks from its delivery depot

## Explanation

A **speculative risk** offers the chance of a gain as well as a loss. A new product line could earn a profit or lose money. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — A fire offers only the chance of loss or no loss: a pure risk.
- **B** — A liability claim can cost the manufacturer money but never earn it any: a pure risk.
- **D** — Theft, like fire, is a pure risk; the best outcome is that no theft occurs.

Pure risks are the traditional subject of insurance, while enterprise risk management considers speculative risks too.
