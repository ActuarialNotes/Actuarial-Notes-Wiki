An insurer writes \$2,400,000 of one-year policies during October and computes its unearned premium reserve by the monthly pro rata method. How much of this October business is earned by December 31 of the same year?

- A) \$400,000
- B) \$500,000
- C) \$600,000
- D) \$1,900,000

## Explanation

The monthly pro rata method assumes business is written evenly through each month, so the average October policy starts mid-October. A one-year policy written in month $m$ is $\tfrac{2m-1}{24}$ unearned at year end, which for October ($m = 10$) is $\tfrac{19}{24}$, leaving $\tfrac{5}{24}$ earned.

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Earned} &= 2{,}400{,}000 \times \frac{5}{24} \\
&= 500{,}000
\end{align*}
$$

The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — \$400,000 is $\tfrac{2}{12}$, which treats all October writings as written on October 31.
- **C** — \$600,000 is $\tfrac{3}{12}$, which treats them as written on October 1.
- **D** — \$1,900,000 is the unearned premium, $\tfrac{19}{24}$ of the writings, not the earned premium.
