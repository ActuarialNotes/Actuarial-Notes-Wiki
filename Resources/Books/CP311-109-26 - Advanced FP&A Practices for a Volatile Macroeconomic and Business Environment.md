An article in McKinsey on Finance number 87 (January 2025) on forecasting when macroeconomic conditions and business dynamics are profoundly complex. It sets out six practical steps for financial planning and analysis (FP&A) teams to deliver more accurate forecasts, particularly under uncertainty; the first is to assign probability values to major assumptions.
