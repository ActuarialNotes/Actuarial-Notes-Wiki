A paper by the Climate Risk Task Force of the International Actuarial Association, dated September 2020. It examines the categories of climate-related risks and their importance for actuaries in identifying, measuring, managing and reporting this type of risk. It reviews their impact on actuarial work under five broad categories: actuarial modelling, product management, risk and capital management, investment management, and disclosure.
