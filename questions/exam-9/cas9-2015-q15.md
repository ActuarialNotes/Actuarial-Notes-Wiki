An insurance company is considering investing in only one new line of business. The company will base its decision on one of two risk measures: either the economic profit or the risk-adjusted return on capital (RAROC). The options are summarized in the table below:

| | Line A | Line B |
|---|---|---|
| Premium | \$10,000,000 | \$8,000,000 |
| Expense Ratio | 10% | 15% |
| Loss Ratio | 80% | 75% |
| Required Capital | \$9,500,000 | \$4,500,000 |

- Investment return is 6.5%.
- Premium is collected immediately.
- Expenses are paid immediately.
- Capital is released at the end of the year.
- Losses are paid at the end of the year.

## Part a (1.5 points)

Demonstrate which line would be preferred for each of the risk measures.

### Explanation
Economic profit is premium less expenses less the (discounted) losses plus investment income, taking the given loss ratio as a discounted loss ratio:

$$\text{Economic profit} = \text{Premium} - \text{Expense} - PV(\text{Loss}) + \text{Investment income}$$

$$A = [10M - 10M \times 10\% - 80\% \times 10M] + 0.065\,[10M - 10\% \times 10M] = 1.585M$$

$$B = [8M - 8M \times 15\% - 75\% \times 8M] + 0.065\,[8M - 15\% \times 8M] = 1.242M$$

Line A is preferred under the economic profit measure.

$$RAROC = \frac{\text{Economic profit}}{\text{Capital}}$$

$$A = \frac{1.585M}{9.5M} = 0.167 \qquad B = \frac{1.242M}{4.5M} = 0.276$$

Line B is preferred under the RAROC measure.

Alternatively:

The report's model answer invests the premium net of expenses for the year: Economic Profit = Premium × (1 - Expense Ratio) × (1 + Investment Return) - Premium × Loss Ratio, giving \$10M × (1 - .1) × 1.065 - \$10M × .8 = \$1.575M (as printed) for A and \$8M × (1 - .15) × 1.065 - \$10M × .75 (as printed) = \$1.242M for B, so A is preferred. RORAC = Economic Profit / Required Capital: \$1.575M / \$9.5M = 16.7% for A and \$1.242M / \$4.5M = 27.6% for B, so B is preferred. It was also acceptable to calculate economic profit as underwriting income and investment income separately: Premium × (1 - Expense Ratio - Loss Ratio) + Premium × (1 - Expense Ratio) × Investment Return.

### Examiner Report
For full credit, a candidate was expected to demonstrate knowledge of and correctly calculate the economic profit and RAROC.

Candidates were also expected to understand what economic profit is, know how to calculate economic profit, and select the preferred line based on the measures.

Candidates generally performed well on this part, especially for RAROC. Common errors included:

- subtracting cost of capital from profit to get the economic profit
- discounting losses to the beginning of the year but investing net premium to the end of the year
- ignoring investment income

Some candidates only calculated RAROC and missed the instructions to calculate both measures. It was also important to clearly identify which line was preferred and why.

## Part b (0.5 points)

Calculate the additional premium that would need to be charged for line A to make its risk-adjusted return on capital equal to line B. Assume the required capital for line A does not change.

### Explanation
Line A's RAROC is set equal to line B's 27.6% with its required capital unchanged. The report accepted several treatments of how expenses and losses respond to the additional premium $P$:

- **All fixed:** $\dfrac{(10M \times (1 - 0.1) + P) \times 1.065 - 10M \times 0.8}{9.5M} = 0.276$, which implies $P = 0.974M$. One sample: $0.276 = \dfrac{(10000)(0.9)(1.065) + \pi(1.065) - 10000(0.8)}{9500}$, so $\pi = 973{,}709$.
- **Fixed losses, variable expenses:** $\dfrac{(10M + P)(1 - 0.1) \times 1.065 - 10M \times 0.8}{9.5M} = 0.276$, which implies $P = 1.082M$. One sample: $0.276 \times 9.5M = (10M + X)(1 - 10\%)(1.065) - 10M \times 0.8$, so $2.622M = 9.585M + 0.9585X - 8M$, $1.037M = 0.9585X$ and $X = 1.0819M$.
- **All variable:** $\dfrac{(10M + P)(1 - 0.1) \times 1.065 - (10M + P) \times 0.8}{9.5M} = 0.276$, which implies $P = 6.540M$. One sample: $27.6\% \times 9.5 = (10 + x)(1 - 0.1) \times 1.065 - (10 + x) \times 0.8$, so $2.622 = 9.585 - 8 + 0.9585x - 0.8x$ and $x = 6.543M$.
- **Directly, as a required change in premium:** $(0.276 \times 9.5M - 1.575M)/1.065 = 0.974M$; one sample wrote $\dfrac{1.585 + R \times 1.065}{9.5} = 0.276$, so $R = 0.974M$.

### Examiner Report
For full credit, a candidate was expected to calculate the additional premium to reach the target
RAROC.
Candidates were expected to know how to adjust the premium to reach the target RAROC.
Candidates performed very well on this part. Common errors included subtracting cost of capital, or providing the total premium instead of the additional premium.
