A perpetuity-immediate has annual payments starting at 1, increasing by 1 each year until it reaches 10, then decreasing by 1 each year until it reaches 5, and remaining at 5 thereafter.

The present value of this perpetuity at an annual effective interest rate of 9% is $X$.

Calculate $X$.

- A) 51
- B) 53
- C) 55
- D) 58
- E) 61

## Explanation

At 9%, an increasing perpetuity-immediate paying $1, 2, 3, \ldots$ has present value

$$
\begin{align*}
(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|} &= \frac{1}{i} + \frac{1}{i^{2}} \\
  &= \frac{1}{0.09} + \frac{1}{0.09^{2}} \\
  &= 134.5679
\end{align*}
$$

Build the payments from three increasing perpetuities. $(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|}$ pays $t$ at time $t$. Subtracting $2v^{10}(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|}$ takes away $2(t-10)$ at each time $t > 10$, leaving $20 - t$: payments of 9, 8, 7, 6, 5 at times 11 to 15. Adding $v^{15}(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|}$ puts back $t - 15$ at each time $t > 15$, leaving $(20 - t) + (t - 15) = 5$ from then on. With $v^{10} = 0.42241$ and $v^{15} = 0.274538$:

$$
\begin{align*}
X &= (Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|} - 2v^{10}(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|} + v^{15}(Ia)_{\overline{\infty}|} \\
  &= 134.5679(1 - 2 \times 0.42241 + 0.274538) \\
  &= 134.5679(0.429718) \\
  &= 57.83
\end{align*}
$$
