At a commercial insurer, which person is most likely to analyze a line's loss experience, conduct underwriting audits, and recommend changes to the line's eligibility rules and authority levels?

- A) A line underwriter deciding individual submissions
- B) A producer with binding authority
- C) A staff underwriter supporting underwriting management
- D) A premium auditor verifying exposures

## Explanation

Staff underwriters develop, interpret and apply underwriting policy and guidelines for a line. They analyze loss experience, follow market and regulatory developments, conduct underwriting audits, train line underwriters and recommend changes to forms, rates, eligibility rules and authority levels. The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Line underwriters *apply* the guidelines to individual submissions and renewals rather than writing them.
- **B** — A producer works within the guidelines and the agency agreement; it does not set them.
- **D** — A premium auditor verifies exposure and classification after a policy ends. The findings can inform underwriting, but setting eligibility rules is not the auditor's job.
