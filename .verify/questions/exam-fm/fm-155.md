## [F-001] Thousands commas inside math render with a thin space
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-28T14:47Z/aa33
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: nit
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, lines 45 and 48
- claim: Math spans write thousands separators as a bare comma (35,000, 65,000, 100,000, 105,000), which KaTeX sets as punctuation followed by a thin space ('35, 000').
- evidence: KaTeX treats ',' in math mode as punctuation (mpunct) and adds a thin space after it; '{,}' makes it an ordinary character. The digits themselves match SOA's solution PDF.
- source_rank: 5
- proposed_action: Brace the thousands commas inside math as {,}; no digit changes.
