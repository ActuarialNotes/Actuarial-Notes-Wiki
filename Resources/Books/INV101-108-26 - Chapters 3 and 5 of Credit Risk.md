An integrated treatment of the conceptual, practical and empirical foundations of credit risk pricing and risk measurement, by two economists who model credit risk to measure portfolio risk and to price defaultable bonds, credit derivatives and other securities exposed to credit risk. It presents both the structural and the reduced-form approaches to pricing defaultable securities and assesses their fit to historical data, with a treatment of credit derivatives that includes credit swaps, collateralized debt obligations, credit guarantees, lines of credit and spread options. The SOA supplies Chapters 3 and 5 as study note INV101-108-26.
