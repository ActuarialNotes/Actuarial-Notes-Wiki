A study note of the Education Committee of the Society of Actuaries, dated May 2025. It considers the conditions under which the Chain Ladder method is applicable to a business case, how its assumptions can be tested, what to explore when they do not hold, and how its prediction error is calculated so as to understand the distribution of reserves rather than rely solely on point estimates.
