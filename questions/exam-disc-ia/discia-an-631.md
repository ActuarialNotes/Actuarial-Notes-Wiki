A dry cleaner is analyzing its commercial property loss exposures. Customers' garments in its care are often worth more than its own equipment. Which statement best describes this exposure and how the BPP can address it?

- A) A bailee's property exposure, insurable as Personal Property of Others with a limit shown
- B) It is not a property exposure, because the dry cleaner does not own the garments
- C) It is covered automatically as stock, because the garments are processed in the business
- D) It is excluded from the BPP and can be insured only under a liability policy

## Explanation

A party can have a property loss exposure in property it does not own: a bailee such as a dry cleaner can be held responsible for customers' property in its care. The BPP addresses it with **Personal Property of Others**, which covers property of others in the insured's care, custody or control, for the owner's account, when a limit is shown for it. The answer is **A**.

**Why not the others**

- **B** — Ownership is not required; a bailee's responsibility for property in its care is a property exposure.
- **C** — "Stock" is merchandise held for sale, raw materials and goods in process or finished goods. Customers' garments are not the dry cleaner's stock.
- **D** — The BPP does cover property of others in the insured's care through its own limit; it is not left to liability insurance.
