## [F-001] Explanation misstates why Plot IV fails
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-10-04T20:47Z/8861
- date: 2026-10-05
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: Explanation, bullet 'Plots III and IV'
- claim: 'Plots III and IV have a PC2 that is not orthogonal to the sloped PC1.'
- evidence: In Plot IV of the paper's figure (fmasi-18.pdf PDF p.42; identical in Media/Attachments/masi-2018f-q38-pca-plots.png) PC1 (solid) is horizontal at y≈0.24 and PC2 (dashed) is vertical at x≈0.5: they are orthogonal to each other and PC1 is not sloped. Plot IV fails because its PC1 is not the direction of greatest variance of the upward-sloping cloud — ISLR2 §6.3.1, p.253 (PDF p.261): 'The first principal component direction of the data is that along which the observations vary the most', with Figure 6.14 drawing the first PC (solid) along the cloud and the second (dashed) perpendicular to it. The bullet is right for Plot III (vertical PC2, sloped PC1). Key A is unaffected.
- source_rank: 2
- proposed_action: Give Plot IV its own reason: PC1 and PC2 are perpendicular but axis-aligned, so PC1 is not along the direction of maximum variance.
