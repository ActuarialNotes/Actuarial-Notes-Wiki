- claim: Annual profit $\sim N(\mu,\mu^3)$. $P(\text{loss})=5\%$. Calculate expected profit.
- evidence: SOA Q231, questions PDF p.98: "A company’s annual profit, in billions, has a normal distribution with variance equal to the cube of its mean. The probability of an annual loss is 5%." The vault omits "in billions". Variance = mean cubed is not scale-invariant, so the unit fixes the answer: P(X<0) = Phi(-mu/mu^1.5) = 0.05 gives mu^-0.5 = 1.645, mu = 1/1.645^2 = 0.3695 in the stated unit (SOA solution p.69: "0.37 in billions, or 370 million" = key A). Read in millions, 0.3695 would be 369,500; in dollars, 0.37 dollars; neither is an option, so the key is reachable only by guessing the unit from the options.
