An investor deposits 1000 at the beginning of each year for five years in a fund earning a 5% annual effective interest rate. The interest from this fund can be reinvested at a 4% annual effective interest rate.

Calculate the total accumulated value at the end of five years.

- A) 5058
- B) 5227
- C) 5436
- D) 5641
- E) 5791

## Explanation

The deposits are made at times 0 through 4, so the balance earning 5% during year $k$ is $1000k$ and the interest paid at the end of year $k$ is $50k$, for $k = 1, 2, \ldots, 5$. That interest is reinvested at 4%.

The accumulated value at the end of five years is the five deposits plus the accumulated interest. Split the interest into a level 50 a year, which accumulates to $50\,s_{\overline{5}|0.04}$, plus the increasing amounts $0, 50, 100, 150, 200$, which accumulate to $50\left[\dfrac{s_{\overline{5}|0.04} - 5}{0.04}\right]$. With $s_{\overline{5}|0.04} = 5.416323$:

$$
\begin{align*}
AV &= 1000(5) + \left[50\,s_{\overline{5}|0.04} + 50\left[\frac{s_{\overline{5}|0.04} - 5}{0.04}\right]\right] \\
   &= 5000 + 50(5.416323) + 50\left(\frac{5.416323 - 5}{0.04}\right) \\
   &= 5000 + 270.82 + 520.40 \\
   &= 5791.22
\end{align*}
$$
