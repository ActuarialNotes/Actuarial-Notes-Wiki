FSRA Approach Guidance No. PE0202APP, effective May 22, 2020. It outlines how the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario reviews applications to transfer commuted values or to purchase annuities under sections 19(4) and (5) of Regulation 909 under the Pension Benefits Act, when a defined benefit plan's transfer ratio has declined by ten per cent or more and the resulting ratio is below 0.9, and how it exercises its discretion in those matters.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-301 (SOA)|Exam RET-301]] — Outcomes 3a, 3b; the whole guidance, as study note RET301-108-25.

## Purpose and Scope
- The guidance is an Approach to reviewing applications to transfer [[Commuted Value|commuted values]] or purchase annuities under sections 19(4) and (5) of Regulation 909.
- It replaces FSCO Policy T800-402, Commuted Value Transfers.

## Rationale and Background
- Conditions Triggering Application of Sections 19(4) and (5)
    - If the administrator knows or ought to know that events since the most recently filed valuation report have reduced the transfer ratio to less than 0.9 when it was at least 1.0, or by at least 10% when it was less than 1.0, all commuted value transfers and Section 43 annuity purchases are suspended.

## Principles
- In reviewing applications FSRA assesses the risks posed to terminating and remaining plan beneficiaries.
