## Part a (0.5 points)

Calculate the unadjusted 24 to ultimate volume weighted average development factor based on the reported loss triangle limited to \$1,000,000.

### Explanation
LDF(24-Ult) = (3348 / 2465) * [(2465 + 2747) / (1354 + 1535)] = 2.4503

Alternatively:

LDF (24-36) = (2465 + 2747) / (1354 + 1535) = 1.804
LDF (36-48) = 3348 / 2465 = 1.358
LDF (24-Ult) = 1.804 * 1.358 = 2.450

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to calculate unadjusted age-to-age and age-to-ult loss development factors.
Candidates generally did well on this question.

## Part b (1.75 points)

Calculate the applicable 24 to ultimate volume weighted average development factor based on the reported loss triangle limited to \$1,000,000 using the procedure outlined by Sahasrabuddhe.

### Explanation
Adjust the triangle to the basic limit (at the AY 2016 cost level):

| AY | 24 | 36 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2013 | 1354 × (119/110) = 1464.78 | 2465 × (210/215) = 2407.67 | 3348 × (244/255) = 3203.58 |
| 2014 | 1535 × (119/115) = 1588.39 | 2747 × (210/214) = 2695.65 | |

$$\text{LDF}(24\text{–}36) = \frac{2407.67 + 2695.65}{1464.78 + 1588.39} = 1.6715$$

$$\text{LDF}(36\text{–}48) = \frac{3203.58}{2407.67} = 1.3306$$

$$\text{LDF}(24\text{–Ult}) = 1.6715 \times 1.3306 = 2.2240$$

Bring to the \$1m limit:

$$2.2240 \times \frac{281/244}{117/119} = 2.6050$$

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to calculate the cumulative reported loss triangle at accident year 2016
cost level and basic limit.  Once calculated, then the incremental and cumulative LDFs at the basic limit and accident year 2016 cost level could be computed.  Finally, the volume weighted average LDFs at limit of triangle and historical cost levels could be computed.

Common errors include:

- Using the incorrect triangle when computing the cumulative reported loss triangle at AY 2016 cost level and basic limit.
- Stopping at the cumulative volume weighted LDF at basic limit at AY 2016 cost level and not continuing with the last step of calculating the volume weighted LDF at limit of triangle and historical cost level
- Using 2014 or 2016 as the 24 month LEV instead of the 2015 year.
