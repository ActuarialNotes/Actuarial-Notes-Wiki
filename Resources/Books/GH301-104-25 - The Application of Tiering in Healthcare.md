A study note, GH301-104-25, supplied by the Society of Actuaries for GH 301 under the title "The Application of Tiering in Healthcare". According to the course strategy guide, it is one of the study notes that present the topics of physician profiling and tiering and how a tiered cost sharing structure could work, as examples of applications of provider contracting techniques.
