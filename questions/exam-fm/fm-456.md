A 20-year bond has an annual coupon rate of 3% paid semiannually. The bond is priced using an annual nominal yield rate of 2% convertible semiannually. The cumulative amount of premium amortization exceeds 50% of the premium at purchase, for the first time, immediately after the $n$th coupon is paid.

Calculate $n$.

- A) 18
- B) 19
- C) 20
- D) 21
- E) 22

## Explanation

Assume the face amount is 1. Each semiannual coupon is $0.015$, the semiannual yield rate is $0.01$, and there are 40 coupons. The price of the bond is

$$
\begin{align*}
P &= 0.015\,a_{\overline{40}|0.01} + 1.01^{-40} \\
  &= 1.1642
\end{align*}
$$

At the given coupon and yield rates, the premium is $1.1642 - 1 = 0.1642$. The cumulative premium amortization is the price minus the current book value, so it reaches half the premium when the book value has fallen to $1 + 0.1642/2 = 1.0821$. Just after the $k$th coupon, $40 - k$ coupons remain:

$$
\begin{align*}
1.0821 &= 0.015\,a_{\overline{40-k}|0.01} + 1.01^{-(40-k)} \\
       &= 0.015\cdot\frac{1 - 1.01^{-(40-k)}}{0.01} + 1.01^{-(40-k)} \\
       &= 1.5 - 0.5(1.01)^{-(40-k)} \\
1.01^{-(40-k)} &= 0.8358 \\
40 - k &= -\frac{\ln 0.8358}{\ln 1.01} \\
       &= 18.03 \\
k &= 21.97
\end{align*}
$$

The book value falls with each coupon, so it first drops below 1.0821, and the cumulative premium amortization first exceeds 50% of the premium, at the next whole coupon: $n = 22$.
