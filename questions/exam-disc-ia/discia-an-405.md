A named insured's PAP carries ISO's underinsured motorists endorsement (PP 03 11) with a \$25,000 limit. An at-fault driver whose bodily injury liability limit is \$25,000 injures her, causing \$60,000 of damages, and that driver's insurer pays its \$25,000 limit. What does her underinsured motorists coverage pay?

- A) \$0
- B) \$25,000
- C) \$35,000
- D) \$60,000

## Explanation

Under PP 03 11 a vehicle is underinsured only if its bodily injury liability limit is **less than the insured's underinsured motorists limit**. Here the at-fault driver's \$25,000 limit equals her \$25,000 UIM limit, so the vehicle is not underinsured and the endorsement pays **nothing**, even though \$35,000 of damages are unpaid. The answer is **A**. Buying a UIM limit no higher than the state minimum gives little protection under this definition.

**Why not the others**

- **B** pays the UIM limit as if the vehicle qualified.
- **C** pays the unpaid damages, which is the result under a state definition that compares the available liability coverage with the insured's damages (Virginia's, for example), not under PP 03 11.
- **D** ignores both the definition and the \$25,000 already paid by the at-fault driver's insurer.
