The number of monthly claims on an insurance policy has a distribution given by

| Number of monthly claims | Probability |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | $s$ |
| 1 | $t$ |
| 2 | $0.75s$ |
| 3 or more | 0 |

A random sample of five policies is selected, and the claims for a given month are recorded. The numbers of claims on the five policies are mutually independent. Let the random variable $Y$ denote the number of policies from the sample with fewer than two monthly claims.

Let $c = P[Y = 5]$.

Determine which of the following is equal to $t$.

- A) $\dfrac{4 - 4c^{0.2}}{3}$
- B) $\dfrac{3 - 7c^{0.2}}{3}$
- C) $\dfrac{4c^{0.2} - 4}{3}$
- D) $\dfrac{5c^{0.2} - 3}{3}$
- E) $\dfrac{7c^{0.2} - 4}{3}$

## Explanation

A policy has "fewer than two" claims when it has 0 or 1, which happens with probability $s + t$. Across five independent policies, $Y$ is binomial with $n = 5$ and success probability $s+t$.

All five succeeding gives

$$
\begin{align*}
c = P[Y=5] &= \binom{5}{5}(s+t)^5 = (s+t)^5
\end{align*}
$$

so

$$
s + t = c^{1/5} \implies s = c^{1/5} - t
$$

The three probabilities in the table must sum to 1:

$$
\begin{align*}
s + t + 0.75s &= 1 \\
1.75s + t &= 1
\end{align*}
$$

Substitute $s = c^{1/5} - t$ and solve for $t$:

$$
\begin{align*}
1.75\left(c^{1/5} - t\right) + t &= 1 \\
1.75c^{1/5} - 0.75t &= 1 \\
0.75t &= 1.75c^{1/5} - 1 \\
t &= \frac{1.75c^{1/5} - 1}{0.75} \\
  &= \mathbf{\frac{7c^{0.2} - 4}{3}}
\end{align*}
$$

Multiplying numerator and denominator by 4 is what turns $1.75$ and $0.75$ into the integers 7 and 3. Choice (C) has the same shape with the wrong coefficients — it comes from using $s + t + s = 1$, forgetting the 0.75 weight on the two-claim row.
