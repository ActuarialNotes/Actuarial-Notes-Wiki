A web article from the Corporate Finance Institute, published 24 April 2019, that defines basis risk as the inherent risk a trader takes when hedging a position with a contrary position in a derivative of the asset, such as a futures contract. It explains that the price spread between the cash price and the futures price may widen or narrow, works a rice farmer's futures hedge through three basis scenarios, and lists four types of basis risk.
