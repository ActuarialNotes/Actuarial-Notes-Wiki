Bond A pays 1 at the end of each year in perpetuity. Bond B is a zero coupon bond that matures in ten years. The Macaulay duration of bond A is twice that of bond B.

Calculate the modified duration of bond A.

- A) 19.0
- B) 19.5
- C) 20.0
- D) 21.0
- E) $\infty$

## Explanation

A zero coupon bond's Macaulay duration is its term, so the Macaulay duration of bond B is $D_{MAC}^{B} = 10$. The Macaulay duration of bond A is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC}^{A} &= 2D_{MAC}^{B} \\
            &= 20
\end{align*}
$$

The price of bond A is $P = \dfrac{1}{i}$, where $i$ is the annual yield. So the modified duration of bond A is

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD}^{A} &= -\frac{P'(i)}{P(i)} \\
            &= -\frac{-1/i^{2}}{1/i} \\
            &= \frac{1}{i}
\end{align*}
$$

The Macaulay duration is the modified duration times $1+i$:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MAC}^{A} &= D_{MOD}^{A}(1+i) \\
            &= \frac{1+i}{i}
\end{align*}
$$

Setting this equal to 20:

$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{1+i}{i} &= 20 \\
i &= \frac{1}{19}
\end{align*}
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{MOD}^{A} &= \frac{1}{i} \\
            &= 19
\end{align*}
$$
