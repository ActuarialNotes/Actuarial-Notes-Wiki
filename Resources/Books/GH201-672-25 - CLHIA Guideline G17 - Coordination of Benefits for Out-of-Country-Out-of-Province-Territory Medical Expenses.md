A CLHIA guideline describing how to coordinate the payments from all plans under which an individual is covered for out-of-country, out-of-province or territory medical expenses, and helping ensure that total payments from all plans do not exceed the total expenses incurred. Its intent is effective claims management, minimal assessment and payment delays, assistance to the covered individual in resolving the claim, fair coordination of benefits among the plans involved, and identification and discouragement of duplicate or fraudulent claims. The copy read carries a 2013 copyright.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201C (SOA)|Exam GH-201C]] — Regulation & Taxation, outcome 3b; the whole guideline (the syllabus reading GH201-672-25).

## 1 Introduction
- Many Canadians have more than one plan providing coverage for out-of-country/out-of-province/territory medical expenses, for example a group plan, an individual travel health plan and a credit card.
- An out-of-country claim has two components: emergency assistance, which the insurer or plan administrator contacted first will generally initiate as case management, and claim payment, where coverage varies from plan to plan.

## 2 Purpose
- It describes how to coordinate the payments from all plans under which an individual is covered, and helps ensure that the total payments from all plans do not exceed the total expenses incurred.
