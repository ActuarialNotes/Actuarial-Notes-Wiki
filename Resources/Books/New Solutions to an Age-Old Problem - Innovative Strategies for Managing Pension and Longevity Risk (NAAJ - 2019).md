A paper by Amy Kessler of Prudential Retirement in the North American Actuarial Journal, published online on 20 November 2019 and later printed in volume 25, Supplement 1 (pp. S7-S24). It reports that the pension and longevity risk transfer market is about a decade old but has already seen more than US$470 billion of deals, mainly buy-outs, buy-ins and longevity swaps in the UK, US, Canada and the Netherlands, and argues that transactions must be simplified, standardized and made available to all pension schemes.
