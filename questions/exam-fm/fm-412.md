A two-year annuity-immediate with level annual payments has a Macaulay duration of $D$ using a force of interest of $\delta$.

Determine $D$.

- A) $\dfrac{1+2e^{-\delta}}{1+e^{-\delta}}$
- B) $\dfrac{1+2e^{-2\delta}}{1+e^{-\delta}}$
- C) $\dfrac{1+e^{-2\delta}}{1+e^{-\delta}}$
- D) $\dfrac{1+e^{-\delta}}{1+e^{-2\delta}}$
- E) $\dfrac{1+2e^{-\delta}}{1+e^{-2\delta}}$

## Explanation

At force of interest $\delta$ the present value of 1 due at time $t$ is $e^{-\delta t}$. The annuity pays the same amount at times 1 and 2, so the payment cancels, and the Macaulay duration is the average of the payment times weighted by these discount factors. Dividing the numerator and denominator by $e^{-\delta}$ in the second step:

$$
\begin{align*}
D &= \frac{e^{-\delta} + 2e^{-2\delta}}{e^{-\delta} + e^{-2\delta}} \\
  &= \frac{1 + 2e^{-\delta}}{1 + e^{-\delta}}
\end{align*}
$$
