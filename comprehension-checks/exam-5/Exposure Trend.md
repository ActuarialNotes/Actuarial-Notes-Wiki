Workers compensation is rated on payroll, and wage inflation is running about 4% a year. An analyst trends the losses but leaves the historical exposures untrended. What is the effect?

- A) No effect — only the numerator needs trending
- B) The two movements cancel out on their own
- C) The historical pure premium is understated, so the indication comes out too small
- D) The historical pure premium is overstated, so the indication comes out too large
