The following information is available for a federally regulated property and casualty insurance company as at December 31, 2018. All amounts are in thousands of dollars (\$000s).

| 20.10 – Assets | Current year | Prior year |
|---|---|---|
| Cash and Cash Equivalents | 0 | 0 |
| Investment Income due and accrued | 0 | 0 |
| **Total Investments** | 380,000 | 350,000 |
| **Recoverable from Reinsurers** – Unearned Premiums | 70,000 | 60,000 |
| **Recoverable from Reinsurers** – Unpaid Claims and Adjustments | 140,000 | 140,000 |
| **Total Assets** | ? | 700,000 |

| 20.20 – Liabilities and Equity | Current year | Prior year |
|---|---|---|
| Unearned Premiums | 140,000 | 120,000 |
| Unpaid Claims and Adjustments | 380,000 | 350,000 |
| **Total Liabilities** | 650,000 | 600,000 |

| 20.30 – Statement of Income | Current year | Prior year |
|---|---|---|
| **Premiums Written** – Direct | 200,000 | 180,000 |
| **Premiums Written** – Reinsurance Assumed | 30,000 | 20,000 |
| **Premiums Written** – Reinsurance Ceded | 80,000 | 50,000 |
| **Underwriting Income (Loss)** | 13,000 | 8,000 |
| **Net Investment Income** | 17,000 | 10,000 |
| Total Income Taxes | 7,000 | 5,000 |
| **Net Income (Loss) for the Year** | 23,000 | 13,000 |

| 20.54 – Statement of Changes in Equity | Current year | Prior year |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Comprehensive Income for the Year** | 30,000 | 18,000 |
| Dividends – Preferred | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Dividends – Common | 14,000 | 9,000 |

The company has no income from subsidiaries and no realized capital gains.

## Part a (2 points)

Calculate the following key financial indicators for the current year:

- i. Return on revenue
- ii. Net loss reserves to equity
- iii. Overall net leverage
- iv. Investment yield

### Explanation
**i. Return on revenue**

$$\text{RoR} = \frac{\text{Net income before taxes}}{\text{GWP}} = \frac{\text{UW income} + \text{Net Investment Income}}{\text{Direct Written Premiums} + \text{Assumed Written Premiums}} = \frac{17{,}000 + 13{,}000}{200{,}000 + 30{,}000} = 13.04\%$$

**ii. Net loss reserves to equity**

- Net loss reserves $=$ unpaid claims and adjustments $-$ unpaid claims and adjustments recoverable from reinsurers $= 380{,}000 - 140{,}000 = 220{,}000$ (as printed; the 208.7% below is the ratio of the correct difference, $380{,}000 - 140{,}000$, to equity)
- Equity $=$ prior year equity $+$ total comprehensive income of the year $-$ dividends $= (700{,}000 - 600{,}000) + 30{,}000 - 1{,}000 - 4{,}000 = 115{,}000$ (4,000 as printed; the question gives common dividends of 14,000, which is what makes the total 115,000)

$$\text{Net Loss Reserves to Equity} = \frac{\text{Net Loss Reserves}}{\text{Equity}} = \frac{220{,}000}{115{,}000} = 208.7\%$$

**iii. Overall net leverage**

- NWP $= 200{,}000 + 30{,}000 - 80{,}000 = 150{,}000$
- Total net liabilities $= 650{,}000 - 70{,}000 - 140{,}000 = 440{,}000$

$$\text{Overall Net Leverage} = \frac{\text{Net Written Premiums} + \text{Total Net Liabilities}}{\text{Equity}} = \frac{150{,}000 + 440{,}000}{115{,}000} = 513.0\%$$

**iv. Investment yield**

$$\text{Investment Yield} = \frac{2 \times (\text{Net Investment Income} + \text{OCI})}{\text{Prior Year Invested Assets} + \text{Current Year Invested Assets} - \text{Net Investment Income} - \text{OCI}}$$

$$= \frac{2 \times (17{,}000 + 30{,}000 - 23{,}000)}{380{,}000 + 350{,}000 - 17{,}000 - (30{,}000 - 23{,}000)} = 6.8\%$$

Alternatively, without OCI:

$$\text{Investment Yield} = \frac{2 \times \text{Net Investment Income}}{\text{Prior Year Invested Assets} + \text{Current Year Invested Assets} - \text{Net Investment Income}} = \frac{2 \times 17{,}000}{380{,}000 + 350{,}000 - 17{,}000} = 4.8\%$$

For the investment yield, both answers based on the MSA reading and the Annual Return were accepted.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to know how to calculate the key financial indicators and their components.
Common errors included:

- Using the Total Comprehensive Income as the Other Comprehensive Income
- Not using Total Liabilities to calculate the Total Net Liabilities
- Using the Total Assets instead of the Total Invested Assets to calculate the Investment Yield

## Part b (1 point)

Comment on the company's financial health.

### Explanation
- RoR is 13.04% > 6.2%: ok
- Net Loss Reserves to Equity is 208.7% > 200%: not ok
- Overall Net Leverage is 513% > 400%: not ok

Overall, the company is in poor financial position as it has good return but it may be exposed to financial distress if reserves are inadequate.

Alternatively: overall the company may be in poor financial condition because the company has good return but there is a risk for the solvency of the company if there are small deviations in outstanding reserves.

### Examiner Report
Candidates were expected to demonstrate knowledge of the key financial indicators thresholds and their meanings

A common error included:

- Not recalling the required threshold
