The owner of a building insured under the BPP has used only 25% of its total square footage for 70 days, with the rest empty and no renovation under way. A fire then causes \$100,000 of covered damage. The deductible is \$1,000. How much does the insurer pay?

- A) \$0
- B) \$84,000
- C) \$84,150
- D) \$99,000

## Explanation

For an owner, the building is vacant unless at least 31% of its total square footage is rented and used, or used by the owner, for customary operations. At 25% it is vacant, and it has been for more than 60 consecutive days. Fire is not one of the six causes the vacancy condition removes, so the payment is reduced by 15%:

$$
\text{Paid} = 0.85 \times (100{,}000 - 1{,}000) = 84{,}150
$$

The answer is **C**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Treats fire as one of the excluded causes (vandalism, sprinkler leakage, building glass breakage, water damage, theft and attempted theft).
- **B** — Takes 15% off the loss and then subtracts the deductible, rather than reducing the amount the insurer would otherwise pay.
- **D** — Treats the building as occupied; partial use below 31% does not avoid vacancy.
