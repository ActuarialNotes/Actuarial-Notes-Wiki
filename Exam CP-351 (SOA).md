# Exam CP-351
**CP 351 – Asset Liability Management** is an exam of the Society of Actuaries' Fellowship (FSA) pathway. Its syllabus covers the Nov 2026/Mar 2027/Jul 2027 sittings.

## Learning Objectives

> [!example]- Objectives of Asset Liability Management {5–20%}
> The candidate will understand the objectives of [[Asset-Liability Management|Asset Liability Management]] ([[Asset-Liability Management|ALM]]).
>
> 1. Explain the principles of [[Asset-Liability Management|Asset Liability Management]]
> 2. Understand the lessons learned from [[ALM Failure|ALM failures]]
> 3. Demonstrate an understanding of various [[ALM Risk|ALM risks]]
> 4. Describe how different [[Pension|pension]] and [[Embedded Option|insurance contracts generate embedded options]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - CP351-100-26: IAA Risk Book - Asset Liability Management (1a)
> - CP351-101-25: ALM for Life, Annuities, and Pensions (sections 1-2) (1a)
> - CP351-102-25: Case Study on General American Life Insurance Company (1b)
> - Financial Enterprise Risk Management, Sweeting, P., 2nd Edition, 2017 (1b)
>     - Ch. 20: Case Studies (sections 1, 2, 4 & 6)
> - What Can Insurers and Pension Funds Learn from Bank Failures (1b)
> - CP351-115-26: IAA Risk Book - ERM Insights from Failures and Near Failures (sections 3.2, 3.3, 3.4 & 3.10 only) (1b)
> - Financial Enterprise Risk Management, Sweeting, P., 2nd Edition, 2017 (1c)
>     - Ch. 8: Risk Identification
> - Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022 (1c)
>     - Ch. 2: Risk Taxonomy
> - CP351-104-25: Life Insurance Risk, Capital, and ALM in the Age of Uncertainty - Paper 1 (1c)
>     - Risk Inventory, Taxonomy, Calibration
> - CP351-101-25: ALM for Life, Annuities, and Pensions (section 3) (1d)
> - CP351-105-25: Chapter 16 of Asset/Liability Management of Financial Institutions, Tilman 2003 (1d)
> - CP351-106-25: Liability Driven Investment Explained Chapters 1-2 (1d)

> [!example]- Measuring Risks from Assets and Liabilities {25–45%}
> The candidate will understand how to measure [[Risk|risks]] from assets and liabilities.
>
> 1. Demonstrate an understanding of various [[Risk Identification|risk identification tools]]
> 2. Evaluate a company’s or a [[Portfolio's Exposure|portfolio’s exposures]] to various [[Risk|risks]]
> 3. Understand measures of [[Regulatory Capital|regulatory capital adequacy]] for [[Life Insurance Company|life insurance companies]] in various jurisdictions
> 4. Evaluate the difficulties of investing for [[Long-Tail Liability|long-tail liabilities]] (i.e. beyond 30 years) such as [[Inflation|inflation]] and [[Longevity Risk|longevity]]
>

> - Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022 (2b)

> [!example]- Tools and Strategies to Manage ALM Risks {40–60%}
> The candidate will understand tools and strategies to manage [[ALM Risk|ALM risks]].
>
> 1. Develop and critique [[Asset Allocation|asset allocation strategies]] appropriate to underlying [[Asset-Liability Management|liability profiles]] such as [[Pension Plan|pension plans]] and [[Long-Tail Liability|long-tail insurance liabilities]]
> 2. Demonstrate an understanding of and apply tools and techniques for measuring and managing [[Interest Rate Risk|interest rate risk]] in an [[Asset-Liability Management|ALM context]]
> 3. Apply [[Stress Testing|stress testing]] and scenario analysis to assess [[Stress Testing|extreme ALM events]]
> 4. Understand and evaluate model and [[Parameter Risk|parameter risks]]
> 5. Explain and implement techniques used to mitigate [[Market Risk|market risks]]
> 6. Understand [[Interest Rate Derivative|interest rate derivatives]] and use them to mitigate [[Interest Rate Risk|interest rate risk]]
> 7. Explain different methods available for [[Risk Transfer|transferring risk]]
>
> **Readings:**
> - CP351-101-25: ALM for Life, Annuities, and Pensions (section 5) (3a, 3e, 3f)
> - CP351-106-25: Liability Driven Investment Explained, Ch. 3-7 (3a)
> - Handbook of Asset and Liability Management, Volume 2, Zenios, Stavros, and Ziemba, William, 2007 (3b)
>     - Ch. 13: Stochastic Programming Models for Strategic and Tactical Asset Allocation - A Study from Norwegian Life Insurance
> - Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022 (3b)
>     - Ch. 6: Copulas
> - CP351-112-25: The Devil is in the Tails: Actuarial Mathematics and the Subprime Mortgage Crisis (3b)
> - CP351-113-25: Chapter 4 of ALM for Banks and Insurance Companies, Habart (excluding 4.3.6) (3b, 3c, 3d)
> - Handbook of Asset and Liability Management, Volume 2, Zenios, Stavros, and Ziemba, William, 2007 (3b)
>     - Ch. 18: A Scenario Approach to ALM
> - Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022 (3c)
>     - Ch. 7: Stress Testing
> - Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022

>     - Ch. 15: Risk mitigation using options and derivatives (3e)

>     - Ch. 5: Forwards and Swaps
>     - Ch. 6: Futures and Options
> - Quantitative Enterprise Risk Management, Hardy, Mary and Saunders, David, 2022 (3g)
>     - Ch. 16: Risk Transfer
