The random variable $X$ follows a distribution with probability function

$$
p(x) = \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^x, \qquad \text{for } x = 0, 1, 2, \ldots
$$

Calculate $\text{Var}\bigl(X \mid X > 1\bigr)$.

- A) $13$
- B) $16$
- C) $20$
- D) $22$
- E) $24$

## Explanation

This is a geometric distribution counting *failures* before the first success, with $p = 1/5$.

First find the conditioning probability:

$$
\begin{align*}
P[X > 1] &= 1 - P[X=0] - P[X=1] \\
         &= 1 - \frac{1}{5} - \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)\left(\frac{4}{5}\right) \\
         &= \frac{16}{25} = \left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^2
\end{align*}
$$

Dividing the probability function by that gives the conditional distribution, for $x = 2, 3, 4, \ldots$:

$$
\frac{\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^x}{\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^2} = \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{x-2}
$$

That is the *original* probability function shifted right by 2 — the geometric distribution's **memorylessness**. A shift moves the mean but leaves the variance untouched, so

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{Var}(X \mid X > 1) &= \text{Var}(X) \\
                         &= \frac{1-p}{p^2} = \frac{4/5}{1/25} \\
                         &= \mathbf{20}
\end{align*}
$$

Recognising the shift is the whole solution — computing conditional moments directly would work but is far longer. The geometric is the one discrete distribution for which conditioning on survival changes nothing but the location.
