Bank A lends a certain sum of money at an annual effective interest rate of 7%. The loan is to be repaid by 16 annual payments of 1000, with the first payment due after one year.

Immediately after receiving the 8th payment, Bank A sells the right to receive the remaining 8 payments to Bank B. Bank A's yield on the entire transaction is an annual effective interest rate of 6%.

Calculate the amount that Bank B paid to assume the loan.

- A) 5159
- B) 5541
- C) 5655
- D) 5971
- E) 6210

## Explanation

Let $K$ be the amount that Bank B paid. The amount Bank A lent is the 16 payments discounted at the loan rate of 7%. Bank A's 6% yield on the entire transaction means that, at 6%, the amount lent equals the present value of what Bank A actually received: the first 8 payments of 1000 and the price $K$ at time 8. That gives the equation

$$1000\,a_{\overline{16}|0.07} = 1000\,a_{\overline{8}|0.06} + \frac{K}{1.06^{8}}$$

Then,

$$
\begin{align*}
K &= 1000\left(a_{\overline{16}|0.07} - a_{\overline{8}|0.06}\right)(1.06)^{8} \\
  &= 1000(9.446649 - 6.209794)(1.593848) \\
  &= 3236.855(1.593848) \\
  &= 5159.05
\end{align*}
$$
