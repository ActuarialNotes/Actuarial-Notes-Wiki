A random variable $T$ represents the total useful life of a machine, in years measured from when it is new, and follows a uniform distribution on $[0, 5]$.

Calculate the expected remaining useful life of the machine, given that it has already survived its first 2 years (i.e., $T > 2$).

- A) 0.5
- B) 1.0
- C) 1.5
- D) 2.0
- E) 2.5

## Explanation

Given $T > 2$, the conditional density is the original density divided by $P(T > 2) = 3/5$:

$$f(t \mid T > 2) = \frac{1/5}{3/5} = \frac{1}{3}, \qquad 2 < t \le 5$$

so $T \mid T > 2 \sim \text{Uniform}[2, 5]$, and

$$E[T \mid T > 2] = \int_2^5 t \cdot \frac{1}{3}\,dt = \frac{5^2 - 2^2}{6} = 3.5$$

The machine has already lived 2 years, so its remaining useful life is $T - 2$:

$$E[T - 2 \mid T > 2] = E[T \mid T > 2] - 2 = 3.5 - 2 = \mathbf{1.5}$$
