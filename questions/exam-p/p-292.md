Let $X$ and $Y$ be discrete random variables with joint probability function

$$
p(x,y) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{1}{21}, & \text{for } x = 0, 1, \ldots, 5 \text{ and } y = 0, \ldots, x \\ 0, & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
$$

Calculate the variance of $Y$.

- A) 1.67
- B) 2.22
- C) 3.33
- D) 5.00
- E) 5.56

## Explanation

The 21 points $(x,y)$ are equally likely. A given value $y$ occurs at $x = y, y+1, \ldots, 5$, that is at $6 - y$ points, so the marginal probability function of $Y$ is

$$
p(y) = \begin{cases} 6/21, & y = 0 \\ 5/21, & y = 1 \\ 4/21, & y = 2 \\ 3/21, & y = 3 \\ 2/21, & y = 4 \\ 1/21, & y = 5. \end{cases}
$$

Then

$$
\begin{align*}
E(Y) &= 0\cdot\tfrac{6}{21} + 1\cdot\tfrac{5}{21} + 2\cdot\tfrac{4}{21} + 3\cdot\tfrac{3}{21} + 4\cdot\tfrac{2}{21} + 5\cdot\tfrac{1}{21} = \frac{35}{21} = \frac{5}{3} \approx 1.67, \\
E(Y^2) &= 0\cdot\tfrac{6}{21} + 1\cdot\tfrac{5}{21} + 4\cdot\tfrac{4}{21} + 9\cdot\tfrac{3}{21} + 16\cdot\tfrac{2}{21} + 25\cdot\tfrac{1}{21} = \frac{105}{21} = 5,
\end{align*}
$$

and

$$
\text{Var}(Y) = E(Y^2) - [E(Y)]^2 = 5 - \left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{20}{9} \approx \mathbf{2.22}.
$$
