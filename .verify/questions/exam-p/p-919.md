## [F-001] Explanation teaches the answer through an MGF, which the Nov 2026 Exam P syllabus leaves out
- entry_type: finding
- author: agent:validate-v1
- run_id: 2026-09-27T19:59Z/bfa2
- date: 2026-09-28
- severity: minor
- status: open
- locus: ## Explanation, first paragraph and display
- claim: For $X \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda)$ with $\lambda = 1/2$ (mean = 2), the moment generating function is: $$E[e^{tX}] = \frac{\lambda}{\lambda - t}, \quad t < \lambda$$
- evidence: SOA Probability Exam syllabus, Nov 2026 (sha256:bed27462...): Topic 2 learning outcomes (a)-(f) list pdf/cdf, conditional probabilities, expected values "including moments, mode, median, and percentiles", variance/SD/CV and insurance payments; no outcome names moment generating functions. The only occurrences of "generating" in the syllabus are exclusions in the Leemis reading list: "Chapter 3 (exclude moment generating functions in 3.4, exclude 3.5)" and "Chapter 6 (exclude continuous, exclude moment generating functions in 6.3, exclude 6.4)". The quantity asked, E[e^{-X}], is on-syllabus as the expectation of a function of X (outcome 2c): direct integration gives integral_0^inf e^{-x}(1/2)e^{-x/2} dx = (1/2)/(3/2) = 1/3, the same key A. So the answer is unaffected; only the taught method is outside the learning outcomes.
- source_rank: 1
- proposed_action: Human decides whether to lead the explanation with the direct integral E[e^{-X}] = integral of e^{-x} f(x) dx and keep the MGF only as an aside. Authoring, not applied.
