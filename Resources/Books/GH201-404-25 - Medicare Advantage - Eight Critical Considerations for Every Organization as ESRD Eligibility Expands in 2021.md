A Milliman white paper, dated December 2019, giving a brief overview of the change that lets Medicare-eligible individuals with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) enroll in Medicare Advantage plans as of January 1, 2021, and eight key questions each Medicare Advantage organization should consider when planning for 2021.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam GH-201U (SOA)|Exam GH-201U]] — Outcomes 4a; the whole paper.

## Background

## Eight critical considerations
- 1 What is your current and projected ESRD financial performance?
- 2 Can your contractual terms with dialysis providers be improved?
- 3 Are you managing care for your ESRD population?
    - Promoting and Facilitating Home Dialysis
    - Facilitating Regularly Scheduled Visits
    - Providing Patient Education and Involvement
    - Preventing and Identifying Complications
    - Preventing ESRD Progression
- 4 Are your benefits attractive to ESRD beneficiaries?
- 5 Does your formulary cover medications attractive to ESRD members?
- 6 Will you make adjustments to your marketing and sales strategy?
- 7 Will administrative costs increase?
- 8 Are your risk-taking providers aware of potential increases in ESRD patients?
