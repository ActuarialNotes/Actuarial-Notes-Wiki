A textbook on private pensions in the United States, published with the Pension Research Council of the Wharton School. The Pension Research Council describes the revised and updated ninth edition as adding to past knowledge while giving new perspectives on the provision of retirement income. Its eight sections run from the forces behind retirement plans through plan design, regulation, actuarial practice and accounting, to investments and the future of pensions.

> [!info] On the syllabus
> - [[Exam RET-101 (SOA)|Exam RET-101]] — Ch. 5 Human Resource Incentives, excluding pp. 154-156 (1a, 1b, 1c; 2b; 4a-4n); Ch. 10 Defined Benefit Design (1a, 1b); Ch. 28 Investing and Defined Contribution Plans (1a, 1c); Ch. 9 Risks of Outliving Resources, pp. 270-289 only (2a, 2d).
