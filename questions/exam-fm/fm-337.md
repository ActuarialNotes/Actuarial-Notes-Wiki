A purchase of 5020 is paid off with a loan at an annual effective interest rate of 6.25%. Payments are made at the end of each year until the loan is paid off. Each payment is 360 except for a final balloon payment, which is less than 720.

Calculate the amount of the balloon payment.

- A) 611
- B) 630
- C) 649
- D) 667
- E) 685

## Explanation

$5020 = 360\,a_{\overline{n}|0.0625} \implies n = 33.85$. So 33 payments of 360 fall just short of repaying the loan, and 34 would overpay it. Use $n = 33$: the loan is repaid by 32 regular payments of 360 and a 33rd, balloon, payment of $360 + X$, where $X$ is the extra amount paid at time 33:

$$
\begin{align*}
5020 &= 360\,a_{\overline{33}|0.0625} + X v^{33} \\
5020 &= 4980.946 + X(1.0625)^{-33} \\
39.054 &= X(0.135252) \\
X &= 288.75
\end{align*}
$$

The balloon payment is the 33rd payment: $B = 360 + 288.75 = 648.75 \approx 649$.

(Paying off the loan one year earlier, with a balloon as the 32nd payment, would take 970.59, more than 720; that is what the stem's "less than 720" rules out.)
