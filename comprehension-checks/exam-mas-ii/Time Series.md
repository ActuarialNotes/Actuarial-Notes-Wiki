A series of quarterly loss ratios holds a steady long-run average, but its swings get visibly wider year after year. Is it weakly stationary?

- A) Yes — a constant mean is what weak stationarity asks for
- B) No — weak stationarity also demands constant variance
- C) Yes, provided the widening is smooth and deterministic
- D) No, but a single round of differencing will always repair it
