A restaurant's BPP shows no higher limit for the fire department service charge additional coverage and a \$1,000 deductible. A kitchen fire is put out by two fire departments, and under a local ordinance the restaurant is billed \$2,200 for their service. How much does the BPP pay toward the bill?

- A) \$0
- B) \$1,000
- C) \$1,200
- D) \$2,200

## Explanation

The fire department service charge additional coverage pays the insured's liability for charges required by local ordinance (or assumed by contract before the loss), up to \$1,000 per described premises unless the declarations show more, regardless of how many departments respond. **No deductible applies** to it. The answer is **B**.

**Why not the others**

- **A** — Treats the charge as wiped out by the deductible, but this additional coverage has none.
- **C** — Subtracts the deductible from the bill and pays the rest, which ignores both the \$1,000 cap and the no-deductible rule.
- **D** — Pays the bill in full; the limit is \$1,000 per premises however many departments respond.
