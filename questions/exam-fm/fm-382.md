A company would to be protected from large interest rate changes. It has a liability of 5000 due at time 3, and three possible sets of asset cash flows:

- I. $A_1 = 1511.72$, $A_4 = 3500.00$
- II. $A_2 = 3481.00$, $A_4 = 1412.20$
- III. $A_1 = 2580.95$, $A_4 = 2625.00$

where $A_t$ is the asset cash flow at time $t$.

The effective interest rate is 5% per time period.

Determine which set(s) of asset cash flows fully immunize the liability.

- A) I only
- B) II only
- C) III only
- D) I, II, and III
- E) The correct answer is not given by (A), (B), (C), or (D).

## Explanation

The conditions for full immunization of a single liability cash flow are:

1. PV(assets) = PV(liability)
2. Duration(assets) = Duration(liability)
3. The asset cash flows occur before and after the liability cash flow.

At 5% per period, the liability has present value $5000v^{3} = 4319.19$ and duration 3.

Checking condition 1, the present values of the three sets of asset cash flows are:

$$
\begin{align*}
PV_{I} &= 1511.72v + 3500v^{4} \\
       &= 1439.73 + 2879.46 \\
       &= 4319.19 \\
PV_{II} &= 3481v^{2} + 1412.20v^{4} \\
        &= 3157.37 + 1161.82 \\
        &= 4319.19 \\
PV_{III} &= 2580.95v + 2625v^{4} \\
         &= 2458.05 + 2159.59 \\
         &= 4617.64
\end{align*}
$$

Only I and II are present value-matched, so III is ruled out at this point.

Checking condition 2, the Macaulay durations of I and II, each time weighted by the present value of the cash flow at that time, are:

$$
\begin{align*}
D_{I} &= \frac{1(1439.73) + 4(2879.46)}{4319.19} \\
      &= \frac{12{,}957.57}{4319.19} \\
      &= 3.000 \\
D_{II} &= \frac{2(3157.37) + 4(1161.82)}{4319.19} \\
       &= \frac{10{,}962.02}{4319.19} \\
       &= 2.538
\end{align*}
$$

Only I is duration-matched, so II is ruled out at this point.

Checking condition 3, the asset cash flows of I do occur before (time 1) and after (time 4) the liability at time 3.

So only I fully immunizes the liability.
