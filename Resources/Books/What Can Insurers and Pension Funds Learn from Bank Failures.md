A report, dated April 2023, on an expert panel discussion organized by the SOA Research Institute after the collapse of Silicon Valley Bank and Signature Bank and the takeover of Credit Suisse. It draws lessons for the pension and insurance industries on asset-liability management, rising interest rates, systemic risks, risk management and governance, and adds comments the moderator solicited from industry experts after the discussion.
